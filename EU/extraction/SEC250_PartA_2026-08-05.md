## Part A records

### E250-01 — MFF heading 7 is named, and it has a named sub-ceiling

```
URL:       (as above)
LOCATION:  "Figures by MFF heading, cluster, programme, section, budget line and
           nomenclature changes", s. 1.1 "Figures by financial framework headings
           (aggregate)", heading-7 rows of the MFF ceiling table
QUOTE:     "7. European Public Administration 10 635,0 11 058,0 11 419,0
           11 773,0 12 124,0 12 506,0 12 959,0 82 474,0 of which: Administrative
           expenditure of the institutions 8 216,0 8 528,0 8 772,0 9 006,0
           9 219,0 9 464,0 9 786,0 62 991,0"
NAMES:     Multiannual Financial Framework 2021-2027
TENSE:     PRESENT
NOTES:     **The first documented anchor for the first two levels of the MFF code
           string.** Heading 7 is named in words — "European Public
           Administration" — and it carries exactly one named sub-ceiling,
           "Administrative expenditure of the institutions", which is the
           smaller of the two components (EUR 62 991,0m of EUR 82 474,0m across
           the seven years).
           That is a two-part partition of heading 7, and the code families
           observed across the eleven budget sections are also a two-part
           partition: `7.1.*` and `7.2.*`. See E250-02, which supplies the other
           half, and G.22 finding 2 for what the pair does and does not settle.
           **The document does not connect the words to the digits.** It never
           writes "7.2" next to "Administrative expenditure of the institutions".
           The correspondence is inferred and is flagged as inferred.
```

### E250-02 — Title 21 is pensions plus European Schools, under heading 7

```
URL:       (as above)
LOCATION:  s. 3.1 "Introduction to the nomenclature", paragraph beginning
           "Title 20 includes"
QUOTE:     "Title 20 includes the administrative expenditure of the Commission
           and Title 21 the pensions (of former staff and members of all EU
           institutions) and the contributions to the European Schools. These two
           titles include the expenditure falling under the MFF heading 7
           'European Public Administration'."
NAMES:     Multiannual Financial Framework (heading 7)
TENSE:     PRESENT
NOTES:     **This is the closest thing to a printed key that has been found in
           three sessions of looking**, and it is worth being precise about what
           it does.
           It states that pensions and European Schools contributions form one
           named group under heading 7, distinct from administrative
           expenditure. The `7.1.*` codes observed in the budget sections are
           *exactly* pensions and European Schools — `7.1.11` and `7.1.12x`
           pensions, `7.1.2x` European Schools (G.21 finding 2) — and the
           `7.2.*` codes are *exactly* the institutions' administrative lines.
           So the grouping the digits imply is documented; **the mapping of that
           grouping onto the digits `1` and `2` is not.**
           DISC-07-03's status therefore changes in a specific, limited way:
           the *semantic content* of the first two levels is now documented, and
           the *encoding* is still inference. The third level — the section
           numeral and the `9<TAG>` series — remains entirely undocumented.
```

### E250-03 — the nomenclature's own account of itself

```
URL:       (as above)
LOCATION:  s. 3.1 "Introduction to the nomenclature", opening two paragraphs
QUOTE:     "The nomenclature for the Multiannual Financial Framework (MFF)
           2021-2027, as decided following the adoption of Budget 2021, allows
           for clearer, more informative and better aligned links between the MFF
           headings and programmes. Since the 2021 budget and in line with the
           political agreement on the MFF 2021-2027, the budget nomenclature is
           structured by programme cluster (policy area) corresponding to the
           'purpose' within the meaning of Article 47 of the Financial
           Regulation."
NAMES:     Financial Regulation
TENSE:     PRESENT
NOTES:     Establishes that a nomenclature *decision* exists and is dated ("as
           decided following the adoption of Budget 2021"), which is the first
           evidence that the taxonomy is a deliberate published scheme rather
           than an internal convention. **It does not cite the decision**, and
           the section goes on to describe the *budget-line* nomenclature
           (titles, chapters, articles, items) in detail without ever describing
           the *MFF sub-heading code* column.
           So the document has an "Introduction to the nomenclature" that
           introduces a different nomenclature from the one DISC-07-03 is about.
           That is why three sessions of searching for a key inside the section
           PDFs found nothing: the key that exists is for the other scheme.
```
