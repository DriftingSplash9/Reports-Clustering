### S05-02 — the budget guidelines the estimate is drawn up under

```

QUOTE:     "As indicated in the guidelines for the establishment of the budget

           expenditure to a maximum of 2 % compared to the 2026 budget."
           Footnote 1: "Letter of 18 December 2025 from Commissioner Serafin to
           President Murphy."
           Footnote 2: "Note of 12 December 2025 Establishment of the 2027
           Statement of Estimates and Draft Budget (DB)."
NAMES:     AGENCY ONLY
TENSE:     PRESENT
NOTES:     Deliberate `AGENCY ONLY`. Both inputs are *named and dated* but
           neither has a title in the §4.3 sense — one is a letter identified by
           sender, recipient and date, the other a note identified by date and
           subject line. The DG BUDG note is plainly an annual instrument (it is
           the budget circular by function) and would satisfy §4.2 on cadence,
           but it fails §4.3. This is `Research.1.md` §7's "disclosure stops one
           level short of a title", in an unusually pure form: we are told
           exactly who wrote it and exactly when, and still cannot name it.
           Terminus candidate, kind `unpublishable`, if the letter and note turn
           out not to be released — not checked.
```
