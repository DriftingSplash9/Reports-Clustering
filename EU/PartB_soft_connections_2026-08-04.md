Output Rule* (added 2026-08-02): every closed session or requested `G.*` hand-off
must also produce a savable list of all current Part B soft connections. This is
the **first time that rule has been honoured** — it went unmet for four sessions
because the governing briefs went unread, and the rule sits after §10's closing
summary, past where the brief looks finished.

> **Strictly provisional and non-authoritative.** Nothing here is an edge.
> `Research.1.md` §6 governs: *any claim in Part B that is not backed by a quote
> in Part A is deleted without being read.* Part B cannot introduce a node, an
> edge, or a fact — it can only re-arrange what Part A already proved. The
> `evidence` column below is a **pointer to where to look**, not a verbatim
> quote, and several entries point at a document rather than a location within
> it. None of these have been verified against Part A this session.
