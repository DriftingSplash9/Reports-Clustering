# Doc restructure — 2026-09-11, `PLAYBOOK-CORPUS.md`

**Thomas asked what could be done about the file, was shown the measurement, and said "do it."**
Not a scheduled sweep — a restructure, in the shape of the 2026-09-09 split, applied to the entries
that had broken it since. Pre-restructure copy: `archive/doc-restructures/PLAYBOOK-CORPUS-before-2026-09-11.md`,
sha256-verified before any edit.

## The measurement that prompted it — and the four handoffs of wrong diagnosis

Handoffs 083, 084 and 085 all said "**§2 is where the next cut goes**". **§2 was 27% of the file.**
Nobody had measured it. Measured 2026-09-11:

| section | chars | share | supposed to be |
|---|---|---|---|
| §6 Known traps | 8.6k | 31% | an INDEX, one line per trap |
| §7 Standing decisions | 8.4k | 31% | an INDEX, one line per ruling |
| §2 Standing rules | 7.6k | 27% | the rules themselves |
| §0 + header | 3.0k | 11% | routing + restructure history |

**The index design was sound; a minority of entries had broken it.** Median bullet in §6 was 94
characters and in §7 was 87 — exactly the one-line pointer the split intended. But **17 bullets out
of 73 carried 10.3k, 37% of the whole file**, the longest 1,716 characters.

**Why, and it is the generalisable part:** the 2026-09-09 split moved the *then-existing* reasoning
out and left an empty index. Rounds 38-45 then wrote full prose straight back into it, because
nothing said not to. *A split without a rule against regrowth buys one handoff.*

## What was done

1. **Seventeen fat bullets → one line each + pointer**, bodies moved **byte-for-byte** into the
   `playbook/` file that owns the subject, under a dated "Moved here 2026-09-11" heading.
2. **The duplicated `_dropped` pair merged.** Two bullets, one in §6 and one in §7, both titled
   "`_dropped` entries come in THREE shapes", 2.6k between them in the same always-read file — and
   **already drifted**: §6 called the third shape a blind spot "no check covers", §7 said
   normalisation "correctly gives them null endpoints". Both true; they read as contradicting. Now
   one schema entry (what to write) plus one §6 line (the hand-check duty), each pointing at the other.
3. **Rule 19 trimmed from 3.2k to the rule itself** — the three tokens, the two consequences to act
   on, and the never-state-a-figure instruction. The "why this is not a fourth grade" essay went to
   `playbook/corpus-evidence.md`.
4. **The three add-and-remove ledger paragraphs deleted** (1.1k). Handoff 083 cut this once already
   and it regrew to three paragraphs in two handoffs. **A ledger of "added, removed nothing" is
   itself the thing it complains about.**
5. **§0's restructure history replaced by the rule that prevents the regrowth:**

   > **AN INDEX LINE IS ONE LINE.** If an entry needs a second sentence of justification, the
   > reasoning goes to the `playbook/` file that owns it and the line points there. **The one stated
   > exception is the schema block at the end of §7** — the closed unions and the `_dropped` shapes
   > are carried in full here, because every data change hits them and a pointer would be read too late.

## Result

**`PLAYBOOK-CORPUS.md` 27.6k → 19.5k (−29%).** Read cost for a corpus round **6.9% → 6.1%**; for a
renderer round unchanged at 5.0%. The file is no longer twice `HANDOFF.md` (14.0k) — it is 1.4×.

**73 bullets before, 73 after. Median 92 characters. Two over 300, both the schema block, both by
the stated exception.** The `playbook/` files absorbed the difference: naming 15.3→18.6k,
evidence 10.9→18.1k, nodes 9.1→10.9k, hosts 5.7→9.5k, route 7.1→8.0k. All five are on-demand.

## Verification

- **Nothing lost.** Every one of the 73 pre-restructure bullet openings was matched against the
  whole lane (the file plus all five `playbook/` files) afterwards: **0 missing.**
- **Every file pointer in the lane resolves** — checked against the filesystem, 0 broken.
- **One defect found and fixed during the pass:** the `### 7d. Parked and closed` heading was
  swallowed when the bullet above it was replaced, orphaning three rulings under §7c. Restored.
  *Splitting a markdown section on bullets attaches a trailing heading to the preceding bullet —
  check headings, not just bullet counts, after any scripted restructure.*

## What this does NOT fix

**`playbook/corpus-naming.md` is now 18.6k and `corpus-evidence.md` 18.1k** — each bigger than the
index that points at them. They are on-demand and a round opens at most one, so this is the intended
trade. But they have no sweep of their own beyond step 5b, and the same regrowth rule does not apply
to them. **If either passes ~25k, it wants splitting by question the way the lane was.**
