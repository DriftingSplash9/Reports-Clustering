### The three findings that cut across every file

**1. `HANDOFF.md` is not the problem, and the numbers say so.** Near-duplicate
detection across 25 consecutive handoffs found **exactly one** paragraph repeating
four or more times — the corpus-state line, which is *supposed* to repeat. §4
step 4's sweep is working. The bloat, where there is any, is in the slow layer.

**2. Thomas's read is correct: the machinery has been eating the rounds.** Of the
25, roughly eight were substantially about the apparatus rather than the corpus —
the grader-rulings round, the transport round, the xlsx extractor, two doc audits,
the doc consolidation, Round C's re-evaluation, the third PDF rendering — and most
of the remainder carried at least one ruling about how a rule works. That is not
waste; it is what a project with a hard evidence standard costs. But it means the
docs are now describing a system that is changing faster than the docs' own
review cadence, which is the next finding.

**3. `HANDOFF.md` gets swept every round and every fifth review; nothing sweeps
anything else.** `PLAYBOOK.md` §1 says this out loud — *"nothing sweeps a
playbook"* — and then provides no counterpart. This audit found **seven live
false statements** in the slow layer, every one of them a sentence that was true
when written:

- `PLAYBOOK-CORPUS.md` §7d describes the `proposed:` domain tags as an open
  question with 624 live tags. They were retired on 2026-09-06; `validate` prints
  **46 approved, 0 proposed**.
- `PLAYBOOK-RENDER.md` §3 says node instancing and the tick burst are unmeasured
  and "that is a `HANDOFF.md` item". `HANDOFF.md` no longer carries it.
- `notes/standing-issues.md` says the validator prints "✓ no dependency cites a
  bare homepage". Round 8 minted two that do.
- `REPORTS.md` says 8 edges lack an `evidence_url`. Zero do.
- `REPORTS.md`'s Decisions index gives a `ColourFamily` union that is two values
  short and one value wrong.
- `REPORTS.md` cites `Research.1.md` and `EU/extraction/…` at paths that moved to
  `archive/` on 2026-08-30.
- `README.md` carries a second, different copy of the sandbox-validate recipe from
  `PLAYBOOK.md` rule 4, missing the two traps rule 4 knows about.

**The structural recommendation, and it is one line:** give the slow layer the
same arithmetic trigger the fast layer has. §4 step 5 fires on
`ls archive/"Previous Handoffs"/HANDOFF-*.md | wc -l` being divisible by 5. Add a
step 5b that fires on the same count being divisible by 25 and checks the slow
layer against the corpus instead of against itself — every count, every union,
every cross-reference, every "as of". This audit is that check run once by hand;
it took one round and found seven defects. Four hand-run passes a year is cheap.
