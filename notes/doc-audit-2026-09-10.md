## A finding about the SWEEP, not the docs — and it needed deciding on this pass

**"Correct in place and show the old wording" makes the always-read files GROW, and at every-five it
makes them grow four times as often.** This pass's five corrections first added **+1.4k to
`PLAYBOOK-CORPUS.md`** — already the largest always-read file at twice `HANDOFF.md` — purely in
histories of numbers that are no longer stated. A sweep whose purpose is keeping the read path under
control cannot be a reliable way of enlarging it.

**Resolved without changing either rule**, because both are already satisfied by splitting them:
- the CORRECTION and the fact that the file changed its mind stay in place, one clause each, so no
  reader is misled and nothing quietly changes;
- the ACCOUNT — the old figures, the dates, why it kept rotting — moves to this file, which step 5b
  already designates as the record of the pass and which nobody reads on the always-read path.

Net after the split: `PLAYBOOK-CORPUS.md` **27,977 → 28,192 (+0.2k)** rather than +1.4k, and
`playbook/corpus-nodes.md` back under its starting size. **The rule for future passes: a correction
marker in the file, the history in the audit note.**
