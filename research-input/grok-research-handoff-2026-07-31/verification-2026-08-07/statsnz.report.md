## The AES disclosure, fetched first-hand

The AES DataInfo+ item (`https://datainfoplus.stats.govt.nz/Item/nz.govt.stats/36809771-984d-4e6b-89a1-576f2118b05b`) was fetched three times this session, successfully, before the domain closed up (see Tooling below). Verbatim:

Under **"Main users of the data"**:

> "The Annual Enterprise Survey data forms the basis of Statistics New Zealand's national accounts statistics including :
> Production/Industry Accounts, Institutional Sector Accounts, Inter-Industry Study, Provisional National Accounts.
> AES provides a wealth of information to economic researchers, forecasters, entrepreneurs and government agencies."

Under **"Usage and limitations of the data"**:

> "In addition to its use in the national accounts, the Annual Enterprise Survey is also a data source for other Statistics NZ existing and upcoming outputs, including: industry benchmarking, the Tatauranga Umanga Māori publication … Longitudinal Research of Business Dynamics project (see Longitudinal business database) Business Price Indexes, Regional Gross Domestic Product (GDP), Tourism Satellite Account, Non-profit Institutions Satellite Account."

So the five named products of the G.4 lead are confirmed on the AES side. The pass's rule, per the task: an edge is minted only where a document states the AES is an input, with the product's own documentation preferred. Four of five got product-side confirmation; one did not.

Mitigation: key quotes were double-fetched; the one variance found (the PPI revaluation sentence tail) is recorded in the edge basis rather than resolved silently. An integrator with raw fetch tooling could hard-verify the five basis documents in one pass.
