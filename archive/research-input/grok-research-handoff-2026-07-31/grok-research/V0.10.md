## 4. Highest-value next moves (ordered)

### A. Dairy / Supply Management (user priority — “oligarch” visualization)
This is the single best new domain for the next agent.

**Goal:** Map the statutory and statistical machinery that makes dairy (and other quota’d farm products) function as a closed, high-barrier system.

**Start with these concrete targets:**
1. Canadian Dairy Commission (CDC) — pricing formulas, support prices, and the relationship to provincial marketing boards
2. Provincial milk marketing boards (Alberta Milk, Dairy Farmers of Ontario, etc.) — quota allocation rules, transferability, and how they interact with CDC
3. Farm Products Agencies Act and the National Farm Products Council
4. StatCan agricultural balance sheet / farm cash receipts / dairy production tables that feed equalization and other formulas (we already have the agricultural balance sheet edge)
5. Any statutory or regulatory text that names how over-quota production is penalized or how quota is capitalized into farm values
6. Visual / graph goal: show the concentration of quota ownership and the barrier-to-entry effects as a dependency / control structure, not as opinion

**Why this is high-value:** It is a pure statutory + statistical closed loop with real money flows, exactly the kind of system the graph was built to expose.

### B. Banking / Credit (already partially mapped — deepen it)
Existing slices: `banking-supervision.json`, `credit-and-mortgages.json`, `rate-transmission.json`, `fed-h15-disclosure.json`

**Next high-value targets:**
- OSFI capital and liquidity rules that name specific StatCan or Bank of Canada series
- CDIC premium formulas
- Mortgage stress-test (B-20) and any statutory or guideline text that names the qualifying rate source
- Bank of Canada balance-sheet and policy-rate transmission into the mortgage market
- Any remaining commercial data vendors named in regulation (beyond what is already captured)

### C. Remaining Equalization / TFF cleanup
A few lower-priority named products and the full Territorial Formula Financing side still have open leads in the original `equalization-named-products.json` and `equalization-payroll-base.json`. Close only what the regulation actually names.

### D. Deeper Alberta energy royalty chain
`alberta-energy.json` already exists. Strengthen the links from physical production (Oil and Gas Extraction Survey) through par prices / WTI / bitumen valuation into the actual royalty formulas and then into provincial revenue that feeds municipal transfers.

### E. Visualization / “coolest looking masterpiece” work
