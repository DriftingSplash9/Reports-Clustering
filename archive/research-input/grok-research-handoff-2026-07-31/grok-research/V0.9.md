## 1. Project Identity & Core Mission

This is a long-running research project mapping **how official numbers and money actually flow** in Canada (with heavy Alberta focus) and selected US/international counterparts.

Primary product is a **dependency graph** of:
- Statistical releases (CPI, LFS, GDP, population, etc.)
- Statutory / regulatory formulas that consume those numbers (tax brackets, benefit rates, indexation, equalization, municipal funding)
- Financing and soft-power relationships with international organisations

The graph is built under strict evidence rules. The user is building it with both Claude and Grok; consistency of format and discipline across agents is critical.

### Standing Rules (never relax these)

- **Research.1.md is immutable.** Do not edit it. It defines the Part A extraction format, Item 1a priority (personal income tax indexation), and evidence standards.
- **V0.12.md** (Claude’s earlier session log) is also treated as historical context — do not edit.
- One provision / one clear claim per entry when doing statutory extraction.
- Prefer verbatim quotes + precise citations over paraphrase.
- Never invent arithmetic or causal links that the source documents do not support.
- Keep **three layers cleanly separated**:
  1. **Main documented graph** — calculated_from / methodology_depends_on / uses_data_from (statutes, IMDB, methodology manuals).
  2. **Financing layer** — who pays for the institutions (quotas, assessed contributions, project grants).
  3. **Soft / interpretive layer** — ritual meetings, agenda resonance, mutual consideration, peer pressure. Explicitly lower confidence; never feeds main-graph authority rankings.
