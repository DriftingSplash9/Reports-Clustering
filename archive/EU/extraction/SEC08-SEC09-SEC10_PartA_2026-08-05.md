### S10-02 — a headcount table that is flat for a documented, categorical reason

```
URL:       https://eur-lex.europa.eu/budget/data/DB/2027/en/SEC10.pdf
LOCATION:  p. 1, §1 "EXECUTIVE SUMMARY DB2027", third paragraph.
           p. 38, STAFF table "S 1 — EUROPEAN EXTERNAL ACTION SERVICE",
           "Grand Total" row.
QUOTE:     Narrative: "A key component of the proposed increase is a core
           Security and Defence package, which includes the creation of 15
           additional posts (10 Seconded National Experts and 5 contract
           agents)."
           Table: "Grand Total 1 750 1 750" [2027 / 2026, identical]
NAMES:     AGENCY ONLY
TENSE:     PRESENT
NOTES:     **This is not the same mechanism as S02-03, S08-02 or SEC07's
           498/497 (G.18) — it is a different, cleaner explanation, and the
           document itself supplies the reason.** The STAFF table (headed
           "Permanent posts / Temporary posts") only ever covers posts
           provided for in the establishment plan under the Staff
           Regulations. Seconded National Experts and contract agents are,
           by name, **not** establishment-plan posts — they are budgeted
           under Title 1.2 "Remuneration and other entitlements relating to
           external staff" (p. 9-10 of this document), a wholly separate
           chapter from the establishment plan. So the 15 posts the
           narrative announces **structurally cannot appear** in the table
           being compared against, not because they have not been recruited
           yet (the SEC02/SEC07 timing explanation) but because they belong
           to a different category of staff that this table was never going
           to show. **Flagged as a caution for reading the whole comparative
           series**: a flat establishment-plan total is not, by itself,
           evidence that a stated headcount change did not happen — it may
           simply mean the change was in a staff category the table does not
           cover. The AD-grade rows do redistribute internally (AD16 21→16,
           AD14 157→173, etc.) within a flat Grand Total, consistent with
           reclassification rather than net growth at the officials level.
```
