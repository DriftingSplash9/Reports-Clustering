**\# G.06 -- Research.2 Handover Note**

**\*\*Date:\*\* 2026-08-03**

**\*\*Strand:\*\* Finances / Monetary & Price Statistics**

**\*\*Status:\*\* Good stopping point after broad high-density and
cross-cutting extraction**

**\## Scope covered in this phase**

**- Core legal/methodological bases: ESA 2010 (Reg. 549/2013 + Annex B
transmission programme), HICP/HPI/OOH (Reg. 2016/792 + 2023/1470), ECB
statistical Guidelines/Regulations (esp. MFI BSI, CBD, non-bank
intermediaries, external statistics).**

**- High-density EU nodes: National accounts families (nama10, nasa_10,
nasq_10, GFS/EDP tables), Supply-Use & FIGARO, MFI balance-sheet
statistics & monetary aggregates, Consolidated Banking Data (CBD2),
Supervisory Banking Statistics (SUP -- SI/LSI), non-bank FC statistics
(IVF, ICB, PFBR, FVC), EBA Risk Indicators / Risk Dashboard.**

**- International layer: Eurostat--OECD Technical Arrangement (national
accounts), Inter-Agency Group on Economic and Financial Statistics (IAG)
primary-validator model, International Data Cooperation (IDC) → IDCS
dataset, ESS--ESCB MoU, UK--Eurostat Arrangement under TCA, broader
Eurostat bilateral relationships (IMF, World Bank, UN, etc.).**

**\## Key hard edges established**

**- Member States / NSIs / NCBs → Eurostat (ESA 2010 TP)**

**- MFIs → NCBs → ECB (BSI / monetary aggregates)**

**- EBA ITS (FINREP/COREP) → CBD2 and → Supervisory Banking Statistics
(SUP)**

**- National SUTs + main aggregates + trade/BOP → FIGARO**

**- Constituency-based primary validation (Eurostat / OECD / IMF) under
IAG + SDMX sharing**

and construction dependencies that are documented but not strict legal
transmission obligations.**
