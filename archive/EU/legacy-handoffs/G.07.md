    into expected ESS practice; later sectoral implementing acts

    Recurrent annual reports to EP/Council assess CoP implementation by
    Eurostat and the ESS as a whole; 2024/2025 reports explicitly follow
    up on (and note non-uptake of) recommended amendments to Regulation
    223/2009 (ESGAB batch).**

4.  **Annual Work Programme (SMP Annex IV) → Reg. 223/2009 Art. 17 +
    ESSC 2026 WP submitted to the European Statistical System Committee
    under Art. 17 of Reg. 223/2009; implements the statistics-specific
    objective of the Single Market Programme (WP-SMP batch).**

5.  **ESAC opinions → draft Annual Work Programmes Mandatory early
    consultation of ESAC (Decision 234/2008/EC) on draft AWPs; recent
    opinions on 2026 and 2027 programmes (WP-SMP-ESAC batch).**

6.  **GFS / ESA 2010 Transmission Programme → EDP notifications (Reg.
    479/2009) Government finance statistics transmitted under ESA Annex
    B must be consistent with the twice-yearly EDP deficit/debt
    notifications; EDP inventories of sources & methods support
    verification (FIN-GFS-EDP-MIP + deeper batch).**

7.  **EDP / GFS government debt → MIP scoreboard General government
    gross debt (% of GDP, 60 % threshold) is a headline MIP indicator
    taken directly from EDP/GFS data (FIN-GFS-EDP-MIP batch).**

8.  **MFI BSI (ECB/2021/2 + Guideline 2021/11) → Monetary aggregates

    source from which the euro-area monetary aggregates are derived

9.  **EBA ITS (FINREP/COREP) → CBD2 and SUP Harmonised supervisory
