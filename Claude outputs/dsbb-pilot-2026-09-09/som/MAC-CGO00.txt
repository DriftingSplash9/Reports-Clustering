I. Analytical Framework, Concepts, Definitions, and Classifications
 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The public accounting system in Macao is designed to serve both the legal and public system. It is not merely a record of government revenues and expenditures, but it also makes sure that all expenses of the government departments are legal and follow the necessary procedures. Macao SAR government passed a series of public accounting regulations to cope with the changing needs of the territory. 
 
 The Basic Law of Macao SAR and Decree-Law no.41/83/M specify that budgeted revenue should cover budgeted expenditure and that current revenue should at least cover current expenditure. In the Macao SAR Budget (OR) all cash inflows are recorded in the revenue side. All cash outflows in the OR are recorded on the expenditure side of the accounts. 
 
 Deficit/Surplus = Current Revenue + Capital Revenue – Current Expenditure – Capital Expenditure 
 
 Macao SAR General Account consists of two parts: 
 
 General integrated account of government; 
 Aggregated account of special organizations. 
 “General integrated account of Government” reflects annual budget execution and financial situation of all agencies that use cash basis accounting system, while “Aggregated account of special organizations” reflects annual budget execution and financial situation of the special organizations which use accrued basis accounting system. 
 
 Due to their special functions, the following autonomous agencies adopt the accrued basis accounting system, and therefore are called special organizations: 
 
 - Monetary Authority of Macao; 
 - Macao Postal Savings; 
 - Macao Post; 
 - Pension Fund; 
 - Automobile and Maritime Security Fund; 
 - Macao Foundation; 
 - Deposit Protection Fund; 
 - Social Security Fund. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization “General integrated account of government” includes the following components: 
 
 Table of integrated public revenue and expenditure; 1.1 Current revenue; 1.2 Capital revenue; 1.3 Total revenue; 1.4 Current expenditure; 1.5 Capital expenditure; 1.6 Total expenditure; 1.7 Deficit/Surplus; 
 Balance sheet; 
 Annotations. 
 The budgetary code is formed by 4 pairs of double digits. Each pair represents “Chapter”, “Group”, “Article” and “Number” accordingly. In order to show in detail, an additional fifth pair “Item” is required.

III. Accounting Conventions
 2.4.1 Valuation All Central government operations of Macao SAR are recorded in the domestic currency, Macao Pataca. Transactions in foreign currency are converted at the market exchange rate prevailing at the time of the transaction. 2.4.2 Recording basis The fiscal year in Macao SAR is the same as the calendar year. Annual revenue and expenditure should be recorded in the respective accounts of that year on a cash basis. The settlement of payment is determined by law every year. 
 
 In terms of Decree-Law No. 41/83/M and the Administrative Regulation no. 6/2006, Public Finance Ordinance (RAFP), the accounting system for the elaboration of OR and accounts of government agencies adopt cash basis, while some autonomous agencies must adopt the accrued basis accounting system because of their special functions (as mentioned in 2.3.1). 
 
 Government Finance Statistics (GFS) data adopted by the classification concepts of GFSM2014 are compiled on cash basis. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures Central government operations are recorded on a gross basis. 

IV. Nature of the Basic Data Sources
 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The Financial Services Bureau (DSF) can readily obtain monthly, quarterly and annual data of the government revenues and expenditures from the public computer accounting system. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness According to RAFP, all autonomous agencies prepare year end accounts regarding the previous fiscal year and submit them for approval by the supervisory authority before the end of the first quarter of the following year. All approved accounts have to be sent to DSF until the month of April of the following year to which they relate. The opinion of the supervisory entity shall focus on the management carried out and on the activities report, assessing the accuracy of the accounts and compliance with applicable standards. 

V. Compilation Practices
 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques In the elaboration of the General integrated account of government, the financial data of all agencies that use cash basis accounting system are integrated by means of eliminating inter-governmental transfers between Central Services and autonomous agencies that occur in the same fiscal year.
