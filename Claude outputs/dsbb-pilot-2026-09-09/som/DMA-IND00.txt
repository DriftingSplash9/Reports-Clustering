II. Scope of the data
 Scope of the data No index of industrial production has been produced. Production data and other data used to estimate production are published in the "Quarterly Economic Indicators" and the "Statistical Digest" by the Central Statistical Office (CSO), and in the "Quarterly Economic and Financial Review" published by the Eastern Caribbean Central Bank (ECCB). The data comprise the following categories (with unit of measure): 
 
 Construction and industrial production (Commercial) building plans approved (number) Imports of cement (number) Production of major industrial commodities (various) Electricity generated (kilowatt-hours) Water consumption(gallons ) Number of vehicles registered by type (number) Air traffic [aircraft movements] (number) Air traffic [airmail and freight services] (metric tons) Ships entering Dominica ports by type (number) Cargo loaded and landed at seaports (metric tons) Telephone traffic (number of subscribers and calls) Tourism Tourist arrivals by type of accommodation, country of residence, and purpose of visit (number) Average length of stay and total bed nights (day) Visitor arrivals by country of origin (number) Cruise ship calls and passenger arrivals (number) Tourism Expenditure (EC$)
