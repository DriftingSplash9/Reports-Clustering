 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Definition: The IIP of Malaysia is a statistical statement representing its balance sheet of the stock of external financial assets and liabilities. The financial items that comprise the position consist of claims and liabilities to non-residents, monetary gold, and SDRs. In relation to the balance sheet of the economy, the net international investment position combined with the stock of its non-financial assets comprises the net worth of the economy. Analytical framework: The analytical framework for the national presentation is in line with the recommendations of the 6th. Edition of the IMF's Balance of Payments and International Investment Position Manual (BPM6). The financial assets and liabilities are broken down into the opening position, net transactions, and non-transactions arising from price, exchange rate and other changes, and the closing position, respectively, of the economy. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The classification of IIP has two dimensions - presenting a distinction primarily between assets and liabilities; the difference between the two reflects the net position. Furthermore, its first sub-classification by function is fully consistent with financial account balance of payments. Assets are divided into direct investment, portfolio investment, financial derivatives, other investment and reserve assets; similarly for liabilities (with the exception for reserve assets). 2.2.1 Scope The classification of IIP has two dimensions - presenting a distinction primarily between assets and liabilities; the difference between the two reflects the net position. Furthermore, its first sub-classification by function is fully consistent with financial account balance of payments. Assets are divided into direct investment, portfolio investment, financial derivatives, other investment and reserve assets; similarly for liabilities (with the exception for reserve assets). Scope of the data Transactions coverage: Best Practices based on Standards and guidelines recommended in BPM6 on residency and enterprise are used in data compilation to ensure the external transactions of the economy are covered. Data on position (stocks), transactions and non-transactions reflecting other changes of potential respondents (including those who are residing in Labuan International Offshore Financial Centre (LIOFC)), all external assets and liabilities, Financial Institution, reporting entities, Custodians that have international financial transactions . Exceptions to coverage Geographic coverage: Malaysia (there are no geographical exclusions). Unrecorded activity There are no unrecorded activities. 2.4.1 Valuation Valuation methods: The market prices agreed upon by transactors are used for transactions. For stocks of assets and liabilities (i.e. at beginning and end of periods), the valuations are basically at market prices prevailing at the time to which the international investment position relates. Respondents are advised to provide the data on accrual basis. 2.4.2 Recording basis Transactions, as far as possible followed the accrual accounting basis. Time of recording: Basically, financial item transactions are recorded when the ownership takes place. The values for the positions are prevailed at the reference point in time. Conversion to unit of account: Data reported in foreign currencies are converted into local currencies at the average of exchange rates in the reference period. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures Not applicable. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The main source of data for the corporate sector is from IIP survey (joint collaboration between BNM and DOSM). Data of the monetary sector are obtained from BNM through its Integrated Statistical System (ISS) Reserve assets: Data for reserve assets are provided by BNM. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording IIP survey gathers information provided by companies. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness All data sources are basically on quarterly basis. 3.2.1 Source data assessment Updating of coverage is performed on a quarterly basis. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Direct investment: IIP Reporting is designed for collecting quarterly data required for compiling detailed statistics on direct investment. The movements in financial assets and liabilities due to other changes (market price, foreign exchange and others) are included. Portfolio investment: IIP Reporting is designed for collecting quarterly data required for compiling detailed statistics on portfolio investment. The movements in financial assets and liabilities due to other changes (market price, foreign exchange and others) are included. Financial Derivatives and Other investment: No adjustments are made to source data. Reserve assets: No adjustments are made to source data. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Seasonal adjustments: No seasonal adjustments to IIP data are presently attempted 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data Intermediate data is assessed in the quarterly IIP’s Technical Meeting with BNM. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs Assessment of discrepancies and other problems will be resolved in quarterly IIP’s Technical Meeting with BNM. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses Revision of quarterly IIP for previous year will be published together with first quarter of the current year. The public is informed of any changes in methodology after the changes have been introduced. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses The revision and final data of respective year are disseminated together with preliminary data of the first quarter of current year. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request The dissemination of requested data must comply with the provision in Statistics Act 1965 (Revised 1989). 5.3.2 Availability of documents and services catalogs Available in DOSM website https:/www.dosm.gov.my .
