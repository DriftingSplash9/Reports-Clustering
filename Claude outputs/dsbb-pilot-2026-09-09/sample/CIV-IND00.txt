 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The index covers production in activities 05 to 22 of the International Standard Industrial Classification (ISIC rev. 2). Scope of the data The industrial production index covers the production of mining and manufacturing industries, the production and distribution of electricity and water. None 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The industrial production index is prepared by the National Statistics Institute (INS). 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures The base period runs from the 4th quarter of 1984 to the 3rd quarter of 1985. The weights are the gross values added to factor costs in industrial activities in 1985. Production quantities are taken from surveys of a sample of 200 companies (covering 95% of total added value).
