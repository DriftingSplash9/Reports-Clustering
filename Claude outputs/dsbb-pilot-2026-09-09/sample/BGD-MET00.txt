 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Concepts and definitions of merchandise trade are consistent with the latest international guidelines. Codes used in the compilation is familiar with the latest harmonized coding system. Customs records are maintained by World ASYCUDA. Customs records are used in the Trade Balance under Balance of Payments. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Customs-based data Total exports and detailed breakdowns by commodity, classified according to the Harmonized System, and by destination of exports are released simultaneously. Total imports and detailed breakdowns by commodity, classified according to the Harmonized System, and are released simultaneously. Payments-based data Data are classified according to the Harmonized System. Trade data are also presented in terms of regions and countries/trading partners with commodity details. 2.2.1 Scope Customs-based data Total exports and detailed breakdowns by commodity, classified according to the Harmonized System, and by destination of exports are released simultaneously. Total imports and detailed breakdowns by commodity, classified according to the Harmonized System, and are released simultaneously. Payments-based data Data are classified according to the Harmonized System. Trade data are also presented in terms of regions and countries/trading partners with commodity details. Scope of the data Customs-based data Total exports and detailed breakdowns by commodity, classified according to the Harmonized System, and by destination of exports are released simultaneously. Data are on a monthly basis and are based on customs reports from the 6 national customs centers. Data are presented in both taka (crores) and US$ (millions). Merchandise exports, recorded on a f.o.b. basis, and categorized into Readymade Garments, Frozen Fish, leather & Leather products etc. Total imports and detailed breakdowns by commodity, classified according to the Harmonized System, and are released simultaneously. In principle, all imports are included whether dutiable or duty-free. Data are on a monthly basis and are based on total customs returns. Data are presented in crores of taka and millions of US$. Merchandise imports, recorded on a F.O.B basis, are categorized into Cotton Fabrics, Mineral fuel & Oil , Iron & Steel etc, i.e., categorized on commodities. Payments-based data are compiled on a monthly basis and are based on detailed reports of authorized exchange dealers. For imports, exchange records data are supplemented by data on imports under loans and grants provided by the Economic Relations Division of the Ministry of Finance. Data are presented in crores of taka and millions of US$. Exceptions to coverage Nil Unrecorded activity Nil 2.4.1 Valuation Merchandise imports and exports are compiled on f.o.b. basis. Only flow data are collected. Stock data are calculated based on a model. Data obtained from the Bangladesh Customs on a monthly basis are in Bangladeshi Taka (BDT). Bangladesh Bank converts the BDT values to USD values based on the monthly average exchange rate. 2.4.2 Recording basis Data are compiled on both customs and payments (cash) basis. Customs-based data Data are recorded when customs has control of the goods and are valued f.o.b. Data are presented in crores of taka (1 crore = 10 million) and USD in million. Payments-based data Exports and imports both are collected through authorized dealers and are recorded on f.o.b. basis. Data are presented in crores of taka (1 crore = 10 million) and USD in million. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures Nil. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Customs-based data on merchandise exports are compiled and disseminated by the Export Promotion Bureau (EPB). Data are on a monthly basis and are based on customs reports from the 6 national customs centers. Customs-based data on merchandise imports are compiled and disseminated by the National Board of Revenue (NBR). Data are on a monthly basis and are based on total customs returns. Data on total imports and exports on a payments basis are compiled and disseminated by the Bangladesh Bank. Data are compiled on a monthly basis and are based on detailed reports of authorized exchange dealers. For imports, exchange records data are supplemented by data on imports under loans and grants provided by the Economic Relations Division of the Ministry of Finance. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Source data are received within 2-3 weeks after the reference month. 3.2.1 Source data assessment Source data are compared with previous trends & cross-checked with other with relevant sources i.e., exports and imports are collected from two sources, namely custom records and banking records. In this case we check consistency between two sources of data. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Mainly two statistical techniques are used to compile trade data. The customs data are compiled and maintained by World ASYCUDA software and banking data are compiled by Oracle and maintained by Enterprise data warehouse (EDW). 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses Sometimes banking data are revised and disseminated without any delay. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses Not Applicable.
