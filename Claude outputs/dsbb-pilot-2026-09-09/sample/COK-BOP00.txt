 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The balance of payments data are an annual summary of the economic and financial transactions of the residents of the Cook Islands with the residents of other countries. These data are compiled in accordance with the Balance of Payments Manual, fifth edition, (BPM5) , published by the International Monetary Fund. At the same time, the balance of payments data are in transition to the BPM6 , which the CISO has begun to implement. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The balance of payments data covers only current account transactions. Consequently, exports and imports of goods and services are distinguished, with current account shown. Scope of the data The scope of the balance of payments data is currently limited to current account transactions (trade in goods and services, income and current transfers). Exceptions to coverage Among current account transactions, certain categories of re-exports (fish caught by foreign ships, imported into Cook Islands, and then re-exported, as well as aircraft parts imported and then re-exported to New Zealand for repairs) are excluded. As the scope of the balance of payments data is currently limited to current account transactions, capital account transactions are not covered. 2.4.1 Valuation Both exports and imports of goods and services are valued on f.o.b. basis in the balance of payments data. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs As said before, the balance of payments data cover current account transactions only. Merchandise goods are measured through customs as described in the Merchandise Trade entry. Other goods included in this component are the jet fuel and the numismatic. Jet fuel is brought into the country as import, and resold to a foreign-owned company (Air New Zealand). Consequently, this is considered as re-exports. Data on the value of numismatic coins is taken from government accounts. The export and import of services are measured in the following accounts: transportation, travel, communication, insurance, financial and government services.
