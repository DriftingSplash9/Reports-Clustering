 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Definition: The volume of external trade represents the totality of goods exchanged between the Republic of Moldova and other countries, which increase or decrease the material resources of the country, as a result of their inflow or outflow from the country. Relationship of national practices with international or regional guidelines: The external trade methodology is fully harmonized with UN recommendations. Any further changes in the UN recommendations are followed and taken into account. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Classification: Exported and imported goods starting with January 2023 are classified according to the Combined Nomenclature of Goods of the Republic of Moldova which is in line with Harmonized Commodity Description and Coding Systems (HS - 2022) and EU Combined Nomenclature (CN) The first six digits of the Nomenclature completely correspond to the Harmonized System (HS - 2022), the first six digits plus the seventh and the eighth digit correspond to Combined Nomenclature of the European Union , the ninth digit is the specification at the national level. Each good's position is uniquely identified in the customs declaration. The Classification of countries and territories developed by UN Statistical Division (ISO Standard 3166-88) used until 2005. The nomenclature of countries and territories used for external trade statistics of European Union and its relations with member-states and third countries is the Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2020/1470 of 12 October 2020. Scope of the data Transaction coverage: There are some goods, which can be subject to customs inspection but according to the methodology of external trade statistics are excluded from the volume of exports and imports of the Republic of Moldova: monetary gold; securities, banknotes and coins that are in current circulation; external goods in transit through the territory of the Republic of Moldova; goods leased for less than one year; goods imported and exported temporarily from the country, that will be later returned (value of goods displayed at the fairs, exhibitions, consignments, tests, commercial samples, transport means, containers and auxiliary equipment); goods imported to replace the other previously imported goods, exported goods that are returned to the country without reaching the final destination; luggage of passengers, goods for the embassies, diplomatic missions, and other organizations placed abroad (traffic of goods between the host country and its organizations from abroad), that is considered to be internal circulation of goods; transport means and equipment sent abroad to be repaired, that are later returned to the host country; exported (imported) goods to pay for the guarantee issued by the supplier; circulation of goods in the duty free zone (from the duty free zone) within the state borders Exceptions to coverage Geographic coverage: The data do not cover the territory of Transnistria region (the left side of the Nistru river and municipality Bender). Unrecorded activity External trade statistics does not include the volume of external trade of physical persons. 2.4.1 Valuation External trade statistics are presented in both value (lei, USD) and in quantitative expression (net weight tones, kg, or specified unit). Conversion to unit of account: The amounts denominated in foreign currencies are converted into Moldovan lei and U.S. dollars accordingly using the official exchange rates set up by the National Bank of Moldova on the date of the customs declaration's registration. Valuation: Exported or imported goods are reported at statistical value. Exported goods are valued FOB (free on board) (the value at which the goods were sold by the exporter including transport costs and other expenditures related to the transportation and loading of the goods on the transport means at the border of the Republic of Moldova). Imports are valued at CIF (the value at which the goods are bought by the importer, including the transport and insurance costs for transporting the goods to the border of the Republic of Moldova). 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Sources: External trade statistics are compiled on the basis of customs declarations filled in for all goods crossing the customs border of the Republic of Moldova. The source of express data for goods transported by cable or pipes is the statistical report presented by the importer or exporter based on the acceptance and delivery act. Customs Service collects the customs declarations that are verified, processed and sent to NBS, which compile and disseminate external trade statistics. Type of data collected: Exported or imported goods are recorded as a net weight (tons or kg), but there are certain goods, which in addition to the weight are recorded in a specific unit of measurement to the given type of goods (pieces, cubic meters, pairs, kwh, etc.). The data regarding the value of external trade is compiled and presented in Moldovan Lei and USD. Basis of country data: External trade with each country follows the principles of the "country of destination" for exports, and the "country of origin" for imports. The "country of destination" is considered to be the country where the goods will be consumed, and the " country of origin" the country where the goods were produced, or where they have been transformed for the last time. If at the moment of the delivery of goods the country of destination or the country of origin is unknown, then the exports or imports are reflected by the country where the goods were bought (sold). Country of sale (purchase) of goods is the country on which territory is placed the company or enterprise, which sells or buys the goods irrespective of its belonging to a certain country. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording The source data are consistent with the definitions, scope, classifications, time of recording required for the elaboration of qualitative external trade statistics. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness The timeliness of external trade data meets requirements for production of statistics according SDDS . 3.2.1 Source data assessment Source data are assessed for their accuracy on a routine basis. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Adjustments to improve coverage/classification/timing/valuation: The sources of express data for goods transported by cable or pipes (electric energy, natural gas) are the statistical reports presented by the importers and exporters companies. Processing system: The ASYCUDA World (an automated system of data entry, control of customs data) system is used by the National Customs Service and a national processing system is used by the NBS. Site of processing: National Customs Service collects and processes the export and import customs declarations. NBS completes checks and processes the monthly data from statistical point of view and compiling them with data on external trade by cable and pipes disseminates the information. Timing of recording transactions: Transactions are recorded at the time when goods enter or leave the economic territory of the country. Reconciliation: For reconciliation of data some mirror analyses have been undertaken. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Seasonal adjustment: Data are not seasonally adjusted. Commodity detail: Information is available by commodity grouping on 2, 4, 6 and 8 digit level of CN with desegregation at 9th National level, 2 digit level of SITC rev.4, 3 digit of BEC. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results The authenticity of the merchandise trade statistics (based on customs declaration) is verified at the following three levels : at the customs border, at the Customs' Service, at the NBS. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses The publications include notes on the revision of data. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request NBS makes available for users' additional statistical information beyond the data presented in the statistical publications, news releases, analytical reports placed on the official website, in the limits of available information, in line with the Law on Official Statistics. Request can be sent personally, by post, by e-mail moldstat@statistica.gov.md or via online web form – https://statistica.gov.md/en under: Products and services / Statistical data request: https://statistica.gov.md/en/information_request . Besides this, NBS can grant access to individual data for scientific research projects in accordance with the Regulation on access to individual data for scientific purposes, approved by NBS Order no. 51 of 31.12.2021. The Regulation and the Annexes to the Regulation can be accessed online - https://statistica.gov.md/en under: Products and services / Request for access to microdata: https://statistica.gov.md/en/request-for-access-to-microdata-205.html . 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points The contact information is given in the publications and on the website.
