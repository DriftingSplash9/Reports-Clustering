 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The methodology, concepts, definitions, and presentation of key central bank aggregates are based on the framework presented in the Monetary and Financial Statistics Manual and Compilation Guide (MFSMCG) 2016 . 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Components of the monetary base include (i) Notes Issued, measured by the Certificates of Indebtedness (CIs), (ii) Coins Issued, and (iii) deposits of domestic banks with the AMCM. Banknotes in Macao are issued by two commercial banks. When new banknotes are issued, the note-issuing banks are required to deliver an equivalent value of Hong Kong dollars to the AMCM in exchange for CIs, which are non-interest bearing instruments issued by the AMCM and act as a foreign exchange back-up for the note issue. Production and distribution of MOP coins are carried out by the AMCM, and extra issuance of coins correspondingly raises the balance of the Treasury account with the AMCM (i.e. AMCM liabilities) and MOP assets of the AMCM before the coins are circulated through the banking sector. 2.2.1 Scope Components of the monetary base include (i) Notes Issued, measured by the Certificates of Indebtedness (CIs), (ii) Coins Issued, and (iii) deposits of domestic banks with the AMCM. Scope of the data Data on monetary base are compiled on the basis of the balance sheet of the Monetary Authority of Macao (AMCM) and administrative information of the AMCM. The data are month-end balances expressed in the domestic currency, Pataca (MOP). 2.4.1 Valuation The value of CIs for the reference month is in fact related to the average value of notes issued in the previous month in line with the actual accounting practice. Notes issued include MOP notes kept in bank vaults as reserves. Coins Issued refer to the outstanding amount of coins in circulation at the end of each month, including MOP coins kept in bank vaults as reserves. Deposits are recorded at their nominal values. 2.4.2 Recording basis Please refer to 2.4.1 for recording basis of CIs and Coins Issued. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures Grossing and netting procedures are broadly consistent with the MFSMCG (2016). Data are collected and compiled on a gross basis. Assets and liabilities are presented on a gross basis in the AMCM balance sheet. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Data for monetary base are collected without any adjustment. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Source data for the CIs, Coins Issued and deposits of the banks with the AMCM are obtained from administrative records of the AMCM. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness The source data (preliminary) are available within 1 month after the end of the reference month. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques No adjustment is made to the source data. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Data are neither seasonally adjusted nor transformed. High-value and suspicious changes are usually confirmed with the data providers. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request The AMCM routinely makes central bank aggregates available. The AMCM also makes the statistics available upon request, depending on the level of detail and type of requested data.
