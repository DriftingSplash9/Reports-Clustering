 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The international reserves, reported in accordance with the SDDS, measures the total of the China’s official holdings of foreign currency reserves, IMF reserve position, Special Drawing Rights (SDRs), gold and other reserve assets. The concept and definitions are consistent with the IMF’s Balance of Payments and International Investment Position Manual (Sixth edition) and International Reserves and Foreign Currency Liquidity: Guidelines for a Data Template. 2.2.1 Scope The international reserves, reported in accordance with the SDDS, measures the total of the China’s official holdings of foreign currency reserves, IMF reserve position, Special Drawing Rights (SDRs), gold and other reserve assets. The concept and definitions are consistent with the IMF’s Balance of Payments and International Investment Position Manual (Sixth edition) and International Reserves and Foreign Currency Liquidity: Guidelines for a Data Template. Scope of the data International reserves comprises external assets which are readily available to and controlled by the monetary authorities of China, in which foreign currency reserves are managed by SAFE on behalf of PBC. 2.4.1 Valuation Valuation: Reserve assets are based on market valuation at prevailing market prices as of the end of reporting month. Conversion rates: Reserve assets are converted into US dollars at prevailing exchange rates as of the end of reporting month. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Data on foreign currency reserves, IMF reserve position, Special Drawing Rights (SDRs), gold and other reserve assets are compiled by SAFE and submitted to PBC. 3.2.1 Source data assessment All items are subject to internal review for accuracy and internal consistency.
