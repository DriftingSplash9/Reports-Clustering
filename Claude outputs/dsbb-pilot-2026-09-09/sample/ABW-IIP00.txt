 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The overall conceptual framework for the IIP of Aruba is in broad conformity with the guidelines in BPM5 . The transition to conform with the guidelines in BPM6 is foreseen in 2017. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The classification of asset and liability positions in the IIP is in accordance with the list of standard components in BPM5 , but is not always available in the same detail. For example, direct investment is not distinguished by equity and other capital. Memorandum items are not covered at the moment. The sectorization in the IIP is in accordance with BPM5 . For example, government-controlled enterprises that are public enterprises are excluded from general government and are included as public enterprises in the appropriate nonfinancial or financial corporations sector; examples are the Water and Electricity Company (WEB) and the telecommunications enterprise SETAR. 2.2.1 Scope The classification of asset and liability positions in the IIP is in accordance with the list of standard components in BPM5 , but is not always available in the same detail. For example, direct investment is not distinguished by equity and other capital. Memorandum items are not covered at the moment. The sectorization in the IIP is in accordance with BPM5 . For example, government-controlled enterprises that are public enterprises are excluded from general government and are included as public enterprises in the appropriate nonfinancial or financial corporations sector; examples are the Water and Electricity Company (WEB) and the telecommunications enterprise SETAR. Scope of the data The IIP covers all asset and liability positions between residents and nonresidents of Aruba. The economic territory of Aruba coincides with the island of Aruba and the neighboring continental shelf. The residency of institutional units is determined on the basis of the criterion of center of economic interest in accordance with the guidelines in BPM5 . For example, unincorporated branches of nonresident construction enterprises active in Aruba are considered residents. Bonded warehouses and factories established in the Free Zone are considered residents, too. The “Arubahuis”, representative office of the government of Aruba in The Hague, The Netherlands is considered resident. So is the Representative Office in Washington D.C. The Dutch army base in Aruba and its staff are considered nonresidentsThe IIP of Aruba is published in Aruban florin. Exceptions to coverage In deviation from the guidelines in BPM5, financial offshore entities legally established in Aruba are not considered residents. Trade credit is only partly covered in the IIP. 2.4.1 Valuation Direct investment positions are mainly valued at book value. Portfolio positions are valued at market value. Financial derivative positions - if they occur - would be valued at market price. Other investment is mainly at nominal value. 2.4.2 Recording basis Private loans and government loans do not include accrued interest. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures In accordance with the guidelines in BPM5 , asset and liability positions are recorded on a gross basis. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Data sources include statements of the other depository corporations and the central bank, reports from the central government and surveys of the other sectors. The latter include surveys specifically designed to collect information for the CDIS and CPIS reports. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Source data are consistent with the definitions and concepts of BPM5 . 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Source data are relatively timely. As regards the reports on foreign bank accounts (FBA) and foreign intercompany accounts (FIA) by enterprises, a lot of effort has to be made by CBA staff to receive the reports on a timely basis. 3.2.1 Source data assessment A plausibility check is performed on the reported data. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures When reports on positions are not available on time, positions are temporarily estimated on the basis of transactions from the balance of payments or information in licenses. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request The CBA provides non-confidential data on request.
