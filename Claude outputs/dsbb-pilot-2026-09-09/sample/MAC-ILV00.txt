 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The definition of international reserves follows the recommendations of the Balance of Payments and International Investment Position Manual, sixth edition (BPM6). 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The residency criterion used to determine AMCM foreign assets is in broad conformity with the guidelines of the BPM6. Assets vis-a-vis the Mainland of China, Hong Kong SAR and Chinese Taiwan are treated as external positions. As mentioned in 2.2.1.1, Macao SAR does not possess any amount of monetary gold, SDRs and unconditional drawing rights with the IMF. The main components of the disseminated data of international reserves are primarily in the form of (1) other claims on banks abroad; (2) financial investment abroad, and (3) other foreign exchange reserves. 2.2.1 Scope The residency criterion used to determine AMCM foreign assets is in broad conformity with the guidelines of the BPM6. Assets vis-a-vis the Mainland of China, Hong Kong SAR and Chinese Taiwan are treated as external positions. As mentioned in 2.2.1.1, Macao SAR does not possess any amount of monetary gold, SDRs and unconditional drawing rights with the IMF. The main components of the disseminated data of international reserves are primarily in the form of (1) other claims on banks abroad; (2) financial investment abroad, and (3) other foreign exchange reserves. Scope of the data International reserves are managed by the Monetary Authority of Macao (AMCM), and appear in the AMCM balance sheet as foreign exchange reserves under foreign assets. Macao SAR does not possess any amount of monetary gold, SDRs and unconditional drawing rights with the IMF, and hence foreign exchange reserves are identical to international reserves. Exceptions to coverage A Fiscal Reserve Act came into force on 1 January 2012 stipulating that fiscal resources formerly included in international reserves should be managed under separate accounts. Due to the official transfer of relevant assets, the international reserves fell dramatically in February 2012. 2.4.1 Valuation Financial assets are valued at market prices if they can be traded in the market, or market-price equivalents if they cannot be traded in the market. Deposits and loans are recorded at their book values. End-of-period midpoint market exchange rates (average of selling and buying rates) are used to convert data on foreign currency-denominated assets into domestic currency equivalents. 2.4.2 Recording basis In principle, all data are recorded on an accrual basis. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures Components of international reserves are presented on a gross basis and are not presented net of liabilities. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The AMCM, responsible for reserves management, maintains the accounts and compiles the data. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Source data are consistent with the definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording of the BPM6. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques No adjustment is made to the source data. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Data are not seasonally adjusted. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs High-value and suspicious changes are inspected. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request The AMCM makes the statistics available upon request, depending on the level of detail and type of requested data.
