 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The balance of payments of Andorra is a statistical statement summarizing the economic transactions of the residents of Andorra with the rest of the world during a given period of time. Balance of payments data are compiled in accordance with the recommendations of the sixth edition of the IMF Balance of Payments Manual (BPM6) as of 2019. The concept and definitions used are broadly in line with those used in BPM6. Balance of payments statistics are compiled in a standard BPM6 presentation and disseminated to the public in a national presentation, which is derived from the standard presentation. The data are published annually, in thousands of euros, on the Andorran Department of Statistics website. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The classification follows the BPM6. The data include the following categories: Current account includes credits and debits of goods, services, income and current transfers. The capital account includes capital transfers and acquisition/disposal of non-produced non-financial assets. The financial account includes net direct investment, portfolio investment, other investment and changes in reserve assets. A separate line item is net errors and omissions. The sectorization follows BPM6 methodology. 2.2.1 Scope The classification follows the BPM6. The data include the following categories: Current account includes credits and debits of goods, services, income and current transfers. The capital account includes capital transfers and acquisition/disposal of non-produced non-financial assets. The financial account includes net direct investment, portfolio investment, other investment and changes in reserve assets. A separate line item is net errors and omissions. The sectorization follows BPM6 methodology. Scope of the data The scope of balance of payments statistics is broadly consistent with accepted international standards, covering all transactions between residents and nonresidents. The data include: Current account: export and import of goods and services, receipts (receipts and payments) and current transfers (receipts and payments). Capital account: capital transfers, acquisition/disposal of non-produced and non-financial assets. Financial account: net direct investments, portfolio investments, other investments and the change in reserve assets. Exceptions to coverage Andorra's balance of payments covers most of the transactions between residents and non-residents. 2.4.1 Valuation Valuation is based on market prices prevailing at the time of the transaction. Both exports and imports of goods are valued on f.o.b. basis. Most international transactions are carried out in euros, the currency used in Andorra, so the euro is the most relevant currency for evaluating the data. 2.4.2 Recording basis Most of the items are largely based on surveys of the different institutional sectors involved in the BOP, using the timing of payments as the basis. Goods are recorded on a customs clearance basis. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures The grossing-up/accounting procedures are broadly consistent with BPM6. Current account transactions are recorded on a gross basis, while financial and capital account transactions are recorded on a net basis. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs A wide range of sources are used to compile the balance of payments. These are mainly merchandise trade statistics, bank reports, monetary statistics, banking statistics and various surveys. Current account General merchandise data are obtained from foreign trade statistics prepared by the Statistical Office of Andorra. The main source of data is the Single Administrative Document (SAD). Data on imports and exports are compiled according to the general trade system. Both exports and imports of goods are valued on the f.o.b. basis. C.I.F to F.O.B. adjustments are also made. The main source of data for services, income and current transfers are different surveys of businesses, households, tourism, the different institutional sectors involved in the BOP, and other administrative records. Capital and financial account The main source of data corresponds to the survey conducted specifically for the Andorran financial sector. Mirror surveys are also used for some data, as well as administrative records. Direct investment in Andorra's balance of payments corresponds to that of the BPM6. The main source of information corresponds to the administrative records of the Ministry of Economy. Portfolio investment covers investments in equity and debt securities not covered by direct investment. The main source of data is the report of the banking entities. For the public sector, in addition to the banks' report, the data sources are other administrative records. Data on Other investments are obtained from various sources. Banking sector statistics are derived from data collected from banks. Transactions in assets and liabilities of other sectors mainly reflect disbursements and repayments between residents and nonresidents that do not have a direct investment relationship. Some data are obtained through mirror statistics. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Source data, scope, classifications, valuation and time of recording are in accordance with the BPM6 manual. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness The data source is currently annual. 3.2.1 Source data assessment Source data are routinely evaluated. Results of surveys conducted are monitored and made available to guide statistical processes. Large transactions are monitored to ensure that they are correctly recorded. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Goods: The statistics department makes all necessary adjustments to the export and import data collected by the Customs Agency. The adjustments made to the statistical data are as follows: adjustment of import classification (insurance and freight components are deducted from imports of goods at c.i.f. value to arrive at f.o.b. value); exclusion of goods for processing without change of ownership. Services: Data concerning the different services are collected through the specific business survey, surveys conducted on households (HBS, LCS), the specific financial sector survey and available administrative records. For tourism and travel services, data are collected from tourism surveys. Data on insurance and pension services are mostly collected through a direct survey. Data on financial services are collected through the financial services specific survey. Items on public goods and services are based on administrative records collected from the responsible ministry at the state level. Primary income: Compensation of employees (following the criterion of one year of residence) covers remuneration received from Andorran residents employed in international organizations and foreign embassies. Remuneration of seasonal/border workers is included in compensation of employees. Data on direct investment income (interest) are obtained as results from the business survey and administrative records. Secondary income: Data on workers' remittances are estimated on the basis of remittances received and sent abroad, collected through bank surveys and household surveys. Capital Account: Data for these items are collected from donors. Financial account: Direct investment: data are collected through a direct survey. The survey is conducted in accordance with the BPM6 and the OECD benchmark definition of the fourth edition. The main valuation method for listed equity is market value, while for unlisted equity the main valuation method is equity at book value. Portfolio investment: This is collected through the specific survey of financial institutions. Other investments: most of it is collected from the statistics of financial institutions. Other data sources are mirror surveys. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures In case of data needs, the statistics department performs data imputation based on different statistical techniques. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results The source data used to compile the BOP are reliable and accurate to the extent possible. The population used as the basis for a sample, sample creation, response rates, coverage, and accuracy of the reported data are monitored and updated on a regular basis. Therefore, the data sources, as well as the data itself, are considered complete and reliable. Data from financial institutions are validated through automated data validation procedures. Data entry procedures include checks to avoid miscoding and misreporting. Queries are made to reporters on unusual transactions and large values. Data sources are checked against available indicators. Intermediate results are validated using other sources whenever possible. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data Potential discrepancies in intermediate data are assessed on a regular basis and the corresponding adjustments are made if necessary. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses The statistics department disseminates working papers and analyses on relevant topics in order to stimulate debate and contribute to better knowledge. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request Yes, ad-hoc request are also supplied, sending a request through e-mail at estadistica@govern.ad
