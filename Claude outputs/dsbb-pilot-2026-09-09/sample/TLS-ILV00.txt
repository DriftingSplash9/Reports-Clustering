 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The Economic and Statistics Division of the Central Bank of Timor-Leste (CBTL) compiles Timor-Leste’ gross official reserves based on the definition given in the IMF’s B alance of Payments Manual, Sixth Edition (BPM6). Accordingly, international reserves comprise external assets that are readily available to and controlled by the monetary authorities for the direct financing of balance of payments needs. In view of this definition, assets of Petroleum Fund are not included in the official reserves. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization International reserves data are published as a total with breakdown into components (see 2.2.1.1.). 2.2.1 Scope International reserves data are published as a total with breakdown into components (see 2.2.1.1.). Scope of the data The Timor-Leste’s reserve assets consist of SDR holdings, reserve position in the Fund, and foreign exchange assets. All external claims of the CBTL in the form of holdings of foreign securities, cash in vault, currency and deposits are considered reserve assets. Currently there is no monetary gold. Exceptions to coverage None. Unrecorded activity None. 2.4.1 Valuation Reserve assets (cash, deposits, and foreign securities) are recorded in US dollar, Timor-Leste’s official currency, at the dollar rate prevailing at the end of the period. 2.4.2 Recording basis Reserve assets are recorded on an accrual basis. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures Reserve assets are recorded on a gross basis. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The source data for compiling gross official reserves come from the CBTL balance sheet, which is prepared by the Accounting Division of the CBTL. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Data provided by accounting division of the BCTL are in line with the concepts and definition of official reserves. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Source data are provided timely to allow timely dissemination.
