 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The definition of gross reserve assets follows the methodology of the IMF's Balance of Payments and International Investment Position Manual, sixth edition (BPM6) and International Reserves and Foreign Currency Liquidity: Guidelines for a Data Template (2013). In accordance with the BPM6 official reserve assets that coincide with gross official reserves are external assets readily available to and controlled by the NBG for direct financing of balance of payments imbalances, for indirectly regulating the magnitude of such imbalances through intervention in the foreign exchange market to support the currency exchange rate, or for other purposes. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The official reserve assets are classified as follows: Monetary gold SDRs IMF reserve position Foreign currency reserves (a) securities (b) total currency and deposits Other foreign reserves 2.2.1 Scope The official reserve assets are classified as follows: Monetary gold SDRs IMF reserve position Foreign currency reserves (a) securities (b) total currency and deposits Other foreign reserves Scope of the data The instrument breakdown of gross official reserve assets includes: foreign currency reserves, reserve position in the IMF, SDR holdings, and other reserve assets. Foreign assets in nonconvertable currencies, as well as all other assets that do not meet reserve assets concepts, are excluded. Exceptions to coverage Geographical coverage: Data do not cover the territories of Abkhazian Autonomous Republic and Tskhinvali Region (former South Ossetian Autonomous Region), a part of Georgian territory not controlled by the central authorities. 2.4.1 Valuation Data on gross official reserve assets and international reserves are compiled and published in mln of U.S. dollars. Data are converted into US dollars equivalents on the basis of official exchange rates announced by the NBG daily. Assets denominated in other currencies are converted into US dollars according to cross-rates based on Lari exchange rates published daily by the NBG. Foreign securities are revalued daily at the end of the reporting period at the market price for each type of instruments. Special Drawing Rights are revalued daily according to the quotation informed by the International Monetary Fund. 2.4.2 Recording basis Data are recorded on accrual basis. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures Reserves data are gross. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Official reserve assets and the reserves template are compiled on the basis of the balance sheet and accounting records of the NBG. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Source data meet the methodological standards and guidelines for compilation International Reserves and Foreign Currency Liquidity Statistics. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness NBG accounting data are received up to 5 days after the reference month.
