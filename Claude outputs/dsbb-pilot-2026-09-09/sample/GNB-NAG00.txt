 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The reference for the national accounts is the former United Nations standard, "System of National Accounts" 1968 (SNA68). GDP is estimated annually as the sum of value added of economic activities (production approach) and as the sum of final uses (expenditure approach). The two approaches are used successively. The income approach is not used. Estimates are compiled at current prices and at prices of base year 1986. The production of enterprises is valued at production cost, in accordance with the chart of accounts approved by the Government of Guinea-Bissau. Imports are valued c.i.f. and exports f.o.b. Government accounts are derived from public accounts data using a specific classification approved by the Government of Guinea-Bissau. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The classification of economic activities used in Guinea-Bissau is an adaptation of the ISIC Rev. 2. Scope of the data The accounts are produced for the entire national territory covering all resident economic agents. Transactions by Guinea-Bissau embassies abroad are included in the production accounts of public administrations. The production and operating accounts are compiled by industry. The national accounts cover all the economic units resident in Guinea-Bissau. Unrecorded activity Values for unrecorded activity are posted to the unstructured sector accounts (non-modern sector). Smuggling is estimated and results in adjustments to foreign trade while prohibited activities, such as drug trafficking, are not included. 2.4.2 Recording basis Most of the accounts are on a cash basis. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Production Approach Primary sector: The data are culled from the different surveys conducted by INEC in the areas covered: agriculture, fishing, forestry, etc. Some of INEC's estimates are based on the demand for products and the source is the Institute's 1986 consumption budget survey. In others, the estimates are based on specific surveys carried out by different international cooperation organizations. The prices used to value production are average CPI prices less commercial margins and transportation. Secondary sector: In Guinea-Bissau, the sectors concerned are mainly agro food activities, manufacture of beverages, wearing apparel, etc. Production is calculated on the basis of data from the surveys of these sectors and administrative information supplied by the oversight ministries. Tertiary sector: Commercial production is determined by the sum of commercial margins, while the production of hotels and restaurants is estimated on the basis of information from a 1986 survey of establishments (sales of beverages, meals, and stays). This approach is used because the response rate by firms to the various annual questionnaires is very low. The list of surveyed companies is not available. Expenditure Approach: Import-export: the balance of payments compiled by BCEAO is the main data source. Gross fixed capital formation is estimated on the basis of imports of capital goods and the assessment of capital expenditure reflected in the public investment program (Ministry of Planning). Government final consumption is calculated on the basis of the state budget. Household final consumption is obtained as a balance. Changes in stocks are estimated on the basis of changes in the export values of large industrial and commercial enterprises. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques As no information is available, no adjustments are made to the source data.
