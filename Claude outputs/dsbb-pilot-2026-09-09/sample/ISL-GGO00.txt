 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Analytical Framework: General government transactions data are organized for analytical purposes using the system of definitions and classifications close to that presented in the Government Finance Statistics Manual, IMF, 2014 ; hereinafter referred to as GFSM 2014 . The GFSM 2014 is consistent with the 2010 edition of the European System of National Accounts ( ESA 2010 ). Relationship with international guidelines: The Icelandic data definitions are in line with those outlined in GFSM 2014 . During a benchmark revision on national accounts, december 2020, the scope of the general government has been revised: https://statice.is/publications/news-archive/public-finance/general-government-finances-benchmark-revision/ 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Classification system: Revenue consists of tax and non-tax revenues classified according to the GFSM 2014 ; Expenditure by function is classified according to the newest SNA classification of the functions of government ( COFOG ). Expenditure by economic type is classified according to the GFSM 2014 . Scope of the data Institutional coverage of general government in reported data: Data cover the institutional sub-sectors of central government, local government and the social security funds. All resident government units are included and all resident NPIs that are controlled and mainly financed by government. Collected data are of complete coverage. 2.4.1 Valuation Valuation of foreign currency transactions: Regarding foreign debt the State Accounting Office uses the end of year foreign currency rates to convert debts to Icelandic krónur. 2.4.2 Recording basis All transactions are recorded on accrual basis. The fiscal year is the same as the calendar year. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures Adjustments for grossing and netting of transactions: Grossing/netting procedures are consistent with international accepted standards and good practices, i.e. revenue and expenditure are on a gross basis and financing on a net basis where required. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Data sources: The data sources for the central government units and the social security funds are detailed accounting records based on their accounts, which are provided by the Financial Management Authority. Until final data on the central government are published preliminary data are based on reports from the Financial Management Authority (FMA) and the Ministry of Finance (MOF). SI is responsible for compiling data for the local governments and cooperates with the Association of the Local Authorities of Iceland (ALA) in that task. Final figures are therefore based on the actual accounts of the local governments, but preliminary data for the local governments are based on a sample survey from the 17 largest munipalities in Iceland, which in total comprise more than 85 percent of the local government revenue and expenditure. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording The accounting systems used by the budgetary central government, the social security funds and the local governments allow for sufficiently detail for the production of required government finance statistics. These systems are mostly on accrual basis Preliminary central government quarterly data, which are provided to SI by FMA on a modified cash basis, need to be adjusted to an accural basis by SI. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness The final annual source data for central government operations on an accural basis are provided to SI by the FMA in August (t+8) the following year, but source data on cash basis for the prelimiary data are provided to the same institute in February (t+2) the following year. A sample set of data for local government are compiled by SI in February each year and data covering all the local governments are provided to SI by municipalities and ALA in August the following year (t+8). 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Consolidation: The use of other statistical procedures is rarely necessary. However, SI makes adjustments to convert the preliminary modified cash data on central government to accrual data. These adjustments are based on differences between the previous years' modified cash data and final accrual data. Preliminary annual data for local governments are obtained by means of a sample survey that covers more than 80 percent of local governments' revenue and expenditure. The results of the survey are then adjusted for full coverage. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses A description of revisions is provided. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request The main aggregates from these publications have been extended by SI and are available on its website ( http://www.statice.is ). More disaggregated tables can be obtained from SI on request.
