 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Definition: Gross external debt of the Czech Republic is the outstanding amount of those actual, current, and not contingent, liabilities that require payments of principal and/or interest by the debtor at some points in the future and that are owed by non-residents of the Czech Republic. The contents, definitions and procedures used in compiling external debt data are based on standards specified in the sixth edition of the IMF´s "Balance of Payments Manual" and new edition of the External Debt Statistics: Guide for compilers and users". The external debt data are consistent with those published in the international investment position of the Czech Republic. The CNB compiles and publishes also the Net Debt Indicator, measuring gross debt to assets held in debt instruments. External debt (external debt of the Czech Republic) is the sum of liabilities with fixed maturity from the investment position vis-à-vis abroad. Net external debt is defined as the residents 'debt liabilities (gross external debt) minus the residents' debt assets, ie the net position of residents vis-à-vis non-residents of the Czech Republic. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Classification: Standard components according to the BPM6 are used to classify external debt liabilities. Data are published in structure including sector breakdown:Original maturity (short term, long term), instrument (currency and deposits, debt securities, loans, trade credits and advances, and other liabilities) and sector of debtor (monetary authorities, commercial banks, general government and other sectors). Deviations from the international guidelines for sector classification are not noted External debt liabilities are attributed to the sector of the immediate borrower External debt liabilities are classified under the sector category of the debtor and not of any guarantor The short-term/long-term classification is on an original maturity basis Instruments included under "other debt liabilities" are transitory items and residual liabilities not included elsewhere Intercompany lending between entities in a direct investment relationship are not separately identified in the total external debt position and they are allocated into relevant sector and instrument. Arrears are not separately identified. Scope of the data Resident institutional units are defined in accordance to the BPM6 concepts of predominant economic interest. Exceptions to coverage Monetary and financial institutions and central government are fully covered. Nonfinancial corporations and Local governments are covered on base of predominant (large) institutions or corporations. Households and non-profit institutions serving households are not fully covered. 2.4.1 Valuation Stocks are valued at market prices if available (similarly to IIP data): o Direct investment-intercompany lending data are recorded at nominal value o Portfolio investments-bonds and notes are valued at market prices o Other investments-loans, trade credits, currency and deposits, and other investments are recorded at nominal value. Conversion to unit of account: The official end of period central bank exchange rate is used to convert assets and liabilities denominated in foreign currencies into CZK as unit of account and conversely to EUR and USD (for publication only). 2.4.2 Recording basis External debt Recording basis: Recording of only those positions which exist between residents and nonresidents. Outstanding liabilities arising from periodic interest costs that have accrued and are not yet payable are partially included in the gross external debt position. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures The assets and liabilities of one particulat item of the IIP are recorded separately, on a "gross" basis, which from the point of ED means that particulat liabilities are not reduced by corresponding assets. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The main data sources correspond to IIP data sources, so they are as follows: a) Statistical statements and surveys: Monthly statement of the foreign exchange and CZK positions of the CNB and commercial banks Source: The ARAD time series database. Copyright(c) Czech National Bank Monthly specification of government loans Monthly and quarterly statement of foreign assets and liabilities of non-bank entities Quarterly statement of the purchase, sale and ownership of domestic securities by non-residents Statistical survey in the area of direct investment (annual) b) Administrative data sources: Supplementary information from commercial banks and various units of the CNB Information (both regular and on request) from the Czech Statistical Office, the Ministry of Finance of the Czech Republic, the Prague Stock Exchange, the Prague Central Securities Depository, the Cadastral Office and other entities c) Information from the media d) The CNB’s own calculations The authorisation to demand information needed to compile ED (and, where relevant, the balance of payments and the international investment position) stems from the provisions of Act No. 6/1993 Coll., on the Czech National Bank, as amended, and Act No. 15/1998 Coll., on Supervision in the Capital Market Area, as amended. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Generally there are no statistical adjustments in coverage, classification, timing, and valuation of data in government and banking sectors. Some statistical adjustments are made in enterprise surveys data. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Debt instruments issued by residents abroad, but in hands of residents are partially excluded. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request There is availability of supplementary information: half-yearly external debt service schedule with separate data for principal and interest , and geographic breakdown of stock data on direct investment abroad and in the Czech Republic (intercompany lending) and portfolio investment.
