 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The depository corporations survey consolidates the accounts of the Monetary Authority of Macao (AMCM) as well as data provided by all commercial banks and one government-owned postal savings office. The methodology, concepts, definitions, and presentation of key aggregates in the depository corporations survey are based on the framework presented in the Monetary and Financial Statistics Manual and Compilation Guide (MFSMCG) 2016 . The data represent month-end balances expressed in the domestic currency, Pataca (MOP). Macao does not have a central bank. The AMCM is a public entity that acts as a central-banking institution, except for the note-issuing function, which is undertaken by two commercial banks acting as agents of the issuing authority, the Macao SAR Government. Production and distribution of MOP coins are undertaken by the AMCM. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The financial assets are classified into the following categories: Currency Deposit by currency, type, sector and maturity Loan by currency, type, industry, sector and maturity Securities by currency, type, sector and maturity The residency criterion used to differentiate external assets and liabilities from domestic assets and liabilities is in broad conformity with the guidelines of the Balance of Payments and International Investment Position Manual, sixth edition (BPM6). Residential institutional units are grouped into three sectors: the public sector, the private sector (including nonfinancial public enterprises) and commercial banks (including one postal savings bank). In published data, separate identity for these three sectors is available. Assets and liabilities vis-a-vis the Mainland of China, Hong Kong SAR and Chinese Taiwan are treated as external positions. The main aggregates compiled and disseminated in the depository corporations survey are: Domestic credit, consisting of net claims on government (lending to government as loans, securities and other receivables minus liabilities to government as deposits, loans and other payables) and claims on other resident sectors. Net foreign assets, consisting of foreign assets less foreign liabilities of the AMCM and commercial banks. Monetary aggregates: (a) M1, comprising currency in circulation and demand (transferable) deposits of resident nonbank customers (including nonfinancial public enterprises); (b) M2, comprising M1 and quasi-monetary liabilities, which include savings, notice, time and other deposits of resident nonbank customers plus the issuance of negotiable certificates of deposit (held by residents). 2.2.1 Scope The financial assets are classified into the following categories: Currency Deposit by currency, type, sector and maturity Loan by currency, type, industry, sector and maturity Securities by currency, type, sector and maturity The residency criterion used to differentiate external assets and liabilities from domestic assets and liabilities is in broad conformity with the guidelines of the Balance of Payments and International Investment Position Manual, sixth edition (BPM6). Scope of the data The depository corporations survey covers the AMCM, all commercial banks, and one government-owned postal savings bank operating in the Macao SAR. It is compiled on the basis of month-end balance sheet data of these institutions and is expressed in the domestic currency, Pataca (MOP). 2.4.1 Valuation Financial assets and liabilities are valued at market prices if they can be traded in the market, or market-price equivalents if they cannot be traded in the market. Deposits and loans are recorded at their nominal values. End-of-period midpoint market exchange rates (average of selling and buying rates) prevailing on the balance sheet date are used to convert data on foreign currency-denominated assets and liabilities into domestic currency equivalents. Derivatives and contingencies are recorded off-balance-sheet. 2.4.2 Recording basis In principle, all data are recorded on an accrual basis. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures Data are collected on a gross basis. Assets and liabilities are consolidated data of the accounts of the AMCM and commercial banks (including one postal savings bank). Most financial flows and outstanding claims and liabilities between domestic depository corporations are cancelled out, while preserving the presentation of data on all stocks and flows that are claims on and liabilities to all other sectors. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The compilation of relevant statistics requires the accounting statements of the AMCM and all banks operating in Macao SAR. Data on the banks are collected monthly by the AMCM based on an electronic reporting system in standardized formats . 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Source data provide broad basis for general classification needed for financial-sector statistics. The source data are prominently subdivided into domestic and external positions. The domestic data are further disaggregated into the public sector, the private sector (including nonfinancial public enterprises) and commercial banks (including one postal savings bank), which are broadly in conformity with the MFSMCG (2016). In addition, banks are required to disaggregate their positions by type of financial instruments and to report them in nine types of currencies, namely the MOP, Hong Kong Dollar, Chinese Renminbi, US Dollar, Euro, Japanese Yen, British Pound, Swiss Franc and other currencies. Currencies other than the MOP should be converted into pataca-equivalent values. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness The source data from banks and the postal savings bank are available within 15 days after the end of the reference month. 3.2.1 Source data assessment High-value and suspicious changes will be confirmed with the reporting institution. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Minor adjustments are made to the source data to conform with valuation rules. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Data are not seasonally adjusted. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results A validation worksheet has been designed in the report form of commercial banks for checking the consistency and rationality of figures in different worksheets of the report form. High-value and suspicious changes are usually confirmed with the reporting institution. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs Statistical discrepancies and other potential problems in statistical outputs are investigated. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request The AMCM routinely makes money and credit aggregates statistics available. The AMCM also makes the statistics available upon request, depending on the level of detail and type of requested data.
