 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The QIIP measures the change in the volume of production (value added) for all producer units (establishments) in scope of the defined industries. The current indices are compiled using 2018 as the reference year. A chain base index is compiled using the Annual Overlap technique to derive the index based on previous year weights. In this method the weight is updated every year. The national accounts aggregates worked out are according to the 2008 System of National Accounts. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Classification: The National Standard Industrial Classification of Economic Activities, NSIC Rev.2 which is based on the International Standard Industrial Classification of Economic Activities, ISIC Rev. 4 is used to classify the principal economic activity (industry) of establishments and enterprises. 2.2.1 Scope Classification: The National Standard Industrial Classification of Economic Activities, NSIC Rev.2 which is based on the International Standard Industrial Classification of Economic Activities, ISIC Rev. 4 is used to classify the principal economic activity (industry) of establishments and enterprises. Scope of the data - Industrial coverage: All establishment classified to: Mining and quarrying (NSIC Section B) Manufacturing (NSIC Section C) and Electricity, gas, steam and air conditioning supply (NSIC Section D) Water supply; sewerage, waste management and remediation activities (NSIC Section E) - Product coverage: All commodities produced by establishments in scope Unrecorded activity Strictly illegal industrial activities are not covered. 2.4.1 Valuation Basic prices are used. 2.4.2 Recording basis The timing rules used for recording flows are mostly in accordance with the 2008 SNA. - Transactions and flows are recorded on an accrual basis. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs - Quarterly estimates are derived and the annual estimates are computed as the arithmetic mean of the quarterly indices. - Nature of quarterly indicators used Mining and quarrying Turnover data from Mauritius Revenue Authority Survey of establishments Deflators used: Relevant components of Consumer Price Index (CPI) Industry groups within manufacturing excluding sugar milling Turnover data from Mauritius Revenue Authority Exports Statistics Quarterly Stock Survey Building permits statistics for small establishments engaged in the manufacture of fabricated metal products and wooden furniture Deflators used: Producer Price index, Export Price Index and relevant components of CPI for small establishments Sugar milling Survey of establishments Production of sugar and price of sugar from Mauritius Sugar Syndicate Deflator used: Gross output is deflated by sugar prices and inputs are deflated by a weighted price index based on relevant components of CPI Electricity, Gas & Steam and Air Conditioning Supply Returns from Central Electricity Board and Independent Power Producers Volume based on quarterly quantity of electricity sold Water Supply; Sewerage, Waste Management and Remediation Activities For Water Supply; Sewerage, Waste Management: Quarterly return form Central Water Authority Volume based on quarterly quantity of water sold For Remediation Activities: Turnover data from the Mauritius Revenue Authority Deflators used: Relevant components of Consumer Price Index (CPI) - Nature of weights: industry value added - Sources of weights: 2018 Census of Economic Activities - Period of current index weight: Reference 2018 and updated annually - Frequency of weight updates: Major update every 5 years based on results of Census of Economic Activities - Item selection: All items 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Source data for the compilation of the index are received within 2 months after the reference quarter. 3.2.1 Source data assessment Verification of data: Standard editing procedures are used to validate the accuracy and reliability of the survey and secondary data. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Treatment of missing prices: Not applicable Selection of replacement items: Not applicable Adjustments for quality differences: Incorporated in price index used for deflation (Change in quality is taken into account when compiling price index used for deflation) Introducing new units and products: New units and products can be incorporated in the index as and when needed. This also applies to closure of units or when a product is no longer produced. Seasonally-adjusted indices: Not computed. Consistency with other indicators of industrial production: The indices are consistent with the Quarterly and Annual National Accounts estimates 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Computation of lower level indices: The index is calculated for each of the lowest level of activity classification Aggregation: Aggregation to broader level is done as a weighted arithmetic average of the lowest level indices using the Laspeyres index Base and weights period are the same major revision after each Census of Economic Activities results are released (every five years) Reference period: 2018 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data Unusual movements in the index are investigated regularly 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs Verification of processing: Editing tables of quarterly growth rates, and graphs at sectoral and sub-sectoral level 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses Revision analysis for the last quarter is conducted in the current quarter 5.1.5 Dissemination on request More disaggregated data can be made available on request, while respecting the confidentiality of data provided. 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points All publications include the name and contact details of the person responsible for the compilation of the index.
