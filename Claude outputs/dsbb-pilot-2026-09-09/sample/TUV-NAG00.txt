 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The CSD compiles the national accounts statistics of Tuvalu according to the methodology recommended by the United Nations, System of National Accounts 1993 . Both current price and constant (2005) price GDP estimates by economic sector are compiled and published. No expenditure components are compiled. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Sectors used are as follows: Agriculture, Forestry, and Fishing; Mining and Quarrying; Trade, Hotels, and Restaurants; Transport and Communications; Finance and Real Estate; Public Administration; Education; Health; Community and Personal Services. Scope of the data Out of nine islands constituting Tuvalu, eight islands are covered. All large businesses are covered. Exceptions to coverage The very small island of Niulakita with a population of 18 people is not covered. Small businesses are not covered. Unrecorded activity Activity on the island of Niulakita is not recorded; small business activity is not recorded. 2.4.1 Valuation Market prices are used for valuation of production. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Following source data are used for sectors. Large businesses—annual accounts. Small businesses—accounts from tax authorities and data from the Provident Fund. Agriculture—Household Income and Expenditure Survey (HIES) 2010. Construction—Funafuti Town Council records. Government—financial data from the Treasury. Banks—balance sheets. None 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses No revision studies done.
