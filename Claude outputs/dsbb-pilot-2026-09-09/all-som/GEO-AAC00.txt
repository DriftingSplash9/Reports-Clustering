 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The methodology underlying the compilation of Monetary and Financial Statistics in Georgia is consistent with the internationally agreed concepts, definitions and classifications recommended in the IMF's Monetary and Financial Statistics Manual and Compilation Guide 2016 , and presented as stock data in millions of Georgian Lari (GEL). National Bank Survey is compiled and disseminated monthly. The analytical accounts of the Central Bank are prepared daily with a one-day delay for internal use only. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Classification/sectorization principles underlying the compilation of Central Bank survey are consistent with Monetary and Financial Statistics Manual and Compilation Guide (2016) concepts. 2.2.1 Scope Classification/sectorization principles underlying the compilation of Central Bank survey are consistent with Monetary and Financial Statistics Manual and Compilation Guide (2016) concepts. Scope of the data Data cover all balance sheet accounts of the NBG. Exceptions to coverage Geographical coverage: Data do not cover the territories of Abkhazian Autonomous Republic and Tskhinvali Region (former South Ossetian Autonomous Region), a part of Georgian territory not controlled by the central authorities. 2.4.1 Valuation Assets and liabilities are reported at the book value. Data are end-of-month positions. Assets and liabilities items which are denominated in foreign currencies are converted into Georgian Lari (GEL) at the official exchange rate. 2.4.2 Recording basis The accounts of the NBG are recorded on an accrual basis in accordance with the requirements of the IFRS 9 standards. Data are presented on a gross basis. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures In line with the general principles of the Monetary and Financial Statistics Manual and Compilation Guide 2016 , assets and liabilities of the NBG are compiled on a gross basis. Claims on particular transactors are not netted against liabilities to those transactors. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Data included in Central Bank Survey are based on balance sheet of the NBG compiled by the Finance and Accounting Department of the NBG. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording The NBG balance sheet items are calculated based on the IFRS 9 requirements. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness The data are provided by the Finance and Accounting Department of the NBG on 5th working day after the end of reference month. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Central Bank Survey is compiled using the monthly balance sheets of the NBG. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Data are not seasonally adjusted. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results The NBG balance sheet data are cross-checked between Monetary Statistics Division and Finance and Accounting Department of the NBG.
