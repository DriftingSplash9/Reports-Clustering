 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The GFS are compiled in accordance with the guidelines of the Government Finance Statistics Manual 2014 (GFSM 2014). 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Classification system: The budget classifications to be followed in the compilation of budget execution reports are contained in Ministry of Finance Orders N65n of July 1, 2013 and N209n of November 29, 2017. In general, the classifications follow national concepts and definitions. The national presentation of revenues is similar to the GFS classification, and can be directly reclassified, with only minor adjustments for sales of assets and precious stones that are included in the financing data In general, the presentation of expenditures by function follows a national functional classification not very different from the Classification of the Functions of Government (COFOG) published by the United Nations, and adopted in the GFSM 2014. The presentation of expenditures by economic classification is similar to the GFS classification, and can be directly reclassified Scope of the data GFS: institutional coverage of the data is General government (including the extra-budgetary units). 2.4.1 Valuation Valuation of foreign currency transactions: All transactions are recorded in domestic currency. Foreign currency denominated transactions are converted to domestic currency using the exchange rate established by the CBR for accounting purposes. This rate is market-based, and it is a single rate (not buying and selling rates). 2.4.2 Recording basis Accounting basis: accrual 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Data sources: The main source of information for the compilation of fiscal statistics on the general government is accounting information (accounting reports). All these data are collected and processed by the Treasury. The main source of information for subnational governments are the monthly reports from the regional ministries of finance, based on accounting data, which are submitted electronically to the Treasury. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Consolidation: Consolidated data are prepared monthly.
