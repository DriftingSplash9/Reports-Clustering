 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions International Reserves Data are disseminated in millions of U.S. dollars on the total gross official reserves of the Banco de la República. The data include foreign reserves, gold, SDRs and the position with the Fund (IMF). The gold is revalued every day at market value. Concept: Gross official reserve assets are external assets that are readily available to and controlled by the Banco de la República for meeting balance of payments financing needs, for indirectly regulating the magnitude of payments imbalances through intervention in foreign exchange markets and for other purposes such as to control the volatility of the foreign exchange rate. Definition: Official reserve assets are the reserve holdings of the Banco de la República, the reserve position in the IMF and other reserves assets. Relationship to international guidelines: Data are compiled on the basis of definitions contained in the six edition of the IMF's Balance of Payments Manual and the IMF's https://www.imf.org/external/np/sta/ir/IRProcessWeb/index.aspx (2013) . Foreign Currency Liquidity The Data Template on International Reserves and Foreign Currency Liquidity is reported in millions of U.S. dollars and include: Official reserve assets and other foreign currency assets Predetermined short-term net drains Contingent short-term net drains Memo items 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Instruments included within the definition of reserve assets are classified into monetary gold, holdings of foreign currency, Special Drawing Rights, the reserve position in the IMF and other reserves assets. Holdings of foreign currency include bonds and notes, treasury bills, deposits with non-resident commercial banks and deposits with foreign monetary authorities. Scope of the data Institutional coverage: The BR controls all of Colombia's official reserve assets. Instrument coverage: All official reserve assets. Financial derivatives coverage: The BR uses interest rate futures contracts and currency forwards as part of the management of its foreign currency portfolio. Exceptions to coverage There are no exceptions in coverage. Unrecorded activity N.A. 2.4.1 Valuation Valuation: The values for SDRs and reserve position in the IMF are determined at the IMF basket valuation for the SDR. Gold is valued every day at the price published by the London Bullion Market Association. The foreign currency value of other overseas assets is based on market quotations; accrued interest is taken into account. Derivative instruments are also revalued using market quotations on a daily basis. Conversion rates: The foreign currency value of overseas assets is converted at end-day market exchange rates published by WM/Reuters Company. Aggregate data are disseminated in U.S. dollars. Frequency of calculation and revaluation: Reserve assets and derivative instruments are revalued daily. 2.4.2 Recording basis Foreign exchange assets are considered to include uncollected interest accruing on deposits, securities, bonds, and other debentures payable abroad on account of credits granted to central banks and special drawing rights holdings, as well as the difference in the central bank's favor between the value of foreign exchange receivable and payable resulting from buying and selling foreign currency other than the national currency. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures Data are also disseminated for net international reserves, which are calculated by deducting short-term credits to the central bank from gross international reserves. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Data of international reserves are obtained from the BR´s balance sheet. The figures are fully consistent with those included in the analytical accounts of the BR´s data. Net creditor positions in regional payments arrangements that involve reciprocal lines of credit and positions in the FLAR (Fondo Latinoamericano de Reservas) are excluded from international reserves information contained in the BR´s balance sheet. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Net creditor positions in regional payments arrangements that involve reciprocal lines of credit and positions in the FLAR (Fondo Latinoamericano de Reservas) are excluded from international reserves information contained in the BR´s balance sheet. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses The central bank’s quarterly “Comportamiento de la Balanza de Pagos de Colombia Periodo -----” includes description of methodological aspects of the international reserves and the main issues that explain its recent evolution. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points Prompt and knowledgeable service and support are available to statistics users. Material to raise awareness on the use of statistics is available (e.g., Reportes del Emisor is used for educational purposes). The BR website prominently displays access points for clients to obtain statistical information. No formal system monitors user assistance, although an informal follow-up system operates through the Section's administrative staff. 5.3.2 Availability of documents and services catalogs A catalog of all issues and articles of La Revista del Banco de la República is available in print and on the Web. In addition, a website-accessible list is available about all documents the BR produces. The Borradores de Economía available on the BR web has a listing of releases at the back of each publication. La Revista del Banco de la República is the only product that carries a minimal charge, and instructions on ordering it can be found on the Web or in the publication.
