 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The Central Bank of Venezuela (BCV) is responsible for compiling data intended to track productive activity. The BCV produces t he volume (Laspeyres type), price (Paasche type) and value of production indices cover the private manufacturing sector, taking 1997 as the reference year and basis for comparison in accordance with the new System of National Accounts. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The indicators are disaggregated to the ISIC Rev. 3 General, Division, Group, and Class of Activity levels. Scope of the data The unit of study is the establishment, which is the unit most appropriate for analyzing the production process, the key objective of the study. None 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The data on production are obtained from sample surveys of 1,077 incorporated enterprises with five (5) or more employees located in the five major cities of the country (Caracas Metropolitan Area, Maracay, Valencia, Barquisimeto, and Maracaibo) and their surrounding areas. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures The design of these indices is closely related to the conceptual and methodological aspects and the scope of preparation of the System of National Accounts, allowing for greater consistency and quality in the results, expressed in terms of real magnitudes, prices, and current values. The weighting variable used in integrating the indices is value added in base year 1997. None
