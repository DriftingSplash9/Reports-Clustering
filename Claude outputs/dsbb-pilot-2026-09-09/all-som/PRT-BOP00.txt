 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The balance of payments compilation is consistent with international standards and guidelines, namely with the six th edition of the Balance of Payments and International Investment Position Manual ( BPM6 ), published by the IMF and the fourth edition of the OECD's benchmark definition of Foreign Direct Investment . 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Transactions are classified to the relevant standard component of the sixt h edition of the Balance of Payments and International Investment Position Manual ( BPM6 ) , according to the international recommendations and guidelines. Institutional units are classified and attributed to the relevant sector according to the BPM6. Economic activity classification of resident and nonresident entities according to international standards (NACE and ISIC). Geographical classifications of counterparts according to international standards (ISO codes and Geo-nomenclature). Scope of the data Transactions coverage: all transactions between residents (central bank, general government, monetary and financial institutions and other sectors) and nonresidents, as specified in the BPM6, are covered in the balance of payments statistics. Geographic coverage: data cover the entire economic territory of Portugal. 2.4.1 Valuation Valuation: transactions valued at market prices. Conversion to unit of account: data reported in foreign currencies are mostly converted to euro units by using daily exchange rates. Less frequently, monthly average exchange rates are used. 2.4.2 Recording basis Transactions are recorded on an accrual basis. In income, estimations are made with an offset recorded in the underlying instrument on financial account. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures Current and capital account transactions are recorded on a gross basis. On financial account, a separate record is made for each individual asset and liability component. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The major sources of data are the direct reporting system to the Banco de Portugal for balance of payments and international investment position purposes, data available at the Banco de Portugal at other statistical domains, the Treasury for General Government's transactions and the Instituto Nacional de Estatística - INE (Statistics Portugal). The direct reporting system defined by Banco de Portugal comprises: the so called COPE report (Comunicação de Operações e Posições com o Exterior - Communication of external transactions and positions) where resident entities (exception made to households) report directly to Banco de Portugal their cross border operations and to some extent the related positions; and the COL report (Comunicação de operações de liquidação - Communication of cross-border settlements) where financial institutions report operations performed in the name of their clients, though with no statistical classification. Specifically: 1. Goods: General merchandise: trade statistics provided by the Instituto Nacional de Estatística - INE (Statistics Portugal). Debits of goods procured in ports by carriers: direct reporting for balance of payments statistics. Illegal activity: methodology shared by Statistics Portugal. 2. Services: Transportation: direct reporting for balance of payments statistics and estimates calculated to covert c.i.f. valued import of goods to f.o.b. valued import of goods. The information for the debit side of the category "freight" for the several means of transportation is taken from the conversion process from c.i.f. to f.o.b. valuation of goods; for the rest of transportation, direct reporting for balance of payments statistics is used. Travel: estimation based on two major components, accommodation expenses and other expenses. The former component follows the behavior of tourism statistics provided by INE-Portugal. The latter encompasses information of the multiple means of payment used, namely debit and credit card payments and cash both in euros and in other currencies. The levels of Travel expenditure are defined as to be coherent with INE's results on International Tourism Inquire, which is regularly launched. FISIM: estimates for FISIM are provided by the INE. Other services: direct reporting for balance of payments statistics. 3. Primary income: Compensation of employees: credits are estimated based on data provided by Portuguese Tax Authority; debits are based on direct reporting for balance of payments statistics. Investment income: direct reporting system and monthly estimates for reinvested earnings, from direct investment stocks surveys; and for accrued interest, from portfolio and other investment stocks statistics. All amounts are in gross terms. Other primary income: direct reporting system; Statistics Portugal for European Union Funds to be classified in this item (amounts delivered to the ultimate beneficiary). 4. Secondary income: European Union transfers - Statistics Portugal (amounts delivered to the ultimate beneficiary) together with direct reporting. Private transfers: Direct reporting system, payments system data and settlements data provided by banks for private transfers (estimates of workers' remittances also consider emigration/immigration statistics). Social benefits: data provided by Portuguese Tax Authority together with direct reporting and data from international organisations. Taxes on income: methodology developed together between Banco de Portugal and Statistics Portugal. 5. Capital account: European transfers: Statistics Portugal for European Union to be classified in this item (amounts delivered to the ultimate beneficiary) together with direct reporting Acquisition/disposal of non-produced, non-financial assets: direct reporting system. 6. Financial account: Direct investment: direct investment transactions are captured through the direct reporting system. Up to 2013, on an annual basis, FDI incorporated the results from a direct investment survey, which was based on a sample. From 2014 onwards direct investment is based on the annual accounts reported by all resident corporations through Simplified Corporate Information (IES - Informação Empresarial Simplificada), an administrative source. Reinvested earnings are estimated on the basis of the FDI main data sources. Portfolio investment: portfolio investment transactions are compiled using direct reporting to Banco de Portugal , on a security-by-security basis, of domestic securities transactions undertaken by nonresidents and on external securities transactions undertaken by residents. Additionally, data comprises information on residents' issuance of securities in foreign markets. There is also information on prices and interest rates (when applicable) for listed securities, provided by Euronext Lisbon (stock exchange), for domestic securities, and for foreign securities the ESCB Centralized Securities Database (CSDB) is used, for both assets and liabilities components of the "Foreign Portfolio Investment" item and related income. Direct reporting to the Balance of Payments – COPE is also an important source for quality control and complete information provided by the internal securities database.In addition, for the unlisted shares we use information from Simplified Corporate Information (IES - Informa ção Empresarial Simplificada). Financial derivatives: A monthly survey conducted through the major institutions of the resident banking system is the main source for compiling this item. The COPE direct reporting system is used as a complementary source of information . Other investment: settlements' data reported through the resident banking system and direct reporting companies. Information is also directly received from the Direcção Geral do Tesouro (Treasury), on the General Government's external transactions. Banco de Portugal provides data on the central bank sector. Other Monetary and Financial Institutions' (MFIs) external transactions are derived from MFI balance sheets, loans and borrowings. Data for monthly trade credits are obtained through estimates based on monthly goods and services transactions and on average days in receivables and payables with non-residents implicit in the Quarterly Survey on Non-Financial Enterprises (ITENF). Reserve assets: balance sheet of Banco de Portugal and Treasury's records. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Source data are consistent with the definitions, scope and classifications of balance of payments statistics. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Data collection and processing timetables are arranged to meet timeliness and periodicity for disseminating the balance of payments statistics. These timetables are distributed to all respondents. 3.2.1 Source data assessment Source data are analyzed for underreporting and/or misreporting and several procedures are applied for outlier’s identification. High-value transactions are confirmed with respondents. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Data compilation employs sound statistical techniques to deal with data sources. Some compilation procedures are implemented in order to minimize processing errors and to estimate missing observations. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Specific procedures are employed to make data consistent with balance of payments requirements: 1. Goods: The trade statistics related to general merchandise (provided by the Statistics Portugal) are adjusted for coverage, valuation, and time of recording on the basis of additional information. Conversion of imports from a c.i.f. to f.o.b. valuation (based on information provided by Statistics Portugal - trade statistics. 2. Services: Transportation services data are adjusted according to estimates (see above) calculated to covert c.i.f. valued import of goods to f.o.b. valued import of goods. Travel is estimated monthly. For insurance, a split is made between the service and the transfer components of insurance. Furthermore estimates are included in debits of insurance services as a result of the procedure undertaken to value imports on a f.o.b. basis. In the case of life insurance and pension funding, there is an estimate of the service included in the premium, the remaining value being allocated to the "Other investment" item, as well as the whole of the value of claims reported. Additionally, insurance (services and premiums) is accrued and insurance technical reserves are estimated. 3. Primary income: Monthly estimates for reinvested earnings and accrued interest on debt securities, loans and deposits. Monthly estimates for investment income attributable to policy holders in insurance. Compensation of employees credits is estimated based on data provided by Portuguese Tax and Customs Authority 4. Secondary income: workers' remittances and other personal transfers are monthly estimated. Social contributions and current taxes on salaries are estimated, based on compensation of employees and main counterpart taxes. Taxes are monthly estimated based on implicit real tax rates together with investment income. Social benefits are estimated based on declared amounts on the tax declarations for fiscal authorities. 5. Direct investment: Data on reinvested earnings are obtained through the results of the annual Simplified Corporate Information (IES) and, for periods for which the information is not available yet, estimates are produced based in other sources, namely the Quarterly Survey on Non-Financial Enterprises (ITENF). 6. Portfolio investment: adjustments are introduced to account for accrued interest in the underlying instrument). 7. Other investment: estimates on trade credits are made. Adjustments are introduced to account for accrued interest in the underlying instrument). Seasonal adjustment is made in current and capital account, using internationally accepted methodologies. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results The press and stock exchange information are used for detecting and analyzing the main operations of each period. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data Investment income is quarterly assessed in relation to the corresponding stock data in the international investment position statistics. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs Cross-checks between entries in the current, capital and financial accounts; Bilateral comparisons with bop data compiled by other countries and international organizations, namely, EU Institutions. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses Studies and analyses of revisions follow the revisions calendar referred in 4.3.1. A statistical quality unit on the department makes periodic report on the quality of the information concerning BOP/IIP (which includes information on portfolio investment) pointing out specific points for further analysis and potential correction if applicable. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request Unpublished but non-confidential statistics can be disseminated upon request . 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points Prompt and knowledgeable service and support are available to users of statistics.
