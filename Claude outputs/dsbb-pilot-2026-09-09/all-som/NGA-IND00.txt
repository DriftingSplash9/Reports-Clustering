 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The principal high frequency measure of production is the manufacturing production index. This index is quarterly in principle, but in recent years it has been produced on an irregular basis due to financial constraints. Regular production of the index has been resumed from August 2001. Scope of the data The survey covers a selection of establishments engaging 10 persons and above from all ISIC activity sectors in the manufacturing industry. The population of establishments in each activity sector determines the number of establishments to be selected in that activity sector. The survey collects monthly data on both quantities produced and value of production. In addition, the survey asks for the establishments number of employees and the total labour cost during the quarter. The capacity utilization of the manufacturing sector is also determined by this survey.
