Analytical Framework: The underlying conceptual framework for the international investment position disseminated under the SDDS is the Balance of Payments Manual, Revision 6 (BPM6). Data on external financial assets and liabilities at the end of the reporting period are compiled into the following categories: direct investment, portfolio investment divided into equity securities, debt securities, financial derivatives, other investment, and reserve assets. The standard components consist of opening and closing positions of external financial assets and liabilities and changes during the period related to transactions, exchange rate fluctuations, price changes, and other changes. • Definition: The international investment position is a statistical statement reflecting the value and structure of the accumulated stocks of external financial assets and liabilities of the Kyrgyz Republic, which is disseminated quarterly within 3-4 months of the reporting period in accordance with the classification and definitions in the Balance of Payments Manual, Revision 6, developed by the IMF. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Data are disseminated in million of US dollars on the international investment position of all sectors of the economy of the Kyrgyz Republic. Scope of the data • Coverage of units: The data cover all resident units of the Kyrgyz Republic as a whole. • Coverage of transactions: The international investment position data cover all financial transactions of residents of Kyrgyzstan with the rest of the world. • Geographic area: The international investment position data cover the entire territory of Kyrgyzstan, including transactions of entities in free economic zones. Exceptions to coverage Unrecorded activity 2.4.1 Valuation Conversion to units of account: Data are presented in millions of US dollars. Transactions recorded in national and other currencies are converted into US dollars at the average exchange rate for the quarter, calculated on the basis of official exchange rate quotations. If the date of the transaction of an international investment position is known, the conversion into US dollars is made at the official exchange rate of the NBKR on the day of the transaction. The corresponding exchange rates are used to convert data on stocks at the end of the period. 2.4.2 Recording basis Recording basis: Data on international investment position are recorded on an accrual basis. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures 3.1.1 Source data collection programs • Direct investment: Data are collected by the NSC from joint and foreign ventures, the NBKR provides data on the banking sector. • Portfolio investment: Data on portfolio investment are collected from internal sources, commercial banks, the NSC and the Financial Market Regulation and Supervision Service under the Ministry of Economy and Commerce of the Kyrgyz Republic. • Financial derivatives: Data on derivative financial instruments are collected from commercial banks. • Other investment: Data on foreign assets/liabilities of commercial banks are received by the NBKR from commercial banks. Data on foreign loans received by the Cabinet of Ministers or under its guarantee, as well as data on debt servicing are provided by the Ministry of Finance of the Kyrgyz Republic. Information on loans serviced by the NBKR is obtained from internal sources of the NBKR. Data on non-government loans are provided by the NSC. Data on accounts payable and receivable of resident enterprises (trade credits) and on enterprise accounts abroad are received from the NSC. Data on other assets (mainly contributions of the Kyrgyz Republic to funds of international financial institutions) are provided by the NBKR. • Reserve assets: Data on reserve assets and IMF loans, as well as other assets and liabilities of the NBKR are obtained from internal sources of the NBKR. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording 3.1.3 Source data timeliness 3.2.1 Source data assessment Quality of data provided by commercial banks is under the strict control. In accordance with this Law, the National Bank, together with the state body in the field of official statistics, prepares the balance of payments and determines the international investment position of the Kyrgyz Republic (paragraph 20 of Article 5 of the Law). State and local government bodies, individuals and legal entities, regardless of their forms of ownership, as well as non-residents located on the territory of the Kyrgyz Republic, are required to promptly provide the National Bank with information on the composition of indicators for compiling and forecasting the balance of payments in the forms and within the time limits determined by the National Bank (paragraph 3 of Article 39 of the Law). The Law of the Kyrgyz Republic "On Official Statistics" dated July 8, 2019 No. 82 guarantees legal entities and individuals the confidentiality of individual data collected or obtained by producers of official statistics. According to paragraph 3 of Article 69 of the Law, employees of the National Bank are prohibited from disclosing official and other confidential information, as well as information constituting a banking secret. Disclosure of such information is carried out in accordance with the legislation of the Kyrgyz Republic.
