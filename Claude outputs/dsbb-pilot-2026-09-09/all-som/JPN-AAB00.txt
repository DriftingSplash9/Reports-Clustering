 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Analytical Framework: Data on Money Stocks are in 100 millions of Yen. Key Indicators are the quantities shown below. "M1" =Currency in Circulation + Deposit Money. Financial Institutions surveyed for M1 (all depository institutions): financial institutions surveyed for M2, Japan Post Bank, and other financial institutions (The Shinkumi Federation Bank, Shinkumi Banks, The Rokinren Bank, Labour Banks, Prefectural Credit Federations of Agricultural Cooperatives, Agricultural Cooperatives, Prefectural Credit Federations of Fishery Cooperatives, and Fishery Coops). "M2" =Currency in Circulation + Deposits at domestically licensed banks, etc. Financial institutions surveyed for M2: Bank of Japan, domestically licensed banks (excluding Japan Post Bank), foreign banks in Japan, Shinkin Central Bank, shinkin banks, Norinchukin Bank, and Shoko Chukin Bank.) "M3 " =Currency in Circulation + Deposits at all depository institutions. ("M3 " = M1 + Quasi-money + CDs (certificates of deposit)) Financial Institutions surveyed for M3: same as those of M1. " L" = M3 + pecuniary trusts + investment trusts + bank debentures + straight bonds issued by banks + commercial paper issued by financial institutions + government securities + foreign bonds Financial Institutions surveyed for L: financial institutions surveyed for M3, trust accounts of domestically licensed banks, central government, insurance companies etc., and foreign bonds issuing organizations. "Currency in Circulation" represents the amount of bank notes and coins held by nonfinancial corporations, individuals and local governments. "Deposit Money" represents the total of demand deposits in the surveyed financial institutions (current deposits, ordinary deposits, saving deposits, deposits at notice, special deposits and deposits for tax payments) held by nonfinancial corporations, individuals and local governments minus the checks and notes held by the financial institutions. "Quasi-Money" represents the total of Time Deposits, Fixed Savings, Installment Savings and Foreign Currency Deposits in the surveyed financial institutions held by nonfinancial corporations, individuals and local governments. "Certificates of Deposit" are those issued by depository institutions and held by nonfinancial corporations, individuals and local governments. "Government Securities" includes Treasury Discount Bills, Treasury Bills, Financing Bills and FILP (the Fiscal Investment and Loan Program) Bonds. "Foreign Bonds" represents bonds issued by non-residents. For the data on Deposits, the data are made by aggregating the monthly average figures reported by individual banks. Money Stocks are the aggregate amount of money, such as currency in circulation and deposits money, held by "money holders" such as nonfinancial corporations and individuals. While money is an asset for "money holders," it is also a liability for the financial institutions that are "money issuers." When looking at the balance sheets of these financial institutions, all changes in money stock are inevitably accompanied by corresponding changes in loans, securities investments, and other assets and/or liabilities other than cash and deposits. Monetary Survey focuses on the above relationship, and is prepared for the purpose of showing the correspondence between changes in M3 in the Money Stocks, and, among other modifications, changes in the assets and liabilities of financial institutions. Structure of concepts and definitions are broadly consistent with the principles outlined by the IMF in the Monetary and Finance Statistics Manual and Compilation Guide (2016). The following two points should be noted; 1) within the resident sector data, the subdivision provided in the source data is less detailed than MFSMCG methodology requires. The sector "other sectors" in Monetary Survey refers to the sum of nonfinancial corporations, households and nonprofit institutions serving households. 2) The definition of residency applied to individuals is taken from FEFTA, and is not identical to the BPM6-MFSMCG definition in the following way. Japanese natural persons residing abroad are considered as nonresident two years (instead of one year) after their departure, while non-Japanese natural persons residing in Japan are considered as resident six months (instead of one year) after their arrival. (The residency status of corporations - say, of foreign-owned corporations in Japan or the Japanese-owned corporations abroad is rightfully determined on the basis of center of economic interest.) The external assets and liabilities of banks, etc. consist of assets, liabilities and net position, each of which is broken down into foreign-currency and yen denominated items. Each of these items is further broken down into long-term and short- term items, each of which shows the figure at the end of the reference month and of the change from the previous month. 2.2.1 Scope Analytical Framework: Data on Money Stocks are in 100 millions of Yen. Scope of the data Money Stock Counted-in-Accounts: Banking account (excluded trust account, external transactions account, and "JOM" account ("Japan Off-shore Market")). Financial Institutions surveyed for M1, M2, M3: see 2.1. The external assets and liabilities of banks, etc. The central bank (Bank of Japan) is not included in the "banks, etc." The definition of "banks, etc." is stipulated in Article 6-2 of the Foreign Exchange Order [Cabinet Order], a copy of which can be obtained from the contact person above. Specifically, banks, etc. cover financial institutions (e.g. city banks, regional banks, trust banks, long-term credit banks, foreign bank's branches in Japan) which fall under any of the following categories: i) those that are approved by the Minister of Finance to open Special International Financial Transactions Account (i.e., banks holding so-called "offshore account"); ii) those that have an aggregate transaction of over 10 billion yen in the previous month in any foreign exchange transactions; and iii) those that are designated by the Minister of Finance as similar to those listed in category (ii). 2.4.1 Valuation Money Stock Valuation:Face value. As for the data on Deposits, monthly average figures of the each individual bank are reported normally 4 business days after the last day of the reference month. The external assets and liabilities of banks, etc. Accounting practices: The figures of assets/liabilities are valued at the price and exchange rate at the end of month. Valuation of foreign-currency-denominated instruments: The reporters file the " Reports on Assets and Liabilities Positions of Banks, etc.", which is presented in yen for yen denominated assets/liabilities, and in US dollar for those denominated in foreign currencies (converted into US dollar with the monthly rate set by a ministerial ordinance). In order to compile the statistics (expressed in yen), the figures reported in US dollar are converted into yen with the market rate at the end of the reference month. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording The amount of banknotes in circulation and coins in circulation: BOJ's accounting records. The amount of bank deposit: Depository Corporations' statistical reports. Depository Corporations submit their statistical reports to BOJ twice; normally once in 4 business days after the end of the reference month (provisional report), on 20th after the end of the reference month (definitive report). The external assets and liabilities of banks, etc. Data sources for other banks: Data are collected under the authority of the FEFTA. The data source is the "Report on Assets and Liabilities Positions." submitted by banks, etc. (including foreign bank's branches in Japan) which report the figures relating to their foreign exchange business under the provisions of article 14 and 23 of the Ministerial Ordinance concerning Reports on Foreign Exchange Transactions etc. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Money Stock Time of recording: Monthly in the case of the data on Money Stock. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Compilation:M1, M2, and M3 are basically compiled by adding up reported figures. Components of L (other than M3) are compiled using various financial statistics as well as reported figures . As for Deposits, a monthly average is an arithmetic average of daily outstanding stock figures. The external assets and liabilities of banks, etc. Statistical adjustments: Statistics are compiled by simply adding up the reported figures in the "Report on Assets and Liabilities Positions of Banks, etc." 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Seasonally Adjusted data are also released (M1, M2, M3, Currency in Circulation, Deposit Money, Quasi-Money, L), and revised once a year. Seasonally Adjusted Based on the Bureau of Census method X-12ARIMA (Version 0.3). 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results Data verification: The reports of the banks are cross-checked against their balance sheets reports.
