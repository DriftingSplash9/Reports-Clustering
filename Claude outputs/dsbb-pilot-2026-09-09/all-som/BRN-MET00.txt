 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The Department of Statistics (DOS) of the Department of Economic Planning and Development (DEPD), Ministry of Finance and Economy is responsible for the compilation and dissemination of international merchandise trade statistics in accordance with United Nation’s IMTS Concepts and Definition 2010. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The documentation of Brunei Darussalam’s international merchandise trade statistics adopts the ASEAN Harmonized Tariff Nomenclature 2017 (AHTN 2017) developed by the World Customs Organization (WCO) and the United Nations Statistical Commission’s Standard International Trade Classification (SITC), Revision 4. Imports are classified by the country of origin for the partner country, while the country of last known destination is used for domestic exports and re-exports statistics. Country of origin is the country where the goods were entirely produced or the last country where a substantial processing took place. The ISO 3166 2-letter classification is used in the classification of country. 2.2.1 Scope The documentation of Brunei Darussalam’s international merchandise trade statistics adopts the ASEAN Harmonized Tariff Nomenclature 2017 (AHTN 2017) developed by the World Customs Organization (WCO) and the United Nations Statistical Commission’s Standard International Trade Classification (SITC), Revision 4. Imports are classified by the country of origin for the partner country, while the country of last known destination is used for domestic exports and re-exports statistics. The ISO 3166 2-letter classification is used in the classification of country. Scope of the data The general system of recording IMTS adopted by Brunei Darussalam covers imports, domestic exports and re-exports that have been cleared through the Royal Customs and Excise Department. The General Trade System of recording is adopted in compiling the merchandise trade statistics as follows: Imports comprise of goods which are brought into Brunei Darussalam. Exports comprise of domestic exports and re-exports of imported goods. Domestic exports comprise of goods which are produced or transformed, that is, manufactured, assembled or processed in Brunei Darussalam. Re-exports comprise of goods exported from Brunei Darussalam in the same form as they have been imported. The data are presented in Brunei Dollars. The geographical territory of reference is the territory of Brunei Darussalam. Exceptions to coverage The general system for recording trade statistics is adopted in Brunei Darussalam. Under this system, all goods imported to and exported from Brunei Darussalam are included in the international merchandise trade statistics with the following major exceptions: currency notes and coins in circulation; personal and household effects which do not incur any Brunei Darussalam customs duty and which are taken by passengers for their own use; stores and fuel supplied to Brunei ships and aircraft; articles on loan or rent for example, equipment for oil surveys and experimental drilling, etc.; goods in transit; fish and other marine products landed from Brunei Darussalam fishing vessels direct from fishing grounds; goods imported and exported by or on behalf of diplomatic services; and goods imported by the armed forces. There are no exclusions on geographic coverage. Unrecorded activity No estimates are made for unrecorded activities. 2.4.1 Valuation Imports: Imports are valued at Cost Insurance and Freight (CIF), that is, the value of goods at the frontier of the exporting country plus the cost of insurance and freight and any other charges when exported to Brunei Darussalam. Custom duties are not included in the value of imports. Exports: Exports are valued at Free on Board (FOB), that is, the value of goods to be purchased abroad up to the point where the goods are deposited on board the outgoing vessel, aircraft and/or vehicle. Goods on consignment are valued at the current import price for imports and the current export price for exports. 2.4.2 Recording basis Brunei Darussalam uses the ‘release date’ as its time of recording, which provides the time/date that stipulated in source documents when goods enter or leave the economic territory. Importers and exporters of goods are required to furnish information in the customs declarations in respect of every consignment of merchandise imported or exported. Permits applications data are transmitted to the Royal Customs and Excise Department via a web-based portal called e-Customs. The e-Customs system provides an integrated electronic trade documentation system that will allow the trading community in Brunei Darussalam to submit applications electronically to the Royal Customs and Excise Department for processing. The system also allows the Royal Customs and Excise Department officers to process application received electronically or perform their day-to-day operations. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs IMTS are compiled on a monthly basis based on the imports and exports data submitted electronically by the Royal Customs and Excise Department, Ministry of Finance and Economy through customs declarations. Data exports and imports related to oil and gas are furnished by the Ministry of Energy, Manpower and Industry. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording The Royal Customs and Excise Department is responsible for the collection and approval of imports and exports permits. However, controlled items are still subjected to approval by the relevant competent authorities. These data are submitted by the merchants to Royal Customs and Excise Department via the e-Customs system. Subsequently, these data would be transferred to the Department for compilation and dissemination. Types of data collected include: Trade type (Domestic Export / Re-Export / Import) Month and year HS Code / product description Partner country Station Quantity Value Insurance (Import data only) Freight (Import data only) Declaration Number Importer/Exporter Exchange Rate (Import data only) 3.1.3 Source data timeliness IMTS data is compiled on a monthly basis. Source data from the Royal Customs and Excise Department and Ministry of Energy, Manpower and Industry are received within 4 weeks after the reference month. 3.2.1 Source data assessment Prior to release, the monthly data is reviewed by comparing with historical trends to identify common reporting errors or in the identification of coverage or other reporting problems. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Adjustments to significant values are made by contacting the source data (Royal Customs and Excise Department and Ministry of Energy, Manpower and Industry). 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Not Applicable None
