 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The National Institute of Statistics, Economic and Demographic Studies (INSEED) of the Ministry of Planning, Development and Cooperation is responsible for compiling external trade statistics on the basis of customs declarations. Scope of the data The data are annual and are compiled in values (millions of CFA francs) and volumes (metric tons). These statistics reflect merchandise imports and exports according to the General System. Import and exports of military equipment from the French military base are included in the statistics. Exceptions to coverage Transshipments of merchandise transiting the country are not taken into account. However, packages sent by mail are included. Chad's military equipment imports are not covered. Owing to its peculiar nature, some trade is not taken into account in the trade statistics per se, namely personal effects or used goods that passengers carry with them and financial instruments, such as monetary gold. Unrecorded activity Estimates of contraband are not taken into account in external trade statistics publications. 2.4.1 Valuation Checks are conducted to detect coding errors (missing codes), consistency of the different values (checks of implicit prices, in particular), as well as missing declarations. Some c.i.f. prices are adjusted from c.i.f.-Douala prices to c.i.f.-Chad border prices. This is the case of petroleum and sugar products mainly. Volumes and values are adjusted by the customs office proportionally to the number of missing declarations. The number of missing declarations represents about 30 percent of total declarations. 2.4.2 Recording basis Imports are recorded at the time Customs takes charge of the merchandise. Exports are recorded at the time the merchandise leaves the Customs office. Imports are recorded at their c.i.f. (cost-insurance-freight) value and exports at the f.o.b. value. Merchandise is identified by the 8-digit tariff position in the customs tariff nomenclature (Harmonized System) with a 2-digit extension for the national nomenclature. Imports are broken down by country of origin, exports by first country of destination. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The external trade unit of INSEED collects the declarations from the statistics unit at the Directorate of Customs and Indirect Taxes (DDDI), processes and publishes them. The declarations collected relate to customs activity nationwide. The average number of declarations in one calendar year is about 10,000. None None
