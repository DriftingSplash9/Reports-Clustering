 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The international trade statistics are in lign with the concept of general trade. The concept of special trade is not applied. As a general rule, the trade statistics are compiled in accordance with United Nations recommendations. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Products are classified according to the Harmonized Commodity Description and Coding System and countries are classified by ISO codes. Scope of the data Information is collected on all merchandise entering and leaving the country, including donations and Customs General Directorate’s own forms are used for this purpose. The National Statistics Institute publishes the original data without adjustments that are received from the Customs General Directorate. The Central Bank of São Tome and Principe does not make seasonal adjustments to the data on international trade. Exceptions to coverage Duty-free areas and commercial warehouses in the territory of São Tome and Principe are not included. Unrecorded activity 2.4.1 Valuation The Customs General Directorate sends the Central Bank the records on exports on an FOB basis and on imports on a CIF basis. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The data on merchandise imports and exports are compiled by the Statistics and Economic Studies Directorate of the Central Bank of São Tome and Principe based on information provided by the Customs General Directorate. The Customs General Directorate sends the Central Bank the records on exports on diskette duly coded by product category, origin, and destination of the merchandise, volume and customs value of exports and imports. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures The Central Bank makes adjustments to the value of imports on a CIF basis for the FOB basis assuming an adjustment rate of 22%, 80% of which represents merchandise freight and 20% the respective insurance paid by non-residents. Some additional corrections are made to the headings for imported investment goods and fuels and cacao exports, based on information received directly from the companies involved. None
