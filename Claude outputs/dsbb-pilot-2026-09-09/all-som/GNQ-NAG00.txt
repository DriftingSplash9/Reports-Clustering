 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions In Equatorial Guinea the National Accounts are drawn up by the National Accounts Service of the Department of Economic Statistics of the National Institute of Statistics of Equatorial Guinea (INEGE). It produces the following: Annual GDP measured using the demand approach (by expenditure component in final consumption, gross fixed capital formation, changes in inventories, exports and imports) and GDP measured using the supply approach (by branches of economic activity), at current and at constant market prices. The base year is 2006. GDP at market prices is the final outcome of the productive activities of resident production units during the period; it is also the sum of the end uses of goods and services in the whole of the economy minus the value of imports of goods and services during the period. Production units are grouped according to principal activity; end uses of goods and services are final public and private consumption, gross fixed capital formation, changes in inventories and exports. The National Accounts are compiled according to the methodological guidelines for National Accounts of the United Nations, the System of National Accounts 1993 (1993 SNA), using the ERETES program recommended by AFRISTAT for its Member States. Currently, INEGE has requested technical assistance from the United Nations Economic Commission for Africa (UNECA) to implement the activities needed to adopt the System of National Accounts 2008. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The system used to classify economic activities is the NAEMA nomenclature (activities) and the NOPEMA nomenclature (products) that AFRISTAT recommended for its Member States, which is not very different from the International Standard Industrial Classification of all Economic Activities (ISIC). GDP measured using the supply approach is disseminated at current and constant prices and includes the following sectors: Primary sector : agriculture; livestock and hunting; forestry and forest development; fishing and fish farming. Secondary sector : agri-food industries; manufacturing of textile, leather, and travel goods; petroleum product refining; other manufacturing industries; electricity, water and gas; construction. Scope of the data The national accounts of Equatorial Guinea cover the economic activities of all resident units. They include legal production activities within the national territory. Also includes are indirect estimates for some informal activities. The informal sector data are obtained using questionnaires, information from administrative sources and estimates made from survey findings. The accounts cover all market transactions and production for self-consumption. Gross value added includes a deduction for financial intermediation services, which are measured indirectly (SIFIM). The information covers the whole of the national geographical territory. Unrecorded activity There are no direct estimates of economic activities carried on by workers on their own account. Illegal activities are not included. 2.4.1 Valuation Transactions are recorded on an accrual basis, in accordance with a function for some government transactions that are recorded under receipts or disbursements. As recommended in the 1993 SNA, the value of production is measured at basic prices, imports are valued as cost, insurance and freight (CIF), local jobs are valued at purchaser’s price, exports are measured free on board (FOB). Market prices and exchange rates are used to convert foreign operations. The rates used are the average rates during the year. Although 1993 SNA recommends also showing total imports valued by f.o.b., hence CIF/FOB. An adjustment is made to total imports. 2.4.2 Recording basis Transactions between economic agents are recorded on an accrual basis, with the exception of certain government purchases that are recorded on a cash basis. The recording period refers to the calendar year. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs 1. Methodological framework of Base Year 2006 To establish a National Accounts base year, and pursuant to SNA 93, standard rules were set for sources of data, operations, products, branches, institutional sectors and economic agents, etc. needed to describe the economy. 2006 was chosen as the base year because that was the year of the first Households Survey in Equatorial Guinea (EEH), which measures household final consumption. The appropriateness of using 2006 as a base year was then confirmed by an analysis of the base years used in other Central African Economic and Monetary Community (CEMAC) countries, in order to enhance regional comparability. To measure private sector, especially business, activity, the national accounts use the Statistics and Tax Declaration (DEF) that companies file with the Office of the Director General of Taxes and Contributions (DGIC) by April 30 of the year following that covered by the DEF. To complete the data obtained from the DEF, an e-mail campaign is organized to gather data from sources. In that way, data are obtained from enterprises not covered by the DGIC. A short questionnaire is devised to reach parts of the private sector that did not file DEF (education, health, commerce, etc.). Collection and processing of primary data by sector This section describes the estimation techniques and specific assumptions applied in each sector and provides a qualitative assessment of the accuracy of the findings for each sector, depending on the data sources available. For most of the other sectors not shown the DEF or data provided were completed by using estimates to balance supply and demand. Subsistence agriculture (branch 001001) : in addition to the data received from the Ministry of Agriculture and Food, estimates are made per product, added to the corresponding imports to obtain the supply per product that is compared with domestic demand, composed of the total consumption of households plus intermediary consumption of the branches. Livestock and hunting (branch 002000) : the data received from the Ministry of Agriculture and Food was completed with estimates based on the Ministry's hypothesis: "for every 3,500 tons of meat imported per year, 150 tons are killed locally." local meat production accounts for 4.3% of imports; from which, the quantity of animals required for said amount of meat was deducted. Forestry (branch 003000) : the data coming from the General Directorate of Forests (DGB) and the Regional Delegation of the Ministry of Agriculture, Livestock, Forestry and the Environment are processed, in order to have a better view of round wood by company (the same is done for wood) transformed from branch 009004) to estimate the export of the wood and generate the data that should be provided by the DEF of the unavailable companies. Petroleum extraction (branch 005001) : production and export data on oil volume by company are available and compared with what oil and natural gas producing companies declare. A price is determined to value these exports, considering the international average price of standard crude types (Brent, WTI and Dubai), minus a discount due to quality and multiplied by the average exchange rate. According to the international production standards, the technical coefficient is known and the DEF of the companies in the branch are also used to have the breakdown of intermediate consumption in products. Regarding natural gas, net production is determined as the difference between production, injection and part of gas burned by each company. In particular, the valued production of natural gas is determined during the processing of the DEFs of the producing companies. The DEF of the oil service companies are processed to obtain the production and intermediate consumption of this type of companies. Other mining and quarrying activities (branch 005002): based on the information provided by the General Directorate of Quarries and the consumption of inputs (an average of 1.3%) by large companies in the "construction" branch, the production necessary to satisfy the consumption of the construction (branch 011000) and of the "factory of blocks" (branch 009003). Manufacture of textiles, leather and other products derived from cotton (branch 00700): the tailors belonging to the informal sector, an analysis of domestic demand showed that it was necessary to have at least a local production equivalent to 36.4% of imports to satisfy said internal demand. As for crafts, given the consumption of households and imports, the necessary production was estimated in order to obtain a balance between domestic supply and demand. Derivative gas refinery (branch 008001) : production and export data are processed in volume by products offered by the General Directorate of Hydrocarbons (DGH), to which a price is applied, obtained by its corresponding international price minus a discount, as done for crude oil for quality reasons. It should be noted that there is a part of oil (condensate 2) that is deducted, because it has already been considered in the branch account "005001" by the DGH. Refinery of petroleum products or fuels (branch 008002): in this branch the bottling and gas distribution activity is estimated. The local sales data (volume and value) of butane or propane of the DEFs of the companies of branch 008001 are used. Manufacture of furniture, machinery, other metal fabrications (branch 009001): it is an activity mostly in the informal sector, based on the DEFs that are obtained from some carpentry, a production is determined that, added to the imports, satisfies the demand. Manufacture of building materials (branch 009003): For the formal part of this branch the DEFs available are processed, and for the informal part, based on the DEFs that are obtained from some blocks, a production that satisfies the demand is determined. Transformation of wood (branch 009004): in this branch, the same processing of the branch "003000" "exploitation of round wood" is applied. Other processing industries (branch 009005) : in this branch, the DEFs of the two or three active companies in this sector are processed. Production and distribution of electricity (branch 010001): the accounting document of the national distribution company is processed and the production of the thermal power station on the island of Bioko is estimated. Water distribution (branch 010002): it is an activity mostly of the informal sector, to obtain data, questionnaires were used to obtain information on the production and distribution of water by trucks in Malabo. This information is completed by estimating the production equivalent to the water demand. Construction (branch 011000): the DEFs of the companies in the branch are processed and compared with the payments made to the companies by the State under the PIP. Trade and repair of vehicles (branch 012000): the DEFs of the branch are processed. For the sub-branch Commerce, the production is completed in such a way that it is equal to the sum of the commercial margins of all the branches of activities of the economy. For the repair of vehicles, estimates are made in such a way that a balance of the supply and demand of this service is reached. Restaurants and Hotels (branch 013000): the DEFs of the branch are processed and completed with the estimates of the same, to reach a balance between supply and demand of this branch. Land transport (branch 014001): the DEFs of the companies of the branch are processed, distinguishing those dedicated to the rental of vehicles with driver (services of this branch) of those that carry out the transport and is completed with the estimates of the service of taxi, trucks and buses, thus achieving a balance between supply and demand of this branch. Air transport (branch 014002): the DEFs of the companies of the branch are processed, distinguishing those that offer services to the airlines of those that carry out the air transport. Maritime transport (branch 014004): the DEFs of the branch companies are processed, distinguishing those engaged in maritime or customs transit services (branch services) from those engaged in maritime transport. Telecommunications and mail (branch 014004): the DEFs of the companies of the branch are processed, distinguishing those that are dedicated to the telecommunications and internet service of those that carry out the mail distribution. Banks and insurance (branch 015001): commercial banks and insurance companies represent the financial sector. DEFs are used to measure the financial activity of the country. Public administration (branch 017001): based on the TOFE, PIP and accounting records of the Autonomous Entities, the set of salaries and consumption of goods and services of the public Administration is obtained. According to the SNA93, public administration production is measured by its cost of operation, which is the sum of wages paid, its intermediate consumption of goods and services and its consumption of fixed capital (CCF). It should be noted that the CCF represents the decline experienced during the accounting period, in the current value of the fixed assets it possesses and those used by the State for the welfare of the population, as a result of physical deterioration, normal obsolescence or damage normal accidents. In other words, the CCF is granted as an average of the updated values ​​at current year prices of the GFCF as a whole of the State assets. The obsolescence factors considered are: Construction and civil engineering works - 25 years; Machineries and furniture - 10 years; and Transport material - 5 years. Note: The public part of the branch Education (002000), Health and social welfare (019000) and Other social activities (002000) is estimated according to the recommendations of SNA 93, that is, in the same way as the public administration. By not having the budget execution (TOFE) by ministry, the public part of these sectors is directly processed without distinction in the Public Administration branch (017001). Social Security (branch 017002): the INSESO accounting document is processed. To break down the social contributions by sector of activity, in the case of non-reporting companies to INSESO, an average rate of 9.612% of the salaries paid is used to estimate their contributions to INSESO, knowing that, in ERETES, the sum of the Payment of contributions from the different branches is reconciled with what the INSESO declares to have received from the sector companies. This method allows obtaining a better distribution of INSESO's contributions by branches. Education (branch 018000): private education is represented by religious and non-religious centers, of which three centers offer accounting data annually, through questionnaires. Based on these data, the activity of the other centers is estimated. Health and social welfare (branch 019000): making use of the DEFs of private clinics and the accounting documents of the La Paz hospitals in Malabo and Bata, an estimation of the sector is made. Other social activities (branch 020000): based on the DEFs of the companies that are sent annually, an estimation of the sector is made in such a way that the supply-demand balance of these services is observed. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques National accounts data are compiled principally on the basis of administrative records, in general accounting documents for all sectors and institutions operating in the national economic territory. For the private sector, statistics and tax returns (DEF) are used. For Central Government ( Administración Central del Estado ) and Autonomous Entities, and partly government-owned enterprises, the National Budget ( Presupuesto General del Estado ) and its execution are used. To estimate GDP aggregates using the production approach, the National Accounts section mainly uses information from two types of sources: sources directly involved in economic activities (taking into consideration the number of economic establishments in the directories, the workforce, hours worked, installed capacity, production, sales, prices, income, expenditure, areas cultivated, end-use of output, and expansion coefficients calculated for the base year) and alternative sources that provide a point of comparison for determining the consistency of the information, such as, for instance, the figures for exports, imports, taxes, short-term surveys by BEAC, and mirror statistics. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results During the data and consistency validation process, and in practice for any calculation involving quantification of GDP using either the production or the demand approach, the information compiled is processed and analyzed and various tables are drawn up with the findings. Then the information is crossed with other related variables, with a view to verifying the validity of estimates, with assistance from the World Bank’s resident expert, AFRISTAT and AFRITAC. None
