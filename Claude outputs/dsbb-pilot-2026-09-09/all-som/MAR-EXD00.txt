 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Analytical framework: Data on the Gross External Debt disseminated in the framework of the SDDS are broadly compiled in accordance with the recommendations of the External Debt Statistics: Guide for Compilers and Users (IMF, 2013) and in line with the 6th edition of the Manuel of Balance of Payments ( BPM6 ) of the IMF. Data are published quarterly and broken down: By economic sectors: general government, monetary authorities, banks, other sectors (State owned enterprises debt and publicly guaranteed debt, private non guaranteed debt). By original maturity: short term (less than one year) and long term (more than one year). By instruments: bonds and notes, loans, trade credits, currency and deposits, money market instruments and other debt liabilities. Data on direct investment inter-company lending are shown separately. In addition, data on external debt are compiled in accordance with data on the IIP. Definition: The gross external debt position, at any given time, is the outstanding amount of those actual current, and not contingent, liabilities that require payments of interest and/or principal by the debtor in the future and that are owed to non-residents by residents of an economy. Thus, the statistics of the Gross External Debt include the whole of liabilities of residents that are owed to non-residents and included in the IIP except for the financial assets coming from the equity liabilities and other similar liabilities, in accordance with the recommendations of the External Debt Statistics: Guide for Compilers and Users (IMF, 2013) . 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Classification: External debt is classified in accordance with the BPM6 and the guide of external debt statistics 2013 . Scope of the data Coverage of external debt: The statistics of the Gross External Debt include the whole those liabilities belonging to residents that are owed to non-residents and included in the IIP except for the financial assets coming from the equity liabilities and other similar liabilities, in accordance with the recommendations of the Guide of External Debt Statistics . The concept of residence, as it is specified in the MBP6 , is not dependent on nationality or on legal criteria. It is based on the concept of center of economic interest. Residents consist of institutional units (households, government agency, corporation...), which have their center of interest inside the economic territory of the country. The non-residents are defined by difference as the institutional units which are not resident in the declaring economy. Geographical coverage: The geographic coverage is based on residency criteria and corresponds to the national economic territory. The data related to the free trade zones is included. Exceptions to coverage Accrued interests which are not yet due are not included in the external debt position. The data related to the free trade zones have not yet been included. 2.4.1 Valuation Conversion to a unit of account: The data are expressed in million of Moroccan dirham. They are converted at the average exchange rates published by the Bank Al-Maghrib (Central Bank) on the last working day of the reference period. Valuation: External loans and Eurobonds are valued, at the reference date, at their face value. 2.4.2 Recording basis Recording basis: The debt is established by the provision of economic value that creates a claim by the creditor. In practice, the time of recording usually corresponds to the settlement date. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs External debt data are disseminated by the Ministry of Economy and Finance using various sources that include data on central government external debt, state owned enterprises external debt, territorial communes external debt, public financial institutions external debt and public utility institutions external debt, compiled by the Department of Treasury and External Finance, external debt of Monetary Authorities and banks compiled by Bank Al-Maghrib (Central Bank), and data on private sector debt not guaranteed by the government compiled by the Foreign Exchange Office. Data on public external debt (central government external debt, state owned enterprises external debt, territorial communes external debt, public financial institutions external debt and public utility institutions external debt) are obtained from the External Debt Database of the Department of Treasury and External Finance set-up to manage the public external debt. Information on monetary authorities and banking sector is derived from the central bank and the financial system's accounting data. Deposit liabilities are determined on the basis of declarations by Bank Al-Maghrib and other banks. These are gross liabilities of the Central Bank and credit balances in the convertible dirham accounts of non-residents. The data on private external debt stock are collected through the system set up by the Foreign Exchange Office to monitor private external debt. This system is based on banking system accounts and declarations by the recipients of foreign borrowing. For trade credits, the Foreign Exchange Office adopted the results of a survey to collect quarterly data on trade credits and advances related to the imports and exports of the Moroccan companies operating in foreign trade. Data required of these companies are pulled directly from their accounting books (drawn from the balance sheet). 3.2.1 Source data assessment Stock and transaction data recorded are cross-checked. The data available to the department are supplemented and/or cross-checked regularly with information provided by different sources. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Generally there are no statistical adjustments in coverage, classification, timing and valuation of data.
