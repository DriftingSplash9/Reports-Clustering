 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The OPS compiles and disseminates Palau’s balance of payments data. These data are compiled broadly following the guidelines recommended in the IMF’s Balance of Payments Manual , sixth edition ( BPM 6 ). The published data are expressed in millions of United States dollars (USD). The balance of payments statement contains all main categories—the current account (with goods, services, primary income, and secondary income); capital and financial account; and the overall balance. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Classification used in the presentation of Palau’s balance of payments data posted on the OPS’s website is as follows: Trade balance Services Income Current transfers Current account (with grants) Current account (without grants) Capital and financial account Overall balance 2.2.1 Scope Classification used in the presentation of Palau’s balance of payments data posted on the OPS’s website is as follows: Trade balance Services Income Current transfers Current account (with grants) Current account (without grants) Capital and financial account Overall balance Scope of the data The balance of payments data, in principle, cover all transactions of Palau’s resident sector with the nonresidents. The scope is broadly consistent with the internationally accepted standards, guidelines, or good practices. 2.4.1 Valuation Balance of payments data are presented at market prices. Both imports and exports are presented in f.o.b. 2.4.2 Recording basis Customs data, used for balance of payments compilation, provide exports and imports on f.o.b. basis. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Following key sources provide data on various components of the balance of payments. Exports and imports—Customs data. Shipping support services—Corporate Registry at the Attorney General’s Office. Airport and sea port services—Ministry of Finance. Travel—Palau Visitors Authority; and tourism survey of 2000. Remittances—HIES External debt—Ministry of Finance Bilateral grants—Ministry of Finance None 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses No revision studies are done.
