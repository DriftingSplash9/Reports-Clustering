 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Analytical framework: The external debt is compiled by BNM and is essentially in compliance with the guidelines published by the IMF. The data is published on the BNM’s website (http://www.bnm.gov.my) on a quarterly basis and is broken down by sectors (General Government, monetary authorities, banking sector and other sectors), by instruments and by maturity (short term/ long term). The data are compiled in accordance with the concept and methodology set out in the IMF’s Balance of Payments Manual , Sixth Edition ( BPM6 ) and the External Debt Statistics: Guide for Compilers and Users (2013) . Definition of external debt: Gross external debt, at any given time, is the outstanding amount of those current, not contingent, liabilities that require payment(s) of interest and / or principal by the debtor at some point(s) in the future and that are owed to non-residents by residents of an economy. Sector attribution of external debt liabilities : External debt liabilities are attributed to the sector of the immediate borrower. Sector classification of external debt liabilities: External debt liabilities are classified under the sector category of the debtor and not of any guarantor. The criteria used for determining whether a liability is a debt instrument or not : Gross external debt, at any given time, is the outstanding amount of those current, not contingent, liabilities that require payment(s) of interest and / or principal by the debtor at some point(s) in the future and that are owed to non-residents by residents of an economy. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The classification used to compile the statistics is generally in line with the IMF's Balance of Payments Manual , Sixth Edition ( BPM6 ). Classification by type of debt instrument. Arrears are not separately identified in the gross external debt. External debt liabilities of households and small private enterprises are included under "other sectors". Financial leases with nonresidents are treated as loans and included in the gross external debt position. Maturity classification: Data are disseminated by tenure i.e long term and short term based on original maturity. Sector disaggregation: Data on disaggregation of "other sectors" into three subsectors (nonbank financial corporations, nonfinancial corporations, and NPISH) are compiled but are not available to the public. Data disaggregation into public sector external debt and publicly guaranteed private sector debt: Data are available to allow disaggregation into public sector external debt and government guaranteed public sector loan but are not available to the public. Intercompany lending: Included in loans for non-banks and currency and deposits for banks. Scope of the data Coverage of external debt: External debt covers all contracted obligations, both with and without interest payments, in which debtors agree to repay the principal amount to the non- resident creditors. The scope of external debt covers all types of non-equity liabilities, regardless of currency denomination and types of debt instrument. Specific items: Intercompany lending between entities in a direct investment relationship is included in the gross external debt position, but not identified separately. Traded debt instruments issued by residents abroad but in hands of residents: Traded debt instruments issued by residents abroad but in hands of residents are not included in the gross external debt position. Exceptions to coverage Instrument coverage : Debt-service payment schedule is not available to the public . Geographical coverage: Debt liabilities of the offshore enterprises -- including banks –: Effective from the 1Q2008 reporting, the external debt of Malaysia has been redefined to include debt exposure of offshore entities in Labuan International Business and Financial Centre (IBFC) vis-à-vis the rest of the world while the debt exposure of residents with offshore entities in Labuan IBFC are excluded. This move aimed to facilitate a better risk assessment of debt exposure for the economy as well as to be consistent with the international standards and best practices on the treatment of offshore entities in external debt compilation and dissemination. 2.4.1 Valuation Conversion to unit of account: External debt is reported in the original currencies and the foreign amount is converted at the end-quarter rate of the period. Valuation: The stocks of external debt are valued based on the book value, or in some cases, the par value. 2.4.2 Recording basis Recording basis: In line with the concept for the recording of Balance of Payment (BOP) and International Investment Position (IIP), a transaction is recorded when “change of ownership” is evidenced. Exchange rate change is taken into account to reflect the change in the level of external debt. Outstanding liabilities arising from periodic interest costs that have accrued and are not yet payable: These liabilities are included as part of the value of the underlying debt instrument. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Data sources: General Government debt: data are obtained from the Ministry of Finance. Monetary authorities: data are obtained from Bank Negara Malaysia. Banking sector: data are obtained from the quarterly IIP reporting by banking institutions covering commercial banks, Islamic banks and investment banks in Malaysia. Non-bank debt : data are obtained through the quarterly IIP survey submitted to BNM. Public enterprise debt: Data of the government guaranteed debt and non-government guaranteed debts are obtained through the quarterly IIP survey submitted to BNM. Loans: data are obtained through the quarterly IIP survey submitted to BNM. Currency and deposits : Quarterly IIP reporting of banks and nonbank financial institutions for data on deposit liabilities to non-residents; administrative data for monetary authorities’ deposit liabilities. 3.2.1 Source data assessment Other: Data is verified against records in the Cash Balance of Payments, banking and financial statistics and other administrative records. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Debt securities: Quarterly IIP survey for debt securities issued abroad; administrative data for debt securities issued abroad by government.
