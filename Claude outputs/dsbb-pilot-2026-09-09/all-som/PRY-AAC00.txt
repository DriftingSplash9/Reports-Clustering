 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Statistic procedures are based on the IMF's 2016 Monetary and Financial Statistics Manual and Compilation Guide (2016 MFSMCG). 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The asset and liability components are presented as follows: (1) Net Foreign Assets, divided in claims on non-residents and liabilities to non-residents; (2) Claims on Other Depository Corporations; (3) Net Claims on Central Government, divided in claims on central government and liabilities to central government; (4) Claims on Other Sectors, divided in claims on other financial corporations, claims on state and local government, claims on public non-financial corporations, and claims on the private sector; (5) Monetary Base, divided in currency in circulation, liabilities to other depository corporations, and liabilities to other sectors; (6) Other Liabilities to Other Depository Corporations; (7) Deposits and Securities Excluded from Monetary Base, divided in deposits and securities included in broad money and deposits and securities excluded from broad money; (8) Loans; (9) Financial Derivatives; (10) Shares and Other Equity; and (11) Other Items Net. Scope of the data The data covered come from the Central Bank of Paraguay’s balance sheet which includes monetary base, monetary regulation instruments, international reserves, foreign assets and liabilities of the public sector. Exceptions to coverage Extra-accounting documents are used for purposes of identifying data related to financial public sector operations. Unrecorded activity No unrecorded activity 2.4.1 Valuation Most of data are valued at market price as the primary concept of valuation. Some financial assets (Bonds-amortized cost value) are not valued at market price. 2.4.2 Recording basis The Balance Sheet of the CBP meets requirements for accrual accounting according to the 2016 MFSMCG. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures Grossing/netting procedures follow the MFM’s methodology. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Source data are mainly based on institutions’ balance sheet. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording • Definitions: The Central Bank survey is based on accrual accounting records of the CBP • Scope: Central Bank • Classifications: Financial instruments are classified in accordance with the methodology of the IMF's 2016 MFSMCG • Valuation: Most data are valued on a market price basis. • Time of recording: Transactions are recorded when ownership of the financial asset is transferred. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Source data timeliness meet the required standard. Central Bank’s final balance is available on a daily basis. 3.2.1 Source data assessment A cross check process is executed against data of the previous month. In case of inconsistencies, pertinent consults are made with the source of reference data. Most of information is directly imported from CBP’s balance sheet. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques The CBP’s balance sheet is inputted electronically and the corresponding tables generated automatically, reducing substantially the chances of error. Because monetary data are based on accounting records, no estimations are needed. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Some econometric procedures are used for statistical analysis. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results All data are validated by formulas and equations that confirm continuously their consistency. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data The same stated in the above item 3.4.1. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs The same stated in the above item 3.2.1. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses Disseminated data are of a final nature, so no regular revision studies are needed. Most of methodological changes resulted from the IMF’s recommendations, and they are highlighted through footnotes. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses As stated in the previous items there is no revisions, therefore no dissemination is needed. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request Upon request, the MFD provides non-published but non-confidential information.
