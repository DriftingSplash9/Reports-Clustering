 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Compilation of Government Finance Statistics for Bosnia and Herzegovina is done in accordance with concepts of GFSM 2014, and three out of four GFS analytical framework reports are compiled and disseminated, namely Statement of Government Operations, Balance Sheet Statement and Other Economic Flows Statement, while Statement On Sources and Uses of Cash is still not being compiled for Bosnia and Herzegovina. The structure of Statement of Government Operations is as given: Revenues Expenses Gross Operational Balance Transactions in Nonfinancial Assets Net Lending/Borrowing Transaction in Financial Assets Transactions in Financial Liabilities Data on annual level is the most detailed data that can be obtained based on the current legally set of financial reports including gross balance sheet. Gross balance sheet reports includes both stocks and transaction for all categories (nonfinancial and financial) Transactions data for Amortization and CFC-Consumption of fixed Capital are missing due to lack of source data in annual as well as quarterly reports. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Classification of General Government Sector is done in accordance with GFSM 2014 and ESA 2010 standards, and is constituted of: Central Government Sector, Social securoty funds, and Local Government Sector. General Government Sector is constituted of: Budgetary Central Government Sector which includes budgetary units of: BH Institutions, two entities: Republic of Srpska and Federation of Bosnia and Herzegovina as well as ten Cantons within Federation of Bosnia and Herzegovina, and Brcko District, Social Security Funds Sector which includes Funds for Employment of disabled persons of both entities Republic of Srpska and Federation of Bosnia and Herzegovina, Employment Funds and Health Care Funds on Entities, Cantonal and Brcko District level, Child Protection Fund of Republic of Srpska, and Other central government bodies which includes Public Enterprises for Highways of both entities Republic of Srpska and Federation of Bosnia and Herzegovina, Public Enterprises for Road Maintenance of two entities Republic of Srpska and Federation of Bosnia and Herzegovina as well as two Public Enterprises for Road Maintenance on Cantonal level. Local Government Sector is constituted of administrations and budgetary users on municipal and city level. 2.2.1 Scope Classification of General Government Sector is done in accordance with GFSM 2014 and ESA 2010 standards, and is constituted of: Central Government Sector, Social securoty funds, and Local Government Sector. General Government Sector is constituted of: Budgetary Central Government Sector which includes budgetary units of: BH Institutions, two entities: Republic of Srpska and Federation of Bosnia and Herzegovina as well as ten Cantons within Federation of Bosnia and Herzegovina, and Brcko District, Social Security Funds Sector which includes Funds for Employment of disabled persons of both entities Republic of Srpska and Federation of Bosnia and Herzegovina, Employment Funds and Health Care Funds on Entities, Cantonal and Brcko District level, Child Protection Fund of Republic of Srpska, and Other central government bodies which includes Public Enterprises for Highways of both entities Republic of Srpska and Federation of Bosnia and Herzegovina, Public Enterprises for Road Maintenance of two entities Republic of Srpska and Federation of Bosnia and Herzegovina as well as two Public Enterprises for Road Maintenance on Cantonal level. Local Government Sector is constituted of administrations and budgetary users on municipal and city level. Scope of the data Data on annual level covers all units which are currently classiffied in General Government Sector. Total number of units classified as general government data are 54 units both for GFSM 2014 and ESA 2010. The national statistical office is responsible for defining, managing and publishing the Official Sector Classification under the ESA 2010 methodology in collaboration with two Entity statistical offices, and currently there is a list of units that should be included in the government sector. However, it still remains unclear which institution should be responsible for implementation of sectorization. The general government sector is currently composed of three sub-sectors: Central government, Social security funds and local government. Exceptions to coverage The exception to coverage exists as mentioned under 2.2.1.1. Unrecorded activity Grants in kind in revenues side are also not fully covered, because in national accounting practice they are treated only as changes in stocks but not transactions. Amortization- CFC in Expenses is missing in Statement of Government Operations due to lack of reliable data sources. Additionally various off balance sheet items are not included due to their unavailability. 2.4.1 Valuation Current market prices are used to value revenue, expenses and transactions in non-financial assets i.e. transactions are recorded on the basis of their value at the time of the underlying economic event, which, for exchange transactions, generally corresponds to market prices. Face and nominal value is used to value transactions in financial assets and liabilities. 2.4.2 Recording basis Data are recorded on Accrual (Expense and Transaction in non-financial assets) and Cash (Revenue) basis. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures The annual data are presented on a fully consolidated basis, that is, transactions between the various items of central government budget have been eliminated from the data during the process of consolidation, as well between different subsectors. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Source data for compiling of GFS for central government, social security funds and local govermnment are: budget execution reports along with other legally prescribed reports cover all monetary transaction of unit reported. For some units on annual level accounting data are also available with data breakdown that is much more detailed then in budget execution reports i.e. certain units add gross balance sheet to previous set of data (all budgetary units, 14 MoFs regularly deliver gross balance sheet reports). For Public Enterprises for Road and Highways source data are financial statements, profit and loss accounts, balance sheets and other reports defined by law. As additional data sources for some types of transactions and cross-checking purposes, CBBH GFS unit uses: IndirectTax authority data on collected indiricted taxes, Report of foreign debt from BH Ministry of finance and treasury covering Government foreign debt, disbursements, payments and debt outstanding by creditors 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Source data consists of Gross Balance sheets, periodical reports of reporting units and additional information that are provided on demand /clarification basis. Time of recording in source data is not always in the line with international acceptable standards. This also applies for classification of units as well as for valuations. Source data for revenues, expenses and transactions in non-financial assets cover all monetary transactions of all units included in General Government Sector. Non-monetary transactions often require additional investigation of different data sources, counterpart information, and sometimes data adjustments. Classification of transaction requires substantial reclassifications, especially in sales of goods and services in revenues and treatments of use of goods and services and social transfers in expenses. Valuation of financial stocks and flows, i.e. transactions in financial assets and liabilities is diversified. Financial instruments are given on market or nominal/face value basis. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Annual Source data delivery deadline is 90 days after the end of referent year. 3.2.1 Source data assessment Source data are based on budget accounting and budget execution reports as legally binding forms of reporting and there is a regular statistical assessments and monitoring of source data. Only for sectorization issues assessment of all units included in General Government Sector is done on irregular basis. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Except for reclassifying transactions within and between categories, in accordance with GFSM 2014, no other statistical technique is used. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures If significant discrepancy between different data sources occurs, further research is carried out to perform data adjustment, time of recording and sometimes to adjust type of valuations (especially for government securities). 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results Validation of intermediate results are applied as follows : Mathematical data validation of totals to be equal to subtotals on level of each unit and on level of each sector and subsector. Logical data validation by types of revenues and expenses and its attribution to sectors and subsectors. Cross-checking of different source data, and if there are substantial discrepancies investigation of possible transactions unrecorded, coverage and scope issues, valuation and time of recording issues. Statistical discrepancies between transactions in revenues, expenses and nonfinancial transactions on one side, and financial assets and liabilities on other side, which can result in data adjustments mostly in expenses side (practices of deviation of accrual to cash treatment of expenses), adjustments of transactions in financial assets and liabilities, mostly unrecorded, in order to narrow discrepancy. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data The intermediate data series compiled by the Bank in accordance with GFSM 2014 methodology are broadly consistent with related data series produced by the MoFs according to the national methodology. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs There are number of differences between GFSM 2014 data and data received from Monetary and financial statistics. The difference/discrepancies are mainly caused sectorization issues, and wrong classification of units within existing sectorization. Discrepancies are analyzed jointly with MFS unit, and where possible corrections are made. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses No revision studies are carried out, but data is regularly revised and updated.
