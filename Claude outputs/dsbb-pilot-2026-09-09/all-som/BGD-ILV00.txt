 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Gross international reserves including monetary gold, foreign exchange, SDRs, and Reserve Position in the Fund are compiled according to the guidelines recommended in the sixth edition of the IMF "Balance of Payments Manual" . International reserves are compiled on daily basis. Reserve templates are prepared and disseminated on monthly basis. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The classification used to compile the statistics is generally in line with the IMF's “Balance of Payments Manual”, Sixth Edition (BPM6). 2.2.1 Scope The classification used to compile the statistics is generally in line with the IMF's “Balance of Payments Manual”, Sixth Edition (BPM6). Scope of the data Information on international reserves is produced and disseminated in millions of U.S. dollars and crores of Taka. Reserves consist of foreign assets monetary gold, foreign exchange, SDRs, and Reserve Position in the Fund as per BPM6. Exceptions to coverage Nil. Unrecorded activity Nil. 2.4.1 Valuation Revaluations of foreign exchange positions and gold holdings are calculated daily. Data are presented in crores of taka (1 crore = 10 million). Gross international reserves cover only the foreign assets of the Bangladesh Bank. Daily data on reserves, covering monetary gold and foreign exchange, are prepared for internal government use. The reserves data in terms of US dollars are converted into taka at the weighted average interbank rates of the Taka vis-à-vis the US dollar. Reserves in other currencies are converted into taka using relevant cross rates to the US dollar. 2.4.2 Recording basis Recording of reserve assets fully meets accrual accounting procedure. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures Internationally accepted standards and guidelines are followed in grossing and netting procedures. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Data is sourced primarily from Bangladesh Bank’s records. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording The major part of the International reserves data are extracted from administrative records which are based on actual transactions. These are broadly in agreement with the definitions, valuations, classifications and time of recording as recommended in the IMF's “Balance of Payments Manual”, Sixth Edition (BPM6). 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Daily, before the close of business. 3.2.1 Source data assessment Source data is verified from administrative records which are based on actual transactions. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Not applicable. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Not applicable. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results Not applicable. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data Not applicable. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs Separate estimation of aggregate transactions are compared with data coming from the accounting system, and any discrepancies queried. If any discrepancy or other problems are found in statistical outputs, they are rectified at the revision stage. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses If there is any revision, is explained and brought to the notice of all data compilers as well as users. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses Not applicable. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request Not applicable.
