 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Formation of foreign trade statistics is carried out in accordance with: the law of the Republic of Uzbekistan "On Official Statistics"; "International Merchandise Trade Statistics Concepts and Definitions-2010"; "Manual on Statistics of International Trade in Services-2010"; "International Recommendations for Tourism Statistics 2008"; "Regulation on the methodology for collecting and processing the data of state and customs foreign trade statistics", approved by the Resolution of the Statistics Agency (No. 11), of the State Customs Committee (No. 01-02/15-23), of the Ministry of Economy (No. 31) and of the Ministry of Foreign Economic Relations, Investments and trade (No. EG-01/10-4715) dated August 27, 2007 (reg. No. 1717 dated September 20, 2007). When forming the foreign trade statistics, the following methodological documents are used as a guide: - Methodological provisions for the organization of statistical accounting of foreign trade in goods - No. 2, dated February 28, 2020; - Methodological provisions for the organization of statistical accounting of foreign trade in services - No. 1, dated January 19, 2018. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization For the generalization and aggregation of information on foreign trade, a classifier of countries of the world is used, prepared by the Statistical Committee of the Commonwealth of Independent States (CIS) on the basis of the official publication of the United Nations Secretariat (UN) "Standard codes of countries or areas for use in statistics". For the classification and coding of goods in the statistics of foreign economic relations, the classifiers "Commodity nomenclature of foreign economic activity of the Republic of Uzbekistan", built on the basis of the Nomenclature of the harmonized system for describing and coding the goods (Harmonized System 2017) , "Standard International Trade Classification 2008", "Classification by Broad Economic Categories" are used. When forming the statistical data on export-import of services, the names of elementary types of services and their aggregated groups correspond to the classification of the Manual on Statistics of International Trade in Services. 2.2.1 Scope For the generalization and aggregation of information on foreign trade, a classifier of countries of the world is used, prepared by the Statistical Committee of the Commonwealth of Independent States (CIS) on the basis of the official publication of the United Nations Secretariat (UN) "Standard codes of countries or areas for use in statistics". For the classification and coding of goods in the statistics of foreign economic relations, the classifiers "Commodity nomenclature of foreign economic activity of the Republic of Uzbekistan", built on the basis of the Nomenclature of the harmonized system for describing and coding the goods (Harmonized System 2017) , "Standard International Trade Classification 2008", "Classification by Broad Economic Categories" are used. When forming the statistical data on export-import of services, the names of elementary types of services and their aggregated groups correspond to the classification of the Manual on Statistics of International Trade in Services. Scope of the data Foreign trade statistics are formed from foreign trade in goods and services: the initial data in the formation of statistics on foreign trade in goods are the data of customs statistics of the State Customs Committee of the Republic of Uzbekistan based on the indicators of the cargo customs declaration (CCD) and the data of statistical observations about goods, the ownership right of which has passed from a resident to a non-resident, without crossing the border of the country and not taken into account in customs declarations; the formation of statistics on foreign trade in services is carried out on the basis of the data of statistical observations. Exceptions to coverage There are no exceptions . Unrecorded activity There is no. 2.4.1 Valuation The valuation of the cost of goods is made in US dollars and national currency at the prices of contracts or covenants indicated in the cargo customs declarations. The cost of services and fulfilled works is taken into account in US dollars and national currency at the prices of contracts or covenants, with a recalculation at the rate of the Central Bank of the Republic of Uzbekistan (regardless of the currency of the contract) at the time of drawing up the act of fulfilled works and services. 2.4.2 Recording basis The time of recording the transactions is made using the accrual-based accounting method. Transactions show up in at the moment when an exchange, transfer, transformation, creation or disappearance of economic values happen. This point in time may differ from the point of the actual payment. Transactions with goods show up in at the time of transfer of ownership right of them, services show up in at the time when they are provided. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures The procedures for calculation the gross/net indicators are carried out in accordance with the Balance of Payments and International Investment Position Manual, sixth edition (BPM 6). 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The object of statistical observation is exported and imported goods. The unit of statistical observation is the legal entity or person, carrying out the foreign economic activity. CCD indicators are used for forming the statistics on foreign trade in goods. Data on the export-import of goods, the ownership right of which has passed from a resident to a non-resident, without crossing the border of the country and not taken into account in customs declarations and customs statistics (i.e. they are placed in the export mode without their actual export outside the Republic of Uzbekistan) are taken into account based on monthly statistical observations. The formation of summary final data on foreign trade in services is carried out: - monthly on the basis of statistical reporting of large enterprises and statistical assessment of exports (imports) of services of small enterprises and micro-firms and of the data from sample surveys of inbound and outbound tourism; - annually on the basis of statistical reporting covering the large, small enterprises and micro-firms and the data from sample surveys of inbound and outbound tourism. The volume of exports and imports of tourism is determined based on the results of surveys conducted by specially trained interviewers according to the forms of statistical observation approved by the Statistics Agency (survey questionnaire). 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Goods are taken into account at the time of importation or exportation outside the economic territory of the country, when the right of ownership passes. The source of primary information on export-import transactions with services are commercial contracts, on the basis of which all transactions are carried out, as well as invoices issued for performed services, as well as registration records of monetary and financial authorities. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness The data collection program is regulated by the statistical program. Respondents are informed of the established deadlines for submitting the reporting. Information on goods formed according to cargo customs declarations is provided by the State Customs Committee on a monthly basis until the 7 th day after the reporting month. Data on services from large enterprises are collected monthly not later than the 5 th day of the month following the reporting period and for small enterprises and micro-firms – annually not later than the 27 th of February. 3.2.1 Source data assessment The compilers of foreign trade statistics systematically carry out the assessment of initial data and, if the necessity arises, initiate the process of modifying them in order to improve the statistical reporting and other observations. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques The cost of imports is estimated in CIF prices – the cost of the goods and the expenses for its insurance and transportation to the border of the importing country. The cost of exports is estimated at FOB prices – the cost of goods and expenses for transportation to the border of the exporting country. Taking into account the geographical location of Uzbekistan and the absence of maritime borders, the FOB price will be equal to DAF price. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures In the foreign trade in goods, adjustments for seasonal fluctuations are not made. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results The data received from the respondents are analyzed and undergo the mandatory control. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data If there are any discrepancies and unexplained large fluctuations in data, their causes are investigated during the direct contact with the respondent. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs Statistical inconsistencies are monitored periodically. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses The trade statistics are not revised. Periodic reviews ensure the adequacy and consistency of the data. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses The statistical collections indicate that separate indicators are being revised for the previous year. The revised (final) data are disseminated through publications of the Statistics Agency and on the official website ( www.stat.uz ). 5.1.5 Dissemination on request According to the Law of the Republic of Uzbekistan "On Official Statistics", the " Statistics Agency under the President of the Republic of Uzbekistan ", the Regulations for the organization of works on the preparation and dissemination of statistical information and other regulatory documents, statistical data are provided to users upon their request in accordance with the established procedure. The dissemination of statistical information is carried out by providing upon the written request of legal or physical entities in the form of printed or electronic spreadsheets, statistical collections, bulletins, express information, as well as by providing in accordance with the agreements of the parties on information interaction.
