 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The concepts and definitions used by the BSP in compiling monetary statistics are consistent with the IMF's Monetary and Financial Statistics Manual and Compilation Guide. The CBS is a consolidation of the BSP balance sheet and the accounts of the Central Government (CG) related to its monetary functions and transactions with the IMF. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The accounts are classified according to transactors or institutional units and residence. The foreign and domestic positions are distinguished based on residence, following the guidelines of the IMF's BPM6, and are segregated into four (4) major categories: Net Foreign Assets (NFA), Domestic Claims (DC), Reserve Money (RM); and Liabilities Other Than Reserve Money. Scope of the data The CBS statistics cover the consolidation of the BSP balance sheet and the Central Government accounts related to its monetary functions and transactions with the IMF. Data are presented in terms of stocks and flows, with the latter calculated as net changes between two stock periods. Exceptions to coverage Not applicable. Unrecorded activity Not applicable 2.4.1 Valuation The CBS is compiled in Philippine Peso (PHP). Foreign currency-denominated accounts are translated to PHP using the real-time exchange rate at reporting date. The financial assets are classified and measured at amortized cost, fair value through revaluation reserves (FVRR), and fair value through other comprehensive income (FVOCI), in compliance with the Philippine Financial Reporting Standard (PFRS) 9 – Financial Instruments as aligned with the provisions of R.A. 7653, as amended by R.A. 11211 (BSP Charter). Revaluation of financial assets, as well as financial liabilities due to change in price and foreign exchange rate, is recognized accordingly. 2.4.2 Recording basis The CBS statistics are based on the BSP balance sheet, which uses the accrual method of accounting. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures The Financial Assets (Due from Banks, Investments, Loans, and advances, etc.) are recorded in gross amounts with a separate allowance for impairment losses and doubtful accounts. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The CBS data are sourced from the accounting records of the BSP's Financial Accounting Department (FAD), Financial Markets (FM), Department of Loans and Credit (DLC), and the Financial Supervision Sector (FSS). 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording The data sourced from various BSP departments generally conform with the guidelines set forth in the IMF's Monetary and Financial Statistics Manual and Compilation Guide. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness The data inputs are received monthly. 3.2.1 Source data assessment The data in the CBS are validated against official financial records of the BSP, details on counterparties, currencies, and values are maintained by the BSP's FAD, FM, DLC, and FSS. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques The CBS is based on actual data from the BSP's FAD, FM, DLC, and FSS. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Not applicable 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results The data are validated by checking the consistency of the details sourced from different reports. Concerned staff, along with senior officers, review the statistical tables, which are then approved by the Deputy Governor of the Monetary and Economics Sector (MES) before their release to the public. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data The data are regularly monitored and assessed by both the BSP staff and senior officers to ensure accuracy. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs The noted discrepancies in data series are validated with the data providers and adjustments are made as necessary. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses The technical notes, explanatory notes, or footnotes on major data revisions are released to the public through the BSP website. However, no revision studies are currently undertaken. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses Not applicable. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request Not applicable. 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points The contact details are available on the BSP website under Contact Us. Any correspondence sent through the contact addresses is forwarded to concerned staff for appropriate action. 5.3.2 Availability of documents and services catalogs The subscription to the SPEI and other publications of the BSP, including their charges, can be viewed at the Media and Research webpage of the BSP website, particularly under the Publications and Reports tab, which can be accessed through https://www.bsp.gov.ph/SitePages/MediaAndResearch/PublicationsAndReports.aspx.
