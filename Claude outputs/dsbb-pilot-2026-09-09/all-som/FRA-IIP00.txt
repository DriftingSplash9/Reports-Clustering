 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The international investment position is the balance sheet of the stock of external financial assets and liabilities. The French international investment position is compiledaccording to the conceptual framework of the sixth edition of the IMF Balance of Payments Manual (BPM6). Annual and quarterly stocks of external assets and liabilities are presented under the following data categories, also used in the balance of payments: direct investment, portfolio investment, financial derivatives, other investment, and reserve assets. This makes it possible to reconcile balance of payments financial flows with the stocks recorded in the international investment position. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Classification system : French external assets and liabilities are classified according the standard components identified in the BPM6. Additional breakdowns (geographical and sectoral) for certain components are published in Banque de France website : https://www.banque-france.fr/en/statistics/balance-payments/balance-payments-and-internati onal-investment-position Scope of the data Coverage of external financial assets and liabilities : The coverage is in line with guidelines provided by the IMF. Geographical coverage : The economic territory of France for IIP statistics includes metropolitan France, the Overseas Departments (Martinique Guadeloupe, Guyane, Réunion and Mayotte) and territorial units(Saint Pierre et Miquelon, Saint Barthélemy and Saint Martin), to which is added the Principality of Monaco. Exceptions to coverage Coverage of external financial assets and liabilities: Securities held by non-financial sector in custody with non-resident banks are not currently reported. Periodical checkswith accounting data or other sources are performed to verify that this exception has no significant statistical impact. Portfolio investment:Foreign securities held abroad by resident non-banks and French securities held abroad by nonresidents are currently not reported. Periodical checks with accounting data or other sources are performed to verify that this exception has no significant statistical impact 2.4.1 Valuation Following guidelines of the BPM6, stocks are valued at market prices when available. In particular, for non-listed companiesdirect investment stocks are valuedat book value.The book value used is the value of own funds as indicated on the liability side of the balance sheets of the direct investment companies, multiplied by the percentage of ownership of the direct investor. Portfolio investment stocks are valued at market prices Financial derivatives: Valuation at market prices is required. Reserve assets:Positions are valued at market prices, in line with the bookkeeping practice of revaluing reserve assets. The same applies for gold Conversion to unit of account: The exchange rate prevailing on the day to which the position data relate is used to convert foreign currencies into the unit of account. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures Financial derivatives : Data are recorded on a gross basis (assets/liabilities). 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Direct investment : Data on outward direct investment stocks of equity capital (including reinvested earnings) are collected via an annual survey conducted by the regional branches of Banque de France, which gather information on companies located within their respective areas. Data on inward direct investment stocks of equity capital (including reinvested earnings) are compiled using different databases, first to identify direct investment companies and then to get the needed accounting data on these companies. Data on both outward and inward direct investment stocks of other capital are collected through a quarterly survey of large insurance and non-financial companies and an annual survey of small and medium companies. Portfolio investment : Portfolio investment stocks are compiled security by security from a quarterly survey of resident custodians which report both French securities hold by nonresidents and foreign securities hold by residents. Liabilities vis-à-vis nonresidents on bonds issued by French residents (except bonds issued by general government) are calculated by subtracting the amount held by residents (reported by resident custodians) from the total amount outstanding (available in the security database managed by the compiler). Financial derivatives : Stocks of financial derivatives are mostly derived from banks' balance sheets. As from January 2011, a new data collection (CRT) from financial intermediaries isused. Other investment: Stocks of monetary authorities and of the general government sector are reported directly by entities included in these categories. Stocks of monetary and financial institutions (MFIs) are derived from banks' balance sheets and reports from the monetary mutual funds. Stocks of other sectors (including deposits, loans and trade credits) are compiled from quarterly or annual surveys of companies. Reserve assets : Stocks are reported directly by Banque de France as monetary authority. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Direct investment : Geographical and sectoral breakdowns are only available for stocks at book value. Data on both outward and inward direct investment stocks of other capital are collected through a quarterly survey of large insurance and non-financial companies and an annual survey of small and medium companies. Portfolio investment : Geographical breakdowns are compiled, on the asset side, according to the country of residence of the issuer. On the liability side, the geographical breakdown is based on the residency of the first counterpart. The quarterly stock includes the value of accrued interest but not yet payable. Reserve assets : Reserve assets include non-euro area currency denominated claims vis-à-vis non residents of the euro area, monetary gold, special drawing rights and reserve position in the IMF. Compared to the BPM6 definition of official reserves, reserve assets do not include non-euro area currency denominated claims vis-à-vis residents of the euro area. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Direct investment : Data on inward direct investment stocks of equity capital and reinvested earnings are compiled using different databases, first to identify direct investment companies and then to get the needed accounting data on these companies. Portfolio investment : the survey gives information on temporary sales or purchases of securities, so that this impact of these transactions on stocks is corrected. As a consequence, the reconciliation between flows and stocks is ensured. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses All publications indicate the most important changes in methodology – in particular, the “Balance of Payments and the International Investment Position” annual report.
