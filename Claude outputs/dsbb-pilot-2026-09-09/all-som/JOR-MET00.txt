 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The data are compiled in broad conformity with international practice and in accordance with definitions recommended by the United Nations for foreign trade statistics, specifically in accordance with the International Merchandise Trade Statistics: Concepts and Definitions, Series M, No. 52, Rev. 2 (New York: United Nations, 1998). The collection of the data and processing of these statistical frameworks follow methodological guidelines that may not fully accord with the definitions and concepts of the UN’s International Merchandise Trade Statistics (IMTS). These difference are clearly articulated, as for example in the balance of payments data, adjustments are made to customs-based trade statistics to facilitate coverage in accordance with the IMF’s Balance of Payments Manual , fifth edition ( BPM5 ). These include revaluation of the trade data on a free on board (f.o.b) basis, and corrections to the customs-based data to exclude goods associated with passive or active processing. A series of adjustments (supplementary trade items) allow for reconcilability of BOP and merchandise trade statistics. Monetary gold are excluded from foreign trade statistics but are included in the BOP. The calculation of the foreign trade data according to the national accounts methods uses general trade data as the original foreign trade data. In contrast to the special trade, the general trade also includes imports of goods for storage and exports of goods from storage. For the use of the national accounts the movements of goods for and from storage by foreign entities is deducted. In addition, the imports are revalued from c.i.f into f.o.b. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Since 1994, the Harmonized System with correlation tables linked to SITC Rev. 3 is used. 2.2.1 Scope Since 1994, the Harmonized System with correlation tables linked to SITC Rev. 3 is used. Scope of the data The data are compiled on the basis of the special trade system, wherein the customs border is also the statistical border, meaning that they include only the commodities that are finally released through customs. However, the Department of Statistics also compiles and disseminates a monthly trade balance according to general trade system. The balance covers the free zones’ data which are available as totals. Transactions coverage: For exports, country of destination; for imports country of both origin and consignment are used. Imports include (1) goods entering directly from abroad, to be distributed on the local market for consumption or use by local industries excluding free zones; (2) goods entering directly from abroad and delivered under bond to factories for processing; and (3) goods imported under bond. Exports include (1) direct exports of Jordanian goods; (2) goods leaving in-bond factories and exported abroad; and (3) foreign goods re-exported without processing after entering the country (settlement of import duties). Exceptions to coverage Trade statistics do not include: Imports and exports of Jordanian free zones with the rest of the world (transactions between the free zones and the rest of Jordan national economy are included); Commodities in Customs warehouses which are not released to the local market; Military commodities for security purposes; Commodities released for exhibitions or for repair that do not include change of ownership; and Temporary admission and drawback of exports and imports. Unrecorded activity There are no estimates for unrecorded activities. 2.4.1 Valuation Valuation: imports are recorded cost of insurance and freight (c.i.f); exports and re-exports are recorded free on board (f.o.b). Transaction prices identified on customs forms are assumed to be reflective of purchasers' price. 2.4.2 Recording basis Conversion to unit of account: currency exchange rate is estimated as monthly average as announced by the Central Bank of Jordan. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures Not applicable. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The main source of imports and exports data are customs declarations. Additional sources are the following: Ministry of Oil for oil & oil products trade; General Organization for Investment & Free Zones for free zones trade (totals); Customs Drawback & Temporary Admission Trade (totals); and Monthly reports on the Exports & Imports of large National Companies. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Customs data are based on the special system of trade; thus change of ownership is assumed to take place at the time the goods clear the customs border. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness The Department of Statistics receives the customs declarations on a daily basis for data entry. Other data sources are monthly, allowing for the timely compilation of foreign trade statistics in line with SDDS timeliness requirements. 3.2.1 Source data assessment The Department of Statistics is in regular contact with Customs to be apprised of any developments that would impact coverage of the source data. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques ASYCUDA is employed for the trade data processing system. The system includes automated validation checks, and also a valuation range edit check for all imports and exports transactions. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results Data are reconciled, to the extent possible with the international transactions reporting system (ITRS) data compiled by the Central Bank of Jordan for the balance of payments. Mirror exercises are also done to compare the data with those of partner countries. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data Reasons of discrepancies are well known; most are due to the application of special system of trade. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses Studies of data revisions are conducted annually and are used to improve statistical processes. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses Analyses of revisions are not published. 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points The Department of Statistics website ( www.dos.gov.jo ) provides a general contact section. All statistical publications also provide general contact information. 5.3.2 Availability of documents and services catalogs An online catalog of publications and CD ROMS, including information on charges are available at www.dos.gov.jo (click on "publications").
