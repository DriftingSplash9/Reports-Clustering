 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Analytical framework and concepts Data are published quarterly and is broken down by instruments, maturity (short term/ long term) and institutional sectors: General government, Central bank, Deposit-taking corporations, except the central bank and Other sectors (Other financial corporations, Nonfinancial corporations, Households and nonprofit institutions serving households (NPISHs)).The technical reports are disseminated on the webpage of INDEC. https://www.indec.gob.ar/ Definitions: Gross external debt, at any given time, is the outstanding amount of those not contingent liabilities that require payment of principal and/or interest by the debtor at some point in the future and that are owed to nonresidents by residents of an economy. A resident is an individual or a legal entity whose main economic interest or activity is located within the territory of the country. As a general principle, an enterprise is resident in an economic territory when the enterprise is engaged in a significant amount of production of goods or services from a location in the territory during a year or more. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Classifications: External Debt (ED) is estimated essentially following international standards contained in the sixth edition of the International Monetary Fund's (IMF) Balance of Payments and International Investment Position Manual (BPM6) and the External Debt Statistics: guide for compilers and users. The short- and long-term attributions of transactions in the General government, Central bank, Deposit-taking corporations, except the Central bank and Other sectors are made according to the original maturity of the financial instrument. Scope of the data Coverage of external debt: The estimates attempt to include all of Argentina's foreign debt liabilities. All institutional sectors are covered. Geographical coverage: The geographical coverage corresponds to the whole national economic territory. 2.4.1 Valuation Conversion to unit of account: ED is compiled in U.S. dollars. The stock positions denominated in other currencies are converted into dollars at the prevailing exchange rate at the time of the transaction. Valuation: Stocks are valued at nominal value and at market prices. 2.4.2 Recording basis The accrual basis is used to elaborate the external debt data following BPM6 indications. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The General government data are obtained from the Secretariat of Finance (reported by SIGADE program), a survey about external debt of local governments, and Caja de Valores S.A. The Central bank data are obtained from Caja de Valores S.A and from the Banco Central de la República Argentina (BCRA). The Deposit-taking corporations, except the Central Bank data are obtained from the balance sheet of entities and from survey on external assets and liabilities of the private sector of the BCRA. For the Other sectors are obtained from survey on external assets and liabilities of the private sector of the BCRA since 1Q2020. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Overall, the source data for compiling the external debt are timely. 3.2.1 Source data assessment Preliminary estimates are cross checked with administrative data. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures No seasonal adjustment to data is made. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results Data are also routinely compared with other available information such as company reports and press information, various administrative records, and monetary and fiscal statistics. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data Consistency procedures are practiced during data collection and compilation, while the consistency between position data and Balance of Payments (BOP) flows is monitored and published in the International Investment Position (IIP) statistics. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs Whenever problems are detected in data collection process, the data are revised to improve the estimates of ED. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses Major revisions are expained and informed to the public. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request Only the disseminated data are available to users. There are no further breakdowns of the data available to users than those published. Any interested party can request to be added to the mailing list for updates on new publications.
