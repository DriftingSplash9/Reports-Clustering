The index weights are based on the census of manufacturing establishments (CME) 2006/07, and includes 20 three-digit industry groups. Altogether 34 commodities were selected under these three-digit industry groups. Exceptions to coverage The commodities which are not included in the index are due to insignificant contribution to total census value added. None 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Data on the quantity of production is collected through the "Quarterly Survey of Manufacturing Establishment" conducted by the Central Bureau of Statistics. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques The base year and the reference year of the index is 2006/07. The weights of the various industries in the basket refer to census output. The weights are derived from the 2006/07census of manufacturing establishments employing ten or more persons. The weights were assigned at two stages. First, census output weights were determined for three-digits ISIC industry groups. At the second stage, the weight of a particular three-digit industry group is allocated to selected commodities of that industry group based on the relative shares of total value of shipments of the selected commodities in the total output of that industry group. The sample of establishments was selected from the list based on 2006/07census of manufacturing establishments. All large establishments are included while a sample of medium establishments is taken following probability proportional size (PPS) sampling technique. None
