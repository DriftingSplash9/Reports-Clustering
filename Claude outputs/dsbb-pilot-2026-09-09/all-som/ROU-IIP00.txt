 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Analytical Framework: · The underlying conceptual framework for the international investment position disseminated is in line with the recommendations of the International Monetary Fund Balance of Payments Manual ( BPM6) sixth edition. In what BPM6 is concerned, Romania as EU member state applies ECB Guideline ECB/2011/23 and Commission Regulation no.184/2005 (with subsequent amendments). The dissemination is currently made under BPM6 format and methodology . Data on end-of-period stocks of external financial assets and liabilities is presented under the following categories: direct investment, portfolio investment broken down into securities providing for participation in capital and debt securities, financial derivatives, other investment, and reserve assets. Standard components consist of the beginning and the end of period positions of external assets and liabilities, and changes in positions during the period associated with transactions, exchange rate changes, price changes, and other adjustments, namely quarterly changes in volume, quarterly changes in prices and quarterly other changes. Geographical breakdown is prepared starting with 2007 annual and quarterly data for all IIP components; Romania's IIP data is produced and disseminated on a quarterly basis by the NBR Statistics Department approximately 45 days after the end of the reference quarter. Definition: International Investment Position is the statistical statement of the value and composition of the stock of the Romania's external financial assets and liabilities at the end of the reference period (quarter, year). In the case of government debt data is compiled taking into account the recommendations of ESA2010 Manual on government deficit and debt – 2013 Edition. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Classification: The IIP data are largely consistent with the recommendations of the International Monetary Fund's Balance of Payments Manual, sixth edition (BPM6): Direct Investment Direct Investment – assets Direct Investment – liabilities Portfolio Investment Equity and investment fund shares Debt Instruments Financial derivatives Other investment Trade Credits and advances Loans Currency and Deposits Insurance technical reserves SDR allocations Other assets/liabilities Reserves 2.2.1 Scope Classification: The IIP data are largely consistent with the recommendations of the International Monetary Fund's Balance of Payments Manual, sixth edition (BPM6): Direct Investment Direct Investment – assets Direct Investment – liabilities Portfolio Investment Equity and investment fund shares Debt Instruments Financial derivatives Other investment Trade Credits and advances Loans Currency and Deposits Insurance technical reserves SDR allocations Other assets/liabilities Reserves Scope of the data Romania's IIP covers almost all residents' external financial assets and liabilities. Resident institutional units are defined according to BPM6 concepts of economic territory and center of economic interest. An integrated statement of external statistics is reconciling IIP with BOP by allocating changes in IIP stock data to financial account transactions, valuation effects resulting from exchange rate and market price movements as well as other adjustments. Data in line with BPM6 is available back to 2007. Geographical coverage: International Investment Position covers operations with nonresidents carried out on the territory of Romania (including the Romanian embassies). Exceptions to coverage For portfolio investment, data coverage is partial since the information from external custodians is not totally available. For improving the coverage some methods are applied, it's the case of domestic bonds where the difference between issued bonds and holdings of residents are allocated to non-residents, IMF-CPIS (Liability annexes) geographical and sector breakdown being used for the compilation of the data. Households' transactions are partially covered, since ITRS was abandoned by the end of 2016. Real estate operations are not currently included. Data related to households' deposits held abroad is brought from BIS locational statistics. New data sources to improve household sector position estimates shall be deemed in the near future. Currently, the real estate mirror data exchanged between European countries on an annual basis, regarding total holdings of real estate by non-residents in Romania and total holdings of real estate by residents abroad were added to the FDI stock data starting with 2020. Unrecorded activity Some households' positions, no SPEs identified, part of residents' direct investment abroad – real estate. 2.4.1 Valuation Valuation: Until June 2003, IIP data was shown in millions of US dollars and starting with June 2003, data has been compiled and published in millions of euro. Outstanding positions are based on amounts received or paid, adjusted to reflect exchange rates at the end of the reporting period. Arrears relating to claims (i.e. loans) on past communist regimes (transferable ruble and US dollar debt) and dividends due for payments but unpaid are also included in the IIP statement. In case of portfolio investment the positions are all assessed at the market prices. Market prices for foreign instruments and domestic bonds issued on external capital market are uploaded into the local system from Centralized Securities Database (CSDB), whereas Bucharest Stock Exchange supplies monthly information about domestic instruments. Direct investment transactions and positions of listed FDI companies equity valuation is made at market value while unlisted FDI companies equity flows and stocks are recorded at book value; adjustments are made in case of significant differences between acquisition price and book value (for example in case of an important amount in goodwill). Reserves assets are recorded in IIP at market value, based on data from NBR's Market Operations Department. 2.4.2 Recording basis The basis of recording outstanding positions is accrual principle for almost the instruments either asset or liability side (FDI, PI, OI). The accrual principle applied for all sectors except central bank and credit institutions for which the balance sheet is used. The same for households. Transactions are recorded when change of ownership occurs, in line with BPM6 recommendations, allowing for stock positions to update accordingly. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures Grossing-up procedures are broadly consistent with the BPM6. Financial account referred positions are FDI quarterly and/or annually surveys as well as quarterly surveys on Other Financial Information on/to Nonresidents, other than FDI and Portfolio Investments (IFRN). 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Direct investment: Operational FDI stocks are supplied by IT application for IIP which compiles them based on data coming from the results of FDI annual and quarterly surveys. Data collected is fully consistent with BPM6 methodological standards. Provisional monthly data (available at t+45 days) is estimated based on historical data and revised once the survey results are available. The statistical population consists of about 7000 reporting entities, quarterly, and 8000-8500, annually, being derived from administrative sources in close cooperation with NIS. The results are cross-checked with administrative sources (balance sheets), with long-term external debt statistics via debt management financial analysis system (DMFAS) and with data monthly received from the National Trade Register regarding companies' equity. Foreign direct investment includes the paid-up capital and the reserves related to a non-resident investor holding at least 10 percent of the voting power or the subscribed share capital of a resident enterprise, the loans between the investor or the group to which the investor belongs and the direct investment enterprise, as well as the reinvested earnings. FDI also includes equity and loans from non-resident enterprises whose voting power or equity participation in the resident enterprise is less than 10 percent, but which belong to the same group as the direct investor in the resident company (fellow enterprises). Permanent debt between affiliated financial intermediaries (banks, non-bank financial institutions) is no longer considered as direct investment. Portfolio investment: Portfolio investment statistics is compiled on a security-by-security basis. The stocks and flows are recorded at market prices. On the liability side of the portfolio investment, the monthly information related to Romanian securities comes from various sources such as Romanian Stock Exchange, Financial Supervisory Authority, the Ministry of Finance, MFIs and IFs direct reporting, NBRs' Market Operations and Payments Departments. On the asset side of the portfolio investment the data about the securities issued by non-residents is extracted from the securities database called CSDB (Centralised Securities Database) managed at the level of ECB. Each month, information related to foreign instruments, provided by CSDB, such as issuer country, issuer sector, type of instrument, currency, amount issued or amount outstanding is used in compilation of portfolio investment. The market prices of both Romanian securities traded on external markets and non-resident securities are also obtained from CSDB. Data about stocks of held instruments are collected on a monthly basis through direct surveys. The reporting agents are the MFIs, IFs, FVCs, insurance corporations (ICs), Pension Funds (PFs) on their behalf and the custodians on behalf of their clients, other than the MFIs, IFs, FVCs, ICs, PFs. Flows included in portfolio investment statistics are derived as a difference between current and previous stocks. Price changes, exchange rate changes and other changes are also compiled at the level of National Bank. Data about listed or non-listed shares, fund units, Global Depositary Receipts (GDRs), Exchange-Traded Funds (ETFs), other equities, bonds and money market instruments are collected security by security (with or without ISIN codes). They are used for classification of financial instruments part of portfolio investment. Reporting gaps exist for Romanian debt instruments purchased by non-residents that are undertaken with external custodians. These gaps (total issued amount minus total held reported instruments) are currently included in external statistics. The breakdown by countries and institutional sectors is derived based on alternative statistics such as CPIS. An important achievement is related to security holding statistics information coming from security holding statistics database (SHSDB), managed by the ECB and Bundesbank, for the compilation of portfolio investment statistics and security statistics. Back-data were collected starting with December 2012, whereas the securities statistics have been available since July 2014. Financial derivatives : stocks shown in IIP are related to those supplied by MFIs, IFs OFIs balance sheets. In the future the information about financial derivatives supplied by quarterly statistical survey (IFRN) will be implemented. Other investment: Long-term stocks liability side comes from DMFAS (Debt Management and Financial Analysis System) for private and public loans. Starting with March 2016, the survey on other financial information vis-à-vis Nonresidents (IFRN) supplied IIP statement for other investment items, for both asset/liability side. The main source for resident entities deposits held abroad compilation is BIS locational statistics (asset side). Banking sector currency and deposits (asset/liability side) are derived from banks' reports (monetary balance sheets) on a monthly basis. Insurance, pension and standardized guarantee schemes are collected using the financial assets and liabilities of insurance and pension companies. The Financial Supervisory Authority provides quarterly data on the financial assets and liabilities of pension funds, while for insurance companies the source of the data relies on direct reporting to NBR. EU funds are registered on accrual basis, the data source being NBR Financial account statistics. Other additional administrative data sources are also used in compilation and quality checks. Reserve assets: IIP data is supplied by Market Operation Department. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording IIP is broadly consistent with BPM6 manual, adjustment procedures exist to ensure that source data comply with the coverage, classification, and valuation and timing conventions. Direct, portfolio and other investment survey items are designed to adhere to BPM6 guidelines. A security-by-security database provides data of equity and debt securities, including the related income. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Overall, the source data for IIP compiling is timely. In some cases (FDI, IFRN) where data is available with a time lag, preliminary estimates are made taking into account the results of previous surveys. Deadlines for respondents are mandated by the Regulation no. 4/2021 regarding statistical reporting requirements, with the subsequent amendments and notices of the NBR for the quarterly surveys. Respondent timeliness is monitored on an ongoing basis. 3.2.1 Source data assessment 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Direct investment: Data is compiled based on results of the annual and quarterly surveys on inward direct investments starting with 2003, and on outward direct investments starting with 2006 (directional principle); compilation is possible for assets and liabilities; the outcome of surveys gives information for all types of flows (transactions and other changes) as for stocks. The same as for quarterly FDI. Other investment: Data for deposits (assets) of other sectors with foreign banks is compiled based on the BIS locational statistics. Data for long term loans of deposit taking corporations except the central bank sector supplied by DMFAS system (external debt database), nowadays being assimilated to deposits in case of liabilities, are checked with banks' balance sheet data on a monthly basis. Externalized financial credits by resident credit institutions to their parents are added to the total liabilities, based on the information supplied by them. The information on SDR allocations comes from NBR balance sheet being monthly updated with the exchange rate. Central bank balance sheet gives stock position on asset side other investment deposit instrument related to Target 2 operations. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures The data is not adjusted for seasonal patterns. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results All data submitted by companies in surveys or through administrative sources are subject to verifying procedures. Where available, data is also confronted with balance sheet information of the various financial sub-sectors or information gathered from the financial press. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses Quarterly data is periodically revised according to the Harmonized European Revision Policy in terms of timing and in depth of revisions. Quarterly and annual data is consistent. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses 5.1.5 Dissemination on request Unpublished non-confidential data is available upon request. 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points Apart from the information published on the NBR website and in other different publications, a dedicated email address, statistici-externe@bnro.ro , has been provided to users.
