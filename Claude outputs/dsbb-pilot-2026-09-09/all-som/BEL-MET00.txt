 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Definition: broadly in line with the United Nations International Trade Statistics Concepts and Definitions. Relationship of national practices with international or regional guidelines: Exports and imports are based on the special trade system. A distinction should be made between the trade with EU-Member States (Intrastat system) and with non-EU-Member States. For the latter, only companies whose trade with EU-Member States is above a threshold are obliged to complete a statistical declaration called ‘Intrastat'. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization For collecting--the Harmonised System (HS) , full detail 6 digit, plus CN8 extension to 8 digits. For dissemination--the HS, CN8 extension to 8 digits, the SITC rev. 3 , the CBEC ( Broad Economic Categories ), data are available in full detail. Country coding--alphabetic ISO - 2 digit. Scope of the data Transactions coverage : are excluded all transactions related to entrepot trade, transit, personal effects, monetary gold, illegal activity. Geographic coverage : Full detail of ISO code. Unrecorded activity No estimates are made for illegal trade. 2.4.1 Valuation Conversion to unit of account : All data are collected and published in EURO. Valuation : FOB (Free on Board) for exports and CIF (Cost Insurance and Freight) for imports. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Sources : For extra EU-trade, customs tariff documents. For intra EU-trade, a national Intrastat document in conformity with the EU Regulations. Data are obtained from custom declarations for trade with non-EU-member States (Extrastat) and from company declarations for trade with EU-member States (Intrastat). Companies whose exports to EU member States is less than 1 million Euro annually are not required to complete an Intrastat declaration (1.500.000 Euro for imports). For those companies, adjustments are made using data from VAT (Value-Added Tax) declarations. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Type of data collected : Value, quantities (kilo net weight and supplementary units), type of transaction, mode of transport, commodity code, partner country, region. Basis of country data : For extra-trade, country of origin and destination. For intra-trade, country of consignment and destination. Timing of recording transactions : Time of clearance. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Adjustments to improve coverage/classification/timing/valuation: Adjustments are made only for the intra-EU trade. For those companies below the Intrastat threshold and for non-response above the threshold, estimates are made, using mainly data from the VAT (Value-Added Tax) declarations. Processing system: in-house developed system. Site of processing: National Bank of Belgium. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Seasonal adjustment: No seasonal adjustments are made. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results Reconciliation : Exercises, on specific demand of Eurostat, with partner countries. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request Geographic detail: Full detail by partner country is available; no information by point of arrival or departure. Commodity detail: Full detail is available for all the commodity classifications used. 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points Contact points are published. 5.3.2 Availability of documents and services catalogs Catalogs and calendars of publication, requirements for source data, etc. are available on the website of the National Bank of Belgium .
