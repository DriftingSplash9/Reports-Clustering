 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Analytical framework The concept and the methodology used are consistent with the European System of National and Regional Accounts (ESA 2010). Definition GDP measures the total value of final goods and services produced by resident institutional units during a certain period of time. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Classification: For the GDP by expenditure category, the following classifications are used: Classification of Individual Consumption by Purpose ( COICOP ); Standard International Trade Classifiation ( SITC ) and the Central Product Classification (CPC). The classification of business entities by activities is in line with NKD 2007 which is directly comparable with NACE Rev.2 ; classification. The Product Classification, which was used for the calculation of gross value added in current and constant prices by activities, is harmonised with the National Nomenclature of Industrial Products and Services, which is comparable with PRODCOM classification. Scope of the data The GDP estimates include all activities within the ESA 2010 production boundary and cover the total territory of the Republic of Croatia. Unrecorded activity The GDP estimates include all economic activities within the ESA 2010 production boundary. In order to achieve the whole and accurate picture of a production / consumption value in a certain period, both registered and unregistered activities have to be covered. CBS had already included unregistered activities in the national accounts coverage. Since September 2014, illegal activities are also included in the national accounts. 2.4.1 Valuation Gross domestic product (GDP) by expenditure approach is valued at market prices and gross value added (GVA) by production approach is valued at basic prices. 2.4.2 Recording basis Time of recording: Accrual basis. Recording period: Calendar quarters. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Data sources: Expenditure approach: The compilation of the QGDP according to the expenditure approach is based on data from regular surveys of the Croatian Bureau of Statistics, the Ministry of Finance, and the Croatian National Bank. Production approach: The calculation of the QGVA is based on data from regular surveys of the Croatian Bureau of Statistics, the Croatian National Bank, the Croatian Financial Services Supervisory Agency, the Croatian Regulatory Authority for Network Industries and the Croatian Institute for Pension Insurance. Besides above mentioned data sources, data used for the calculation of QGVA are Tax Administration data. Data on taxes on products are compliant with published quarterly government accounts data and, according to the national accounts, present data on government cash receipts adjusted to the concepts of ESA methodology in a way that time-adjusted cash (TAC) method is implemented. Data on subsidies on products are based on data of the Ministry of Finance. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording CBS published Quarterly National Accounts Inventory on CBS and EUROSTAT website. All details about definitions, scope, valuation and time of recording by activity and expenditure categories can be found in the QNA Inventory. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness For quarerly estimates most of the basic data sources are available on time. Some important indicators are available internaly from statistical departments and other insitutions for the quarterly estimate purpose. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures The quarterly GDP is compiled form expenditure, production and income approach. Expenditure approach: Final consumption expenditure of households is calculated from regular surveys of the CBS on turnover data for retail trade and for hotels and restaurants. Expenditure on other services, which are not covered by regular surveys of CBS, was extrapolated from the base year quarterly values by using volume indices from the production approach and adequate subindices of the consumer price indices (CPI) and adequate subindices of the CPI. Data on household consumption at constant prices are calculated by using adequate CPI subindices. The Government final consumption expenditures data and the Non-profit institutions serving households expenditureare calculated by using the Quarterly Statistical Report on Income and Expenditure of Budget and Budgetary Users and Non-Profit Organisations and on the Ministry of Finance data on the general government. For government expenditures and Non-profit institution serving households expenditure, an assumption of constant productivityis applied, so that the wage and salary indices at constant pricesare calculated dividing the data on current expenditures on wages and salaries by indices of the number of persons employed. Expenditures on other goods and servicesare deflated by using adequate CPI sub-indices and industrial producers' price indices. Gross fixed capital formation dataare calculated by means of a commodity flow method, using data for production and import of capital goods and construction activity. Gross fixed capital formation dataare deflated by domestic and import prices of capital goods and the implicit deflator for construction activity by production approach. Changes in inventories are calculated by using data of the CBS on changes in inventories. Statistical discrepancy is included in this category (difference between GDP production and expenditure approach). Changes in inventoriesof final goods and work in progressare deflated by prices of industrial products. Changes in inventories of raw materialsare deflated by industrial producers' price indices and import prices of raw material. For deflation of changes in inventories of goods purchased for resale, a relevant CPIis used. Import and export of goods and services are based on the balance of payments data of the Central National Bank (BPM 6 Manual is applied). Export of goods is deflated by using Industiral producer price indices on non-domestic market and Fisher-type unit value indices. Imports of goods is deflated by using foreign countries Industrial producer price indices on non-domestic marker and Fisher-type unit value indices. Export of servicesis deflated by relevant prices indicesfor services on the domestic market. Import of servicesis deflated by relevant prices indices on the markets of the most significant trading partner countries. Production approach: The calculation of the quarterly gross value added at current prices is based on data fromregular surveys of the Croatian Bureau of Statistics, the Croatian National Bank, the Croatian Financial Services Supervisory Agency, the Croatian Regulatory Authority for Network Industries and the Croatian Institute for Pension Insurance. Besides above mentioned data sources,the Tax Administration data on taxable and non-taxable supplies are used. These data are obtainable from value added tax(VAT)declarations submitted by enterpreneurs on the monthly andquarterly bases. The data are collected separately for legal entities and natural persons. The calculationis done for each group at the 2-digitNKD 2007 level. The calculation of quarterly GVA in constant prices is done at prices of the previous year and at constant prices of the referent year (2021 =100). Data on taxes on products are compliant with published quarterly government accounts data and,according to the national accounts, present data on government cash receipts adjusted to the concepts of ESA methodology in a way that time-adjusted cash (TAC) method is implemented. Data on subsidies on products are based on data of the Ministry of Finance. For the compilation at constant prices, various indicators from regular surveys of the CBSare used as well as data from other institutions. Appropriate volume indices at constant pricesare used for data calculation in agriculture, forestry and fishing activities,industrial production, construction, trade, hotels and restaurants, transport, communication and financial activities. The compilation is done at the 2-digit and 4-digit NACE 2007 level. Input indicators (number of employed persons in most cases) at the 4-digit NACE 2007 level areused for most other services for constant prices' compilation. The gross domestic product at market prices is derived from the gross value added at basic prices by adding taxes less subsidies on products to the latter total. Income approach: The data sources for the primary income categories calculation are to a large extent the same as for GDP by the production approach, administrative source. The income approach is not an independent estimate of GVA in the national accounts. Income components are estimated either directly or as a residual item. The income approach is used as a residual method and not as an approach to calculating surveyed income at origin. The income approach provides estimates of the GDP and its income components at current market prices. The main quarterly indicator used in the process of estimation of compensation of employees is the average monthly gross earnings. The main data source for taxes and subsidiesare quarterly general government accounts andMinistry of finance database. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs The statistical discrepancy is included in category: Changes in inventories (different between GDP production and expenditure approach). 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses Every time the revision is made, revised series are published on CBS website and the methodological changes and analyses is provided to users. 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points In the First release on Quarterly GDP data on contact points can be found.
