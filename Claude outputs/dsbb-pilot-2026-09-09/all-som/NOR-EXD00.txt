 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The Norwegian External Debt data are based on the definitions and standards in the 6th edition of the IMF's Balance of Payment manual (BPM6) and External Debt Statistics: Guide for Compilers and Users . 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Classification /sectorization follows BPM6 and SNA 2008/ESA 2010. The external debt data are classified into four sectors - general government, central bank, deposit-taking corporations except the central bank and other sectors. Other sectors include non-bank financial corporations, non-financial enterprises and households. Other financial enterprises include insurance companies, pension funds, financial holding companies, mutual funds, investment companies, mortgage companies and finance enterprises. Intercompany lending between entities in a direct investment relationship is shown separately as direct investment. Scope of the data External debt stock data of all resident sectors. Data are per end of each quarter. Each sector are broken down by maturity (short and long term) and by debt instrument. Exceptions to coverage There is no breakdown of short term debt into arrears and others. 2.4.1 Valuation Market value are used if market prices are available, otherwise book/face value is used. Stocks of external debt reported in foreign currencies are converted into NOK using the end of period midpoint exchange rates. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The general government: Data are based on balance sheet data from the general government’s accounts, the Government Pension Fund – Global, the government petroleum insurance fund and the national insurance scheme fund. The central bank of Norway (Norges Bank): Data are based on balance sheet data from Norges Bank. Banks: Data are based on quarterly survey data reported to statistics Norway and the Financial supervisory of Norway. The survey covers 100 percent of the population. Other sectors: Other sectors include non-bank financial corporations, non-financial enterprises and households. Non-bank financial corporations are based on surveys that cover 100 percent of the population with a few exceptions. Data on non-financial enterprises and households are based on a quarterly balance of payments survey which covers approximately 95 percent of the population. Intercompany lending: The data source is a quarterly balance of payment survey witch covers approximately 95 percent of the population. Data on securities for most of the sectors are collected from an administrative source – the Norwegian securities depository/register. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Market values are used all places market prices are available. For the sectors non-financial enterprises, households and investment companies sample surveys are used. The survey covers approximately 90 percent of the population Data are scaled up by statistical methods to approximate 100 percent of the population. Foreign investments in holiday houses/cottages in Norway are estimated. 3.2.1 Source data assessment There is a yearly routine assessment of source data for the sample survey population. Administrative records are continuously followed up. If response rate are low in sample surveys imputation methods are used.
