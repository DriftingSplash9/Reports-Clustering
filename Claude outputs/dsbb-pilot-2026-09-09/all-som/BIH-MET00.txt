 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Agency for Statistics of BiH is in charge of the production of BiH international trade in goods statistics. Indirect Taxation Authority is currently the only source of data (Single Administrative Documents – declarations). BiH international trade in goods statistics are based on the methodological requirements of: General statistical legislation Regulation (EC) No 223/2009 of the European Parliament and of the Council on European statistics Legislation applicable as of 1 January 2022 Regulation (EU) 2019/2152 on European business statistics - Implementing Regulation (EU) 2020/1197 laying down technical specifications and arrangements pursuant to Regulation (EU) 2019/2152 - Implementing Regulation (EU) 2021/1225 specifying the arrangements for the data exchanges and amending Implementing Regulation (EU) 2020/1197, as regards the Member State of extra Union export and the obligations of reporting units - Delegated Regulation (EU) 2021/1704 further specifying the details for the statistical information to be provided by tax and customs authorities and amending Annexes V and VI of Regulation (EU) 2019/2152 Extra-EU trade legislation (or Extrastat) - legislation applicable up to 1 January 2022 Basic Act: Regulation (EC) No 471/2009 of the European Parliament and of the Council - Implementing Commission Regulation (EC) No 92/2010 - Implementing Commission Regulation (EC) No 113/2010 International Merchandise Trade Statistics: Concepts and Definitions , United Nations, 2010 EBS compiler's manual for ITGS, Eurostat BiH statistics on the trading in goods are generally harmonized with the international recommendations 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization BiH international trade in goods statistics use standardised classifications for commodities and countries. Regarding the product classification, Customs Tariff of BiH, in line with Combined Nomenclature and Harmonized System, is applied for the detailed data whereas the Standard International Trade Classification, Revision 4 is used for aggregated data. Geonomenclature is used as the country classification. It is based on ISO 3166 alpha 2 codes. 2.2.1 Scope BiH international trade in goods statistics use standardised classifications for commodities and countries. Regarding the product classification, Customs Tariff of BiH, in line with Combined Nomenclature and Harmonized System, is applied for the detailed data whereas the Standard International Trade Classification, Revision 4 is used for aggregated data. Geonomenclature is used as the country classification. It is based on ISO 3166 alpha 2 codes. Scope of the data Agency for Statistics of BiH uses the special trade system (relaxed definition) when compiling BiH international trade in goods statistics. As regards partner country, it is the country of origin of goods on import side, and country of last known destination on export side. Exceptions to coverage The following transactions are excluded from BiH international trade in goods statistics for conceptual or methodological reasons: - Goods in simple transit: The reporting on operations in BiH is excluded when the goods travel directly through BiH or stop for reasons only related to its transport - Temporary trade: Goods moving abroad or entering BiH for a well-defined duration and without undergoing any change except normal depreciation (e.g. hire, operational leasing) are excluded - Particular trade is not subject of a commercial transaction (e.g. advertisement material, commercial samples) - Monetary gold - Means of payment which are legal tender - Goods moving between BiH and territorial enclaves of other countries or international organisations - Personal property belonging to natural persons transferring their normal place of residence; personal property acquired by inheritance; school outfits, educational materials and related household effects etc. Repairs Unrecorded activity Illegal trade is not recorded in BiH international trade in goods statistics. 2.4.1 Valuation In general, all goods should be valued with the total amount which would be invoiced in case of sale or purchase at the national border of Bosnia and Herzegovina. Statistical value for export is defined by Indirect Taxation Authority (ITA) in the following way: - if the place of delivery is abroad, statistical value comprises invoice value minus transport, insurance and other costs from the border of BiH to the place of delivery abroad, - if the place of delivery is in BiH, statistical value comprises invoice value plus transport, insurance and other costs from the place of delivery in BiH to the border of BiH. Statistical value for import is defined by ITA in the following way: - if the place of delivery is abroad, statistical value comprises invoice value plus transport, insurance and other costs from the place of delivery abroad to the border of BiH, - if the place of delivery is in BiH, statistical value comprises invoice value minus transport, insurance and other costs from the border of BiH to the place of delivery in BiH. Value for export is a F.O.B. (free on board), and value of import is C.I.F. (cost, insurance and freight) 2.4.2 Recording basis Transaction is recorded and included in BiH international trade in goods statistics when Indirect Taxation Authority assesses and accepts declaration as valid. It is a good approximation of border crossing moment, which is a reference point. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Agency for Statistics of Bosnia and Herzegovina (BHAS) uses Single Administrative Documents (SADs) as the only source of data. Indirect Taxation Authority (ITA) sends tables from ASYCUDA (Automated System for Customs Data – application which is used by ITA to collect and process Single Administrative Documents), to BHAS on a regular monthly basis in the form of an Access database. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Indirect Taxation Authority (ITA) is a single authority in Bosnia and Herzegovina responsible for enforcement of legal provisions and policy on customs and indirect taxation. ITA collects and processes Single Administrative Documents (SADs). Definitions, scope, product classification, valuation and time of recording which ITA use when collecting data are generally in line with international customs recommendations and needs of Agency for Statistics of Bosnia and Herzegovina (BHAS). BHAS uses data from SADs to compile BiH international trade in goods statistics according to international statistical recommendations. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Indirect Taxation Authority (ITA) submits to Agency for Statistics of Bosnia and Herzegovina (BHAS) data within seven days after the end of the reference month. In fact, every month, ITA submits to BHAS, in one Access database, the revised data for the period from the beginning of the year onward. There are no delays in data deliveries. 3.2.1 Source data assessment Data are examined to identify outliers. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Indirect Taxation Authority uses ASYCUDA (Automated System for Customs Data) as data processing application. It does not execute any statistical data controls before the data are transferred to Agency for Statistics of Bosnia and Herzegovina (BHAS). It is mainly focused on providing sufficient data quality according to customs policy standards. BHAS is in charge of performing validity and credibility checks using e.g. quartile or linear regression method. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results Basic (validity) controls: this means checks on the validity of data and concerns all the variables collected. A validity error is found when a record is not correct therefore it may cause processing problems, it cannot be included in the statistical analysis and so it is removed or corrected. Examples of this kind of errors are incorrect or missing codes, missing values in the declarations, character data in numeric field and vice versa, etc. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data Complex (credibility) controls: this means checks on the accuracy of data and assessment of possible errors associated with the data. Some data passing the previous validity checks can still be incorrect. Residual errors can be pointed out by looking for variable inconsistencies (e.g. unit value of a product should not fall outside the predefined range). 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs Mirror analyses, macro level analysis, time series analysis. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request Agency for Statistics of Bosnia and Herzegovina (BHAS) has a users' database. Users request mostly detailed level information.
