 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Analytical framework: The GDP data are compiled using expenditure and production methods. The compilation of annual and quarterly national accounts in Serbia is based on the principles and definitions set up in the European System of National and Regional Accounts 2010 (ESA 2010), the System of National Accounts 2008 (SNA 2008), as well as other relevant methodological manuals (Eurostat Handbook on Price and Volume Measures in National Accounts and Handbook on Quarterly National Accounts , IMF Government Finance Statistics Manual , Eurostat Manual on Government Deficit and Debt , IMF Quarterly National Accounts Manual , etc.). Definition: GDP is a measure of the total value of final goods and services produced by resident institutional units during the reference period. From the expenditure side, GDP can be measured as follows: GDP = private final consumption expenditure (P.3 in S14+S15) + government final consumption expenditure (P.3 in S13) + gross fixed capital formation (P.51G) + changes in inventories (P.52) + acquisition less disposal of valuables (P.53) + exports (P.6) - imports (P.7) 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Institutional sectors are defined in accordance with the ESA 2010/SNA 2008. The activity classification is based on the national Classification of Activities 2010 (KD 2010), which is harmonized with NACE Rev.2/ISIC Rev.4. Final consumption expenditure is classified by purpose - household consumption by COICOP and general government partially by COFOG, while COPNI needs to be introduced. Gross fixed capital formation is cross-classified by industry and type of capital goods. 2.2.1 Scope Institutional sectors are defined in accordance with the ESA 2010/SNA 2008. The activity classification is based on the national Classification of Activities 2010 (KD 2010), which is harmonized with NACE Rev.2/ISIC Rev.4. Final consumption expenditure is classified by purpose - household consumption by COICOP and general government partially by COFOG, while COPNI needs to be introduced. Gross fixed capital formation is cross-classified by industry and type of capital goods. Scope of the data Unit coverage: data cover resident non-financial corporations, financial corporations, general government units, households and non-profit institutions serving households, as stipulated in the ESA 2010/SNA 2008. Transaction coverage: data cover all activities within the production boundary outlined in the ESA 2010/SNA 2008. Annual and quarterly data on GDP by production and expenditure approach are available at current prices, at previous year prices and as chain-linked volume measures (reference year 2010); available is also seasonally adjusted GDP data as chain-linked volume measures (reference year 2010). Annual GDP data are available from 1995, whereas quarterly GDP data are available from the first quarter 1996. Geographical coverage: data cover the territory under the administration of the Government of the Republic of Serbia. From 1999 data for Kosovo and Metohia are not included. Exceptions to coverage Starting from 1999 certain data relative to AP Kosovo and Metohia may not be available and therefore these data are not included in the coverage for the Republic of Serbia. Unrecorded activity All unrecorded activities within the boundaries of the ESA2010 are included. 2.4.1 Valuation In line with ESA 2010, gross value added (GVA) by production approach is valued at basic prices, while gross domestic product (GDP) by expenditure approach is valued at market prices. 2.4.2 Recording basis Transactions are recorded on an accrual basis, except for general government expenditure where cash basis is used. Time of recording is calendar year/calendar quarter. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs For compilation of both annual and quarterly national accounts, SORS uses a wide range of data sources: regular monthly, quarterly, and annual surveys covering a range of economic activities, as well as administrative sources (data from the National Bank of Serbia, the Ministry of Finance, the Tax Administration, the Custom Administration, the Treasury Administration, the Chamber of Commerce, etc). These data sources cover a range of economic and financial indicators. When compiling quarterly data much effort is made to use the same sources and calculation methods as in the annual accounts. However, the basic data available quarterly are less comprehensive. When preparing the estimates of national accounts aggregates at a quarterly level all the data sources that are available on monthly or quarterly basis are used. The main data source for output indicators is the Quarterly Structural Business Survey (SBS‐03). The other data sources from the statistical system used in the current price calculations are: values of construction works done, data on sales and purchase values of agricultural products, retail trade turnover values, wholesale trade turnover values, trade of motor vehicles turnover values, catering turnover values, wages and salaries and number of employees, producer price indices, consumer price indices, composite price indices for buildings and structures, etc. Data used in the current price calculations from the administrative sources are monthly data on expenditures and revenues of government budget (Ministry of Finance), monthly statements on deposits and bank claims and quarterly data on insurance premiums (National Bank of Serbia). The main source of data on taxes on products is Information on distributed public revenues and earnings of the Treasury Administration of the Ministry of Finance. The main data sources for subsidies are budgetary data. The data sources for HFCE are based on: quarterly household budget survey (HBS) data, quarterly retail trade and catering turnover data and quarterly output and GVA for some activities at current prices, Balance of Payments (BoP) data (inflow and outflow from tourism) from the National Bank of Serbia are used as the source and CPIs. The data sources for GFCE are based on Expenditures and revenues of government budget from the Ministry of Finance, employment data and CPIs and PPIs. Quarterly GFCF calculations at current and constant prices apply monthly or quarterly data at current prices from regular statistical surveys, various data on prices and quantities, and other indicators from the statistical system and other available data sources. Quarterly exports and imports calculations at current and constant prices use monthly or quarterly data at current prices from regular statistical surveys, various data on prices and quantities, and other indicators from the statistical system and data from National Bank of Serbia. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording The definitions, classifications, valuation and time of recording used in the questionnaires basically comply with the recommendations of SNA 2008/ESA 2010 . Quarterly National Accounts Inventory is published on SORS and EUROSTAT website. All details about definitions, scope, valuation and time of recording by activity and expenditure categories could be found in the Inventory. 3.2.1 Source data assessment Routine assessment of source data is provided by branch departments of SORS. None 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses Annual national accounts data may be revised due to: a) methodological issues (changed concepts, definitions, classifications), b) changes of statistical methods, c) changes of data sources, d) upgraded coverage of reporting units, e) corrections of errors and corrections of original data and estimations. QNA data may be revised due to: a) changes in annual accounts; b) changes/revisions in the short‐term indicators used – inclusion of a more complete/additional data source or a change in the data source; c) availability of indicators and the replacement of forecasts with actual data; improvement of methodology due to a change in the statistical method or a change in classifications, concepts and definitions. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses When revising data, revised data series are published and the methodological changes and analyses are provided to users.
