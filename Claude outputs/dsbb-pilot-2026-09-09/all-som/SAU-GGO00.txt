 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Analytical framework: The general government data is organized for analytical purposes based on the framework of the IMF Government Finance Statistics Manual 2014 (GFSM2014) and the System of National Accounts 2008. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The fiscal data is compiled by calendar year. According to the data compiled for the previous years for Central Government, the data fully consistence with GFSM 2014 standards. COFOG is yet to be rooted. For the meantime, MOF imposed some rules to serve allocating expenditure for COFOG, however, the new Chart of account and new guidelines make it mandatory for each transaction to be mapped to the correspondent GFS as well as to match with the relevant COFOG. 2.2.1 Scope The fiscal data is compiled by calendar year. Scope of the data General government sector in Saudi Arabia includes the budgetary central government, social security funds, and extra-budgetary funds. There are no state or local governments. The accounts of the budget cover 545 agencies including ministries, commissions, public entities, municipalities and other agencies. The extra-budgetary accounts include 15 agencies; Capital Market Authority, Saudi Industrial Development Fund, Agricultural Development Fund, Human Resource Development Fund, Zakat,Tax and customs Authority, Communications, Space and Technology Commission, Real Estate Development Fund, Saudi Council of Engineers, And General Commission for The Guardianship of Trust Fund for Minors and Their Counterparts, Social Development Bank, The Saudi Authority for Industrial Cities and Technology Zones, and Water and Electricity Regulatory Authority, Saudi Bar Association, Saudi Organization for Chartered and Professional Accountants, Insurance Authority. The social security funds accounts include 1 agency; General Organization for Social Insurance. GFSY 2025 targets the inclusion of the Saudi Contractors Authority, with potential expansion to other entities under review. The Social security funds include: (i) the General Retirement Agency that receives the contributions of civil servants (civilian and military) and employers' portion, and disburses pensions; and (ii) the General Social Insurance Corporation which is a scheme designed to perform the same functions as the former but for the benefit of private sector employees (as well as sickness insurance). The government budget and the related executive instructions are legislated through a Royal Decree and decisions of the Council of Ministers. The budget decree contains the details revenue and expenditure for the consolidated budget of the central government without extra budgetary. It contains mainly instructions to ministries on the implementation of the budget, including on: the need to implement projects budgeted for; the need not to exceed allocations and approved staff positions. 2.4.1 Valuation Flows are valued on the basis of the amount of payments in cash, or cash collection (e.g., fixed term contractual obligations). Amounts denominated in foreign currency are converted to national currency using market rates on the date of recording. 2.4.2 Recording basis Other (cash basis and accrual basis) is used to record financial transactions. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Central government: The data on revenue is obtained from the revenue calculations of the Ministry of Finance. Expenditure is obtained from the expenditure calculations of the central government and the commitment figures published by the Ministry of Finance. Information on financing is provided by DMO and SAMA. For budgetary agencies, data is available on a quarterly basis. For Extra-budgetary: the data is collected based on their reported data and available on an annual basis, yet agencies are working towards quarterly basis of reporting. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Detailed source data for the revenue and expenditure of the domestic operations of the budgetary central government are consistent with the GFSM 2014. And for the extra budgetary funds, the work is ongoing. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Source data are available on annual basis and with timeliness of about six months. 3.2.1 Source data assessment Data sources based on accounting records gives assurance that the best sources have been used in the compilation of fiscal data. A new governance was introduced by beginning of 2019, between the MFPD and General department of accounts. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Compilation of fiscal statistics is based on comprehensive source data. There is no need to estimate or impute missing data. Statistics are final when first published. Reporting dates allow sufficient time to ensure that complete and accurate information is reported. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results The likelihood of inconsistencies is minimal since the data are prepared from accounting records.
