 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The compilers use the International Monetary Fund, Balance of Payment Manual 6 , for their concepts and definitions for compiling the FSM’s IIP statistics except for FDI. FDI concept is based on Nationality, not on residency. Most FDI in the IIP is owned by FSM resident with foreign nationality. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The IIP follows the standard BPM6 classification/sectorization. 2.2.1 Scope The IIP follows the standard BPM6 classification/sectorization. Scope of the data The IIP statistics cover the whole territory of the FSM—namely, the four states, which are Kosrae, Pohnpei, Chuuk, and Yap. Exceptions to coverage Private sector (mostly fishing, wholesale and retail) transactions are not covered. 2.4.1 Valuation The IIP data are presented in current US dollars. 2.4.2 Recording basis The IIP data are on accrual basis. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures IIP data are on a gross basis. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The IIP data are compiled from the following sources. Assets and Liabilities - Department of Finance and Administration, Public Audits, State and National Foreign Investment Offices, and the Banking Board Office None None
