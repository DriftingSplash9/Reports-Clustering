 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The CBC complies with the guidelines published in the IMF's Monetary and Financial Statistics Manual and Compilation Guide 2019, the System of National Accounts 2008 and with the internal procedures related on treatment of external data. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Data on the SDDS Plus ‘OFCs Survey’ include Investment funds, Insurance corporations, pension funds, mutual funds and other financial intermediaries (other than insurance corporations and pension funds), The counterpart sectors contain: § Non-residents § General governments § Depository corporations § Other sectors, including financial institutions, non-depository corporations (Investment funds, Other non-monetary financial institutions, and Insurance corporations and pension funds) and non-financial corporations (NFCs). 2.2.1 Scope Data on the SDDS Plus ‘OFCs Survey’ include Investment funds, Insurance corporations, pension funds, mutual funds and other financial intermediaries (other than insurance corporations and pension funds), The counterpart sectors contain: § Non-residents § General governments § Depository corporations § Other sectors, including financial institutions, non-depository corporations (Investment funds, Other non-monetary financial institutions, and Insurance corporations and pension funds) and non-financial corporations (NFCs). Scope of the data The Chilean SDDS Plus ‘Other Financial Corporations Survey’ data contains: a) Other Financial Corporations claims on non-residents and liabilities to non-residents, presented on both a gross and a net basis as components of net foreign assets. b) Claims on residents disaggregated into: (i) claims on general government; (ii) claims on resident depository corporations and (iii) claims on other resident sectors. Data are presented including not only net claims but also gross assets and liabilities. Financial instruments includes deposits, debt securities loans, financial derivatives, and insurance and pension schemes. c) Shares and other equity liabilities. Exceptions to coverage There are no exceptions to coverage in the SDDS Plus ‘Other Financial Corporations Survey’ data. Unrecorded activity There is not unrecorded activity in the SDDS Plus ‘Other Financial Corporations Survey’ data. 2.4.1 Valuation In general, financial positions are recorded at market value and financial flows at transaction values. Foreign-currency positions are represented in Chilean pesos by using the average exchange rate in the last day of the quarter, while foreign-currency flows are expressed in Chilean pesos by using the average exchange rate of the quarter. 2.4.2 Recording basis Quarterly data on an accrual basis. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Data on OFCs are obtained mainly from statistical statements from their different regulators: CBC, the Chilean Pensions Supervisor and The Financial Market Commission. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording The relevant information obtained from statistical statements is recorded monthly or quarterly on an accounting or market value basis. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness The relevant information is available on time to compile the financial accounts and the SDDS Plus data. 3.2.1 Source data assessment Data coverage encompasses the whole population. The information obtained from the different available sources should be consistent with the quarterly data included in the Financial Accounts of the Chilean Economy. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results There is a continuous process of validation and assessment of primary data. Data on the Financial Accounts of the Chilean Economy are validated with different statistical outputs from the CBC, Chilean Pensions Supervisor and Financial Market Commission. None
