 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Analytical framework: External debt data are compiled in accordance with the recommendations of the IMF's 2013 External Debt Statistics: Guide for Compilers and Users and the sixth edition of the IMF's Balance of Payments and IIP Manual (BPM6) . External debt by domestic sectors is shown in the same manner as in the BOP. The principal and interest payment projection is also shown. Data is broken down by following sectors: General government, Central Bank (Croatian National Bank), Deposit-Taking Corporations exc. Central Bank and Other sectors while direct investments are a separate category. Each sector (except direct investrment) is further divided by original maturity (short term and long term) and by debt instruments. Debt instruments cover all BPM6 defined debt instruments. Definition: Gross external debt is defined as the external debt liabilities of residents to nonresidents, including: principal and interest arrears, accrued interests and not yet matured principal. Scope of the data Coverage: Monthly data on external debt liabilities are collected from the banking sector, the other sectors (other financial institutions, sample of enterprises and households) and the government. The data are based on concluded credit agreements and debt securities reported with the Croatian National Bank. Geographical coverage: The economic territory consists of the geographic territory, administered by the government of Croatia, in which persons, goods, and capital circulate freely. 2.4.1 Valuation Conversion to unit of account: Data reported in foreign currencies are converted to EURO according to the CNB's midpoint exchange rate at the end of the period. Valuation: The debt instruments are valued at the reference date at nominal value using accrual metodology. 2.4.2 Recording basis Recording of income: Principle used for recording debt position data is on accrual basis. The debt balance includes principal and interest arrears, accrued interests, and future principal payments. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Portfolio investment (Debt securities): Data on external issues of debt securities are obtained from statistics on foreign credit relations of the CNB, which are based on concluded credit and bonds agreements individually reported with CNB and appropriately corrected for the part of external issues bought by resident, and, from December 2005 on, nonresident investments in debt securities issued in the domestic market. Data for the part of issues bought by residents are collected via monetary statistics, pension funds and other financial statistics and via securities issues and holding statistics. Other investment: Trade credits: Short term trade credits are covered by a survey conducted among largest importers and exporters. Long term trade credits are covered by statistics on foreign credit relations of the CNB, which is based on concluded credit agreements individually reported with CNB. Credits (Loans): Data are based on concluded credit agreements individually reported with CNB for all public sector entities, other financial institutions and for sample of non financial enterprises. Data from the banking sector are collected via monetary statistics reports. Currency and deposits: Data are collected from the banking sector via monetary statistics reports. Direct investment (Intercompany loans): Data are based on concluded credit agreements individually reported with CNB for all other financial institutions and sample of non financial enterprises. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Portfolio investment: Both external and domestics issues of debt securities are adjusted to cover only neresident holding of debt securities. Other investment: Trade credits: includes sale of goods from foreign suppliers to Croatian importers for delayed payment. Credits (Loans): include all other credit obligations. No adjustments are made to the data from statistics on foreign credit relations of the CNB. Currency and deposits: includes changes in those foreign exchange and kuna denominated foreign liabilities of the monetary authorities (CNB) and banks, based on current accounts, time accounts, letters of credit, guarantees, sight deposits, and demand deposits. A classification adjustment is made by excluding loans that are received from or extended to nonresidents within FDI relationships (those data are obtained). Also, since the first quarter of 1999, 85 percent of net unidentified FOREX inflows on assets/currency and deposits/other sectors are excluded from the financial account, and changes of DMB's assets on transaction basis (assets/currency and deposits/banks) are estimated. No verification procedures are in place. Direct investment: Direct investment includes borrower-lender transaction of other sectors that are interrelated by ownership (borrower or lender owns more then 10 percent of the other). No adjustments are made to the data from statistics on foreign credit relations of the CNB. None
