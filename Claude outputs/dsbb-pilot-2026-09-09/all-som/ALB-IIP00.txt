 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions For the compilation of IIP is used concepts, definitions and conceptual framework and is consistent with the IMF's manual – International Investment Positions – a guide to data sources, 2002 and Balance of Payments and International Investment Position Manual, IMF, 6-th edition, 2009. IIP is compiled in millions of ALL and it is disseminated in millions of EURO. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The IIP classification system follows the recommendations of BPM6 Assets Direct investment (Equity capital and reinvested earnings, Debt Instruments); Portfolio investment (Equity and investment fund shares, Debt Securities); Financial derivatives (other than reserves); Other investment (Currency and deposits, Loans, Trade credits and advances, Other accounts receivable); Reserve assets (Monetary gold, Special drawing rights, Reserve position in the IMF, Other reserve assets). Liabilities Direct investment (Equity capital and reinvested earnings, Debt Instruments); Portfolio investment (Equity and investment fund shares, Debt securities); Financial derivatives (other than reserves); Other investment (Currency and deposits, Loans, Trade credits and advances, Other accounts payable, SDRs). Also tables are classified as follows: - by institutional sector (general government, monetary authorities, monetary and financial institutions, other non-financial sector); - by original (short term up to one year and long term) and remaining maturity; - by instrument (currency and deposits, debt portfolio, loans, trade credits and advances and other accounts payable) 2.2.1 Scope The IIP classification system follows the recommendations of BPM6 Assets Direct investment (Equity capital and reinvested earnings, Debt Instruments); Portfolio investment (Equity and investment fund shares, Debt Securities); Financial derivatives (other than reserves); Other investment (Currency and deposits, Loans, Trade credits and advances, Other accounts receivable); Reserve assets (Monetary gold, Special drawing rights, Reserve position in the IMF, Other reserve assets). Liabilities Direct investment (Equity capital and reinvested earnings, Debt Instruments); Portfolio investment (Equity and investment fund shares, Debt securities); Financial derivatives (other than reserves); Other investment (Currency and deposits, Loans, Trade credits and advances, Other accounts payable, SDRs). Also tables are classified as follows: - by institutional sector (general government, monetary authorities, monetary and financial institutions, other non-financial sector); - by original (short term up to one year and long term) and remaining maturity; - by instrument (currency and deposits, debt portfolio, loans, trade credits and advances and other accounts payable) Scope of the data The IIP compiled shows the Albania’s positions of external financial assets and liabilities at a particular point in time. Exceptions to coverage There are no exceptions to coverage. Unrecorded activity Household deposits abroad are not estimated Portfolio investment abroad held by other sectors are not estimated. Informal economy transactions are not estimated There may miss information for loans taken by private companies from nonresidents not in direct investment relationship, but still no data are caught from International Transactions Reporting System, so not relevant for the moment. 2.4.1 Valuation IIP is compiled in millions of domestic currency ALL. All the items of IIP are compiled in nominal value, except: -reserves and portfolio investment (assets) that are compiled in market value and -portfolio investment (liabilities), for which historic cost is used. -position of the direct investment assets and liabilities is reported at book value. It is disseminated in millions of EURO. The positions recorded in national currency is converted in EURO using the official exchange rate as of the last working day of the reporting period at the end of the period published by Bank of Albania. 2.4.2 Recording basis All the items of IIP are recorded on accrual basis, except for inputted data derived from Balance of Payments transactions and other evaluations made in the compilation of part of direct investment and portfolio investment (liabilities). The distinction between short-term and long-term debt is based on the original maturity. The long-term debt includes all liabilities with original maturity of more than a year, and the short-term those with original maturity of one year or less). 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures IIP is compiled and published on gross basis. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Direct investment For the compilation of the Direct Investment (DI) are used different data sources: the annual data on direct investment positions are collected from the National Registration Center (NRC); the unified reporting system (URS) collects monthly data from banks and branches of foreign banks and quarterly from the non-bank financial institutions (to determine resident banks’ assets and liabilities vis-à-vis non-residents; direct reporting from direct reporters, companies with a high degree of cross border transactions and with high position on direct investment; the National Agency for Natural Resources (NANR) reports quarterly data on drilling enterprises active in Albania and International Transaction Reporting System (ITRS). Main data source for compiling direct investment abroad is ITRS. A comprehensive and up-to-date business register of DI is available for the purpose of identifying the largest direct investment companies which is provided by Institute of Statistics. Also ITRS is used to for the purpose of identifying new largest companies. Portfolio investment Custodians report monthly the holdings of securities issued by residents and non-residents in their custody and held for their own account or on behalf of their resident and non-resident customers. The reports provide details for each security and certain characteristics. This information ensures the accuracy of the allocations by instrument, geographical zone and issuing sector. This information is used to estimate the quarterly flows of portfolio investment. Due to the lack of source data on portfolio investment liabilities, results of the IMF’s Coordinated Portfolio Investment Survey (CPIS) are used to estimate liability positions for nonfinancial corporations. Other investment Bank of Albania manages the central government’s foreign exchange reserves under the rules of the IMF and reports the position data. The central bank also provides information about all of its other transactions, which is mainly presented under the items recording the monetary authorities’ portfolio investment and “other investment”. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Source data approximate the definitions, scope, classifications, valuation of recording required. The data for portfolio investment liabilities, generated from the IMF website are data from the creditors. They are used, because of lack of private sector data, but they are annual data, published on the IMF website with one year delay. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Data collection and processing timetables are adequate to meet timeliness and periodicity for disseminating the IIP. The data are reported to Bank of Albania in a quarterly basis, adequate for compiling the quarterly Gross External Debt. Annual data are used for evaluations and revisions. 3.2.1 Source data assessment Data are checked for temporal consistency. The accuracy of the data from direct reporting, or the information from administrative data and other supplementary sources is routinely assessed. The inclusion and accuracy of high-value positions are confirmed. With the use of periodic reviews and estimates of the various parameters employed, the External debt adjustment procedures are sound. Estimates are made in the case of portfolio investment liabilities, and the data are revised when the figures are published on the IMF website. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques For the compilation of IIP data, MS Excel spreadsheet applications are used. Additional IT support is working for enhancing accuracy (processing errors such as coding, editing and tabulation errors) and timeliness by putting in place an automation process for the compilation and reconciliation of external sector statistics. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results Intermediate data are validated using other sources whenever possible. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data While discrepancies in intermediate data are often addressed satisfactorily, given the independent, unlinked spreadsheets used for compilation, and the manual entry of intermediate results, the current procedures are error prone. International Investment Positions indicators (transactions) are reconciled with the Balance of Payments (Financial Account) indicators and the External Debt indicators. The difference between the opening position and closing position value of external debt data for each liability category is equal to the sum of transactions, price changes, exchange rate changes, and other adjustments for that category. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs The stock of general government debt and public debt is reconciled with the same stock disseminated by the Ministry of Finance. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses Revisions of individual records are monitored. Revisions are documented as to the cause, identified by the staff member making the amendment, and signed off by a supervisor. Data are checked to ensure that the revisions flow through to aggregate results. After the first publication of the IIP statistics, the statistics are continuously adjusted as revised when annual data become available. This implies that the publication of the quarterly IIP statistics is not finished until the end of September of next year. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses Analysis of differences between the revised and preliminary data is published together with the revision note and is always part of the statistical publications in the respect of the transparency dimension towards the data users. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request Users can submit specific request for statistics data through the e-mail address: statistics@bankofalbania.org (contacts in BOA webpage) The data are disseminated also to international organizations.
