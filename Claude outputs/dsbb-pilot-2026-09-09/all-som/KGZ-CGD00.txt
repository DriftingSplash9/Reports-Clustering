 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions • Analytical Framework: The definition of government debt is consistent with the definitions in the IMF External Debt Statistics Manual 2013. • Government debt is the sum of disbursed and outstanding domestic debt and external public debt of the Kyrgyz Republic on a given date under a loan agreement or other obligations of the state. Government debt is regulated by the Budget Code and the Medium-Term External Debt Strategy. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization • Classification System: The data disseminated are classified as follows: 1) Types of creditors: multilateral, bilateral, private (private, in turn, are divided into subtypes, commercial banks, other financial institutions) 2) Loans on a preferential basis: preferential and non-preferential. 3) Loan maturity: long-term and short-term. 4) Loans in foreign currency: US dollars, SDRs, etc. 5) Debt flows: payments and charges (debt repayment and interest). 6) Public and publicly guaranteed debt and private non-guaranteed debt. 2.2.1 Scope • Classification System: The data disseminated are classified as follows: 1) Types of creditors: multilateral, bilateral, private (private, in turn, are divided into subtypes, commercial banks, other financial institutions) 2) Loans on a preferential basis: preferential and non-preferential. 3) Loan maturity: long-term and short-term. 4) Loans in foreign currency: US dollars, SDRs, etc. 5) Debt flows: payments and charges (debt repayment and interest). 6) Public and publicly guaranteed debt and private non-guaranteed debt. Scope of the data Institutional coverage: Data cover budgetary institutions only. Transactions of extra-budgetary units are not included. Transactions under the social security program (Social Fund) are not included in the data. Exceptions to coverage Unrecorded activity 2.4.1 Valuation • Accounting basis: Data for the central government are compiled on a cash basis. The unified methodological basis for accounting and financial reporting for budget institutions is the Government Finance Statistics Manual 2014. • Valuation method: The exchange rate used to convert foreign currency transactions is based on the exchange rate announced by the National Bank of the Kyrgyz Republic. For inflows - the rate established on the basis of daily transactions, for stocks - at the rate at the end of the period. • Additional time: In special cases, by order of the Minister of Finance of the Kyrgyz Republic 2.4.2 Recording basis Data sources: Data are compiled from treasury records and reports. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures Accounting Basis Regulation: Accounting and reporting of financial statistics is based on the Government Finance Statistics Manual 2014. The accounting is on a cash basis. Adjustment of Gross and Net Transactions: Central government lending transactions reflect net disbursements. Consolidation: The revenue and expenditure sides of the budget are prepared on a consolidated basis. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Data sources: Data are compiled from treasury records and reports. Revenues are recorded on the basis of receipts. Grants are similarly included in central government domestic currency transactions data after they have been used. Expenditures are recorded on the basis of cash disbursements. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording 3.1.3 Source data timeliness 3.2.1 Source data assessment 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results For cross-checking of data recording documents of creditors (banking notices) are used. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses 5.1.5 Dissemination on request 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points 5.3.2 Availability of documents and services catalogs
