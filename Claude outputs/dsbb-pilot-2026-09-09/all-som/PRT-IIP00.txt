 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The compilation of the international investment position statistics is consistent with international standards and guidelines, namely with the sixth edition of the Balance of Payments Manual ( BPM6 ), published by the IMF and the fourth edition of the OECD's benchmark definition of Foreign Direct Investment . 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Positions are classified according to the international recommendations and guidelines, namely 6th edition of the Balance of Payments Manual ( BPM6 ) : The standard components of the IIP are fully presented for the five broad categories of data, namely: Direct investment: assets and liabilities by type of instrument (equity and reinvested earnings, debt instruments) and by institutional sector of the resident investor/issuer; Portfolio investment: assets and liabilities by type of instrument (equity and investment fund shares, debt securities) and by institutional sector of the resident investor/issuer; Financial derivatives (other than reserves) and employee stock options: assets and liabilities, by institutional sector of the resident counterpart; Other investment: assets and liabilities (other equity, currency and deposits, loans, insurance, pension and standardized guarantee schemes, trade credit and advances and other receivable/payable) by institutional sector of the resident counterpart; Reserve assets: assets (monetary gold, SDR's, reserve position in the IMF and other reserve assets). Institutional units are classified and attributed to the relevant sector according to the BPM6. The short-term/long-term classification is on an original maturity basis. Scope of the data Data collected in IIP comprise investment positions, in all external financial assets and liabilities held by central bank, general government, other monetary and financial institutions, non-monetary financial institutions except insurance companies and pension's funds, insurance companies and pensions funds, non-financial corporations and households and non-profit institutions serving households. These positions include interest accrued but not yet paid for debt instruments. Data is broken down by country of residence of the counterpart. For reserve assets, the Eurosystem's harmonised concept is used. Therefore, all central bank external claims on Euro Area residents and/or denominated in Euros are excluded. Unrecorded activity Not applicable. 2.4.1 Valuation Positions valued at market prices. 2.4.2 Recording basis Conversion to unit of account: positions are converted to euro by making use of end-of-period exchange rates. Recording basis: The valuation of the positions follows the accrual principle, which means that interest accrued but not yet paid is recorded as part of the value of the underlying instrument. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures The international investment position follows the six th edition of the Balance of Payments Manual ( BPM6 ). 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Source data are consistent with the definitions, scope and classifications of IIP. Source data covers the full range of institutional sectors and is kept under continuous review to ensure that the data collection program is comprehensive. Also these data collection programs are sufficiently open and flexible to provide for new developments in sources. The main sources for compiling IIP are: Direct Investment: transactions are captured through the direct reporting system for external accounts statistics named COPE ( Comunicação de Operações e Posições com o Exterior ) where resident entities (exception made to households) report directly to Banco de Portugal their cross border operations and to some extent the related positions. Up to 2013, on an annual basis, FDI incorporated the results from a direct investment survey, which was based on a sample. From 2014 onwards direct investment is based on the annual accounts reported by all resident corporations through Simplified Corporate Information ( IES - Informação Empresarial Simplificada ), an administrative source. Portfolio Investment: Stocks data are collected monthly from major respondents, on a security-by- security basis. Monetary Financial Institutions report monthly their own assets outstanding amounts. In addition, for the unlisted shares we use information from Simplified Corporate Information (IES - Informa ção Empresarial Simplificada) Financial Derivatives: Monthly survey to major institutions of the resident banking system. COPE ( Comunicação de Operações e Posições com o Exterior) is used as a complementary source of information. Other Investment: COPE ( Comun i c a ç ã o de Operações e Posições com o Exterior ), Banco de Portugal's balance sheet, Treasury, banks' balance sheets, Deposit taking corporation's balance sheet, iinternational banking statistics from BIS and a quarterly survey for trade credits. On an annual basis, exhaustive data on trade credit is obtained from the Simplified Corporate Information (IES) – for further information on statistics compiled from the IES please refer to Supplement 1/2008 to the Statistical Bulletin. Reserve Assets: balance sheet of Banco de Portugal and information from the Markets and Reserves Department of Banco de Portugal . 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Source data are consistent with the definitions, scope and classifications adopted for international investment position statistics. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Data collection and processing timetables allow us to meet timeliness and periodicity for disseminating IIP. These timetables are distributed to all respondents. 3.2.1 Source data assessment Direct Investment: Statistics for direct investment are compiled according to estimates mainly based on the accumulation of BOP transactions data. Once the results of IES are made available, the exercise of reconciliation of stocks and transactions data aims at verifying and checking the statistical information concerning direct investment. Portfolio Investment: Information concerning other Monetary and Financial Institutions is checked with money and banking statistics; as regards listed shares, information made available by Euronext Lisbon is used for verification. Financial Derivatives: Reconciliation of stocks and transactions is performed. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Data compilation employs sound statistical techniques to deal with data sources. Some compilation procedures are implemented in order to minimize processing errors and to estimate missing observations. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Direct Investment: Final data for direct investment components of the IIP are compiled from information captured through enterprise administrative and survey data (IES). Portfolio Investment: A security-by-security reporting system, based on the ISIN code, provides the information for portfolio investment. Besides the security-by-security information, the assets side of investment funds is covered by an additional source of information (CMVM – Securities Market Commission); the balance sheet of Banco de Portugal provides data for compiling the central bank sector. Other Investment: Data on Central Bank assets and liabilities are derived from Banco de Portugal's balance sheet. Data on banks assets and liabilities are collected from external positions of the banks' balance sheets, as reported for Money and Banking statistics. Currently, for the other sector, data on transactions and stocks is collected through a direct reporting system (COPE). For the past, a reconstruction of series was made based on the old data and the data available from the new report system. Also, for deposits (assets side) a mix methodology is used, once mirror data from the International Baking Statistics information is incorporated. For quality control, a quarterly survey to the major exporters / importers is used to produce estimates of stock data on trade credits. On an annual basis, exhaustive data on trade credit is obtained from the Simplified Corporate Information (IES) – for further information on statistics compiled from the IES please refer to Supplement 1/2008 to the Statistical Bulletin. Reserve Assets: Data are obtained from the balance sheet of Banco de Portugal and information from the Markets and Reserves Department of Banco de Portugal . 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results Direct Investment: Once the results of the data sources are made available, the exercise of reconciliation of stocks and transactions data aims at verifying and checking the statistical information concerning direct investment. A comparison with some EU partner country data is also used as a supplementary tool. Portfolio Investment: Information concerning other Monetary and Financial Institutions is checked with money and banking statistics; as regards listed shares, information made available by Euronext Lisbon is used for verification; for foreign securities, the ESCB Centralized Securities Database (CSDB) is used. The report of portfolio investment includes positions and transactions in quantity and amount. Consistency checks are performed automatically. Whom-to-whom tables comparison with the internal database on securities for debt securities – portfolio + direct investment and by instrument. Financial Derivatives: Reconciliation of stocks and transactions is performed. Other Investment: Deposits and loans are compared with international banking statistics of the BIS, balance sheet of monetary and financial statistics for the bank's sector. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data Statistical discrepancies in intermediate data are investigated, and all the information is checked. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses Studies and analyses of revisions follow the revisions calendar referred in 4.3.1. A statistical quality unit on the department makes periodic reports on the quality of the information concerning BOP/IIP (which includes information on portfolio investment) pointing out specific points for further analysis and potential correction if applicable. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses Revisions are measured, assessed, and explained internally but normally these differences are not communicated on a regular basis unless as stated above the revision implies substantial modification of previously provided figures. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request Unpublished but non-confidential statistics can be disseminated upon request . 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points Prompt and knowledgeable service and support are available to users of statistics.
