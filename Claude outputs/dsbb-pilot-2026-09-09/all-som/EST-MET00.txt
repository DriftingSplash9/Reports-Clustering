 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Definition: The data about trade between Estonia and EU Member States are collected by means of a statistical survey (Intrastat). The data about trade between Estonia and non-EU countries (so-called third states) are based on customs declarations (Extrastat). Foreign trade statistics are compiled on the basis of the Intrastat survey, Extrastat data and an additional monthly survey on the sale of fish and crustaceans in foreign waters and at ports. In the case of Intrastat, the concepts of dispatches and arrivals are used and in the case of Extrastat, the concepts of exports and imports are used. Imports and exports concepts are used for dissemination purposes to determine all merchandise trade flows with the EU and non-EU countries. International standards: The compilation of merchandise trade statistics is in compliance with EU legislation on international trade in goods (European Parliament and of the Council Regulation (EU) No. 2019/2152 on European business statistics, Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No. 2020/1197, Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No. 2021/1225 and Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) No. 2021/1704). 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Classification: Commodities are classified according to the 8-digit codes of the Combined Nomenclature (CN); introduced in May 2004. The CN is based on the Harmonized System (HS) used in international trade. The first six digits equal the HS. Classifications such as the Standard International Trade Classification (SITC), Classification by Broad Economic Categories (BEC), Statistical Classification of Products by Activity in the European Economic Community (CPA), Statistical Classification of Economic Activities in the European Community (NACE) are mainly used for the dissemination of the data. Classification of countries: Nomenclature of Countries and Territories for the External Trade Statistics of the Community and Statistics of Trade between Member States (GEONOM), which is based on the international standard ISO 3166, is used. Scope of the data Coverage of flows: Exports – the exportation of goods produced in Estonia and imported from a foreign country, re-exportation after inward processing, and supplies for foreign vessels and aircraft stores. Exports exclude transit and services. Exports of goods of Estonian origin – the exportation of goods produced or processed in Estonia to other countries. Imports – the importation of goods into Estonia for domestic consumption and for resale to a foreign country, importation for inward processing with a notification of intended return and re-importation after processing outside of Estonia. Imports exclude transit and services. Geographical coverage: All partner countries are covered. Exceptions to coverage Exclusions: Means of payment which are legal tender, and securities a) Monetary gold; b) Emergency aid for disaster areas; c) Goods benefiting from diplomatic, consular or similar immunity; d) Goods for and following temporary use, provided all the following conditions are met: No processing is planned or made, The expected duration of the temporary use is not longer than 24 months, The dispatch/arrival has not to be declared as a delivery/acquisition for VAT purposes; e) Goods used as carriers of information such as floppy disks, computer tapes, films, plans, audio and videotapes, CD-ROMs with stored computer software, where developed to order for a particular client or where they are not subject of a commercial transaction, as well as complements for a previous delivery, e.g. updates for which the consignee is not invoiced; f) Provided that they are not the subject of a commercial transaction: Advertising material, Commercial samples, Goods for display or use at exhibitions, fairs, meetings or similar events; g) Goods for and after repair and the associated replacement parts. A repair entails the restoration of goods to their original function or condition. The objective of the operation is simply to maintain the goods in working order; this may involve some rebuilding or enhancements but does not change the nature of the goods in any way; h) Goods dispatched to national armed forces stationed outside the statistical territory and goods received from another Member State which had been conveyed outside the statistical territory by the national armed forces, as well as goods acquired or disposed of on the statistical territory of a Member State by the armed forces of another Member State which are stationed there; i) Spacecraft launchers, on dispatch and on arrival pending launching into space, and at the time of launching into space; j) Sales of new means of transport by natural or legal persons liable to VAT to private individuals from other Member States. Unrecorded activity Estimates of unrecorded transactions, such as smuggling and 'shuttle' trade, are presently not made. In Extrastat, there are no data losses caused by non-response or by other issues. No estimates are made. In Intrastat, the data under the statistical threshold, non-response or late response are replaced with estimations. The estimations are calculated at the 2-digit commodity code and partner country level. 2.4.1 Valuation Valuation: The value of goods is based on their value at the Estonian frontier (statistical value). Exports are valued in FOB-type value (sale price) and imports are valued in CIF-type value (purchase price). Conversion to unit of account: In Intrastat all data is collected in euros. In the case of Extrastat, the European Central Bank's average rate of the reporting month is used for the conversion of other currencies into euros. 2.4.2 Recording basis Timing of recording transactions: In the case of extra-EU trade, imports/exports are recorded at the time when they enter/leave the economic territory, as noted on the corresponding customs declarations. In the case of intra-EU trade, arrivals/dispatches are recorded in the month in which the goods physically enter or leave the statistical territory of Estonia. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Sources: 1. Extrastat - customs declarations - Single Administrative Document (SAD) about export and import trade with non-EU countries 2. Intrastat - Statistical questionnaire about exports to EU Member States submitted by enterprises. 3. Intrastat micro-data about imports from other EU Member States received via micro-data exchange (MDE) 4. An additional statistical questionnaire on the sale of fish and crustaceans in foreign waters and ports submitted by enterprises. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Type of data collected : In the case of the Intrastat statistical survey, the commodity codes, nature of transaction, net weight, supplementary quantity, invoice value, country of origin and country of destination/consignment are collected. In customs declarations almost all data categories are collected. Basis of country data : For exports, the partner country is the country of destination; for imports, it is the country of consignment. Data about the country of origin are available on request. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness In the case of Intrastat, the 14th calendar day after the end of the reference month is the submission deadline for monthly Intrastat reports. For MDE data, transmission deadline is 30th day after the end of reference month. In the case of Extrastat, trade is recorded at the time of clearance by Customs and the data are transmitted to Statistics Estonia on a monthly basis. Data transmission to Statistics Estonia occurs 20 days after the end of reference month. 3.2.1 Source data assessment During data processing, errors detected by Statistics Estonia are corrected or forwarded for validation to the Estonian Tax and Customs Board (Extrastat data), to the reporting enterprise (Intrastat data) or to the other Member States (MDE data). 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques For intra-EU exports, Intrastat questionnaires do not need to be filled in by enterprises whose turnover is below the statistical threshold. For enterprises whose exports to the EU are below the threshold, estimates based on the VAT data of the Tax and Customs Board are used. Data losses due to non-response or late response are also estimated based on the VAT data. For intra-EU imports, estimates compiled by other EU Member States are used for trade below threshold and non-response since reference year 2025. Estimates have been compiled at the level of two-digit commodity codes of the Combined Nomenclature (CN) and partner countries. Only trade value is available for estimated data (net mass and quantity are not estimated). 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Seasonal adjustment: The data are not seasonally adjusted. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results Regular checks for validity (commodity codes, codes of partner country etc.) and credibility (price, weight controls) are made. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data Intrastat data (highest values) are compared with VAT data. Also, data discrepancies compared to the data of other Member States and outliers are checked monthly. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs For analytical purposes, the time series of specific enterprises and the time series of enterprises by commodity codes are examined. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses Revisions and analyses are carried out monthly during the data processing stage. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request More detailed or other unpublished non-confidential data are available on request. 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points Information on contact points is provided in each publication and news release of Statistics Estonia. All tables in the statistical database are supplemented with information on contact points (under the link “Definitions and Methodology”). 5.3.2 Availability of documents and services catalogs All information about publications and services can be found on the website of Statistics Estonia ( https://www.stat.ee ). The subject area "Foreign trade" ( https://www.stat.ee/en/find-statistics/statistics-theme/economy/foreign-trade ) includes published statistics on merchandise trade.
