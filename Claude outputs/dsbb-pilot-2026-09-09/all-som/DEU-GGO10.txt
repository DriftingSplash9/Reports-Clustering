 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The German system of National Accounts (NA) is based on the European System of National Accounts (ESA 2010), which in turn originated from the System of National Accounts (SNA 2008). Yearly and quarterly data for Germany according to ESA 2010 start at the beginning of 1991. The methodology used for the compilation of nonfinancial accounts of general government as regards revenue, expenditure and net lending/net borrowing is based on ESA 2010 (Regulation (EU) No 549/2013) and the Eurostat Manual on Government Deficit and Debt (MGDD). Transactions in financial assets and liabilities, that is, net acquisition of financial assets and net incurrence of liabilities are recorded according to ESA 2010. The breakdown into domestic and foreign transactions on a separate basis for each financial instrument is currently not available in the framework of the ESA 2010 financial accounts. However, the missing information is approximated by combining the results of the financial accounts with the information provided in BOP statistics (according to BPM 6). From a methodological point of view, both accounting systems are in principle harmonized due to the recent revisions. Based on that, for the presentation of transactions with foreign counterparts balance of payment date are used. The financial transactions with domestic counterparts are calculated as the differences between the total amounts of transactions in financial assets and liabilities and the respective foreign components. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The general government sector and its sub-sectors are defined in accordance with the regulations of ESA 2010 which are also consistent with the SNA 2008 and GFSM 2014. In addition the rules and clarifications provided in the Eurostat MGDD are applied. The sector general government in Germany consists of the central government, state governments, local governments and social security funds. Destatis publishes GFS data for each sub- sector as well as for the consolidated general government sector. Transactions in financial assets/liabilities broken down by instruments are published by the Deutsche Bundesbank. 2.2.1 Scope The general government sector and its sub-sectors are defined in accordance with the regulations of ESA 2010 which are also consistent with the SNA 2008 and GFSM 2014. In addition the rules and clarifications provided in the Eurostat MGDD are applied. The sector general government in Germany consists of the central government, state governments, local governments and social security funds. Destatis publishes GFS data for each sub- sector as well as for the consolidated general government sector. Transactions in financial assets/liabilities broken down by instruments are published by the Deutsche Bundesbank. Scope of the data Data on general government operations cover the core budgets of central government, state government, local government and social security funds as well as extra-budgetary units classified in general government sector. The non-financial transactions consist of the expenditures and revenues as well as the consumption of fixed capital of general government as defined in ESA 2010. The data on financial operations cover the change in financial assets and financing for the whole general government sector. Exceptions to coverage Not applicable. Unrecorded activity Not applicable. 2.4.1 Valuation All nonfinancial transactions are valued in accordance with ESA 2010. Financial transactions are valued partly in accordance with ESA 2010 and partly in accordance with BPM 6 (cf. 2.1.1). 2.4.2 Recording basis Data on nonfinancial transactions of general government are recorded in line with ESA 2010, i.e. the principal of accrual recording is applied. Basis for recording of the transactions in financial assets and liabilities is ESA 2010, i.e. for the recording of net acquisition of financial assets and incurrence of liabilities in principle accrual accounting is applied. For the split of the totals into domestic and foreign transactions partly balance of payments data are used (cf. 2.1.1). Balance of payments data are recorded in line with BPM 6 which also stipulates accrual accounting. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures The grossing/netting procedures are in accordance with the provisions of ESA 2010. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The data sources for general government revenue and expenditure according to ESA 2010 are derived from administrative records. These data are supplemented by comprehensive quarterly and annual surveys for extra-budgetary units. To calculate the items of revenue and expenditure of central government and its special funds, which are used to calculate the net lending/net borrowing of central government in accordance with the NA definition, the Federal Ministry of Finance (BMF) provides quarterly and up-to-date reports on cash revenue and expenditure. These are complete and provide a detailed breakdown of all characteristics of the budget classification for the central government budget and for the special funds of the central government. They are identical with the results recorded at a later date for the central government and its special funds as part of the annual accounting statistics of the public finance statistics. The main data source for calculating the items of revenue and expenditure of the state governments used to derive the net lending/net borrowing as defined in NA are the annual accounting figures of public finance statistics in the case of final results, and the quarterly cash results of public finance statistics in the case of provisional results. The main data source for calculating the items of revenue and expenditure of the local government bodies and the non-profit institutions "Zweckverbände" used to derive the net lending/net borrowing as defined in the NA are the annual accounting figures of public finance statistics in the case of final results, and the quarterly cash results for municipalities of public finance statistics to produce provisional results. The definition and scope of the units covered by the accounting figures on the expenditure and revenue of the overall general government budget are determined by the legal bases and the reporting population for public finance statistics. These are secondary statistics where the data are taken from the annual statement of accounts of institutional units and of other institutions in the public finance statistics' reporting population. It is an exhaustive survey of all units from the public finance statistics reporting population. The reporting population of the survey is laid down in §2 of the Law on public finance statistics. For more detailed information on data sources of general government nonfinancial accounts please see the EDP inventory for Germany following this link http://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/government-finance-statistics/excessive-deficit-procedure /edp-inventories . Figures on the financing of general government are provided by the Deutsche Bundesbank which is responsible for the financial accounts and for balance of payments statistics in Germany; detailed information on financial accounts and balance of payments are available on the website of Deutsche Bundesbank following the links https://www.bundesbank.de/en/statistics/macroeconomic-accounting-systems/financial-account s and https://www.bundesbank.de/en/statistics/external-sector/balance-of-payments . 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording The budgetary classification of the Federation and the Länder is prescribed in administrative regulations, that is, the general note on the system of classification by nature and by function ( Verwaltungsvorschriften des Bundes, Allgemeine Hinweise zum Gruppierungs- und Funktionenplan ). For municipal finances, the municipal functional classification and the system of classification by nature ( kommunaler Produktrahmen, kommunaler Gruppierungsplan ) are used. The source data are classified according to “the key for processing the results of annual accounts of the finance statistics for general government”, which is adjusted annually to changes in budgetary sub classifications. Detailed bridge tables exist to process the data from those units which report their data on the basis of a commercial accounting system and from social security funds which also use a budgetary recording system according to commercial accounting principles. A detailed description of data sources used for the compilation of government nonfinancial accounts can be found in the EDP inventory for Germany following this link http://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/government-finance-statistics/excessive-deficit-procedure /edp-inventories . 3.1.3 Source data timeliness By and large, the data collection and processing timetables are adequate to meet timeliness and periodicity for dissemination of national accounts statistics. A standard procedure informs respondents of the deadlines set for reporting to the statistical offices. 3.2.1 Source data assessment GGO-data are based on very comprehensive administrative records. Transmission errors are possible only to a limited extent, and the data can be checked by using the budgets and annual accounts data. More detailed information on source data assessment can be found in the quality reports on public finance statistics following this link https://www.destatis.de/DE/Methoden/Qualitaet/Qualitaetsberichte/Oeffentliche-Finanzen/ein fuehrung.html (available in German only). 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques The budgetary classifications of the federal and the state ( Länder ) level, on the one hand, and the municipalities and municipal associations, on the other, serve as a structural basis for recording data in the context of finance statistics for general government. Because data sources consist of budget reports no statistical techniques are needed. Errors in the source data are identified by means of plausibility checks and corrected. Non response is usually minimal. If source data are not available on time, they are estimated by means of trend estimations or on the basis of detailed indicators and relevant additional information for the purpose of the first publication in a press release. The estimated data are replaced by the actual data when data are available. For extra budgetary units, social security funds and those municipal governments which report their data on the basis of a commercial accounting system, it is necessary to transform the source data from commercial accounting concepts to budgetary concepts. Detailed bridge tables exist for processing the data. For more information please see the EDP inventory for Germany following this link http://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/government-finance-statistics/excessive-deficit-procedure /edp-inventories . 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures For the compilation of nonfinancial accounts of general government detailed bridge tables exist to transform public finance statistic data based on the budgetary classification and business accounts classification into ESA transactions. Consolidation is established between the sub-sectors of government for the transactions interest, other current transfer, capital transfer and financial transactions. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results Validation of intermediate results is carried out by performing automatic and manual data checks and validation procedures (e.g. compilation of growth rates, year over year comparisons, reconciliation with background information such as business reports). Reconciliation with the financial accounts is carried out. Checks whether the compilations are accurate, proper and consistent, comparisons with previous reporting periods and analyses of inconsistencies are implemented. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data Assessment of intermediate data is carried out: After the validation step still existing unexplained deviations are analyzed in detail. The results are evaluated in the context of the economic situation. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs GGO-data are compared with both the source data and the data from the Bundesbank and the MOF and checked for plausibility. If results do not correspond to what was expected, for instance if there are outliers, the causes are studied. If no statistical reason is found for the discrepancy, economic causes are examined to see whether they explain the phenomenon. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses Articles containing an analysis of revisions are published in the FSO series “Wirtschaft und Statistik” (Economy and Statistics). In addition, the general inventory of methods is revised after a major revision, so that it can be used as a reliable source of changed methods and data. Also the EDP Inventory which provides detailed information on the compilation of general government revenues and expenditures is amended if required. Apart from statistical publications on revisions, meetings with main users of national accounts take place where revisions are explained. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request Most national accounts data that are fit for dissemination are made available in GENESIS-Online and the online publication service. However, to a limited extent, other data or customized tabulations to meet specific requests can be made available upon request. Depending on the requested statistics and the amount of work involved, they are provided free of charge or for a small fee. 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points All publications containing the National Accounts data include information about contact points. The national accounts information and support service is available Mondays to Thursdays from 8 p.m. to 5 p.m. and Fridays from 8 p.m. to 3 p.m. The Destatis information service can always be contacted by using the Destatis contact form https://www.destatis.de/EN/Service/Contact/Contact.html . 5.3.2 Availability of documents and services catalogs All publications are available on Destatis website: http://www.destatis.de/ .
