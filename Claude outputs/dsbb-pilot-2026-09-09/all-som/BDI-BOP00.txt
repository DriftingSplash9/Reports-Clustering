 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Burundi's balance of payments is generally compiled in accordance with the concepts, definitions, and methodology in the 6th edition of the IMF Balance of Payments Manual and International Investment Position Manual ( BPM 6 ). Its compilation is the task of the the balance of payments division, Departement of Statistics, Directorate of Economic Research and Statistics at the Bank of the Republic of Burundi (BRB). 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The balance of payments transactions are classified in line with the standard components of the BPM6. The components are classified into four major accounts that provide an analytical presentation of the balance of payments and facilitates identification of the overall balance. Errors and omissions Scope of the data Burundi's balance of payments covers all transactions between residents and non-residents at a given period. It also covers Burundi's diplomatic enclaves abroad. On the other hand, transactions of diplomatic enclaves and international agencies in Burundi with other economies, for instance those of United Nations agencies, are excluded. In principle, all transactions are covered, namely, imports and exports of merchandise and services, secondary incomes, primary incomes, capital transfers, and financial transactions. However, within the BOP statement, there are difficulties in: •covering some items under goods services and income because of the limitations of the source data used; •Same data are not collected on the regular basis. 2.4.1 Valuation All ttransactions in BOP are valued at current market price. 2.4.2 Recording basis Export and imports of Goods are recorded on accrual basis. Exports and imports of services, primary incomes, secondary income and capital transfers are recorded on cash basis because using the data from ITRS except for the transportation debit services which are estimated. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The balance of payments statistics are compiled on the basis of a mixed system comprising reports of banking operations in foreign currency submitted by the Commercial banks to the BRB and supplemented with data from administrative sources at the Customs, the Ministry of Finance, the Department of Reserve asset Management at central bank , the Financial and Monetary statistics division at the BRB, Money Transfer Operators (MTOs) and survey for some components of the BOP. Include also estimations of some components like the Fret and informal cross border trade. Merchandise trade Statistics on merchandise imports and exports come from customs offices, which record exports, f.o.b. and imports, c.i.f. Data on tea exports come from the Burundi Tea Office (OTB). Transportation The data on merchandise transportation are currently estimated at 18 percent of customs declarations of imports after the deduction of imports intended for diplomatic enclaves and international agencies Other services Information on all other type of services come from declarations of Commercial Banks and the Central Bank of all transactions between residents and non-residents. However, some data could be adjusted by adding data from FDI survey if available. Primary income Compensation of employees Data on compensation of employees are obtained from exchange records from banks and central bank. Investment income Information on interest on the official exchange reserves is obtained directly from the BRB. Information on interest paid by the government on its external debt is obtained from the Ministry of Finance and information on interest on private nonbank debt is obtained from banks' declarations on their exchange operations or directly from the banks themselves in the case of income on their assets and liabilities. Private interest data could be adjusted by adding data from FDI survey when available.. Secondary income Data on current transfers are obtained from the exchange records from commercial banks and central bank. Are adjusted by adding data on humanitarian aid captured trough embassies and international organizations imports for the grants in kind. Capital account This item consists mainly of project grants of bilateral and multilateral assistance. The information is obtained from the banks through which this assistance transits. It include also the debt forgiveness by the partners to the Burundi. Information on official debt forgiveness is collected from administrative source Financial Account Direct investment Data on inflows and outflows are mainly obtained from FDI survey. However, because of lack in financial resources, a survey is done every two years to update the data on the direct investment. Portfolio investment Data on inflows and outflows are obtained with FDI surveys. Other investment Transactions in government assets and liabilities are obtained from data provided by the Ministry of Finance, supplemented by information obtained from the Treasury. Data on foreign asset and liabilities of the BRB and commercial banks are obtained from the Monetary and Financial Statistics division of BRB (1SR & 2SR). It includes also the trade credit on import and exports estimated base on the total import value and the amount paid for imports or the total value of export and the value of money paid for exports. However, the data could be adjusted by the information on trade credit from FDI survey. Data on other components of other investments could be collected through FDI survey Reserve assets Information on the reserve assets is provided by the MFS department, but data on the SDR holding and reserve tranche position to IMF can found via the IMF website. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording The source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording are focus generally on BPM6 manual. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Data are monthly (customs data, MTOs, tea, fuel from airport) and data from ITRS (commercial banks and central bank) are sending on daily basis. 3.2.1 Source data assessment The assessment is frequently done by technical committee of trade (Central Bank, ISTEEBU, Burundi authority revenue etc.). This assessment is done also on the Informal cross border trade survey and FDI survey. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Merchandise The following adjustments are made to the figures reported by customs on total imports: Exclusion of imports intended for non-resident enclaves (embassies and international agencies); Deduction of transport and insurance costs, estimated at about 18 percent of the net value, c.i.f., of imports by the above-mentioned non-resident enclaves; Addition of undeclared imports, estimated at about 5 percent of the total declared to take account of informal cross-border trade, Freight and insurance on imports are estimated on the basis of a factor estimated at 18 percent of imports by Burundi resident units Adjustment of trade credit for financial account . Other Investment and reserve assets : Derivation of specific transaction from position in assets and liabilities. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results Existence of technical validation committee on the trade. This committee is made by staff from the Central Banks, National Bureau of Statistics, and the Burundi Authority revenue, Ministry of commerce and the Agency of mining and quarries. The results from Informal Cross Border Trade and Foreign Direct Investment surveys have to be validated by BRB and National Bureau of Statistics prior release 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data There is no assessment in IIP, but discrepancies assessment is always done to check the consistency between other macroeconomic data. Data on the reserve position in the IMF and SDR allocation are verified through the published IMF accounting data. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs FDI survey results have to be analyzed and validated by the technical team before to be published to the public. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses There is no policy of systematic revision and but when any change is made, we proceed by update. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses Not done. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request Aggregate data can be disseminated on the individualised request
