 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The CBS is compiled broadly based on Monetary and Financial Statistics Manual and Compilation Guide (MFSMCG, 2016) methodology. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The principles for classifying financial institutions’ clients by economic sector in monetary data for Myanmar are as follows: Concerning definition of residents/nonresidents, The Foreign Exchange Management Law (of August 10, 2012 ) states: (a) Residents are:  Individuals who have been physically present or have had their main residence in the State for a period of at least 183 days during the preceding 12-month period, with the exception of foreign civil servants on diplomatic or similar assignments.  Legal entities incorporated under the local law and branches of legal entities incorporated under foreign law that are established in the State;  Myanmar civil servants on diplomatic or other foreign assignments. (b) Non-resident are those who are not included in the list of residents. The definition of residents/nonresidents in The Foreign Exchange Management Law (of August 10, 2012 ) is broadly consistent with the application of the center of predominant economic interest criteria (see below). However, the current practice is still the one in which the banks identify a client as a resident/nonresident based on the “passport:” physical persons with foreign passports, even those qualified as “resident foreigners” based on their visa, are classified as nonresidents. Legal persons/entities, such as enterprises, which are foreign-controlled (as identified in their Articles of Associate/Incorporation and the operating license issued by the Ministry of National Planning and Economic Development) are classified as nonresidents. The resident sector is further subdivided according to the sectorization scheme of the IMF’s Standardized Report Forms (SRFs)—namely, central government; state and local governments; other financial corporations; public nonfinancial corporations; and private sector. The following three comments are in order on the application of this scheme. The fiscal decentralization envisaged in the Constitution of 2008 would provide autonomy to the local governments (state/regional/municipal authorities). However, at present, local authorities are effectively an extension of central government at a local level. In the current monetary statistics compilation framework, their positions with the banks are classified as the banks’ positions with the central government and state/local government. The banks recognize a client as a publicly-owned enterprise (a public nonfinancial corporation ) based on the documentation, issued by the Budget Department of the MOF, that identifies the client as a “state economic enterprise” (SEE). These are 100 percent state-owned. The banks’ accounting framework provides for a separate identification of the SEE accounts (under the SFA(SEE)) and the summary positions of the main SFA(SEE) are included in the source data made available to the CBM’s compilers. A large number of MFSM -defined financial instruments can be discerned in the source data currently used as the basis for Myanmar monetary statistics. But, in addition to loan , securities , shares , current / time / saving accounts, there are a number of accounts, arising from the Myanmar current settlement/payment system, which are identified in various ways as “settlement/payment/receivable/payable” accounts. Some of these accounts have been classified in settlement accounts under other accounts payable/receivable with respect to the economic sector of counterparty, if identified. 2.2.1 Scope The principles for classifying financial institutions’ clients by economic sector in monetary data for Myanmar are as follows: Concerning definition of residents/nonresidents, The Foreign Exchange Management Law (of August 10, 2012 ) states: (a) Residents are:  Individuals who have been physically present or have had their main residence in the State for a period of at least 183 days during the preceding 12-month period, with the exception of foreign civil servants on diplomatic or similar assignments.  Legal entities incorporated under the local law and branches of legal entities incorporated under foreign law that are established in the State;  Myanmar civil servants on diplomatic or other foreign assignments. Scope of the data The scope of Myanmar’s central bank survey is the Central Bank of Myanmar—its headquarters and domestic branches. Exceptions to coverage The CBM is a depository for the IMF.
