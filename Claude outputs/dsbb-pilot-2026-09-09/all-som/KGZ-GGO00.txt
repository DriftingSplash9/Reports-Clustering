 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The methodology for compiling government finance statistics is consistent with the analytical framework set out in the IMF's A Manual on Government Finance Statistics (GFSM) 2014. The documentation on the methodology is contained in the Resolution of the Government of the Kyrgyz Republic No. 488 of August 16, 2017. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization • Analytical framework: Budget classification of the Kyrgyz Republic, approved by the Resolution of the Government of the Kyrgyz Republic dated December 21, 2017 No. 161-P, which provides for the unification of the budget classification and international comparability of budget data - Budget Code of the Kyrgyz Republic dated May 16, 2016 No. 60. - IMF Government Finance Statistics Manual 2014 (GFSM-2014). • Classification system: - Cash receipts from operating activities (income), include: tax revenues, contributions/deductions for social needs, grants and non-tax revenues. In the revenue part, non-tax revenues also reflect extra-budgetary (special) funds of budgetary organizations. - Cash payments for operating activities (expenses) are broken down according to the economic classification of expenses, including: compensation of employees, use of goods and services, interest, subsidies, grants, social benefits and payments, other expenses. - Cash flows in connection with investments in non-financial assets, including: acquisition and sale of non-financial assets. - Cash flows in connection with financing transactions, including: net acquisition of financial assets and net assumption of liabilities. Scope of the data Institutional coverage : The data cover only budget institutions. Off-budget operations and social security program operations (Social Fund) are not included in the data. Exceptions to coverage Off-budget operations and social security program operations (Social Fund) are not included in the data. 2.4.1 Valuation Valuation method: For conversion of transactions from foreign currency (in US dollars and other currencies), the official rate of the National Bank of the Kyrgyz Republic is applied. 2.4.2 Recording basis • Accounting basis: Accounting and reporting on financial statistics is carried out on the basis of the Manual on Government Finance Statistics 2014. The cash method is used for accounting. • Consolidation method used: Consolidated data are compiled for government bodies as a whole, including special funds of budgetary institutions 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Data sources: data are compiled based on recording, treasury reports and accounting of the budget institutions. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Consolidation technique applied: Consolidated data on general government are compiled based on the data for central government, local authorities, including special funds of budgetary institutions.
