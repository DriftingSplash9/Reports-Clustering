Miscellaneous 4.1% Scope of the data At present, about 200 formal manufacturing establishments are covered. These establishments are estimated to cover about 70 percent of manufacturing Value Added. For these establishments survey data (sales values) is collected monthly by field staff. Data on Coffee, Tea Processing and Cotton Ginning are collected in aggregate from Uganda Coffee Development Authority, Uganda Tea Association and Cotton Development Organisation respectively. For these commodities, procurement data is used as a proxy for production. After computing the value index, an appropriate index (The PPIs) is used to deflate the value index. Exceptions to coverage The index covers only the formal manufacturing establishments; it excludes other sectors of the economy like the Utilities and Mining. Unrecorded activity The informal manufacturing establishments are excluded in the compilation of the IoP. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures In computing the index, the weights are hierarchical and the laspayeres methodology is used 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Data is collected by field staff on a monthly basis. Where there are gaps, survey data is bridged by VAT data from Uganda Revenue Authority. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording The Data collected from Business establishments across the country meets the required definition, scope, classification and time of recording. 3.2.1 Source data assessment Source data is checked by comparing data from other businesses in the same industry, ensuring correct replacement for products out of production with new products, imputing for missing data, in case of errors in the data, calls are placed to the respondents to obtain clarification. Plus also the data from the survey is verified using the data collected from URA. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques The ISIC coding of data from URA is verified to ensure correct coding. Data on the activity of the business is obtained as one of the variables 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Sales data is collected from URA. This is data is then validated using survey data (Both sales and production) collected from the formal manufacturing establishments. But in cases of Coffee, Tea and Cotton quantity data is collected from the authorities that deal in these products. A value index is computed and deflated by the Producer Price Index. A Laspeyres formula is used to calculate the index. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results Ø The system has an inbuilt mechanism for checking data quality. Ø We use the Tukey Outlier detection model which leverages the Interquartile range (IQR) to detect outliers in a data set also use of Scatter plots/Box Plots to identify outliers, standard errors among others. Ø Computation of percentage change in quantity for each product. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data For all data collected, scatter graphs are used to identify outliers. Where there are discrepancies, information is sought from the business. In addition the VAT data is used to check the accuracy of the survey data, this comparison is used to remove inconsistencies. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs In case of a problem with the output or discrepancies; The data processing is revisited to ensure that the correct data was captured. A call is placed to the establishments that’s if problem could have been misreporting or change of respondents. For missing quantities and/or outliers, appropriate imputation techniques are applied. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses The UBOS revision policy allows for revision of the indices for only one previous period after dissemination. Hence, the IoP data produced is revised for the previous quarter. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses The revised data are disseminated during the subsequent release. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request The Central bank and the Ministry of Finance also require some specific data which is disseminated on request. It is also shared during the Weekly Tripartite meeting between UBOS, central bank and the Ministry of Finance, planning & Economic Development
