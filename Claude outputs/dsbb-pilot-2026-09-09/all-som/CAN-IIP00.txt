 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Analytical framework: Canada's international investment position is the statistical statement that presents the value and composition of financial assets and liabilities with non-residents at period-end. Data are published quarterly with additional detail available annually. The Canadian statement is compiled largely in conformity to international standards and conventions described in the sixth edition of the Balance of Payments and International Investment Position Manual (BPM6) of the International Monetary Fund (IMF). 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Classification system: Data recorded in the Canadian International investment Position are largely classified according to international standards and conventions described in the sixth edition of Balance of Payments and International Investment position Manual (BPM6). The most important classification issues are related to Derivatives which are classified under “Other investment”. Also, positions related to Real Estate held by households are included in “Other asset/Other liability”. Scope of the data The majority of economic sectors, as identified by BPM6 (Governments, Central Bank, Non-financial corporations, financial institutions, Pension funds), are fully covered by the International Investment Position. Households and Non-profit institutions serving households are also covered but with some data gaps. Data cover the entire Canadian territory and include financial assets and liabilities vis-à-vis all countries of the world. Instrument coverage: positions on Derivatives are partially covered. 2.4.1 Valuation Canada's external financial assets and liabilities are recorded at market value as recommended by international standards (BPM6). Book value measures are also available as supplementary information. Conversion to unit of account: Positions denominated in original currencies are converted to Canadian dollars using the closing rate of exchange at period-end. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The statistical information used to compile the International Investment position comes from a variety of sources (such as business surveys, government public accounts and statements from financial institutions) and portray the economic situation, at a point in time, between residents and non-residents. The data are also closely monitored using a variety of other sources such as foreign source banking data, company reports, business publications, and public financial data. Direct investment: Most of the data used to derive direct investment positions originates from economic surveys of Canadian companies that are asked to consolidate all of their Canadian operations and accordingly are referred to as Canadian enterprises. A number of administrative and other data sources are also used such as, the Bank of Canada, the financial press, electronic business publications and company reports. Portfolio investment: A coordinated Portfolio Investment Survey is the main source for the benchmark annual estimates of portfolio asset. Other infra annual surveys are used to build quarterly positions. On the liability side, annual and quarterly surveys, administrative and public sources are the main sources of data. Official international reserve assets: Month-end outstanding positions of reserve assets obtained from administrative sources. Other investment: Data are collected from surveys, statements of commercial banks and other administrative sources. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Direct investment: Annual surveys of the book value of direct investment form the basis of the benchmark estimates for the year-end positions. Quarterly positions between these benchmarks and for periods subsequent to the latest benchmark data are estimated using quarterly flows at market value and appropriate revaluation factors. Market valuation is calculated using (i) market values for listed companies and (ii) a market capitalization ratio (derived for listed companies) for unlisted companies, over a certain size threshold. Portfolio investment: Asset: the Canadian Portfolio Investment Survey (CPIS) provides a benchmark for annual positions at market value. Quarterly positions between these benchmarks and for periods subsequent to the latest benchmark data are estimated using quarterly flows at market value and appropriate revaluation factors. Liabilities: A detailed system is used to process data on Canadian bonds, listed shares and money market instruments. Positions are derived on an issue by issue basis and can be automatically produced on a quarterly basis. Annual benchmark positions are obtained from annual surveys. Official international reserve assets: Official international reserve assets are available on a monthly basis. Other investment: Outstanding loans issued by banks and governments are available on a quarterly basis. Loans from other corporations are derived from annual surveys and quarterly flows. Deposits are also available from the banks on a quarterly basis. The other miscellaneous accounts are based on annual surveys adjusted to quarterly positions using quarterly flows. Currency fluctuations: In addition, outstanding positions for all accounts denominated in foreign currency are re-evaluated each quarter to reflect changes in exchange rates. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Quarterly market value positions between benchmarks and for periods since the latest benchmark data are projected using monthly and quarterly flows collected from surveys and other changes, in particular currency valuation and price changes. Revisions are performed when new annual benchmarks are introduced, increasing the quality of both annual and quarterly estimates. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses Major changes to the estimates resulting from the implementation of new concepts and methodologies are explained to users through special studies (example: Revisions to Canada's International Investment Position ). Users are also informed of reference periods that have been revised at the time the data are disseminated.
