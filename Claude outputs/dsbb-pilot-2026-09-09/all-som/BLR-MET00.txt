 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions As set out in the balance of payments methodology, merchandise trade statistics of the Republic of Belarus is compiled in accordance with international standards set out in the sixth edition of The Balance of Payments and International Investment Position Manual (IMF, 2009) (BPM6). According to the balance of payments methodology, merchandise trade covers goods whose economic ownership is changed between a resident of the Republic of Belarus and a nonresident. 2.2.1 Scope As set out in the balance of payments methodology, merchandise trade statistics of the Republic of Belarus is compiled in accordance with international standards set out in the sixth edition of The Balance of Payments and International Investment Position Manual (IMF, 2009) (BPM6). According to the balance of payments methodology, merchandise trade covers goods whose economic ownership is changed between a resident of the Republic of Belarus and a nonresident. Scope of the data Transactions coverage : According to the balance of payments methodology, the exports and imports of goods items show the value of goods ownership of which within a reporting period transferred from residents to nonresidents (exports) and from nonresidents to residents (imports). The Goods item also includes re-exports and nonmonetary gold. Geographic coverage : The economic territory of the Republic of Belarus covers the country's geographic area under the jurisdiction of the Government of the Republic of Belarus, within which labour, goods and capital can move freely. The country's economic territory includes land and air space, territorial enclaves in other countries (distinct areas situated in other countries, which belong to or are leased by the Government of the Republic of Belarus for diplomatic, military, scientific or any other purposes, free zones or customs warehouses and enterprises used under control of customs authorities). The economic territory of the Republic of Belarus does not include enclaves of foreign governments and international organizations located within the geographic area of the Republic of Belarus. Exceptions to coverage The exports and imports of goods are not included goods in the following specific categories: parts of travel, construction, and government goods and services n.i.e. 2.4.1 Valuation Goods are evaluated at market value at the point in customs frontier of the economy from which the goods are first exported. The costs of goods delivery beyond the borders of the exporting country is included in transportation, i.e. recognized as services. Exports and imports of goods are valuated in FOB prices (FOB means sales condition according to which the price of goods includes its value and expenses for insurance, freight, and shipping on board of the carrier vehicle in frontier of the exporting country). Data on merchandise trade are compiled in US dollars. Transactions denominated in other currencies are converted into US dollars using the official foreign currencies rates fixed by the National Bank as of transaction date. 2.4.2 Recording basis A major criterion for transactions in goods to be included in the balance of payments is transactions in goods should be recorded as of time that the change of economic ownership takes place. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures According to the balance of payments methodology, data on merchandise trade are recorded on gross terms. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The following are major sources of information used for compilation of merchandise trade within the balance of payments: information of the National Statistics Committee of the Republic of Belarus (the Belstat) on merchandise trade of the Republic of Belarus; information of the State Customs Committee of the Republic of Belarus; reporting on foreign economic activity submitted to the National Bank; the National Bank's estimates: adjusting imports of goods from CIF to FOB prices; adjusting the cost of imports of goods to market value; partner country data. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Export and import data are developed on the basis of the Belstat international merchandise trade statistics and adjusted in accordance with the Balance of Payments methodology (BPM6). To compile the monthly statistics of merchandise trade, the Belstat: uses customs statistics on foreign trade in goods of the Republic of Belarus with the states that are not members of the Eurasian Economic Union (EEU), and statistics of mutual trade with the EEU member states; considers State statistical reporting on exports and imports of certain commodity items (oil, natural gas liquids, electric power, potassium and nitrogenous fertilizers), exports and imports of bunker used for aircraft fueling; assesses the volume of exports and imports of certain commodity items unrecognized by official statistics: assessment of exports and imports of certain commodity items unrecognized by the foreign trade customs statistics is based on a balancing method that measures the difference between available supplies of each commodity item and their use (consumption); uses statistics of sampling survey of natural persons in road border inspection posts of the Republic of Belarus aimed at studying irregular delivery and removal of goods by natural persons, and trends of international travel flows; uses calculation of volume of imports and exports of goods purchased by natural persons from web shop, based on the National Bank's survey of cross-border commodity flows under electronic commerce. The National Bank adjusts the Belstat's statistics on merchandise trade in accordance with the balance of payments methodology (BPM6): volumes of exports and imports are adjusted against the volume of goods procured in ports by carriage providers using data obtained from the International Transaction Reporting System (ITRS); transactions that do not represent exports and imports of goods according to the balance of payments methodology: returned goods, goods sent for processing, goods returned from processing, shall be excluded from the Belstat's volumes of export and import of goods; imports of goods in CIF prices (with allowances made for cost of freight and insurance services) according to the Belstat is converted to FOB prices (exclusive of cost of freight and insurance services); valuation of imports of goods is adjusted to the market price. Other adjustments can be made by the National Bank in exceptional cases. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness The timetable of source data input ensures timely compilation of merchandise trade data in accordance with the balance of payments methodology and comply with the requirements of the Special Data Dissemination Standard of the International Monetary Fund (SDDS) pertaining to dissemination of data of the external sector. 3.2.1 Source data assessment Compilers of merchandise trade data in accordance with the balance of payments methodology assess source data on a consistent basis and initiate the source data modification process when necessary, to improve statistical reporting. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques The National Bank adjusts the Belstat statistics on merchandise trade in accordance with the balance of payments methodology. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures In the balance of payments, data on merchandise trade are not detailed by types. The data is not seasonally adjusted. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results Merchandise trade data recognized in the balance of payments are checked against information of payments obtained from the ITRS. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data Intermediate data are analyzed in terms of internal and temporal consistency. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs When unaccountable wide variation of data occurs, its causes are studies through direct contacts with information providers. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses Data for the reporting period are intermediate when first released. The data revision policy for merchandise trade statistics in accordance with the balance of payments methodology is determined by data revision rules and described in the National Bank's quarterly data analytics bulletin "The Balance of Payments, International Investment Position, and Gross External Debt of the Republic of Belarus", though notes are made that the Balance of Payments statistics may be modified in future publications. A separate annex contains the list of modifications introduced in the Balance of Payments, indicating the reason and period for which they have been introduced (quarterly), per each item. Modifications may be introduced due to update of reported data, changes in methodology or methods of calculation of certain indicators, development of new sources of information, reclassification of instruments (indicators), update of reported data upon agreement with partner countries, for other reasons. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses Historical data series of data on foreign trade in goods in accordance with the balance of payment methodology from 2003 to 2011 were made accessible in accordance with the fifth edition of the Balance of Payments Manual and starting from 2000 to the present day in accordance with BPM6. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request Unpublished non-confidential statistics may be provided on request. 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points The metadata placed on the National Bank's official Internet site and the quarterly data analytics bulletin "The Balance of Payments, International Investment Position, and Gross External Debt of the Republic of Belarus" specify the name, telephone numbers, e-mail address of the contact person. 5.3.2 Availability of documents and services catalogs The list of publications of the National Bank is available on the National Bank's official Internet site.
