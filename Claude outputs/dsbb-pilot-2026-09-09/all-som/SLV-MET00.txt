 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Definition: Following the International Trade Statistics: Concepts and Definitions UN 2010 (UN ITS), exports/imports are defined as the value of goods which subtract from/add to the stock of the country's material resources. The methodology with national specifications is published on the website. Relationship of national practices with international and regional guidelines: The collection of merchandise trade statistics is in line with the recommendations of UN ITS and IMF's Balance of Payments and International Investment Position Manual (6th edition). 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Classification: Goods are classified using the Central American Tariff System (10 digits), and the Harmonized System (6 digits). There are also participations by co-participating countries. The trade of goods is registered in gross values. Exports and imports are also classified into: Agriculture, livestock farming, forestry and fishing: Gold coffee and other unprocessed coffees and other products Mining and quarrying Manufacturing: Sugar cane and other sugars and processed coffee Electricity, gas, steam and air conditioning supply Wholesale and retail trade; repair of motor vehicles and motorcycles Manufacturing of Maquila: Maquila of other products, maquila of point dress garments and maquila of Textiles The imports are classified into: consumer goods, which are classified into durable and non-durable goods; intermediate goods, divided into manufacturing, agricultural, construction, and other goods; capital goods, further divided into manufacturing, transportation, agricultural, construction, commerce, electricity, gas and water, and other goods; and maquila goods. Foreign trade statistics apply 17 international classifiers that group data by economic activity, product type, technological intensity, geographical area, gender, biodiversity, among others. Scope of the data Transactions coverage: All international transactions in merchandise goods excluding monetary gold, goods in transit and goods to and from territorial enclaves. Geographical coverage: Entire economic territory of the country coinciding with the customs territory. Exceptions to coverage Transactions of entities that are not under obligation of filling out statements of goods are not included. Unrecorded activity Unrecorded activity: No estimates are made for smuggling, other illegal trade and for unrecorded transactions. Valuation: Exports and imports are valued free on board (FOB). Imports are also valued inclusive of cost, insurance and freight (CIF). The information also contains the value FOB of the imports, which is used for ends (purposes) of the record of the goods in the balance of payments. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures The trade of goods is registered in gross values. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Data Sources: Customs declarations forms. Type of data collected: Value of imports and exports in US dollars, and quantities by detailed classification. Basis of country data: Country of origin (for imports) and country of destination (exports). The data sources for both exports and imports are the customs declarations and the administrative records of the institutions responsible for administering special products. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Besides the information coming from declarations of goods, complementary information is obtained from other sources on goods procured at airports. In general, there is a good degree of application of the definitions, scope, classification and time of registry prescribed by the Balance of Payments and International Investment Position Manual (6th edition). 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Agreements for delivery of data are pursuant to the presentation dates of statistics. In practice, we have complied with the dates of delivery of information agreed with the sources. 3.2.1 Source data assessment Different data verification and validation criteria were applied, most are included directly in the processing system available. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Adjustments to improve coverage/classification/timing/valuation: There are on-going exercises to remove errors in the data arising from incorrect completion of the customs documentation or through failings in the processing system (for example, keying errors). There are also adjustments when liquidations of goods are carried out with some delay. Timing of recording transactions: Imports/exports are recorded at the time when they enter/leave the economic territory as noted on the corresponding customs declarations. Reconciliation: Imports/exports data are compared with partner country data but there are no corrections on this basis. It is recognized that differences in trade statistics could, as well, be due to different customs procedures, different classifications, under-reporting, timing, etc. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Processing system: Customs data are processed automatically in a nationally developed computerized system. Site of processing: Central Reserve Bank. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results Foreign trade data by country is compared with those of the main business partner and other partners. Although no adjustments have been carried out from those comparisons. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data Comparisons of the results with variables of the real sector and the economic behavior and results of the main business partner's trade are made. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses No such studies have been carried out. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses Bank’s publications disclose users the reasons giving way to review the balance of payments statistics, although the studies explain in detail such changes dare not available to the public. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request At the public's request, non-confidential information that is not disclosed in the bank’s publications is provided. 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points Adonay Jiménez: adonay.jimenez@bcr.gob.sv Kevin Ramírez: kevin.ramirez@bcr.gob.sv 5.3.2 Availability of documents and services catalogs There is no catalog of publications, documents and other services.
