 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Analytical framework: Data disseminated for the monetary authorities accounts are compiled on a daily basis and are based on the consolidation of the Central Bank of Armenia (CBA) balance sheet data, following the IMF's Monetary and Financial Statistics Manual and Compilation Guide ( MFSMCG ) methodology. Positions held outside the CBA balance sheet are not included in the analytical accounts of the CBA. The data are presented in stock positions in millions of national currency units. These data include the components of monetary base: currency outside the CBA (currency issued excluding currency at the CBA), correspondent accounts of commercial banks at the CBA (both in national and foreign currencies), other accounts in national and foreign currencies. Other data disseminated on the CBA accounts are as follows: Net international reserves are broken down by gross international reserves in convertible currencies and gross foreign liabilities in convertible and national currencies. Net domestic assets, including separate data on gross claims on and liabilities to the general government, claims on commercial banks, securities issued by the CBA (with negative sign), and other net domestic assets. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Classification: Foreign positions are distinguished from domestic positions on the basis of residency. The statistical definition of residency is given in the CBA’s Manual on Compilation of Monetary and Financial Statistics in Armenia (section "Sectors of Economy") and conforms to that of "Balance of Payments and International Investment Position Manual", sixth edition ( BPM6 ). Domestic positions are classified by sectors of economy: government, banking sector (other than the central bank), non-financial corporations, households. Within each category accounts are broken down by currency (domestic currency, foreign convertible currencies, foreign non-convertible currencies). 2.2.1 Scope Classification: Foreign positions are distinguished from domestic positions on the basis of residency. The statistical definition of residency is given in the CBA’s Manual on Compilation of Monetary and Financial Statistics in Armenia (section "Sectors of Economy") and conforms to that of "Balance of Payments and International Investment Position Manual", sixth edition ( BPM6 ). Domestic positions are classified by sectors of economy: government, banking sector (other than the central bank), non-financial corporations, households. Within each category accounts are broken down by currency (domestic currency, foreign convertible currencies, foreign non-convertible currencies). Scope of the data Institutional coverage: Analytical accounts of the central bank include all units and branches (if any) of the CBA. 2.4.1 Valuation Valuation of domestic currency denominated financial instruments: Loans and deposits are valued at nominal value of contracts. Securities held for sale and trading securities initially are recorded at their acquisition cost and are revalued daily based on fair value. In monetary statistics, securities held to maturity are valued at market price at the end of the month. Valuation of positions with the IMF: Positions with the IMF are revalued daily. The basis for calculation of these positions is the average market Armenian dram/U.S. dollar exchange rate announced by the CBA and USD/SDR rate of the IMF. Valuation of foreign-currency-denominated instruments: assets and liabilities denominated in foreign currencies are revalued daily at average market exchange rates announced by the CBA. 2.4.2 Recording basis Accounting practices: The accounts of the CBA are recorded on accrual basis. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures Provisions: Central bank’s and the commercial banks’ provisions for loan losses and other purposes are included in other accounts receivable/payable of the monetary survey. Provisions are not netted against domestic credit. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Data sources: The source of data is the balance sheet of the CBA. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Source data meet the internationally accepted practices. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness The source data are available timely to meet recommended standards of timeliness and periodicity for dissemination of monetary statistics. The CBA balance sheet is available daily. 3.2.1 Source data assessment The source data are analyzed for accuracy by the Statistics and Accounting Departments, and assessment procedures are sound. The software program for generating monetary statistics offers the possibility to cross-check data. The central bank data are reviewed on a regular basis by the Internal Audit Department of the CBA. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques The Central Bank Survey is based on actual accounting records and compiled daily on the basis of balance sheet of previous day. Statistical adjustments: No statistical adjustments are made to the data. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results Data verification: Analytical accounts of CBA are verified by the following methods: Accuracy of daily changes of each account is verified by individual transactions. The basic accounting equivalency of net foreign assets plus net domestic assets to total liabilities plus capital accounts is ensured. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data Data are regularly monitored to investigate statistical discrepancies and to ensure data accuracy. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs The CBA is committed to investigate any significant discrepancies that may be found. When relatively large fluctuations occur in the accounts due to random effects, they are thoroughly explained in statistical publications. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses When data are revised, current revisions are in the footnotes and comments to the tables. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request Additional information (non-confidential) is available upon request. Users can request additional information either by contacting the contact point or the Public Relations Service of the CBA, which will forward the inquiries to the relevant department for response. The contact points are available on the CBA website and in the publications for inquiries. 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points Contact information (e-mail addresses and telephone numbers) are available on the CBA website. Assistance and support are provided to users, who can pose queries by contacting the contact point or the Public Relations Service of the CBA, which will forward the inquiries to the relevant department. 5.3.2 Availability of documents and services catalogs The CBA website provides users with considerable amount of information on publications and documents.
