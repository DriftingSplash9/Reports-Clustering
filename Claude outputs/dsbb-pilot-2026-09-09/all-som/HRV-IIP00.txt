 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Analytical Framework: BPM6 and ESA2010 are used as the underlying conceptual framework. End-of-period stocks of external financial assets and liabilities are presented under the following data categories: direct investment, portfolio investment (broken down into equity and debt), financial derivatives, other investment and reserve assets. Definition: The IIP is the statistical statement that provides information on stock positions of Croatia's external financial assets and liabilities at the end of the reporting period. Direct investment: In line with the definitions set forth in BPM6, direct investment covers equity, reinvested earnings and other capital. The 10% rule is strictly applied. Portfolio investment: The position portfolio investment covers portfolio equity investments and portfolio debt investments (long term and short term separately). Other investment: Regarding BPM6 methodology the position Other investment covers: other equity investments, currency and deposits, loans, trade credits, other accounts receiveble/payable and SDR on the liability side. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The classification of stock position is based on the sixth edition of Balance of Payments Manual (BPM6) and the ESA 2010. Scope of the data Coverage of external financial assets and liabilities: According to the BPM6 . Sectorial coverage: The sector classification of the portfolio and other investment involves the sector classification of residents according to ESA2010 and is fully harmonised with the sector classification of the gross external debt by domestic sectors and the balance of payments. Geographical coverage: A geographical attribution of the data is available for all partner countries with the exception for portfolio investment liabilities, but due to confidentiality reason not all countries will be published. 2.4.1 Valuation Conversion to unit of account: Data reported in foreign currencies or in domestic currency are converted to euro using Croatian National Bank's end of period midpoint exchange rate for stocks conversion. Valuation: Assets and liabilities are mostly valued at market prices which holds true for most of portfolio investment data and reserve asset. For direct investment in listed enterprises market values are also used, while unlisted enterprises are valued at own funds at book values. For other investment nominal values are the main source of valuation. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Direct investments: Data are collected through CNB enterprise Survey on foreign direct investment and the Central Depository and Clearing Company. Portfolio investments: Portfolio investments data are collected through CNB enterprise survey on foreign direct investment and securities statistics. The central bank's external debt division provides data on debt portfolio investments, based on reports provided by reporting institutions. Other investments: Data on foreign assets and liabilities are compiled from reports provided by the central bank, the government, banks and other sectors. Financial derivatives: From the first quarter od 2010 in the international investment position are included stocks arising from contracts concluded with the characteristics of financial derivatives. Reporting institutions are commercial banks and other financial institutions and from the fourth quarter of 2012 reporting population was expanded to non-financial institutions which are usually included in these jobs to protect against changes in market conditions. Reserve assets: The central bank's accounting department provides data on reserve assets. None None
