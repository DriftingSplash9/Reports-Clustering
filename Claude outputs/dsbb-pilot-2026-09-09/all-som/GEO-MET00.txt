 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Foreign trade statistics of Georgia are compiled according to the international methodology ("International Merchandise Trade Statistics", United Nations, New York, 2011) and "International Merchandise Trade Statistics: Compilers Manual, Revision 1" (IMTS 2010-CM)), (United Nations, New York, 2013).. The data are presented by months and on a cumulative basis from the beginning of the year. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The imported and exported goods are classified according to the Harmonized Commodity Description and Coding System (HS 2022). The HS-based Nomenclature of External Economic Activities for EU countries (on 6-digit level) is used. The data are converted in accordance with a one- and five-digit level of the Standard International Trade Classification (SITC, rev. 4) and with a one- and three-digit level of the Broad Economic Categories (BEC, rev. 4). The country classification – GNC 007-2016 (ISO 3166) and currency classification – GNC 008-2017 – are also used. 2.2.1 Scope The imported and exported goods are classified according to the Harmonized Commodity Description and Coding System (HS 2022). The HS-based Nomenclature of External Economic Activities for EU countries (on 6-digit level) is used. The data are converted in accordance with a one- and five-digit level of the Standard International Trade Classification (SITC, rev. 4) and with a one- and three-digit level of the Broad Economic Categories (BEC, rev. 4). The country classification – GNC 007-2016 (ISO 3166) and currency classification – GNC 008-2017 – are also used. Scope of the data External trade statistics on goods covers all operations concerning external trade on goods which are recorded by official agencies, on the territory under jurisdiction of Georgia's government. Exceptions to coverage The data do not cover transit goods passing through the country, temporary imports or exports, securities, banknotes, coins in circulation, monetary gold, etc. Also, the data of Simplified Customs Declarations (Form #4). Geographical coverage: Data do not cover the territories of Abkhazian Autonomous Republic and Tskhinvali Region (former South Ossetian Autonomous Region), a part of Georgian territory not controlled by the central authorities. Unrecorded activity External trade data do not cover shuttle trade. 2.4.1 Valuation The valuation of imports is on a CIF basis, and exports are valued on a FOB basis. Imports on FOB basis will be available from Q4, 2026. The value is shown in USD; the conversion is made using the official currency exchange rate published by the National Bank of Georgia for the transaction day. The volume of goods is recorded in kilograms and in supplementary units, if the latter are requested. 2.4.2 Recording basis All goods crossing the border of the country (territory controlled by the central authorities of Georgia) for subsequent sale or exporting are recorded. Export includes also re-export and import includes re-import. In accounting the General Trade System is used for the external trade data. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures All the import and export data including the goods to be processed are accounted for on gross basis, except repairing of goods which is accounted for in net basis and the corresponding data are used only for Balance of Payments purposes. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The information on export and import of goods is processed by the Revenue Service using the ACYCUDA program. The database of customs declarations is presented to the NSO as an MS Excel file. NSO uses MS Access and also MS SQL as a new special (in the test mode) software for data processing. To complete the data information on gas and electric power is obtained from the corresponding sources. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Statistics on imports/exports of goods are compiled based on data from customs declarations, which are included in customs declarations database by the Revenue Service. The latest are transmitted to the NSO. In addition, NSO collects data on volume of exports and imports of goods, which are not declared at the customs border (i.e. electrical energy and natural gas) from the companies which transport or distribute these goods: the Georgian State Electric system, Electricity System Commercial Operator and Georgian Gas Transportation Company. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness The data on exports and imports transactions for the reference month are presented to the NSO 3 days after the end of the reference month, on exports/imports of electrical energy – 12 days after the end of the reference month, on exports/imports of natural gas – 12 days after the end of the reference month. 3.2.1 Source data assessment Data are not assessed, as all data are received from administrative sources. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques The information received from Revenue Service is processed by the NSO in MS Access. Data are checked on codes and customs regimes accuracy, correctness of supplementary units, assessment of goods value and their belonging to merchandise trade are also made. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs Periodically, in case of discovering methodological errors or other inaccuracies, they are corrected and previous months statistics is also checked. Every month the data for previous months are controlled. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request Available, every user can address electronically (or by any other form) the contact person, when they need the data. 5.3.2 Availability of documents and services catalogs The list of NSO publications is available on the NSO website at http://www.geostat.ge/
