 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions • Concept: The definition of international reserves follows the methodology of the IMF Balance of Payments Manual, sixth edition. International reserves are reported in millions of U.S. dollars. The concept of the International Reserves and Foreign Currency Liquidity Template follows that of the IMF. • Definition: Gross international reserves consist of external assets available and controlled by the monetary authorities for directly financing the balance of payments or indirectly adjusting the magnitude of imbalances by intervening in the foreign exchange market to influence the exchange rate and/or for other purposes. The liquid portion of international reserves is defined as gross international reserves less collateral, equity, and assets with limited liquidity and assets classified as losses. • Classifications: The residence criterion is used to classify the external assets of a country's financial institutions. The definition of residence is consistent with the Balance of Payments Manual, sixth edition. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Classifications: The residence criterion is used to classify the external assets of a country's financial institutions. The definition of residence is consistent with the sixth edition of the Balance of Payments Manual. 2.2.1 Scope Classifications: The residence criterion is used to classify the external assets of a country's financial institutions. The definition of residence is consistent with the sixth edition of the Balance of Payments Manual. Scope of the data International reserves are presented in millions of US dollars (including the dollar value of precious metals). Coverage of instruments: Collateral assets have significant restrictions. Equity interests are not included in reserve assets. They are included in gross international reserves but are reported in Section IV. Equity interests and assets classified as losses are not included in reserve assets. Financial derivatives: Residents may enter into swap transactions. Exceptions to coverage Coverage of instruments: Collateral assets have significant limitations. Equity interests are not included in reserve assets. They are included in gross international reserves but are reflected in Section IV. 2.4.1 Valuation Revaluation: The international reserves of the National Bank of the Kyrgyz Republic are presented in millions of US dollars. Nominal exchange rates of currencies (including SDR) against the US dollar are used to calculate the dollar value of assets. The book value of precious metals is based on the London Bullion Market Association morning gold fixing. Exchange rates: The dollar value of foreign currency assets is calculated on a daily basis. The dollar value of gold assets is calculated using the London Bullion Market Association (LBMA) morning fixing. Frequency of calculations and revaluations: Exchange rates for calculating the dollar equivalent of assets denominated in other currencies are set (fixed) on a weekly basis. Gold is revalued on a monthly basis in accordance with the LBMA morning fixing on the last business day of the month. The value of securities is revalued on a monthly basis. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The data is based on accounting system of the National Bank. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Statistical adjustments: The total volume of assets is adjusted for exchange rates on a weekly basis (as exchange rates set (fixed) for calculating dollar value of foreign currency assets change) and for revaluation of gold (monthly) and securities (monthly).
