 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The definition of external debt in both sets of data is consistent with the definition in the IMF’s 2001 “A Manual on Government Finance Statistics” and the domestic/external residency criteria are consistent with the IMF’s “Balance of Payments Manual, fifth edition” (BPM5). 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization According to GSM 2001, BPM5 classification Scope of the data Two sets of data on public and publicly guaranteed external debt are produced disseminated by the authorities: 1) data compiled by the Treasury Department that are published in the annual “Report of the Accountant General,” and 2) data compiled by the Central Bank of Barbados (CBB) that are published on the CBB’s website “ www.centralbank.org.bb ” which include monthly, quarterly and annual data. Data published by the Treasury Department do not distinguish between domestic and external debt, although that information is available from the Treasury records. Data published by the CBB show domestic and external debt separately. Data produced and disseminated by the CBB include debt outstanding, debt service payments (principal and interest), and disbursements. Debt is recognized when the loan agreement is signed. Data are disaggregated by: Creditor -- multilateral, bilateral, and private (commercial banks and suppliers’ credits). Bonds are identified separately; Debtor (public, publicly guaranteed debt, and private commercial debt not publicly guaranteed); Currency of loans by debtor categories (central government, central bank, government guaranteed and private non-guaranteed). The CBB disseminates quarterly a table of debt service payments for public and publicly guaranteed debt that provides information on payments of interest and amortization for domestic and external debt. The table also identifies the end of period balance of the sinking fund , which represent the sums set aside for the redemption of domestic and foreign debt and which are invested in foreign securities. Private commercial external debt is available from the CBB’s records at the Exchange Control Department. These data are comprehensive because domestic enterprises must obtain CBB authorization before undertaking external debt obligations. Separate data on private debt not guaranteed by the government are not published, but are reflected in the annual balance of payments statement produced and disseminated by the CBB. Exceptions to coverage Neither the CBB nor the Treasury Department currently disseminates a debt service schedule, but the CBB prepares one for internal use (see plans for improvement). 2.4.1 Valuation Stock data are translated to Barbados dollars (BDS$) using the end of period exchange rate. 2.4.2 Recording basis Currently Treasury data are recorded on accrual basis.
