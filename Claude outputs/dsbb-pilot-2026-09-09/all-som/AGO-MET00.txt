 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The data are classified mainly on the basis of the United Nations Harmonized System (HS). Scope of the data Merchandise imports are published by type of product on the tariff schedule, adding the economic classification (goods for consumption, goods for intermediate consumption, and capital goods). Exports are published by groups of products (crude oil, refined oil products, gas, diamonds, coffee, and other). 2.4.1 Valuation The data on imports supervised by SGS/BIVAC are reported on an F.O.B. basis (excluding freight and insurance). In compiling the rest of imports for the BOP, adjustments are made to convert them to F.O.B. values using a fixed coefficient of 10 percent for freight and 1 percent for insurance. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Foreign trade statistics are compiled by the Research and Statistics Directorate (DEE) of the National Bank of Angola (BNA), based on various sources. Data on imports come from the following sources: a) The company in charge of quality and valuation control of merchandise, namely consumer and capital goods, ( Société générale de Surveillance—SGS, and since March 2002, BIVAC-International; b) BNA Reserve Management Directorate (since November 1, the savings and loan bank Banco de Poupança e Crédito) – for the cash transactions corresponding to the foreign exchange plan; BNA Debt Office for financing uses; c) Surveys of oil sector companies—imports for their own use, attributable to their operations; d) Surveys of NGOs receiving assistance in kind. Data on exports are collected from information requested from businesses in the oil and diamonds sector, Ministry of Petroleum, Ministry of Geology and Mines, Ministry of Fisheries, Coffee Institute, Cement Company of Angola, and Wood Preparation and Processing Company. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data The INE also publishes quarterly data on merchandise trade but which do not match the BNA figures owing to coverage differences from the use of divergent sources ( the INE uses the records of the National Customs Directorate) and to adjustments made for estimating F.O.B. values in compiling the balance of payments.
