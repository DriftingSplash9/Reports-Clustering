 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Data on GGO are released using the Government Finance Statistics Manual (GFSM) 2014 framework – the Statement of government operations. The data are obtained by transmission from the Czech system of National Accounts that is based on the European System of National Accounts (ESA 2010). For this reason it is used the "GFS2014 – ESA2010 transmission bridge" received from the IMF. The methodology used for the compilation of national accounts (nonfinancial accounts of general government as regards revenue, expenditure and net lending/net borrowing, as well as transactions in financial assets and liabilities, i.e. net acquisition of financial assets and net incurrence of liabilities) is based on ESA 2010 (Regulation (EU) No 549/2013) and the Eurostat Manual on Government Deficit and Debt. There are some differences in the treatment of certain activities according to ESA and GFS. In GFS the services fees related to interest are not separately identified, because they can be estimated only indirectly by considering data for all sectors of the economy, such as in the national accounts. In SNA the values for FISIM are derived by partitioning interest. For this reason the items "Use of goods and services", "Interest" as well as the item "Other revenue" are adjusted by FISIM. In GFS compensation of employees, use of goods and services, and consumption of fixed capital incurred in own-account capital formation are excluded from expense and are recorded as a component of the cost of the acquisition of the nonfinancial asset. In SNA the full costs of compensation of employees, use of goods and services, and consumption of fixed capital are recorded as well as the acquisition of the nonfinancial asset. Therefore, the items "Compensation of employees", "Use of goods and services" as well as "Consumption of fixed capital" on the expenditure side and the item "Other revenue" on the revenue side are adjusted by own-account capital formation. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The general government sector and its subsectors are defined in accordance with the ESA 2010 guidelines and the ESA 2010 Manual on Government Deficit and Debt, which are also consistent with the 2008 SNA and GFSM 2014. The general government in the Czech Republic consists of central government, local government, and social security funds. The CZSO compiles and disseminates government finance statistics for each subsector as well as for the general government sector as a whole. Sector classification of units is checked on yearly basis and changed if necessary. One of the priorities of the CZSO is keep GFS data and sector accounts consistent as far as the sector delimitation is concerned. 2.2.1 Scope The general government sector and its subsectors are defined in accordance with the ESA 2010 guidelines and the ESA 2010 Manual on Government Deficit and Debt, which are also consistent with the 2008 SNA and GFSM 2014. Scope of the data The institutional coverage corresponds to general government sector according to the methodology ESA 2010. In the Czech Republic the CZSO is responsible for the delimitation of general government sector and its updating. This section describes the coverage of the general government sector and its sub-sectors. The general government sector in the Czech Republic is composed by 3 subsectors: S.1311 – Central government subsector, S.1313 – Local government subsector, S.1314 – Social security funds subsector. Central government subsector (S.1311) Central government subsector includes following units, structured into budgetary and extra-budgetary ones. 1. Therefore e.g. debt is valued at face value for the EDP purposes; however particular items are valued at market price in national accounts. Other items are valued at market prices as requested by the methodology. 2.4.2 Recording basis Data are expressed in millions of national currency and are recorded on an accrual basis. The accounting year (fiscal year) is the calendar year. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures The CZSO follows the rules set by the regulation (ESA 2010). 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Statistical data are collected by the CZSO on the base of Decree ( https://csu.gov.cz/vykazy/program_statistickych_zjistovani) . General policy of the CZSO is to take statistical data for the general government sector as supplementary data filling existing gaps in administrative data sources. Data sources (profit and loss statement and balance sheet) for budgetary organizations, state funds and semi-budgetary units are collected and processed by the MoF (including budgetary reports, i.e. data on revenue and expenditure of budgetary organizations and state funds). Data for financial institutions classified as government units are mainly provided by the CNB. Administrative data mentioned above are in case of need supplemented by statistical questionnaires surveying mainly indicators on natural wage, investments, etc. Data for other units – public universities, health insurance companies, public research institutions, public hospitals, Czech TV and radio (public broadcasting companies), Financial Market Guarantee System, other non-profit institutions and public corporations are collected directly by the CZSO. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Beyond the replies mentioned above, time of recording utterly respects the rules, i.e. revenues and expenditures are recorded in period of their occurrence irrespective of cash transactions. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness All administrative data and statistical questionnaires are available for compilation of quarterly general government national accounts in time t+50 (or t+70) days after the end of previous quarter. 3.2.1 Source data assessment Source data are based especially on budgetary reports and financial statements that cover most of government units, i.e. response is 100%. Non-profit institutions provide information through statistical questionnaire – and statistical survey is exhaustive – response is usually 100%. Assessment is carried out during the processing of the statements; results are monitored and extreme mistakes are discussed with the concrete respondent. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Since the compilation of the government accounts is based especially on budgetary reports and financial statements, the response is usually 100%. In some cases, the statistical questioning is not exhaustive (for example semi-budgetary organizations). However, the CZSO disposes of total figures (i.e. total assets and their structure) so that only structures of some sub-items are surveyed. Budgetary classification of revenue and expenditure is very detailed and supplement accounting data for budgetary organizations, state funds and semi-budgetary organizations (from the Auxiliary Analytical Overview - AAO) are also detailed. It means that the CZSO disposes of data for all units classified in the general government sector. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Data adjustments are carried out in compliance with the ESA methodology. Following main (regular and exceptional) methodological adjustments are carried out: payment for non-life insurance services (P.2 and D.71 divided for direct insurance, i.e. D.711 and reinsurance D.712): calculation is carried out in compliance with ESA 2010 methodology; acquisition of combat aircraft (Gripens) - has been included under P.51g in 2005, consumption of fixed capital under P.51c and repayments for combat aircraft – Gripens under D.41, F.42; leasing of these combat aircrafts is treated as financial leasing and is included in government debt; deliveries of other military equipment are classified under P.51g or P.52; accrualisations of data on inventories changes for budgetary organization under P.1, P.2, P.52 items; adjustments of data on output due to increasing in of standing timber (P.1); financial leasing (P.2 (-), D.41, F.42); splitting of travel expenses between wages in kind and intermediate consumption (P.2, D.11): 95% of the travel expenses is included to P.2 and 5% to D.11 (as catering allowance); wages in kind (D.1); imputed interest (D.41), to resources of the corresponding sectors: this imputation to resources is in the same amount, which is included under wages in kind; imputed social contributions and benefits sickness paid by employers direct to employees during the first 14 days sick leave from 2008, 21 days from 2011 to 2013 and again during the first 14 days since 2014 (due to the sickness legislation) under D.1222, D.6122 and D.6222; imputed subsidies to employers: i.e. reimbursement of sickness contributions in the amount 50% (D.39) from 2008 by the end 2011, that the burden of employers was not so sudden and great; FISIM (D.41, P.2); fixed capital formation (P.51g): Information on fixed capital formation is not available from the financial statements (used in the Czech accounting system). Therefore the calculation of P.51g - for T-1 - was based on (i) data from the budgetary reports for BO (in cash basis) and (ii) on non-financial assets stocks data obtained from balance sheet for T-1 used for quarterly accounts compilation, for estimation of GDP and for the April EDP notification. For the October EDP notification the data were based on the annual statistical questionnaires which include more detailed data e.g. on individual types of fixed assets acquired. Since 2013, fixed capital formation is based especially on data from the AAO statements – for all central BO, SF and SBO and for selected (but important) local BO and SBO. The AAO provides detail information on individual types of tangible and intangible fixed assets and inventories and also on forms of acquisition – on purchases and sales and on acquisition free of charge and simultaneously information on counterpart sectors. Information on fixed capital formation for other government units is used from the statistical questionnaires (VI 1-01, NI 1-01 or Zdp 5-01; P 5-01, Pen 5-01 and Poj 5-01); calculation of fixed capital consumption (- P.51c) based on PIM method (method of perpetual inventory method), because data on accounting or tax depreciations are not used in the national accounts; neutralization of impact of the EU grants on B.9 – elimination of transfers to final users classified in other sectors from government revenue and their recording under corresponding national accounts items (D.73, D.74, D.75, D.9); Important adjustments are made under accrualisation of cash data for the BOs – i.e. adjustments of output and intermediate consumption, taxes, interest, social and health insurance contributions and of penalties within health insurance companies – by using the time adjustment method (connected with receivables / payables F.8). Methodological adjustments are made also within balance sheets items (in stocks) and financial or revaluation accounts etc. (e.g. imputation of shares of government, or adjustment of government debt - forgiveness or assumption of non-government units), i.e. on data that are not included in administrative sources. Within accounts of the State Agricultural Intervention Fund (SAIF) is eliminated any effect of the EU subsidies on products; amount of inventories of the SAIF is excluded and as a fictive unit is classified to S.11001 - public non-financial corporations (under AN.12, P.52 items). The same amount is recorded as other equity (AF.5, F.5) of central government sector. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results Validation of intermediate result is carried out automatically during the processing of data sources, because information on non-financial and financial transactions for each unit is obtained e.g. from Profit and Loss Statement and Balance sheet – comparison with the previous report and individual questioning is used. As to validation of data from statistical questionnaire – the CZSO Regional Administrations ensures collection, processing and control of quarterly and annual statistical questionnaires. For this purpose, so called "Technical Projects" are elaborated that include technical linkages between indicators and draw attention e.g. to extreme or negative value. During the compilation process, yearly changes in relative and absolute terms are checked and verified. If possible, also information from annual reports of reporting companies is used as supplementary information. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data Assessment and investigation of statistical discrepancies (between B.9 and B.9f) is carried out during the compilation of government accounts for each group (e.g. central budgetary or semi-budgetary organizations) and also for individual units classified in S.13. Items of exceptional value are checked, individual questioning (with the unit) is used, if necessary. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs Statistical output is compared with the data from banking statistics (e.g. on interest or deposits) and with data from the Final state budget. This discussion with the CNB and the MoF is regular and it is already performed during the compilation of government accounts. The comparison of statistical output for individual unit with its annual report has also an important role. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses Quarterly data are revised within current year. Previous year´s quarterly data are revised after publication of annual national accounts or after revision of annual national accounts and also after revisions in previous year´s data in EDP notification tables. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request 5.3.2 Availability of documents and services catalogs CZSO Website The main source of statistical information for users is the website https://csu.gov.cz/home Publication Activities An overview of all CZSO products can be found in the Catalogue of Products, which is available on the website for users.
