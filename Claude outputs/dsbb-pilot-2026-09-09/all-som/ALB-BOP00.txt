 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The methodological framework followed in the compilation of the Balance of Payments is defined in the IMF’s Balance of Payments and International Investment Positions Manual, Sixth Edition (BPM6) and the European system of national and regional accounts for the denominator (ESA 2010). Balance of Payments Manual fifth edition (BPM5) was the reference for the data published by BOA until 2008. Additional methodological references used are the United Nations' Manual on Statistics of International Trade in Services (MSITS 2010) and the OECD Benchmark Definition of Foreign Direct Investment (BD4). Balance of payments data are published in millions of Euro, and comprise data on exports and imports of goods and services; primary and incomes; capital account; direct investments; portfolio investments; other financial transactions; reserve assets; net errors and omissions. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The balance of payments transactions are classified in accordance with standard components of "BPM6". The standard components are classified in four main categories: 1) Current account, 2) Capital account, 3) Financial account, and 4) Net errors and omissions. 2.2.1 Scope The balance of payments transactions are classified in accordance with standard components of "BPM6". The standard components are classified in four main categories: 1) Current account, 2) Capital account, 3) Financial account, and 4) Net errors and omissions. Scope of the data The geographic territory of reference is the territory of the Republic of Albania. The balance of payments of Albania covers in principle all transactions between resident and nonresident units. The residence of each institutional unit is the economic territory with which it has the strongest connection, expressed as its center of predominant economic interest. Each institutional unit is a resident of one and only one economic territory determined by its center of predominant economic interest. However, some informal activities are not covered. Exceptions to coverage Adjustments in balance of payment data are applied in current account/import of goods for informal imports of goods (smuggling). Also, informal worker’s remittances sending from abroad (cash remittances) are included in estimation of worker’s remittances. Unrecorded activity Informal compensation of employees, coming from abroad (cash compensation of employees) are not yet estimated. Also, estimation of FISIM is not yet recorded. 2.4.1 Valuation The recording of transaction broadly consists with BPM6 methodology, in the form of double-entry accounting, with each credit entry balanced by an offsetting debit entry, and vice-versa. Data are compiled in ALL and published in millions of euro. Transactions recorded in other currencies are converted to the ALL using monthly exchange rates applicable at the time of the transaction. Where transactions are derived from outstanding stocks of financial claims and liabilities at the beginning and the end of the recording period, adjustments are made to isolate actual transactions from valuation and other changes affecting the stock data. Transactions are valued at market prices, broadly consistent with the BPM6 methodology. Exports and imports of goods are valued on f.o.b. basis (imports are adjusted to exclude transport and insurance). 2.4.2 Recording basis Goods, the time of recording of exports and imports of goods is at the time of customs declaration clearance, or the month in which dispatches or arrivals. Services such as manufacturing, travel and freight are recorded at time when are provided or supplied. Other services are recorded at time when payments are made. Primary income related to income from investments is recorded on accrual basis. Compensation of employees are recorded when payments are made. Secondary income are recorded when payments are made. Grants for general government are recorded when disbursed. Capital transfers are recorded when payments are made. Financial transactions are recorded on accrual basis. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures All transactions in balance of payments are recorded on gross basis. Exception is made for net premiums on nonlife insurance as secondary income, recorded as net difference of gross premiums with service charge. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Primary data source: The data collection system can be classified as a direct reporting system (direct reporting from company, ITRS, etc.). Balance of payment compilation is based on actual collected data (data collection on a monthly or quarterly frequency), except in some cases where data are only available on an annual frequency. This is the case for equity and reinvestment of earnings in direct investment, where the largest companies are subject to quarterly direct reporting, but data for other companies comes from an annual survey (balance sheet). Goods: The data on imports and exports is based on monthly customs declarations, as the codes used in them are after the Harmonized System for Description and Coding of Commodities of the World Customs Organization. BOA and the National Statistics Institute jointly apply methodology developed by them for compilation of imports at FOB prices and receipts and payments regarding the freight transportation. The methodology is based on the analysis of the CIF-FOB correlations for the imports of goods depending on the different imports delivery categories, as well as on the different mode of transportation and nationality of the carrier. The conversion from CIF-FOB is based on coefficients on transport and insurance cost provided in the customs database. Imports and exports of goods for processing are deducted from the total imports and exports. Smuggling trade in imports is estimated using coefficients resulted from annual foreign trade survey (each five years). Services: The BOA receives monthly information from commercial banks, quarterly information from other non-financial institutions and undertakes surveys and estimations for specific items of the services account. The main data source for the majority of services transactions is the reporting system of commercial banks (bank customer transactions, ITRS) with the following exceptions: Manufacturing services on physical inputs owned by others : data are compiled based on the information provided monthly from Customs database on goods for processing in Albania and goods for processing abroad. Goods for processing in Albania comprise goods received and goods returned. The processing fee (fee is assumed to be included in the value of the return flow of the good after processing) is calculated as the difference in the values of the goods after and before processing. Goods for processing declared are available in the Customs database (the export and the import flows of goods subject to an inward processing arrangement). Goods for processing abroad comprise goods sent and goods returned. The processing fee is reported to the Customs as the difference between the value of goods returned after processing and the value of goods sent abroad for the purpose of processing. Maintenance and repair services n.i.e : Data on maintenance and repair services on goods are acquired from the Customs database. Freight services : This item is estimated based on the coefficients of transportation costs estimated from the data provided from customs database. Travel services: BOA undertakes a quarterly survey to estimate travel services. Furthermore, the Ministry of Interior (General Directory of Police), provides quarterly data on arrivals and departures of Albanian nationals and foreign nationals in/from the country. Those data, together with sample results derived from the travel survey, are used to gross up figures and to compile the figures of receipts and expenditures on travel services. Insurance services: Insurance services of goods are estimated based on the coefficients of insurance costs estimated from the data provided from the customs database. The rest of insurance services related to other forms of insurance are compiled based on the information provided quarterly by the Financial Supervision Authority in aggregated form for all resident insurance companies together. Government services: Debits of government services data are mainly provided by the Ministry of Finance on quarterly basis. Primary Income : Data on compensation of employees are obtained from commercial banks in the form of cross-border bank customer settlements. Data on investment income: Direct investment income : Data on credit side are compiled based on direct reporting of commercial banks and non-financial institutions on their direct investments abroad. Data on debit side are compiled based on direct reporting of foreign owned enterprises in Albania, foreign owned commercial banks and non-financial institutions. Data include reinvested earnings and ex-dividends. Portfolio investment income : The major data source are the resident commercial banks, which report on themselves for foreign equity less than 10 percent and on securities held abroad/ and securities held by nonresidents in commercial banks in Albania. Other investment income : Data are provided by Ministry of Finance, financial institutions and non-financial institutions on their accrued interest on currency and deposits, loans, other accounts receivable/ payable. S econdary Income: Data related to general government is compiled from the information provided by Ministry of Finance on a quarterly basis. Other secondary income consist from personal transfers, mainly from workers remittances. Workers remittances are estimated based on: monthly information on banks customer transactions (ITRS); monthly information on Money Transfer Operators; semiannual sample survey on workers remittances. Other personal transfers are compiled based on monthly information on banks customer transactions (ITRS) and Money Transfer Operators. Capital account: The data source on capital transfers and acquisition/disposal of non-produced non-financial assets is ITRS. Data on capital transfers related to general government are obtained from Ministry of Finance on foreign grants and ITRS. Other capital transfers consist of transfers of nonprofit institutions and transfers between households, based on information from ITRS. Direct Investment: Main data source rely on direct reporting of biggest direct investment enterprises; on direct reporting of financial institutions and nonfinancial institution; on the National Agency for Natural Resources reports data on investments on drilling enterprises active in Albania; on national registration center and information on purchases of real-estate by nonresidents reported by banks on behalf of the customers. Portfolio Investment : Data source for portfolio investment is monthly reports from banks and nonbanks financial institutions, banks reports as custodians, CPIS database for portfolio investment liabilities on private sector, information from Ministry of Finance on Albanian government Eurobond held by nonresidents. Other Investment: Data on other investment are obtained from monthly reports from banks and nonbanks financial institutions, from BOA (Monetary Operations Dept. and Payment Systems, Accounting and Finance Dept.), monthly information on public debt from Ministry of Finance and estimation of trade credits and advances using coefficients resulted from annual foreign trade survey (each five years). Reserve Assets: Data are obtained monthly on BOA balance sheet and monthly operational reserve, balance sheet on accounts and portfolio and deposits holdings from Monetary Operations Department. Information on Reserve Position in the Fund is updated from IMF official website. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Source data are consistent with the definitions, scope and classifications, and time of recording of the balance of payments. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Data collection and processing timetables are adequate to meet timeliness and periodicity for disseminating the balance of payments. Respondents are made aware of the deadlines set for reporting through detailed instructions. Most of data source are available on monthly reports, while surveys are quarterly and semiannual. ITRS is available 10 days after the reference period. 3.2.1 Source data assessment Source data are analyzed in the context of revisions. The source data are analyzed for underreporting/misreporting, in particular to check for: - temporal consistency, - consistency with related data sources Sample surveys are checked regularly for a better coverage (travel and remittances); trade in goods is assessed frequently in order to be harmonized with other statistical institutions; external debt in public sector is checked regularly with Ministry of Finance. The direct reporting of direct investment enterprises is assessed annually after the book year, in order to better coverage reinvested earnings. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Goods: The goods item is obtained after several adjustments to the information reported from Customs. First, data on exports and imports of goods for processing are excluded. Estimated smuggled goods are included to the imports of goods. This data are then subject of CIF to FOB adjustment, using the cost of transport and insurance. No adjustment is made for exports. Nonmonetary gold is reported apart from general merchandise. Services: Manufacturing services are compiled using information on imports and exports of goods. Processing fee in credit is calculated as a difference of goods sent and goods received for processing. Processing fee in debit is reported to the Customs under specific procedure codes. Transport and insurance services related to trade in goods are compiled based on the information on the customs database. Freight services may be arranged by resident and nonresident carriers. In credit side are included freight services provided by resident carriers to exports of goods. In debit side are included freight services provided by nonresident carriers to imports of goods. The same estimation is used for insurance of goods. No estimates are made for other transport services. Travel services are estimated by conducting quarterly surveys in order to calculate average duration of stay and average daily expenditure per person. Data provided by the Ministry of Interior are converted from nationality basis into residency basis. The basic assumption is that a part of entries and exits of Albanian nationals regard Albanian migrants who visit Albania with an Albanian passport but are resident of another economy. The estimated figure is considered as nonresidents and is added up to the number of foreign nationals provided by the Ministry of Interior. Data from Institute of Statistics on the number of foreign nationals who live and work in Albania is used to adjust the number of foreign nationals who cross the border. The estimated figure of foreign nationals being resident in Albania is deducted from the total number of the foreign nationals passing the border for a visit to the country and is added up to the number of residents leaving the country for a journey. No estimation is done for other categories of services accounts. Secondary Income: Data on workers remittances are estimated by conducting semiannual sample survey in order to obtain the share of value remitted by banks, by MTO’s and in cash (by informal channels). The grossing up process combines these coefficients with the total of money reported by banks and by MTO’s to calculate the workers remittances send through informal channels. Financial account: Adjustments are made to put the financial account transactions and income on accrual basis. Estimates are made for portfolio investment liabilities in private sector, and the data are adjusted after the publication of CPIS (Coordinated Portfolio Investment Survey). Currency and deposits liabilities are adjusted for households sector, adding up the deposits held by Albanian migrants (living abroad) in domestic banks. Trade credits and advances are estimated based on coefficients of foreign trade survey. This survey of a sample of operators engaging in foreign trade in goods is used to determine the “trade credit” items in the balance of payments. The coefficients are shares of payments in imports and exports for different periods until the maturity period. These trade credits and advances are short term, as their maturity is less than one year. Net errors and omissions are calculated residually, as defined in BPM6 and constitute an accounting offsetting entry. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Balance of payments data are not seasonally adjusted. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results Cross-checking techniques are implemented for verifying the accuracy of the data. Balance of payments data are assessed and investigated using other source when available. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data Trade in goods intermediate data, in balance of payments, is assessed to be harmonized with national accounts statistics. Intermediate balance of payments items are analyzed reporter by reporter for the data behavior and, when necessary, reporting units are contacted for additional information. Data on financial account is reconciled with International Investment Positions and Gross External Debt statistics. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs Net errors and omissions are monitored. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses Revisions of individual records are monitored. Revisions are documented as to the cause, identified by the staff member making the amendment, and signed off by a supervisor. Data are checked to ensure that the revisions flow through to aggregate results. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses Analysis of differences between the revised and preliminary data is published together with the revision note and is always part of the statistical publications in the respect of the transparency dimension towards the data users. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request Users can submit specific request for statistics data through the e-mail address: statistics@bankofalbania.org (contacts in BOA webpage) The data are disseminated also to international organizations (IMF, Eurostat, National Institute of Statistics and other international organizations).
