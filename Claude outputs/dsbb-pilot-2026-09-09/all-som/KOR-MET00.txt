 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions All goods which add to or subtract from the stock of material resources of a country by entering (imports) or leaving (exports) its economic territory are recorded. However, goods simply being transported through a country (goods in transit), or goods temporary admitted or withdrawn are not included in the international trade statistics since they do not affect the stock of material resources of a country. Statistical territory of a country which trade data are being compiled is determined from the geographical, administrative and national economic perspectives, and in the majority of countries including Korea, adopt the boundary of their statistical territory as their customs territory, where the customs law of a state applies. Therefore, the statistics of the exchange of goods across customs borders will be the International Trade Statistics. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Classification: The Harmonized Commodity Description and Coding System (WCO) is used for the classification of imported/exported goods. Scope of the data General trade statistics General International Trade Statistics means the statistics on goods which are physically moved or scheduled to be moved across the border between Korea and foreign countries, and included in international trade statistics. Statistical territory of a country which trade data are being compiled is determined from the geographical, administrative and national economic perspectives, and in the majority of countries including Korea, adopt the boundary of their statistical territory as their customs territory, where the customs law of a state applies. Therefore, the statistics of the exchange of goods across customs borders will be the International Trade Statistics. Principle of the statistical data compilation In principle, data on goods which add to or subtract from the stock of material resources of the country by crossing border between Korea and foreign countries shall be complied for the international trade statistics. International trade statistics shall be compiled based on the recommendations of "International Merchandise Trade Statistics; Concepts and Definitions(Statistical Papers, Series M, No52, 1998 Rev 2), which was published with the endorsement of the United Nations Statistical Commission Method of attributing import and export countries Classification of countries is based on the ISO country codes. In the case of imports, the country of origin is recorded and for exports, the country of last known destination is recorded for the statistics by principle. Standards of valuation The Value of trade transactions is expressed in U.S dollars, and a FOB-type value is used for the statistical value of exported goods while a CIF-type value is used for the statistical value of imported goods. Special International Tarde Statistics There are logistics statistics, transit trade statistics and intermediary trade statistics. Logistics Statistics Logistics Statistics (exported and imported goods statistics) is the statistics on admitted (imported) and dispatched (exported) goods, as well as goods being transported to a third country via Korea Transit Trade Statistics Transit trade statistics refers to the statistics for goods which are shipped from a foreign country to be transported to a third country. These goods are temporary stored in ports or bonded area without passing through the clearance process, or simply transshipped to other means of transportation to be shipped out to its country of destination. By principle, the owner of the transit trade cargo shall reside in a foreign country. Logistics Statistics shall be compiled on the date of port entry and the date of departure. Intermediary Trade Statistics Intermediary trade statistics refers to the statistics of goods imported for the purpose of going through the export clearance of Korea to be re-exported to a third country without being released out of the bonded area in accordance with provisions of the article 154 of customs act or the non bonded area permitted for storage of goods under provisions of the article 156 of customs act. By principle, the owner of the intermediary trade cargo shall reside in Korea. Additional (Trade) Statistics Port Arrival and Departure of Means of Transportation Statistics Port arrival/departure of means of transportation statistics refers to the statistics on the record of arrival and departure of foreign vessels (aircrafts) under the provisions of the article 134 of customs act of open and closed port of Korea, or the record of cross-border traffic of vehicles and trains between customs clearance area/stations and bonded zone of Korea and foreign countries. Port arrival/departure of means of transportation statistics is compiled by Korea Customs Service's division in charge of trade statistics with relevant information sent from the port and airport surveillance department. Passenger Arrival and Departure Statistics Passenger arrival and departure statistics is compiled by the Korea Customs Service's division in charge of trade statistics with arrival and departure declaration information of passengers sent from the Ministry of Justice and airlines. Exceptions to coverage Transactions Coverage Exceptions Merchandise trade statistics exclude the following: Goods in bonded sales shop; Goods in transit via Korea; Goods returned prior to import approval; Monetary gold and silver, negotiable securities, and monetary coins and paper money in current circulation; Goods of A.T.A. Carnet; Goods for improvement or repair; Goods temporarily imported or exported for international events, athletic meetings, exhibition, etc.; Goods consigned to diplomatic missions; Goods on lease for less than one year; Goods used for commercial samples or advertisement; and, Goods considered not to be related to merchandise trade. Geographic Coverage Exception Goods carried in from or carried out to North Korea are excluded from merchandise trade statistics. 2.4.1 Valuation Conversion to unit of account: By the exchange rate of Won-to-every nation's currency which Commissioner of the Customs Service notify weekly, after every nation's Currency is converted to won currency, won currency is also converted to U.S. dollars, and then converted U.S. dollars is put down at the declaration of import and export. Valuation: The imported goods are valued by C.I.F. valuation and the exported goods by F.O.B valuation. The WTO Agreement on Valuation is used for the customs valuation on imported goods. 2.4.2 Recording basis Time of recording: The imported and exported goods are included in the statistics when their declarations are accepted at the customs houses. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Sources: The merchandise trade statistics are based on the clearances data of the customs houses. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Type of data collected: The volume, weigh, price, country of origin, country of destination, and other informaiton as well as the HS code shall be reported online via electronic customs clearance systems under the Customs Act: Export: country of destination Import: country of origin 3.1.3 Source data timeliness All import/export data that meet recording conditions are automatically generated daily as a basic resource for statistics. 3.2.1 Source data assessment Processing procedure - Import and export declaration data can be filed directly to the KCS e-clearance system. - Data stored in e-clearance system is transferred to the main system every day and this is designed to collect trade statistics every month. - Collected data is finalized in the mid-week next month. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Seasonal adjustment: The data are not seasonally adjusted. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results Intermediate data are to be assessed and investigated in the point of view of confidentiality of personal secrets. Confidentiality about the intermediate data: In the case that the publication of statistics would contravene the national laws concerning the confidentiality of an individual person or enterprise, the release of detailed statistics is limited. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data Error Checking Reconciliation Exercises bet.
