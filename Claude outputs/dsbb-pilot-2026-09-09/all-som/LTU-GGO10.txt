 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The presentation form corresponds to the IMF´s Government Finance Statistics Manual 2014 framework (Statement of operations). Data on General Government Operations are in accordance with European System of Accounts 2010 (ESA 2010) and the Manual on Government Deficit and Debt (MGDD). 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The general government sector and its subsectors (central government, local government, and social security funds) are defined in accordance with the ESA 2010 guidelines and the ESA 2010 Manual on Government Deficit and Debt. Classification system: Revenue of General Government consists of taxes, social contributions, grants, and other revenue Taxes: taxes on income and profits (individuals, corporations); taxes on payroll and workforce; taxes on property (tax on real estate, inheritance tax, tax on land); taxes on goods and services (value added tax, turnover tax, excises, sugar tax, taxes on lotteries and gambling, motor vehicle taxes, pollution tax, charges); taxes on international trade and transactions. Social contributions: compulsory and voluntary contributions of employers, employees and self-employed or non-employed. Grants: grants from foreign governments, grants from international organizations, European Union funds and grants from other general government units. Other revenue: property income (interest, dividends, residual profit of the Bank of Lithuania, tax on the rent of state land and waters, tax on the state natural resources, tax on the oil and gas resources), sales of goods and services (rent of buildings, consular tax, tuition fees, other sales of goods and services), fines and penalties, voluntary transfers, other than grants, other revenue. Expenditure by function: general public services; defense; public order and safety; economic affairs; environmental protection; housing and community amenities; health; recreation, culture and religion; education; social protection. Economic classification of expense: Compensation of employees: wages and salaries, social contributions. Use of goods and services: payments for the electricity, heating, transport, communication services, staff training, maintenance and repair of fixed assets, payments for the rental of buildings, transport equipment and other purchases of goods and services. Property expense: interest, rent. Subsidies: import subsidies, subsidies on products and subsidies on production, i.e., all subsidies from budgetary sources. Grants: current and capital grants to foreign governments, to international organizations and to other general government units. Payments to EU budget: traditional own resources, plastics, VAT, GNP-based own resources. Social benefits: social security benefits, social assistance benefits, employer social benefits. Other expense: current and capital(debt assumption, debt cancellation, capital transfers to market enterprises andnon-profit institutions to finance their costs of acquiring non-financial assets,compensations according to court decisions). Expenditure from EU funds and co-financing: current and capital expenditure from EU funds, including EU subsidies to agriculture. Transactions in non-financial assets: acquisition and disposals of: land and subsoil assets, buildings and structures, machinery and equipment, valuables, intangible assets, stocks. Transactions in financial assets and liabilities (financing): currency and deposits, securities other than shares, financial derivatives, loans, shares and other equity, insurance technical reserves, other accounts receivable/payable. Also, financial assets and liabilities are classified according to the residence into domestic and foreign. The standard tables are used to disseminate information on general government operations. The data in these tables are compiled strictly in accordance with the methodological requirements set in the GFSM 2014. Two analytic balances are derived in the Statement of General Government Operations. Revenue less expense equals the net operating balance . Net operating balance minus net acquisition of non-financial assets results in net lending (+)/borrowing (-) . Net lending/borrowing is also equal to the net result of transactions in financial assets and liabilities. 2.2.1 Scope The general government sector and its subsectors (central government, local government, and social security funds) are defined in accordance with the ESA 2010 guidelines and the ESA 2010 Manual on Government Deficit and Debt. Exceptions to coverage There are no exceptions to coverage. Unrecorded activity There is no unrecorded activity. 2.4.1 Valuation Valuation of assets and liabilities: the value of an acquisition or disposal of financial asset or liability is its exchange value. Accrued interest is added to the principal of the underlying instrument (until 2013 accrued interest was classified under accounts payable). Valuation of foreign currency transactions: The official exchange rates set by the LB on the day of transaction are applied. 2.4.2 Recording basis Accounting basis: Operations of the State Social Insurance Fund and Compulsory Health Insurance Fund, public enterprise Turto bankas, public enterprise "Ignalina Nuclear Power Plant", public health care institutions, public higher education institutions, State Enterprises: Deposit and Investment Insurance, Lithuanian Energy Agency, National Center of Remote Sensing and Geoinformatics "GIS-Centras", Agriculture Information and Rural Business Center, "Invega", Agricultural Credit Guarantee Fund, Regitra, Agricultural Credit Guarantee Fund, UAB "LTG Link", the State Investment Management Agency (VIVA), the State Investment capital (VIK) and the State Aid Fund for Business (PVF) are recorded on an accrual basis. Data of extra-budgetary funds are recorded on a cash basis. Data of municipalities and State budget data are on a cash basis, but for statistical reporting interest receivable/payable, accruals of current expense (arrears), other adjustments for accrued data are used. Accruals of value-added tax, personal income tax and excises are derived from time-adjusted cash data, with the adjustment added concerning the deferred value-added tax. Data of municipal enterprises "Vilniaus miesto būstas" and "Vilniaus vystymo kompanija" are recorded on an accrual basis. Consumption of fixed capital data of budgetary central government and local governments is provided by Statistics Lithuania. Liquidation or complementary period: None, except for unused appropriations for financing special programs and construction, which have to be refunded within 10 days after year end. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures Adjustments for grossing and netting of transactions: In line with the international standards, the data are recorded on a gross basis. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Data sources: The main data source is the accounting records of central and local budgetary units and extra-budgetary funds. These data sources are received from the Ministry of Finance. Other data sources are the data of social security funds, which are received from these funds, and data provided by Central Bank. The annual financial statements of public health care institutions and higher education institutions are obtained from public sector accounting and reports consolidation information system (VSAKIS) database managed by Ministry of Finance. Quarterly financial statements of these institutions are obtained directly from them. Financial statements and statistical surveys are used for public corporations classified in General Government. Additional data sources are also used. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Classification system used for central and local budget revenues and expenditure in most cases matches ESA 2010 and GFSM 2014 . Nevertheless, reclassifications are necessary to obtain GFS, it concerns consolidation, EU funds (according to the final beneficiary criteria), interest swaps, etc. Source data meet the requirements of the ESA 2010 and Eurostat Manual on Government Deficit and Debt regarding definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Source data are available on a timely basis for producing government finance statistics that meet SDDS Plus requirements. 3.2.1 Source data assessment GFS compilation relies extensively on administrative data sources. Transmission errors are possible only to a limited extent and the data can be checked by using the budgets and annual accounts data. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Consolidation: consolidation is made in full compliance with the methodology set in the ESA2010 and GFSM 2014. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs Due to use of different data sources insignificant statistical discrepancy between financial and non-financial accounts occur. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses 5.1.5 Dissemination on request The SL provides statistical information on individual request (enquiry) if requested information is available and is not confidential. 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points The information on contact person is presented in each publication in the Official Statistics Portal. 5.3.2 Availability of documents and services catalogs Catalogue of statistical publications of the SL is available on Official Statistics Portal: https://osp.stat.gov.lt/statistikos-leidiniu-katalogas , terms and conditions for subscription are available on: https://osp.stat.gov.lt/e.-paslaugos .
