I. Analytical Framework, Concepts, Definitions, and Classifications
 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Currently there is no production index compiled by the Statistical Office . Data are compiled using internationally accepted standards and good practice. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization For manufacturing activities - broad classification according to ISIC Rev3.1. 

II. Scope of the data
 Scope of the data Production data (and other data used to estimate production ), published in the "Digest of Statistics" by the Statistics Office, comprise the following categories of physical indicators: Construction and industrial production (with unit of measure): Manufacturing activity: Manufacture of food products, beverages, and tobacco (various)   Manufacture of Wood and products of wood and cork including furniture (number)   Manufacture of containers of paper and paperboard (number)   Manufacture of other articles of paper and paperboard (case)   Printing and service activities related to printing (number)   Manufacture of other products (various)   Manufacture of plastic products (number)   Manufacture of animal feed (pounds)   Manufacture of selected construction materials (various)   Production of major industrial commodities various   Electricity generated and distributed (kilowatt-hours)   Water production (gallons) Transport and communication   Number of vehicles registered by type (number)   Road accidents reported (number)   Cargo loaded and landed at seaports (metric tons) Tourism   Tourist arrivals by country of residence, purpose of visit, (number )   Total visitor arrivals by first port of entry (number) Exceptions to coverage There are no exception. 

III. Accounting Conventions
 None 

IV. Nature of the Basic Data Sources
 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Production data for selected products manufactured products are obtained from quarterly survey of business establishments.  Data are obtained on quantity produced, value of goods produced, quantity and value of goods exported and sold domestically. Other data are obtained from administrative records 

V. Compilation Practices
 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures There are no data adjustments.
