 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The official reserve assets and the data template on International reserves and foreign currency liquidity are compiled in accordance with the IMF's International Reserves and Foreign Currency Liquidity Guidelines for a Data Template, 2013 , and the sixth edition of the Fund's Balance of Payments and International Investment Position Manual (BPM6) . The framework of the Data Template is built on two related concepts: (1) International Reserves (Reserve Assets) and (2) Foreign Currency Liquidity. The BPM6 sets forth the definition of the country's international reserves: "those external assets that are readily available to and controlled by monetary authorities for meeting balance of payments financing needs, for intervention in exchange markets to affect the currency exchange rate, and for other related purposes (such as maintaining confidence in the currency and the economy, and serving as a basis for foreign borrowing)." The concept of foreign currency liquidity is broader than that of international reserves. It refers to the amount of foreign exchange resources that is readily available to the authorities to meet a sudden increase in the demand for foreign exchange and the potential (net) drains on foreign currency resources resulting from the short-term foreign currency liabilities and off-balance-sheet activities of the authorities. Relationship to international guidelines: The concepts, definitions and classification underlying the data template on International reserves and foreign currency liquidity disseminated by the BNB are based on IMF's International Reserves and Foreign Currency Liquidity Guidelines for a Data Template, 2013, and the BPM6. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Classification: The official reserve assets and the data template on International reserves and foreign currency liquidity are compiled in accordance with the IMF's International Reserves and Foreign Currency Liquidity Guidelines for a Data Template, 2013, and the sixth edition of the Fund's Balance of Payments and International Investment Position Manual (BPM6) . In line with that the data are classified as follows: (1) Official reserve assets and other foreign currency assets incl. Official reserve assets: Foreign currency reserves (Securities; Currency and deposits); IMF reserve position; SDRs; Gold; Other reserve assets (accrued, but not paid interest), and Other foreign currency assets (Central Government deposits with local banks, gold not included in the official reserves, Brady bonds collateral); (2) Pre-determined short term net drains on foreign currency assets; (3) Contingent short-term net drains on foreign currency assets and (4) Memo items. Scope of the data Institutional coverage: Reserve assets data comprise official reserves of the Bulgarian National Bank. Template data also cover activity of the central government, excluding social security funds. Exceptions to coverage There are no exceptions to coverage. 2.4.1 Valuation Valuation: The International reserves are revaluated daily at market exchange rates. Securities positions are revaluated daily at the previous working day market prices of the instruments concerned. Monetary gold is revaluated daily at the current market price. Interest is accrued daily and classified under item 1(A). Conversion rates: Data are converted into national currency (euro). Frequency of calculation and revaluation: International reserve assets including monetary gold are calculated and revaluated daily. Securities positions are revaluated daily at the previous working day market prices of the instruments concerned. Reserve data template is calculated and revaluated monthly. Exceptions and special cases: None. 2.4.2 Recording basis The data is recorded daily by the Accounting Department of the BNB. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures Not applicable. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Data sources: Bulgarian National Bank, Ministry of Finance. Data on reserve assets are provided by the Accounting Department of the BNB; The Ministry of Finance provides information on pre-determined and contingent short-term net drains on foreign currency assets. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Source data are consistent with the definitions, scope and classifications, and time of recording of the “International reserves and foreign currency liquidity data template”. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Data collection and processing timetables are adequate to meet timeliness and periodicity for disseminating the “International reserves and foreign currency liquidity data template”. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Statistical adjustments: No statistical adjustments are applied to accounting records. 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points Prompt and knowledgeable support is available to users of on International reserves and foreign currency liquidity statistics.
