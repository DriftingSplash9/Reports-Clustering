 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Analytical Framework:The IIP statistics are established in accordance with the recommendations of BPM6 since the fourth quarter of 2013. The IIP therefore includes the stock of financial assets and liabilities between residents and nonresidents, including those held by non residents on entities located in the Tangiers free and offshore zones. The IIP started being compiled on quarterly basis since 2014. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The external assets and liabilities are classified according the standard components identified in the BPM6 (by financial instrument and by institutional sectors). Additional breakdowns (geographical and sectoral) for certain components are published in the Annual Report on International Investment Position and on the Office des Changes' website.. 2.2.1 Scope The external assets and liabilities are classified according the standard components identified in the BPM6 (by financial instrument and by institutional sectors). Additional breakdowns (geographical and sectoral) for certain components are published in the Annual Report on International Investment Position and on the Office des Changes' website.. Scope of the data Coverage of external financial liabilities: The stock of direct investment liabilities is based on : - the data of the annual survey covering the end-of-year stock and taking into account price changes (calculated according to the market capitalization method) ; - the data of real estate census estimated and reevaluated at the end of the year ; - the quarterly stock is estimated by adding to the starting stock (census and survey data) the transactions of the quarter, while taking into account exchange rate and/or price changes. - The data of trade credits between affiliates is based on a quarterly survey conducted among importers and exporters of goods and services. - The data of intercompany and shareholder's loans is based on a hybrid method combining the results of a quarterly survey of entities that used external financing and flows data collected from the banking system. The stock of portfolio investment is based on the data of the annual survey of international investments: - The end-of-year stock is obtained from the data collected directly from the declarants (survey) - the quarterly stock is calculated by adding to the starting stock (survey data) the transactions of the quarter concerned, while taking into account exchange rate effects. For investments of non-resident in listed companies, their data are obtained from the stock market regulator. Other investments are obtained through either administrative sources or appropriate survey. 2.4.1 Valuation Conversion to the unit of account: The IIP is compiled in dirhams and the amount outstanding denominated in different foreign currencies are converted into domestic currency at the average exchange rate of the dirham on the end of the reference quarter. This conversion method applies to the components of financial assets denominated in foreign currencies (Moroccan investments abroad), external debt, and foreign exchange deposits. Regarding foreign direct investment in Morocco, listed equity is valuated at market value while unlisted equity's market value is estimated by using Market capitalization method. Real estate liabilities are valuated at market price and Debt between affiliates is valuated at its nominal value. Moroccan direct investment abroad is valuated, for equity, according to the Own Funds at their Book Value method (data obtained from the foreign direct investment enterprise's books). For portfolio investment the stock is istablish according to the approch recommended by the CDIS guide interms of assets and liabilities (equity at their book value fot unlisted companies and at market values for listed companies). Valuation: The valuation principle used to compile the IIP is based on the market price. This applies to liabilities resulting from equity securities held by non residents in companies listed on the Casablanca stock exchange, debt securities issued by the resident sector (both public and private), and deposits. For Moroccan (direct and portfolio) investment assets abroad, their valuation is based on the method of own funds at bookvalue as reported by resident investors. 2.4.2 Recording basis Basis of recording:Balances of financial assets and liabilities are recorded under the IIP headings as soon as the claims and debts between residents and non residents arise, i.e. at the time the change in ownership takes place. In practice, this date usually corresponds to the settlement date. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Direct investment : Direct investment is composed of "Equity and investment fund shares", including real estate, and "Debt instruments". Equity and investment fund shares comprise equity held by direct investors, resident in an economy, in branches, subsidiaries, and associates located in another economy. To be recorded as direct investment, the investor's participation should meet the 10% threshold of the voting power. Reinvested earnings are also classified under this category. It comprises the resident direct investors' proportion, in terms of equity held, of the earnings that branches, subsidiaries, and associates do not distribute as dividends. Debt instruments cover intercompany loans and Shareholder's loans, Trade credits and advances between affiliated enterprises, as well as cash pooling : The data of trade credits between affiliates is based on a quarterly survey conducted among importers and exporters of goods and services. Data required of these companies are pulled directly from their accounting books (drawn from the balance sheet). The data of intercompany and shareholder's loans is based on a hybrid method combining the results of a quarterly survey of entities that used external financing and flows data collected from the banking system. Data on cash pooling assets are collected directly from a few economic operators that use this type of financing practice. For assets, the data are compiled on the basis of the Annual Foreign Investment Survey (AFIS) forms reported every year by enterprises investing abroad, completed, for the IIP quarterly version, by direct investment flows recorded in the balance of payments of the reference quarter, revalued at the exchange rate of the dirham at the end of the reference quarter. In addition to this, real estate held by resident individuals abroad is collected via administrative data sources, including the various spontaneous regularization operations. (Voluntary measures launched in 2014, 2020, and 2024, allowing Moroccan residents to declare assets held abroad in violation of the regulations on changes and tax legislation). Concerning liabilities, data on listed equity held by non-residents are collected from the Moroccan Capital Market Authority, whereas unlisted securities are assessed from the Annual survey forms reported by the main Moroccan direct investment enterprises. Portfolio investment : Portfolio investment is composed of "Equity and investment fund shares" and "Debt securities". Equity and investment fund shares cover securities that are not direct investment or reserve assets (less than 10% of the voting power). Debt securities are negotiable instruments serving as evidence of a debt. The stock of portfolio investment are collected through of the annual survey of international investments: The end-of-year stock is obtained from the data collected directly from the declarants (survey) The quarterly stock is calculated by adding to the starting stock (survey data) the transactions of the quarter concerned, while taking into account exchange rate effects. For investments of non-resident in listed companies, their data are obtained from the stock market regulator. Other investment : Trade credits: Since the first quarter of 2014, the Foreign Exchange Office adopted the results of a survey to collect quarterly data on trade credits and advances related to the imports and exports of the Moroccan companies operating in foreign trade. Data required of these companies are pulled directly from their accounting books (drawn from the balance sheet). Loans and borrowings: Public External Debt : Public external debt is based on data supplied to the Foreign Exchange Office by the Department of Treasury and External Finance (Ministry of Economy and Finance). This debt consists of state debt (including debt guaranteed by the state), public banks debt and other sectors debt (public enterprises and local communities) Private External Debt : The outstanding of private external debt is determined on the basis of the arrangement set up by the Foreign Exchange Office to monitor the debt contracted abroad by the private sector. This device consists in a quarterly survey of economic operators using external financing. This survey allows to collect data on stocks of private external debt, nature of the loans contracted, their maturity, funder's country of residence, resident debtor's field of activity, drawdowns and repayments terms. Private External loans (assets) : The monthly publication of monetary and financial statistics from the Central Bank of Morocco is used to identify private external loans granted ton non-residents. Currency and deposits: This aggregate use, as sources of information, the monthly publication of monetary and financial statistics from the Central Bank of Morocco (including offshore banks assets and liabilities), and direct statements of the most important economic operators holding accounts abroad. Other equities : Other equities in terms of assets consist primarily of Moroccan investments in some international organizations. Special Drawing Rights allocations: Data on SDR's allocations is provided by the Central Bank of Morocco (monetary and financial statistics). Financial Derivatives: Financial derivatives data are apprehended trough a bank reporting system which provide information on all types of derivatives: swaps, futures, forwards, options. Reserve assets: Data under this heading are compiled on the basis of central bank data. Survey forms used to collect the data from the respondents are available on the website of Office des Changes at www.oc.gov.ma . 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording 3.2.1 Source data assessment Data verification: Stock and transaction data recorded in the Balance of Payments and International Investment Position are regularly cross-checked.
