 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Compilation and dissemination of International Investment Position is done according to the internationally recognized standards, set by the International Monetary Fund (IMF) and most recently published in 2009 in the sixth edition of Balance of Payments and International Investment Position Manual (BPM6). Definition: The International Investment Position (IIP) is a statistical report that provides the stocks values and composition at the end of the reporting period of: –financial assets of residents of an economy, representing claims of that economy residents on nonresidents, as well as gold bullions held as reserve assets, and –financial liabilities of residents of an economy, representing liabilities of that economy residents towards nonresidents. IIP report for BH is not presented as an integrated IIP statement (does not show opening position, transactions, other changes, and closing position). The IIP compiled by the CBBH presents the position at the end of quarter. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The classification in BH IIP is based on the IMF's Balance of Payments and International Investment Position Manual, sixth edition (BPM6). Standard components of IIP are classified as: - Assets: direct investment; portfolio investment; financial derivatives; other investment and reserve assets - Liabilities: direct investment; portfolio investment; financial derivatives and other investment. International investment position is compiled by sectors as recommended in BPM6: Central Bank; Deposit-taking corporations, except central bank; General government; and Other sectors including Other financial corporations and Nonfinancial corporations, households, and NPISHs. 2.2.1 Scope The classification in BH IIP is based on the IMF's Balance of Payments and International Investment Position Manual, sixth edition (BPM6). Standard components of IIP are classified as: - Assets: direct investment; portfolio investment; financial derivatives; other investment and reserve assets - Liabilities: direct investment; portfolio investment; financial derivatives and other investment. International investment position is compiled by sectors as recommended in BPM6: Central Bank; Deposit-taking corporations, except central bank; General government; and Other sectors including Other financial corporations and Nonfinancial corporations, households, and NPISHs. Scope of the data The geographic territory of reference is the territory of Bosnia and Herzegovina (BH). Residency principle, as defined in the BPM6 manual, represents the basic principle followed for recording of international investment positions for BH. Data are disseminated in millions KM, showing positions of reserve assets, assets and liabilities of direct investments, portfolio investments, financial derivatives and other investments. The data cover the entire BH economy. Sectorial coverage: The sector classification involves the sector classification of residents according to ESA 2010. It is fully harmonized with the sector classification of the gross external debt by domestic sectors. Geographical coverage: The International Investment Position is presented in relation to the rest of the world, without geographical breakdown. Exceptions to coverage The compilation of portfolio investment data in IIP is incomplete regarding coverage. The direct investment survey is developed with the ability to compile data on portfolio investment, as well. Thus, covered are only portfolio investment of the companies with direct investment. As an additional data source MFS data are used for DTC and other financial corporations. Assets of monetary authorities in the form of debt securities are included in reserve assets. Regarding assets and liabilities of General government sector, data are obtained from Government Finance and Financial Accounts Statistics. Unrecorded activity Unrecorded activities are estimated using different statistical models (based on correlation with similar data series compiled through surveys, from administrative data sources, ITRS, etc.). 2.4.1 Valuation Assets and liabilities are mostly valued at market prices for most of portfolio investment data and reserve assets. For direct investment in form of equity main valuation method is own funds at book values. Nominal value as main valuation method is used for direct investment in form od debt and for other investment. For conversion to unit of account data reported in foreign currencies are converted to BAM using Central Bank od Bosnia and Herzegovina 's end of period midpoint exchange rate. 2.4.2 Recording basis Principle used for recording debt position data is on accrual basis. Data providers are encouraged to report on accrual basis where the debt balance includes principal and interest that have accrued but not yet payable, interest arrears and future principal payments. Requirements for accrual recording are met to a high degree but not fully, the Government loan liabilities are exception as these do not include accrued interest. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures Grossing and netting procedures, followed in CBBH's presentation of data, correspond to BPM6 guiding principle. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The international investment position statement is compiled in conformity with the methodology set forth in the IMF's BPM6. Primary data sources for compiling IIP statistics are: – Direct Surveys (survey on direct investment, survey on trade credits and advance payments), – Statistical and Administrative sources (publications of the IMF, Bank for International Settlements, various BH state ministries, BH statistical institutions), and – Statistics compiled within the CBBH (Monetary and Financial Statistics (MFS), Government Finance and Financial Accounts Statistics (GFFAS), and data received from Banking Department, Cash Management Department, Monitoring and Analyses Department, Accounting and Finance Department, and BH Balance of Payments Statistics). These data collection programs proved to be comprehensive providing reliable and accurate data. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording The framework for compilation of the IIP fully follows the concepts and definitions prescribed in BPM6. Direct surveys (survey on direct investment, survey on trade credits and advance payments) are prepared and conducted in line with recommendations stated in BPM6 and OECD benchmark definition of DI fourth edition. These surveys have detailed explanations and definition of terms used. Accrual recording, appropriate valuation method, classifications and time of recording are required by the surveys and met to a high degree by reporting units. GFS statistics follows GFSM manual 2001 for compilation of general government (GG) statement on government operations. External debt of the GG is compiled in accordance with External debt statistics guide for compilers and users 2013. Monetary statistics data as a source for compilation of the IIP follows standards and rules set up in the Monetary and Financial Statistics Manual edition 2000. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Source data required for compilation of quarterly IIP statistics are available with frequency and timeliness as follows: - Survey on direct investments – quarterly frequency with 45 days timeliness, - Survey on direct investments – annual frequency with 6 months timeliness, - Survey on trade credits and advances – annual frequency with timeliness by the end of March, - Monetary and Financial Statistics (MFS) – monthly frequency with 4 to 5 weeks timeliness, except for Other Financial Corporation (quarterly frequency with 4 month timeliness), - Government Finance and Financial Accounts Statistics (GFFAS) – quarterly frequency with 45 days timeliness 3.2.1 Source data assessment Data on direct, portfolio and other investment collected through survey on direct investment are assessed against Monetary and financial statistics for sector of Deposit Taking Corporation and Other Financial Corporation. Data on direct investment, obtained through the survey, are assessed against the data from financial statements of enterprises, following positive law regulations collected by FIA (Financial Information Agency) and APIF (Intermediary Agency for IT and financial services), two official agencies who are in charge of collecting financial statements of enterprises. Assessment refers to the data on profit and loss, reinvested earnings, loans and dividends. This assessment improves the coverage of the companies with small amount of registered equity, but significant amount of reinvested earnings and other capital, including loans. Data on external debt of government sector obtained from Government Finance Statistics are also assessed against data from BH Ministry of Finance and Treasury. Stocks related to SDR and IMF accounts are assessed against the IMF accounts available on the IMF web site. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Direct investment: CBBH conduct a regular quarterly (on a sample) and annual (full coverage of companies with DI exceeding a KM 100,000 threshold) direct survey on DI. The survey includes financial and non–financial corporations which have, or are assumed to have, foreign investment from abroad, which invest abroad or are borrowing funds from foreign countries. For the companies with direct investment below the threshold, CBBH makes estimates based on data obtained by courts on registered invested foreign capital. The survey is conducted in accordance with BPM6 and OECD benchmark definition of DI fourth edition. For the purpose of compilation of quarterly IIP data on deposit-taking corporations (DTC) and other financial corporations are collected through DI regular quarterly surveys. For non-financial corporations data obtained from annual survey are used for quarterly estimates. The estimate takes into consideration the consequences of financial transactions and other economic changes which took place over the observed period. According to the applied IMF methodology retained earnings are presented on a quarterly basis for the period when earned. Data on profits and reinvested earnings are collected on annual basis and published within the deadline T + 8 months. During the year, quarterly data on equity capital are adjusted for projected data on reinvested earnings. For quarterly BOP reinvested earnings of other financial and non-financial corporations are estimated on the basis of an aggregated estimate and available information on distributed dividends. The annual aggregated value is projected, based on previous periods and index of industrial production (published by BH Agency for Statistics). For DTC sector data on reinvested earnings for each particular bank are estimated based on available cumulative data on profit and distributed dividends for past period of current year provided by the MFS. Estimated data are subject to revisions once annual survey results are obtained. Portfolio investment: The direct investment survey is developed with the ability to compile data on portfolio investment, as well. However, compilation of portfolio investment data in this respect is incomplete regarding coverage. The survey covers only portfolio investment of the companies with direct investment. As an additional data source for DTC and other financial corporations, MFS data are used while for the non-financial corporations ITRS is additional data source. For the General government sector data are obtained from (GFFAS) Government Finance and Financial Accounts Statistics. Assets of monetary authorities in the form of debt securities are included in reserve assets. Other investments: The data mostly obtained from the Monetary and Financial Statistics, GFFAS, CBBH Banking Department. Data from Bank for International Settlements (BIS) quarterly reports are used as a source of currency and deposits of non-financial corporation. Data on other investment for other sectors are obtained from the CBBH survey on direct investment (which covers data on other investment of companies), and data on loans of nonfinancial corporations provided by Entities Ministries of Finance. Data on trade credits and advances for DTC and Other financial corporations are obtained from Monetary and Financial Statistics. The data for non-financial corporations are compiled through the CBBH direct quarterly survey on trade credits and advance payments. Trade credits and advance payments survey covers the biggest importers and exporters. Data on trade credits collected through DI survey are crosschecked with data collected through survey on trade credits and advance payments to avoid overlaps, and these are treated as direct investments. Data from the survey, are also used to estimate quarterly positions on trade credit and advances. Reserve assets: Source data obtained from the CBBH Banking Department, Monitoring and Analyses Department and Accounting Department. Coverage is fully complete and data are reliable and accurate with full respect of accrual recording. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Trade credits and advance payments survey is conducted on a sample and statistical techniques are used to estimate data for entire population. Data on trade credits collected through DI survey are crosschecked with data collected through survey on trade credits and advance payments to avoid overlaps, and these are treated as direct investments. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results Source data used for the compilation of IIP are reliable and accurate to the extent possible. The population used as a base for a sample, the creation of sample, response rates, coverage, and accuracy of reported data are regularly monitored and updated. Thus, data sources as well as data itself are considered comprehensive and reliable. Results of the assessments carried out are monitored and made available to guide future statistical processes. Validation of intermediate results are applied as follows: - Logical and mathematical data validation in surveys. Opening balance should be in line with the closing balance that are reported in the past period. Closing balance must be equal to sum of opening balance, net financial transactions and other changes. - Cross-checking of different source data, and if there are substantial discrepancies investigation is conducted to check if certain transactions are unrecorded, or coverage and scope issues exist, or there are some valuation and time of recording issues. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs Assessment of discrepancies is conducted as follows: · Data on direct investment, obtained through the survey, are assessed against the data from financial statements of enterprises, collected by FIA (Financial Information Agency) and APIF (Intermediary Agency for IT and financial services), two official agencies who are in charge of collecting financial statements of enterprises. Assessment refers to the data on profit and loss, reinvested earnings, loans and dividends. · Data on direct, portfolio and other investment collected through survey on direct investment are assessed against Monetary and financial statistics for sector of Deposit Taking Corporation and Other Financial Corporation. · Data on external debt of government sector obtained from Government Finance Statistics are also assessed against data from Ministry of Finance and Treasury BH. Discrepancies are analysed jointly with GFS and MFS Sections, and where possible corrections are made. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses No formal revision studies are carried out, but data is regularly revised and updated. Please see 4.3.1. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses There are no formal studies and analyses of revisions nor dissemination. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request Data are disseminated upon request in accordance with confidentiality principles. 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points The email statistics@cbbh.ba includes email of Head of Section for Balance of Payments Statistics. 5.3.2 Availability of documents and services catalogs N/A
