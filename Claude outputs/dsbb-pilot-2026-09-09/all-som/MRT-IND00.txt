 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Since 2007, the National Statistics Office (ONS) has prepared and disseminated a new Industrial Production Index (IPI). It covers the modern industrial businesses (manufacturing or processing) that reside in and produce in the national territory. The base period is the average quarter between Q3 2016 and Q2 2017. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The Industrial Production Index is generated and published according to the classification of AFRISTAT Member States ( NOPEMA ), adjusted to the Mauritanian context. This classification is from the international type classification, by industry, of all branches of economic activity (ISIC, rev4). The IPI covers 58 products and 165 varieties. The IPI is a Laspeyres index. Scope of the data The Industrial Production Index covers extraction activities (Divisions 06 to 08), the manufacturing industries (Divisions 10 to 33), the production and distribution of electricity, natural gas, and the catchment, treatment and distribution of water (Division 35 to 39).
