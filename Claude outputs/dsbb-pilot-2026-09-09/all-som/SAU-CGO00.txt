 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Analytical framework: The central government data are organized for analytical purposes based on the framework of the IMF Government Finance Statistics Manual 2014 (GFSM2014) and the System of National Accounts 2008. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Fiscal data is compiled by calendar year. According to the data compiled for the previous years for Central Government, data is fully consistence with GFSM 2014 standard. Functional classification of expenditures (COFOG) is yet to be compiled. In the meantime, MOF imposed some rules to facilitate allocating expenditure for COFOG, however, the new Chart of Accounts and new guidelines make it mandatory for each transaction to be mapped to the correspondent economic classification as well as to match with the relevant COFOG category. 2.2.1 Scope Fiscal data is compiled by calendar year. According to the data compiled for the previous years for Central Government, data is fully consistence with GFSM 2014 standard. Functional classification of expenditures (COFOG) is yet to be compiled. In the meantime, MOF imposed some rules to facilitate allocating expenditure for COFOG, however, the new Chart of Accounts and new guidelines make it mandatory for each transaction to be mapped to the correspondent economic classification as well as to match with the relevant COFOG category. Scope of the data Central government sector agencies are covered, The accounts of the budget cover 545 agencies including ministries, commissions, public entities, municipalities and other agencies The government budget and the related executive instructions are legislated through a Royal Decree and decisions of the Council of Ministers. The budget decree contains detailed revenue and expenditure for the consolidated budget of the central government. It contains mainly instructions to ministries on the implementation of the budget, including on: the need to implement projects budgeted for; the need not to exceed allocations and approved staff positions. 2.4.1 Valuation Flows are valued on the basis of the amount of payments in cash, or cash collection (e.g., fixed term contractual obligations). Amounts denominated in foreign currency are converted to national currency using market rates on the date of recording. 2.4.2 Recording basis The cash basis is used to record financial transactions. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Central government: The data on Revenue, Expenditure is obtained from the Ministry of Finance’s accounting system. Information on financing is provided by NDMC and SAMA. For budgetary agencies, data are available on a quarterly basis. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Detailed source data for the revenue and expenditure of the domestic operations of the budgetary central government are consistent with the GFSM 2014 . 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Source data are available on quarterly basis and with timeliness of about one month. 3.2.1 Source data assessment Data sources based on accounting records gives assurance that the best sources have been used in the compilation of fiscal data. A new governance is introduced since the beginning of 2019, between the MFPD and General Department of Accounts. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Compilation of fiscal statistics is based on comprehensive source data. There is no need to estimate or impute missing data. Statistics are first published as preliminary and then revised as final. Reporting dates allow sufficient time to ensure that complete and accurate information is reported. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results The likelihood of inconsistencies is minimal since the data are prepared from accounting records.
