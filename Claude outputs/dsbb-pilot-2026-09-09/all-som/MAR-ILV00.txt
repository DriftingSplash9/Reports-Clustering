 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Analytical Framework: Gross International Reserves are compiled according to the guidelines set out in the Sixth Edition of the Balance of Payments Manual . The Data Template on International Reserves and Foreign Currency Liquidity are compiled according to the recommendations set out in the International Reserves and Foreign Currency Liquidity: Guidelines for a Data Template . Concepts: The international reserves posted on the Dissemination Standards Bulletin Board (DSBB) and on the Bank’s website in the Foreign Assets Table (line 6), include liquid assets in foreign exchange held directly by Bank Al-Maghrib. Definition: The international reserves or gross foreign assets of Bank Al-Maghrib correspond to claims in foreign exchange on non-residents. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Sectorization: The residency criterion is consistent with the definition of IMF's Balance of Payments Manual, 6th edition (BPM6). Indeed, the concept of residency is not linked to nationality or legal criteria but is based on the centre of economic interest. Therefore, resident units comprise: Moroccan and foreign individuals who have their main centre of economic interest in Morocco, Moroccan public officials stationed abroad, and Moroccan or foreign legal persons for their establishments in Morocco. Non-residents include Moroccan or foreign individuals who have their main centre of economic interest abroad, foreign public officials stationed in Morocco and Moroccan, and Moroccan or foreign legal persons for their establishments abroad. In addition, the transactions of Moroccans living abroad with resident banks are treated as with residents. Although Moroccan expatriates are considered non-residents, their assets are generally intended to finance current expenditure and/or investment in Morocco. Classification: The Template for International reserves and foreign currency liquidity covers include assets in foreign exchange (securities, cash, and deposits), current assets in gold and SDR as well as the IMF subscription-reserve tranche. | The standard form for international reserves and liquid assets in foreign exchange covers the official reserve assets (Table I) as well as projected short-term net outflows in foreign exchange (Table II) and the composition of the foreign exchange reserves (Table IV). Table 1 "Official reserve assets and other assets in foreign exchange" provides detailed information on the official exchange reserves by type of financial instrument (securities, cash, and deposits broken down by institution domiciled in Morocco or abroad, IMF position/ reserve tranche, and current assets in gold and in SDR). Table II "Projected short-term net outflows of foreign exchange resources" records short-term public external debt obligations (external debt of the Treasury and guaranteed debt) compiled on the basis of the amount outstanding and end-month data. These projections are broken down by type of debt (direct Treasury or guaranteed debt), by type of obligation (principal and/or interest), and by maturity (≤ 1 month, > 1 month and ≤ 3 months, > 3 months and ≤ 1 year). Forwards and swaps are reported in Section II.2 of the reserves data model. It should be noted that this item also includes currencies to be delivered by the Central Bank in foreign exchange swap operations with resident domestic banks. Thus, in order to inject additional liquidity in domestic currency, the Central Bank purchases foreign currencies and undertakes to resell them forward against domestic currency. Table IV "Memorandum Items," under heading 2a) "Composition of foreign exchange reserves," shows the amount of official exchange reserves denominated in the currencies making up the SDR basket. Scope of the data Institutional Coverage: The international reserves cover the exchange reserves held by Bank Al-Maghrib and shown on its books. Instrument Coverage: In addition to the IMF subscription/reserve tranche, the official reserves are held in the form of securities, cash, deposits in foreign exchange, and assets in gold and in SDR. 2.4.1 Valuation Valuation: Operations are generally recorded on the settlement-delivery date, on a gross basis and at the acquisition price. Frequency of Calculation and Revaluation Foreign currencies and deposits denominated in assets and liabilities are converted into dirhams at each balance sheet date, based on the daily average of the buying and selling exchange rate of currencies quoted by Bank Al-Maghrib, on the date of the balance sheet. Gold is valued at the price of an ounce of gold as set in USD at the London fixing on the last business day of the week SDR holdings and allocations are revalued at the average exchange rate of SDR applicable on the date of the balance sheet. Since November 2015 debt securities held by Bank Al-Maghrib are revalued at the market price on the date of the balance sheet. Moreover, historical data series going back to January 2013 have been reconstructed and a progressive approach will be adopted for the reconstruction of the previous series. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Data Sources: The data in Tables I, II and IV of the Data Template for International Reserves and Foreign Currency Liquidity are compiled by the Department of Statistics and Data management (DSGD)on the basis of statistics produced by : the Finance and Strategy Department and the Department of Monetary and Exchange Operations of Bank Al-Maghrib ; the Department of Treasury and External Finance of the Ministry of Economy and Finance 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Statistical Adjustments: No statistical adjustments are made.
