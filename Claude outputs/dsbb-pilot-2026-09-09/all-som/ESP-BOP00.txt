 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Balance of Payments data are elaborated according to the International Monetary Fund's sixth edition of the Balance of Payments and Internantional Investment Position Manual (BPM6) guidelines. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The classification used to compile Spain's Balance of Payments is essentially the one set forth in the BPM6 and fully consistent with the SNA2008 (System of National Accounts). Exceptions are noted below. The sector breakdown, functional categories and instrument classification are fully consistent with the BPM6. Scope of the data The data cover the entire Spanish territory. Resident institutional units are defined in conformity with the sixth edition of the Balance of Payments and International Investment Position Manual's concepts of economic territory and center of economic interest. Exceptions to coverage As regards direct reporting to the Banco de España of the Other Resident Sector, if the amounts of transactions during the immediately preceding year, or balances of assets and liabilities on December 31 of the previous year, are less than €1 million, the form is only to be sent at the express request of the Banco de España within a maximum period of two months from the date of request. Unrecorded activity There is no unrecorded activity. 2.4.1 Valuation Valuation is based on the market prices prevailing when the transaction takes place. In Foreign Trade statistics, the market price is collected even if the delivery is part of an unrequited transfer. In transactions collected through the Direct Reporting System of the Banco de España, the actually paid amount is reported, which in most cases reflects market prices. Conversion to unit of account: In Foreign Trade statistics, transactions with other EU countries are denominated in euros, whereas extra-Union transactions are denominated in the original currency and converted into euros at the rate applied by the Customs Authorities. In the Direct Reporting System of the Banco de España, transactions are converted into euros at the average market exchange rate (the average of the buying and selling rates) of the date on which the transaction is made. In the International Trade in Services Survey, transactions are recommended to be converted into euros at the quarterly average exchange rate. 2.4.2 Recording basis In general terms, the Balance of Payments is compiled following the accrual principle. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures Broadly consistent with the BPM6. The Current and Capital Account are recorded on a gross basis. The Financial Account transactions are recorded on a net basis. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The following blocks can be distinguished: 1. Data collection by the Statistics Department of the Banco de España. 1.1 Reporting of foreign economic transactions and financial asset and liability balances by Spanish residents. It covers individuals and legal persons (public or private) resident in Spain, other than providers of payment services entered in the official register of the Banco de España, when they carry out transactions with non-residents (acts, business and operations that involve or derive in charges, payments and/or exterior transfers, as well as variations in accounts or financial debtor or creditor positions) or maintain assets or liabilities overseas. Data reported: a) Operations with non-residents, whatever their nature and regardless of how these are settled, i.e. by means of foreign transfers or through payment or debts in bank accounts or inter-company settlements, be it by offsetting or by delivery of cash. b) Balances and changes in assets or liabilities abroad, in whatever form they materialize (accounts in banking or financial entities, accounts between companies, deposits of cash or securities, equity, instruments of debt, financial instruments, derivatives, real estate, etc.). 1.2 Reporting of foreign economic transactions by payment service providers. The Payment Service Providers (PSP) registered at the Bank of Spain's special Registry are required to declare: a) International receipts and payments of residents and non-residents (other than PSP) settled through a resident Payment Service Provider. b) Credits and debits to/from non-residents (other than PSP) accounts. c) Sending and receiving of euro notes and coins by Payment Service Providers to/from their foreign correspondent. 1.3 Reporting of transactions and stocks of marketable securities. Information on tradable securities is also collected from resident custodians. For tradable securities held by residents through non-resident custodians, the declaration is made directly by the holder under the system described in the first paragraph or under the integrated declaration of financial statements by credit institutions if the holder pertains to this sector. The ECB Centralized Securities DataBase (CSDB) and Securities Issues Statistics - CSEC are is used for compilation purposes. 2. Data provided by other departments of the Banco de España. Money transfers operators' data, credit cards data, General Government transfers channeled through the Banco de España, operations on own account of the Banco de España, credit institutions' financial statements reported for statistics and supervision purposes. 3. Data provided by other statistical agencies. 3.1 Tax Authority: a) Cross border trade of big services enterprises from the statistics on Sales, Employment and Wages in Large Companies, that takes the data from Tax forms VAT related. b) Tax form 720, related to the declaration of overseas assets held outside of Spain. 3.2 National Statistics Office: a) International Trade in Services (and other operations) survey. Sample enterprise survey with quarterly frequency. Covers Other services, Goods under merchanting, Miscellaneous Current Transfers, Acquisitions/Disposals of non-produced, non-financial Assets (except natural resources) and Capital Transfers (investment grants, taxes and other capital transfers). b) Tourism Surveys: Tourist Expenditure Survey (EGATUR) and Resident Travel Survey (ETR). c) National Accounts estimates. Insurance, freights and imputed accommodation services in own dwellings (mainly), among others. d) Other National Statistics Office operations. Economically Active Population Survey, Population Census (in Spain and of Spaniards abroad), Housing price indexes, migration statistics. 3.3 Ministry of Economy, Trade and Business: Foreign Direct Investment Register (RIE). 4. Other General Government data directly provided to the Banco de España (mainly from administrative registers): 4.1 General Audit Office: data for some of the secondary income and capital account items. 4.2 Ministry of Inclusion, Social Security and Migrations: data on work permits, compensation of employees paid abroad, social contributions and social benefits. 4.3 Ministry of Defense: services, compensation of employees paid abroad and transfers. 4.4 Ministry of Foreign Affairs, European Union and Cooperation: data on primary and secondary income. 4.5 Ministry of Economy, Trade and Business: Spanish Treasury, Instituto de Crédito Oficial (ICO), Spanish Executive Resolution Authority and Directorate General of Insurance and Pension Funds. 5. Other agencies: General Council of Notaries (transactions of real estate located in Spain). 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Definitions, scope and classifications used in source data are broadly in line with the BPM6. In some cases, adjustment procedures exist to ensure that source data conform to the coverage, classification, valuation and timing conventions of the BPM6. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Some sources are only available on an annual basis, being necessary to estimate quarterly figures based on models and indicators. Population Census of Spaniards living abroad. Stock data are considered stable all through the year. These figures are used to estimate personal transfers. Annual data reported by Spanish residents different from credit institutions on foreign economic transactions and financial asset and liability balances. As regards direct reporting to the Banco de España, if the amounts of transactions during the immediately preceding year or balances of assets and liabilities on December 31 of the previous year are less than €100 million, the form is only to be sent annually. Most are distributed among the year linearly. Income on direct investment equity for Other Residents Sector. The Foreign Direct Investment Register (RIE) provides annual data. 3.2.1 Source data assessment For Goods, the Customs Department provides estimates of operations under the threshold of declaration and of non-response. The successive modifications of the penalty rules have led the latter to be really small. In addition, the International Trade in Services and Other Operations Suvey of the NSO provides information on merchanting, including certain operative related to the outsorcing of production process (factoryless goods production). For services and other transactions covered by the quarterly survey International Trade in Services and Other International Operations, the National Statistics Office (NSO) provides the results of the Survey, having made the imputations for non-response. The sampling process of the International Trade in Services and Other Operations Survey of the NSO is mainly based on: a) until 2022 data, the Banco de España's reporting population of foreign transactions; b) from 2023 data onwards on tax records (VAT form on intra EU transactions of services). This population is complemented with information from Customs on reporters involved in transactions of goods for processing/processed and for repairing/repaired. Financial account is based broadly on censuses. Non-response is checked in every update of information by crossing different sources of information and by assessment of temporal consistency for which checking of consistency between flows and stocks plays an important role. Direct interaction with the respondents assures the subsequent collection of information. Examples of sources used to cross-check the data are: data on Foreign Direct Investment equity transactions from the Investment Register of the Ministry of Economy, Trade and Business; information from the CNMV-National Commission of the Securities Market (agency in charge of supervising and inspecting the Spanish Stock Markets and the activities of all the participants in those markets). To estimate insurance and pension fund technical reserves hold by households, mirror data compiled by ECB from insurance corporations and pension funds under statistical reporting regulation are used. For deposits abroad of the Other Residents Sector, the data of the direct reporting system has potential coverage problems and therefore mirror data from the Bank for International Settlements' statistics "Locational banking statistics by residence", Balance Sheet Items (BSI) statistics (ECB statistics based on aggregated or consolidated balnace sheet of the monetary financial institutions sector) and form 720 of the Tax Agency (Declaration of assets held abroad) for the remaining countries are used. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques 1. Goods: In Foreign Trade statistics there are two different sources: 1. In extra-trade (merchandise with countries outside the European Union), the statistical value is collected, which includes all distribution costs for the goods free on board (f.o.b.) of the reporting area. 2. In Intrastat (data collection system for trade between member countries of the European Union), the statistical value is only collected for big companies, whereas the rest has to report only the invoice value, which is recalculated to a statistical value by the Customs Department. Further adjustments are made for coverage and valuation to conform the data with the concepts of the BPM6, in particular to change imports to a f.o.b. basis. An estimation of transactions below the threshold is also included. 2. Travel: Data on travel credits are obtained from the Tourist Expenditure Survey (EGATUR), of the NSO, adjusting the results in order to deduct international transportation and tour-operators´commissions. Travel debits are estimated on the basis of: a) the evolution shown by payments abroad made with credit cards issued against accounts held in Spain, b) geographical information from the Resident Travel Survey (ETR), of the NSO, and c) mirror data. Both for credits and debits, imputed accommodation services in own dwellings estimated by the National Accounts (NSO) are included. 3. Other services: data on transport services are corrected in order to ensure consistency with the f.o.b. valuation of merchandise trade. For freight, an estimation method is used based on ratios which are derived from statistical information on foreign trade volumes from Foreign trade statistics of the Customs department, and freight rates and settlements for transportation obtained from different sources (main national transport companies, Asociación de Navieros Españoles (ANAVE), Sea rates and Port Authority and administrative sources (Ministry of Transport and Sustainable Mobility for road transport among others). In addition, the estimation incorporates the information on the country breakdown of the freight transport services (invoice value) by transport mode collected in the ITSS data. Insurance: Gross premiums paid and gross claims received are collected from the International Trade in Services Survey (NSO). In order to estimate BOP services (and the corresponding transfers), two additional sources are considered. In the case of non-life insurance and reinsurance, ITSS data are complemented with information provided by the Directorate General of Insurance and Pension Funds. In the case of freight transport insurance, the estimates combine ITSS data with Foreign trade statistics of the Customs Department and freight transport statistics of the Ministry of Transport and Sustainable Mobility. 4. Estimates of illegal or non-legally regulated activities are provided by the National Accounts/NSO following recommendations established at the European level and included as goods or services. 5. Compensation of employees. Credits estimates are based on information on Spaniards working for establishments abroad out of the NSO's Economically Active Population Survey combined with the National Accounts estimates on compensation per employee (taking into account country and sector of economic activity). Debits combine data on work permits from the Ministry of Inclusion, Social Security and Migration, tourists coming to Spain for business purposes (from the tourism surveys) and the National Accounts estimates on compensation per employee (broken down by activity sector). Furthermore, data are directly reported by the General Government on local staff hired in extraterritorial enclaves. All data are estimated in gross terms (including income taxes and social security contributions). 6. Investment income: Foreign Direct Investment Reinvested earnings are estimated on the Current Operating Performance Concept (COPC) basis combining accountancy data on total profits declared to the Investment Register of the Ministry of Economy, Trade and Business for the ORS, for the MFI's data are collect from the annual accounts and the financial statements reported to the Banco de España. Foreign receipts and payments related to the distribution of earnings directly declared to the Banco de España. The information provided by the Ministry is only available with a delay of 15 months. For the last two years, ratios are provided by the Central Balance Sheet Data Office of the Banco de España and are supplemented with some additional information: dividends data declared to the Banco de España; accountancy data on non-financial resident enterprises provided by the Central Balance Sheet Data Office of the Banco de España and ad-hoc investigation of accountancy statements of major investors/direct investment enterprises. The accrual principle is applied to the Portfolio Investment income on debt using the debtor approach. Estimations on reinvested earnings on investment funds are included in the Portfolio Investment income. In portfolio investment liabilities, accrual is calculated considering the stock estimated by the residual approach, on a security-by-security basis, and the income from Securities Issues Statistics – CSEC (ESCB statistics), also on a security-by-security basis.The accrual principle is followed in most of other investment cases. Reference interest rates are combined with stocks data. 7. Social benefits. Debits are directly reported by the Ministry of Inclusion, Social Security and Migration. Credits are estimated on the basis of: a) information provided by the Ministry on the number of residents that have paid contributions in other countries and on information of benefits per person published in the framework of the National Accounts by main counterpart countries; and b) mirror data reported by other EU/EFTA members and by the United Kingdom to the European Comission and published in statistical reports on intra-EU labour mobility and EU social security coordination. 8. Workers' remittances. Data are estimated on the basis of information on transfers channeled through Money Transfer Operators. In the case of credits, MTOs data are complemented with data from the NSO population census of Spaniards abroad. In the case of debits, these estimates are checked periodically analyzing demographic statistics and bilateral information that we obtain contacting directly with main counterpart contries receiving remittances from Spain. 9. Portfolio Investment: Transactions in negotiable securities are assigned to the related institutional sector on the basis of the resident issuer in the case of liabilities, and to the resident sector underwriting or buying the securities in the case of assets. The liabilities of debt instruments in portfolio investment begin to be estimated using the residual approach for all sectors, on a security-by-security basis since 2020 data. This estimate is obtained as the difference between the total issuance and the portfolios of resident investors. Data from Securities Issues Statistics - CSEC (issuance statistics with information from the ECB's centralized securities database, security by security, and including principal indexation in inflation-indexed issuances) and data on holdings, also security by security, of resident investors are used. 10. Other Investment: For deposits abroad of the Other Residents Sector, the data of the direct reporting system has potential coverage problems and therefore the Bank for International Settlements' statistics "Locational banking statistics by residence", Balance Sheet Items (BSI) statistics (ECB statistics based on aggregated or consolidated balance sheet of the monetary financial institutions sector) and form 720 of the Tax Agency are used. To estimate insurance and pension fund technical reserves hold by households, mirror data compiled by ECB from insurance corporations and pension funds under statistical reporting regulation is used. 11. Direct Investment: Foreign investments in real estate in Spain are calculated on the basis of the following: a) information on sales and purchases from the General Council of Notaries, b) data on tourists staying in their own dwellings from the INE's FRONTUR/EGATUR surveys, c) prices estimated on the basis of the detailed information used for the INE's housing price index, d) population indicators from the INE's Statistics on Migration and Changes of Residence and e) information compiled by the AEAT on the basis of the cross-checking of the tax returns of non-residents against information available in the Cadastre 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Seasonal adjustment: None. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs a) Monitoring of errors and omissions. b) Consistency with: Foreign Trade statistics, Rest of the World Account, Balance Sheet Items statistics (ECB statistics based on aggregated or consolidated balance sheet of the monetary and financial institutions sector) and Securities Holdings statistics (ECB Statistics collected on a security-by-security basis). c) Several bilateral comparison exercises: active participation in FDI European Network, Asymmetry Resolution Meeting (ARM) or Real Estate ECB bilateral exercise (for instance), and, for some items, specific direct contact with main counterpart. d) European System of Central Banks (ESCB) and European Statistical System (ESS) Quality reports. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses Revisions are stored and assessed on an ongoing basis. Data are considered definitive in the fourth year after the reference year, according to the revisions calendar specified in 4.3.1. Monitoring of revisions is considered to be a normal part of the compilation process. Studies of the causes, magnitude and direction of revisions over a given period of time are part of the set of quality indicators that the compilers go through in every dissemination cycle. This kind of quality indicators is also included in the ECB's biannual quality report and Eurostat's annual quality report. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses A detailed analysis of differences between revised and preliminary data is only performed for internal use as part of every compilation/pre-dissemination cycle. Studies and analyses of revisions with explanation of the reasons underlying changes and of the impact on the data are disseminated only when major methodological changes occur, or when there is a break due to a new collection system. This is done in the monthly press-release of Balance of Payments or in the quarterly press-release of Balance of Payments and International Investment Position and/or the news section of the Statistical Bulletin, the methodological note or by a specific statistical note posted at the website under the External Sector section of 'Statistics'. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request The Banco de España data laboratory (BELab) has been created with the aim of providing the research community with greater access to high-quality microdata (anonymized), either onsite or remotely (depending on the sensitivity level of the data), in a controlled environment that ensures data confidentiality. 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points Assistance on statistics compiled and/or disseminated by the Banco de España can be requested by users by means of the telephone (34 91 338 5651) or by a contact form ( https://app.bde.es/gnt_seg/en/contacto?a=NwbElZKVDdF0cMf6ebybP9BztpFPLW91 ).
