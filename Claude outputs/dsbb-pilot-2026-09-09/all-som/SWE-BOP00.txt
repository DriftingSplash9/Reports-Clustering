 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The presentation is in accordance with the sixth edition of the IMF's Balance of Payments and International Investment Position Manual . The data are organized in ten broad categories presented under three main headings, namely: Current Account: Imports and exports of goods Imports and exports of services Primary income Secondary income Capital Account: Acquisition/disposal of non-produced non-financial assets Capital transfers Financial Account: Direct investment Portfolio investment Financial derivatives Other investment Reserve assets Definition: The Balance of Payments is a statistical statement summarizing the economic transactions of Swedish residents with the rest of the world during a given period of time. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Classification system: Transactions are classified in accordance with the sixth edition of the IMF's Balance of Payments and International Investment Position Manual (BPM6). Scope of the data Geographic coverage: Data cover the territory of Sweden. Geographic detail: Information is available by country for all BOP items. Unrecorded activity: Only partial coverage for financial services. 2.4.1 Valuation Conversion to unit of account: Data reported in foreign currencies are converted in Swedish kronor at period-average exchange rates. Valuation: Transactions are valued at market prices. 2.4.2 Recording basis Recording of income: Investment income is recorded on an accrual basis. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures Conversion to unit of account: Data reported in foreign currencies are converted in Swedish kronor at period-average exchange rates. Valuation: Transactions are valued at market prices. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Data source: The Swedish data collection system is basically a direct reporting system supported by settlement data. Other sources are listed below. Goods: Customs statistics and Intrastat supplemented by data from ship registers, surveys on bunkers and other goods procured in ports. Sea transport: Quarterly sample survey. Services: data on trade in services is collected from a survey. The survey is divided into two parts, the first of which covers general foreign trade in services, and it is aimed at the industry and size of the companies. The second part collects information about travel currency, and it is aimed at banks. Investment income: From an annual sample survey and estimations. Portfolio investment: Debt securities: Data from reference period 2019 and onwards is based on national databases Securities holding statistics and Securities issues. Foreign holdings in Swedish debt securities are estimated by subtracting Swedish investors' holdings from total Swedish issues of debt securities. Direct investment: Stock data and reinvested earnings are based on an annual sample survey. Trade credits: Quarterly sample survey Reserve assets: Data are based on Sveriges Riksbank's balance sheet data data but för interest-bearing securities, data from reference period 2019 and onwards is based on national databases Securities holding statistics and Securities issues. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Interest variables are the characteristics that exist in a transaction in the balance of payments and in position values in the international investment position. Goal variables correspond to interest variables and can differ depending on which account item the transaction / position value refers to, but can for example be: Sector, Currency, Term, Country, Export, Import, Assets. Liabilities Observation variables usually correspond to target variables, but an exception is the land distribution in direct investments that are modelled. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness For the balance of payments, the reference period is month, quarter, half year and year. For the international investment position that relates to investment values, the reference period is the end of the quarter or the year. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Goods: Adjustments are made to convertCIF imports to a FOB basis. Services: Calculations are made for service charges embedded in insurance premiums, and financial services related to FISIM and secondary market trade in equity shares and debt securities. Income: Adjustments are made for income on securities in order to arrive at an accruals basis. Direct investment: Verification and other practices: Acquisitions and establishments of FDI enterprises are checked with other registers. Portfolio investment: Adjustments: Income on securities is deducted and recorded in Income. Verification and other practices:Reported transactions estimates assets are reconciled with an semi-annual benchmark portfolio investment position survey. Reported transactions estimates liabilities are reconsied with monthly survey on positions. Repurchase agreements (repos) are treated as loans. Other investment: Verification and other practices: As from BPM6 distinction is made between loans and deposits 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results When the majority of the incoming data for the current reference period has been received and all significant calculations have been made, a review of macro data for the entire balance of payments begins. This review includes a fair consideration of any major values in different units and sub-items, that these are correct and that they also have a counterpart in the balance of payments. There are also controls so that the items automatically should have a counterpart in the balance of payments also have it, and if the values are in accordance with established rules and assumptions. The review of macro data also includes making a general so-called reconciliation between transactions in the financial balance sheet and changes in the stocks' values regarding assets and liabilities in the international investment position. In addition to transactions, stock values are affected by e.g. of interest rate changes and exchange rate changes as well as of the development of the various stock markets. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses Statistics Sweden recently conducted an analysis of the revisions in BOP and IIP statistics. This work was also aiming at developing tools for a more systematic follow up on future revisions. This includes identifying BOP and IIP items that are systematically revised according to the measures Mean Absolute Revision (MAR) and Mean Revision (MR). This more systematic approach will potentially help identifying statistical production processes that could be improved. 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points Information about contact points is published in the form of e-mail and telephone numbers and is found on Statistics Sweden website 5.3.2 Availability of documents and services catalogs Contact information and documents regarding our statistics are available on Statistics Sweden website.
