 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Concept and definition: The data are compiled in accordance with the IMF's Balance of Payments and International Investment Position Manual - Sixth Edition (BPM6) (imf.org) and the ECB recommendation on Eurosystem reserve assets (see methodological note https://www.ecb.int ). The reserve assets must: (i) be under the effective control of the Oesterreichische Nationalbank; and (ii) refer to highly liquid, marketable and creditworthy non-euro area currency-denominated claims on non-residents of the euro area as well as gold, special drawing rights and the reserve positions in the IMF of Austria. Deposits with banks located in Austria and/or in other euro area Member States are therefore not considered as part of the official reserves. Reserve assets are compiled on a gross basis without any netting of reserve-related liabilities (with the exception of those reserve assets included in the sub-item of financial derivatives, which are recorded on a net basis). All reversible gold transactions are treated as collateralized loans and recorded accordingly in the national reserves statistics. For the 'Data Template on International Reserves and Foreign Currency Liquidity' also the liquid assets in foreign currency of the central government sector are taken into account as other foreign currency assets. Data are compiled in accordance to the IMF's International Reserves and Foreign Currency Liquidity - Guidelines for a Data Template which provides the complete metadata for the reserves template and in accordance to ESCB methodology layed down in the document ECB Eurosystem's international reserves, July 2000 . The methodology applied conforms to the guidelines provided by the IMF for the implementation of the template on international reserves and foreign currency liquidity, with the exception of the treatment of claims arising from reverse repos vis-a-vis NCBs or private financial institutions, which are classified under currency and deposits instead of under other reserve assets. Scope of the data Institutional coverage: Oesterreichische Nationalbank. For the 'Template' Oesterreichische Nationalbank and Austrian Treasury (Oesterreichische Bundesfinanzierungsagentur). Instrument coverage: For the reserve assets, the coverage is claims on non-euro area residents denominated in foreign currencies, monetary gold, SDRs and reserve position in the IMF. For other currency assets, the coverage is claims of the central bank on euro-area residents in foreign currency and the liquid assets in foreign currency of the central government. Financial derivatives coverage: Financial derivatives can be identified separately. 2.4.1 Valuation Valuation and conversion rates: The valuation is based on market prices by using: (i) the prevailing market prices at the time when the transaction takes place for transactions; and (ii) the closing mid-market prices at the end of the appropriate period for holdings. For the data of the central government sector transactions as well as holdings are valuated at the closing mid-market prices at the end of the appropriate period. From 1999 onwards the value of both the gold holdings and the foreign currency holdings are based on market valuation. Gold is revalued at market rate at the end of each quarter. The conversion of foreign currency-denominated assets into Euro is based on: (i) the mid-point foreign exchange rate of the day when the transaction takes place or; and (ii) as regards stocks, the mid-point foreign exchange rate on the last day of the reference period. For the data of the central government sector the conversion into the Euro is based on the mid-point foreign exchange rate of the last day of the reference period. 2.4.2 Recording basis Frequency of calculation and revaluation: Reserve assets are calculated on a monthly basis. The figures are revalued on a monthly basis to meet the b.o.p./i.i.p. standards and to take into account the accruals principle. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Data sources: Accounting department of the Oesterreichische Nationalbank. For the 'Template' Oesterreichische Nationalbank as well as the Austrian Treasury (Oesterreichische Bundesfinanzierungsagentur). For the Data Template, the data are generated by two different sources: Data relevant for the monetary authorities are generated by the Oesterreichische Nationalbank; Data relevant for the federal government part are generated by the Austrian Treasury (Oesterreichische Bundesfinanzierungsagentur).
