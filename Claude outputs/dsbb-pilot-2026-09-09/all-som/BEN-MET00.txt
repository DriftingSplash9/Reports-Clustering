 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Merchandise trade statistics are compiled and published by the National Institute of Statistics and Economic Analysis (INSAE), under the supervision of the Minister responsible for Coordination of Government Action under the Development and Employment Promotion Plan (MCCAG-PDPE). These statistics are compiled and published based on the World Trade Organization's Harmonized System (HS). They cover domestic imports and exports as well as re-exports (exports of imported goods, which are marketed in the neighboring countries) and total exports. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Direct imports: These are goods shipped out of a storage, a transit area, a bonded warehouse or a tax-free zone. These goods are taken into account when they meet the following criteria: goods declared for domestic consumption (including the goods whose taxes are refunded and goods previously granted temporary admission). goods granted temporary entry goods placed in bonded plants or industrial free zones goods placed in in-bond factories or export processing zones goods imported after temporary export. Exports, which include: goods of domestic origin declared as final exports goods exported after temporary import goods exported from bonded plants or industrial free zones goods exported ex-factory. Special trade is separated from general trade, depending on the case. Special import trade: Records merchandise from abroad, namely, goods declared for domestic consumption (including the goods bearing refundable taxes and goods previously granted temporary admission), goods granted temporary admission, goods granted temporary entry, goods placed in bonded plants, goods placed in in-bond factories. Special export trade: This includes local goods declared for final export, re-exported goods released from temporary admission, temporary exports for inward processing, goods exported from bonded plants, goods exported ex-factory. General import trade: The goods recorded here are special import trade merchandise plus goods entering bonded warehouses and free zones, less goods exiting bonded warehouses and free zones for domestic use. General export trade: The goods covered here are special export trade merchandise plus goods dispatched from bonded warehouses and free zones for re-export, less domestic or nationalized goods entering bonded warehouses or free zones. Scope of the data The statistics published in the INSAE documents cover 80 percent (in value terms) of all the customs transactions in Benin. The external trade concepts used in the data currently compiled cover all merchandise trade between Benin and the rest of the world. They cover domestic imports and exports as well as re-exports (exports of imported goods, which are marketed in the neighboring countries) and total exports. 2.4.1 Valuation Imports are valued c.i.f., while exports are valued f.o.b. A f.o.b. valuation of imports is used in compiling the balance of payments. The unit of measure for volume is the ton, unless otherwise indicated, whereas values are measured in CFA francs. However, foreign trade value data can be provided in other currencies using EUROTRACE software. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The statistics published in the INSAE documents are largely drawn from the computerized customs offices and comprise official trade data. The data are collected by the Directorate General of Customs and Indirect Taxes (DGDDI) of the Ministry of Finance and Economy (MFE). The INSAE collects the customs declarations forms that are not processed by the DGDDI and enters the data manually (for the non-computerized customs offices). After processing and checking these data, INSAE publishes Benin's foreign trade statements. A Statistics Validation Committee for biannual and annual data meets prior to each publication, i.e., twice a year. Thus, the statistics published in the INSAE documents are largely drawn from the computerized customs offices and comprise official trade data. In compiling foreign trade statistics, INSAE uses EUROTRACE software, which enables it to process the gross data taken from customs and to publish these statistics on a monthly, quarterly, biannual, or annual basis with product-country or country-product breakdowns, using the "Consultation and Editing" module. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data A Statistics Validation Committee for biannual and annual data meets prior to each publication, i.e., twice a year.
