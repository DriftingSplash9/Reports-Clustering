 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Analytical framework: The data are based on the IMF's External Debt Statistics: Guide for Compilers and Users (2014) . The external debt is compiled by the Swiss National Bank and complies as far as possible with the guidelines published by the IMF. The data are broken down by sectors (general government, monetary authorities, banking sector and other sectors) and maturity (short-term/long-term). Criteria used to determine whether a liability is a debt instrument: Future requirement to make payments (principal and interest). Domestic and foreign currency debt owed to non-resident: External debt data measure both domestic and foreign currency debt owed to non-residents. Debt positions between residents and non-residents: External debt data are compiled on the basis of covering debt positions between residents and non-residents. Maturity classification: The data are disseminated by maturity on the basis of remaining maturity. The short-term debt data refer to those with maturities of 12 or fewer months. Definition: The gross external debt position reflects Switzerland's outstanding overall financial liabilities towards countries abroad. 2.2.1 Scope Analytical framework: The data are based on the IMF's External Debt Statistics: Guide for Compilers and Users (2014) . The external debt is compiled by the Swiss National Bank and complies as far as possible with the guidelines published by the IMF. The data are broken down by sectors (general government, monetary authorities, banking sector and other sectors) and maturity (short-term/long-term). Criteria used to determine whether a liability is a debt instrument: Future requirement to make payments (principal and interest). Domestic and foreign currency debt owed to non-resident: External debt data measure both domestic and foreign currency debt owed to non-residents. Debt positions between residents and non-residents: External debt data are compiled on the basis of covering debt positions between residents and non-residents. Maturity classification: The data are disseminated by maturity on the basis of remaining maturity. The short-term debt data refer to those with maturities of 12 or fewer months. Definition: The gross external debt position reflects Switzerland's outstanding overall financial liabilities towards countries abroad. Scope of the data Coverage: In comparison to the liabilities of the international investment position (IIP), the external debt data do not include any financial liabilities arising from participation rights, i.e. equity capital or reinvested earnings. In accordance with the debtor-creditor principle, borrowing from counterparties abroad is classified as liability. The IIP follows the asset/liability principle in line with BPM6. Arrears are included in the gross external debt position, but cannot be separately identified. The private sector debt is included in the gross external debt position under other sectors. Deposits of non-residents with domestic banks are included. Trade credits owed to non-residents are included.Intercompany lending between entities in a direct investment relationship is included in the gross external debt position. Intercompany lending between entities in a direct investment relationship is included in the gross external debt position. Positions in financial derivatives, equity securities and equity capital are excluded from the gross external debt position. Securities issued in the domestic markets by residents and purchased by non-residents are included in the external debt position. Geographic coverage: In accordance with bilateral agreements, the Principality of Liechtenstein belongs to the Swiss customs and monetary territory. In the external debt statistics, it is considered as a domestic entity. 2.4.1 Valuation Conversions to unit of account: Stock data reported in foreign currencies are converted to local currency at the end-of-period exchange rates. Valuation: Stocks are valued at market price. Valuation of tradable instruments: All tradable instruments are valued at market price. 2.4.2 Recording basis Outstanding liabilities arising from periodic interest costs that have accrued and are not yet payable are included in the gross external debt position. They are recorded as part of the value of the underlying debt instrument. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Intercompany lending: Loans between affiliated companies are collected by the Swiss National Bank through annual and quarterly surveys. Debt securities: Portfolio investment covers bonds and money market instruments. Loans, currency and deposits: Reports of the government and domestic banks on their foreign lending, balance sheet data reported by resident banks on their liabilities to non-residents, data reported by enterprises for other sectors. Central Bank: Data is provided by the accounting unit of the Swiss National Bank. Deposit taking corporations, except the central bank: Banking surveys. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording There is no data available that would allow disaggregation into public sector external debt and publicly guaranteed private sector debt. There is no data available that would allow disaggregation of other sectors into three subsectors (non-bank financial corporations, non-financial corporations and non-profit institutions serving households). There is not data available that would allow identification or estimation of external debt transactions. Information about foreign currency-linked external debt is not available. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Intercompany lending: The quarterly data are partly estimated by adding quarterly financial flows to the stock at the end of the quarter and adjusting for exchange rate changes of positions in foreign currency. The debt securities of the other sectors also include the debt securities of banks. Traded debt instruments issued by residents abroad, but in the hands of residents are excluded from the gross external debt position. They are identified by inquiry. Banking sector: For balance sheet data reported by resident banks, the concept of remaining maturity is used. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses The data are preliminary when first released. This status is clearly indicated in the publications. The data are revised in the following quarter and at the end of the year together with the direct investment data and the data on the international investment position (IIP). Data revisions are clearly identified in the publications.
