 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The concepts and definitions comply with the sixth edition of the International Monetary Fund's Balance of Payments and International Investment Position Manual (BPM6). 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The classification and sectorization comply with the sixth edition of the International Monetary Fund's Balance of Payments and International Investment Position Manual (BPM6). 2.2.1 Scope The classification and sectorization comply with the sixth edition of the International Monetary Fund's Balance of Payments and International Investment Position Manual (BPM6). Scope of the data In general all economic transactions of residents of Latvia with the rest of the world are recorded. Exceptions to coverage No geographical units are excluded. No transactions are systematically excluded. 2.4.1 Valuation Market prices are the basis for the valuation. Nominal value as defined by the European System of Accounts 2010 (ESA 2010) is used for currency and deposits, loans and other accounts receivable/payable. Own funds at book value approach is used for the foreign direct investment in unlisted companies. 2.4.2 Recording basis Recording is based on an accrual basis. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Data for general government sector are obtained from the Treasury and administrative data sources. Information on central bank sector is derived from the balance sheet and profit and loss statement of Latvijas Banka. Monetary Financial Institutions (MFIs; excl. central bank) sector is covered by MFIs statistics compiled by Latvijas Banka. Data collection system is based on the surveys for financial corporations (excl. MFI) and non-financial corporations and households sector. In addition, non-bank external payment data (ITRS) and data on supervision statistics compiled by Latvijas Banka as well as data received from the Central Statistical Bureau of Latvia (CSB), the Register of Enterprises of the Republic of Latvia, the Real Estate register, Road Traffic Safety Directorate and the European Central Bank (ECB) are used. Quarterly surveys conducted by Latvijas Banka: For non-financial corporations: Survey on foreign assets and liabilities covering data on positions at the beginning and end of a period and changes in the relevant positions (transactions, price changes, exchange rate changes, other adjustments) for the compilation of foreign direct investment in Latvia and abroad, assets and liabilities between fellow enterprises, other investment (assets and liabilities), income flows related to foreign assets and liabilities; all items are shown in the breakdown by country; stratified simple random sampling is applied. For financial corporations: Survey on assets and liabilities (balance sheet items in breakdown by country, counterpart sector, currency and maturity, non-resident investor category: direct investor, fellow enterprises, direct investment company and other investor); cut-off sampling is applied. Survey on transportation and intermediary services covering data on transportation services rendered/received by mode of transport. Survey on services covering data on services rendered/received for compiling items of other services. Survey on card-based payment transactions covering data on payments abroad with cards issued in Latvia and payments in Latvia with cards issued abroad (merchant category code, payment initiation channel, partner country). Data on portfolio investment covering assets and liabilities for all sectors are derived from the data on holders of securities (data reported on a security-by-security basis by securities custodians) compiled by Latvijas Banka and the Centralised Securities Database of the ECB. Foreign trade statistics data compiled by the CSB are used. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording The definitions, scope, sectorization, classifications, valuation and time of recording comply with the sixth edition of the International Monetary Fund's Balance of Payments and International Investment Position Manual (BPM6). 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Data sources are available in a timely manner for the compilation of statistics at 65 days after the end of the reference period. 3.2.1 Source data assessment Data controls are carried out once data have been received. All reports are checked automatically to verify their completeness, consistency and plausibility. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Goods Data under general merchandise are derived from foreign trade statistics provided by the Central Statistical Bureau of Latvia (CSB). They include Intrastat data on trading of goods with the European Union (EU) Member States and customs cargo declaration data on trading of goods with countries outside the EU (Extrastat data). In compiling foreign trade statistics in Latvia, a special trade system scheme is used (where goods are imported to customs warehouses from abroad and exported from customs warehouses abroad, such imports and exports are excluded from the total). Balance of Payments (BOP) and foreign trade statistics generally show discrepancies, due to different methodologies. In particular, certain monthly transactions not covered by foreign trade statistics are added to BOP figures, namely: Merchanting – information on Net exports of goods under merchanting is derived from the non-bank external payment data and reported under goods. Goods procured in ports (export: fuel sold in the national economy to non-resident carriers; import: fuel procured by resident transport operators to non-resident transport providers) – information from survey on transportation and intermediary services compiled by Latvijas Banka about the goods procured by non-resident carriers for own consumption in Latvia (exports) and resident carriers for own consumption abroad (imports) is included. Adjustment from CIF to FOB – adjustment from CIF to FOB values of goods imports is made, i.e. freight and insurance costs are deducted from the value of goods (Intrastat data adjustments are made separately for each country of consignment, while those of Extrastat data made in the breakdown by resident and non-resident carrier for each mode of transport and each country of goods' origin separately). Exports from shipping vessels – data compiled by the CSB on exports from Latvian shipping vessels fishing in the extraterritorial waters are included. Banknote production and coinage costs – information on banknote production and coinage costs from profit and loss statement of Latvijas Banka is included. Manufacturing – information on goods sent outside Latvia for manufacturing if the goods are subsequently returned to Latvia, as well as information on goods sent to Latvia for manufacturing if they are subsequently returned to the same country which sent them to Latvia for manufacturing is excluded. The net value of transactions with goods excluded from general merchandise is reported under services. Imports of used cars by individuals – estimate based on data from the Road Traffic Safety Directorate on registration of used cars and data from the CSB on average prices. Data on illegal import of goods, calculated by the CSB, is included. Information on exports or imports of goods without change of ownership is excluded. Payment card data has been used to assess e-commerce of goods. Services Data under manufacturing services are derived from foreign trade statistics provided by the CSB. Information on maintenance and repair services is derived from Latvijas Banka's survey on transportation and intermediary services and non-bank external payment data. Data on transportation services are derived from Latvijas Banka's survey on transportation and intermediary services and survey on services, as well as non-bank external payment data. The CSB's foreign trade statistics are also used to obtain the difference between CIF and FOB values of imported goods. The share of transportation services rendered by non-residents is set apart and recorded under Transport in the BOP consistently with the respective mode of transportation. Data on passenger transportation by air are obtained by using information on the number of passenger departures in the breakdown by airline provided by the state joint stock company Riga International Airport, the CSB monthly data on the number of residents and non-residents crossing the border of the Republic of Latvia by air, and information on the average ticket prices of each airline. Data on travel (till 2019) are obtained from the CSB statistics on persons entering and leaving the Republic of Latvia. State joint stock company Riga International Airport conducted random surveys of travellers on a permanent basis; travellers were polled at other border checkpoints four times a year, thus obtaining information about non-residents' spending in Latvia and residents' spending abroad. The average spending of a traveller was calculated by mathematical methods, thereafter obtaining total travellers' spending. The number of travellers crossing the state border at border checkpoints with Russia and Belarus, railway, sea and airport checkpoints was provided by the State Boarder Guard of the Republic of Latvia. Data on travel (starting with 2020) are obtained from payment card data. For evaluating foreign student expenditure in Latvia, data compiled by the CSB on the number of foreign students and income gained by institutions of higher education from their tuition fees, broken down by country, are used. For evaluating expenditure of Latvian residents studying abroad, UNESCO data on the number of Latvian residents studying abroad and estimates of average expenditure per student, broken down by country, are used. Information on explicitly charged financial services is obtained from the survey on services, non-bank external payments and Monetary Financial Institution (MFI) statistics compiled by Latvijas Banka, and profit and loss statement of Latvijas Banka. The indirectly measured financial services are calculated on the basis of the surveys of financial and non-financial corporations on assets and liabilities and MFI statistics compiled by Latvijas Banka, as well as Treasury data on general government sector. Information on other services is mainly obtained from the survey on services, on non-bank external payments and MFI statistics compiled by Latvijas Banka, profit and loss statement of Latvijas Banka, data on revenue and expenditure of the Republic of Latvia embassies, representative offices and consulates provided by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Republic of Latvia, data on disbursed direct insurance and reinsurance premiums and claims compiled by Latvijas Banka in the frame of supervision statistics, and information provided by the Ministry of Finance of the Republic of Latvia on contributions to the EU budget are also used. Data on expenditure of foreign embassies, representative offices and consulates in Latvia are calculated using information of the State Revenue Service on VAT repayments to foreign embassies, representative offices and consulates. Payment card data has been used to assess e-commerce of services. Primary income Information for compensation of employees credit entries is obtaines from survey on contracts concluded by sailors for working abroad compiled by Latvijas Banka. Information on wages and salaries of residents employed at foreign embassies, representative offices and consulates in Latvia is obtained from data compiled by the State Revenue Service on social security contributions made by foreign embassies, representative offices and consulates. Latvijas Banka assesses the amount of compensation of Latvia's residents abroad using also information provided by foreign embassies on labour permits issued to the residents of Latvia for working abroad. Debit entries are based on labour statistics compiled by the CSB and revenue and expenditure data of the Republic of Latvia embassies, representative offices and consulates of the Republic of Latvia compiled by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Republic of Latvia. Data on direct investment income are obtained from surveys of financial and non-financial corporations on assets and liabilities, data on holders of securities, and MFI statistics compiled by Latvijas Banka. Dividends are recorded at point in time when share price starts to be quoted on an ex-dividend basis. For companies excluding listed companies, due for payment day and ex-dividend day are the same. Reinvested earnings are recorded in the period in which they are earned. Income on debt instruments (interest) is recorded on an accrual basis. Data on portfolio investment income are obtained from data on holders of securities, surveys of financial and non-financial corporations on assets and liabilities compiled by Latvijas Banka, and profit and loss statement of Latvijas Banka. Income on portfolio investment is recorded on an accrual basis, using the debtor principle. Data on other investment income are obtained from surveys of financial and non-financial corporations on assets and liabilities, non-bank external payment data and MFI statistics compiled by Latvijas Banka, profit and loss statement of Latvijas Banka, the Treasury data on general government sector, data on assets and liabilities of insurance companies; private pension funds, pension plans managed by private pension funds, state-funded pension scheme funding, investment funds, brokerage firms, investment management companies, authorized payments institutions, authorized electronic money institutions as well as data on direct insurance and reinsurance technical reserves compiled by Latvijas Banka in the frame of supervision statistics. Income on other investment is recorded on an accrual basis. For other primary income data on taxes on production and imports are obtained from information on traditional own resources (customs duties) provided by the Ministry of Finance of the Republic of Latvia. Data on subsidies are obtained from monthly information provided by the Treasury. Starting with the data for 2014, EU funds are reported in accordance with the expenditure principle, i.e. in the period when the respective spending from the Latvian central government budget is made. EU funds are reported in the BOP in Primary income, Secondary income or Capital account, depending on type of Latvian central government budget expenditure and EU fund. Depending on the type of Latvian central government budget expenditure, EU funds are additionally categorised on the basis of final beneficiary sector as General government or Financial and non-financial corporations, households. At the same time, the difference between EU funds related central government expenditure and revenue within the respective period are reported in the financial account of the BOP as an increase or a decrease in government claims on the European Commission. Secondary income Data on secondary income are obtained from monthly information provided by the Treasury, information provided by the Ministry of Finance of the Republic of Latvia on payments to the EU budget (VAT own resource, the rebate of the UK, as well as own resources of the national gross income). Starting with the data for 2014, EU funds are reported in accordance with the expenditure principle, i.e. in the period when the respective spending from the Latvian central government budget is made. EU funds are reported in the BOP in Primary income, Secondary income or Capital account, depending on type of Latvian central government budget expenditure and EU fund. Depending on the type of Latvian central government budget expenditure, EU funds are additionally categorised on the basis of final beneficiary sector as General government or Financial and non-financial corporations, households. At the same time, the difference between EU funds related central government expenditure and revenue within the respective period are reported in the financial account of the BOP as an increase or a decrease in government claims on the European Commission. Data on secondary income of financial institutions, non-financial corporations and households are derived from non-bank external payment data compiled by Latvijas Banka (including data on all types of payments made by private persons using services of credit institutions). Data on direct insurance and reinsurance premiums and claims paid compiled by Latvijas Banka in the frame of supervision statistics as well as information compiled by the State Social Insurance Agency on pensions paid to residents by foreign governments and pensions paid to non-residents and other sources are also used. Capital Account Capital account data are obtained from monthly information provided by the Treasury and non-bank external payment data compiled by Latvijas Banka. Starting with the data for 2014, EU funds are reported in accordance with the expenditure principle, i.e. in the period when the respective spending from the Latvian central government budget is made. EU funds are reported in the BOP in Primary income, Secondary income or Capital account, depending on type of Latvian central government budget expenditure and EU fund. Depending on the type of Latvian central government budget expenditure, EU funds are additionally categorised on the basis of final beneficiary sector as General government or Financial and non-financial corporations, households. At the same time, the difference between EU funds related central government expenditure and revenue within the respective period are reported in the financial account of the BOP as an increase or a decrease in government claims on the European Commission. Direct investment Information on direct investment is derived from surveys of financial and non-financial corporations on assets and liabilities, data on holders of securities and MFI statistics compiled by Latvijas Banka and data on assets and liabilities of insurance companies, private pension funds, pension plans managed by private pension funds, state-funded pension scheme funding, investment funds, brokerage firms, investment management companies, authorized payments institutions, authorized electronic money institutions compiled by Latvijas Banka in the frame of supervision statistics. Non-bank external payment data compiled by Latvijas Banka and Register of Enterprises of the Republic of Latvia data are also used. To estimate the data on investment in real estate in Latvia the State Unified Computerised Land Register data are used. Data on investment in real estate abroad are derived as mirror data from partner countries. To the extent possible, foreign direct investment in equity capital in Latvia is recorded at market value. The value of investment in unlisted and listed companies is valued differently. The value of investment in unlisted companies is obtained by using the equity capital approach (own funds at book value) recommended by the ECB. The value of investment in listed companies is obtained in a similar way, with an exception that the price of one share at the end and beginning of the reporting period is obtained using NASDAQ CSD Riga data. Assets and liabilities of fellow companies are classified depending on the ultimate controlling parent. If the ultimate controlling parent of a Latvian direct investment company is a non-resident, all mutual assets and liabilities are reported as direct investment in Latvia. In case the ultimate controlling parent of a Latvian direct investment company is a Latvian resident, all mutual assets and liabilities are reported as direct investment abroad. Debt transactions and positions between MFIs, investment funds and other financial intermediaries except insurance corporations and pension funds within an existing direct investment relationship are excluded. Holdings (above 10%) of investment funds shares are recorded as portfolio investment. Portfolio investment Data on portfolio investment are derived from data on holders of securities and MFI statistics compiled by Latvijas Banka and the balance sheet of Latvijas Banka. Portfolio investment transactions are marked to market, using the information on financial instrument prices from the Centralised Securities Database of the ECB. Financial derivatives Data on financial derivatives are derived from MFI statistics and non-bank external payment data compiled by Latvijas Banka, the balance sheet and profit and loss statement of Latvijas Banka and the Treasury data on general government sector. Other investment Data on other investment are derived from surveys of financial and non-financial corporations on assets and liabilities and MFI statistics compiled by Latvijas Banka, data on assets and liabilities of insurance companies, private pension funds, pension plans managed by private pension funds, state-funded pension scheme funding, investment funds, brokerage firms, investment management companies, authorized payments institutions, authorized electronic money institutions as well as data ondirect insurance and reinsurance technical reserves compiled by Latvijas Banka in the frame of supervision statistics, Latvijas Banka's balance sheet and the Treasury data on general government sector. Reserve assets In the BOP, Reserve assets are made up of monetary gold, Special Drawing Rights, reserve position in the International Monetary Fund, as well as of other reserve assets (currency, deposits and securities and other claims). Starting with January 2014, the following is classified as reserve assets: claims on non-euro area residents denominated in currencies other than the euro, gold, reserve position in the International Monetary Fund and Special Drawing Rights. Financial assets that are at the disposal of the central bank and cannot be classified as Reserve assets are recorded under Portfolio investment or Other investment. Data on Reserve assets are derived from the balance sheet of Latvijas Banka. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Tramoseats (JDEMETRA+) is used for seasonal adjustments of the current account items required by international organisations. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results Data checking at different aggregation levels is carried out. Significant fluctuations are analysed to identify the underlying reasons. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs Data checks are applied to statistical output to analyse the data time series. All outliers are analysed individually to understand the reason. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses Data revisions are analysed to understand the underlying reasons. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses Revisions are analysed to understand the underlying reasons but revision analyses are not disseminated. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request Unpublished non-confidential data can be disseminated on request. 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points The division in charge of the compilation of the data can be contacted by e-mail: statistika@bank.lv .
