 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The System of National Accounts (SNA) is compiled in line with the European system of national and regional accounts (ESA 2010). SNA is a set of indicators for consistent and interrelated description of the most important processes and phenomena of the economy: production, income, consumption, formation of capital and finance. The Gross Domestic Product (GDP) is an integrated indicator of the country's economic development which characterizes the result of residents' production activity within the country's economic territory and is measured by aggregate value of goods and services produced by them for final use. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Classification systems: The GDP components are compiled using the new version of the Classification of Types of Economic Activity (CTEA-2010) (harmonized with NACE, rev.2),nomenclature for industrial products which is harmonized with the Statistical classification of products by type of EU economic activity (CPA, ver.2.1) and based on the List of industrial products for the EU (PRODCOM, 2021) and includes types of energy products. When developing the national version of the Classification of Individual Consumption by Purpose, the model European Classification of Individual Consumption Expenditure of the European Union (COICOP-HBS) was taken as a basis. The compilations of the final consumption expenditure of the general government are defined by the Classification of General Government's Functions. The compilations of final consumption expenditure of non-profit organizations servicing households follow international classification of purposes of non-profit organizations servicing households. To compile exports/imports of goods and services, information of foreign trade is used, namely Ukrainian Classification of Goods for Foreign Economic Activity and Classification of Foreign Economic Services. The Classification of Institutional Sector of Economy of Ukraine is based on methodological principles of SNA. Scope of the data Production boundaries at large meet the SNA and are defined as activity of resident units of the national economy (including the activities of foreign and joint organizations that have their centers of economic interests in Ukraine and work in it on continuing basis) to produce goods and services. Production activity covers enterprises and organizations that produce goods and render services. Production boundaries include also production activity of households. It covers production of goods and services both to be sold on market (market output) and to be used in households (output for own final use). Geographical scope of survey covers all regions of Ukraine excluding thetemporarily occupied territories of the Autonomous Republic of Crimea, the city of Sevastopol (since 2010) and part of the temporarily occupied territories in the Donetsk and Luhansk regions (since 2014); excluding the territories which are temporarily occupied by the russian federation and part of territories where combat actions are/were carried out (since 2022).. The non-financial assets boundaries include: residential dwellings; other dwellings and structures; machine and equipment; weapon systems; cultivated bio-resources; expenditure connected with the transfer of ownership rights for non-produced assets; intellectual property products: R&D; exploration and evaluation of mineral resources; computer software and databases; entertainment programs and original copies of literary and artistic works. They do not include historical monuments and values apart from the values that belong to households and banks. Exceptions to coverage The national accounts do not include the territorial enclaves of other countries (embassies, consulates, military bases, etc) and international organizations located on Ukraine's territory. Unrecorded activity The definitions and concepts of the non directly observed economy are consistent with recommendations of the manual Measurement of the Non-Observed Economy prepared by the OECD, IMF, ILO and the Statistical Committee of the CIS in 2002 and the methodological regulation Eurostat Table Approach to Exhaustiveness. 2.4.1 Valuation Find below the basic value estimates that meet SNA: output of goods and services is valued at base prices by types of economic activity, institutional sectors of economy and market prices for economy; output of goods and services for own final consumption is valued at equivalent market prices; intermediate consumption is valued at actual buyers' prices; imputed payment for services is compiled as the difference between the actual rates and base interest rate. It is distributed by institutional sectors of economy and types of economic activity; estimate of elements of gross fixed capital formation is made on the basis of acquisition prices (market prices) if they have been bought or base prices if they have been produced by own efforts; value of inventories stock is defined taking into account the re-estimation of price of the reference period into average prices; value of services for consumption in own housing is compiled using the method of users' expenditure which is recommended by the Organization on Economic Cooperation and Development; expenditures of households are valued at buyers' prices; imports/exports of goods and services are valued at FOB/CIF prices. The external trade transactions in foreign currencies are re-estimated into the national currency according to the official exchange rate of the National Bank of Ukraine at the moment they have been made. The calculations are given at current prices, prices of the previous and base year, hryvnia million. 2.4.2 Recording basis Time for transaction recording. To record transactions, Ukraine makes use of accrual method, namely: capital formation is recorded during the period of production; income and expenditure of the state-run establishments are reflected in the national accounts by accrual method, i.e. taking into account the adjustment of budget data (constructed on the cash basis) and arrears in payments to budget and from budget; external trade transactions are recorded at the moment when goods entered or left the economic territory which is confirmed by the relevant customs declarations; exports/imports are reflected exclusively on the basis of change on ownership (flows of goods between the owner-country and the country that render services for re-processing should not be included into indicators of exports/imports of goods, i.e. exports/imports composition should reflect services for re-processing of goods). 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures The procedures for compilation on gross/net basis meet SNA. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs To compile the SNA, the statistical reporting of enterprises and organizations (structural survey of enterprises and sectoral statistics), financial reporting as well as sample surveys of the population are employed. As a rule, the state statistical observations are undertaken as complete enumerations for large and medium enterprises, and sample survey is carried out for small enterprises. Balance of payments data are compiled by the National Bank of Ukraine (NBU). Also, the administrative reporting from other statistical data producers is used: NBU and other regulators of financial services regarding activity of banks, the NBU, insurance organizations, non-state-run pension funds, etc; Ministry of Finance: reports on execution of the state and local budgets and financial reporting of budgeting establishments; income and expenditure of Pension Fund; State Tax Service regarding activity of natural persons, entrepreneurs. Also, experts' estimates and assumptions are used. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Indicators of input data are made consistent with the definitions, sphere of coverage and classifications of the SNA. When revising the forms from the state statistical observations, the basic requirements to the compliance of indicators with the SNA principles are taken into account. The annual national accounts are compiled on the basis of data for the reference year while quarterly national accounts are based on discrete data for the reference quarter apart from reports on execution of the state and local budgets which are submitted on the cumulative basis. The details of quarterly and annual data obtained from statistical surveys permit if necessary to make adjustment in order to reach compliance with the SNA principles. Data from alternative sources are analyzed and compared between themselves where they are comparable. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness The GDP quarterly compilations by production method, income method and by category of final use at actual (current) prices and constant prices (average prices of the 2021 benchmark year) are made on the 70th day after the end of the reference quarter and are provided in the SDDS format in line with its requirements. 3.2.1 Source data assessment Input data from all sources of information are analyzed and validated as to their consistency with the methodology and the SNA classifications. The discrepancies are analyzed and the causes of occurrence are detected, if necessary the corrections are made in the reporting. To compile selected components of the quarterly GDP and due to the limited information base, the annual ratios, experts' review of selected indicators, as well as econometric methods and models are used. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques The GDP by production method and by category of final use is compiled at actual and constant prices, the GDP by income category is compiled at actual prices only. To define the GDP at constant prices (the 2021 average prices), at first the GDP components of the reference quarter is compiled into average prices of previous year, after that the chain annual deflator- indices are used, every element is revalued into average prices of benchmark year. The revaluation of data into constant prices (the 2021 average prices) is made following international standards on the basis of chain indices method. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Balancing: The GDP estimates by three methods at actual prices do not have statistical discrepancies. While the GDP revaluation by production method and final use method into constant prices does not make provision for additivity of its components. Seasonal adjustment including adjustment for the effect of calendar days, time series of the GDP quarterly estimates and its selected components at constant prices is made using DEMETRA by applying TRAMO/SEATS method. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results Data obtained from the state statistical surveys are reconciled with data presented in other sources including data from financial reporting of enterprises, administrative data. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data The State Statistics Service of Ukraine (SSSU) analyzes discrepancies in intermediate data and makes adjustment to data from the state statistical observations or makes use of data from alternative sources. When compiling national accounts, balance of payments data are not adjusted and data from reports on execution of the state and local budgets are reflected in line with the SNA requirements as to the transactions to be recorded by accrual method. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs Every year Input-Output Tables are produced. Thereby, input data are revised, the reasons for discrepancies are explored, certain adjustment to the scheme to produce the SNA indicators is made. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses The GDP data are revised and made more precise in accordance with availability of input information. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses When revising data, the systematic analysis of deviations between the GDP quarterly estimates is made in order to improve the technique to produce selected components of the GDP and inform users about the reasons for making adjustment. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request According to users' requests, data can be provided in electronic or paper formats. 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points All statistical publications have information about the persons responsible for compilation of information. 5.3.2 Availability of documents and services catalogs The SSSU official website http://www.stat.gov.ua/ (section Publications) has the list of statistical publications with information about social and economic situation in the country by type of economic activity and region. Section Ac cess to Public Information presents the procedure for access, form of request to obtain public information and contact information to submit the request.
