 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The Manufacturing Industry Physical Volume Index (INVOFIM) measures trends in manufacturing industry output, both at the general level and by groups of economic activity. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The overall index is obtained by group of activity (4 digits), grouping (3 digits), and industrial division (2 digits) of the ISIC rev. 2. Scope of the data Geographical coverage is nationwide, excluding Pando, and covers 85 percent of the value added of manufacturing industry. The base year for the indicator is FY 1990. Groups of activity have a weight in the index calculated on the basis of the share of that group's manufacturing industry value added in the industry total for 1990. The products chosen from within each activity group represent more than 70 percent of the value of output of the product. For each product selected, the reporting enterprises chosen were those accounting for more than 70 percent of the value of the output of the product. In accordance with base year data, 35 groups of economic activity, approximately 500 enterprises, and the output volumes of 120 products are considered. The data are collected by means of a quarterly survey of selected enterprises. INVOFIM is a Laspeyres quantity index. The manufacturing industry sales index (INVOVIM) is prepared simultaneously. This indicator is derived from data on quantities sold by product or line of products drawn from the quarterly survey of a sample of industrial enterprises. The index is prepared both at the general level and by groups of economic activity. Geographic coverage is nationwide, excluding Pando, and sectoral coverage includes all industrial groups, as does the industrial production index. INVOVIM is a Laspeyres quantity index.
