 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Scope of the data The Monthly Survey of Industrial Production covers about 600 firms engaged in manufacturing, which are the producers of the 200 major products. The response rate is 30-40 per cent. The selection of establishments is based on the central register of establishments (the business register). From the register of establishments the sample to be surveyed is derived from the firms that produce 80 per cent volume of each product. Exceptions to coverage The Monthly Survey of Industrial Production does not adequately cover rapid entry and exit of firms. Unrecorded activity 2.4.1 Valuation 2.4.2 Recording basis 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The Monthly Survey of Industrial Production (MSIP) gathers data on output of major products (about 200), both quantities and values. This is used to calculate quarterly and annual volume indices of industrial production. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Quarterly Index (1979=100). About 80 output indicators are used in the compilation of thee index. This is a binary quantity index compiled at 4-digit ISIC levels and compares output of a quarter in the reference year and a corresponding quarter of the base year (1979). There are 144 indicators used in the calculation of indices. Quantum Index (1976=100). This is the annual volume index. There are 194 indicators used in the calculation of the index out of which 192 are physical output indictors and 2 are employment indicators. It is a chained Laspeyers base year weighted index compiled at the 4-digit level of ISIC. The weights used are a three year moving average derived from Values Added at the 4 digit ISIC level. Annual estimates are grossed up using earnings from the Labour Enumeration (L.E) with 1976 as the base year.
