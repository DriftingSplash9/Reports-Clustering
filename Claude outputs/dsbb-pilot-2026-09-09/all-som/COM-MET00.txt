 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions External trade statistics cover imports and exports. Import and export statistics are produced in both volume and value terms, with c.i.f. figures used for imports and f.o.b. figures used for exports. The nomenclature used is consistent with the Harmonized Commodity Description and Coding System. The compilation of external trade statistics follows international standards. The concepts and classifications are consistent with those of the Common Market for Eastern and Southern Africa (COMESA), of which the Comoros is a member. The data are drawn from the customs data base, which uses Asycuda++, and are processed using Eurotrace. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The nomenclature used is consistent with the Harmonized Commodity Description and Coding System approved by the World Customs Organization (WCO) and used by COMESA member countries. The data are classified by: - products for import and export (vanilla, ylang ylang, cloves, other essences, and other products) - country of origin for imports and country of destination for exports. Scope of the data External trade figures cover the transactions between Comorian residents and nonresidents that are declared to customs. Exceptions to coverage Contraband transactions are therefore not included. Given the absence of a land border, this factor is considered to be relatively insignificant in quantitative terms. 2.4.1 Valuation Imports and exports are valued on the basis of the customs declarations, which corresponds to prevailing prices. 2.4.2 Recording basis The time of recording of external trade figures corresponds in general to the time that the goods pass through customs. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures External trade figures are compiled on a gross basis. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs External trade figures are derived from customs declarations. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques External trade figures from customs are derived from customs declarations and are not subject to statistical treatment other than the retreatment automatically carried out by the Eurotrace software, which performs quality controls. to treat these data, in particular by carrying out quality controls and settings to the standards 5.1.5 Dissemination on request External trade statistics are currently provided to users only upon request.
