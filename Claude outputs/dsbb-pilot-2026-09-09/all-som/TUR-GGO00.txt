Scope of the data In accordance with Article 52 of Law No. 5018, Ministry of Treasury and Finance (General Directorate of Public Accounts) is authorized to determine the general government sector by taking international classification into account and getting the opinions of the Ministry of Treasury and Finance (General Directorate of Public Finance), Central Bank of the Republic of Türkiyeand the Turkish Statistical Institute. Beside this, Ministry of Treasury and Finance (General Directorate of Public Accounts) is also authorized to compile finance data of the public administrations within the scope of general government sector. This section describes the coverage of the general government sector and its sub-sectors. The general government sector in Türkiye is composed by 3 subsectors: · Central government subsector Budgetary Extra-budgetary · Local government subsector · Social security funds subsector Exceptions to coverage No exceptions. Unrecorded activity No unrecorded activity. 2.4.1 Valuation Administrative records are used in the preparation of the tables and market value is taken as basis in the domestic and foreign debt data. 2.4.2 Recording basis Accrual basis of accounting is used in each level of general government sector with some exceptions. For tax revenue and social security revenue, cash data is used. Cash adjustment and time adjustment is implemented for the tax revenue of the budgetary central government. Additionally, cash adjustment is also made to the tax revenue in local government and to the social security revenue in social security institutions. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures During preparetion process of finance statistics of general government sector, consolidation transactions actualized inter and intra the subsectors. The following reciprocal transactions between and within the subsectors are eliminated; a) Current and capital transfers, b) Interest income and expenses, c) Taxes paid as a taxpayer, ç) Purchase and sale of goods and services, d) Acquisition and disposal of non- finance assets, e) Loans, debt securities and other debts and receivables, f) Deposited capital. The following internationally accepted statistical methods are applied when needed for consolidation; a) One Side Rule b) Top Down Rule c) Borrowing Party Rule Some other methods used apart from consolidation are as follows; - Cash adjustments for tax revenue and social security revenue, - Time adjustment for tax revenue, - And cash data is used when its not possible to compile accrual data for some accounts, like administrative fines etc. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Administrative records are used in the preparation of general government operations. For budgetary central government, accounting data are monthly compiled by the Integrated Public Financial Management Information System. For social security institutions, local administrations and extrabudgetary central government, accounting data are derived via the Public Expenditure and Accounting Information System quarterly. Extra information for internal and external debt data is obtained from the General Directorate of Public Finance. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording General Government Operations · Concepts and definitions: General government finance statistics are prepared by using the concepts, definitions, and presentational framework in Government Finance Statistics Manual (GFSM 2014). · Scope: Scope of data is General Government Sector. The general government sector consists the subsectors of central government, local governments and social security institutions. · Classification/sectorization: Principles in GFSM 2014 are used for classification and sectorization. · Valuation: Administrative records are taken as basis in the preparation of the tables. Market value is used in domestic and foreign debt data. · Time of recording: The data are recorded on accrual basis for each level of general government sector. But cash data is also used for tax revenue and social security revenue. · Grossing/netting procedures: Consolidation is used in subsectors and inter subsectors. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Data is monthly compiled for budgetary central government and quarterly compiled for social security institutions, local administrations and extrabudgetary central government. 3.2.1 Source data assessment During the compilation of the data by the Public Expenditure and Accounting Information System (KBS) and the Integrated Public Financial Management Information System (BKMYBS), automatic controls are performed by the systems. In addition to this, units those uploading their data to the systems have to do checks of their data over the system. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Basically the principles stated in GFSM 2014 are taken as basis. The techniques applied are briefly as follows; In consolidation transactions, statistical methods such as one side rule, top down rule and debtor-creditor are used, Cash data and time adjustment method is used for tax revenue when it is necessary. But for social security revenue, only cash adjustment is made. For some accounts, like administrative fines etc., cash data is used when it is not appropriate to use administrative records according to international criteria. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Raw data includes accounting codes in economic 4 details. Since the data is based on administrative records, it comes with accounting codes. There is a GFS code corresponding to each accounting code. After the accounting codes are matched with the GFS codes, proccessing begins. In each subsector, transactions are controlled and analyzed in detail numerically. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results In each subsector, transactions are controlled and analyzed in detail numerically. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data Controls are carried out continuously at all stages of compiling the data and preparing the tables. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs Statistical discrepancies and other problems in each subsector are carefully examined in detail. It is very important for us that statistical discrepancies are acceptable level 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses Revisions are divided into two as routine and major. · Routine revisions are made in order to match the data, in case of any changes in the data of an unit. Routine revisions are performed quarterly and yearly. Main revisions are made for the changes in the scope of general government sector, classification, definition, method and implementation structure, source data or legislation. Major revisions are made in 5 years periods. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses In accordance with the revision policy, revision measurement table that shows changes in amounts are prepared and released. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request - 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points Contact information exists on the website.
