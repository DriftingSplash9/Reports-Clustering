 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Analytical framework and concepts: Data on revenue, expenditure, budget balance, deferred payments, other deferred payments and advances are disseminated. The use of deferred payments, other deferred payments, and advances reflect the use of reporting revenue and cash on a payment order basis rather than a cash basis. Also, there are five financing items: net foreign borrowing, net domestic security issuance borrowing (Government Bonds-net, T. Bills-net), net lending, privatization revenue, SDIF revenue surplus, currency/deposit and other transactions. Relationship with international guidelines: The IMF's Government Finance Statistics Manual 2014 (GFSM 2014) is the basis for all reporting of fiscal data aside from exceptions identified separately in 2.3.1 and 2.4.2 below. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Classification system: Revenue and expenditure classification systems are similar to standards of the GFSM, except for identified item; There is no lending minus repayment item category above the line in the central government budget. All lending and repayments transactions are included in financing. 2.2.1 Scope Classification system: Revenue and expenditure classification systems are similar to standards of the GFSM, except for identified item; There is no lending minus repayment item category above the line in the central government budget. All lending and repayments transactions are included in financing. Scope of the data The data cover budgetary central government and exclude the state economic enterprises, the extrabudgetary funds, and the social security institutions. Central Government Budget (General Budget, Special Budget Agencies, and Regulatory and Supervisory Institutions) data are disseminated under the SDDS. Exceptions to coverage State economic enterprises, extrabudgetary funds, social security institutions, municipalities, and revolving funds are not included in the Central Government Budget. 2.4.1 Valuation Valuation of transactions: Prevailing market rates. 2.4.2 Recording basis Recording basis: Data are reported broadly on a cash basis. The data are recorded on a cash basis, with expenditure recorded on a commitment basis. Revenues are recorded when cash including checks is actually received. Tax revenue data are net of refunds paid. Expenditure are recorded on the issuance of payment orders. The issuance of payment orders give rise to "Deferred Payments" arrears unless accompanied by corresponding check issuance and Deferred Payments are cleared out with check issuance. Deferred Payments represent the difference of the reporting system with the cash basis system. Budget Balance is translated into Cash Balance (Budget Balance in cash basis) taking into account Change in Deferred Payments and Advances. Fiscal year: The fiscal year is the same as the calendar year. Complementary period: January 1–February 15. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures The data are presented on a consolidated basis, that is, transactions between the various elements of central government budget have been eliminated from the data during the process of consolidation. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Data sources: The data are compiled from the accounting records of actual outturns of the central government units. The revenue and expenditure data are compiled by the Ministry of Treasury and Finance, General Directorate of Public Accounts and Fiscal Control, and the financing data are gathered from records of the Accountancy Office. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Consolidation: General Budget, Special Budget Agencies, and Regulatory and Supervisory Institutions data are consolidated. Transfers to Special Budget Agencies from the general budget are deducted. In this way, double counting is prevented in the consolidation activity. 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points E-mail addresses and telephone numbers of the CGO compilers are provided along with the data for users' needs and questions. In addition, questions and demands about financing received within the context of The Law of Acquiring Knowledge are formally answered by the Ministry of Treasury and Finance.
