 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Analytical framework : The data for central government operations are compiled from reports of the central government budgetary units which are established on a cash basis. The concepts used in compilation of data on central government operations broadly follow those of the IMF "Manual on Government Finance Statistics, 2001 ("GFSM 2001"), in the analytical framework of the Statement of Sources and Uses of Cash. The data are compiled and disseminated according to the national methodology and IMF’s GFSM 2001. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The consolidated central government operations include the following accounts and funds: • Central government: - the State Budget accounts – the “Central Budget”, budgets of ministries and other government bodies, National Assembly’s budget, judiciary authorities’ budget, National Audit Office’s budget; - autonomous budgets – Public Universities; Bulgarian Academy of Science, Bulgarian National Television and Bulgarian National Radio, Bulgarian News Agency (BTA), Enterprise for management of environmental protection activities (EMEPA), Fund “Security of the electrical system”, State-owned enterprise "Single System Operator"; - Accounts for resources from the EU (Agricultural Fund; National Fund with the Ministry of Finance; and other accounts for resources from the EU). • Social Security funds – National Social Security Institute in charge of all pension and many social security benefits (Pension Fund, Pensions Generated from Other than Labor Activity Reasons Fund,Industrial Injuries and Professional Illness Fund, General Illness and Maternity Fund, Unemployment Fund).Other funds included in social security sector are Employees’ Receivables Guarantee Fund, Teachers’ Pension Fund and the National Health Insurance Fund. The State Budget data are based on cash accounting records of the central government operations, and reflect the transactions of the following units: 1) the Central Budget, where mainly tax revenues and interest payments transactions as well as transfers to other units of the general government are executed; 2) line ministries and agencies (called also the first level spending units - FLSU), executive agencies (units supported by the FLSU); 3) the National Audit Office (NAO); Classification system : Monthly data on the consolidated central government are disseminated within a breakdown to revenue, expenditure, balance, net acquisition of assets other than cash (domestic and foreign), net incurrance of liabilities (domestic and foreign) and net change in the stock of cash. Monthly data on the constituent budgets of the consolidated central government as a part of the general government report are compiled and disseminated according to the national methodology under the following classification: Revenue and grants Tax revenue Non tax revenue Grants Expenditures Wages and salaries Social and health insurance contributions Maintenance Interests: External Domestic Social expenditures, scholarships Subsidies Capital expenditures, State reserve gain, and purchases of agricultural production Transfers Transfers/loans from/to Central Budget (net) Other transfers Contribution for the EU budget Deficit /surplus Financing External (net) Borrowing from abroad (net) Deposits (net) Domestic (net) o.w. Advance payments form the national budget the account of the EU Repayments for the account of the EU Privatization, net acquisition and net lending o.w. revenue from privatization Monthly and quarterly data in the GFS format in accordance with GFSM 2001 methodology (Statement of Sources and Uses of Cash) are compiled and disseminated for the consolidated central government on the website of the Ministry of Finance and through the Integrated Collection System to the IMF, and the annual data for the central government, as a part of the general government, are disseminated in the Government Finance Statistics Yearboo k of the IMF through ICS in the GFSM 2014 format. Scope of the data Institutional coverage of central government in reported data: The data cover all central budgetary organizations and accounts for resources from the EU. In accordance with the Public Finance Act, effective from 01.01.2014, all extra-budgetary accounts and funds were terminated and their revenues and expenditures were included in the corresponding budgets. In the budgets of the budgetary organizations should not be included only the extra-budgetary accounts existing for the purpose of administering European Union funds. From the entry into force of the Public Finance Act they should be considered accounts for European Union funds. According to the Act these accounts are financial and legal form for budgetary organizations to receive, allocate and spend European Union funds and the related national co-financing. The coverage of the central government operations comprises the following units: Budgetary organizations included in Central Government subsector: National Assembly, President's office; Council of Ministries' office, ministries and agencies, Supreme Judicial Council, Constitutional court, National Audit Office; Other budgetary organizations included in Central government subsector; Public universities, Bulgarian academy of science; Other budgets under art. 13 par. 3 of PFA - Bulgarian National Television; Bulgarian National Radio, Bulgarian News Agency (BTA); Budgets under art. 13 par. 4 of PFA – Enterprise for management of environmental protection activities (EMEPA), Fund "Security of the electrical system", State-owned enterprise "Single System Operator"; Accounts for EU funds (Agricultural Fund; National Fund with the Ministry of Finance and other accounts for resources from the EU); Social security funds: National Social Security Institute (Public social security, Teachers pension fund, "Guaranteed receivables for workers and employees" fund); National Health Insurance Fund. Exceptions to coverage The coverage of the central government accounts follows the GFSM'2001 classification system except for the units reclassified in the central government sector in abidance to the new ESA 2010 rules for fiscal notification (EDP) purposes. In accordance with the implementation of the new ESA 2010 the following public corporations were reclassified for the period 2010-2016: Eco Medet EOOD; Ecoengeneering RM EOOD; National Company Industrial Zones EAD; Eco Anthracite EAD; State Enterprise Radioactive Waste; Montages – Sofia EOOD (only for 2013) ; Energy Efficiency and Renewable Sources Fund; State Consolidation Company; Zeminvest EAD; Gorna Oryahovitza Airport EAD ; Bulgarian Deposit Insurance Fund; Fish resources EOOD – Sozopol; Center For Psychiatric Health – Sofia EOOD; Bulgarian institute for starndartization; Bulgarian-American commission for edicational exchange; Fund Maganer of financial instruments in Bulgaria EAD; Sofia Tech Park AD; Ruse Airport EOOD and was reclassified only for 2013 and 2014 . The following units were reclassified in central government sector in accordance with the old ESA 1995 as well: Bulgarian Railway Company's entity "BDZ Passengers services EAD" was reclassified in central government sector in 2011, National Company Railway Infrastructure reclassified since 2010 for the period starting since 2007, the Fund for Local Authorities and Government (FLAG), hospitals with central government ownership (reclassified since 2006 starting from 2001 data), compensation vouchers since 2006 starting from 2001 data, Public Investment Company for the time of its existence (2005-2007), Bank Consolidation Company – until its liquidation in 2005. 2.4.1 Valuation Payments in foreign currency, such as amortization payments, are converted into the local currency using the mid-point exchange rate between the buying and selling market on the date of the transaction. The exchange rates are provided by the Bulgarian National Bank. 2.4.2 Recording basis The official data is reported on a cash basis in accordance with the Public Finance Act. The chart of accounts for budget enterprises harmonized with the ESA 2010 and GFSM 2001 requirements provides information on accrual basis. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures All transactions are shown on a gross basis, except for tax refunds and financing transactions, which are shown net. Fiscal data on the subsectors of the central government are recorded on a gross basis and intersectoral flows are eliminated in consolidation. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Data sources: The monthly fiscal data are compiled from unaudited accounting records. The annual data are certified from the National Audit Office. Basis for recording receipts: cash received and bank accounts credited. Basis for recording payments: cash paid and bank accounts debited. The web-based system, where the data for compiling the reports of the monthly and quarterly CGO is collected, is open and flexible to the needs of the data suppliers. The requirements set in the international standards are taken into account in its development. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness The instructions for monthly source data are published only once in the beginning of the current year and sets exact date on which the budgetary units are supposed to upload their reports in the system. 3.2.1 Source data assessment The information supplied by the institutional units is checked for internal consistency by automatic procedure checks set in the web-based system for data collection. It indicates to the users if their data is not correctly filled in the form. Another type of cross-check is made by the Ministry of finance through the sums reported in the bank accounts of the institutional units, uploaded to the system and the information accounted in the Bulgarian National Bank for the same amounts. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Adjustments for accounting basis: No adjustments are made to convert the data to an accrual basis. Adjustments for grossing and netting transactions: All transactions are recorded on a gross basis, except for tax refunds and financing transactions, which are shown net. Consolidation: Transfers between the different levels of Central Government are consolidated. Other Compilation Issues: Unrealized profits and losses from the revaluation of foreign exchange holdings are treated as financing. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures The fiscal year begins on January 1 and ends on December 31. Seasonally adjusted data are not currently prepared. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs The statistical outputs are checked horizontally and vertically and when an internal discrepancy is identified in the data is investigated and resolved.
