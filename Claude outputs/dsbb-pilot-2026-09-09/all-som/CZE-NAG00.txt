 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Analytical framework: Overall structure of the ESA 2010 . The data are compiled in accordance with the methodology set out in ESA 2010. Definition: GDP measures the final result of the productive activity of resident producer units without deductions for consumption of fixed capital 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Classification system: Local versions of NACE Rev. 2 (activities) and CPA https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/ramon/nomenclatures/index.cfm?TargetUrl=LST_NOM_DTL&StrNom=CPA_2008&StrLanguageCode= EN&IntPcKey=&StrLayoutCode=HIERARCHIC 2008 (products), as well as the COICOP (individual consumption by purpose), COFOG (functions of government), and CN https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/ramon/nomenclatures/index.cfm?TargetUrl=LST_NOM_DTL&StrNom=CN_2022&StrLanguageCode= EN&IntPcKey=&StrLayoutCode=HIERARCHIC (international merchandise trade) are utilized. QSA are compiled for individual sectors (S.11, S.12, S.13, S.14+S.15, S.2). Scope of the data Transactions coverage: Transactions compiled in quarterly frequency are the following: All transactions in goods and services (at current, previous year's average, and 2020 constant prices), compensation of employees, taxes on products, taxes on production and imports, subsidies on products, subsidies on production, gross operating surplus and mixed income. For the general government and households sectors (only at current prices) – all transactions in goods and services, distributive transactions, transactions in financial instruments, and consumption of fixed capital. Quarterly (non-financial) sector accounts (QSA) were compiled regularly and experimentally from March 2007 to September 2007 in the frame of the QSA project organized by Eurostat. The QSA were released for the first time in January 2008, then in April 2008, in both cases only for Eurostat. The QSA data have been disposable for users at the CZSO web page since June 2008. Two new outputs based on QSA figures have been released also at the CZSO web page (Table Quarterly sector accounts - time series and Table Quarterly sector accounts – integrated accounts) since the end of June 2008: https://csu.gov.cz/produkty/hdp_ts. Geographic coverage: The whole Czech economic territory. Unrecorded activity Unrecorded activity: Most of illegal activities are not included. 2.4.1 Valuation According to ESA 2010 guidelines. This implies, among other things, output at basic prices, use at purchasers' prices, output for own final use at the basic prices (without agricultural production for own use of households, this is evaluated at purchasers' prices at which it could be sold if offered for sale), other nonmarket output as the sum of costs, imports and exports of goods at f.o.b. at the border of the exporting country. 2.4.2 Recording basis Time of recording: The collected statistical data are predominantly available on an accrual basis in accordance with business accounting regulations. The administrative data for the government sector are available on a cash basis. Data on taxes are adjusted to an accrual basis for the current quarterly GDP estimates. After the reconciliation with annual national accounts' figures they are adapted to an accrual basis. Recording period: Calendar quarters. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Production approach: The main data source for output, gross value added and intermediate consumption in sector of non-financial corporation and sector of households is the information about VAT return by companies. The data is acquired in two frequencies - monthly and quarterly. From this data source is constructed the indicator for benchmarking the annual data from questionnaire P5-01 to a quarterly frequency. After the acquisition, the data is controlled for mistakes and extreme values which can affect the final results. For the GDP Preliminary Estimate (30 days after the quarter), there is limited data availability. The first two months are available for companies which report data on monthly basis. The companies which report every quarter are not available as the last month of the quarter of companies which report every month. For the next estimates of GVA, there is a full data set for the estimated quarter. The indicator of output is constructed by the sum of the specific items in the VAT return form and is mainly based on turnover and relevant items (these are generally items involving delivery of goods or provision of services).The data is divided into activities on the NACE 2 level and categorized into the size group. Size groups are the groups of products of companies calculated as deciles. In Preliminary Estimate the missing data is estimated at the level of sums of missing data by ARIMA/ARIMAX models. After that, the total figures by the NACE 2 category and size group is taken as an indicator for benchmarking. These totals represent the indicator of output. The category size represents the different trends across one activity. The indicator for the GVA is calculated as the mean ratio of GVA to the indicator from the last years Expenditure approach: Estimate of the final consumption expenditure is primarily based on retail sales data. Information on retail sales is obtained from the "Monthly surveys for trade and services" (SP 1-12). Estimate for alcoholic drinks and tobacco is based on the quarterly estimate of the previous year, price indices and on the annual volume index of the previous year. Government final consumption expenditure and capital formation are estimated from (complete) budget records kept by central government. Private gross fixed capital formation is largely based on data obtained from statistical survey (the questionnaire named "P6-04"). This questionnaire comprises stock financial indicators, which are necessary for compilation of quarterly financial accounts and increases and decreases of intangible and tangible fixed assets. The stocks of inventories are also included here. Only enterprises with significant amounts of assets are obliged to fill in this questionnaire. Approximately two thousand respondents fill in the questionnaire. Respondents send filled questionnaires up to 30 days after the end of the quarter (up to 35 days for 4th quarter). The survey "P6-04" is conducted as a census type. This statistical survey is mainly used for compilation of regular estimates of GDP. Such statistical survey ("P6-04") is supplemented by foreign trade data, data on construction output, and other secondary sources. Changes in inventories are estimated by means of the same source as mentioned under gross fixed capital formation - statistical survey ("P6-04"). Data on imports and exports of goods are taken from foreign trade statistics but they have been a little adjusted since 2008 data; data on imports and exports of transport and other services have been taken from the CZSO's statistical surveys since the first quarter of 2007. The revisions have to be made to the previously published data from the period following the Czech Republic's accession to the European Union. Starting September 2011, the CZSO newly publishes in national accounts data on imports of goods and exports of goods in national concept which is strictly based on the change of ownership. Income approach: The data sources are the same as for the production approach. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording In the course of surveys, first of all the following indicators are surveyed: number of employees, total wages, selected components of revenues and cost, increases and decreases of intangible and tangible fixed assets. 3.2.1 Source data assessment Routine assessment of source data is provided primarily by branch departments of the CZSO. When National accounts departments as main users of those data are doubtful about some results they have to refer to branch departments, not to a reporting unit. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Production approach: For the data on sales (or revenues, used for calculation of output) and purchases (or cost, used for calculation of intermediate consumption) from the quarterly enterprise surveys corrections are made for non-response . Then, grossing up is done for sample surveys. Expenditure approach: As with the production approach, the basic sources are adjusted for nonresponse. Then, grossing up is done for sample surveys. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures General: The quarterly aggregates are built up as absolute levels, using techniques and data sources similar to those in the annual accounts. Production approach: The data on sales (or revenues, used for calculation of output) obtained from VAT return by companies and purchases (or cost, used for calculation of intermediate consumption) are adjusted for holding gains/losses on inventories. Letting of dwellings the services and owner-occupied housing are estimated in the same way as in annual national accounts by the same specialist (since 2000 - the user costs method for calculation of dwelling services in owner occupied dwellings). Priority is to reach comparability of output (at basic prices) and intermediate consumption (at purchasers´ prices) with the same quarter in the previous year. This priority materializes in the estimation procedures in two ways: At first some quarterly figures are obtained by the same methods (e.g. exports, imports, final household consumption) and from the same data sources as annual ones. Methodology is the same. At second reconciliation between quarterly and annual figures is provided in this sense. The calculations are made in a breakdown according to the intermediate aggregation of NACE classification (that is, 44 categories). Expenditure approach: Certain data on households' consumption and on gross fixed capital formation are estimated by extrapolating the annual estimates. In case of households expenditures it mainly concerns estimation of consumption of alcoholic beverages, cigarettes and imputed rents. Figures on gross fixed capital formation are estimated by using annual data figures for the sector of non-profit institutions and in the sector of non-financial enterprises for units with less than 20 employees. Income approach: In the Czech national accounts, the income approach to estimating GDP cannot be considered as an independent method because operating surplus and mixed income are not calculated separately. They are obtained in the form of balancing items between gross value added obtained by production approach and other components of gross value added (compensation of employees, taxes on production minus subsidies on production). The compilation and data sources of compensation of employees, taxes on production minus subsidies on production are similar to the compilation of components of GDP by production approach. Constant prices:The methodology of converting quarterly national accounts data from current prices into constant prices was harmonized with the procedure used in annual national accounts: the indicators are converted first into the previous year's average prices. Since 2004, as a rule, the indicators have been converted at first into the previous year's average prices and then chained into basic year prices (2020 year prices since June 2024) by means of annual average price indices. As a result of using this method of chain-linking (which is recommended by Eurostat) aggregates are not additive. It means that values of aggregates at 2020prices do not agree with sums of individual components. Base year: Base year is changed about every five years; presently the base year is 2020. Seasonal adjustment: Regular seasonally adjusted data have been calculated with the help of the Tramo-Seats method (Demetra+1.0.2.), and published for GDP, individual components of the GDP expenditures and the gross value added by kind of activity since 2002 year. A correction for the influence of a different number of working days in individual periods has been performed since March 2005 on selected time series (for which this influence is statistically significant). Geographic detail: Quarterly accounts exclusively refer to the Czech Republic as a whole. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs Reconciliation: Reconciliation between various data sources on the most detailed possible level takes place. The production approach is considered as more reliable for current prices, therefore the discrepancies are removed in balancing process by adjustments in the inventories category. On the other hand, deflation of expenditure components is considered as more reliable than deflation of output and intermediate consumption. This is why the discrepancies at previous year’s prices are allocated mainly to intermediate consumption. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses Certain regularity was set up in the system of quarterly national accounts' revisions.
