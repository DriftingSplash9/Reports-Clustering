 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions General Government data compilation is carried out in line with the "Special Data Dissemination Standard: guide for subscribers and users" 2013 (IMF), as well as according to the "Government Finance Statistics Manual" 2014 (IMF). 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization For Government Finance Statistics compilation is used national budget classification, approved by the Ministry of Finance of Ukraine according to the art.8 of Budget Code of Ukraine. National Budget classification consists of: Revenue budget classification (divided into tax and non-tax revenue, receipts from capital operations, transfers, special targets funds); Expenditure and lending budget classification : functional expenditure and lending/borrowing classification; agent-by-agent expenditure and lending/borrowing classification; economic classification of expenditure; program classification of expenditure and lending/borrowing; budget lending/borrowing classification. Budget financing classification : financing by creditor type; financing by type of debt instruments. Debt classification : debt classification by creditor type; debt classifications by debt instrument. Budget classification approaches the standards of the Government Finance Statistics Manual 2014 (IMF). 2.2.1 Scope For Government Finance Statistics compilation is used national budget classification, approved by the Ministry of Finance of Ukraine according to the art.8 of Budget Code of Ukraine. National Budget classification consists of: Revenue budget classification (divided into tax and non-tax revenue, receipts from capital operations, transfers, special targets funds); Expenditure and lending budget classification : functional expenditure and lending/borrowing classification; agent-by-agent expenditure and lending/borrowing classification; economic classification of expenditure; program classification of expenditure and lending/borrowing; budget lending/borrowing classification. Budget financing classification : financing by creditor type; financing by type of debt instruments. Debt classification : debt classification by creditor type; debt classifications by debt instrument. Budget classification approaches the standards of the Government Finance Statistics Manual 2014 (IMF). Scope of the data Institutional coverage of General Government sector is comprehensive. It comprises data on Central Government bodies financed from the State Budget of Ukraine; Local Government bodies, financed from the Local Budgets, and state pension and social security funds: the Pension Fund of Ukraine and the Unemployment Social Insurance Fund of Ukraine. Exceptions to coverage Starting April, 2014 budgetary and financial reporting do not cover data for temporarily occupied by the russian federation territories of Ukraine. Unrecorded activity Not applicable. 2.4.1 Valuation State treasury bodies provide accounting and reporting in national currency - Hryvnia. Operations denominated in foreign currency are reflected in reports in national currency by recalculating foreign currency amount using the National Bank of Ukraine exchange rate. 2.4.2 Recording basis Budget execution transactions are recorded on cash basis with accrual elements for spending units’ liabilities. The fiscal year is the same as the calendar year. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures Data are recorded on a gross basis. Tax repayment is deducted from revenue. Financing operations are recorded on a net basis. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Input data is comprehensive. According to the Budget Code of Ukraine reports on consolidated, state and local budget execution on revenue, expenditure, lending/borrowing, financing and debt are prepared by the State Treasury Service of Ukraine and provided the Ministry of Finance of Ukraine in due time. Information on state social and pension security funds' operations is provided directly by the funds and being reclassified in line with GFSM 2014; there is a cross-sector consolidation applied. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Data definitions, coverage and classifications are presented in the Budget Code of Ukraine, in the MoF order №11 of 14.01.2011 (as amended) «On Budget Classification» and other regulative acts. State pension and social security funds data definitions, coverage and classifications presented in regulation acts issued by relevant fund and the Ministry of Finance orders. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness State and local budgets execution reports and state social and pension security funds' execution reports are provided within the time specified in budget regulations. Availability of quarterly reports on budget execution is not later than 35th day after the end of the reference quarter. Availability of quarterly reports on state social and pension security funds budget execution is not later than 40th day after the end of the reference quarter. The data on fourth quarter on general government is considered to be final after the final budget execution reports are available, after submitting the execution report of the Law on State Budget of Ukraine to the Verkhovna Rada, which is not later than the 1st of April of the year following the reporting one. Annual reports on social and pension security funds budget execution are available not later than March, 15 of the year following the reporting one. However, following the full-scale military aggression of the russian federation against Ukraine there were amendments made to the Budget Code of Ukraine in March, 2022 to suspend the deadlines observation for monthly, quarterly and annual budget reporting during martial law in Ukraine. 3.2.1 Source data assessment Statistical data collection system is automated and provides control over the data on each stage of its preparing. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques There is national cash data reclassification to be in line with international classifications and to meet GFS 2014 IMF Manual requirements. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures There is data consolidation between state and local budgets and state social and pension security funds budgets, which means elimination all transactions between mentioned above unities, and also mutual transactions between funds. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results Intermediate data are adjusted according to the quarterly and annual reporting. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data Intermediate data are subject to logical and formal verification. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs Any statistical discrepancy are analyzed and solved. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses There is a statistical data analysis during the year to be in line with international standards concerning the methodology of government finance statistics compilation. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses Disseminated times series data and metadata are revised annually and adjusted where necessary. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request Data can be obtained on official request through the contact person. 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points Information on contact persons is available at website of the Ministry of Finance of Ukraine in the "Special Data Dissemination Standard IMF (SDDS IMF)" section (
