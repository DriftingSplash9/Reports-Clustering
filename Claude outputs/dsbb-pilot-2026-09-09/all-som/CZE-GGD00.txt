 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Debt statistics are consistent with the definition adopted in the Protocol on the Excessive Deficit Procedure of the Maastricht Treaty (following the methodology stated in Council Regulation (EC) No 479/2009; usually known as Maastricht debt). This definition does not include financial derivatives; shares and other equities; insurance, pension and standardized guarantee schemes; and other accounts payable. SNA 2008/ESA 2010 criteria are adopted to identify general government units as well as debt instruments. The concepts set out in the System of National Accounts 2008 are harmonized with those of Public Sector Debt Statistics: Guide for Compilers and Users 2011 and Government Finance Statistics Manual 2014. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The general government sector and its subsectors are defined in accordance with the ESA 2010 guidelines and the ESA 2010 Manual on Government Deficit and Debt, which are also consistent with the 2008 SNA and GFSM 2014. The general government in the Czech Republic consists of central government, local government, and social security funds. The CZSO compiles and disseminates government finance statistics for each subsector as well as for the general government sector as a whole. Sector classification of units is checked on yearly basis and changed if necessary. One of the priorities of the CZSO is keep GFS data and sector accounts consistent as far as the sector delimitation is concerned. 2.2.1 Scope The general government sector and its subsectors are defined in accordance with the ESA 2010 guidelines and the ESA 2010 Manual on Government Deficit and Debt, which are also consistent with the 2008 SNA and GFSM 2014. Scope of the data The institutional coverage corresponds to general government sector according to the methodology ESA 2010. In the Czech Republic the CZSO is responsible for the delimitation of general government sector and its updating. This section describes the coverage of the general government sector and its sub-sectors. The general government sector in the Czech Republic is composed by 3 subsectors: S.1311 – Central government subsector, S.1313 – Local government subsector, S.1314 – Social security funds subsector. Central government subsector (S.1311) Central government subsector includes following units, structured into budgetary and extra-budgetary ones. 1. Budgetary: Central budgetary organizations (CBO), i.e. ministries, central offices and organizations established and managed by ministries or other central authorities, whose revenues and expenditures are part of the State budget 2. Extra-budgetary: a) State Extra-budgetary Funds (SF) – established as legal persons by a corresponding ministry according to special laws b) Central (state) semi-budgetary organizations (SBO) – organizations established by a central budgetary organization to carry out some of the government functions, especially in the areas of cultural, education or social services c) Other extra-budgetary funds and central government units (e.g. public universities, public research institutions) d) The central government subsector included also organizations that were cancelled during the past years (e.g. transformation institutions – financial institutions established in the nineties and engaged in sales and purchases of state bad claims, especially foreign). Local government subsector (S.1313) Local government subsector includes following units: 1. Local budgetary organizations: a) Territorial Self-governing Divisions and Offices, i.e. regional offices, municipalities and town councils b) Voluntary Associations of Municipalities c) Regional Councils of Cohesion Regions - they were cancelled as of December 31, 2021 2. Other local organizations: a) Local semi-budgetary organizations, i.e. organizations established by a local budgetary organization to carry out some of the government functions. These organizations operate in different branches of the national economy and that are linked with the local budget. b) Non-profit institutions, which provide services only to local government units or they are financed especially from local budgets c) Public "non-market" corporations - companies providing technical services and services in culture and sports d) Public health facilities with a legal form other than semi-budgetary organization that are owned by an entity in the general government sector (mainly municipalities and regions) e) Public financial corporations Social security funds subsector (S.1314) The social security funds subsector includes only institutional units administrating general (compulsory) health insurance and units serving to these health insurance companies. 1. Therefore e.g. debt is valued at face value for the EDP purposes; however particular items are valued at market price in national accounts. The accounting year (fiscal year) is the calendar year. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures The CZSO follows the rules set by the regulation (ESA 2010). 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Statistical data are collected by the CZSO on the base of Degree ( https://csu.gov.cz/vykazy/program_statistickych_zjistovani ). General policy of the CZSO is to take statistical data for the general government sector as supplementary data filling existing gaps in administrative data sources. Data sources (profit and loss statement and balance sheet) for budgetary organizations, state funds and semi-budgetary units are collected and processed by the MoF (including budgetary reports, i.e. data on revenue and expenditure of budgetary organizations and state funds). Data for financial institutions classified as government units are mainly provided by the CNB. Administrative data mentioned above are in case of need supplemented by statistical questionnaires surveying mainly indicators on natural wage, investments, etc. Data for other units – public universities, health insurance companies, public research institutions, public hospitals, Czech TV and radio (public broadcasting companies), Financial Market Guarantee System, other non-profit institutions and public corporations are collected directly by the CZSO. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness All administrative data and statistical questionnaires are available for compilation of quarterly general government national accounts in time t+50 (or t+70) days after the end of previous quarter. 3.2.1 Source data assessment Source data are based especially on budgetary reports and financial statements that cover most of government units, i.e. response is 100%. Non-profit institutions provide information through statistical questionnaire – and statistical survey is exhaustive – response is usually 100%. Assessment is carried out during the processing of the statements; results are monitored and extreme mistakes are discussed with the concrete respondent. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Since the compilation of the government accounts is based especially on financial and accounting statements, the response is usually 100%. In some cases, the statistical questioning is not exhaustive (for example semi-budgetary organizations). However, the CZSO disposes of total figures (i.e. total assets and their structure) so that only structures of some sub-items are surveyed. Budgetary classification of revenue and expenditure is very detailed and supplement accounting data for budgetary organizations, state funds and semi-budgetary organizations (from the Auxiliary Analytical Overview - AAO) are also detailed. It means that the CZSO disposes of data for all units classified in the general government sector. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Data adjustments are carried out in compliance with the ESA methodology. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results Validation of intermediate result is carried out automatically during the processing of data sources, because information on non-financial and financial transactions for each unit is obtained e.g. from Profit and Loss Statement and Balance sheet – comparison with the previous report and individual questioning is used. As to validation of data from statistical questionnaire – the CZSO Regional Administrations ensures collection, processing and control of quarterly and annual statistical questionnaires. For this purpose, so called "Technical Projects" are elaborated that include technical linkages between indicators and draw attention e.g. to extreme or negative value. During the compilation process, yearly changes in relative and absolute terms are checked and verified. If possible, also information from annual reports of reporting companies is used as supplementary information. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data Items of exceptional value are checked, individual questioning (with the unit) is used, if necessary. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs Statistical output is compared with the data from banking statistics (e.g. on interest or deposits) and with data from the Final state budget. This discussion with the CNB and the MoF is regular and it is already performed during the compilation of government accounts. The comparison of statistical output for individual unit with its annual report has also an important role. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses Quarterly data are revised within current year. Previous year´s quarterly data are revised after publication of annual national accounts or after revision of annual national accounts and also after revisions in previous year´s data in EDP notification tables. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request 5.3.2 Availability of documents and services catalogs CZSO Website The main source of statistical information for users is the website https://csu.gov.cz/home Publication Activities An overview of all CZSO products can be found in the Catalogue of Products, which is available on the website for users.
