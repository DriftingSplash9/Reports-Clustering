 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The data are compiled in broad conformity with international practice and in accordance with definitions recommended by the United Nations for foreign trade statistics. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The product nomenclature used is that of the Harmonized System. The country nomenclature used is the ISO numeric 3-digit nomenclature. Scope of the data The data cover the customs territory or the entire national territory and are drawn up in accordance with the General Trade System. All goods are recorded upon crossing the national border. Imports include: goods entering directly from abroad, to be distributed on the local market for consumption or use by local industries; goods entering directly from abroad and delivered under bond to factories for processing; goods imported from abroad and stored in customs warehouses for subsequent delivery to the domestic market or for re-export; and goods imported under bond. Exports include: direct exports of Tunisian goods; goods leaving in-bond factories and exported abroad; foreign goods re-exported without processing after entering the country (settlement of import duties); and goods leaving customs bonded warehouses and exported abroad. Exceptions to coverage Operations excluded are Military goods; Monetary gold; Banknotes; Personal effects. Unrecorded activity No estimates of missing data are made. 2.4.1 Valuation Valuation : f.o.b. values for exports; c.i.f. values for imports. Sales at best price are valued at market prices. Conversion to unit of account : The rate used for converting the value of the transaction into local currency terms is the market exchange rate on the date of the transaction. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Customs declarations 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Type of data collected : Office code Type of declaration Declaration number Date Importer/exporter code Country of final destination/of origin Currency code Amount in foreign exchange Item number NSH Customs regime Country of origin Statistical regime Sup. unit code Sup. unit Net weight F.o.b. value Customs value Basis of country data : country of origin for imports country of destination for exports Timing of recording transactions : The data for the reference month (n) are received on the 1 st or 2 nd of month (n+1). The initial results for the reference month (n) by group and sector are published on or about the 12 th of month (n+1). 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Processing system : Customs uses a computerized customs clearance system (SINDA). The National Statistics Institute (INS) uses its own computer program for processing foreign trade data. Site of processing: The INS is responsible for processing and producing foreign trade statistics. Seasonal adjustment : The published data are not seasonally adjusted. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request Geographic detail: The data by country and by Harmonized System of Nomenclature (HSN) are published at the end of each month. The INS does not publish statistics broken down by port.
