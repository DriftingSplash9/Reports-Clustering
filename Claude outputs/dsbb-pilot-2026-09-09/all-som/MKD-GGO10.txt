 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Data are compiled broadly in accordance with the statistical framework set out in the IMF Government Finance Statistic Manual 2014 (GFSM 2014). 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Data are compiled and disseminated according to GFSM 2014 Framework, in line with the requirements of the SDDS Plus. The following items are disseminated R evenue Tax Social Contributions Grants Other revenue E xpense Compensations of employees Use of Goods and services Interest Subsidies Grants Social Benefits Other Expense Gross Operating Balance Net Acquisition of NonFinancial Assets Fixed assets Valuables Non -Produced Assets Expenditure Net Lending / Net Borrowing Net Acquisition of Financial Assets Domestic debtors External debtors Net Incurrence of Liabilities Domestic creditors External creditors The data are presented in amounts of MKD millions. Scope of the data General Government operations includes operations of all budgetary government units. This includes the core central government (ministries, agencies, government departments included in the Budget) as well as the three social securities funds (Employment Fund, Health Fund and Pension Fund) and ~80 municipalities. Exceptions to coverage General government operations data currently excludes a number of entities that are substantially or largely financed from the budget, and which are recognized to be part of the general government sector when applying the definitions and guidance in GFSM 2014. Missing entities include Macedonia Radio Television, Agency for Electronic Communication, Macedonia Railway Infrastructure, Public Hospitals and others. Unrecorded activity Data on consumption of fixed capital and inventories are not disseminated. These two items are accrual accounting / non-cash concepts, for which estimates are not available from the cash based accounting data used to compile general government operations data. 2.4.1 Valuation All transactions are recorded at market value. Payments in foreign currency, such as amortization payments, are converted into the local currency using the mid-point exchange rate between the buying and selling market on the date of the transaction. The exchange rates are provided by the NBRM. 2.4.2 Recording basis General government operations are recorded on a cash basis. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures All transactions are shown on a gross basis, except for taxes (which are shown net of tax refunds and transactions in assets and liabilities (which are calculated as acquisitions less disposals or disbursements minus repayments respectively). 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The annual fiscal data are compiled from accounting records and from treasury information system. The annual data are certified by the National Audit Office. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording General government operations data is compiled from data sourced from the Treasury Information System. This data is compiled using a national chart of accounts, and this data is bridged / mapped to the general government operations data though a bridge table, at a granular level. The Chart of Accounts follows a national presentation, which has some similarities to the GFSM 1986. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Source data timeliness is adequate for producing and disseminating General Government debt within the timeliness required under the SDDS Plus. The primary source of data is the Treasury Information System, which provides finalized cash data for general government units within 1 month of the accounting period. 3.2.1 Source data assessment Source data is regularly assessed for the purposes of data compilation. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Consolidation: Transfers between the different levels of General Government are consolidated. Data in the national presentation is mapped to GFSM 2014 concepts using a bridge table, developed with the help of IMF technical assistance. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures There are a number of adjustments to the source data to ensure general government operations is well aligned with the concept and definitions in GFSM 2014. In addition, adjustments made to the source data relate to the consolidation of general government for which intergovernmental transfers are eliminated. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results Part of the data on domestic central government debt related to structural bonds are cross-checked with the records of Central Security Depository. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses The revisions to the data are typically very small, typically as a result of debt transactions occurring later in the quarter and not appearing in the accounting systems. These are captured before the end of the subsequent quarter and data for the previous quarter is revised if required. 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points Contact persons for fiscal data are published on the IMF Dissemination Standards Bulletin Board
