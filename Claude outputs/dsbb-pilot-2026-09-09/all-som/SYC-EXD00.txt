 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The balance of payments statistics are compiled broadly following the guidelines recommended in the IMF’s Balance of Payments and Investment Position Manual , sixth edition ( BPM6 ). None 2.4.2 Recording basis To the extent possible, external debt data are compiled on an accrual basis. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures The compilation and reporting is on a gross basis 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Data on the public external debt cover the external borrowing of the Government and the parastatal companies. Parastatal companies are defined as corporations in which the Government owns more than 50 percent of the ordinary shares. Data on parastatals include external debt contracted with or without the guarantee of the government. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness External Sector data are compiled and disseminated on a quarterly basis consistent with the SDDS requirement 3.2.1 Source data assessment Survey results are assessed on an annual basis with the objective to improve response rate and/or find alternative means of collecting the required statistics when appropriate None 5.1.5 Dissemination on request Non-confidential unpublished data are made available on request
