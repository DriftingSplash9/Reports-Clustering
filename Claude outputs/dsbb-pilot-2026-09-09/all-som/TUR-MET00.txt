 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions International trade in goods statistics are in line with the UNSD's and EU's methodology on ITGS. Statistics include goods which enter/leave the economic territory of Türkiye from/to other countries and are placed: under the customs normal export and import procedures under customs inward and outward processing procedures border trade 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Commodity Classifications: The commodity codes used for compiling ITGS are the 12-digit codes called Customs Tariff Statistical Position (GTIP). The first 6 digits (HS) are based on the Harmonized Commodity Description and Coding System prepared and managed by World Customs Organisation and used by United Nations, the first 8 digits are based on the Combined Nomenclature (CN), used in EU and the last 4 digits are added by Türkiye for national purposes. Furthermore, the data are available in commodity classifications such as SITC Rev.2, SITC Rev.3, SITC Rev.4, BEC, CPA2008, CPC, NST2007 and Product Group by Technology Intensity classifications. Activity Classifications: Data are available in ISIC Rev.2, ISIC Rev.3, ISIC Rev.4, NACE Rev.2 Country and Country Group Classification: Geonomenclature, European Union's country classification, is used as country and country group classification. The classifications for the compilation of statistics on ITGS are laid down international Organisations' classifications (UN, Eurostat) Detailed information on classifications used for ITGS is available on TurkStat website. https://biruni.tuik.gov.tr/DIESS/ChangeLocaleAction.do?dil=tr 2.2.1 Scope Commodity Classifications: The commodity codes used for compiling ITGS are the 12-digit codes called Customs Tariff Statistical Position (GTIP). Data by Special Trade System is also available. · Geographical coverage is based on economic territory. Statistical territory is is equal to economic territory in accordance with the general trade system principles. · Measures: · Statistical value · Net mass · Supplementary quantity Dimensions: · Type of commodity · Country of origin for imports · Country of last known destination for exports · EU's country and country group classification, geonomenclature, is used as country classification. Included items: · Normal export and import procedures · Under customs inward and outward processing procedures · Border trade · Imports by financial leasing · Non-monetary gold · Re-export/re-import Returned goods Exceptions to coverage In international trade in goods statistics, 100 US $ has been determined as statistical threshold and transactions below the threshold haven't been covered in statistics until 2021. As of January 2021, the statistical threshold is removed and all transactions are included in the statistics. Unrecorded activity The following classes of goods are excluded from the ITGS: · Transit trade · Shuttle trade · Temporary import and export · Commodities not crossed border · Monetary gold, means of payment which are legal tender, securities (HS 710820, 4907) · Goods for repair and maintenance · Goods imported / exported by operational leasing · Goods sent abroad for military troops · Goods benefiting from diplomatic, consular or similar immunity TurkStat and the Central Bank of Türkiye make estimates on shuttle trade for balance of payments data, these are not included in the ITGS disseminated by TurkStat. 2.4.1 Valuation ITGS are published as "statistical value". Value does not include any tax. All goods covered in international merchandise trade statistics, whether sold, exchanged or provided without payment, must have a statistical value. Value of a product is the transaction value that the price actually paid or payable for goods including some adjustments in accordance with the WTO Agreement on Customs Valuation. The valuation of goods is FOB for export and CIF for import. Data of FOB-type imports are available. ITGS are available in US Dollar, Euro and Turkish Lira. 2.4.2 Recording basis The calendar month is the basic period for compiling the data. Time of recording is the date when goods enter to or leave from the customs territory. Values of transactions are collected in transaction currencies and afterwards converted into US Dollar, Euro and Turkish Lira by using daily exchange rates published by the Central Bank of the Republic of Türkiye 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures Value of goods sent or received for processing is the value of materials for processing purposes. Value of goods sent or received after processing is defined by the value of materials entrusted for processing and the value of processing fee. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs All declarations and forms used for production of ITGS are compiled by the Ministry of Trade. The program BILGE and Electronic Data Interchange (EDI) and Simplified Customs Declarations (BGB) are used by the Ministry of Trade to compile trade data. Data are received directly from customs declarations. In addition, data of natural gas and electricity traded by public authorities are received from traders via survey. Data of financial leasing are received from the the Association of Financial Leasing. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Data are collected in accordance with definitions, scope, classifications, valuation and time of recording based on the UNSD's and EU's methodology on ITGS as described in 2.1 - 2.4. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness All declarations and forms used for production of ITGS are compiled by the Ministry of Trade and source data are received timely from the Ministry of Trade. 3.2.1 Source data assessment Data quality is verified in data control procedure. In every stage of the data production, automatic formal and logical checks are carried out. Cumulative trade data are compared with the Data Bulletin of the Ministry of Trade. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Data used for production of ITGS are collected automatically. All data published eithin the referance year considered as preliminary data. Data of referance year can be updated until the referance year is closed. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures The data are seasonally and calendar adjusted. Seasonal and calendar related effects prevent observing the general tendency of data because of their temporary characteristics. Therefore, identification of seasonal patterns of short term indicators plays a crucial role in making reliable comparisons between consecutive periods. The seasonal adjustment of ITGS is carried out using TRAMO-SEATS methodology. The software that is used for the application of this method is JDemetra+ developed by the National Bank of Belgium (NBB) in cooperation with the Deutsche Bundesbank and Eurostat in accordance with the Guidelines of the European Statistical System. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results ITGS are validated in the IT systems in TurkStat. Checking of aggregated data are carried out in order to find erroneous results which have a great impact on trade figures. Special attention is put to the validation of statistical value especially transactions having value over 500 000$ . 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data SAS programs developed by TurkStat are used in the production phase of statistics. There are control processes at every stage of the program. Before the publication stage, the cumulative values are controlled both automatically and by expert personnel according to different variables. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs Published data are not regularly compared with other country' data by using the mirror statistics method. This process is carried out only upon requests from other countries. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses Revisions are monitored with monthly vintage tables. The reasons for high rate revisions are investigated and information is provided on the subject when necessary. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses Revision studies are used for internal purposes. Revision studies are also a part of the Institutional Quality Report. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request Specific request regarding ITGS can be provided on request from info@tuik.gov.tr https://www.tuik.gov.tr/Kurumsal/Bilgiye_Nasil_Erisilir 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points All news releases contain contact details, names and telephone number. https://www.tuik.gov.tr/Kurumsal/Bilgiye_Nasil_Erisilir 5.3.2 Availability of documents and services catalogs TurkStat publishes catalog containing all publications. https://biruni.tuik.gov.tr/yayin/views/visitorPages/index.zul
