 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The international investment position (IIP) is compiled by the Balance of Payments Division of the National Bank of Rwanda (central bank or BNR ). It reflects Rwanda’s stock of financial assets and liabilities vis-à-vis the rest of the world. The IIP makes it possible to determine the net financial position of Rwanda vis-à-vis the rest of the world. The IIP is prepared in millions of USD and reported annually to the IMF using accounting values. In compiling its IIP estimates, the BNR makes use of a number of different sources, in particular the foreign exchange settlement balance, data provided by government agencies, and the annual survey of enterprises. The surveys are annually conducted on foreign private capital including FDI, Portfolio and other investments. The IIP publication is annual. Reserve assets do not include monetary gold. They are compiled using the BNR’s general balance document. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization With regard to the classification (analytical presentation) of the balance of IIP, it is published on the BNR website as well as the one disseminated to IMF is in BPM a6. The BPM6 formats starts from 2010 onwards. 2.2.1 Scope With regard to the classification (analytical presentation) of the balance of IIP, it is published on the BNR website as well as the one disseminated to IMF is in BPM a6. The BPM6 formats starts from 2010 onwards. Scope of the data Geographic Coverage: The territory of Rwanda, including Rwanda’s embassies abroad. Transaction Coverage: All the assets and liabilities of the sectors for which information are available are collected. Unit Coverage: Rwanda’s international investment position in principle include all the assets and liabilities of the residents of the country. Unrecorded activity From 1993 to the present, Rwanda’s IIP and BOP does not record the effects of migrants’ activities. It has been difficult, for example, to gauge the effects of the return of former Rwandan refugees who came back to the country in 1994, 1995, 1996 and thereafter, since they were not registered by customs upon their return. However, the assets repatriated by these Rwandan nationals were of sufficient magnitude to have a significant impact on the country’s balance of payments. Similarly, the BNR has not been able to obtain data on the supplies and equipment left behind at no charge by the United Nations Mission for Rwanda at the end of its mandate. Illegal transactions are not covered. Under the BPM6, migrant transfers have been reclassified to international investment position in other changes in volume. 2.4.1 Valuation Market value is used for assets and liabilities other than direct investment. Direct investment liabilities are valued at their book value. When claims and liabilities are denominated in foreign exchange, they are converted to Rwanda franc terms at the average end-of-period rate. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Sources: Data on the IIP account components are obtained from different sources: Direct Investment, Portfolio Investment and Other Investment are obtained from the foreign capital census results that is conducted every year. Currency and deposits are obtained from the central bank’s monetary survey and Reserves are obtained from the cash flow plan. External private borrowing is obtained from foreign private capital census and the list of companies is compiled by the bank supervision Department at the bank. A specific survey instrument is conceived and addressed on the concerned companies. A list of entities or enterprises to be surveyed (or sampled) has been prepared using various sources: The list of companies is updated each year using list of new registered foreign companies by Rwanda Development board (RDB). The BOP team also makes good use of enterprise register that is frequently updated by the annual integrated business enterprise survey conducted by National institute of statistics of Rwanda (NISR). 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Data collection instruments such as questionnaires and templates used in data collection are in line with standard BPM6 definitions, conventions and standards, scope and economic sectorization. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness BNR and NISR have improved source data timeliness to meet SDDS requirements. There are plans to start conduct quarterly foreign private capital surveys to meet the SDDS requirements. 3.2.1 Source data assessment The assessment is frequently done by the technical team with regard to censuses, sample surveys and templates used to collect administrative data. This done every time there is plan to conduct a new survey or if there is change in reporting requirements. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques As the IIP corresponds with the financial account in BOP, the value of current financial transactions between residents and non-residents. The entries are recorded with headings net acquisition of assets and net in currencies of liabilities for each of the components such as Direct investment, portfolio investment, financial derivatives and other investment. The estimates are derived from foreign private capital survey, monetary survey as well as administrative data on public external borrowing from MINECOFIN Direct investment The data are derived from the annual foreign private capital census results. Other investment: other investment is obtained from the foreign private capital census and some administrative data on both public and private external borrowing from MINECOFIN and BNR Monetary authorities The data is sourced from the BNR ’s monetary survey. The data on the liabilities of the monetary authorities relate to changes in the accounts held by foreign official entities (such as the IMF). General government The information under this heading is obtained from the Public Debt Division of the Ministry of Finance and Economic Planning. This heading currently covers project support lending (support for the Public Investment Program), loans to support the balance of payments, and subscriptions to the capital of non-monetary international organizations. Banks The data relate to changes in the foreign assets and liabilities of the commercial banks. They are derived from the monetary survey tables prepared by the Monetary Statistics Division in the BNR Statistics Department. Other sectors The information under this heading is obtained from the Bank Supervision Department of the BNR . This heading currently covers private residents borrowing from nonresident. The other investment heading covers also data on trade-related credit and the deposits of nonresidents with banks. The figures are extracted from Bank Supervision Department of the BNR and the foreign exchange operations statements. RESERVE ASSETS The data on reserve assets and Rwanda's reserve position in the IMF is obtained from BNR cash flow plan. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures The data are initially compiled in millions of U.S. dollars at the stock exchange rate, and then converted into Rwanda francs on the basis of end of period exchange rate. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results A review of the IIP table is done to check whether all the existing data sources and estimation methodologies have been exhausted. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses BNR is currently drafting revisions policy that will guide the process of revising statistics. The policy will be aligned to bank’s values of Integrity, Accountability, Efficiency, Effectiveness, Mutual respect and Teamwork. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses Revisions are communicated to the users. Commonly, changes are communicated at the time of introducing them, using footnotes, explanatory notes or special articles released simultaneously with the publication of the updated data. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request Upon request, unpublished IIP data can be shared to partner institutions. Normally it is posted on the BNR website.
