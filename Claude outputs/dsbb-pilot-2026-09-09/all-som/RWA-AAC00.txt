Scope of the data Transaction Coverage: All operations recorded in the General ledger of National Bank the balance sheets are covered by the Central Bank survey excluded Off-balance-sheet operations. Unit Coverage: Central Bank: The BNR The data are drawn up in terms of gross balances in millions of Rwanda frances; the primary aggregates of the BNR statement are as follows. 1. Assets Net Foreign assets: encompasses all gross claims of the BNR on nonresidents, including SDR holdings net of liabilities vis-à-vis nonresidents including SDR allocation. The residence criterion used to determine these claims and liabilities is consistent with the definition used in the 6 th edition of the Balance of Payments Manual . Claims on other depository institutions: encompasses all claims on other depository corporations in respect of money market loans, including foreign exchange swaps. Net claims on central and local governments: encompasses all categories of claims on the central and local (Provinces and Districts) governments net of all liabilities vis-à-vis the sub-sector. Claims on other resident sectors: encompasses claims on the following sectors: Claims on other nonfinancial corporations: reflects shares held by the Central Bank in the company which mission is to promote electronic payment system Claims on other resident sectors: largely reflects claims on central bank employees and former employees; 2. Liabilities Reserve Money: encompasses currency in circulation outside the BNR , the deposits in foreign exchange and Rwanda francs of the other depository corporations, all deposits with the BNR in Rwanda francs and foreign exchange of resident sectors and securities other than shares included in the national definition of broad money. Other liabilities vis-à-vis other depository corporation: encompasses amounts borrowed by the BNR from the money market, using various monetary policy instruments, including foreign exchange swaps . This exclude amounts borrowed for the fiscal policy purposes. Shares and other equity: this category encompasses the funds contributed by owners, retained earnings, general and special reserves, and valuation adjustment. Other items net : Liabilities not elsewhere classified net of assets not elsewhere classified. 2.4.1 Valuation Valuation rules: The accounting systems of the BNR (except for operations relating to its special missions) are maintained in accordance with the standards of the International Accounting Standards Board (IASB), in particular as regards valuation methods. Valuation of financial instruments in foreign exchange: Financial instruments in foreign exchange (including assets and liabilities in SDRs) are converted into national currency terms using the exchange rate in effect on the date of compilation of the monetary statistics. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The analytical statement of the BNR is produced daily from its accounting database. Data compilation is automated with the introduction of a new core banking software in April 2014, using software for which the parameters adhere to the methodology of the IMF’s “Monetary and Financial Statistics Compilation Guide ” ( 2008 ) and the output report is generated in the format of 1SR on daily basis. For some aggregates (currency in circulation and banks’ reserves) BNR uses administrative data. 3.2.1 Source data assessment Administrative data are used for currency out of BNR and Banks deposits at BNR since the introduction of a new core banking software ERP-T24 in 2014. The counterpart adjustments are done in the miscellaneous items. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures The statement of Rwanda’s central bank is automatically calculated on the basis of the detailed accounting statement of the BNR available on its computer network, but the compilation of the analytical survey and its consolidation with the other corporations’ survey are done manual.
