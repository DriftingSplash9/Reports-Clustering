 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The production index is the volume index of the Manufacturing Sector, which measures short-term developments in the volume of the output in Manufacturing establishments, which on average has a labour force of fifty or more persons. However, in Lesotho the index includes establishments with less than 50 persons, basically to include those sub branches, which would otherwise not be represented in the compilation of the index. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The sample firms are grouped into four sub-industries by International Standard Industrial Classification (ISIC) codes: Sub-industries ISIC (Rev 3) NO. of firms Food and beverages 15 6 Textiles and clothing 17 and 18 34 Leather and footwear 19 3 Other industries 20 - 36 23 Total 66 Other Industries in Lesotho manufacture products such as TVs and umbrellas. Scope of the data The Bureau of Statistics (BOS) surveys a sample of firms engaged in manufacturing activities in main industrial areas every quarter, using a stratified sampling procedure. The survey covers sampled firms’ production by major products, sales value, total wages and salaries paid to employees, and the number of workers by sex. The number of workers includes the temporary and permanent staff, home workers, unpaid family workers and working proprietors. Where it is not feasible to get the quantity data for production, the sales values are deflated by related Producers Price Indices of South Africa or Lesotho’s Consumer Price Indices. Given the close relationship between Lesotho and South African economies, it is assumed that the South African price trends approximate Lesotho price movements. The survey covers sampled firms’ production by major products, sales value, total wages and salaries paid to employees, and the number of workers by sex. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures The manufacturing production index aims at measuring in physical terms the production that took place in the manufacturing sector. The formula used to compile the production index is of a Laspeyres type, where the individual sub-sectors production volumes are aggregated with the weights that are based on the sales of respective sub-sectors in 1997. The use of sales revenue as the weights provides a more meaningful production index. Q t = O ( q ti /q io )* w io Where Q t = manufacturing production index in period t q ti = production volume of sub-sector i in t q io = production volume in sub-sector i in the base year 2000 w io = the share of sales revenue of sub-sector i to total sales revenue of the manufacturing sector in the base year, which is 2000.
