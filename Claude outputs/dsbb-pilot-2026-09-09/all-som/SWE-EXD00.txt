 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Analytical framework: The underlying conceptual framework for external debt statistics disseminated under the SDDS is the sixth edition of the Balance of Payments and International Investment Position Manual (BPM6) and External Debt Statistics: Guide for Compilers and Users (External Debt Guide). Quarterly gross stocks of external debt liabilities are presented under the following data categories: for each of four sectors – monetary authorities, general government, MFIs, other sectors – data are broken down by maturity (short- and long-term) on an original maturity basis and by debt instrument as specified in the BPM6 – debt securities, trade credits, loans, other debt liabilities, and intercompany loans; data on intercompany lending (liabilities) under direct investment relationships are separately identified without sector attribution. Arrears : arrears cannot be separately identified under "short-term, other debt liabilities". Classification by type of debt instrument : There are no major deviations from the concepts, definitions, and classifications outlined in the BPM6 except from the fact that currency and deposits are not compiled separately but included in loans. Deposits of nonresidents with domestic banks are included. Instruments included under "other debt liabilities": For MFI only, liabilities other than borrowing and deposits e.g., closed but not settled transactions. Trade credits owed to nonresidents are included, but data are only available for non-financial companies. Criteria used for determining whether a liability is a debt instrument or not : Gross external debt is defined as the outstanding amount of all actual current, and not contingent, liabilities that require payment(s) of principal and/or interest by the debtor at some point(s) in the future. Debt positions between residents and nonresidents : gross external debt is defined as the outstanding amount of all actual current, and not contingent, liabilities that are owed to nonresidents by Swedish residents. External debt liabilities of households and small private enterprises : external debt liabilities of households and small private enterprises are included under "other sectors." External debt liabilities of the monetary authority : external debt liabilities of the monetary authority are separately identified from the liabilities of the general government. Financial leases with nonresidents : Repurchase agreements and financial leases with nonresidents are treated as loans and included in the gross external debt position. Intercompany lending between entities in a direct investment relationship : data on intercompany lending (liabilities) under direct investment relationship are separately identified without sector attribution. Maturity classification : Data are broken down by maturity (short- and long-term) on an original maturity basis. Reclassification : major reclassifications are not identifiable through table footnotes. Sector attribution of external debt liabilities : external debt liabilities are attributed to the sector of the immediate borrower. Sector classification of external debt liabilities : External debt liabilities are classified under the sector category of the debtor. Sector disaggregation : data are available to allow disaggregation of "other sectors" into three subsectors (nonbank financial corporations, nonfinancial corporations, and NPISH), but the coverage of NPISH is lower than for other sectors. Data disaggregation into public sector external debt and publicly guaranteed private sector debt : data are not available to allow disaggregation into public sector external debt and publicly guaranteed private sector debt. Definition : statistical statement of the value and composition of the gross stock of Sweden's external debt liabilities; gross external debt is defined as the outstanding amount of all actual current, and not contingent, liabilities that require payment(s) of principal and/or interest by the debtor at some point(s) in the future and that are owed to nonresidents by Swedish residents. Relationship to international guidelines : There are no major deviations from the concepts, definitions, and classifications outlined in the BPM6. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Classification: The system used to classify external debt liabilities is the standard components according to the BPM6 . Scope of the data Coverage of external debt: Repurchase agreements and financial leases are treated as loans; as interest payments are recorded on an accruals basis in the balance of payments, entries are made on account of interest costs accrued but not yet payable. Arrears : Arrears are included in the gross external debt position. Coverage of private sector debt : The data refer to the immediate debtors' foreign position. Naturally, banks supply foreign exchange loans to the private and other sectors, refinancing themselves abroad. Such loans are attributed to the bank sector as being the immediate debtor. Coverage of short-term debt : Short-term debt covers the short-term instruments as defined in the BPM6. Debt liabilities of the offshore enterprises—including banks—that are resident in the reporting economy : Debt liabilities of the offshore enterprises—including banks—that are resident in the reporting economy are included in the gross external debt position, but offshore companies rarely occur in Sweden. Asset data for such enterprises are disseminated in the international investment position. Domestic and foreign currency debt owed to nonresidents . Data on the gross stock of Sweden's external debt include liabilities denominated in domestic and foreign currencies. External debt liabilities payable in goods and/or services : if external debt liabilities payable in goods and/or services were to occur, they would in principle be included in the external debt position. External debt transactions : data are available to identify or estimate external debt transactions, mostly on a net basis. Foreign currency-linked external debt : The split between domestic and foreign currencies is based on the contractual currencies. Intercompany lending between entities in a direct investment relationship : data on intercompany lending (liabilities) under direct investment relationship are separately identified without sector attribution. Positions in financial derivatives, equity securities and equity capital: positions in financial derivatives, equity securities, and equity capital are excluded from the gross external debt position. Securities issued in the domestic markets by residents and purchased by nonresidents : External debt include securities issued in the domestic markets by residents and purchased by nonresidents. Traded debt instruments issued by residents abroad but in hands of residents : traded debt instruments issued by residents abroad, but in hands of residents are excluded from the gross external debt position. Adjustment is made on the basis of transaction data. Geographical coverage : The data cover the gross debt liabilities of Sweden. 2.4.1 Valuation Conversion to unit of account: Debt liabilities denominated in foreign currencies are converted into Swedish kronor at the exchange rate at the end of the period. Valuation: The gross stock of external debt is valued at nominal value except for bonds and money market instruments which are recorded at market value. 2.4.2 Recording basis Recording basis: Debt position data are recorded according to the accrual principle. Outstanding liabilities arising from periodic interest costs that have accrued and are not yet payable: as interest payments are recorded on an accruals basis in the balance of payments, entries are made on account of interest costs accrued but not yet payable. Those liabilities are recorded as part of the value of the underlying debt instrument, following the accounting principles of the respondents. Valuation of tradable instruments: The gross stock of external debt is valued at nominal value except for bonds and money market instruments which are recorded at market value 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Debt securities: Sources of data for debt securities are surveys for debt securities issued abroad; survey of custodians for domestically issued debt securities held by nonresidents. Data from reference period 2021 and onwards is based on national databases Securities holding statistics and Securities issues. Trade credits: Data for trade credits is derived from an enterprise survey. Loans: The sources of data for loans are monthly and quarterly surveys. Currency and deposits: Data on currency and deposits are not collected separately, but included in loans. Other debt liabilities: The sources of data for other debt liabilities are monthly and quarterly surveys. Intercompany loans: The sources of data for intercompany loans are monthly and annual surveys of direct investors and direct investment enterprises. 3.2.1 Source data assessment Debt securities: Data from reference period 2021 and onwards is based on national databases Securities holding statistics and Securities issues. Foreign holdings in Swedish debt securities is estimated by subtracting Swedish investors' holdings from total Swedish issues of debt securities. Verification and other statistical practices: coverage of debt securities is compared against results of the creditor data published in the Coordinated Portfolio Investment Survey. Intercompany lending: liabilities to affiliated enterprises recorded on a directional basis (i.e., under direct investment abroad) in the IIP statistics are recorded in the external debt statistics. Permanent debt of branches of foreign banks is treated as external debt. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Debt securities: Data from reference period 2021 and onwards is based on national databases Securities holding statistics and Securities issues. Foreign holdings in Swedish debt securities is estimated by subtracting Swedish investors' holdings from total Swedish issues of debt securities. Verification and other statistical practices: coverage of debt securities is compared against results of the creditor data published in the Coordinated Portfolio Investment Survey. Intercompany lending: liabilities to affiliated enterprises recorded on a directional basis (i.e., under direct investment abroad) in the IIP statistics are recorded in the external debt statistics. Permanent debt of branches of foreign banks is treated as external debt. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses Statistics Sweden recently conducted an analysis of the revisions in BOP and IIP statistics. This work was also aiming at developing tools for a more systematic follow up on future revisions. This includes identifying BOP and IIP items that are systematically revised according to the measures Mean Absolute Revision (MAR) and Mean Revision (MR). This more systematic approach will potentially help identifying statistical production processes that could be improved. 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points Information about contact points is published in the form of e-mail and telephone numbers and is found on Statistics Sweden website 5.3.2 Availability of documents and services catalogs Contact information and documents regarding our statistics are available on Statistics Sweden website.
