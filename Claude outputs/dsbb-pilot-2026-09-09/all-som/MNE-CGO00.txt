 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions For the production of the statistics published in the Public Finance Bulletin , nationally developed concepts and definitions of GFS that are followed are similar to those in the GFSM 1986. The coverage of the Budget of Montenegro corresponds to the budgetary subsector in conformity with the concept of budgetary government in the GFSM 1986 and partially compliant with GFSM2014 in terms of allocating financial transactions from the scope of the deficit. Revenue and expenditure include all non-grants receipts and payments other than those associated with transactions in financial assets and liabilities; “net lending” (corresponding to the GFSM 1986 ’s lending minus repayments) is classified “above the line” and is a determinant of the surplus/deficit. Privatization receipts, which should be included in lending minus repayments according to that methodology are classified in financing (in accordance with the GFSM 2001 and ESA 2010 methodologies); grants are included in the calculation in revenues, and therefore they are included in the calculation of the deficit. The analytical framework is therefore as follows: Revenue Expenditures Surplus/Deficit equals minus Financing (domestic and foreign borrowings and privatization receipts) Detailed classification is based on the criteria of the GFSM 1986 . Revenue is broken down between tax revenue (according to the nature and base of the tax), nontax revenue and capital revenue; Expenditure is classified according to “economic” categories (compensation of employees, purchase of goods and services, interest, subsidies, social transfers and capital expenditure). Financing distinguishes between financing outflows (foreign and domestic amortization, repayments of contingent liabilities and acquisition of financial assets) and financing inflows (foreign and domestic borrowing, privatization proceeds). The fiscal year begins on January 1 and ends on December 31. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The Budget of Montenegro covers the following: direct budget users (covering the three branches of government, President’s office, ministries, parliament, judiciary, other budget agencies) and social security funds - the Pension and Disability Insurance Fund, the Health Insurance Fund, the Employment Office, the Compensation Fund and Labor Fund) Most but not all transactions are classified by using the methodology of the GFSM 1986 . Taxes are classified according to their nature and base (Personal income tax, corporate income tax, VAT, Excises, Customs, and other tax revenue); nontax revenue include property income, sales of goods and services, administrative fees and fines and penalties; capital revenue consists capital transfers receipts. Expenditure is classified according to the economic classification and distinguishes between current and capital expenditure. Current expenditure includes expenditure for employees, purchases of goods and services, interest payments, subsidies, and social transfers (for social assistance and insurance). Capital expenditure includes the acquisition of nonfinancial assets and capital transfers. Net lending (Lending minus repayments) consists of lending operations (and repayments) to other sectors of the economy for policy purposes, excluding privatization receipts as already noted. Financing is broken down between domestic and foreign financing on the basis of the residency criterion and privatization proceeds adjusted for change in deposits. 2.2.1 Scope The Budget of Montenegro covers the following: direct budget users (covering the three branches of government, President’s office, ministries, parliament, judiciary, other budget agencies) and social security funds - the Pension and Disability Insurance Fund, the Health Insurance Fund, the Employment Office, the Compensation Fund and Labor Fund) Most but not all transactions are classified by using the methodology of the GFSM 1986 . Scope of the data There is presentation of GFS for consolidated central government (including state funds). 2.4.1 Valuation Transactions are recorded on the basis of their value at the time of the underlying economic event, which, for exchange transactions, generally corresponds to market prices. Foreign currency denominated transactions are converted in domestic currency at the market exchange rates at the date of the transaction. 2.4.2 Recording basis Data are recorded on a cash basis, but according to the Law on Budget and Fiscal Responsibility arrears are only included in the calculation of the deficit in the Final Account , it is recorded a net change of obligation respectively. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Montenegro’s central government operations data are compiled on the basis of Budget of Montenegro execution reports and no specific source data collection programs are used. The data source can be considered broadly comprehensive as it relates to the central constituting entity of Montenegro’s general government. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Source data consist State budget execution reports that are produced regularly and are available from the reporting units on the next working day after the end of the reference month. 3.2.1 Source data assessment The statistical sources are the product of the budget execution process. The statistics, therefore, reflect budgetary developments and their adequacy for the production of GFS does not therefore need to be assessed. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques For the most part, the compilation of Montenegro’s GFS consists in reclassifying the information contained in comprehensive budget execution reports into the categories of the GFSM 1986. This task does not require the use of statistical techniques. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Adjustments or statistical analysis are needed for pensions and wages and other expenditures which are related to contributions. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs To the extent that totals are computed from their underlying components, there are no internal discrepancies in Montenegro’s GFS. Possible discrepancies in the source data are investigated and eliminated during the compilation process, as is the case of possible differences between the Surplus/deficit and financing. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses During the year, Ministry of Finance is obliged to prepare Analysis of consolidated public expenditure and Analysis of the government debt. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request More detailed data are available on request.
