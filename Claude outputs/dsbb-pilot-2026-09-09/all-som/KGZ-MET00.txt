 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Definitions: Exports are goods withdrawn from free circulation in the Kyrgyz Republic. These can be goods produced in the country, as well as goods originally imported from another country and processed. Imports are goods imported into the free circulation of the Kyrgyz Republic for consumption in the country and for re–export. The statistics of foreign trade in goods keep records of exports and imports based on the "whole" system of foreign trade, that is, all goods imported into or exported from an economic territory. The statistics of foreign and mutual trade in goods of the Kyrgyz Republic keeps records of exports and imports of goods based on the "common" accounting system for foreign and mutual trade, which takes into account all goods imported into or exported from the economic territory of the Republic. Statistics on foreign and mutual trade in goods are conducted in accordance with the methodological provisions of the United Nations Statistical Commission Statistics on International Trade: Concepts and Definitions. UN New York, 2010. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Classification: Trade in goods is classified according to the Classification of International Trade in Goods for the countries of the European and Asian Economic Community (TNVED European and Asian Economic Community). Compliance of national practices with international and regional guidelines: The Classification of International Trade in Goods for the countries of the European and Asian Economic Community (TNVED of the European and Asian Economic Community) has been developed on the basis of the six-digit Harmonized Commodity Description and Coding System (HS). For the economic analysis of foreign trade in goods, SITC is also used, classifying goods depending on the stage of production. 2.2.1 Scope Classification: Trade in goods is classified according to the Classification of International Trade in Goods for the countries of the European and Asian Economic Community (TNVED European and Asian Economic Community). Compliance of national practices with international and regional guidelines: The Classification of International Trade in Goods for the countries of the European and Asian Economic Community (TNVED of the European and Asian Economic Community) has been developed on the basis of the six-digit Harmonized Commodity Description and Coding System (HS). For the economic analysis of foreign trade in goods, SITC is also used, classifying goods depending on the stage of production. Scope of the data Data coverage corresponds to the General Trade Principle available in the UN publication International Trade Statistics: Concepts and Definitions. Export covers all goods exported from Kyrgyzstan (including export of goods temporarily imported for processing, goods previously imported in free circulation, re-export and export of goods from custom warehouses). Import covers goods for domestic consumption of the economy and re-exports (including temporarily imported goods for processing and imported goods for custom warehouses). Transactions coverage: Merchandise foreign statistics counts all goods that increase the stock of goods of the country and decreases it as a result of import or export. Nonmonetary gold, precious metals that are not the means of payment, securities, banknotes and coins that are not in the circulation and these items are estimated at commercial cost; Intergovernmental deliveries including export and import of goods by Governmental programs of external aid are recorded regardless of whether these goods are delivered as a technical assistance of the UN, grants, loans, barter or war reparations or restitution; Humanitarian and technical aid; Goods rented for one year and more are recorded at the full cost of the rested good as of the date of export (import); Military goods; Goods shifted as part of consignment agreements; Import of goods as a contribution in capital stocks and export of goods produced by enterprises with foreign investments; Goods used as the carriers of information and software. This category includes, for instance, packed set of diskettes and compact disks of computer software for general use (designed not for individual order) with or without instruction, as well as audio and video tapes used for commercial purposes; Returned goods. Previously exported and returned goods should be recorded as import as of the date of return (re-import). Similarly, previously imported and returned goods should be recorded as export as of the date of return (re-export); Goods crossing the border as a result of operations carried out between head corporations and enterprises they directly invest (branches/subsidiary); Bunker oil (fuel) sold on the territory of the country for foreign air planes and trucks and, accordingly, sold for domestic planes and trucks at abroad; Goods sold (purchased) in fairs, exhibitions etc carried out on the territory of the country and abroad regardless of the time of import and export; Goods (above the norm established by the national legislation in terms of value and quality) purchased by all the categories of visitors including non-resident workers. Exceptions to coverage Transit goods temporarily imported or exported (except for goods intended for domestic or external processing) do not increase or decrease the stock of goods and are not to be included in custom statistics of external trade. Goods not recorded by merchandise foreign trade statistics: Currency values (including monetary gold) in circulation used by banks as an exchange unit for settlements; Banknotes, securities and coins in circulation are not included in foreign trade statistics; Goods that are not the means of commercial operations and included in service trade: periodicals (newspapers, magazines) sent directly to subscribers; information carriers (diskettes, compact disks) with information or computer programs provided that this information was collected and computer programs were designed by order; audio and video cassettes with original records; drawings made to order etc.; Goods shifted by short-term rent contract (less than 1 year); Goods delivered, but not crossing the border: re-export without delivery into the country; domestic goods purchased by foreigners on the territory of the country and given to a citizen of that country for processing on customs territory; goods exported outside the custom territory of the country and intended for embassies, consulates, representatives under international organizations and other official representatives at abroad; goods shifted through custom border between military units of the country dislocated on custom territory of the country and outside this territory. Transport means and equipment shipped for the repair and returned after the repair; Goods delivered by way of reclamation, guarantee maintenance. 2.4.1 Valuation Assessment: In accordance with the international methodology, the cost is estimated: a) export – at FoB prices; b) import – at CiF prices. The statistical value of the goods is estimated in US dollars. 2.4.2 Recording basis Accounting time: Goods are accounted for by the date of arrival and departure from the economic territory of the country. In customs statistics, exports and imports of goods by water, rail, road and air are accounted for by the date when the customs authorities issued permits for the export or import of goods, i.e. stamps in cargo declarations. The date of export and import of goods supplied by pipeline transport, as well as electricity, is considered to be the date of issuance of the acceptance certificate at border control points or other control points of pipelines or power transmission lines. The export and import of bunker fuel sold on the territory of the Republic for foreign aircraft and trucks and, accordingly, sold for domestic aircraft and trucks abroad are recorded from the date of transfer of ownership of the delivered goods or the moment of payment. The export-import of bunker fuel is accounted for in the country of purchase (sale), that is, in the country where the company or enterprise that bought or sold the goods is located, regardless of nationality. An airplane or other heavy vehicle is considered the territory of the country to which it belongs. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Sources: After providing the country in the EAEU with data on the country's foreign trade from two databases, namely: 1) Statistics of foreign trade with the third amendments, which are compiled on the basis of information, are included in the customs declaration of cargo (TDG) and customs receipt (TC). The data of enterprises are included in these reports (p. 1-B). 2) Statistics of mutual trade with the EAEU member States - according to the report No. 1 on mutual trade "Report on mutual trade in goods by the Member States of the Eurasian Economic Union". Statistical data from official company reports have been added to this report (p. 1-B and No. 10-Animal Husbandry). In addition to this source, after the Kyrgyz Republic was introduced into the new Republic form No. 1 – "Mutual Trade", covering all business entities (legal entities and individuals) engaged in foreign trade activities with the EAEU amendment. To further expand the coverage, starting in August 2015, it is necessary to take into account a sample survey of individuals on the transportation of goods through the border points of the Kyrgyz Republic and the Republic of Kazakhstan. These data have been added to the database on trade between the EAEU member States. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Type of data collected: Information contained in Customs declarations on goods and statistical form No. 1 - mutual trade: - the direction of trade turnover - country of origin, country of destination, country of delivery - the product and its code - net weight - code and additional unit of measurement - quantity by additional unit of measurement - type of transaction - type of customs clearance - statistical significance - region. Country data base: imports relate to the country of origin and exports to the country of destination. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques The State Customs Service sends monthly to Natstatcom a database containing information on the foreign trade of the Kyrgyz Republic with third countries. The resulting database is checked and adjusted using the following methods: statistical significance; average price of exports and imports of goods; compliance of names and codes of enterprises with the data of the Unified State Register of Statistical Units (USGS), country codes of the international classification of countries, product codes, units of measurement, weights and additional units of measurement with the FEA and FEA of the EAEU; Logical control of exports and imports of certain types of goods. About 45 basic tables are compiled monthly for the republics, all countries, EAEU member states, goods, types of transactions, procedures, TNVED, HS EAEC, SITC sections, regions; monthly – for enterprises with foreign investments, small and medium-sized businesses, farms, free economic zones. The data obtained as a result of the analysis of mutual trade information were supplemented by a sample survey of individuals moving goods through checkpoints on the state border of the Kyrgyz Republic and the Republic of Kazakhstan. Customs statistics have been added to them, on the basis of which a common database on foreign trade statistics of the Kyrgyz Republic has been created. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Place of processing: The data is processed in a central computer center, where it is received from the customs authority. Product Information: The goods are available at the 5-digit and 9-digit code levels according to the Foreign Trade Nomenclature (CNETA). The level of the 6-digit code coincides with the 6-digit encoding and classification of HS. Seasonal adjustment: The data is not seasonally adjusted. None
