 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The quarterly Index of Industrial Production (QIPS) measures changes in the level of production by various sectors in industry. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The establishments are classified and the data tabulated at the 4-digit level of ISIC rev.2. Scope of the data The index covers mining and quarrying, manufacturing, and electricity. None 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Over time, the index has been affected by closures of sampled establishments and non-inclusion of new establishments in the sample. Consequently a new, expanded sample was introduced in 2000. Currently, the data for the compilation of the index are collected from 200 business establishments in the provinces with high economic activity (Lusaka, Copperbelt, Southern, Central, Eastern). Data are collected on the number of wage and salary earners; total quarterly wages and salaries paid out; quantities of main products sold; quantities produced; expected production and maximum quarterly production capacity. The establishments covered by the index collection are from the formal sector. The response rate is about 75 percent. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques The Laspeyres formula is used to calculate the index. The weights for the current index introduced as of 2000 are the sales/turnover values for the year 2000. For the earlier period the weights were based on the census of industry for 1980 and were value added weights at the industry level. The two periods have not been linked to provide a longer time series. The weighting profile used in constructing the index currently is derived from the turnovers of the sampled establishments and is as follows: Mining and Quarrying (0.350); Manufacturing (0.511); and Electricity (0.139). The sectors covered in the manufacturing industry with their weights are: Food and Beverages and Tobacco (0.235); Textile and Clothing (0.060); Wood and Wood products (0.006); Paper and Paper Products (0.017); Chemicals, Rubber and Plastics (0.059); Non-metallic Mineral Products (0.025); Basic Metal Industries (0.009); and Fabricated Metal Industries and Other (0.100). 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures None
