 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The Central Bank of the Dominican Republic (BCRD), as established in Article 15 of the Monetary and Financial Law 183-02, is responsible for compiling, processing and publishing statistics on the balance of payments, monetary sector, Real Sector and Financial and other steps necessary for the fulfillment of their duties. The BCRD, only the International Department is responsible to compile, consolidate, process and analyze the international investment position (IIP) of the country. International investment position (PII) statistics are prepared by the International Department (DI) of the BCRD in accordance with the Balance of Payments Manual ( BPM5 ) of the International Monetary Fund (IMF), from 2004-2013 statistics were developed and presented under the recommended in the fifth edition of the Balance of Payments Manual methodology used (BPM5 ). From 2014 will be prepared and submitted in accordance with the recommendations of the sixth edition of the Balance of Payments and International Investment Position (BPM6). The Central Bank of the Dominican Republic (BCRD) compiles and disseminates the international investment position (PII) for the Dominican Republic in millions of U.S. dollars. Analytical Framework: The IIP is compiled by the Central Bank of the Dominican Republic (BCRD), and generally complies with the provisions of the Fifth edition of the IMF Balance of Payments Manual (BPM5). The concepts and definitions are in line with IMF recommendations. The IIP registered transactions are consistent with the items of the financial account of the balance of payments. Data are available on the website of BCRD annually from the year 2005 and quarterly from 2011. The PII is reported to the Department of Statistics of the IMF for publication. Definition: The International Investment Position (IIP) is the balance sheet holdings of financial assets and liabilities of an economy's external at a given time. Its evolution over a period determined by the financial transactions of the balance of payments, they do vary the volume of assets and liabilities, by differences in the valuation of stocks due to changes in prices or exchange rates and other adjustments, which consist mainly of reclassifications of external assets and liabilities. Determining asset or liability is always from the perspective of a right or obligation of residents to nonresidents of the Dominican economy. The difference between the external financial assets and liabilities of an economy’s net IIP, which can be positive or negative. When positive means that the local economy is a net external asset, if the situation is negative net debtor to the rest of the world. External Assets: includes rights or financial holdings Dominican residents to nonresidents, while external liabilities consist of “liabilities issued by residents “held by non-residents. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The IIP statement released on the website of BCRD and other economic reports published by the institution and evolution shows the balance of assets and liabilities according to the BPM5 standard components. Ranked assets and liabilities broken down by functional category: • Foreign Direct Investment. • Portfolio investment • Derivative financial instruments, and • other investment, and reserve assets. By institutional sector Monetary Authorities, Central Government, Banks and Other Sectors. Scope of the data Coverage of data The IIP data include the balances of assets and liabilities between residents and non-residents of the Dominican economy, in accordance with criteria established residence in BPM5, so its manufacture can meet the standards and recommendations of the IMF on residence. However, there are difficulties in covering some transactions in the financial account, foreign investment, corporate bonds and other assets and liabilities to nonresidents The geographical coverage covers the entire national territory. No asset or financial liability is not excluded intentionally. Is a resident of the Dominican economy, any natural person or legal entity whose center of economic interest is in the Dominican Republic and its economic activity is subject to applicable tax laws, and their stay in the country is for a period of a year or more, respecting the objections raised in the manuals of the IMF. Overall this is to use the accrual method and market prices. In the case of the monetary authority, foreign currency transactions are translated into Dominican pesos and the balances of assets and liabilities are revalued daily, according to the exchange rates resulting from the weighted average of all transactions for the purchase or sale made ​​in market by authorized intermediaries, as provided in Section 11 of the Exchange Rules of the Monetary and Financial Law 183-02 dated November 22, 2002. Balances originally valued in Special Drawing Rights (SDRs) are converted using the exchange rate that the International Monetary Fund (IMF) determined. The calculation Gold revaluation is performed based on the market price of London, England, at the close of the day preceding the date of filing of the Financial Statements of BCRD, established according to the 6th. Resolution of 3 June 2003. The assets and liabilities of the financial system are obtained from the information reported by the institutions balances the CBDR purposes monetary and financial accounts. Such amounts are valued using the exchange rate at the date that corresponds, daily established by the institution. In the case of foreign direct investment, transactions and balances are reported in U.S. dollars and are, in some cases, the accumulation of flows in the balance of payments, and reports provided by companies to BCRD through forms requesting information that is applied. Data assets and liabilities are nominal and historical values ​​recorded in books of companies. With regard to private sector debt, transactions and balances are reported in the original currency in which it was agreed the operation, information is collected via forms requesting information that is applied. In the case of assets and liabilities in different currencies the unit of account, with the exception of balances denominated in SDRs are converted with the average exchange rate for the period for transactions and balances the exchange rate is used to end of period. With regard to investment stocks portfolio consisting mainly in the case of liabilities, for the bonds issued by the General Government and by the private sector, information is supplied to denominations and for the development of the PII these emissions are valued at market prices. Portfolio investment in the financial sector (banks) is valued at market prices and non-financial private sector is at nominal value and historical cost. With respect to assets, the valuation is made as to liabilities. Other investment: In the case of the financial sector (banks) the information supplied in assets and liabilities are valued at market prices. The details of the non-financial sector correspond to the information received by BIS deposits abroad of resident non-bank sector. The information in the external debt of the monetary authorities, general government, banks and other sectors is compiled nominal and historical value, both short and long term. Reserve Assets: In the case of foreign securities that are part of the international reserves are revalued daily, according to the exchange rates resulting from the weighted average of all the buying or selling in the market made ​​by the authorized intermediaries, as provided in Article 11 of the Foreign Exchange Regulation 183-02 of the Monetary and Finance dated 22 November 2002 Act. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs To prepare of the international investment position, the International Department compiles information from different sources across the forms (monthly, quarterly and annually, as appropriate) that refers to the various public and private institutions, international organizations, Bank for International Settlements (BIS) as well as the CBDR own records, as detailed below. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Specific items: • Direct investment: Data on assets and liabilities are obtained through the forms that send the quarterly Balance of Payments Branch of the International Department businesses.
