 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The definition of international reserves follows, in principle, the recommendations of the sixth edition of the Balance of Payments Manual ( BPM6 ).Foreign assets are defined in accordance with the BPM6 residency criteria. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The stock of holdings of foreign assets (end of year data) are disseminated in the CBU Annual Report (Table 3-2, Investment of the Central Bank's Foreign Assets), broken down by the following categories: Central Bank’s Investments in Securities, Premium Bonds and Treasury Bills; Deposit Accounts and Cash with Banks Abroad; and Other Foreign Assets. 2.2.1 Scope The stock of holdings of foreign assets (end of year data) are disseminated in the CBU Annual Report (Table 3-2, Investment of the Central Bank's Foreign Assets), broken down by the following categories: Central Bank’s Investments in Securities, Premium Bonds and Treasury Bills; Deposit Accounts and Cash with Banks Abroad; and Other Foreign Assets. Scope of the data Components of international reserve assets that are disseminated are the stock of foreign assets, which also includes SDR Holdings and Reserve Tranche Position with IMF, and gold holdings. Exceptions to coverage None. 2.4.1 Valuation Securities are considered to be held until maturity and are valued at their acquisition cost. Deposits in foreign exchange are revalued at the exchange rate prevailing at the time of reporting. In order to convert data on foreign currency denominated assets into their national currency equivalents, end-of-period market exchange rates are utilized. 2.4.2 Recording basis International reserves are disseminated on an accrual basis. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures CBU foreign assets are disseminated on a gross basis. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The Financial Control Department at the CBU & Statistics Division in the Research & Statistics Department compiles the data on foreign assets based on information in the CBU balance sheet. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Source data comprise foreign positions vis-à-vis different institutional sectors (CBU balance sheet). 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Twenty days after the end of the reference period. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques No adjustments are made to the source data. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Data are not adjusted.
