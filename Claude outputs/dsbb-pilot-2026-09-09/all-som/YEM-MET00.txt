 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The CSO follows the Standard International Trade Classification (SITC, rev.2), while the Customs Authority uses the 1996 Harmonized System. Scope of the data 2.4.1 Valuation Imports are recorded c.i.f. while exports are recorded f.o.b. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Data on exports of crude oil are obtained from the Ministry of Oil and Mineral Resources (MOMR), and data on imports and re-exports of oil products from Aden Refinery. The Customs Authority also compiles foreign trade data, but the coverage of exports and re-exports is not comprehensive because it does not include exports of crude oil and imports and re-exports of oil products.
