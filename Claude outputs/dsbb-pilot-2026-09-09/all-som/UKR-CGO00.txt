 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Central Government data compilation is carried out in line with the "Special Data Dissemination Standard: guide for subscribers and users" 2013 (IMF), as well as according to the "Government Finance Statistics Manual" 2014 (IMF). 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization For Government Finance Statistics compilation is used national budget classification, approved by the Ministry of Finance of Ukraine according to the art.8 of Budget Code of Ukraine. National budget classification consists of: Revenue budget classification (divided into tax and non-tax revenue, receipts from capital operations, transfers, special targets funds); Expenditure and lending budget classification : functional expenditure and lending/borrowing classification; agent-by-agent expenditure and lending/borrowing classification; economic classification of expenditure; program classification of expenditure and lending/borrowing; budget lending/borrowing classification; Budget financing classification : financing by creditor type; financing by type of debt instruments; Debt classification : debt classification by creditor type; debt classification by debt instrument. Budget classification approaches the standards of the Government Finance Statistics Manual 2014 (IMF). 2.2.1 Scope For Government Finance Statistics compilation is used national budget classification, approved by the Ministry of Finance of Ukraine according to the art.8 of Budget Code of Ukraine. National budget classification consists of: Revenue budget classification (divided into tax and non-tax revenue, receipts from capital operations, transfers, special targets funds); Expenditure and lending budget classification : functional expenditure and lending/borrowing classification; agent-by-agent expenditure and lending/borrowing classification; economic classification of expenditure; program classification of expenditure and lending/borrowing; budget lending/borrowing classification; Budget financing classification : financing by creditor type; financing by type of debt instruments; Debt classification : debt classification by creditor type; debt classification by debt instrument. Budget classification approaches the standards of the Government Finance Statistics Manual 2014 (IMF). Scope of the data Institutional coverage of Central Government sector is comprehensive. It comprises Central Government units financed from the State Budget of Ukraine. Exceptions to coverage Starting April, 2014 budgetary and financial reporting do not cover data for temporarily occupied by the russian federation territories of Ukraine. Unrecorded activity Not applicable. 2.4.1 Valuation State treasury authorities provide accounting and reporting in national currency - Hryvnia. Operations denominated in foreign currency are reflected in reports in national currency by recalculating foreign currency amount using National Bank of Ukraine exchange rate. 2.4.2 Recording basis Budget execution transactions are recorded on cash basis with accrual elements for spending units' liabilities. The fiscal year is the same as the calendar year. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures Data are recorded on a gross basis. Tax repayment is deducted from revenue. Financing operations are recorded on a net basis. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Input data is comprehensive. According to Budget Code of Ukraine reports on state budget execution on revenue, expenditure, lending/borrowing, financing and debt are prepared by the State Treasury Service of Ukraine and provide the Ministry of Finance of Ukraine in due time. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Data definitions, coverage and classifications are presented in the Budget Code of Ukraine, in the MoF order №11 of 14.01.2011 (as amended) «Budget Classification» and other regulatory acts. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness State budget execution reports, pension and social security funds' execution reports are provided within the time specified in budget regulations. Monthly reports on state budget execution are available not later than the 25th day of the month following the reporting one. Quarterly reports on budget execution are available not later than the 35th day after the end of the reporting quarter. Availability of final annual budget execution report may change over time – after submitting the execution report of the Law on State Budget of Ukraine to the Verkhovna Rada, which is not later than the 1st of April of the year following the reporting one. Quarterly reports on state social and pension security funds' budget execution are available not later than the 55th day after the end of the reporting period, annual - no later than the 15th of April of the year following the reporting one. However, fallowing the full-scale military aggression of the russian federation against Ukraine there were amendments made to the Budget Code of Ukraine in March, 2022 to suspend the deadlines observation for monthly, quarterly and annual budget reporting during martial law is imposed in Ukraine. 3.2.1 Source data assessment Statistical data collection system is automated and provides control over the data on each stage of its preparing. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques There is national cash data reclassification to be in line with international classifications and to meet GFS 2014 IMF Manual requirements. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures There is a data consolidation between state and local budgets which means elimination of all transactions between mentioned above unities. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results Intermediate data are adjusted according to the quarterly and annual reporting. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data Intermediate data are subject to logical and formal verification. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs Detected statistical discrepancy are analyzed and solved. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses There is statistical data analysis during the year to be in line with international standards concerning the methodology of government finance statistics compilation. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses Disseminated times series data and metadata are revised annually and adjusted where necessary. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request Data can be obtained on official request through the contact person. 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points Information on contact persons is available on the website of the Ministry of Finance of Ukraine in the "Special Data Dissemination Standard IMF (SDDS IMF)" section ( https://mof.gov.ua/en/specialnij-standart-poshirennja-danih-mvf-sspd-mvf-statistika-za-gfs m ). 5.3.2 Availability of documents and services catalogs Not applicable.
