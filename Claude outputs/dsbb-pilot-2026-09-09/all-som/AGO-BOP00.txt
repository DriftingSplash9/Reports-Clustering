 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The Balance of Payments (BOP) of Angola covers all the economic transactions of the country's residents with the rest of the world during quarter. The BOP of Angola is compiled in US Dollars by the Statistics Department of the National Bank of Angola (BNA), according to the 6th Edition of the Balance of Payments and International of Investment Position Manual (BPM6) of the International Monetary Fund (IMF). Transactions are recorded based on the commitment criterion ("accrual basis") consistent with the concepts of BPM6. The data are compiled quarterly with monthly breakdown and published quarterly. The information for the BOP are obtained from different sources, namely, Government Institutions, Debt Management and Financial Analysis System (DMFAS), Commercial Banks, General Tax Administration (AGT), Financial Institution Portal (PIF), Balance Sheets for Monetary Accounts (2SR standard report forms), Integrated System of Financial Management of the State (SIGFE), International Air Transport Association (IATA), Bank for International Settlements (BIS), Public and Private Companies, Institute for the Promotion and Coordination of Community Aid (IPROCAC) and so on. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The BOP classification/sectorization of Angola basically conforms to the BPM6. Scope of the data The overall scope and coverage of the BOP statistics are broadly consistent with the guidelines presented in the BPM6. On principle, the BOP covers all transactions between residents and non-residents in the economy. On practice, it covers the main current and financial transactions carried out, albeit with difficulties regarding data on travels, telecommunications, computer, and information services and others. Exceptions to coverage The geographic coverage spans the territory of the Republic of Angola, according to the BPM6 definition of economic territory. There isn't any exception, in term of coverage. Unrecorded activity No informal or illegal activity is recorded in the Balance of Payment of Angola. 2.4.1 Valuation The Balance of Payments of Angola is compiled in US Dollars. The data reported in domestic currency are converted into US Dollars at the average exchange rate for the period in which the transaction took place. The transactions are valued at market prices. Merchandise imports and exports are valued on FOB basis. 2.4.2 Recording basis The time of recording is the time when the economic value that is the object of the transaction is created, transformed, exchanged, transferred, or extinguished. In case of imports and exports, it is the time when the merchandise crosses the border. Some data are recorded on a cash basis. Although data on both accrual and cash basis are available for some transactions from the accounting records. Goods are recorded at the time they cross the border; Services are recorded at the time the service is rendered; Financial transactions are largely recorded on accrual basis. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures The procedures that we use are broadly consistent with internationally accepted standards, following the recommendations of the BPM6. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The BOP is compiled from various sources including administrative records, account balances, enterprise surveys and information from government and private institutions: Merchandise exports and imports Export data are collected mainly through the surveys requested from enterprises of the oil, diamond and other export sectors, and their information is validated by the ministries responsible for such companies and institutions. The data on imports are obtained from online access to the Integrated System of Customs (SIADU/SIG) and ASYCUDA's system of General Tax Administration (AGT). They are excluded from the total imports of imports by embassies. Services The information is obtained mainly from surveys requested from oil, diamonds, insurance, airlines, services providers and construction enterprises and by online access to the Integrated Financial Management System (SIGFE). The relevant headings are "transportation, travel, insurance, royalties, rights and licenses, and other government services n.i.e." Primary income Information is obtained through online access to DMFAS and surveys of oil companies (compensation of employees and direct investment) and the commercial banks. Since 2000, estimates of income from the foreign assets of the nonfinancial sector have been included, based on data from the Bank for International Settlements. Secondary income The Grants received from foreign governments and institutions are recorded based on information obtained from Institute for the Promotion and Coordination of Community Aid (IPROCAC). The remittances inflows and outflows are recorded from the the information reported by commercial banks and the surveys (companies). Capital transfers Debt forgiveness granted by creditors to the government of Angola is recorded based on information obtained from the external debt records. Investment Information on direct and portfolio investment in Angola is obtained from surveys of oil and diamond companies. The data on other investment are obtained from oil company surveys, monetary surveys of the banking system and external debt records extracted from DMFAS, BIS and so on. Reserve assets These data are compiled from the monetary statistics produced by the BNA (Monetary and Financial Statistics Division and Reserve Management Department). 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording The BNA use several sources of information to compile the BOP, including surveys directed to various companies. The surveys format and instructions used by the BNA were prepared in collaboration with an IMF team of consultants. The surveys cover all accounts of the BOP and the instructions provide guidance to ensure that data reported approximate the BOP requirements. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness In general the timeliness of the source data needs to be improved although in general it allows to meet the GDDS recommendations. 3.2.1 Source data assessment The Data collected for the BOP are cross checked with information reported from the line ministries and other sources of information alternatives. Discrepancies are analyzed and the results are used to improve data collection. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Because of some lack of information about some kind of services and freight and insurance of goods, is used some techniques to estimate those information. Example, for the valuation of freight and insurance of goods, 17% of the imports fob is considered as freight and 1% of that value is considered as insurance. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Seasonal adjustments are not made​​ in the BOP of Angola. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results It is observed. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data It is observed. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs It is observed 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses It is not observed 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses Not applicable 5.1.5 Dissemination on request It is observed. 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points The Statistics Department contact information (telephone numbers) is disseminated in the Statistical Bulletin.
