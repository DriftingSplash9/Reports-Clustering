 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The Manufacturing IPI includes a comprehensive survey of all the economic activities of the manufacturing sector. To measure monthly production performance, various variables are used: output in physical units, sales in physical units, use of inputs for manufacturing products, apparent consumption, man-hours in the production process and deflated sales at current prices. These variables generate individual production indices for each subclass of the manufacturing industry. As a whole, all of the selected variables provide monthly data on more than 5,000 manufacturing industry establishments (a non-probabilistic panel). 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization In order to make the construction of the general level of the index and the results of each branch of economic activity of the Manufacturing IPI compatible with the estimates of the production account of the System of National Accounts, the International Standard Industrial Classification of All Economic Activities Revision 3.1 (ISIC Rev3.1) is used. Scope of the data The geographic scope of the data is national, and the available series start from January 2016. The Manufacturing IPI is calculated with an aggregation of individual indices to produce the general level, using the weights of the gross value added at basic prices of the manufacturing industry, base year 2004=100, according to the revision of the National Directorate of National Accounts. The methodology used to calculate each subclass is included in the methodology for the manufacturing industrial production index (Manufacturing IPI), available at https://www.indec.gob.ar/ftp/cuadros/economia/metodologia_ipi_manufacturero_ingles.pdf 2.4.1 Valuation The Manufacturing IPI provides sector data on the performance of the manufacturing industry's output volume. 2.4.2 Recording basis Data are collected monthly. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The data sources for the Manufacturing IPI are surveys to manufacturers, data produced by various public agencies and INDEC databases of the basic producer price index (IPP) and foreign trade. Additionally, data provided by business chambers and associations are used as a source. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Weights are updated with the results of the National Economic Census (usually held every 10 years), and/or with the results of special undertakings such as an output/input matrix. The Manufacturing IPI base year is 2004. The weighting pattern is based on the value-added by each sector upon the industry aggregate in 2004, measured in basic prices (net of internal and oil taxes). The weights for the Manufacturing IPI by activity code can be found in the methodology for the manufacturing industrial production index (Manufacturing IPI), available at https://www.indec.gob.ar/ftp/cuadros/economia/metodologia_ipi_manufacturero_ingles.pdf 5.1.5 Dissemination on request Only released data are available to users.
