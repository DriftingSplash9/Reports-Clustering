 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Definition: Government debt presents the comprehensive stock of recognized, direct liabilities of the government to the rest of the economy and the world as defined in Public sector debt statistics guide , IMF, 2011; (PSDG). 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Classification: As prescribed by the SDDS+, the general government debt data are broken down by the following characteristics: by type of instrument, by currency of denomination and by residence of the creditor. The breakdown of debt by residence is compiled according to the residency criteria which are consistent with the IMF's Balance of Payments Manual, Sixth Edition. The breakdown of debt by instrument is according to PSDG. 2.2.1 Scope Classification: As prescribed by the SDDS+, the general government debt data are broken down by the following characteristics: by type of instrument, by currency of denomination and by residence of the creditor. The breakdown of debt by residence is compiled according to the residency criteria which are consistent with the IMF's Balance of Payments Manual, Sixth Edition. The breakdown of debt by instrument is according to PSDG. Scope of the data Scope of the data Data cover consolidated general government sector 2.4.1 Valuation Valuation practices: The debt is valued at nominal value in the following way The domestic debt (including government marketable securities), linked to the Consumer Price Index or to the foreign exchange rate, is revalued at the end of the reported period according to the change of the CPI or the exchange rate as relevant and includes the premium/discount and accrued interest to be paid in redemption. The foreign-currency-denominated debt is valued, using the representative market exchange rates. 2.4.2 Recording basis The debt is recorded according to the accrual accounting principles. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures Gross vs. net: The debt data are recorded on a gross basis and consolidated according to the PSDG instructions. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The detailed data on the central government domestic currency debt are provided by the Government Debt Management Unit (GDMU) of the Ministry of Finance. The source of the data for the foreign currency central government debt is the Bank of Israel (BoI). The data on domestic currency debt held by non-residents and data on foreign debt held by Israelis abroad are provided by BoI to compile the breakdowns of debt by the residence of the creditor The data of the local government debt are derived from the quarterly financial reports of the local authorities to the Ministry of the Interior. The data on the public non-profit institutions are estimated using the quarterly financial data from the counterpart sectors combined with the annual financial reports of the institutions. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording The net issuance figures given are net changes in the stock of the given debt elements from previous year to the current year. This also includes the change in value resulting from the movement of the exchange rates in the case of foreign currency debt elements and other changes such as debt assumptions. 3.2.1 Source data assessment Revisions for past years’ quarterly data, when necessary, are made once a year. During the current year, revisions are made on an ongoing basis, as necessary. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Consolidation: The consolidation was applied on the following items: government debt held by the National Insurance Institute, internal funds managed by the Treasury and loans provided to the local authorities and public NPI's by the government.
