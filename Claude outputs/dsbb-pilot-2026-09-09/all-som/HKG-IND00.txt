 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The compilation of industrial production indices in Hong Kong makes close reference to the guidelines set out in the International Recommendations for the Index of Industrial Production (draft) 2009 published by the United Nations Statistics Division. Index of industrial production is a measure of change in the volume of production for all establishments in manufacturing industries and sewerage, waste management and remediation activities in Hong Kong. The index uses a chained Laspeyres formula. Sales data are used as weights for aggregating production indices at product level into indices at industry level. The industry value added of the respective industries is used when further aggregating the production indices at industry level into higher industry groupings. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Establishments are classified according to the Hong Kong Standard Industrial Classification (HSIC) which is based on the International Standard Industrial Classification (ISIC) with local adaptation to cater for the specific economic structure of Hong Kong. HSIC Version 2.0 (HSIC V2.0) has been adopted to replace HSIC V1.1 as from the reference quarter of the first quarter of 2009. An industry-based product classification is also adopted. The list of products is compiled based on the results of consultation with major producers and data users. 2.2.1 Scope Establishments are classified according to the Hong Kong Standard Industrial Classification (HSIC) which is based on the International Standard Industrial Classification (ISIC) with local adaptation to cater for the specific economic structure of Hong Kong. HSIC Version 2.0 (HSIC V2.0) has been adopted to replace HSIC V1.1 as from the reference quarter of the first quarter of 2009. An industry-based product classification is also adopted. The list of products is compiled based on the results of consultation with major producers and data users. Scope of the data Industrial coverage: All local establishments in manufacturing industries and sewerage, waste management and remediation activities . Product coverage: Only primary products of establishments are covered. A total of about 700 products are covered. 2.4.1 Valuation Deflated production values, i.e. production values deflated by the producer price indices of the corresponding products produced by the sampled establishments are used in compiling the Index of Industrial Production. 2.4.2 Recording basis Data are collected quarterly from the sampled establishments. Surveys are conducted right after the end of each reference quarter and span over about two months' time. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures The quantity/deflated production value relatives (i.e. ratio of production of product/service for the reference quarter to the average production of the same product/service in the preceding year) of different products/services of an industry are aggregated using the relative sales values of the products/services concerned as obtained in the latest round of the Annual Survey of Economic Activities. Indices at the industry level are further aggregated, using the industry value added of the industries concerned as weights, to form indices at industry grouping level, and successively of industry section level. Figures pertaining to the industry value added of industries concerned are also obtained from the latest round of the Annual Survey of Economic Activities. The above result, being an index at the industry level with previous year as base, is converted to an index with year 2015 as reference period by the method of chaining. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The data for compiling the production index are obtained from the Quarterly Survey of Industrial Production. As from the first quarter of 2009, the sample covers about 700 establishments, including all those in manufacturing industries engaging 100 or more persons as well as those in sewerage, waste management and remediation activities engaging 50 or more persons and a sample of smaller establishments in the manufacturing and waste management sectors. In compiling the index, quantity indicators are used for some products, while production values deflated by producer price indices are used in cases where production is heterogeneous. The weights for the aggregation of production indices at various industry levels are derived from data from the latest Annual Survey of Economic Activities. Sources of weights: Sales values from the latest Annual Survey of Economic Activities. Sample size of the annual survey is about 700. Product selection: The list of product is compiled based on the results of consultation with major manufacturers and data users. Establishment selection: All establishments are covered in manufacturing industries and sewerage, waste management and remediation activities. Basically, systematic sampling with stratification by industry by employment size is applied. Establishments in manufacturing industries engaging 100 or more persons as well as those in sewerage, waste management and remediation activities engaging 50 or more persons are selected as certainties. Sample sizes: About 700 establishments are sampled and around 700 product volumes/product values (which will be deflated by relevant producer price indices) are collected. Data collection methods: Questionnaires are mailed to sampled establishments. Data are collected by post, supplemented by telephone or face-to-face enumeration and verification where necessary. Data recorded in paper forms are input into computer for further processing. An electronic template of the questionnaire is also available for respondents upon request. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Quantity indicators are used for some products, while production values deflated by producer price indices are used in cases where the product is heterogeneous in nature. A well-structured questionnaire is adopted in the Quarterly Survey of Industrial Production to collect the required data objectively and accurately. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness The Quarterly Survey of Industrial Production is conducted on a quarterly basis, with each calendar quarter taken as an individual survey cycle. Enumerators working in the survey are required to complete data collection for a reference quarter within two months after the end of that reference quarter. 3.2.1 Source data assessment A high response rate of about 80% is achieved for each quarterly survey cycle. Adequate arrangements are made to follow up those non-contacted and refusal establishments, e.g. non-contact letters are left in the letter boxes of those non-contacted establishments after each visit so as to increase the chance of establishing contacts with them; also, experienced enumerators are deployed to re-visit the refusal establishments with a view to persuading them to co-operate in the survey. A detailed instruction manual is available to ensure coherence in data collection by enumerators. Enumerators are also required to carry out on-the-spot checking for key variables during the data collection process, so that major inconsistencies and significant changes in reported data could be verified with respondents immediately. To ensure the quality of the data collected, an elaborate fieldwork quality control system is established. Key fieldwork control statistics for individual field teams are compiled and reviewed regularly. To further safeguard the quality of the collected data, completed questionnaires are subject to manual and computer editing processes through checking against a set of well-defined validation rules. Dubious cases are verified by enumerators with the relevant respondents. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Procedures for non-response: Non-response cases are cases where the selected establishments (which are still in operation) can be located but the required data cannot be collected from them. Contributions of non-response cases are normally estimated by the average of reporting cases in the corresponding stratum. Introducing new products: Subject to results of consultation with major manufacturers and data users in the annual review. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Nature of weights: Sales values in aggregating products and industry value added in aggregating industries. Period of index weights currently used: Reporting period of the latest available Annual Survey of Economic Activities. In general, when compiling industrial production indices for the four reference quarters of year T, the results of the annual survey in respect of the reference year T-2 are used as weights. The preferred reporting period for the annual survey in respect of a reference year is the 12 months ending December, but respondents are allowed to report data covering any 12 month period between January 1 of that reference year and March 31 of the following year. Frequency of weight updates: Annual. Computation of lowest level indices: Weighted arithmetic average of quantity relatives or deflated value relatives. Aggregation: Chain index formula is adopted. The basic item quantity relatives or deflated value relatives are aggregated, using weights from the latest Annual Survey of Economic Activities, to obtain component, group, and total indices. The results, being indices with the preceding year as base, are converted to production indices with 2015 as reference year by the method of chaining. Linking of re-weighted index to historical index: Not applicable as a chain index formula is adopted and the weights are updated annually. Reference period (the period when the published index = 100): 2015. Seasonally adjusted indices: The seasonally adjusted Index of Industrial Production is compiled by the X-12 ARIMA method. The X-12 ARIMA method and its related versions are the standard method commonly employed in compiling seasonally adjusted data series. The method can be used to detect whether outlier(s) is present in the original series for estimation of seasonal factors. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results Intermediate results derived for each reference quarter are cross-checked against the historical time series data at detailed breakdown level. Any significant changes from the past trend are investigated. Where applicable, they are also checked against other relevant statistical indicators showing the prevailing economic conditions and performance of manufacturing industries and sewerage, waste management and remediation activities in Hong Kong. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data Reported production information is subject to thorough checking by office staff and detailed validation checks by computer before tabulation. Such checking includes completeness of entries, consistency among data items and creditability of reported data. Dubious cases are followed up by telephone enquiry or by field verification visit. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs Weights newly compiled are compared with weights of previous year. Industry groupings and products with abnormal changes in weights are further studied to ensure the accuracy of the weights. References have been made with publications of UN and other statistical authorities in setting estimation and aggregation formulae. The computing system for compiling industrial production indices was fully tested before implementation thus securing the reliability of the compilation processes. Manual checking of aggregation of selected products and industry groupings' indices is also performed. Industry groupings with changes inconsistent with those of their component industries are further analysed. References are made to other relevant statistics such as those on domestic exports to help identify any irregularities during the compilation processes. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses As from the first quarter of 2009, HSIC V2.0 has been adopted and the reference year has been changed from 2000 to 2008. To facilitate comparison, indices from 2005 to 2008 have been re-compiled according to HSIC V2.0 with reference year changed to 2008. Starting from the second quarter of 2019, indices have been re-referenced, taking year 2015 as the new reference period. Relevant statistics are released on the C&SD website ( https://www.censtatd.gov.hk/en/ ). 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses Revision of provisional figures is due to the availability of more data for index compilation. The discrepancy between the provisional figures and revised figures is generally small. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request Requests for additional statistics which are not available from the existing tabulations will be considered on a case-by-case basis having regard to such factors as the nature of the request, data relevance and precision, and data confidentiality. Where applicable and in consultation with users, customised tabulations will be provided at a charge under the cost recovery principle. 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points Details of contact for enquiries, including phone number, fax number, email address and office address, are clearly given in the survey report Quarterly Index of Industrial Production for Manufacturing and Waste Management Sectors and on the C&SD website ( https://www.censtatd.gov.hk/en/ ). The information is readily available to the public. Prompt and knowledgeable support is provided in accordance with the performance pledge of the C&SD. The timeliness of response to enquiries from the public is regularly monitored. 5.3.2 Availability of documents and services catalogs The availability of the survey report Quarterly Index of Industrial Production for Manufacturing and Waste Management Sectors is publicised in the quarterly press releases for disseminating the updated Index of Industrial Production. Such information is also available on the C&SD website ( https://www.censtatd.gov.hk/en/ ). Lists of publications, feature articles and other services available are also obtainable from the C&SD website ( https://www.censtatd.gov.hk/en/page_1273.html ).
