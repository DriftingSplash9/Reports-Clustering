 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The concepts and definitions comply with the sixth edition of the International Monetary Fund's Balance of Payments and International Investment Position Manual (BPM6) and the International Monetary Fund's External Debt Statistics: Guide for Compilers and Users, 2013. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The classification and sectorization comply with the sixth edition of the International Monetary Fund's Balance of Payments and International Investment Position Manual (BPM6) and External Debt Statistics: Guide for Compilers and Users, 2013. 2.2.1 Scope The classification and sectorization comply with the sixth edition of the International Monetary Fund's Balance of Payments and International Investment Position Manual (BPM6) and External Debt Statistics: Guide for Compilers and Users, 2013. Scope of the data In general all debt-related liabilities of residents of Latvia with the rest of the world are recorded. Exceptions to coverage No geographical units are excluded. 2.4.1 Valuation Market prices are the basis of the valuation. Nominal value as defined by the European System of Accounts 2010 (ESA 2010) is used for the currency and deposits, loans and other accounts receivable/payable. 2.4.2 Recording basis Recording is based on an accrual basis. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Data for general government sector are obtained from the Treasury and administrative data sources. Information on central bank sector is derived from the balance sheet of Latvijas Banka. Monetary Financial Institutions (MFIs; excl. central bank) sector is covered by MFI statistics compiled by Latvijas Banka. Data collection system is based on the surveys for financial corporations (excl. MFI) and non-financial corporations and households sector. In addition, non-bank external payment data (ITRS) and data on supervision statistics compiled by Latvijas Banka are used. Quarterly surveys conducted by Latvijas Banka: For non-financial corporations: Survey on foreign assets and liabilities covering data on positions at the beginning and end of a period and changes in the relevant positions (transactions, price changes, exchange rate changes, other adjustments) for the compilation of foreign direct investment in Latvia and abroad, assets and liabilities between fellow enterprises, other investment (assets and liabilities), income flows related to foreign assets and liabilities; all items are shown in the breakdown by country; stratified simple random sampling is applied. For financial corporations: Survey on assets and liabilities (balance sheet items in breakdown by country, counterpart sector, currency and maturity, non-resident investor category: direct investor, fellow enterprises, direct investment company and other investor); cut-off sampling is applied. Data on portfolio investment covering assets and liabilities for all sectors are derived from the data on holders of securities (data reported on a security-by-security basis by securities custodians) compiled by Latvijas Banka and the Centralised Securities Database of the European Central Bank. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording The definitions, scope, sectorization, classifications, valuation and time of recording comply with the sixth edition of the International Monetary Fund's Balance of Payments and International Investment Position Manual (BPM6) and External Debt Statistics: Guide for Compilers and Users, 2013. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Data sources are available in a timely manner for the compilation of statistics at 65 days after the end of the reference period. 3.2.1 Source data assessment Data controls are carried out once data have been received. All reports are checked automatically to verify their completeness, consistency and plausibility. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques The data are derived from the International Investment Position (IIP). Source data statistical techniques are the same as those for the IIP Direct investment, Portfolio investment, Financial derivatives and Other investment. Direct investment Information on direct investment is derived from surveys of financial and non-financial corporations on assets and liabilities, data on holders of securities and Monetary Financial Institution (MFI) statistics compiled by Latvijas Banka and data on assets and liabilities of insurance companies, private pension funds, pension plans managed by private pension funds, state-funded pension scheme funding, investment funds, brokerage firms, investment management companies, authorized payments institutions, authorized electronic money institutions compiled by Latvijas Banka in the frame of supervision statistics. Non-bank external payment data compiled by Latvijas Banka and Register of Enterprises of the Republic of Latvia data are also used. To estimate the data on investment in real estate in Latvia the State Unified Computerised Land Register data are used. Data on investment in real estate abroad are derived as mirror data from partner countries. To the extent possible, foreign direct investment in equity capital in Latvia is recorded at market value. The value of investment in unlisted and listed companies is valued differently. The value of investment in unlisted companies is obtained by using the equity capital approach (own funds at book value) recommended by the European Central Bank (ECB). The value of investment in listed companies is obtained in a similar way, with an exception that the price of one share at the end and beginning of the reporting period is obtained using NASDAQ CSD Riga data. Assets and liabilities of fellow companies are classified depending on the ultimate controlling parent. If the ultimate controlling parent of a Latvian direct investment company is a non-resident, all mutual assets and liabilities are reported as direct investment in Latvia. In case the ultimate controlling parent of a Latvian direct investment company is a Latvian resident, all mutual assets and liabilities are reported as direct investment abroad. Debt transactions and positions between MFIs, investment funds and other financial intermediaries except insurance corporations and pension funds within an existing direct investment relationship are excluded. Holdings (above 10%) of investment funds shares are recorded as portfolio investment. A reconciliation exercise between stocks and flows is carried out on the basis of price changes (for equity capital), exchange rate changes and other adjustments. The geographical allocation is based on the debtor/creditor principle. Portfolio investment Data on portfolio investment are derived from data on holders of securities and MFI statistics compiled by Latvijas Banka and the balance sheet of Latvijas Banka. Portfolio investment positions are marked to market, using the information on financial instrument prices from the Centralised Securities Database of the ECB. A reconciliation exercise between stocks and flows is carried out on the basis of price changes, exchange rate changes and other adjustments. Financial derivatives Data on financial derivatives are derived from MFI statistics and non-bank external payment data compiled by Latvijas Banka, the balance sheet and profit and loss statement of Latvijas Banka and the Treasury data on general government sector. Other investment Data on other investment are derived from surveys of financial and non-financial corporations on assets and liabilities and MFI statistics compiled by Latvijas Banka, data on assets and liabilities insurance companies, private pension funds, pension plans managed by private pension funds, state-funded pension scheme funding, investment funds, brokerage firms, investment management companies, authorized payments institutions, authorized electronic money institutions as well as data on direct insurance and reinsurance technical reserves compiled by Latvijas Banka in the frame of supervision statistics, the balance sheet of Latvijas Banka and the Treasury data on general government sector. A reconciliation exercise between stocks and flows is carried out on the basis of exchange rate changes and other adjustments. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results Data checking at different aggregation levels is carried out. Significant fluctuations are analysed to identify the underlying reasons. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs Data checks are applied to statistical output to analyse the data time series. All outliers are analysed individually to understand the reason. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses Data revisions are analysed to understand the underlying reasons. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses Revisions are analysed to understand the underlying reasons but revision analyses are not disseminated. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request Participating in the World Bank's and the International Monetary Fund's Quarterly External Debt Statistics program (QEDS) for Special Data Dissemination Standard's subscribers since 2004 (submission of Tables 1 and 2 and Supplementary Tables 1.1., 1.2., 1.5. and 1.6.). Unpublished non-confidential data can be disseminated on request. 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points The division in charge of the compilation of the data can be contacted by e-mail: statistika@bank.lv .
