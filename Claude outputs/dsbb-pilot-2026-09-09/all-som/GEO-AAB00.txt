 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The methodology underlying the compilation of monetary and banking statistics in Georgia is consistent with the internationally agreed concepts, definitions and classifications recommended in the IMF's Monetary and Financial Statistics Manual and Compilation Guide (2016) Data are presented as stock data in millions of Georgian Lari (GEL). The Depository Corporations Survey is derived from the sectoral balance sheet of the National Bank of Georgia (1SR) and sectoral balance sheet of Other Depositary Corporations (2SR), which includes resident banks. Assets are presented as NBG's and banks' claims on other resident sectors and nonresidents, and liabilities are shown by financial instruments in order to liquidity. Claims on and liabilities to central government and nonresidents are presented on a net basis. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Classification of financial assets and liabilities as well as sectorization of institutions used in the Depositary Corporations Survey is fully in line with IMF's Monetary and Financial Statistics Manual and Compilation Guide (2016). General principles and structure of the survey follows the compilation rules and structure presented in the IMF's Monetary and Financial Statistics Manual and Compilation Guide (2016). The Depository Corporations Survey Shows Net foreign assets, Net claims on central government, claims on other financial corporations, state and local government, public nonfinancial corporations, other nonfinancial corporations and other sectors. On the liability side, the survey illustrates Currency outside depository corporations, Broad money deposits by counterpart sectors, Securities other than shares, loans, Shares and other equity and other items. 2.2.1 Scope Classification of financial assets and liabilities as well as sectorization of institutions used in the Depositary Corporations Survey is fully in line with IMF's Monetary and Financial Statistics Manual and Compilation Guide (2016). General principles and structure of the survey follows the compilation rules and structure presented in the IMF's Monetary and Financial Statistics Manual and Compilation Guide (2016). The Depository Corporations Survey Shows Net foreign assets, Net claims on central government, claims on other financial corporations, state and local government, public nonfinancial corporations, other nonfinancial corporations and other sectors. On the liability side, the survey illustrates Currency outside depository corporations, Broad money deposits by counterpart sectors, Securities other than shares, loans, Shares and other equity and other items. Scope of the data The Depository Corporations Survey consolidates assets and liabilities of NBG and the other depository corporations (ODC). ODC subsector in Georgia comprise 17 banks and 2 microbanks. The data of the nonbank depository corporations (Credit Unions), the size of which is not significant, are not included in the Depository Corporations Survey. Exceptions to coverage Geographical coverage: Data do not cover the territories of Abkhazian Autonomous Republic and Tskhinvali Region, a part of Georgian territory not controlled by the central authorities. 2.4.1 Valuation Data provided by the reporting agents are end-of-month positions. Assets and liabilities items which are denominated in foreign currencies are converted into Georgian Lari (GEL) at the official exchange rate on the last day. Exchange differences arising from such conversion are recognized as non-interest income or expense in the period in which they are arisen. 2.4.2 Recording basis The accounts of the NBG and other depository corporations are recorded on an accrual basis in accordance with the IFRS 9 requirements. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures In line with the general principle of the MFSM , assets and liabilities of the NBG and banks are collected and compiled on a gross basis. Claims on particular transactors are not netted against liabilities to those transactors. Data presented on a net basis (e.g., "foreign assets (net)" and "Claims on central government (net)") are also shown with the underlying gross data. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Sectoral balance sheet of the National Bank of Georgia (1SR) is derived from the monthly balance sheet compiled by the Finance and Accounting Department of the NBG. Sectoral Balance sheet of other depository corporations (2SR) data comes from SebStat – NBG's statistical information system that collects information from all resident banks and is fully adjusted to the monetary statistics compilation requirements. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording SebStat methodology is comprehensive and well-documented so that all resident banks can easily access and understand definitions and data compilation rules. Sebstat methodology is in line with IMF's Monetary and Financial Statistics Manual and Compilation Guide (2016). 3.1.3 Source data timeliness The data from NBG is provided within 5 working days after the end of reference month and resident banks submit their data via SebStat no later than 12th day after the end of the reference month. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Data are not seasonally adjusted. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results Intermediate results are checked by looking at the trends and understanding the rationale behind the numbers. 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points Information on contact points is available on the NBG's website. 5.3.2 Availability of documents and services catalogs NBG's publications catalog is available on the website (https://nbg.gov.ge/en/publications).
