 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The Directorate of Statistics and Census compiles statistics on imports, exports, and re-exports using the United Nations "Special Trade System." Scope of the data The "Special Trade System" records merchandise entering and leaving the customs territory, which constitutes the statistical border. The customs territory, therefore, does not include the Colón free zone, the Canal area, bonded warehouses, the petroleum free zones, export processing zones, and assembly plants. However, the Directorate of Statistics and Census prepares separate statistics on the exports, re-exports, and imports of these free zones. Monthly, quarterly, and annual data are compiled on external trade in balboas, using c.i.f. values for imports and f.o.b. values for exports. Data on the unit of measure, quantity, net and gross weight are also compiled annually. The data are classified using the Harmonized Commodity Description and Coding System (HS). Only the quarterly and annual data are disseminated.
