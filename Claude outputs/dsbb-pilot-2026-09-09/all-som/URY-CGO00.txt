 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions In the case of the financing operations, a specific methodology is available on the Central Bank's website. This methodology is based on international standards and conventions described in GFSM 2001-2014, and specifies cases of deviations from these manuals. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Data are mostly compiled following the Government Finance Statistics Manual 2014. However, since Uruguay is still transitioning from previous standards (GFS manual 1986), some classifications and sectorizations may differ from those suggested by the GFSM 2014. 2.2.1 Scope Data are mostly compiled following the Government Finance Statistics Manual 2014. However, since Uruguay is still transitioning from previous standards (GFS manual 1986), some classifications and sectorizations may differ from those suggested by the GFSM 2014. Scope of the data Data are disseminated in millions of Uruguayan pesos and cover budgetary central government operations and the main social security institutions ( Banco de Previsión Social and security funds of police and military units). The budgetary central government is the group of civil service bodies or agencies (Legislature and Judiciary Offices, Presidency, all ministries, Electoral Court, Audit Office, Tribunal for Administrative Disputes) that carry out traditional government functions and are subject to a uniform, centralized budgetary and financial system.
