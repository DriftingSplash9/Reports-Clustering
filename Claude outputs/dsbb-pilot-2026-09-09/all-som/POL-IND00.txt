 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Concept : The industrial production index (IPI) measures changes in the volume of industrial production in monthly periods with reference to the base period. Definition : The IPI is based on the value of sold production which is deflated by the relevant monthly producer price index at the 3-digit level of NACE Rev. 2. It includes the revenues from the sale of own products (goods and services) and the value of manufactured products not categorized as sale but included in sales. The following are excluded: VAT invoiced to the client by the producer and taxes which fall on the products and services when they leave the factory (e.g. excise tax). Subsidies on products and services are included in the value of sold production. International standards : Definition of industrial production index (IPI) is compliant with Eurostat recommendations and STS methodological manual. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Classification : Polish Classification of Activities (PKD2007) based on statistical classification of economic activities in European Community – NACE Rev.2 and compatible with International Standard Industrial Classification – ISIC Rev. 4. 2.2.1 Scope Classification : Polish Classification of Activities (PKD2007) based on statistical classification of economic activities in European Community – NACE Rev.2 and compatible with International Standard Industrial Classification – ISIC Rev. 4. Scope of the data Industrial coverage : Population of entities (producers) with 10 or more persons employed, classified to the following sections of NACE Rev. 2: section B —mining and quarrying, section C—manufacturing, section D—electricity, gas, steam and air conditioning supply, section E—water supply; sewerage, waste management and remediation activities. The coverage rate of the target population in relation to the full scope of population in terms of sold production amounts to about 92%. Exceptions to coverage None Unrecorded activity None 2.4.1 Valuation Nature of indicators used : Data on industrial sold production are collected at current basic prices and, after grossing-up from the sample to the population level, data are deflated by the relevant producer price index. The following taxes are excluded from the value of sold production : VAT invoiced to the client by the producer and taxes which fall on the products and services when they leave the factory (e.g. excise tax). Subsidies on products and services are included in the value of sold production. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures Grossing-up is done on the basis of employment and labour productivity at NUTS 2 level and the division level of NACE Rev. 2. Responses are grossed up from the sample to the population for units with 10–49 employees. Grossed up results are used to compile indices and also to publish absolute figures. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Industrial production index is compiled on the basis of data on sold production obtained from entities classified to the sections B,C,D,E of NACE Rev.2. Unit selection : The selection of entities is based on the Statistical Business Register (BJS) supported by the National Official Business Register (REGON). The following entities are selected to the survey: all entities employing 50 or more persons (about 9000 entities as of December 2024), at least 10% sample of entities employing 10-49 persons – the sample selection is applied at the NUTS 2 level as well as at the section and division level of NACE Rev.2 (about 2600 entities as of December 2024) Data collection methods : Reporting units are obliged to fill out monthly questionnaire until the 5th working day after the end of the reference month. Non-responding units are contacted via telephone or e-mail in order to obtain the missing data Data collection methods media : Electronic questionnaire is disseminated in the Reporting Portal. Timing of production observations : month Frequency of data collection : monthly. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Definitions and classifications are compliant with EU Regulation 2019/2152 on European business statistics and Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2020/1197 which came into force in 2021. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Reporting units are obliged to fill out monthly questionnaire until the 5th working day after the end of the reference month. Electronic questionnaire is disseminated in the Reporting Portal. 3.2.1 Source data assessment Accuracy and reliability of source data is under control of the staff of regional statistical office and Statistics Poland - central office. Data validation is mainly based on special programme procedures used for errors checking when data are inserted to the system and also is based on information given by an enterprise on the reasons of substantial difference in the level of data reported in the current period as compared to the equivalent data for the previous period (e.g. cancellation of all previously-issued invoices, correction of invoices, reducing/increasing the number of orders, employee dismissals). In the case of any doubts about data correctness there is a direct contact with an enterprise in order to explain outliers. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Procedures for non-response : Data for non-response are imputed on the basis of units' responses from previous periods. When imputation is not feasible (lack of information), missing data are estimated according to the grossing-up procedure Grossing-up procedure : Grossing-up is done on the basis of employment and labour productivity at NUTS 2 level and the division level of NACE Rev. 2. Responses are grossed up from the sample to the population for units with 10–49 employees. Grossed up results are used to compile indices and also to publish absolute figures 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Nature of weights : Weights are not used. Data for industrial production are collected in absolute values (in current basic prices). Computation of lowest level indices : The value of sold production in current basic prices is deflated by the relevant producer price index (Paasche formula) at the group level of NACE Rev.2. Aggregation : The higher aggregation levels are calculated by summing up values in constant prices, i.e. after eliminating the influence of price changes on the production value. On the basis of the value of sold production in constant prices (volume) the production index is calculated. Linking of reweighed index to historical index : Chain index is used. The following indices are calculated: 2021 = 100; previous period = 100; corresponding period of previous year = 100. Reference period : the current base year is 2021 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results Verification of data : Accuracy and reliability of collected data is under control of the staff of regional statistical office and Statistics Poland - central office. Data validation on aggregated level is conducted at least at the three-digit level of NACE Rev.2 and NUTS 2 level. Both the values and dynamics are compared with previous month and corresponding month of the previous year. Any discrepancies are scrutinized before dissemination of national indices. Movements in industrial production are compared with the results of the monthly business cycle survey in industry, monthly quantitative survey (production by PRODCOM), quarterly and annual financial results. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data See point 3.4.1. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs Results of the monthly industrial production are compared with the results of the monthly business cycle survey in industry, monthly quantitative survey (production by PRODCOM), quarterly and annual financial results. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses Revision analyses are carried out every month after producing the final data and every 5 years when data are revised due to the change of the base year. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses Studies and analyses of revisions are not disseminated. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request Data on request are available at Education and Communication Department of Statistics Poland. 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points Information on contact points is disseminated in publications and on the Internet in Polish: https://stat.gov.pl/kontakt/ and in English: https://stat.gov.pl/en/contacts/ 5.3.2 Availability of documents and services catalogs Documents and services catalogs are available in Education and Communication Department of Statistics Poland.
