 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The International investment position (IIP) framework is based on the BPM6. Broadly same coverage as recommended. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization IIP data are classified the same way as the financial account classification in the BOP statistics. 2.2.1 Scope IIP data are classified the same way as the financial account classification in the BOP statistics. Scope of the data The IIP data compiled by the CBK cover the full range of foreign assets and liabilities of the Kuwaiti economy to the extent possible .The data compiled rely on direct reporting by official agencies, financial and non-financial private sector institutions. 2.4.1 Valuation IIP data are compiled and published in millions of KD. Stocks are converted to the KD using end of period exchange rates .The CBK is able to identify transaction changes from valuation changes. Data are recorded principally on cash basis. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs IIP data rely mainly on the same sources providing BOP data. None None
