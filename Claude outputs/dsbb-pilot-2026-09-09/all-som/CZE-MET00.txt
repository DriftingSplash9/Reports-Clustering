 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Definitions: In general, the definitions used are in accordance with the UN International Trade Statistics Concepts and definitions. After the accession of the Czech Republic to the European Union, cross border movements of goods of the CR is made up of the sum of intra-Union trade (trade with the EU Member States) and trade with non-member countries. Cross border movements of goods statistics are based on two systems of data collection: Intrastat measures the movement of goods within the European Union intra-Union imports and intra-Union exports) and Extrastat measures trade with other than EU Member States (extra-Union imports and extra-Union exports). Exports give the value of goods that were exported abroad and crossed the state border for the purpose of being left abroad, permanently or temporarily. Total exports consist of goods exported to the EU Member States and exported to non-member countries. Imports give the value of goods that were imported from abroad and crossed the state border for the purpose of being left in the Czech Republic, permanently or temporarily. Total imports consist of goods imported from the EU Member States and imported from non-member countries. International comparability: Among the decisive changes that gradually standardize the methodology of cross border movements of goods statistics of the CR with practice used in the Member States of the European Union are in particular implementation of the Single Administrative Document implementation of the Combined Nomenclature implementation of Intrastat – statistical system measuring the movement of goods between the Member States of the European Union – since 1 May 2004. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Data on cross border movements of goods of the CR are produced and published in the following breakdowns: According to commodity classifications: Combined Nomenclature (CN 8), Harmonised System (HS 2, HS 4, HS 6), Standard International Trade Classification, Rev. 4 (SITC 1, SITC 2, SITC 3, SITC 4, SITC 5), Classification of Products by Activity (CZ-CPA); According to Geonomenclature (nomenclature of countries with 2-character alphabetic codes). Scope of the data Transactions included in statistics: Cross border movements of goods statistics include data on all trade transactions against payment irrespective of their manner, including barter trade and also e.g. trade carried out at the government account. Included also are trade between linked enterprises (for example enterprises with common ownership), data on goods intended for uncertain sale (e.g. on goods in consignment, distribution and similar warehouses), data on goods intended for processing as per contract and for the purpose of inward processing, and on goods returned after these transactions. Cross border movements of goods statistics also include data on goods intended for financial leasing. Exceptions to coverage Transactions not included in statistics: Cross border movements of goods statistics do not include data on goods in transit procedure and goods in simplified circulation between the Member States, data on goods temporarily transported to/from the Czech Republic for repair or maintenance and returned after repair or maintenance (since 1 January 2005 they are included in statistics of international trade in services). Further, cross border movements of goods statistics do not include data on goods in the customs procedure of temporary use (goods for trade fairs, exhibitions, tests, etc.); goods released for free circulation after inward processing procedure or after processing procedure under customs supervision. They also do not cover illegal trade, operational leasing and other temporary import and exports of goods not connected with a change of their owner for a period not exceeding two years, and also data on the following items crossing the border: monetary gold, means of payment that are legal tender and securities, goods subject to diplomatic or similar immunity, goods as emergency relief for areas hit by natural disasters, and other goods excluded from cross border movements of goods statistics on the basis of Commission Regulation (EU) No 1197/2020. Further, Intrastat does not include individual trade transactions carried out by persons not registered as VAT payers. From January 2009 to 2018 duty to report to Intrastat does not apply to reporting units below the threshold of CZK 8 million a year for intra-Union import and CZK 8 million a year for intra-Union export, but their trade is included in statistics through mathematical and statistical imputation. From 2019 to 2023 duty to report to Intrastat does not apply to reporting units below the threshold of CZK 12 million a year for intra-Union import and CZK 12 million a year for intra-Union export, but their trade is included in statistics through mathematical and statistical imputation. From 1 January 2022, reporting units may submit the Simplified Report if they exceed the threshold of CZK 12 million and at the same time do not exceed the value of CZK 20 million and do not trade in goods listed in the Communication of the CZSO No 497/2021 Coll., on the list of goods not intended for simplified reporting. Since January 2024 duty to report to Intrastat does not apply to reporting units below the threshold of CZK 15 million a year for intra-Union import and CZK 15 million a year for intra-Union export, but their trade is included in statistics through mathematical and statistical imputation. From January 2024, reporting units may submit the Simplified Report if they exceed the threshold of CZK 15 million and at the same time do not exceed the value of CZK 30 million and do not trade in goods listed in the Communication of the CZSO No 497/2021 Coll., on the list of goods not intended for simplified reporting. The exhaustive definition is set out in legal regulations on external trade statistics 2.4.1 Valuation Valuation: Exports are published at f.o.b. valuation. Imports are published at both c.i.f. and f.o.b. valuation. Import (f.o.b.) are used by the Czech National Bank for calculation of the balance of payments. Definitions follow the GATT/WTO agreements and the Incoterms of the International Chamber of Commerce. Barter trade, processing trade, goods on consignment, etc. are valued at usual market prices in the same month. Conversion to unit of account. Intrastat: For conversion of the data on value of goods in foreign currency to Czech crowns is to be used exchange rate for VAT purposes according to the Government Regulation No. 333/2021 Coll., from 23 August 2021 on the implementation of certain provisions of the Customs Act in the scope of statistics, as amanded Extrastat: Exchange rate fixed in compliance with the provision of article 53 of Regulation (EU) No 952/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council, laying down the Union Customs Code, published on the web pages of the Customs Administration of the Czech Republic is used for the conversion of values in foreign currency to Czech korunas. 2.4.2 Recording basis Trading system: Intastat: The system of statistics on intra-Union trade called Intrastat measures movement of Union goods between Member States of the European Union. In accordance with the provision of Article 5 Regulation (EU) No 952/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council, laying down the Union Customs Code, Union goods are goods that are fully gained or produced on the Community's territory, goods released for free circulation by states and territories outside the customs territory, and goods produced from such goods. Under specific conditions, Intrastat measures also movements between Member States of goods under the customs procedure of inward processing or processing under customs supervision, but these cases are rare. Intrastat includes data on goods irrespective of their origin and trading country Extrastat: Statistics on cross border movements of goods with third countries (non-EU states) are based on collecting data from customs declarations (Single Administrative Documents - SADs). Extrastat does not include data on goods put in a free customs zone or a free customs warehouse released for the customs procedures of storage in a customs warehouse and temporary use; neither included are data on goods re-exported immediately after one of these customs approved destinations. Data on goods in transit customs procedure are not recorded in Extrastat either Timing of recording transactions: Imports: the date that the goods are released by Customs for free circulation in the Czech Republic. Exports: the date the goods are brought under Customs' supervision. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures Intrastat: In response to of Government Regulation No. 333/2021 Coll., from 23 August 2021 on the implementation of certain provisions of the Customs Act in the scope of statistics, as amanded. Formerly: According to Commission Regulation (EC) No 1915/2005, since the 1st January 2006 data on net mass in kg is provided only for those items of goods in the Combined Nomenclature for which the quantity is not given in supplementary units. Extrastat: both – collected net mass in kilograms and supplementary units. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Intrastat: The source of data on goods intra-Union exported and intra-Union imported are Intrastat forms; whose collection and first check are made by the Customs Admistration of the Czech Republic while the Czech Statistical Office (CZSO) is in charge of further processing, checks and publishing; Extrastat: The source of data on extra-Union exports and extra-Union imports is customs statistics taken from Single Administrative Documents (SAD). Data collection and first check is made by the DGC, whereas the CZSO is responsible for further processing, checks and publishing. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Type of data collected: statistical value, invoiced amount, type of commodity, net weight or quantity in supplementary units, country of origin/destination/ consignment, type of customs procedure, terms of delivery, etc. Basis of country data: country of origin, country of consignment, and country of destination. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness The data collection is always completed on the 20th working day and data on total cross border movements of goods are published on the 37th calendar day after the end of the reference month. 3.2.1 Source data assessment Assessment of source data – administrative data (Single Administrative Documents, Intrastat forms, VAT reporting). 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Processing system: Nationally developed system for automated processing cross border movements of goods data. Site of processing: Starting from February 2004 (i.e. the processing of data for January 2004), data collection and the first checks are carried out by the Customs Administration of the Czech Republic, whereas the Czech Statistical Office (CZSO) is in charge of further data processing, checks and publishing. Adjustments to improve coverage/classification/timing/valuation: Method to compile adjustment for trade below the exemption threshold: INTRA-EU Trade: Data source available: PSIs just above threshold and VAT data. Adjustments are compiled according the Combined nomenclature; missing data are estimated by product and partner country. EXTRA-EU Trade: No adjustment Method to compile adjustment for non/late response INTRA-EU Trade: Time series of the PSI's month values of intra-Union imports and intra-Union exports. Missing data are estimated firstly at global level, secondly on CN8 level. Adjustments are not compiled for partial non- response. PSIs with partial non-response are excluded and are adjusted like a late response. Adjustments for VAT fraud are not compiled. EXTRA-EU Trade: No adjustment. Estimation techniques for unrecorded transactions: INTRA-EU Trade: PSIs are exempted from the obligation to report the statistical value and adjustment for statistical value is compiled. Starting from 1 July 2005 statistical value is calculated from the invoiced value using the ratios for intra-Union imports intra-Union exports derived from groups of countries, terms of delivery and means of transportation. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Seasonal adjustment: Starting with the News Release for January 2016 (March 8, 2016) seasonally adjusted data in cross border movements of goods statistics are not published. Geographic detail: Country of origin/ consignment /destination.
