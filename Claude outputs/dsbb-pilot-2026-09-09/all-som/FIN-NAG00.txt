Unrecorded activity Unrecorded activity: Estimates for illegal activities and under reporting have not been made explicitly. 2.4.1 Valuation European System of Accounts 2010 2.4.2 Recording basis European System of Accounts 2010 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures European System of Accounts 2010 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Production approach: The main sources of the data are statistics collected and released by Statistics Finland: monthly Index of turnover (manufacturing, trade and services), Volume index of industrial output, Building and dwelling production statistics. Most of these source statistics are based on a combination of register data and complementing survey data. Value added tax register data is also used extensively for public sector (S.13), household (S.14), and non-profit (S.15) estimates. Expenditure approach: The main sources are Balance of payments, monthly Turnover of trade statistics (for private consumption), registration of cars, Building and dwelling production statistics, Index of turnover of construction, Statistics on manufacturing and trade inventories. Income approach: The main source for wages and salaries is Statistics Finland’s monthly Wage and salary indices statistics. Operating surplus/mixed income is a residual item. Employment: Statistics Finland’s monthly Labour force survey. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques The compilation method is mostly indirect. Source data time series are used as indicators only. The proportional Denton benchmarking method is used to benchmark the source data indicators to the levels of annual national accounts. The latest quarters are compiled by extrapolating the latest annual national accounts level with source data indicator (i.e. using the latest annual ratio of ANA and source data indicator). Production approach: The value added is compiled for 200 sector/industry groups using benchmarking and extrapolation with various turnover, price and volume indicators. Expenditure approach: Exports and imports are estimated directly from Balance of payments source data. Other transactions are compiled indirectly. Income approach: Wages and salaries are compiled indirectly. Operating surplus/mixed income is a residual item. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Balancing: Supply and use are partially balanced, but they are not forced to be equal. Any remaining statistical discrepancy is shown separately. Seasonal adjustment is performed at base series level. Current price aggregates are summed from the adjusted series. Seasonally adjusted volume series at previous year prices are calculated from seasonally adjusted chain-linked volume series and aggregated. Seasonally adjusted volume aggregates are chain-linked. Geographic detail: No subnational detail. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs The balancing item between GDP calculated as a sum of production and GDP as a total of expenditure is statistical discrepancy. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses Revision analyses for GDP and the main expenditure components are done quarterly. Revisions in GDP, imports and exports are published quarterly.
