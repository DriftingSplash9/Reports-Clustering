 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Definition: The IIP of the country represents the stocks of External Financial Liabilities and Assets as at the end of the reference period. It represents the value and composition of the economy's claims on the rest of the world and value of that economy's financial liabilities to the rest of the world as at the end of the reference period. The difference between the two sides (i.e. External financial assets and liabilities) of the economy gives the net international investment position of the economy. The IIP of India is compiled in conformity with the guidelines given in the BPM6 of the IMF. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Classification system: As per the BPM6 . o Liabilities cover: - Foreign direct investment in India in equity and debt instruments - Portfolio Investment in equity and debt securities; - Other Investments including trade credits, loans, currency and deposits, other liabilities, and special drawing rights (Net incurrence of liabilities). . o Assets include: - Direct investment abroad by India in equity and debt instrument. - Portfolio investment in equity and debt securities; - Other investments including trade credits, loans, currency and deposits, and other assets. - Reserve assets. Scope of the data · Coverage of external financial assets and liabilities: The IIP of India covers all sectors (as furnished in the BPM6) and their external financial assets and liabilities. · Geographical coverage: The economic territory consists of the geographic territory, administered by the Government of India, and also non-resident counter-parties in all countries. 2.4.1 Valuation · Conversion to unit of account: The exchange rate used is the quarter end exchange rate compiled at RBI/Financial Benchmarks India Pvt Ltd. (FBIL); · Valuation: Transactions are valued either at nominal value or historical cost prices and market prices as far as possible. 2.4.2 Recording basis · In respect of the integrated IIP statement, the components are compiled from stock data of respective items, wherever available. If stock data are not available, closing position is estimated based on opening position, transactions during the period and other changes. The quarter-end position is disseminated. Data are compiled quarterly, and the reference period is end of March, June, September and December. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs · Data sources: Data collected by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI)from all the available sources on foreign assets and liabilities, viz. India's External Debt, International Reserves and Foreign Currency Liquidity and Balance of Payments, Locational Banking Statistics, etc., and are integrated together to compile the International Investment Data. In addition, annual census on foreign liabilities and assets are conducted for collecting annual data from the corporate sector, insurance sector and mutual fund/asset management companies by the RBI for estimating reinvested earnings of the foreign direct investors and other liabilities of the concerned sectors. · Direct investment: Based on BoP data. · Portfolio investment: Based on Coordinated Portfolio Investment Survey (CPIS) data, BoP data, International Banking Statistics (compiled by the RBI as per BIS guidelines). · Other investment: Assets & Liabilities Statement submitted by banks, Head office funds of the overseas branches of Indian Scheduled Commercial banks, External debt statistics etc., records maintained at RBI etc., census on foreign liabilities and assets. · Reserve assets: Foreign exchange reserves as published by the RBI under SDDS template. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Quarterly · Component wise sources of data released on the RBI website ( https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/IIPDetails.aspx?Id=144 ). 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures For compilation of direct investment, flow data are used to arrive stock position at the quarter end (data are adjusted for exchange rate changes); Other items are compiled based on various returns submitted to the RBI by the public/published sources and based on various surveys; None
