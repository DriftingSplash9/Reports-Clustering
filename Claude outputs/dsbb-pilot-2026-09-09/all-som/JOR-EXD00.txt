 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The public Debt Management Law No. 26 of 2001, which entered into force on July 16, 2001 and published in the official gazette defined the following words and terms as follows.The dissemination framework is consistent with the IMF's A Manual on Government Finance Statistics (GFSM) , 2001 and with the External Debt Statistics: Guide for Compilers and Users, IMF 2003. a) The public Debt Management Law No. 26 of 2001, which entered into force on July 16, 2001 and published in the official gazette defined the following words and terms as follows. Public Debt: The current unpaid balance of direct and indirect obligations valued in Jordanian Dinars and payable by the Government. External debt: the public debt payable in currencies other than the Jordanian Dinars. Domestic debt: the public debt payable in Jordanian Dinars. b) External publicly guaranteed debt was defined as the external obligation of own-budget government agencies guaranteed for repayment by the central government on behalf of the borrower. External public debt data are classified by financing source (Bilateral, Export, Commercial Banks, Funds....etc), maturity (short term, medium -long term), creditor and purpose. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The data is broken down by: Creditor By currency Maturity Financing Source 2.2.1 Scope The data is broken down by: Creditor By currency Maturity Financing Source Scope of the data The public external debt data cover all the central government units represented by the central government budget and include debt guaranteed by the central government on behalf of autonomous government agencies. The data also cover the monetary authorities, banking sector and other sectors and are further disaggregated by maturity (short term and long term) and provided on an original maturity basis and by financial instrument, as set out in the IMF's Balance of Payments and International Investment Position Manual, Sixth Edition (BPM6), and the External Debt Statistics: Guide for Compilers and Users (2013) . General government data are sourced from the Ministry of Finance (MOF), but the loan data, reported in this table (under general government, long term loans) differ from the loan data on public debt published by the MOF because two adjustments are made to the MOF public debt data in order to make the data compliant with the international statistical methodology. These adjustments are: (1) use of the IMF and Arab Monetary Fund credit and loans are reclassified under monetary authorities (long term loans) and (2) loans guaranteed by the central government on behalf of public enterprises are reclassified under other sectors (long term loans) in this table. The data for monetary authorities cover currency and deposit (short-term) liabilities of the Central Bank of Jordan (CBJ), and IMF and AMF credit and loans; the latter are derived from MOF data. The data for banks is based on licensed banks monthly reporting to the CBJ, and cover all debt liabilities of resident banks vis-à-vis nonresidents. Thus, the currency and deposits of nonresidents of Jordan are included in the external debt of the resident licensed bank sector. Data for both the CBJ and banks presented in this table are identical to the data sources employed by the CBJ in compiling international investment position statistics. Data for other sectors are compiled as follows: (1) short term trade credits are compiled by the CBJ; (2) bonds and notes, long term, are derived from MOF published data; (3) loans, long-term, are the sum of MOF data on loans guaranteed by the central government on behalf of public enterprises and outstanding of International Finance Corporation (IFC) loans to the private sector (resident) for the period prior to 2006, and for the period after 2006 the data represents the outstanding of private sector external loans according to the CBJ estimates. · Exceptions to coverage None Exceptions to coverage None 2.4.1 Valuation Data for external debt denominated in Jordan Dinars are recorded at nominal (face) value. Transactions in foreign currency denominated debt are converted into Jordan Dinars at the exchange rates announced by the CBJ on the date of the transactions. The outstanding external debt data are converted into Jordan Dinars at the rates prevailing at the time the debt is converted (Market price). 2.4.2 Recording basis Data on external debt are prepared monthly on both commitment and cash basis. These data are disseminated in General Government Financial Bulletin of MOF. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures All data are recorded on a gross basis. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The disbursement from the development project loans is provided by the Ministry of Planning and International Cooperation to MOF on a monthly basis. For other loans, data are sourced from creditors. External Debt service is prepared on the basis of actual payments made as indicated on payment application provided by the lender and approved by Ministry of Planning and International Cooperation. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording The source data for compiling debt data are mostly in accordance with 2001 GFSM definitions for classification, valuation, and time of recording. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness The disbursement from the development project loans is provided by Ministry of Planning and International Cooperation to Ministry of Finance MOF on a monthly basis. For other loans, the disbursements are sourced from creditors. Overall, the source data for compiling the external debt are timely. 3.2.1 Source data assessment Source data received by reporters are checked by the staff for consistency, compared to formerly submitted data, and cross-checked between reporters having identical activities. No adjustments are made to the basic data sources in the compilation of government debt data for dissemination. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques DMFAS is currently applied and all debt data is derived from its reports. Other advanced reports not available in its standard reports are produced through other programs (Oracle Discoverer tools). 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Other statistical procedures are not employed in compiling public debt data. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results Public debt data are obtained from MOF records. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data Not applicable. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs Not applicable. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses Not applicable. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses Not applicable because data are not routinely revised. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request The decision on the disclosure of requested information is made considering the confidentiality of individual agencies data. Therefore, requests for data not routinely disseminated are considered by the MOF case-by-case and depend on confidentiality considerations. 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points All information may be obtained from contact point by e-mail, telephone or postal address. This information is dissemination in MOF's statistical publications. 5.3.2 Availability of documents and services catalogs The MOF publishes catalog of publications to users.
