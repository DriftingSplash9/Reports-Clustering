 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The concepts of residency, valuation and classification of stocks included in the International Investment Position correspond to those in the 6th edition of the IMF's Balance of Payments Manual . 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization National presentation disseminated on web site is broadly consistent with MBP6 guidelines Scope of the data 1. Includes assets and liabilities of the resident financial system, investment funds, insurance companies, non financial private sector and monetary authority; liabilities of the nonfinancial public sector (general government and public enterprises); assets and liabilities of direct investment enterprises located in the country; assets (deposits) of the nonfinancial private sector, and commercial papers as money market instruments. Unrecorded activity The coverage of all activity and sectors recorded on balance of payments statistics are included on IIP statistics. Assets on Portfolio Investment are recorded from the annual Coordinated Portfolio Investment Survey since year 2001. 2.4.1 Valuation Bonds issued by the government are recorded at market value; Treasury bills issued by the government are recorded at their present value; Banks' assets and liabilities are recorded in accordance with the valuation standards of the Superintendent of Financial Institutions, which for statistical purposes are consistent with the criteria set forth in BPM6 . Assets representing deposits of the nonfinancial private sector are obtained from the Bank for International Settlements (BIS); the net worth of direct investment enterprises is calculated on the basis of their accounting statements. 2.4.2 Recording basis Other transactions rather Bonds issued by the government and Treasury bills issued by the government are recorded on accrual basis. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures Stocks are estimated as mentioned on 2.4.1 and flows follow the guidelines of MBP6, it means, on a net basis. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Foreign direct investment information corresponding to foreign direct investment enterprises located in the country which is obtained from annual surveys. With the exception of the information published by the BIS, which is obtained from its website, the liabilities of the nonfinancial public sector which is obtained from the Central Bank's internal records, and the portfolio of nonfinancial private sector which is obtained from the CPIS annual survey, the remaining stocks are obtained from accounting information supervised by the Monetary Authority through the relevant Superintendence’s. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Stocks on Government Bonds or Treasury Notes made on the secondary market are valuated a market prices with the average price reported by the Montevideo Stock Exchange (Bolsa de Valores de Montevideo) and data from international specialized publications or agencies. Public External Debt outstanding are recorder from CBU´s debt unit and had been conciliated with creditor’s balances. CPIS data had been conciliated with specialized agencies to check the market value reported by respondents. Assets and Liabilities stocks from financial sector and foreign direct investment abroad and in the country stocks from surveys are recorded as book values. Foreign direct investment liabilities for land and real estate investments are valuated as a transaction value and an estimate value with qualitative data from real estate agents, constructions index and other relevant sources respectively. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Timely of data used to compile international investment position are broadly in accordance with GDDS guidelines. Survey’s respondents are notified of the dead line date to report questionnaires. The legal framework joint with an efficiency management of quarterly and annual surveys allow achieve a very good respond rate (approximately hundred per cent). 3.2.1 Source data assessment Routinely is evaluated the assessment of surveys, administrative records and other data sources used to the compilation of international investment position. In particular for foreign direct investment liabilities, departures on historical behavior or errors founded have been fixed using cross checking techniques with alternatives sources or after discussing with the respondent. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques With the exception of Foreign Direct Investment, Portfolio Investment Assets of private nonfinancial sector which is mostly obtained by means of surveys and Abroad money and deposits which is obtained by BIS Quarterly Report Table 7B, the remaining transactions correspond to the universe of Institutional Units. Data are expressed in current dollars and correspond to the calendar year. In general, firms responding to questionnaires report their data in accordance with the calendar year, and make the requisite adjustments in the event that the fiscal year does not coincide with the calendar year. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Statistical analysis was made to data reported by surveys. (e.g. CPIS and Foreign Direct Investment). 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results Most of the stocks was recorded from data validated with balance sheets or other alternatives sources. There is not an International Transactions Reporting System in Uruguay 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data Flows and Stocks recorded on international investment position are conciliated with balance of payments statistics and external debt statistics. No distinction was made on “other flows” rather transactions. To improve the conciliations the new questionnaires used since Year 2007 surveys transactions include a break down of “other flows” as: exchange, price, and other. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs Continuously the changes on foreign direct investment liabilities stocks are valuated to identify compilations errors or transactions not recorded. Because the legal statistics secret there is not possible to made a cross checking of each foreign direct investment transaction with counterparty countries transaction to transaction. However a general cross check could be done to identify, at least, the magnitude of discrepancies with the main countries involved on this kind of investments. Not have an ITRS constrain other counterparty cross check. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses Periodically studies of new sectors or transactions were made to improve the quality of data recorded, especially on Foreign Direct Investment. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses Significant revisions, if occur, are included on next released with an explanatory footnote. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request Other public data commonly not disseminated on web site could be released prior requested by user trough Institutional Communication Center link on the site. 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points All users’ assistance must be requested only trough Institutional Communication Center link on CBU´s web site 5.3.2 Availability of documents and services catalogs There is not a catalog of CBU publications
