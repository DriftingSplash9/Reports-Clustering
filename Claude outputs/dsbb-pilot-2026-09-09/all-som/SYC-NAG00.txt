 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The national accounts for Seychelles are compiled and disseminated by the National Bureau of Statistics (NBS). The framework used is the "System of National Accounts, 2008" ("2008 SNA"). The partial transition to SNA 2008 was finalized during the compilation of the annual national accounts statistics for 2020 which were published in December 2021. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Economic activities are classified according to a customized version of the UN International Standard Industrial Classification of All Economic Activities (ISIC Rev4) which has been labeled Seychelles Industrial Classification (SIC). The categories used in national accounts are: A01 Agriculture A03 Fishing C1020 Manufacture of fishery products C1010, C1030-C1080 Manufacture of other food C11 – C12 Manufacture of beverages and tobacco C13-C33 Manufacturing, other D Electricity, gas, steam and air conditioning supply E Water supply; sewerage, waste management and remediation activities F Construction G Wholesale and retail trade; repair of motor vehicles and motorcycles H Transportation and storage I Accommodation and food service activities J Information and communication K Financial and insurance activities L68 Real estate activities L Owner occupied dwellings M Professional, scientific and technical activities N Administrative and support service activities O Public administration and defence; compulsory social security P Education Q Human health and social work activities R Arts, entertainment and recreation S Other service activities Household final consumption expenditure is classified using the UN Classification of Individual Consumption according to Purpose (COICOP). 2.2.1 Scope Economic activities are classified according to a customized version of the UN International Standard Industrial Classification of All Economic Activities (ISIC Rev4) which has been labeled Seychelles Industrial Classification (SIC). The categories used in national accounts are: A01 Agriculture A03 Fishing C1020 Manufacture of fishery products C1010, C1030-C1080 Manufacture of other food C11 – C12 Manufacture of beverages and tobacco C13-C33 Manufacturing, other D Electricity, gas, steam and air conditioning supply E Water supply; sewerage, waste management and remediation activities F Construction G Wholesale and retail trade; repair of motor vehicles and motorcycles H Transportation and storage I Accommodation and food service activities J Information and communication K Financial and insurance activities L68 Real estate activities L Owner occupied dwellings M Professional, scientific and technical activities N Administrative and support service activities O Public administration and defence; compulsory social security P Education Q Human health and social work activities R Arts, entertainment and recreation S Other service activities Household final consumption expenditure is classified using the UN Classification of Individual Consumption according to Purpose (COICOP). Scope of the data The economic activity of all resident units is covered, including the activity of resident workers from abroad. GDP by Production approach is being compiled, in both current and constant prices. GDP by Expenditure approach is being compiled on an experimental basis, at current prices only. QGDP by production approach at current and constant prices are being compiled. QGDP by expenditure approach are being compiled at current prices only. National accounts cover the whole territory of the Republic of Seychelles, including free trade zones. Exceptions to coverage Illegal activities Informal Sector Non-observed economy 2.4.1 Valuation Valuation: Estimates of value added in goods and services for the various industries are recorded at basic prices. GDP at market prices are arrived at by adding taxes less subsidies on products to the sum of value added at basic prices. Domestic expenditures are valued at purchasers' prices, while both imports and exports are valued FOB. 2.4.2 Recording basis Recording basis: In principle, all transactions are recorded on an accrual basis. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The sources and methods used for compiling the Production account in current and constant market prices can be broadly summarized as follows: Production approach: Use monthly data on Value added Tax (VAT) by industry to calculate quarterly VAT turnover Monthly statistics on generation and consumption of electricity Monthly statistics on volumes of water sold and produced. Monthly statistics on fish catch and production of certain manufactured products (canned tuna, brewery products, construction products) Monthly tourism related statistics (visitor arrivals, bed and room occupancy, average length of stay) Monthly data of the financial institutions (Income and expenditure statements) Monthly general government statistics (Fiscal reports/Budgets/GFS) Consumer Price Index (CPI), Producer Price Index (PPI), Industrial Production Index (IPI), used as deflators. The sources and methods used for compiling the Expenditure approach in current prices can be broadly summarized as follows: Expenditure approach: Household Budget Survey data for final consumption expenditure (HFCE) in base year 2014 Monthly imports data for plants, machinery, transport and equipment for Gross Fixed Capital Formation (GFCF) Quarterly Government Finance Statistics (GFS) for GFCE Quarterly Construction output (Dwellings and non- dwellings - other building and structures) for GFCF Quarterly Balance of Payments (BoP) data for Imports and exports of goods and services 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording The economic activity of all resident units is covered, including the activity of resident workers from abroad. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Data collection and processing are satisfactory but improvements are needed in some areas to meet timeliness and periodicity for disseminating the national accounts statistics. 3.2.1 Source data assessment The quality of primary source data is satisfactory. However, to a large extent, the compilation of national accounts are based on information from administrative records mostly data from the Seychelles Revenue Commission (SRC). This has been made possible through the signing of a confidentiality agreement between the NBS and the SRC. The quality of the data remains a concern. There are issues on coverage, data gaps, industrial classification and timeliness. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques The methods used can be summarized as follows; For current prices : Direct compilation from financial statements for the financial and non-financial corporations and fiscal report and GFS for Public Administration, Health and Education. Growth rate/ movements based on output/turnover indicators of relevant industries and VAT turnover data to extrapolate gross output in current prices. Inflation using the most appropriate CPI component available. For constant prices : Extrapolation using appropriate volume indicator for gross output in constant prices. Deflation using the most appropriate CPI component available. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Uses the Benchmark software (Denton) for estimating quarterly GDP. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request This can be entertained depending on the level of disaggregation requested. 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points Available on the NBS website www.nbs.gov.sc 5.3.2 Availability of documents and services catalogs Available on the NBS website www.nbs.gov.sc
