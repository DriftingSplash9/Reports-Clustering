 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The Central Bank of Honduras (BCH) compiles the Monthly Index of Economic Activity (IMAE), which reflects the performance of the key short-term indicators of quantity and volume related to the production of GDP at constant prices, using the production approach. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The classification of economic activities is very similar to the International Standard Industrial Classification of all economic activities (ISIC rev. 2). The most representative sector of manufacturing industry is the manufacture of food, beverages and tobacco, broken down on the basis of ISIC rev.2 as follows: slaughtering of cattle, preparation of poultry, dairy products, production of seafood, animal fat, oil, grain mill products, sugar production, animal foods, distilled spirits, blended liquors, beer and tobacco. Scope of the data At the aggregate level, the IMAE comprises 12 economic activities, which represent 74 percent of total GDP. The Laspeyres formula is applied with base year 1986=100. The weighting of each activity is determined by the relative share of its value added in GDP, in the 1986 national accounts. The following products are covered for the activity Agriculture, Forestry, Hunting and Fishing: coffee, bananas, sugar cane, tobacco, African palm, plantain, roundwood, beef cattle, chicken, hen eggs, shrimp and lobster. Of these products, information is compiled monthly for: coffee, bananas, chicken, hen eggs, shrimp and lobster. As no information on monthly volumes is available for the others, annual values are projected based on information from key companies, which are converted to a monthly basis using the corresponding expenditure-time curves estimated from BCH survey data on the 1986 cost structure. The products considered for measuring the indicator of metal mining are: extraction of silver, lead, zinc, and gold, For quarrying, activity is directly related to construction, so the index for the latter activity is used as the indicator. The most representative sector of manufacturing industry is the manufacture of food, beverages and tobacco, broken down on the basis of ISIC rev.2 as follows: slaughtering of cattle, preparation of poultry, dairy products, production of seafood, animal fat, oil, grain mill products, sugar production, animal foods, distilled spirits, blended liquors, beer and tobacco. If the data are not obtained, they are estimated in a preliminary manner based on the past performance of the company or the activity of which they are a part. This estimate is replaced when definitive information is received. For the Electricity and Water activity, the volume of electricity production and water consumption in Tegucigalpa and San Pedro Sula are taken. This represents 3.62 percent of the IMAE. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures The weights, at the level of the 12 economic activities referred to, are as follows: ACTIVITIES Weighting AGRICULTURE, HUNTING, FORESTRY, AND FISHING 22.0% MINING AND QUARRYING 1.0% MANUFACTURING INDUSTRY 15.0% ELECTRICITY AND WATER 2.0% CONSTRUCTION 1.0% WHOLESALE AND RETAIL TRADE 14.0% TRANSPORT, STORAGE AND COMMUNICATION 7.0% BANKING AND INSURANCE 6.0% HOME OWNERSHIP 7.0% PUBLIC ADMINISTRATION 6.0% MISCELLANEOUS SERVICES 10.0% MISCELLANEOUS ACTIVITIES 9.0% The composition of the manufacturing industry index is as follows: ACTIVITIES Weighting 31 FOODS, BEVERAGES, AND TOBACCO 54.2% 32 WEARING APPAREL AND FOOTWEAR 5.7% 33 WOOD PRODUCTS AND FURNITURE 6.9% 34 PAPER PRODUCTS, PRINTING AND PUBLISHING 7.5% 35 CHEMICALS AND CHEMICAL PRODUCTS 10.8% 36 NON-METALLIC MINERALS 6.4% 37 BASIC METAL INDUSTRIES 0.8% 38 METAL PRODUCT INDUSTRY 6.0% 39 OTHER INDUSTRIES 1.7% To calculate the construction index, the surface area under private construction permits in the main cities of the country is used as an indicator. Government construction is not included separately, owing to significant changes in the data on a cash basis and difficulties estimating the data on an accrual basis. The indicator of wholesale and retail trade is constructed weighting the volume of agricultural goods, manufacturing industry, and imports, with weights of 26.0 percent, 24.0 percent and 50.0 percent, respectively. The transport and communications index includes land transport, sea transport, and telecommunications, with weights of 56.0 percent, 26.0 percent, and 18.0 percent, respectively. Interurban transport, cargo transported by rail, and the volume of loaded and unloaded sea cargo are taken into account in land transport. In banking and insurance, the indicator is constructed based on estimates of value added at current prices based on financial statements provided by the National Commission on Banking and Insurance. The value added is deflated by the Consumer Price Index (CPI). The indicator of home ownership corresponds to the projection of annual value added at constant prices, converted to a monthly basis. The indicator of Public Administration is broken down into monthly estimates based on the annual projections. Miscellaneous services include a wide variety of activities. Public and private services are estimated separately. Public services are used as an indicator of the compensation paid in the main education and health institutions, deflated by the CPI. Quantity and volume indicators are used for private services, the volume indicators being constructed by deflating current values by the appropriate components of the price indices. The following are examples of these components: number of cinema tickets, deflated taxes paid on public shows, and number of days spent in private hospitals, etc.. Other activities: for lack of data, this item is estimated using the weighted average of changes in the other activities. As a time series, the IMAE can be broken down into three components: cyclical trend, seasonality, and irregular component. The X11ARIMA method is used to measure the cyclical trend component. None
