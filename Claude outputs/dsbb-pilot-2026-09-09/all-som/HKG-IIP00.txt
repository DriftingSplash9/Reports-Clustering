 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Analytical framework: In Hong Kong, the compilation of international investment position (IIP) statistics is largely in conformity with the guidelines set out in the Sixth Edition of the Balance of Payments and International Investment Position Manual (BPM6) released by the International Monetary Fund (IMF) in 2009. The end-of-period positions of external financial assets and liabilities are presented under the following data categories: direct investment, portfolio investment, financial derivatives, other investment and reserve assets; net IIP is also presented. Definition: The concepts and definitions adopted in the Hong Kong’s IIP statistics are largely in conformity with the guidelines set out in the BPM6 . IIP is a balance sheet showing the stock of external financial assets and liabilities of an economy at a particular time point. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The classification/sectorization systems of the BPM6 are largely adopted. For institutional sector, only the breakdowns for “banks” and “other sectors” are published. Data in regard to the central bank and general government are subsumed in “other sectors”. For classification by type of financial instrument, the BPM6 ’s recommended convention of classifying all interbank positions other than securities and accounts receivable/payable under deposits is adopted. 2.2.1 Scope The classification/sectorization systems of the BPM6 are largely adopted. For institutional sector, only the breakdowns for “banks” and “other sectors” are published. Data in regard to the central bank and general government are subsumed in “other sectors”. For classification by type of financial instrument, the BPM6 ’s recommended convention of classifying all interbank positions other than securities and accounts receivable/payable under deposits is adopted. Scope of the data Coverage of external financial assets and liabilities: Standards and guidelines recommended in the BPM6 on residence are closely followed and all external financial assets and liabilities positions of Hong Kong are broadly covered. Geographic coverage: All territories under the administration of the Government of the HKSAR are covered. Positions vis-a-vis Chinese Mainland are treated as external positions and are included in the IIP statistics of Hong Kong. Exceptions to coverage Financial derivatives: No separate estimate is made for employee stock options (ESOs) due to the lack of information and the relative insignificance of such positions in Hong Kong, as signified by the small share of compensation of employees in Hong Kong’s BoP over the years. Other investment: No estimate is made for pension entitlements due to the lack of information and the relative insignificance of such positions in Hong Kong. Unrecorded activity External investment positions in relation to local enterprise groups, as well as those invested through local intermediaries and agents, are comprehensively gauged in the "Survey of External Claims, Liabilities and Income" (SECLI). Investment not going through the formal sector, if any, is not captured. Unrecorded transactions (e.g. individuals' direct investment in Chinese Mainland without setting up companies in Hong Kong, individuals' real estate investment in Hong Kong and overseas countries.), if any, are estimated whenever applicable to derive the resulting investment positions. 2.4.1 Valuation Valuation: In principle, data are valued at market prices. Unlisted equity in direct investment is valuated by net asset value. For nonnegotiable instruments such as loans, deposits, and other accounts receivable/payable, the valuation is based on nominal value. Conversion to unit of account: Source data reported in foreign currencies are converted to local currency using market exchange rates as at the end of the reference period. 2.4.2 Recording basis Accrual basis is adopted in recording all resource flows that may affect the IIP. If accrual accounting is not in place, approximates are derived by using accounting and statistical models. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures Positions of financial assets and liabilities are recorded on a gross basis, i.e. positions of the same type of a financial instrument held as both a financial asset and a liability are presented separately. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The required position data for the following functional categories of investment are mainly collected from the Survey of External Claims, Liabilities and Income (SECLI), a mandatory survey conducted by the C&SD at quarterly intervals. Direct investment: A Hong Kong enterprise group (HKEG) is taken as the basic unit for data reporting on direct investment in SECLI. The Hong Kong parent company of an HKEG is designated as the target company for reporting data for the enterprise group as a whole since relevant data can normally be found in the consolidated accounts of the enterprise group. Direct investment outside Hong Kong made by individuals, but not through a registered company in Hong Kong, are not captured by SECLI. Given the close economic links between Hong Kong and Chinese Mainland, it is common that some residents have made direct investment in Chinese Mainland without setting up companies in Hong Kong. To account for such external transactions not covered by SECLI, other data sources are used to derive estimates of their position, flow and related income. Resident individuals' real estate investment in popular overseas countries and non-resident individuals' real estate investment in Hong Kong are estimated based on administrative records and data models. The input parameters for the data models are mainly derived from media reports and statistics released by overseas governments. Portfolio investment and financial derivatives: The major data suppliers for statistics on portfolio investment and financial derivatives are local financial intermediaries or agents such as banks, securities houses, share registrars, nominees, custodians, trusts and fund managers that are often engaged in external financial investment activities. They are required to report external claims and liabilities under their own account and those on behalf of their clients. For portfolio investment, data are collected on a security-by-security basis for shares listed on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange and on an aggregated basis for other securities. Other investment: The main data suppliers for statistics on other investment are banks. They are required to report external claims and liabilities under their own account and those on behalf of their clients. To make up for any omission of data on offshore deposits placed by and offshore loans owed by Hong Kong's nonbank residents (i.e. other sectors: other investment, assets, deposits; and other sectors: other investment, liabilities, loans), reconciliation with relevant statistics published by the Bank for International Settlements (BIS) is conducted regularly. Reserve assets: Changes in and the positions of reserve assets are compiled from data on foreign currency reserve assets of the Exchange Fund managed by the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA). 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording A complete set of well-structured SECLI questionnaire, including a checklist, data reporting instructions and a separate reference guide, is used to collect the required data from the respondents accurately. The use of the checklist is to enable easy identification of specific reporting sections in the questionnaire that need to be completed by a respondent. Detailed reporting requirements are given in the data reporting instructions, while relevant explanatory notes as well as other useful information for assisting respondents to report relevant data are provided in the reference guide. Specially designed data entry forms and supplementary information sheets are provided for respondents to fill in the required data. Separate reporting instructions are designed for different economic sectors to facilitate survey respondents in easily focusing on the reporting requirements that are relevant to them. Through the carefully designed questions, respondents can be appropriately classified and they can provide data in accordance with the concepts and definitions adopted in the survey, which follow closely the guidelines set out in the BPM 6 . In addition, new developments in the external environment are regularly monitored to ensure that the existing data collection programme remains as comprehensive as practicable. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness The SECLI adopts an integrated survey approach with three reporting frequencies. Under this approach, all prominent data suppliers selected in the sample are required to report data on direct investment, portfolio investment and other investment on a quarterly basis, of which some are required to report portfolio investment data on a monthly basis. The less prominent data suppliers are required to fill in survey returns on either quarterly or annual basis. This concept of "One Survey, Three Frequencies" helps to minimise the reporting burden to some extent. A work schedule for the compilation and dissemination of IIP statistics is drawn up and each involved party is well informed of the schedule beforehand. Enumerators are required to complete data collection for a survey period before the end of that survey cycle. Deadline is stated clearly in the survey questionnaires of SECLI. 3.2.1 Source data assessment For compiling the IIP statistics of Hong Kong, comprehensive consistency checking and revision study are performed before the statistics are published. All survey data are carefully edited, and subject to a comprehensive checking and validation mechanism. Any dubious reported data are carefully verified with respondents. Validity checks are computerised to minimise human errors. Imputation techniques for missing data are in place under the compilation system. There are compilation manuals that document the detailed compilation procedures for each of the published components of the IIP. The questionnaires are designed in a way that makes them easy to complete and appropriate for computer processing and the survey samples are selected scientifically using stratified rotational replicated sampling techniques. As a significant portion of survey data are collected through fieldwork, a designated team was set up in 2002 within the C&SD to perform an internal audit exercise on fieldwork control procedures. The focus of the audit is to ascertain whether reported control procedures have been effectively implemented in the survey systems. Non-sampling errors are carefully controlled with the strength of the C&SD field teams. The field teams would arrange face-to-face interviews with the data suppliers to avoid misclassification and measurement error by giving clear explanation and assisting them in completing the returns, and non-response rate is significantly reduced by continuous persuasion. In addition, there are in-house operations and checking mechanisms to reduce non-sampling errors, such as frequent updating of the survey frame; checking for validity of reported data; and conducting research work on special topics on the IIP statistics to ensure conceptual consistency and comprehensiveness of the system. Another important step of consistency checking is to cross-check the IIP statistics with press reports and other information available in the public domain. For example, international banking statistics released by the BIS, and local banking statistics published by the HKMA are regularly used for validation of the IIP statistics at aggregate level. The underlying reasons accounting for any major changes in positions and trends will be analysed and documented. Revisions to the IIP statistics are regularly assessed and the magnitudes of such revisions for individual IIP components are closely monitored. The additional information leading to each revision will be examined and documented as source of reference. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Census enumeration is conducted on prominent data suppliers while for the less prominent data suppliers, data are enumerated on a sample basis. Sample is selected using stratified rotational replicated sampling method based on meaningful stratifying variables. The statistical technique of grossing up is applied on the sampled cases to obtain the population estimate. For the non-responding cases, imputation is applied with reference to (i) publicly available information as far as possible and (ii) stratum average based on reported cases by broad business group and investment scale. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Geographic detail: Geographic breakdowns for direct investment are available. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results Statistics and reports published by international and local organisations, such as banking statistics published by the HKMA and statistics published by the BIS, are used to check the validity of the IIP statistics. Annual financial reports and news on prominent external positions / transactions, such as mergers and acquisitions, are used to verify data suppliers' returns. Also, any unusual movements of the IIP components will be examined and cross-checked with press reports and other available relevant information. Extreme-value positions / transactions are confirmed with respondents wherever possible. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data Statistics from external sources, such as banking statistics published by the HKMA and statistics published by the BIS, are used for consistency checking on banks' data collected under SECLI. Position data on foreign currency reserve assets published by the HKMA are used for checking transaction flows and positions data on reserve assets. Consistencies on flow-stock relationship and investment yield are ensured at company level with reference to the data on stocks, flows, incomes and valuation changes which are being collected under SECLI. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs Statistics from external sources where appropriate are used for cross-checking large unexplained movements in the IIP data. Bilateral comparisons/reconciliations are conducted with data of other countries and international organisations. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses Revisions to the IIP statistics are periodically assessed and the percentage changes resulting from each revision are closely monitored. Trends in the revision pattern are periodically assessed to identify the existence of systematic bias if any. Results of the analysis of various key quality indicators in the revision studies were published in the first quarter 2013 and the first quarter 2025 issues of Balance of Payments, International Investment Position and External Debt Statistics of Hong Kong ( https://www.censtatd.gov.hk/en/EIndexbySubject.html?pcode=B1040001&scode=260 ). 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses The preliminary estimates are subsequently revised when more complete data become available. Revisions to the IIP statistics are regularly assessed and the magnitudes of such revisions for individual IIP components are closely monitored. The additional information leading to each revision will be examined and documented as source of reference. Results of the analysis of various key quality indicators in the revision studies were published in the first quarter 2013 and the first quarter 2025 issues of Balance of Payments, International Investment Position and External Debt Statistics of Hong Kong ( https://www.censtatd.gov.hk/en/EIndexbySubject.html?pcode=B1040001&scode=260 ). 5.1.5 Dissemination on request Requests for additional statistics which are not available from the existing tabulations will be considered on a case-by-case basis having regard to such factors as the nature of the request, data relevance and precision, and data confidentiality. Where applicable and in consultation with users, customised tabulations will be provided at a charge under the cost recovery principle. 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points Details of contact for enquiries, including phone number, fax number, email address and office address, are clearly given in the quarterly report Balance of Payments , International Investment Position and External Debt Statistics of Hong Kong, the Performance Pledge of the C&SD and on the C&SD website ( https://www.censtatd.gov.hk/en/page_105.html ). The information is readily available to the public. Prompt and knowledgeable support is provided in accordance with the performance pledge of the C&SD. The timeliness of response to enquiries from the public is regularly monitored. 5.3.2 Availability of documents and services catalogs The availability of the report Balance of Payments , International Investment Position and External Debt Statistics of Hong Kong is publicised in the quarterly press releases for disseminating the updated IIP statistics. Such information is also available on the C&SD website ( https://www.censtatd.gov.hk/en/ ). Lists of publications, feature articles and other services available are also obtainable from the C&SD website ( https://www.censtatd.gov.hk/en/page_1273.html ).
