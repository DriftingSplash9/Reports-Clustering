 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The balance of payments is a statistical statement designed to provide a systematic record of economic transactions between residents of Malaysia and the rest of the world for a specific time period. The Balance of Payments Manual Sixth Edition (BPM6) concept of residency is followed. The analytical framework for the national presentation follows the BPM6. The broad categories of data presented under three headings, namely, the current account: imports and exports of goods, services, primary income and secondary income; the capital account: non-produced non-financial assets and capital transfers; the financial account: assets and liabilities of direct investment, assets and liabilities of portfolio investment, financial derivatives and other investments, reserve assets and net errors and omissions. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The classification as stipulated in BPM6 is adopted. Scope of the data Transactions coverage: Best Practices based on Standards and guidelines recommended in BPM6 on residency and enterprise are used in data compilation to ensure the external transactions of the economy are covered. The transactions between Labuan International Offshore Financial Centre (LIOFC) and non-residents are also included. Geographic coverage: Malaysia (there are no geographic exclusions). Exceptions to coverage Transactions coverage: No transaction is intentionally excluded or included. Geographic coverage: There are no geographic exclusions. Unrecorded activity There are no unrecorded activities. 2.4.1 Valuation Transactions, whenever possible, are valued at market prices. 2.4.2 Recording basis Transactions, as far as possible followed the accrual accounting basis. Time of recording: Basically, all transactions are recorded when the change of ownership takes place. Conversion to unit of account: Data reported in foreign currencies are converted into local currency at the average of exchange rates in the reference period. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures Not applicable. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The data for the current account are obtained mainly from administrative records and the Quarterly Survey of International Trade in Services (ITS) while for the Financial Account and Investment Income, the main source of data is through International Investment Position (IIP) Joint-Survey (joint collaboration between BNM and DOSM). 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Goods: Statistics on good transactions (merchandise trade statistics) are prepared by DOSM from customs returns compiled by the Malaysia Royal Customs Department, and DOSM's ITS Survey of Transportation (for data on goods procured in ports). Services: The primary data sources are the Quarterly Survey of ITS conducted by DOSM and the Integrated Statistical System (ISS) conducted by BNM. For manufacturing services, maintenance and repair, transports, telecommunication, construction, personal, cultural & recreational, financial, charges for the intellectual properties, insurance & pension, and other business services, the sources are the Quarterly Survey of ITS and BNM's ISS data. For travel services, DOSM has developed a data model for both credits and debits based on information supplied by the Immigration Department, Tourism Malaysia and Malaysia Healthcare Travel Council (MHTC) as well as BNM's ISS data. For government goods and services, data are provided by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, International organisations, and various government departments and statutory authorities. Primary Income: The primary data sources are from IIP survey (for investment income of private sector) and BNM (for investment income of Monetary Authority, Federal Government and other sectors, and compensation of employees). Secondary Income: The data sources are from administrative records of other government agencies as well as BNM's ISS. Capital account: The data sources are from administrative records of other government agencies, BNM's ISS. Direct investment: The main data source for various components of direct investment is from IIP Joint-Survey. Unit of reporting of the survey is enterprise. Portfolio investment: The main data source for various components of portfolio investment is from IIP Joint-Survey. Under this survey, the data providers are end investors and custodians. Financial derivatives: The major data providers are financial intermediaries particularly banks and gathered through IIP Joint-Survey. Other investment: The data source for various components of other investment is IIP Joint-Survey (for private sector) as well as from BNM (for Monetary Authority, Federal Government and other sectors). Reserve assets: Data for reserve assets are provided by BNM. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness All data sources are basically on quarterly basis. 3.2.1 Source data assessment Updating of survey coverage is performed on a quarterly basis. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Goods: Coverage adjustments are made by DOSM to include exports of water, electricity, ships, aircraft and smuggled goods and to exclude goods for processing and storage (no change of ownership), goods for repairs and returned goods. For merchandise imports, DOSM makes coverage adjustment to include military imports, purchases of goods in foreign ports by resident shipping companies, imports of ships, aircraft, smuggled goods, water and electricity, and to exclude goods for processing and storage (no change of ownership), goods for repairs and returned goods. Services: Transports: For debits, DOSM uses a data model that is built up from import c.i.f. of goods (to derive import f.o.b.). Travel services credits; DOSM uses a data model that is built up from data for tourist expenditure (from the Annual Departing Visitors' Survey conducted by the Tourism Malaysia and tourist arrivals (from the Department of Immigration). For debits, a similar model is used. For border travellers, benchmark data on expenditure is based on the survey conducted by DOSM. Insurance services: For debits insurance on freight, the data are extracted from import c.i.f. of goods. Financial services: FISIM elements are calculated by using relevant information from IIP survey. Other services: No adjustments are made to source data. Primary Income: No adjustments are made to source data. Secondary Income: No adjustments are made to source data. Capital account: No adjustments are made to source data. Direct investment: IIP Joint-Survey is designed for collecting quarterly data required for compiling detailed statistics on direct investment. The movements in financial assets and liabilities due to non-transactional items (market price, foreign exchange and others) are excluded from the value of transactions. Portfolio investment: IIP Joint-Survey is designed for collecting quarterly data required for compiling detailed statistics on portfolio investment. The movements in financial assets and liabilities due to non-transactional items (market price, foreign exchange and others) are excluded from the value of transactions. Other investment: IIP Joint-Survey is designed for collecting quarterly data required for compiling detailed statistics on direct investment.No adjustments are made to source data. Reserve assets: No adjustments are made to source data. Flows are computed from outstanding positions and thus include valuation changes. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Seasonal adjustment: No seasonal adjustment of balance of payments data is attempted. Geographic detail: A geographic breakdown of the customs data for merchandise trade is published according to final destination for exports and country of origin for imports by the External Trade Division of DOSM. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data Intermediate data are assessed in quarterly BOP’s Technical meeting with BNM. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs Statistical discrepancies and other problems will be resolved in quarterly BOP’s Technical meeting with BNM. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses Revision of quarterly BOP for previous year will be published together with first quarter of the current year. The public is informed of any changes in methodology after the changes have been introduced. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses The revision and final data of respective year are disseminated together with preliminary data of the first quarter of current year. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request The dissemination of requested data must comply with the provision in Statistics Act 1965 (Revised 1989). 5.3.2 Availability of documents and services catalogs Available in DOSM website ( https://www.dosm.gov.my/ ).
