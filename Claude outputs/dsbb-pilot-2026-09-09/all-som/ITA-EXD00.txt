 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Definitions: The external debt is a statistical statement showing on a quarterly basis the value and composition of Italy's gross external debt. Gross external debt is defined as the outstanding amount of all actual current, and not contingent, liabilities that require payments of principal and/or interests by the debtor at some point in the future and that are due to nonresidents by residents of the economy. External debt liabilities are attributed to the sector of the immediate borrower. Concepts: The data are compiled in accordance with the methodology and concepts set out in the sixth edition of the IMF Balance of Payments and International Investment Position Manual (BPM6), and the IMF's External Debt Statistics: Guide for Compilers and Users (2013). End-of-quarter stocks of external debt liabilities are presented broken down by: - resident sector: central bank, general government, deposit taking corporations except the central bank, other sectors; - original maturity: short and long-term; - type of instrument as specified in the IMF's Guide: Currency and deposits , Debt securities, Loans, Trade credit and advances, Other debt liabilities, Special drawing rights (allocations), Intercompany lending. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Italy's External debt is a subset of the International Investment Position. External debt liabilities are classified according to the sector category of the debtor. Data on external debt are available and disseminated for four sectors (general government, central bank, deposit-taking corporations except the central bank, other sectors), to the extent that data are compiled in accordance with the methodology and concepts set out in the sixth edition of the IMF Balance of Payments and International Investment Position Manual and in the IMF's External Debt Statistics: Guide for Compilers and Users (2013) (a) the short-term/long-term classification is on an original maturity basis. (b) Deposits of non-residents with domestic banks are recorded under currency and deposits. (c) Other debt liabilities include among the others accounts payable, arrears, liabilities of life insurance companies. External debt liabilities of households and small private enterprises are included under "other sectors". (d) External debt liabilities of the central bank are separately identified from the liabilities of the general government. Outstanding liabilities arising from periodic interest costs that have accrued and are not yet payable are included in the gross external debt position; they are classified under "bonds and notes". Data on intercompany loans under direct investment relationships are separately identified without sector attribution, and data on intercompany lending (liabilities) under direct investment relationships are separately identified without sector attribution. Repurchase agreements are treated as collateralized loans. Scope of the data Transaction coverage: external debt data cover debt positions between residents and non-residents and measure both domestic and foreign currency debt owed to non-residents. Repurchase agreements are treated as collateralized loans. In the case of debt securities: External debt data cover bonds and notes and money market instruments. Data on arrears are not separately identified. External debt includes securities issued in the domestic markets by residents and purchased by non-residents. Financial leases with non-residents are treated as loans and included in the gross external debt position. Trade credits owed to non-residents are included.. Intercompany lending: Liabilities to direct investor recorded in the International investment position statistics (under direct investment in the compiling economy) are presently recorded in the external debt statistics net of the item "claims to direct investor". Maturity coverage: Short-term debt includes liabilities with an original maturity less or equal to one year. Exceptions to coverage Transaction coverage: External debt liabilities payable in goods and/or services are not included in the external debt position. Positions in financial derivatives, equity securities, and equity capital are excluded from the gross external debt position. Geographic coverage: San Marino and Vatican City are not considered part of the Italian economic territory. Institutional coverage: Offshore branches of resident enterprises debt are not included in the gross external debt position. Unrecorded activity 2.4.1 Valuation Type of prices: Sector "Monetary authorities": listed securities are valued at end-of-month market prices; all other securities are valued at historical cost or fair value. Sectors "General government"/ "Other sectors": listed securities are valued at the market prices prevailing at the end of the reference period. Unlisted securites market values are estimated by means of information derived from Monetary financial institutions balance sheets. Sector "Deposit-taking corporations except the central bank": positions in listed and unpledged securities are valued at end-of-month market prices; all other securities are valued at the historical cost or at the fair value). Conversion to unit of account: Assets and liabilities denominated in foreign currency are converted into the unit of account at end-of-period (midpoint) exchange rates. 2.4.2 Recording basis 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Sectors "Monetary authorities" and "Deposit-taking corporations except the central bank": with the exception of liabilities in debt securities, pure stock figures for liabilities are reported on a monthly basis. Information is broken down by type of liability, currency, maturity and debtor country. Sector "General government" and "Other sectors": with the exception of liabilities in debt securities, for stocks data the main source is represented by the "Direct reporting system" ( a set of surveys which allows for the collection of flows and/or stock data from a sample of insurance and non-financial enterprises). For PI liabilities the main sources of data are the monthly s-b-s indirect reports by custodians, including the Italian Central Securities Depositary, complemented with direct reporting by Monetary financial institutions on their own assets and with estimates of securities directly held in custody abroad as derived from a sample of insurance and non-financial enterprises. Traded debt instruments issued by residents abroad but in hands of residents are to be excluded from the external debt position. At this aim, a statistical reporting to the Bank of Italy identifies the cases in which a resident buys from a non resident an Italian security issued abroad. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Currently available details for the external debt data categories do not allow disaggregation of "other sectors" into three subsectors (nonbank financial corporations, nonfinancial corporations, and NPISH). 3.1.3 Source data timeliness In general, source data are timely as regards the compilation of external debt statistics. The Bank of Italy (BI) and commercial banks report their positions within 12 working days after the end of the reference month. For non-banks sectors, Direct Reporting data are provided within 30 days after the end of the reference month. 3.2.1 Source data assessment The population of reporting banks, an essential element in the Italian data collection system, is precisely defined in the framework of ECB regulations addressing the reporting population for monetary policy purposes. A long-standing relationship with the banking community is also linked to the Bank of Italy supervisory functions on the Italian banking system. All banks and non-banks data reporting are automatically checked for internal consistency. BI provides respondents with free software supporting electronic reporting with build-in accounting checks. Once the data reach the BI, a full set of quality checks are re-run. In case of wrong or implausible returns, the respondents are contacted for solving the problem. The selection of samples in the "Direct reporting" system is based on a specific BOP business register. Nominative information on enterprises performing external transactions with non-residents, as provided by the resident banks community, are used in order to increase the efficiency of the sample selection. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Sectors "Monetary authorities" and "Deposit-taking corporations except the central bank": Pure stock figures for liabilities (with the exception of liabilities in debt securities) are provided on a monthly basis. Sector: "General government": With the exception of liabilities in debt instruments, stocks are calculated by cumulating flows to a benchmark, taking into account changes due to movements in exchange rates and market prices. Sector "Other sectors": The main source of data are stocks derived from the "Direct reporting" system (a set of surveys addressed to insurance and non-financial corporations". For PI liabilities the main sources of data are monthly s-b-s indirect reports by custodians, including the Italian Central Securities Depositary, complemented with direct reporting by Monetary financial institutions on their own assets and with estimates of securities directly held in custody abroad as derived from a sample of insurance and non-financial enterprises. Intercompany lending: Liabilities to affiliated enterprises recorded in the International investment position statistics on a directional basis (i.e. as reduction in assets for under direct investment abroad) are presently not recorded in the external debt statistics. Liabilities to direct investor recorded in the International investment position statistics (under direct investment in the compiling economy) are presently recorded in the external debt statistics net of the item "claims to direct investor". 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results A number of validations on intermediate data and statistical outputs are in place. Time series checks are performed for each reporting bank and for the main items. Time series and cross-section checks are performed for main "Direct reporting" enterprises. Also a number of external checks is performed through comparison of the survey results and balance-sheet information. The results of the annual survey on stock data ("Direct reporting" system) is used to check the accuracy of nonbanks’ provisional stocks derived by accumulation of flows. Cross-border holdings of the banks are checked against the statistics based on the consolidated balance sheets. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses Revisions are stored and assessed on an ongoing basis. In case of revisions stemming from a major break in the approach followed in data collection, surveys are addressed to anticipate the most likely direction of the revision. In some cases the studies are made available with advance communications to the public. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses Studies and analyses of revisions are made available only when major methodological changes occur, or when there is a break due to a new collection system. This is done by a press-release followed by an explanation of the reason underlying the change and the expected impact on the data. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points In the BI's Statistical database a dedicated e-mail address can be used for any questions on the disseminated data.
