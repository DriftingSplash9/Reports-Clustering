 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The Foreign Trade Department of the National Statistics Institute (INE) compiles and disseminates monthly and cumulative data for the past year, corresponding to imports and exports and the balance of trade, in volume and in millions of bolívares and U.S. dollars. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The classifications of the United Nations Harmonized System and the tariff nomenclature of the Andean Group (NANDINA) are used. Statistics are produced by International Standard Industrial Classification (ISIC), Revision 3, on a quarterly basis. Scope of the data The data include imports and exports authorized by SENIAT, the national integrated tax administration service. Imports are registered and valued when the fees and duties, including freight and insurance, are paid. The basic statistics on merchandise imports record all goods that increase or reduce the stock of material resources of a country (customs territory). Additionally, in accordance with international standards, monetary gold is not included in these data. 2.4.2 Recording basis Exports are registered and valued f.o.b. when they leave the customs territory. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The data are obtained from customs manifests, with the exception of the data on exports of electricity, for which data are submitted by the EDELCA company, and government petroleum and iron exports, which are compiled by the Ministry of Energy and Petroleum and the Ministry of Basic Industries and Mines, respectively. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques The data are adjusted to reflect goods in transit and temporarily admitted or released goods. This is because goods that are simply being transported through a country (goods in transit) or are temporarily admitted to or released from it (except for goods to be processed domestically or abroad) are not added to, and do not remain part of, the stock of material resources of a country; consequently they are not included in the international merchandise trade statistics. Finally, no seasonal adjustment is applied to the merchandise trade data. None
