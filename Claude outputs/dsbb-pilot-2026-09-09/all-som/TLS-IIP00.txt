 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The definition of IIP is taken from the IMF’s Balance of Payments Manual , Sixth Edition ( BPM6 ). 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Upon request, IIP data are available by sector. 2.2.1 Scope Upon request, IIP data are available by sector. Scope of the data The IIP data cover financial assets and liabilities. Assets cover direct investment, portfolio investment, other investment, and reserve assets. Liabilities cover direct investment, portfolio investment, and other investment. Exceptions to coverage Insurance companies are not yet covered. See also future plans (9.2.2.) 2.4.1 Valuation The valuation is in line with BPM6 recommendation, e.g., market value for debt securities and equity, and nominal value for loans and deposits. 2.4.2 Recording basis Recording is on the basis of accrual accounting. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures Timor-Leste publishes both gross and net IIP, where the net IIP is defined as the difference between its financial assets and financial liabilities. Assets are defined as the sum total of direct investment, portfolio investment, other investments, and reserve assets. Liabilities are defined as the sum total of direct investment, portfolio investment, and other investments. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Source data for the IIP are largely obtained from the BOP financial account and are checked against data sources which include: (1) Quarterly Enterprise Surveys conducted by the CBTL on foreign direct investment; (2) Other Depository Corporation survey data; (3) CBTL’s international reserves assets data, and (4) the Central Government Petroleum Fund account. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Source data are largely in line with the BPM6 requirements. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Timeliness of source data allows for the publication of the IIP in quarterly basis, which is in line with the GDDS recommendation. 3.2.1 Source data assessment No routine assessment at this stage. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques N.A. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures N.A. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses N.A. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses N.A. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request Yes.
