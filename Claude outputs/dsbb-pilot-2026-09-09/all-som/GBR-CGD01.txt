 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Definitions: Foreign Currency Central Government Gross Debt (CGGD) is the stock of foreign currency UK central government liabilities to the rest of the economy and the world. It is the foreign currency denominated central government component of General Government Gross Debt (GGGD). GGGD is as defined in the protocol annexed to the 1992 Treaty on European Union ('Maastricht Treaty'). Central Government includes all UK government departments and other central agencies whose competence extends over the whole economy. Also included are those non-profit institutions which are controlled and mainly financed by central government and whose competence also extends over the whole economy. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Classifications: All foreign currency CGGD is classified according to financial instrument and components are recorded at original maturity. The total of domestic foreign currency debt with less than one year's residual maturity is shown separately (as is total foreign currency CGGD). The central government sector is defined in accordance with the regulations of ESA 2010 and the rules and clarifications provided in the Eurostat's Manual on Government Deficit and Debt (MGDD), which are also consistent with the 2008 SNA and GFSM 2014. The ONS economic statistics classifications process is used to determine which units belong within the government sector and which transactions are taxes. https://www.ons.gov.uk/methodology/classificationsandstandards/economicstatisticsclassific ations/ukeconomicstatisticssectorandtransactionclassificationstheclassificationprocess Scope of the data The institutional coverage of the data is the same as that for central government operations. Data represent market holdings of foreign currency CGGD, defined as all holdings outside the central government sector. But holdings by local government, public corporations (government organisations with a degree of management and financial autonomy) and the Bank of England are included as part of market holdings. Foreign currency CGGD consists of the following financial instruments: Bonds and notes - consists of various issues of foreign currency bonds and notes. Short term loans - consists of debt with a maturity of less than one year. Medium and long-term loans- consists of debt with maturity greater than one year: primarily North American debt from the 1940s (which was repaid during Q4 2006) and ECS and assigned debt. This is net borrowing, primarily by UK local government, under the 1969 and subsequent exchange cover schemes (ECS). This includes foreign currency borrowed through the UK banks and from abroad, the issue of foreign currency securities and any debt owed under the scheme by privatised public corporations at the time of privatisation. The final ECS repayment was made 30 October 2007 Liabilities due to SDR allocation – these are not included in the central government debt statistics for the UK Other liabilities - liabilities arising from repurchase agreements, forward and swap positions and unsettled trades. 2.4.1 Valuation All data are recorded at nominal values, amounts outstanding at the end of each month. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures All data are recorded on a gross basis. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Monthly/quarterly accounting data, administration records and surveys.
