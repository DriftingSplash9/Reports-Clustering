 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The analytical accounts of the NBM are derived from the balance sheet of the central bank which shows on a gross basis reserve money, domestic assets and liabilities as well as foreign assets and liabilities. Reserve money and its sources are compiled and disseminated on a monthly basis by the NBM. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The standards for classifying institutional units by belonging to a particular sector correspond to analytical standards for the presentation of monetary and financial statistics data of the International Monetary Fund (Monetary and financial statistics manual and compilation guide, MFSMCG 2016). The concept of residency is consistent with the MFSMCG and 2008 System of National Accounts . The main sectors by residency include Domestic Sectors and Nonresidents. The Domestic Sectors are categorized for statistical and economic analysis purposes into the following groups based on their main activity: a) The National Bank of Moldova (Monetary authority); b) Banks; c) Non-banking financial institutions; d) Central government; e) Local governments; f) Public non-financial corporations; g) Private non-financial corporations; h) Other sectors (households and non-profit institutions serving households). Classification of financial instruments is consistent with the MFSMCG recommendations . The following groups of financial instruments are identified in monetary data compiled by the NBM: a) monetary gold and SDR; b) currency and deposits broken down into transferable deposits and other deposits; c) debt securities; d) loans; e) shares and other equity; f) other accounts receivable/payable. Maturity classification involves categorization of instruments into three categories: a) Short-term instruments, which cover transactions with a maturity of up to 1 year; b) Medium-term instruments, with an initial maturity ranging from 1 to 5 years; c) Long-term instruments, which include those with an initial maturity of more than 5 years. Scope of the data The analytical accounts of NBM are based on its accounting records. Data for the central bank are not consolidated with any other institutional unit or activity. Exceptions to coverage Unrecorded activity 2.4.1 Valuation Valuation of financial instruments - all instruments are recorded at their nominal value and are not revalued. Foreign currency denominated positions are recalculated at the corresponding official end-of-period exchange rate set by the NBM. 2.4.2 Recording basis Valuation standards – the accounts of the NBM are recorded on an accrual basis. Accrued interest receivable and payable is classified, to the extent permitted by the source data, under the appropriate sector and instrument category. Definition of the reporting period – the reporting period ends on the last calendar day of the month. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures Provisions of the NBM are included in the capital accounts within Other items (net) indicator. Grossing/netting procedures: the accounts of the NBM are collected and compiled on a gross basis. Netting is done in accordance with MFSMCG. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Data in the Central Bank Survey are an analytical consolidation of the balance sheet of the NBM. The currently available source data provide sufficient information for classification of the balance sheet accounts by financial instrument, economic sector of the counterparty, currency of denomination, and original maturity. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording The data are compiled using the balance sheet of the NBM where all financial instruments are classified in accordance with actual NBM Chart of accounts. This data source ensures that data are produced with adequate consistency with the basic concepts, definitions, scope and accounting principles of monetary statistics and compliance with the recommendations of international statistical manuals, as well as exclude the possibility of discrepancy between statistical and balance sheet data. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness The current data collection system ensures the timely receipt of source data by the MFSD for the compilation of monetary statistics and dissemination of produced statistics in accordance with the IMF SDDS requirements, on the NBM's website in line with the Advanced Release Calendar. 3.2.1 Source data assessment The NBM maintains, at all times, accounts and records adequate to reflect its operations and financial condition, in accordance with sound internationally accepted accounting practices. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Central bank Survey is compiled on the basis of automatically reclassified NBM balance sheet. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures The correct conversion of data concerning financial instruments denominated in foreign currency is checked in the process of the NBM's verification of compliance of data sources with accounting and reporting rules. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results The accuracy of balance sheets and supplementary information from data sources is verified, wherever possible, by cross-checking with data sets available primarily from the Department of Financial Markets and the Department of Budget, Finance and Accounting. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data The MFSD assesses statistical discrepancies in intermediate data and identifies the main causes of the discrepancies. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs In the case of significant statistical discrepancies and fluctuations in monetary aggregates, the causes are thoroughly analyzed. If necessary, revisions are made. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses The nature and the scope or revisions are studied on a regular basis. The conclusions and revisions undertaken during the data compilation process are taken into account in the compilation of data for future periods. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses Revisions are subject to measurement, evaluation, and clarification. 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points Users of monetary data are provided with assistance and support. 5.3.2 Availability of documents and services catalogs The NBM’s website provides users with information about electronic publications and documents.
