 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Concepts, definitions and conceptual framework used for compiling IIP statistics are to a large extent in line with the sixth edition of the IMF Balance of Payments and International Investment Position Manual (BPM6). The IIP is defined as the statistical statement that provides information on stock positions of Serbia’s external financial assets and liabilities at the end of the reporting period. IIP consists of two broad categories: assets and liabilities, which cover following items: (1) direct investment (equity and debt instruments), (2) portfolio investment (equity and debt securities), (3) financial derivatives and employee stock options, (4) other investment, (5) reserve assets (only assets side). 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The classification of stock position is broadly consistent with guidelines set in the sixth edition of Balance of Payments Manual (BPM6). 2.2.1 Scope The classification of stock position is broadly consistent with guidelines set in the sixth edition of Balance of Payments Manual (BPM6). Scope of the data The scope is broadly consistent with internationally accepted standards, covering all types of financial assets and liabilities between residents and nonresidents at the end of the reporting period. Exceptions to coverage No data are systematically excluded. 2.4.1 Valuation Assets and liabilities are mostly valued at market prices (loans are valued at nominal value, while unlisted enterprises are valued at own funds at book values) and converted to the euro using official end-of-period middle exchange rate of the National bank of Serbia for stocks conversion. 2.4.2 Recording basis Loans are recorded on due-for-payments basis. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures IIP data are presented on a gross basis. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Direct investments : Data are collected through enterprise survey on foreign direct investment, reports relating to external debt on loan-by-loan basis, Central Depository and Clearing House and Business Registry Data. Portfolio investments: Portfolio investments data are compiled using Central Depository and Clearing House data and direct reporting on portfolio investments. Other investments: Data on foreign assets and liabilities are compiled from reports of residents on loan-by-loan basis, reports regarding external assets and liabilities of banking sector as well as insurance and pension funds (reported for monetary and financial statistics purposes), direct report of residents on accounts abroad. Financial derivatives: Estimates made based on transactions data. Reserve assets: The National Bank of Serbia’s department responsible for management of reserve assets is the source of data. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording The source data generally cover definitions, scope, classifications, valuation and time recording, almost fully in line with the BPM6 methodology. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Source data are collected within adequate time in line with set timeliness and periodicity for disseminating IIP. 3.2.1 Source data assessment Source data are regularly assessed and cross-checked for coverage and response error. The results of the assessments are for internal use only. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Direct investments : Data are collected through enterprise reports on foreign direct investment, reports relating to external debt on loan-by-loan basis, Central Depository and Clearing House and Business Registry Data. Stock of direct investments is corrected for non-respondents using annual data from financial statements of enterprises sent to the Business Registry Agency. Portfolio investments: Portfolio investments data are compiled using Central Depository and Clearing House data and direct reporting on portfolio investments. Other investments: Data on foreign assets and liabilities are compiled from reports of residents on loan-by-loan basis, reports regarding external assets and liabilities of banking sector as well as insurance and pension funds (reported for monetary and financial statistics purposes), direct report of residents on accounts abroad. Financial derivatives: Estimates made based on transactions data. Reserve assets: The National Bank of Serbia’s department responsible for management of reserve assets is the source of data. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures There are no additional data adjustments. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results Cross-checking is done for verifying the accuracy of the data. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data Intermediate data provided by reporters are analyzed and in the case of unusual data reporters are contacted to obtain additional information. Changes in the stock data are reconciled with changes in the corresponding financial assets/liabilities data collected for the financial account items of the balance of payments. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs Statistical discrepancies and other potential indicators of problems in statistical outputs are monitored and investigated. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses Analyses of revisions are regularly conducted (before publication of new IIP) and are for internal use. During the year previous quarterly data are revised with publishing of new quarter. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses Studies and analyzes of the revisions are conducted as a part of the data quality control process. Studies and analyses of revisions are used internally. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request It is possible to provide unpublished but non-confidential statistics on official request.
