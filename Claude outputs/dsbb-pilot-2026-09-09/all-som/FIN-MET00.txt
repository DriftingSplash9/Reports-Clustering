 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The compilation of Finnish trade statistics follows the internationally agreed principles for statistics of international trade in goods. EU legislation serves as the analytical framework for compiling the international trade in goods statistics: EBS Basic Act, Regulation (EU) No 2019/2152 of the European Parliament and of the Council. EBS General Implementing Act (EBS GIA): Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 2020/1197. EBS Delegated Act (EBS DA): Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2021/1704. Finland's international trade statistics is based on EU-regulations and follows the Community concept with the exception that for intra-EU arrivals, like some other Member States, Finland records also the country of origin as the partner country, whereas the Member State of consignment appears in Community statistics relating to the same movements. Statistics are compiled following Eurostat's compilers manual for European statistics on international trade in goods. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Commodity coding: The Harmonized System (HS) and Combined Nomenclature (CN). Standard International Trade Classification system (SITC), Revision 4 (from 2007 onwards). Classification of Products by Activities (CPA). Nomenclature générale des activités économiques dans les communautés européennes (NACE). Classification by Broad Economis Categories (BEC). Scope of the data Data to be registered into statistics The Finnish international trade statistics follow the European Union concept. As a general rule all goods exported from and imported into Finland incur in the statistics on the internal and external commodity trade. The trade of services is not included in these statistics. The compilation of statistics requires that the merchandise arrives in or departs from the country physically, except for ships and aircrafts for which the compilation takes place after the ownership has changed. On the other hand, it is not important from the viewpoint of the compilation of statistics whether any payments take place at the importation or exportation of the merchandise Exceptions to coverage Data not to be registered into statistics Certain phenomena have not been included in the official International trade in goods statistics, the following specification includes data not included in the international trade in goods statistics: 1. Any discounts granted are deducted, and goods delivered free of charge are valued on the basis of the price of similar products exported or imported. The statistical value of processed goods is determined in both intra-Community and external trade as if those goods were entirely obtained in the country of processing. The statistical value of goods imported or exported for processing is the value of the goods at the date of movement of the goods. When returning goods after processing the value of processing as invoiced as well as the value of additional materials are added. 2.4.2 Recording basis Conversion to unit of account: Values of external trade are converted into euro based on the monthly exchange rates published by the Finnish Customs. The reference rate published by the European Central Bank on the penultimate Wednesday of each month is used for currency conversion to determine the customs value at customs clearance. The same exchange rates are used for imports and exports. In intra-Community trade, declarants may choose between the above-mentioned exchange rates and the rates applied for value added taxation on internal trade. The rates applied for value added taxation on internal trade are either based on the date of movement of the goods or on the invoice date. In intrastat declarations values are in euro. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures Not applicable 3.1.1 Source data collection programs For extra-trade: The source of the data are customs records, for intra-trade: the EU-intrastat system and the VAT system. From 2022 it is possible to use in imports also data collected by an other EU member state, which is exchanged between the member states. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Type of data collected: Intra-trade: CN8 product code, statistical value (partly optional), invoice value, partner country, country of origin, partner ID, mode of transport, nature of transaction, quantity (kilogram/net and supplementary units). Extra-trade: CN8 product code for exports, statistical value, quantity (kilogram/net and supplementary units), country of origin, and country of consignment for imports, country of destination for exports, nature of transaction, mode of transport, internal mode of transport, container transport code, the actual Member State of consignment for exports, the actual Member State of destination for imports, customs procedure code. Basis of country data: for exports: country of destination. For imports: country of origin, also country of consignment is collected. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness For extra-trade, timing of recording is determined by the date of import or export of the goods irrespective of the date of clearance. For intra-trade, timing of recording is determined by a combination of the invoice date and the date of the movement of the goods. 3.2.1 Source data assessment Non-response and below threshold trade are estimated on HS8 level and partner country in EU internal trade from the statistical year 2023. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Processing system: ADP System; allowing the declarations to be given in computer readable form, and including automatic validation checks based on different logical and conditional tests. Manual controlling of discrepancies and contacting data providers when needed. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Seasonal adjustment is calculated for preliminary data on total import and export, on EU-level import and export and on Euro zone level import and export using TRAMO SEATS –method recommended by Eurostat. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results Adjustments to improve coverage/classification/timing/valuation: Monthly control based on VAT declarations (only for Intra-trade). 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses Monthly revisions and analyses on statistical data.
