 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Concept, Definition, and Relationship to international guidelines: In line with the BPM6 methodological requirements and the ECB recommendation on Eurosystem reserve assets (see methodological note http://www.ecb.int ). The reserve assets must: (i) be under the effective control of the Bank of Finland and (ii) refer to highly liquid, marketable and creditworthy non-euro area currency-denominated claims on non-residents of the euro area as well as gold, special drawing rights and the reserve positions in the IMF of Finland. Deposits with banks located in Finland and/or in other euro area Member States are therefore not considered as part of the official reserves. Reserve assets are compiled on a gross basis without any netting of reserve-related liabilities (with the exception of those reserve assets included in the sub-item of financial derivatives, which are recorded on a net basis). All reversible gold transactions are treated as collateralised loans and recorded accordingly in the national reserves statistics. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization In line with the BPM6 methodological requirements and the ECB recommendation on Eurosystem reserve assets (see methodological note http://www.ecb.int ). Scope of the data Institutional coverage: Bank of Finland. Instrument coverage: All official reserve assets are readily available. Financial derivatives coverage: For the template on international reserves financial derivatives are identified separately as a sum total. 2.4.1 Valuation Valuation: International reserves are valued at market prices. Conversion rates: Reserves data is converted into the national currency and into a U.S. dollar equivalent at ECB Euro Reference Rates of the last banking day of the month. 2.4.2 Recording basis Frequency of calculation and revaluation: International reserves data is calculated and revalued monthly for statistical reporting. The balance sheet of the Bank of Finland is valued to market price quarterly. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures Reserve assets are compiled on a gross basis without any netting of reserve-related liabilities (with the exception of those reserve assets included in the sub-item of financial derivatives, which are recorded on a net basis). 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Data sources: The general ledger and the banking system of the Bank of Finland. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Statistical adjustments: None. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results Data verification: The reserve assets figures are compared with the Bank of Finland balance sheet figures (for details see summary methodology for analytical accounts of the central bank).
