 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The methodological provisions take into account the UN Statistical Division recommendations, «Statistics of international trade in goods: concepts and definitions», «Statistics of international trade in goods: manual for compilers (IMTS 2010-CM)».The methodological provisions are also incorporate the requirements of EU Regulation No 2019/2152 of November 27, 2019 of the European Parliament and of Council on European business statistics. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The Ukrainian Classification of Commodities in External Economic Activity (UKTZED) based on 6-digit Harmonized Commodity Description and Coding Systems (HS) and 8-digit Combined Nomenclature (CN) of the European Union is used to record exports and imports of goods. Also, the List of codes of the world countries for statistical purposes and the List of codes of currencies for statistical purposes are used. They are approved by SSSU decrees No 32 and 33 (with changes) accordingly dated on January 8, 2020. Scope of the data In Ukraine's statistics of external trade in goods, the general trade system in which the boundaries of the statistical territory of the country coincide with its economic territoriy is applied. Geographical coverage: data exclude the territories temporarily occupied by the russian federation and part of territories where combat actions are/were carried out. Statistical observations1 over the volumes of external trade in goods are conducted on the basis of data from the customs declarations and forms of the state statistical observation : Quarterly: Report on acquisition/sale of goods to ensure life activity of transport means, needs of the passengers and crew members. Exceptions to coverage Statistical observation covers goods the customs value of which exceeds the amount prescribed by the national legislation for written declaration of goods by customs authority. Statistical threshold for legal persons and natural persons-entrepreneurs is 100 EUR; for natural persons it is 200 EUR (300 EUR per commodity unit) with weight limitation of 50 kg. Unrecorded activity Informal ("shuttle") trade. 2.4.1 Valuation Value of goods is determined by: for exported goods - at FOB prices; for imported goods - at CIF prices. The statistical value is calculated by converting the value of goods into US dollars at the rate set by the National Bank of Ukraine on the date the customs declarations were completed. For goods that are submitted according to statistical reports, the recalculation of the value of commodities into US dollars is made using the average quarterly official exchange rates set by the National Bank of Ukraine. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Statistical observations over the volumes of external trade in goods are based on data from the customs declarations and statistical reports of enterprises and organizations of Ukraine. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Mainly it meets the EU statistical requirements (module 2.3.2 of the EU Statistical Compendium). 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Meets the requirements to timeliness of provision. 3.2.1 Source data assessment Information obtained from respondents is subject to logical and arithmetical control, comparisons of relevant indicators in time series. Data from all information sources used to compile statistics of foreign trade in goods are analyzed as to their conformity with the methodology for statistics of foreign trade in goods. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Pursuant to the Agreement on Information Cooperation between specially authorized central body of executive power that implements the state policy in the sphere of the state customs procedures and specially authorized central body of executive power in the sphere of statistics, the last one receives the revised information on exports/imports of goods based on the data from e-copies of customs declarations as of March 1 of the year, following the reporting one. Based on these data, the specially authorized central body of executive power in the sphere of statistics compiles the final annual data. If it detects errors, the request to the authorized central body is submitted to make corrections. When making changes to any national statistical classification used to compile statistics on external trade in goods, the revision and adjustment of the time series are made. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Seasonal data adjustment is made. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results The control over reported data correctness is made by staff from territorial statistics bodies (at regional level) by comparing data from the previous periods, making logical and arithmetical controls using specially tailored software. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses Data for the reference period are published along with the revised information for the previous period. Data are monthly updated. The final revision of data is made in May next to the reference year. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses If earlier published data are revised, the revised data are given in the forthcoming publications along with the relevant SSSU explanatory notes to them. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request Information requested by users may be prepared as tables by observing the principles of confidentiality. 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points Publications contain information about the experts responsible for their compilation. 5.3.2 Availability of documents and services catalogs Catalogue of Official Statistical Publications is posted on the SSSU website at www.stat.gov.ua ( https://stat.gov.ua/uk/publications ).
