 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Polish fiscal aggregates disseminated under the SDDS are compiled: 1) according to the ESA 2010 starting from 2015 (annual data for 2014): According to the ESA 2010 data on general government operations are presented on accrual basis and consolidated i.e. after the elimination of transactions (property income, other current transfers and capital transfers) between all units within the general government sector. 2) in accordance with the Government Finance Statistics Manual, the IMF, 2001 (GFSM 2001) for 2002-2013; According to the GFSM 2001 framework data on general government operations are presented on an accrual basis and after the elimination of transactions between all units within the general government sector. Additionally the table “Statement of Sources and Uses of Cash” is compiled on cash basis. All the GFSM 2001 data is published in “Poland Government Finance Statistics 2002–2013”. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The annual reports cover a detailed presentation of comprehensive fiscal data on operations of the general government sector divided by central government, local government and social security funds. The Statistics Poland monitors continuously the scope of the general government sector. 2.2.1 Scope The annual reports cover a detailed presentation of comprehensive fiscal data on operations of the general government sector divided by central government, local government and social security funds. The Statistics Poland monitors continuously the scope of the general government sector. Scope of the data The Statistics Poland compiles accounts of general government divided into 3 subsectors: central government, local government and social security (according to the ESA). Data for social security funds for the IMF purposes are presented with central subsector. General government sector includes entities of the national economy: a) operating according to principles described in Law on Public Finances ( budgetary units, appropriated funds and since 2010 also local budgetary establishments, executive agencies and budget institutions as well as until 2005 special funds of budgetary units, in 2005 - 2010 own income account of budgetary units and until 2010 - budgetary establishments and auxiliary units of budgetary units including conducting economic activity ones as well as motivation funds), b) entities for which the financial system was defined in special regulations, and for which the subsides from the state budget are the main source of financing (public higher education institutions, the Polish Academy of Sience and entities established by it, the National Road Fund and other funds managed by Bank Gospodarstwa Krajowego, agriculture advisory units and state agencies), c) independent public self-government health care facilities, d) state and local government institutions of culture and national film institutions, e) funds with the legal personality which are connected with state or local government units budgets, f) institutions managing the social security funds (Social Insurance Institution, Agricultural Social Insurance Fund) and these funds as well as the National Health Fund (in the period 1.01.1999 - 31.03.2003 - health funds), g) public hospitals operating in the form of limited companies and public hospitals operating in the form of research institutes, h) Bank Guarantee Fund, i) other units, including public corporations and non-commercial institutions that meet the ESA 2010 classification criteria to the general government sector, j) non-profit institutions controlled by general government sector, k) captive financial institutions, l) public units in liquidation with ratio below 50% (sales to costs), m) agricultural advisory units, n) State Treasury Foundations, o) non-market public transport companies. A detailed list of general government sector units and also a press release concerning general government deficit and debt data is published on the Statistics Poland website: http://stat.gov.pl/obszary-tematyczne/rachunki-narodowe/statystyka-sektora-instytucji-rzad owych-i-samorzadowych Data for the IMF purposes are published on the Statistics Poland website: http://stat.gov.pl/banki-i-bazy-danych/sdds/dane-gospodarcze-i-finansowe-dla-polski/ The disseminated details include following components: transactions affecting net worth (revenue, expense, the gross operating balance, and net operating balance), transactions in nonfinancial assets (net acquisition of nonfinancial assets), net lending/borrowing, and transactions in financial assets and liabilities—financing (net acquisition of financial assets, net incurrence of liabilities). Net acquisition of financial assets and net incurrence of liabilities are broken down into domestic and foreign transactions. Exceptions to coverage The annual government finance statistics cover all institutional components of the general government sector. The scope of general government sector is determined jointly by the Statistics Poland, the Ministry of Finance and the National Bank of Poland according to the ESA 2010, Manual of General Government Deficit and Debt (MGDD), guidances and decisions of the Eurostat. Unrecorded activity All identified activities are recorded in the GFS. Stocks of assets, holding gains in assets and liabilities, other changes in the volume of assets and liabilities are not disseminated. 2.4.1 Valuation GFS data are disseminated in millions of zlotys. 2.4.2 Recording basis All general government operations are compiled on an accrual basis. Only the statement of sources and uses of cash is compiled on a cash basis. All revenues and expenditures of general government units are codified into the ESA 2010 transaction. For budget reporting and profit and loss accounts we use key to transition to the ESA 2010 classification. Data from budget reporting are on cash basis and we have to adjust this data to accrual basis. Algorithm of transition from cash to accrual data is as follows: For revenues central and local budgetary units: Claims from year n minus Total outstanding claims from year n-1 plus Overpayments from year n-1). For expenditures: central government budgetary units : Expenditure execution from year n plus Execution from year n form Rb28NW (Expenditures executions, which has not expired with the end of current budgetary year) plus Liabilities stock at the end of the reporting period from year n minus Liabilities stock at the end of the reporting period from year n-1. local government budgetary units: Expenditure execution from year n minus Expenditures executions, which has not expired with the end of current budgetary year from year n plus Expenditures executions, which has not expired with the end of current budgetary year from year n-1 plus Liabilities stock at the end of the reporting period from year n minus Liabilities stock at the end of the reporting period from year n-1. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures All transactions in non-financial assets are recorded as "net acquisition" of a particular asset (net transactions in fixed assets, valuables, and non-produced assets equal acquisitions minus disposals and consumption of fixed capital). Information on gross fixed capital formation (GFCF) includes outlays on new fixed assets as well as improvement of existing ones, i.e. rebuilding, enlargement, modernization, reconstruction. In those accounts routine repairs and maintenance are not included. Transactions in financial assets and liabilities are presented as the "net acquisition" of each category of financial assets and net incurrence of each category of liability. When the same type of financial instrument is held both as a financial asset and a liability, transactions in financial assets are presented separately from transactions in liabilities. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The Statistics Poland has access to data sources based on public accounts under the Law on Official Statistics dated 29 June 1995 and the annual regulations of the Council of Ministers on the PBSSP. The aggregated data is delivered to the Statistics Poland in electronic editable format by mail on address of Informatic Repository of All-Poland Statistical Data (ISODS) in Statistical Computing Centre of the Statistics Poland. However, in accordance with the above ordinance individual data are received directly from administrators of data by staff of Statistical Computing Centre according to prepared procedures guaranteeing the security of the transmission and acceptance, along with an accompanying transfer protocol. All data available in an electronic format are stored on the servers in the Statistics Poland. Data delivered in database are aggregated without the possibility to match revenues and expenditures with the particular budgetary unit. Normally, data for particular unit are obtained from various suppliers in different forms. In case of receiving different data, the Statistics Poland contacts suppliers and verify data. The main reason of differences are various types of data aggregation (for example: profit and loss accounts in comparative and calculative versions). All above data relate only to central government units and it is not necessary to exclude any flows for non-financial and financial accounts. All positions in profit and loss statements and balance sheets are classified to the particular ESA transactions. Most of positions in financial statements allow us to distinguish ESA categories, but there are some items, for which we have some problems. These are: other revenues and other expenditure from profit and loss statements, other receivables, other liabilities, prepayments and accruals from balance sheets. The outstanding amounts of assets and liabilities in balance sheets are not split enough to identify particular revenue and expenditure. There are only some items in balance sheets like: payables and liabilities for goods and services, payables and liabilities relating to taxes, subsidies, duties, social contributions and other benefits, liabilities relating to wages and salaries. Data from budget reporting which is used for the compilation of financial accounts are sufficient to make consolidation and identify counterpart sector. There are some units (public corporations, funds managed by BGK), for which we do not have budget reporting and we use only balance sheets. Data from profit and loss statements and balance sheets are not fully adequate to make consolidation in non-financial accounts and in financial accounts. In order to make proper consolidation we compare data with budgetary reports for state budget and we directly contact entity to get more information. Information from financial statements does not identify counterpart sector. We can only assume this on the kind of unit’s activity. Statistical surveys used as a basic data source, concern: - public health care institutions, - institutions of culture, - public universities, - public corporations and other units that are classified to the general government sector, but do not belong to public finance sector. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Source data are based on national concepts and definitions, different from the ESA2010/GFSM 2014 methodology. Adjustments are needed in the process of compiling GFS data, in particular concerning the state budget and local budgets data, which are recorded on a cash basis, whereas other general government units' data are recorded on an accrual basis. According to the national methodology institutional coverage of the public finance sector excludes the National Road Fund and other funds managed by Bank Gospodarstwa Krajowego (Municipal Investments Development Fund (until 2013), National Capital Fund, Student Loan and Credit Fund, Thermomodernization Fund, Inland Waterways Fund, Zone Fund, Subsidy Fund, Railway Fund, Borrowers Support Fund, National Guarantee Fund, Polish Science Fund, Counteracting Fund COVID-19, Liquidity Guarantee Fund, Ecological Sureties and Guarantees Fund, Interest Rate Subsidy Fund, Tourist Refund Fund), health care institutions in the form of companies, hospitals in the form of research institutions, and public corporations, which meet the ESA 2010 criteria for the general government sector and also non-profit institutions controlled by general government sector, captive financial institutions, public units in liquidation with ratio below 50% (sales to cost), State Treasury's Foundations and non-market transport companies. Data on the general government debt are presented at market value (State Treasury securities) and nominal value. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness The deadline for receiving data are described in the PBSSP and it is sufficient to calculate data in September. 3.2.1 Source data assessment During the compilation of government finance statistics some of source data are preliminary. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Please see point 2.3 and 2.4. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures To compile statistics for the activities of sub-sector level, the accounts for all their budgets, funds, and other units have been collected and consolidated, eliminating payments and receipts between organs of this level and combining all "external" transactions in commonly defined categories (intergovernmental consolidation). Next, in the process of intergovernmental consolidation, payments and receipts between levels of government have been eliminated. So, all identified transactions between units of general government (property income, other current transfers and capital transfers) have been eliminated in consolidation. In consolidating transactions adjustments are made for any discrepancies between payments and receipts to ensure symmetry of consolidation. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results Validation of intermediate results is carried out by performing manual data checks and validation procedures (e.g. compilation of growth rates, year over year comparisons, reconciliation with background information such as business reports). Reconciliation with the financial accounts is carried out. Checks whether the compilations are accurate, proper and consistent, comparisons with previous reporting periods and analyses of inconsistencies are implemented. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data Assessment of intermediate data is carried out: after the validation step still existing unexplained deviations are analyzed in detail. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs Discrepancies are analyzed and discussed within the GGSWG and with data providers if necessary. Different data sources are cross checked. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses Revision studies and analyses are performed both in the Statistics Poland and the Eurostat. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses GFS data calculated according to the ESA are published with all information about revisions.
