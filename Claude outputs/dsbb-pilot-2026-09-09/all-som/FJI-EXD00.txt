 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The definition of external debt is consistent with the definition in the IMF's 2014 Manual of Government Finance Statistics; and the external residency criteria are consistent with the IMF's Balance of Payments Manual, Sixth Edition (BPM6). 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Data on (a) and (b) are broken down by: Creditor, (multilateral, bilateral, private) Debtor (public sector institutions) Instruments (loans, securities, trade credits) Original maturity (short, medium, long term) Loans are broken down by currency. The debt service schedule (d) includes data on interest, amortisation of principal, and total debt service payments. The schedule includes historical data as well as forward servicing requirements. Quarterly and annual data are generated. 2.2.1 Scope Data on (a) and (b) are broken down by: Creditor, (multilateral, bilateral, private) Debtor (public sector institutions) Instruments (loans, securities, trade credits) Original maturity (short, medium, long term) Loans are broken down by currency. The debt service schedule (d) includes data on interest, amortisation of principal, and total debt service payments. The schedule includes historical data as well as forward servicing requirements. Quarterly and annual data are generated. Scope of the data Data available from CSDRMS include: (a) External Debt of the public sector (b) External Debt guaranteed by the public sector (c) External debt of the private sector, not guaranteed by the public sector (d) Debt service schedule for (a) and (b) for two years ahead The public sector consists of the general government (central and local government) and public sector (non financial) institutions. Data is in millions of Fijian Dollars and cover the fiscal year (Jan-Dec). Stock data are converted to Fijian Dollars using current exchange rates. Debt is recognized when disbursements of funds are effected. 2.4.1 Valuation Stocks and flows are recorded at current market price. 2.4.2 Recording basis Central Government External Debt is on cash accounting basis. Private Sector, Public Corporations and Local Government External Debt is on accrual basis. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Public External Debt [Central Government only] data is compiled by the Ministry of Economy using the Commonwealth Secretariat Debt Recording amp; Management System [CS-DRMS] while the Private Sector External Debt including Public Corporation External Debt is compiled by the Fiji Bureau of Statistics [FBoS] using the International Investment Survey [IIS] as the main data source. The public external debt CS-DRMS database is housed at the Ministry of Economy and data is updated monthly. The CS-DRMS database on public external debt stores information on central government and public corporation debt. The database can generate reports on the currency composition of the debt, the maturity profile, the category of lending institution, the loan portfolio by economic sector composition, debt servicing forecasts, etc. The private external debt figure is sourced from the quarterly International Investment Survey that is conducted by FBoS. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Central Government debt data– Monthly Private and Public Corporations debt data - Quarterly 3.2.1 Source data assessment The survey covers all the Private Establishments and Public Corporations that have external debt with rest of the world. The information from surveys is verified with the Overseas Exchange Transactions [OET].
