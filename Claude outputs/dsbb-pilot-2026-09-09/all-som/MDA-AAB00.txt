 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions A monetary survey is compiled and disseminated monthly, broadly in accordance with concepts and definitions described in Monetary and financial statistics manual and compilation guide (MFSMG, 2016). 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Monetary and financial statistics are based on the classification of transactions according to the counterpart residence. In general, in accordance with the IMF's Monetary and financial statistics manual and compilation guide (MFSMCG, 2016) and 2008 System of National Accounts (SNA) the residence criterion is based on the counterpart's center of predominant economic interest. The classification of the sectors of the Moldovan economy, for statistical and economic analysis purposes, is broken down by their primary activity into the following sectors: a) The National Bank of Moldova (Monetary authority) b) Banks c) Non-banking financial institutions d) Central government e) Local governments f) Public non-financial corporations g) Private non-financial corporations h) Other sectors (households and non-profit institutions serving households) Classification of financial instruments is consistent with the MFSMCG recommendations. The following groups of financial instruments are identified in monetary data compiled by the NBM: a) monetary gold and SDR; b) currency and deposits broken down into transferable deposits and other deposits; c) debt securities; d) loans; e) shares and other equity; f) other accounts receivable/payable. Maturity classification: a) Short-term instruments cover transactions with a maturity of up to 1 year; b) Medium-term instruments, with an initial maturity ranging from 1 to 5 years; c) Long-term instruments, which include those with an initial maturity of more than 5 years. Classification System Net Foreign Assets is the difference between foreign assets and foreign liabilities of the Moldovan banking system. The part of foreign assets which belongs to NBM constitute the foreign reserves. With regards to banks, foreign assets mainly comprise holdings of foreign currency, claims on non-resident foreign depository corporations, securities issued by non-residents, shares and other capital invested in non-resident financial corporations by banks. Foreign liabilities are mainly composed of liabilities to the non-resident foreign depository corporations, loans from non-residents and international financial institutions, deposits of non-resident legal entities and individuals. Net domestic assets include Net Claims on General Government and Net Claims on the national economy. Claims on General Government include claims on the Moldovan Government, while claims on the national economy include claims on non-financial corporations which majority ownership is public, non-financial corporations which majority ownership is private, non-banking financial institutions and other resident sectors. Monetary aggregates include currency in circulation M0, narrow money M1, broad money M2, and money supply M3. M0 is defined as currency outside the banking system; M1 is defined as M0 plus sight deposits in domestic currency; M2 equals M1 plus term deposits in domestic currency plus money market instruments. Money supply M3 includes M2 plus foreign currency deposits. Detailed monthly balance sheet data are compiled and disseminated separately for the NBM and for banks. Scope of the data Unit Coverage: The monetary survey covers the accounts of the NBM and banks. Transaction Coverage: All operations are included. Exceptions to coverage Geographic Coverage: All Moldovan banks located in the Republic of Moldova, excluding banks operating on the left side of river Nistru and municipality Bender. 2.4.1 Valuation Valuation of financial instruments: all instruments are recorded at their nominal value and are not revalued. Valuation of foreign currency denominated positions: foreign currency positions are recalculated at the corresponding official end-of-period exchange rate set by the NBM. 2.4.2 Recording basis Valuation standards: the accounts of the NBM and those of the banks are recorded on an accrual basis. Accrued interest receivable and payable is classified, to the extent permitted by the source data, under the appropriate sector and instrument category. Definition of the reporting period – the reporting period ends on the last calendar day of the month. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures Provisions: general and specific provisions of the NBM and banks are included in the capital accounts within Other items (net) indicator. Grossing/netting procedures: the accounts of the NBM and Moldovan banks are collected and compiled on a gross basis. Netting is done in accordance with MFSMCG. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Data in the Depository corporations survey for the NBM are an analytical consolidation of the balance sheet of the NBM and banks. Banks data are obtained from reports, where balance sheet accounts are disaggregated by the principle of counterpart residency sector attribution, instrument and currency. All Moldovan banks follow the same chart of accounts, which facilitates data collection and classification. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording The data are compiled using the balance sheets of the NBM and other Moldovan banks, where all financial instruments are classified in accordance with the Chart of Accounts of NBM and with that the banks. These data sources ensure that data are produced with adequate consistency with the basic concepts, definitions, scope and accounting principles of monetary statistics and compliance with the recommendations of international statistical manuals, as well as exclude the possibility of discrepancy between statistical and balance sheet data. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness The current data collection system ensures the timely receipt of source data by the MFSD for the compilation of monetary statistics and dissemination of produced statistics in accordance with the IMF SDDS requirements, on the NBM's website in line with the Advanced Release Calendar. 3.2.1 Source data assessment The NBM maintains, at all times, accounts and records adequate to reflect its operations and financial condition, in accordance with sound internationally accepted accounting practices. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques The NBM collects data from Moldovan banks pursuant to Articles 84 and 85 of the Law on the activity of banks, which require that banks shall submit to the central bank reports in a format, content and time limits established by the NBM. Monthly data concerning monetary statistics are reported to the NBM within 5 calendar days of the end of the reference month. Reported data are validated by the NBM through different mathematical, logical and other checks and cross-checks with other reports. Compilation process is usually finalized within 20 days and compiled data are disseminated thereafter. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures The correct conversion of data concerning financial instruments denominated in foreign currency is checked in the process of the NBM's verification of compliance by banks with accounting and reporting rules. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results The accuracy of balance sheets and supplementary information from data sources is verified, wherever possible, by cross-checking with data sets available primarily from the Department of Financial Markets, the Department of Banking Supervision, and the Department of Budget, Finance and Accounting. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data The MFSD assesses statistical discrepancies in intermediate data and identifies the main causes of the discrepancies. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs In the case of significant statistical discrepancies and fluctuations in monetary aggregates, the causes are thoroughly analyzed and, if necessary, revisions are made. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses Final data are compared to the preliminary data and the nature and scope of revisions are studied on a regular basis. The conclusions and revisions undertaken during the data compilation process are taken into account in the compilation of data for future periods. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses Revisions are subject to measurement, evaluation, and clarification. 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points Users of monetary data are provided with assistance and support. 5.3.2 Availability of documents and services catalogs The NBM's website provides users with information about electronic publications and documents.
