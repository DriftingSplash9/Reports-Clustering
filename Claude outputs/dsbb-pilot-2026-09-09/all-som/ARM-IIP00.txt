 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Analytical framework: The underlying conceptual framework for the international investment position is the Sixth edition of Balance of Payments and International investment position Manual ( BPM6) . Data on end-of-period stocks of external financial assets and liabilities are presented under the following categories: direct investment (broken down into equity and investment fund shares and debt instruments), portfolio investment (broken down into equity and investment fund shares and debt securities), financial derivatives (other than reserves) and employee stock options, other investment (broken down into other capital; currency and deposits; loans; insurance, pension, and standardized guarantee schemes; trade credit and advances and other accounts receivable) and reserve assets. Standard components consist of the beginning and the end of period positions of external assets and liabilities, and changes in positions during the period associated with transactions, exchange rate changes, price changes, and other adjustments. Definition: International Investment Position is the statistical statement of the value and composition of the stock of the Armenia’s external financial assets and liabilities at the end of the reference period (quarter, year). Relationship to international guidelines: IIP of RA is compiled by the Central bank of Armenia (CBA) in line with the concepts, definitions, and classifications outlined in the BPM6 . 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Classification: For the classification of external financial assets and liabilities standard components according to the BPM6 are used. 2.2.1 Scope Classification: For the classification of external financial assets and liabilities standard components according to the BPM6 are used. Scope of the data Data cover the entire economy of the Republic of Armenia, and include all operations between residents and nonresidents. Quarterly and annual BOP data are disseminated on the basis of standard components, in accordance with the requirements of the IMF Balance of Payments Manual and International Investment Position, Sixth Edition ( BPM6 ). Transactions coverage: All transactions are covered, defined by BPM6 methodology. Geographic coverage: The geographical territory of Republic of Armenia (RA) matches with its economic territory. Unrecorded activity Unrecorded activity: No estimates are made for outflow investments of the private sector abroad. 2.4.1 Valuation Conversion to unit of account: Data in several currencies are converted to common currency (U.S. dollars) on the exchange rate adopted by the Central Bank at the last day of the reference period. Valuation: External financial assets and liabilities are valued at the book values, except government securities and asset side banking sector securities. 2.4.2 Recording basis Recording basis: Majority positions in IIP are recorded on accrual basis. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures External financial assets and liabilities are recorded on a net basis. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs CBA uses some own data for IIP compilation. It obtains also data from all financial institutions, Ministry of Finance of RA, as well as fromArmstat, which collects data from non bank private corporations having external financial assets and liabilities with nonresidents. List of such entities has first been made up based on the survey results on foreign investment organizations conducted by the Armstat in 1997. The surveyed organizations’ list is periodically updated based on the business register data of the Armstat. Direct investment: Direct investment data in Armenia are reported to the Armstat quarterly broken down into equity and investment fund shares and debt instruments by organizations with foreign investment (organizations, in which a direct foreign investor owns no less than 10% of shares or voting power and subsidiaries, in which a direct foreign investor owns more than 50 percent of shares or voting power). Data on direct investment abroad are statistically insignificant. Portfolio investment: Portfolio investment data on banking sector and general government sector are collected quarterly by the CBA and data on the real sector are reported by the organizations with foreign investment. Data are broken down into equity and investment fund shares and debt instruments. Financial derivatives (other than reserves) and employee stock options: There are no financial derivative markets in Armenia presently. Other investment: Data on general government sector credits are reported by the Ministry of Finance of RA. Banking sector data (credits, currency and deposits, other assets and liabilities) are collected by the CBA. Private sector data (trade credits, loans, other assets and liabilities) are collected from organizations and provided to the CBA by the Armstat. The Armstat provides data on short-term trade credits using data of one-time surveys of organizations engaged in external economic activity. Reserve assets: Data on reserve assets are compiled by the CBA based on the balance sheet. The definition of reserve assets follows the methodology of the "Balance of Payments and International Investment Position Manual, sixth edition" (BPM6). 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording The framework, concepts, and definitions are in conformity with the methodology set out in BPM6. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Quarterly report forms are sent to the respondents at the end of the reference period and should be returned within four weeks. Reports on foreign assets and liabilities of financial sector are collected quarterly. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Direct investment: Some adjustments are made by using the results of the sample surveys on remittances conducted by the CBA. Other investment: No statistical adjustments are made. Reserve assets: Quarterly and annual data on reserve assets of the Central Bank of Armenia are revised correspondingly in two months and in three months after first release. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Seasonal adjustment: Seasonal adjustment is not made. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results Cross-checks of mirror data is conducted periodically with partner countries and international organizations on remittances, foreign direct investments, trade and other data. 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points Central bank of Armenia Contact information (e-mail addresses and telephone numbers) are available on the CBA website. Assistance and support is provided to users, who can pose queries by contacting the contact point or the Press Office of the CBA, which will forward the inquiries to the relevant department. Statistical Committee of RA The Statistical Information Dissemination Division of the Armstat is responsible for statistical issues. Information on contact points is specified in all the publications of the Armstat, respectively. In addition, the Statistical Yearbook and the ad-hoc publications include contact information on respective responsible persons (name, surname and telephone). 5.3.2 Availability of documents and services catalogs Central bank of Armenia The CBA website provides users with considerable amount of information about electronic and paper-based publications and documents. The CBA publications can be obtained free of charge from the contact point upon request. Statistical Committee of RA According to the Annual Statistical Program, every year the Statistical Committee disseminates the Calendar of Statistical Publications. The Calendar presents each publication by title, through a short summary of its contents, periodicity and publication deadline. The Calendar aims to guide users about the statistical products published by the Armstat.
