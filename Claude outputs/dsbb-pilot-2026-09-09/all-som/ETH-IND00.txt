 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions In Ethiopia, there had been a practice of compiling a manufacturing production index from 1964 to 1974. However, it was discontinued due to various reasons in 1974 for about 25 years. Now, in line with the Central Statistical Authority's (CSA's) plan to improve the quality of the data and to fill-in the data gap in this sector, it was decided to continue the compilation of large and medium scale manufacturing production index numbers at least on an annual basis since 1993 Ethiopian Calendar onwards. Following this decision, for the first time, the Authority has compiled and published an index of industrial production on February 2001 for the periods 1990/1991–1998/1999 Gregorian Calendar (G.C.) or 1982-1991 Ethiopian Fiscal year (E.F.Y.). 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The grouping of the index of manufacturing industries is based on the industrial classification. Four-digit International Standard Industrial Classification of all Economic Activities (ISIC rev. 3) and by items of production that add up to major groups (2 digits). Scope of the data The scope of the index of industrial production is limited to the production of Large and Medium Scale Manufacturing Industries (defined, by CSA, as all manufacturing establishments employing 10 persons and above and at the same time using power driven machinery), for which annual census (full coverage) data is readily available from the Large & Medium Scale Manufacturing Survey. The index covers all publicly and privately owned large and medium scale manufacturing establishments in all regions of the country. A total of 153 production series were selected according to their share of value added to total large and medium scale manufacturing. The indices are produced for the period 1990/91 to 1999/2000 so far and the base year is 1995/96 (1988 Ethiopian Fiscal year), which is in line with the new national accounts base year. Exceptions to coverage All the other goods production industrial activities; i.e., small scale manufacturing, and cottage/handcraft manufacturing, mining and quarrying, construction, electricity and gas, are not included.
