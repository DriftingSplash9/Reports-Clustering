 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Definition: Budgetary Central government debt is defined as the stock of direct liabilities in the form of securities and loans. Additional data on currency swaps, net, are given. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Classification system: GFSM 2014 Scope of the data Budgetary central government (BCG) and social security funds (SSF). Exceptions to coverage Quarterly data on extrabudgetary units of the central government is not available. 2.4.1 Valuation Valuation of transactions: Transactions denominated in foreign currency are converted to ISK by using prevailing market rates. 2.4.2 Recording basis The fiscal year is the same as the calendar year. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Data sources: The Financial Management Authority provides data to Statistics Iceland 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Prelimiary data is provided (t+2). 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Other changes in the stock of debt: Normally, no other factors than central government net borrowing and/or revaluation of the foreign currency debt may lead to changes in the stock of central government debt. The only possible exceptions are extraordinary cancellations or extinctions. Consolidation: Data are mostly consolidated. None
