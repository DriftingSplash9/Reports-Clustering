 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The IIP data are compiled based on the Balance of Payments and International Investment Position manual, Sixth edition. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The International Investment Position (IIP) is compiled in accordance with the standard set out in the Balance of Payments and International Investment Position manual, Sixth edition. Scope of the data The IIP statistics comprise all outstanding assets and liabilities of Icelandic residents with non-residents. The IIP data covers the four major institutional units: Central Bank; General governments (central and local governments); Banks (deposit money banks); and Other sectors (other financial and non-financial public and private companies). 2.4.1 Valuation Assets and liabilities under derivatives, portfolio investment and reserve assets are valued at market value. The direct investment position is based on own fund book value. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The compilation of the IIP data is based on the foreign positions of banks and other direct reporting entities. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Direct investment: Majority of FDI companies and foreign affiliates in Iceland report directly to the CBI. The largest companies report quarterly and most other annually. For those not covered by direct reporting the CBI relies on balance sheet data acquired from the Tax Authorities and the Icelandic Company Registry. Portfolio investment: Domestic securities, held by non-residents: CBI receives daily data from The Icelandic Securities Depository on the stock of all securities issued in Iceland and held by non-residents. Foreign securities, held by residents: Data on foreign securities is collected monthly from all financial companies and large investors. Flows for other investors are estimated on the basis of data collected from local custodians. Other investment: Other financial assets and liabilities are derived from data collected from both financial and non-financial reporting entities. Reserve assets: Data is sourced from monthly balance sheet of the CBI. It includes stocks, transactions as well as revaluations due to exchange rate and price changes. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques The quarterly IIP data and the first compilation of annual data may partly be based on estimations and are marked as preliminary data in CBI publications. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request
