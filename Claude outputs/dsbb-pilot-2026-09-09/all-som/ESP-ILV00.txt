 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The data are compiled in line with the BPM6 methodological requirements and the ECB recommendation on eurosystem Reserve Assets (see methodological note http://www.ecb.int ). Both, the official Reserve Assets and the complete template are consistent with the IMF's operational guidelines. The Reserve Assets must: a) be under the effective control of the Banco de España; and b) refer to highly liquid, marketable and creditworthy non-euro area currency-denominated claims on non-residents of the euro area as well as gold, special drawing rights and the reserve positions of Spain at the IMF. Scope of the data Institutional coverage: the Banco de España for official reserves; and Banco de España and Spanish Treasury for the rest of the template. 2.4.1 Valuation Valuation and conversion rates: Following the International Reserves and Foreign Currency Liquidity Guidelines for a Data Template, stocks are valued at market prices at the end of the reference period as far as they are available .The valuation is based on market prices by using the closing mid-market prices at the end of the appropriate period for holdings. The conversion of foreign-currency-denominated assets into euros is based on the mid-point foreign exchange rate on the last day of the reference period. As from 1999, the value of the gold holdings is based on market value on a monthly basis. 2.4.2 Recording basis Frequency of calculation and revaluation: Reserve Assets are calculated on a monthly basis. The figures are revalued on a monthly basis to meet the Balance of Payments/International Investment Position standards and to take into account the accruals principle. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The data are based on the administrative records of the Operations Department of the Banco de España and of the Spanish Treasury. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness The Reserve Assets data is collected on a monthly basis. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs Monitoring the consistency with other reserves assets statistics. 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points Assistance on statistics compiled and/or disseminated by the Banco de España can be requested by users by means of the telephone (34 91 338 5651) or by a contact form ( https://app.bde.es/gnt_seg/en/contacto?a=NwbElZKVDdF0cMf6ebybP9BztpFPLW91 ).
