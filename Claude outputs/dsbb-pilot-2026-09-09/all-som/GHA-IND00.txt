The manufacturing index is based on a sample with representation of manufacturing establishments in ISIC groups listed below. The index base is 1977 = 100 and the sample weights used in 1977 has not been changed. The sample was selected considering: (1) the contribution of the establishment to total manufacturing output determined by the 1977 industrial survey; None 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The Ghana Statistical Service has been compiling the production index on a monthly and annual basis. Data are collected through an annual questionnaire in which the establishments provide data on the goods produced, the quantities produced on a monthly and annual basis. The sample provides for replacement of non-respondents. The index of production is not seasonaly adjusted. Various units of measure are used. Examples are Kilograms, Litres and Tonnes. The mining index is based on all the establishments that produce minerals. The base year is 1977. Weights are those of 1977. Data are collected from the Department of Mines. Total Electricity produced is compiled from Volta River Authority. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques The methodology for the compilation of the index is straightforward. The index is calculated applying the Laspeyre’s formula for quantity items in which each of the ISIC sub-groups is weighted according to the output share to the total output of all establishment in the base year with prices of the base year. The indices have not been re-based in spite of fact that since 1977 there has been the industrial census of 1988. There are plans to rebase using the 2003 Industrial Census which is currently ongoing. None
