Scope of the data The RSD compiles monthly MFS for the CBS, which contains data on financial positions vis-à-vis central and local governments, other depository corporations, other financial corporations, and nonresidents. Exceptions to coverage Data on currency in circulation are not available as U.S. dollar is used as the country’s de facto currency, while no Somali Shilling bank notes are officially recognized or recorded in the CBS’s financial statement. Unrecorded activity U.S. dollar and other foreign currency bank notes in circulation are not captured in the MFS. 2.4.1 Valuation Market or fair value is the general basis for valuation of tradable financial instruments. Monetary gold is valued at market price, but the revaluation is made on an annual basis. Deposits and loans are recorded at nominal value. 2.4.2 Recording basis For conventional financial transactions, accrual accounting is adopted, but data for the CBS do not separately identify accrual interest. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The CBS’s monthly financial statement produced by the FAD is the data source for compiling the MFS for the CBS. There is a data sharing arrangement between the RSD and the FAD. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording The CBS’s current financial statement is simple, reflecting the limited central bank operations carried out by the CBS at this stage. Assets are comprised of cash and gold holdings, deposits, loans, investments, miscellaneous assets, and fixed assets, while liabilities include mainly government and bank deposits and a few other types of domestic liabilities with small outstanding balances. IMF accounts, reflecting Somalia’s financial positions with the IMF were incorporated in the CBS’s financial statement from November 2020 onward. Some reserves, such as revenue reserves (retained earnings) and general and revaluation reserves, but no capital, are reported. The FAD is responsible for daily validation checks to ensure that all entries in the financial statement are accurately recorded. Any errors that the FAD identifies are reflected in a suspense account at end of the business day. Then, the FAD investigates the causes of the errors and corrects them as needed and hence total assets are normally equal to the sum of total liabilities and reserves. If there are imbalances, adjustments are made to the affected accounts at the end of the year. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Based on the data sharing arrangement between the RSD and the FAD, the CBS’s financial statement is provided to the RSD as soon as it is available. So, in general, timelines is not an issue. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques The basic accounting identity underlying the CBS's financial statement is that total assets equal total liabilities, and total assets and total liabilities are the sum of their reported components. Shares and other equity in the liability side include only retained earnings, general and special reserves, and valuation adjustments, but not funds contributed by owns. The CBS has only one shareholder, which is the Federal Government of Somalia. The Ministry of Finance is yet to provide the authorized capital to the CBS. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Source data are not adjusted for compiling MFS. Source data for the CBS were mapped to the Standardized Report Form 1SR for the central bank to derive the MFS for the CBS based on the MFSMCG . The mapping involved transforming data in the CBS’s financial statement to the conventional categories of assets and liabilities by type of financial instruments and by counterpart institutional sector. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results The RSD is responsible for validation checks (e.g., vertical checks) after source data for a reference period are entered in the MFS compilation spreadsheets.
