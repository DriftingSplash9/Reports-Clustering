 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Import and export data are compiled using the World Customs Organization’s Explanatory Notes to the Harmonised System and the International Merchandise Trade Statistics concepts and definitions as published by the United Nations. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The Harmonized System Rev 96 is used as the primary classification to capture and disseminate the data even though data are also produced and disseminated using the Standard International Trade Classification Rev 3. Detailed data (eight digit level HS; seven digit SITC) can be provided with the same timeliness as total imports and exports. 2.2.1 Scope The Harmonized System Rev 96 is used as the primary classification to capture and disseminate the data even though data are also produced and disseminated using the Standard International Trade Classification Rev 3. Detailed data (eight digit level HS; seven digit SITC) can be provided with the same timeliness as total imports and exports. Scope of the data Merchandise trade data are published in the Central Bank of The Bahamas “Quarterly Statistical Digest”. Time series data of imports and exports by SITC commodity group are published. Country and Region tables of non-oil imports and exports are published. Trade data are also published in the “Quarterly Foreign Trade Summary Report” (Department of Statistics), from first quarter 2003. Detailed data are published in the Department of Statistics publication “Annual Foreign Trade Statistics”. Exceptions to coverage Oil products imported and exported on foreign clients accounts, that do not add to the wealth or material resources of the country are excluded from the statistics. 2.4.1 Valuation Imports are recorded cost insurance and freight (c.i.f.). Exports are recorded free on board (f.o.b.). Oil products imported and exported on foreign clients accounts, that do not add to the wealth or material resources of the country, are excluded from the statistics. 2.4.2 Recording basis Goods are recorded in Bahamian dollars (par to US$) at the time of lodgment of the customs declarations. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The Foreign Trade Section of the Department of Statistics compiles imports and exports statistics.
