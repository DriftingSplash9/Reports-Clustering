 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The overall conceptual framework used to compile the BoP statistics is in broad conformity with the sixth edition of the IMF's Balance of Payments and International Investment Position Manual (BPM6). In particular, the following key concepts and definitions from the manual are observed: transactions, economic territory, residence, accounting principles, Current and Capital Accounts categories, Financial Account functional categories, instruments and maturity. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The components of the BoP are grouped as follows: current account, capital account, financial account and errors and omissions, with the classification of transactions and sectorization of residents following BPM6 principles. In BCB’s official publications, there are also additional breakdowns that are of special interest in Brazil. An example is the breakdown of liabilities in debt securities by market/currency of issuance (domestic versus foreign). 2.2.1 Scope The components of the BoP are grouped as follows: current account, capital account, financial account and errors and omissions, with the classification of transactions and sectorization of residents following BPM6 principles. In BCB’s official publications, there are also additional breakdowns that are of special interest in Brazil. An example is the breakdown of liabilities in debt securities by market/currency of issuance (domestic versus foreign). Scope of the data Following the BPM6, in principle, all resident-nonresident transactions are included in the BoP. Exceptions to coverage Exceptions of coverage, due to data source limitations, include services settled abroad and some financial transactions that may not be measured by BCB’s ITRS and are only partially captured through surveys or other sources. Unrecorded activity Among these, due to data source limitations, are services settled abroad, and some financial transactions that may not be measured by the ITRS and are only partially captured through surveys or other administrative sources. 2.4.1 Valuation ITRS transactions are valued according to settled values, which represent actual market prices as requested by BPM6. If the compilation process detects that a transaction from the ITRS or other data source was not reported according to its market value, a value imputation may be done. Total exports and imports are valued on a FOB basis and collected directly in USD. Agencies responsible for the SISCOMEX (the trade register IT system) may impute values to goods going through customs if they consider the reported ones do not correspond to market values. Data from other sources, such as surveys and reserve assets, are reported according to market values or proxies. 2.4.2 Recording basis In general, change of ownership is the principle governing the recognition of transactions and their time of recording. With a few exceptions, transactions are recorded on a cash basis, which is by definition the basis used in the ITRS. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures Grossing/netting procedures are consistent with BPM6. Transactions are recorded on a gross basis, including financial account items, given that the main data source is the ITRS reports, in which both incoming and outgoing payments must be detailed, for both assets and liabilities. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The main source is BCB’s FX system (an ITRS). Other sources include: Secretaria de Comércio Exterior (SECEX), the foreign trade secretariat; Sistema Especial de Liquidação e Custódia (Selic), a settlement and custody system; BCB’s surveys, such as Census of Foreign Capital in Brazil and Brazilian Capitals Abroad; Departamento das Reservas Internacionais (Depin), BCB´s international reserves department; Plano Contábil das Instituições do Sistema Financeiro Nacional (Cosif), which provides the balance sheets for resident financial institutions; among others. Metadata for each series used to compile the BoP detail the specific sources. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Source data obtained from the ITRS are consistent with the definitions, scope, classifications, and time of recording of the BoP statistics. As preparation to the implementation of BPM6, the whole set of codes in the ITRS system was revised so it could match BPM6 requirements for classification and sectorization. Administrative records used to compile BoP statistics, such as customs data, provide a good approximation of the methodological requirements of the BoP. The Secex trade data permits the identification of deviations from BoP requirements, which facilitates the necessary adjustments. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Data collection and processing timetables are adequate to meet timeliness and periodicity for disseminating BoP statistics. ITRS and reserve assets data are available one day after the transactions are settled. Banks´ final balance sheet data are available 45 days after the end of the reference period. Survey data are available in general 4 to 8 months after the reference period, although available data allow for highly accurate estimates. 3.2.1 Source data assessment Quality and comprehensiveness of data sources are continually assessed. ITRS data are thoroughly monitored by Dibap and other BCB departments in charge of banking supervision, financial stability monitoring and money laundry prevention. Opening and closing values of banks' accounts are reconciled with transactions. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Compilation procedures minimize processing errors such as coding, editing, and tabulation errors. Data sources are received and processed electronically through IT systems. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Data on trade in goods from SECEX are adjusted to account for methodological differences between IMTS and BPM6; estimates on freight transportation are used to adjust data from ITRS; data from the ITRS on securities issued in domestic markets are broken down by maturity according to data from the National Treasury (STN); data on purchases in Brazilian free shops made by residents and paid in foreign currency are used to adjust the travel account; and banks’ foreign assets are estimated based on ITRS reports and later replaced for balance sheet data. BCB has been in contact with the national statistical office (IBGE) in order to harmonize the current account computed by BCB and the one implicit in the national accounts compiled by IBGE. This is an useful way to identify needs for adjustments in source data, as gaps in the current account often cause discrepancies between the BoP and the national accounts. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results In general, intermediate results are checked across a wide range of data sources. Information reported in the financial press is routinely used to verify investment transactions. IIP data, which are reported and compiled in a separate structure from the balance of payments, are used for cross-checking the consistency of both datasets. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data The series behavior is routinely cross-checked with related series and indicators. Most detailed cross-checking occurs between equity income and direct investment positions, and debt income and other investment. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs BCB monitors developments in the errors and omissions and seek to understand them. As a rule, all significant discrepancies are investigated. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses There are revisions when factual errors are identified. There are revisions when new data sources are available, allowing to improve coverage, improve quality of estimations, or to fill some gaps. The balance of payments statistics are revised according to the Review Policy of the statistics (https://www.bcb.gov.br/content/statistics/methodologicalnotes_docs/ Review_Policy_of_Official_Economic_Statistics.pdf). 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses The general policy of the BCB is that major changes in methodology are announced at the time they are introduced. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request Observed data disclosure rules, statistics not routinely disseminated can be made available upon request. 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points Prompt and knowledgeable service and support are available to users. Contact points for enquiries are available on the metadata section of each statistics series available at the BCB website (www.bcb.gov.br), including e-mail addresses of the staff in charge. 5.3.2 Availability of documents and services catalogs All Brazilian BoP data and metadata are available to the public on the BCB website at no charge.
