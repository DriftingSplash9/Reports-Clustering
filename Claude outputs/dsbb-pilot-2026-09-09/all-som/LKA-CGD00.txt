Exceptions to coverage No Unrecorded activity No 2.4.1 Valuation Government debt is compiled and presented both in terms of US dollars and Sri Lankan rupees. The government debt figures are valued at face value. Stocks of government external debt are always valued at end-period exchange rates. . 2.4.2 Recording basis The data are recorded as of the day proceeds of borrowing are received and payments made. Loans in kind are recorded as close as possible to the date when goods are received. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures All debt data are compiled and disseminated on a gross basis. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs All debt data are obtained from administrative records maintained by the Ministry of Finance (MOF), and the PDD of the CBSL. Information on foreign debt originates with the MOF and process by CBSL. Creditor records are also used to verify external debt. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording The source data reasonably approximate the definitions, scope, classifications, valuation and time of recording required by the IMF’s Public Sector Debt Statistics: Guide for Compilers and Users - 2011 . 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Monthly data with 5-6 weeks lag Annual data with 3 months lag 3.2.1 Source data assessment Source data is verified from administrative records which are based on actual transactions. The actual transactions of the authorized dealers are periodically inspected by the CBSL. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Government debt data checked and verified with source documents at different levels of data compilation. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data Cross checking is taken place at different levels of data compilation. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs If any discrepancy or other problems are found in statistical outputs, they are rectified at the revision stage. 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points Contact points for each subject field are not disseminated. However, CBSL along with Ministry of Finance stand ready to answer any question on any component of government debt.
