 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The Balance of payments of Montenegro is a statistical statement summarizing the economic transactions of residents of Montenegro with the rest of the world during a given period of time. Balance of payments data are compiled in accordance with the recommendations of the sixth edition of the IMF's Balance of Payments Manual (BPM6) starting from June 2014. The concept and definitions used are in broad conformity with those used in BPM6. The balance of payments statistics are compiled in a BPM6 standard presentation and disseminated to the public in a national presentation, which is derived from the standard presentation. The data are published on a quarterly and annual basis, in thousands of euro, at the website of the Central bank of Montenegro. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The classification follows BPM6. The data include following categories: · Current account includes credits and debits of goods, services, income and current transfers. · Capital account includes capital transfers and acquisition/disposal of nonproduced, nonfinancial assets. · Financial account includes net direct investment, portfolio investment, other investment and the changes in reserve assets. · Separate item is the net errors and omissions. The sectorization follows BPM6. Scope of the data In principle, the scope of BOP statistics is broadly consistent with international accepted standards, covering all transactions between residents and nonresidents. The data include: · Current account: export and import of goods and services, income (receipts and payments) and current transfers (receipts and payments). · Capital account: capital transfers, acquisition/disposal of nonproduced, nonfinancial assets · Financial account: net direct investment, portfolio investment, other investment and the change in reserve assets. For items not covered or poorly covered in existing data sources adjustments and estimations are made. An important improvement of scope of data has been achieved by introducing direct reporting by enterprises in 2010– an annual survey for data on foreign direct investment and external debt and monthly survey on transactions through held by resident’s accounts in foreign banks. Exceptions to coverage The balance of payments of Montenegro covers in principles most of the transactions between residents and nonresidents apart from informal flows. 2.4.1 Valuation The valuation is based on market prices prevailing when the transaction takes place. Both exports and imports of goods are valued on f.o.b. basis. Most of the international transactions are conducted in euro, official currency in Montenegro, so the euro is the most relevant currency for assessing the data. 2.4.2 Recording basis Most of the items are largely based on the ITRS, which uses the time of payments for its basis. Travel and tourism revenues are calculated based on estimations. The recording of goods is according to custom clearance. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures The grossing/netting procedures are broadly consistent with BPM6. Current account transactions are recorded on a gross basis, while capital and financial account transactions are recorded on a net basis. In addition, gross data are also available for some components of the financial account. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs A wide range of sources are used to compile the balance of payments. These are mainly: merchandise trade statistics (Monstat), banks reports (ITRS), CBM (monetary statistics, banking operations statistics) and various surveys and estimations. Current account General merchandise data are obtained from the foreign trade statistics compiled by Statistical Office of Montenegro. The primary data source is the single administrative document (SAD). Data on imports and exports are compiled according to the special trade system. Both exports and imports of goods are valued on f.o.b. basis. The Central bank of Montenegro makes coverage adjustments to trade statistics and records this under “coverage adjustment” for goods exported/imported without customs declarations and net exports of goods under merchanting. C.i.ff.o.b adjustments are made as well. The ITRS and survey are used as a source for these adjustments. The main source of data for services, income and current transfers are the ITRS and the CBM. Data on travel and tourism revenues are estimated on the basis of number of overnight stays and daily spending of foreign tourists. The data on travel revenues, besides estimates, includes data on expenditures by those traveling for health and educational purposes based on the ITRS. The travel expenditures abroad by Montenegrin residents are based on the data compiled from the ITRS. Data on compensation of employees (credit side) from the ITRS are supplemented by estimated data on salaries of resident sailors in foreign vessels (sailors). Capital and Financial account Capital Account: The data source on capital transfers and acquisition/disposal of non-produced non-financial assets is the ITRS. The CBM records foreign direct investment on the basis of reported payments through domestic banks. The definition of direct investment in Montenegro’s balance of payments corresponds to that of BPM6. Direct investment comprises not only the initial transaction establishing the relationship between the investor and the enterprise, but also all subsequent transactions between them and among affiliated enterprises. Intercompany debt transactions between affiliated enterprises (10% or more of capital share) are recorded as a direct investment – other capital. Data on the value of investment in kind are not included, as they are not available. For the time being, this item does not include data on reinvested earnings. The main source of data is the ITRS. These data are supplemented by data from the database of the Central Depositary Agency (the central registry of securities), enterprises, the media and other sources. For verification purposes, foreign direct investment data obtained from the enterprise survey are compared to ITRS data. Portfolio investment covers investments in equity and debt securities not covered by direct investment and reserve assets. The main source of the data is the ITRS. For the government sector, beside the ITRS, the sources of data are CBM (Financial and Banking Operation Department) and Ministry of Finance. The data on Other investment are obtained from various sources. Banking sector statistics are derived from the data collected from banks by the monetary statistics. The asset and liability transactions of other sectors reflect mainly disbursements and repayments of short and long-term loans between resident and nonresident enterprises that do not have a direct investment relationship, are derived from the ITRS. The data on currency and deposits for other sectors are estimated, while source of data for banks is monetary statistics. For the time being the data on trade credits are not available. The data on government sector from the ITRS are compared and supplemented by data supplied by Ministry of Finance on government external debt. Reserve assets reflect changes in the reserve assets during the reporting period. Data are based on the CBM balance sheet. The CBM has introduced a new annual survey (direct reporting by companies) in 2010 for stock and flow data on foreign direct investment and private external debt and transactions through residents account held abroad. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording The data sources used for the Balance of payments of Montenegro are generally in line with BPM6. The timing of goods is according to custom clearance, which is only an approximation for the time of change of ownership. Other transactions measured through the ITRS are based on the time of payment, which is adopted as an acceptable approximation to an accrual basis. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness The data source is timely. The ITRS records, provided through electronic system, are available within ten days after the end of the reference month. Data on merchandise trade are available within 25 days after the end of reference month. Data on number of overnights of foreign visitors are available within 31 days after the end of reference month. 3.2.1 Source data assessment Source data are routinely assessed. The results of assessments are monitored and made available to guide statistical processes. Large transactions are monitored to make sure they are recorded properly. The data on FDI and external debt from the ITRS are checked and reconciled with data from the annual survey. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results Data from commercial banks (ITRS) are validated using automated data validation procedures. The data entry procedures include checks to avoid miscoding and misreporting. Queries are made to reporter concerning unusual transactions and large values. Data sources are checked with any available indicators. Intermediate results are validated using other sources whenever is possible. Newspapers and information agencies are monitored for information about large transactions, and it is confirmed that the corresponding flows are covered in the balance of payments. Survey data are checked against ITRS records and fully reconciled. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data Potential discrepancies in intermediate data are assessed on a regular basis and the corresponding adjustments are made if necessary. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs The supply and use framework is used to identify and research discrepancies and ensure that the statistical results are consistent. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses Revisions are carefully monitored for each individual record. Studies and analyses of revisions are carried out and used internally to inform statistical processes. The aggregates are compared with data for the same month as reported in the previous month’s publication. Changes are checked, to ensure that they can be explained. Significant revisions are noted in publications. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses CBM disseminates working papers and analysis on relevant topics in aim to stimulate discussion and contribute to better knowledge. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request The balance of payments of Montenegro is published at the Central bank of Montenegro website. The Central bank of Montenegro meets requests for additional information without a fee.
