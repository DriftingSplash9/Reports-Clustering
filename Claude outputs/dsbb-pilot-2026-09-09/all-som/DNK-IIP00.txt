 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The international investment position (IIP) is compiled by Danmarks Nationalbank. The methodology used for the compilation is the Sixth edition of the IMF's "Balance of Payments Manual" . The international investment position is the balance sheet of the stock of external financial assets and liabilities. The statistics are compiled on the basis of five data catagories: Direct investments, portfolio investments, financial derivatives, other investments and reserve assets. A direct investment implies that an investor holds 10 per cent or more of the own funds or voting rights in a foreign company. A direct investment comprises the initial investment and all subsequent financial transactions between the investor and the foreign company, and between consolidated companies. Portfolio investments are divided into equity securities and debt securities. However, securities are defined as a direct investment if the investor owns more than 10 per cent of the own funds or voting rights in the issuing company. Financial derivatives are instruments that are linked to a specific financial instrument, indicator or commodity through which financial risk can be traded. Other investments include trade credits, accounts with banks, loans including financial leasing, and other outstanding investments. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Sixth edition of the Balance of Payments Manual (BPM6) and the European Central Bank Guidelines as well as the European manual for national accounts (ESA2010) thus in line with SNA2008. Scope of the data Geographic coverage: The statistics comprise all outstandings of residents denominated in DKK and foreign currency with non-residents. Residents are individuals who are permanently resident in Denmark, as well as companies and institutions domiciled in Denmark. As from July 2000 the Faroe Islands and Greenland are considered to be non-residents for statistical purposes. Institutional coverage: All institutional sectors are covered by the statistics. Data are compiled for the following sectors: Danmarks Nationalbank (reserve assets); General government (central government, local governments and social security funds); Banks; Private companies and households (broken down by Insurance companies and pension funds; Investment funds, other financial companies; non-financial companies; households including non-profit institutions serving households). The sectoral breakdown is performed according to the breakdown described in the European System of Accounts (ESA2010). Transaction coverage: The coverage ratio is generally considered to be high for all sectors. It has been assessed that: Reserve assets as well as banks' and the central government's foreign assets and liabilities are covered 100 per cent; The coverage ratio for direct investments is around 90 per cent; Portfolio investments of private companies and households are covered between 96 and 99 per cent; The coverage ratios for trade credits, loans/deposits, currency as well as other investments are at least 80 per cent. Estimates are used to reach a coverage ratio of 100 per cent on all sectors and instruments. Exceptions to coverage Geographic coverage: As from July 2000 the Faroe Islands and Greenland are considered to be non-residents for statistical purposes. 2.4.1 Valuation Outstanding amounts are disseminated in Danish Kroner (DKK). Assets and liabilities in other currencies are converted at the exchange rate at the end of the reference period. Data can be reported in Danish Kroner, Euro or US dollars. Data are disseminated at marked value. For direct investment, equity own fund at book value is used for non-listed companies. 2.4.2 Recording basis The methodology used for the compilation is the the Sixth edition of the IMF's "Balance of Payments Manual" . The final uploaded database figures of the Danish international investment position are based on pure stock data. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures We do not collect information for the whole population of non-financial dorporations and household. Based on data from a commercial dataprovider (Burea van Dijk/Moodys) we gross up but only for FDI equity, loans and other investment. Reserve assets, Portfolio investments and Financial derivatives are full covered. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Denmark's international investment position is compiled as of the end of each quarter. Both annual and monthly sources are used. A few sources are available only on an annual basis and they are projected. Therefore, the quarterly statistics for periods extending beyond the most recent questionnaire surveys (cf. item 3.1.2) are provisional and based on a mix of projections and actual holdings (balance figures). Each data source is based on the following identity: stock, beginning of period + transactions during period + valuation changes, etc. during period = stock, end of period, where stock data corresponds to the international investment position and transaction data to the financial account of the Balance of Payments. The final uploaded database figures of the Danish international investment position are based on pure stock data. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Direct investment: Data on direct investments are gathered via Danmarks Nationalbank's questionnaire survey of the external accounts of approximately 1,200 private enterprises. Since market values are not available in general, investment capital is entered as book values (net asset value), except when the direct investment is noted and traded on a stock exchange. Direct investments are also obtained from the Danish securities statistics. Real Estate: Claims arising from household's acquisition of real estate abroad and foreign households acquisition of real estate in Denmark is shown as financial assets and liabilities under direct investment. Real estate is estimated based on data from the Danish tax authorities. Portfolio investment: The asset items of portfolio investment stocks are compiled monthly from Danish custodian banks, supplemented by information about securities deposited abroad from the questionnaire survey and by the balance sheets of the MFI sector and the balance sheets of investment associations. The liability items of the portfolio investment stocks are obtained from the Danish securities statistics supplemented by data drawn from the balance sheets of the MFI sector and investment associations, respectively. Other investment and financial derivatives: Trade credits and other investment are compiled from the questionnaire survey, whereas loans, currency and deposits and also financial derivatives are compiled partly from the balance sheets of the MFI sector and partly from the questionnaire survey. Reserve assets: The reserve assets are compiled from the balance sheets of Danmarks Nationalbank. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Monthly reporting by the 10 working day after end of reference period Annual reporting 4, 5 or 6 months after end of year depending of type of entity. In line with Danish law for reporting of Annual Financial Report to the Danish Authorities 3.2.1 Source data assessment The questionnaire forms used in the questionnaire survey are designed by Danmarks Nationalbank and are revised to reflect developments in international investment position standards and accounting standards. The coverage of questionnaire survey participants is checked annually. The reporting population has been gradually reduced since 1991 using thresholds. There is a population reporting monthly with large transactions and/or stocks and a population reporting annually with small transactions and large stocks. Both populations jointly contribute to compilation of the international investment position. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques When determining the population coverage private data sources are used to find new potential reporters. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Based on data from a commercial data provider we do gross up holdings 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points Information about contact persons can be found at Danmarks Nationalbank's website: https://www.nationalbanken.dk/media/dyrjrbmx/danmarks_udlandsformue_kilder_og_metoder_eng. pdf
