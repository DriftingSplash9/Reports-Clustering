 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions National Accounts are the financial accounts of Palau is compiled and presented in an internationally accepted system. This system provides a framework for integrated economic accounts. One of the commonly used outputs from National Accounts is Gross Domestic Product (GDP), which is the value of all goods and services produced in a given year. Palau broadly follows the concepts and definitions given in the United Nations, System of National Accounts 2008 . Palau compiles and publishes GDP at constant fiscal year 2015 prices, as well as GDP at current prices. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The classification of economic activity followed in compiling Palau’s National Accounts, which is taken from International Standard Industrial Classification, Revision 4, is as follows: Agriculture; Fisheries; Mining and Quarrying; Manufacturing; Electricity, Gas and Water; Construction; Trade; Hotels and Restaurants; Transport and Communication; Finance and Insurance; Real Estate and Business Services; Public Administration; and Other Services. Imputed bank charges and import duties are separately identified. In addition, National Accounts are also presented by the following institutional classification: Private Enterprise; Public Enterprise; Depository Corporations; Other Financial Intermediaries; National Government; Government Agencies; NGOs and Non-profits; and Households. 2.2.1 Scope The classification of economic activity followed in compiling Palau’s National Accounts, which is taken from International Standard Industrial Classification, Revision 4, is as follows: Agriculture; Fisheries; Mining and Quarrying; Manufacturing; Electricity, Gas and Water; Construction; Trade; Hotels and Restaurants; Transport and Communication; Finance and Insurance; Real Estate and Business Services; Public Administration; and Other Services. Imputed bank charges and import duties are separately identified. In addition, National Accounts are also presented by the following institutional classification: Private Enterprise; Public Enterprise; Depository Corporations; Other Financial Intermediaries; National Government; Government Agencies; NGOs and Non-profits; and Households. Scope of the data National Accounts of Palau cover all productive activities on Palau’s territory. Exceptions to coverage There are no exceptions to the coverage of National Accounts of Palau. 2.4.1 Valuation Purchaser’s prices are used for valuation in National Accounts. Thus, GDP at purchaser’s prices is calculated as GDP at basic prices plus taxes minus subsidies. 2.4.2 Recording basis All transactions are recorded on accrual basis. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Source data for GDP compilation are derived from the following sources: Business Gross Receipt Tax (BGRT) provide source data for most major sectors of the economy, namely, trade and restaurants, industry, and construction. Fisheries contribution is estimated from the volume of fish exports provided by the Customs and the volume of fish domestically sold as reflected in the BGRTs. In addition, payroll data are used for estimation of the government’s contribution. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques One technique used is application of industry-wise fixed ratios on gross revenues of private sector companies to estimate operating surpluses. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses No revision studies are prepared.
