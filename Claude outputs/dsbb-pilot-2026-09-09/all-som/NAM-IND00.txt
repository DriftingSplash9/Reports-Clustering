 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The mining and quarrying index The index of mining and quarrying measures the level of production of different major minerals produced within the border of Namibia. This is compiled by means of a fixed basket. The beverage index The beverage index measures the volume and international trade of beverages. This is compiled by means of alcoholic and non-alcoholic produce. Electricity Index The electricity is a form of energy used to give heat or light. Domestic sales - The sales that takes place within an economy Electricity Exports - The cross boarder selling of electricity Composite index - The index that aggregates many indices to represent many indicators. Kilowatt's hour - Unit of measuring electricity Livestock Index Livestock refers to domesticated animals raised in an agricultural setting such as cattle, sheep, goats and Pigs. Composite index - This index groups together many different indexes in order to create a representation of overall market of livestock 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization International Standard Industry Classification (ISIC) of All Economic Activities, Rev.4 is used for the industry classification. Harmonized System (HS) 2022 (trade) 2.2.1 Scope International Standard Industry Classification (ISIC) of All Economic Activities, Rev.4 is used for the industry classification. Harmonized System (HS) 2022 (trade) Scope of the data The mining and quarrying index The mining and quarrying index covers production of diamond, uranium, gold and ‘zinc concentrate and contained' as per the International Standard Industry Classification (ISIC) of All Economic Activities, Rev.4, Section B. The beverage index The beverage production index covers the production of alcoholic, and non-alcoholic beverages as per the International Standard Industry Classification (ISIC) of All Economic Activities, Rev.4, Section C. The electricity Index The electricity index covers production of electricity (own generation) and import; electricity sales (domestics sales and export) as well as different types of sources of electricity Geographical coverage for the three indexes above: Is the entire Namibia (Country). Livestock Index The Livestock index covers heads of cattle marketed to export approved abattoirs and butchers', cattle live export on hoof, heads of small stock marketed to export approved abattoirs and butchers', Live exports of small stock and pigs' local slaughters. Geographical coverage: The coverage of livestock marketed are mainly from the South Veterinary Cordon Fence (S-VCF) through export approved abattoirs and butchers are cattle, sheep and goats while livestock marketed in the N-VCF through export approved none. The selected livestock marketed in the index are monitored through the ear tacking tracing system 2.4.1 Valuation The mining and quarrying index Volume of selected minerals are recorded on a monthly basis in their respective units of measurements. In tons in case of uranium, copper and zinc, In carats for diamonds, and In kilograms for gold. No grossing up is used and indicators of selected mineral in the index monitored accounts about 93 percent of the mining and quarrying industry. The beverage index Volume data of alcoholic beverages and non-alcoholic beverages in hectoliters are recorded monthly. No grossing up is used as indicators in the index cover mainly all the products in the beverage sector. The electricity index Different Sources and sales are recorded on a monthly basis in their respective units of measurements. Recorded in KWH Livestock Index Selected numbers of animals are recorded on a monthly basis by type. Heads of livestock 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The mining and quarrying index Sources of weights: The source for the weights are value added figures in Namibian Dollars from the national accounts and is updated once the national accounts is rebased. Source for production: Secondary data of volume is sourced from the Bank of Namibia collected from respective mining companies. Source data for volume are received every 20th of each month. The beverage index Sources of weights: The source for alcoholic and non-alcoholic weights are volume data from respective sources in hectoliters. Source for production: Administrative data and secondary data for Beverages are sourced from Namibia breweries, ABInBev, Coca-Cola Beverage Africa, and NSA trade data. Source data in volume are received every 15th of each month. The electricity index Sources of weights: The index of sales and sources weighted uses the volume data of 2015. Source for production: The data sources for KWH of electricity is sourced from Nam-power. These are data generation in local production, Import, export and domestic sales. Unit measurement is in kilowatts per hour (kWh) for all the variables. Source data for numbers in kWh are received every 15th of each month. Livestock Index Sources of weights: Sources of weights: The source for the weights are value added figures in Namibian Dollars from the national accounts and is updated once the national accounts is rebased. Source for production: Secondary data in numbers are sourced from the Meat board of Namibia. The data are received every 20th of each month. 3.2.1 Source data assessment All secondary data used in the compilation of national accounts are assessed for validity and quality before enforced. These data are checked for consistency, coverage and validity. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Data received from the source are scrutinized and verified for correctness by observing outliers and whether data is reflecting economic reality. In terms of inconstancies found in the data, it is referred back to the source for confirmation. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses Revised estimates are published in the subsequent report for sector statistics and up to the latest three years while major revisions made in referencing following the rebasing of the national accounts periodicity.
