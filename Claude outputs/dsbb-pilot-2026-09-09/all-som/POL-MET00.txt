 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Statistics on foreign trade in goods are in line with the UN Statistics Division methodology and EU Regulations. The subject of foreign trade statistics is the movement of goods which add to or subtract from the stock of material resources of Poland by entering or leaving the economic territory. For the majority of transactions (exceptions - some specific goods or movements) the goods must physically move between the Member States. Polish foreign trade statistics are based on data collected from two compilation systems: intra-EU trade based on data collected from traders (the date of accession to the EU is May 1, 2004). Small traders are released from reporting and data on their trade are estimated, extra-EU trade (trading of goods with non-EU Member States) based on records from customs declarations. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Classification of Commodities: Classification of commodities used for reporting relating to imports and exports is Customs tariff and Combined Nomenclature. This classification is based on the Harmonized Commodity Description and Coding System (HS). In addition Chapter 99 of CN is applied. Chapter 99 is used for simplified coding in certain cases allowed by the legislation. CN codes can be transferred into codes of Standard International Classification (SITC Rev.4) for the purposes of publication or international comparisons. They can be also transferred into PKWiU (Polish Classification of Products and Services) codes, which enable comparisons between statistics relating to foreign trade and production statistics. Classification of countries and regions: Classification of countries in foreign trade statistics is the Country Nomenclature for the External Trade Statistics of the Community (Geonomenclature). Data related to foreign trade statistics are also presented in breakdown by several groups of countries: Developed Countries: Organization for Economic Co-operation and Development Countries (OECD) European Union (EU), European Free Trade Zone (EFTA), Central and Eastern European Countries Developing Countries Details on Country grouping are available in introductory comments in publications. Scope of the data Quantities and values of goods (movable property) in arrivals and dispatches are classified and described primarily by commodity types and partner countries assigned to foreign trade transactions (dispatches and arrivals). Measures: statistical value (in PLN, USD and EUR) net mass (in kilograms) quantity in supplementary unit Dimensions: type of commodity; country of origin/consignment for arrivals country of destination for dispatches Information on partner country is reported and collected according to the Geonomenclature valid at the time. Exceptions to coverage In INTRASTAT, according to regulations of the Customs Law, entity responsible for submission of information is natural or legal person registered for VAT purposes and trading with the EU Member States. In order to decrease the burden which INTRASTAT imposes on traders, the system of statistical thresholds is applied. The reporting obligation to INTRASTAT concerns persons achieving an annual value of arrivals or dispatches exceeded statistical thresholds defined in value and announced by the President of Statistics Poland and published annually in The Programme of Statistical Surveys of Public Statistics. For the year 2025 the obligatory transmitted data covered at least 97% of the total dispatches and at least 93% of the total arrivals expressed in value according to Eurostat's recommendations. In EXTRASTAT all of the data relating to the trading of goods are collected, except goods purchased on trade credits, investments in kind, gifts in kind, and goods settled through accounts in foreign banks and through netting (because such data are derived from cash-based domestic banking records). Unrecorded activity Polish foreign trade is based on a special trade system. As a result the following types of transactions and goods are not included in foreign trade statistics: trade in services, except from inward and outward processing, commodities in transit, commodities placed temporarily in customs warehouses and in duty free areas, commodities admitted temporarily to free circulations (excluding inward and outward processing), means of payment and papers such as stocks, bonds, securities or shares, commodities for diplomatic institutions, monetary gold. 2.4.1 Valuation Statistical value is the value of the commodity at the Polish border. The value of imports is presented on the basis of CIF Polish port or franco Polish border. The value of exports is presented on FOB basis. Tariffs, taxes or other charges levied on imports or exports are not contained in the statistical value. The principal method of valuation for transactions is the invoiced value. 2.4.2 Recording basis The reporting period is a calendar month. In general, from the point of view of foreign trade statistics the time of physical movement of goods determines the reporting period, to which transaction should be assigned. Data for merchandise trade settlements are collected in transaction currencies and afterwards converted into EUR and USD on the basis of exchange rate announced by Narodowy Bank Polski for the day of transaction. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures Value of goods sent or received for the purpose of processing is the value of materials entrusted for processing purposes. Value of goods sent or received after processing is defined by the value of materials entrusted for processing and the value added as a result of processing. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Foreign trade turnover is calculated mainly on the basis of: INTRASTAT declarations for dispatches and arrivals transmitted to NRA each month. Obligation of submitting monthly declarations refers to companies trading with the EU Member States and liable for providing statistical information on intra-EU trade on the basis of the EU legislation, Statistical law and Customs law. estimations of trade values for parties trading with the EU Member States but not exceeding statistical thresholds and not submitting INTRASTAT declarations. In the publications of Statistics Poland full scope of data (including estimations) is presented starting from 2017. Correctness of INTRASTAT declarations is guaranteed thanks to the comparison of data obtained from INTRASTAT declarations with the data coming from VAT declarations. Registers of declaring parties help to monitor enterprises which are obliged to the reporting. Since 1 January 2006 alternative data sources are used in Polish foreign trade statistics. Alternative data sources are used for specific goods or movements and for following purposes: additional, supplementary sources of statistical information, used for checks and for supplementing the data coming from customs declarations and INTRASTAT declarations for the following goods: sea vessels – data obtained from the Polish Permanent Register of Ships conducted by Maritime Chambers at District Courts in Gdansk and Szczecin, aircraft – data obtained from the Civil Aircraft register of Poland conducted by the Civil Aviation Authority, sea products, including purchase/sale of fishery which were the subject of trade transaction before being landed by sea-going vessels – data collected by the Fishery Monitoring Center in Gdynia - organizational unit subordinated to the Polish Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Development data on electricity and natural gas are obtained from the statistical surveys conducted by the Energy Market Agency subordinated to the Ministry of Energy. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Data are collected in accordance with definitions, scope and classifications described in points 2.1.1 – 2.4.3. For businesses which are not obliged to provide statistical value to INTRASTAT the statistical value is calculated on the basis of invoiced value of transactions declared. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness In INTRASTAT the reference (reporting) period to which the transaction is assigned is the calendar month, in which intra-EU trade took place (dispatch or arrival of goods). INTRASTAT declaration shall be submitted by 10th day of a month following the reference period. The principle of physical movement of good across the border is also applied to the EXTRASTAT system, therefore customs declarations are recorded immediately after records are approved by customs and goods move through the border. It means, that the date of customs clearance of the goods determines its assignment to the reference period.In general, the time of physical movement of goods determines the reporting period, to which transaction should be reported. 3.2.1 Source data assessment Data quality is verified in data checks procedure. At production stage foreign trade input data are subject to automatic formal and logical check. Registered data are checked before statistical data set is produced (this is under the responsibility of NRA). Statistical data sets are also verified during the logical check carried out in the Foreign Trade Division of Statistics Poland. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques In foreign trade statistics the data sets are produced on the basis of the "open" sets. All data published within the reference year should be considered as preliminary data. Corrections for all reference months are included continuously in data sets until the reference year is closed. Final results are presented in the Yearbook of Foreign Trade Statistics published annually. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Merchandise trade data are not seasonally adjusted. In INTRASTAT the trade turnover with the EU countries for enterprises not exceeding the established threshold is estimated. Estimation procedure is also used in case of the delayed declarations or non-responses in a given reference period, but in this case the estimated data are replaced in the subsequent reference periods by the real data coming from the delayed declarations or declarations provided as a result of reminders or penalties applied for non-responded enterprises. For the delays or non responses the data are estimated on the basis of historical information and information collected by the tax system (VAT data compared with VIES data). Estimations are included into the data disseminated to the public as of 2017. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results Statistical data relating to foreign trade, validated in the IT systems in NRA, is the subject to ex-post checks in the Statistics Poland. At first, so called 'quick checks' of aggregated data are carried out in order to find erroneous results which have a great impact on the country trade or trade pattern of turnover. Special attention is put to the validation of statistical value. In case of error appearance, NRA is required to implement corrections immediately and afterwards the data set generation is repeated. If errors do not appear, the data set is accepted by Statistics Poland and it is used for the production of statistics. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data Accepted data sets are the subject of further ex-post checks by the Foreign Trade Division of Statistics Poland. Foreign trade specialists, who are responsible for checking the correctness of each commodity codes decide if data sets are correct. All records considered by the experts as erroneous, are sent back to NRA for further examination and final acceptation. Reconciliation between trade data compiled by Narodowy Bank Polski and the customs based trade data compiled by Statistics Poland is carried out annually. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs Data derived from INTRASTAT system are checked and compared with VAT and VIES data. If any discrepancies occur, they become a subject for further explanation with data providers. Foreign trade data are also checked in mirror exercises with other MS periodically. All discrepancies during reconciliation exercises are analised in order to find reasons for discrepancies and to avoid them in the future. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses As the data set is "open" all revisions and changes are compared to the previously generated data set during ex-post validation process and logical checks in Statistics Poland. The result of such analyses is a tool for identification of possible errors which is used only for internal purposes. The differences between the cash-based trade data compiled by Narodowy Bank Polski and the customs-based trade data compiled by Statistics Poland are presented in the Yearbook of Foreign Trade Statistics under the web page: https://stat.gov.pl/en/topics/statistical-yearbooks/statistical-yearbooks/yearbook-of-foreign-trade-statistics-of-poland-2025,9,19.html 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses Revision studies are used for internal purposes. Revision analyses are also a part of the quality report produced for EUROSTAT annually. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request Data are disseminated on request on the lowest level of aggregation (CN8 level), provided that requested data are not confidential. There is no possibility to apply for detailed data on individual transactions, such data can be used only for statistical purposes.
