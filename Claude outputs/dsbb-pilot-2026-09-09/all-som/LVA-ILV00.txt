 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The concepts and definitions comply with the sixth edition of the International Monetary Fund's Balance of Payments and International Investment Position Manual (BPM6). Data on international reserves and foreign currency liquidity are compiled according to the Operational Guidelines on International Reserves and Foreign Currency Liquidity as provided by the International Monetary Fund. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Reserve assets include monetary gold, Special Drawing Rights, reserve position in the International Monetary Fund (IMF), as well as other reserve assets (currency and deposits, securities, financial derivatives (net) and other claims) denominated in convertible foreign currencies. Starting with January 2014, the following is classified as reserve assets: claims on non-euro area residents denominated in currencies other than the euro, gold, reserve position in the IMF and Special Drawing Right. Other foreign currency assets are comprised of deposits with foreign financial institutions, net position arising from derivative financial instruments, as well as other foreign currency denominated assets not included in reserve assets. Predetermined short-term net drains on foreign currency assets include principal and interest payments of loans and deposits as well as aggregate positions arising from currency swap arrangements, involving the forward leg in the national currency. There are also included outflows related with repos (principal amount and accrued interest of the repo loan). Contingent short-term net drains on foreign currency assets include guarantees issued by the central government with respect to foreign borrowings as well as non-drawn unconditional credit lines provided by the IMF. Memorandum items include assets pledged as a result of transactions with interest rate future contracts, securities lent and repoed, financial derivative instruments and currency composition of reserve assets. 2.2.1 Scope Reserve assets include monetary gold, Special Drawing Rights, reserve position in the International Monetary Fund (IMF), as well as other reserve assets (currency and deposits, securities, financial derivatives (net) and other claims) denominated in convertible foreign currencies. Scope of the data Reserve assets comprise external assets that are readily available to and controlled by Latvijas Banka. Other foreign currency assets comprise assets held by Latvijas Banka and the central government. 2.4.1 Valuation Gold, debt securities other than those held to maturity, participating interest (except the participating interest in the European Central Bank), currency future contracts and interest rate derivatives are accounted for at fair value. Forward exchange rate contracts and currency swap arrangements are included in the net position of the respective currency on the transaction day at the spot rate of the transaction and are recorded in the balance sheet in euro at the exchange rate of the respective currency at the end of the reporting period. Other derivative financial instruments are reported in the balance sheet at fair value. 2.4.2 Recording basis Conversion to unit of account Stocks in foreign currency are converted to euro using the European Central Bank's (ECB) exchange rate prevailing at the end of the reference period. The price of gold is set on the basis of the gold market price in US dollars and the exchange rate of the US dollar against the euro published by the ECB. The exchange rate of Special Drawing Rights (SDRs) is set on the basis of the weights of the SDRs basket of currencies published by the International Monetary Fund and the exchange rates of the respective currencies published by the ECB. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Latvijas Banka's financial statements that are prepared in accordance with the Guideline of the European Central Bank of 11 November 2010 on the legal framework for accounting and financial reporting in the European System of Central Banks (recast) (ECB/2010/20), Financial Accounting Policy of Latvijas Banka approved by Latvijas Banka's Council, and the requirements of the Law on Latvijas Banka governing financial reporting.
