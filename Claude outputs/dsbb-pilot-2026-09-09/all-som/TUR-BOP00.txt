 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The data are compiled in accordance with the sixth edition of the IMF's Balance of Payments and International Investment Position Manual (BPM6) . Analytical Framework: There are 3 main headings, namely current account: composed of goods, services, primary income and secondary income, capital account and financial account: composed of direct investment, portfolio investment, other investment and reserve assets (and net errors and omissions). Definition: Statistical statement summarizing the economic transactions of residents of Türkiye with the rest of the world during a month. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Classification System: The source for the basic classifications and definitions of balance of payments statistics of Türkiye is the sixth edition of the IMF Manual. 2.2.1 Scope Classification System: The source for the basic classifications and definitions of balance of payments statistics of Türkiye is the sixth edition of the IMF Manual. Scope of the data Data are disseminated in millions of U.S. dollars. Transactions Coverage: Türkiye's balance of payments attempts to cover all transactions of residents with non-residents. There are no transactions intentionally excluded. Geographic Coverage: The data cover the entire Turkish economy and all transactions. Exceptions to coverage Financial derivative transactions are classified under Portfolio Investment. Unrecorded activity Unrecorded Activity: There is no unrecorded activity. 2.4.1 Valuation Conversion to Unit of Account: Banks are obliged to report all foreign exchange transactions carried out by them both in original currencies and their USD and TRY equivalents in an aggregate form. Transaction currencies are converted to USD and TRY based on the rate prevailing at the transaction time. Valuation: Transactions are valued at market prices. 2.4.2 Recording basis Recording of Primary Income: The income of the securities held in reserve assets within the Central Bank are recorded on an accrual basis, while other income items are recorded on a cash basis. Deposit Accounts of nonresidents held within the CBRT are also recorded on an accrual basis. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Data sources: The data are obtained from a variety of sources, including the foreign trade statistics of the TurkStat, the surveys conducted by TurkStat (for data on travel and shuttle trade, International Trade in Services Statistics (ITSS), short-term trade credits), direct reports of the related resident and non-resident enterprises, the foreign exchange records of the CBRT and banks, trial balances of resident banks, and transactions derived from stocks on liabilities of equity securities and government domestic debt securities. Goods: The foreign trade statistics are compiled by TurkStat from customs documents on a general trade basis, in which the goods entered to or left from the free-zones and customs warehouses are also recorded in the trade statistics (exports f.o.b. and imports c.i.f.) starting with 2013 data. Before 2013, the foreign trade statistics based on a special trade basis are converted to "general trade" basis through an adjustment item using the data on free trade zones. Data on import c.i.f. basis are converted to import f.o.b. basis through an adjustment item using the data on freight and insurance in import c.i.f. figures provided by TurkStat. Adjustments are also made to account for goods purchased in Türkiye by shuttle traders for resale abroad. To include the data on imports of fuel and provisions such as catering procured at airports by aircrafts, the surveys are conducted by the CBRT on domestic airline operators. To include the data on exports of catering, domestic catering companies have been surveyed. "Net exports of goods under merchanting" data, which are obtained from the banks' reports, are also recorded under "Goods". Data on imports of aircrafts in the form of financial leasing have been collected from both the related airline operators and Turkstat. Services: With the new study (the International Trade in Services Statistics (ITSS) Survey), carried out by the Turkstat,data sources have been enriched by using direct reporting of companies/enterprises and administrative records of Revenue Administration, in complement to ITRS data. With the help of new study, micro-level data have been produced including type of services and detailed country breakdown. As a result, ITSS, announced by the Turkstat, have become the main data source for the services item of the balance of payments statistics starting from March 5, 2020. Accordingly, the data sources for the survey's main framework are as follows; * The enterprises (export/import values of which are above the threshold and which benefit from the exemption in the selected codes within the scope of the full exemption of the VAT No.1 Declaration and have import values in the VAT No.2 Declarations.) * In addition, the enterprises undertaking with value of maintenance, repair, processing and processing fees within the scope of inward and outward processing customs regimes in customs records are included. * The enterprises acting in the insurance and finance sectors are included as a complete population. The classification system used for ITSS is the Extended Payments Balance Services Classification (EBOPS), according to which international services are displayed under twelve headings. Travel: Mainly based on sample surveys conducted by Turkstat on behalf of the CBRT and the Ministry of Culture and Tourism, the expenditures of foreign visitors in Türkiye plus citizen visitors residing abroad are deemed to be "travel revenues", while the "travel expenditures" cover the expenditures of domestic residents on their trips abroad. The expenditures of citizen visitors residing abroad are included starting from January 2003. In addition, the credit card data of the Interbank Card Center (BKM), the results of the health tourism research, and the fees paid by the international students, compiled from the Council of Higher Education (YÖK), are other data sources. Primary Income: Income data on direct investment, portfolio investment and other investment are derived from the banks' reports. The figure on reinvested earnings' debit item is calculated from "Inward Direct Investment Survey", which is conducted annually. Wages earned by Turkish employees/workers working abroad and wages earned by the Turkish staff working in foreign diplomatic and consular missions in Türkiye are recorded under "Primary Income/Compensation of Employees/ Credit" item, wages earned by non-residents working in Türkiye and wages earned by the foreign staff working in Türkiye's consulates and embassies abroad are recorded under "Primary Income/ Compensation of Employees/ Debit "item. Wages data are calculated on the basis of the number of Turkish employees working abroad in the Turkish Employment Agency's records, the number of non-residents working in Türkiye who have been granted work permits by the Ministry of Family, Labor and Social Services, the number of the Turkish and foreign staff working in the consulates, embassies and international institutions according to the Republic of Türkiye Ministry of Foreign Affairs' records. Secondary Income: This item has been divided into two sub-items as "General Government" and "Other Sectors", which has been further divided into "Personal Transfers" including "Workers' Remittances" and "Other Current Transfers". The data source for the "General Government" item is the reports of the CBRT and the banks. "Personal Transfers"include current transfers in cash and "Workers' Remittances" covering the foreign exchange remittances of Turkish citizens residing abroad. Workers' remittances are sent through the domestic banks and converted to domestic currency. The data are obtained from the banks' reports. Data on net value of earned premiums and received claims vis-à-vis the paid premiums and incurred claims regarding nonlife insurance obtained from the related companies are recorded under "Other Transfers" item. Financial Account: "Direct Investment / Net Acquisition of Financial Assets/ Equity capital" is compiled from the banks' reports. "Direct Investment / Net Acquisition of Financial Assets / Other Capital" data is compiled from banks' reports and reflected in the balance of payments statistics starting with 2010 data. "Direct investment/ Net Incurrence of Liabilities / Equity capital" is compiled from the banks' reports, annual "Inward Direct Investment Survey", monthly complementary transactions provided by the Ministry of Industry and Technology "Daily Bulletins" of Borsa İstanbul and financial press. Data on "Direct Investment / Net Incurrence of Liabilities / Other Capital" are obtained from banks' reports providing details on external loan disbursements and repayments of the private sector on a transaction basis. Starting with January 2003 data, the net real estate transactions of non-residents in Türkiye were reflected in the balance of payments under Direct Investment / Net Incurrence of Liabilities / Real Estate". Data source is the records of the General Directorate of Land Registry and Cadastre for the figures on traded real estates with a country breakdown and the acquisitions and sales prices. Portfolio investment: "Net Acquisition of Financial Assets" covers residents' security transactions in external assets. Also included are purchases and sales of bonds issued by residents (Ministry of Treasury and Finance, banks and other sectors) in international markets. The data source is the banks' reports comprising the sectoral breakdown. "Net Incurrence of Liabilities/Equity Securities" covers nonresidents' security transactions in domestic markets associated with equity securities. Data on equity transactions at Borsa İstanbul are derived from stocks provided by the Central Registry Agency starting from 2005 on a security by security basis by adjusting for foreign exchange and market price changes. This item is further broken down into "Banks" and "Other Sectors" presenting the sector of the investment starting with 2011 data. "Net Incurrence of Liabilities/Debt Securities" covers transactions regarding residents' bond issues in domestic markets (the net purchases by nonresidents of the debt securities issued by the Ministry of Treasury and Finance, banks and other sectors in the domestic market) and international markets. Data on nonresidents' net purchases of government domestic debt securities, which are recorded under "Net Incurrence of Liabilities/Debt Securities/General Government/In Türkiye"are derived from stocks provided by custodian banks on a security by security basis and adjusted for foreign exchange and market price changes. Nonresidents' net purchases of domestic debt securities issued by banks and other sectors are also treated in the same manner, where the data source is the Central Registry Agency. Actual gross borrowing and repayment figures regarding the Ministry of Treasury and Finance, banks and other sectors' international bond issues are obtained from the CBRT's and the banks' reports and are recorded under the "Portfolio Investment/ Net Incurrence of Liabilities /Debt Securities/ "Abroad" items and residents'net purchases of these bonds are recorded under the "Portfolio Investment/ Net Acquisition of Financial Assets". Hence, based on the residency principle, the "Portfolio Investment" main item reflects the actual change in external debt securities liabilities. Other investment: Data on loans, currency and deposits, and other assets and liabilities are obtained from the reports of the banks and the CBRT. Loans: Net Acquisition of Financial Assets comprise loans extended by the CBRT, General Government and banks to nonresidents, the placements of resident banks within foreign non-correspondent banks, as well as funds extended abroad by banks through security-related reverse repo transactions. Data sources are the reports of the CBRT and the banks. Net Incurrence of Liabilities comprise loans extended by nonresidents to residents (including the disbursements from the International Monetary Fund) on a sector and maturity basis. Data are compiled from the CBRT's and banks' reports. External transactions between foreign creditors and domestic banks through security-related repos, collaterals or lending transactions are also recorded under this item. Details on long and short-term external loans (where intercompany loans and long-term trade credits can separately be identified) and bonds of the private and public financial and nonfinancial corporations, which comprise information on borrower and creditor, currency denomination, drawings, principal/interest payments and repayment schedule are compiled via the intermediary resident banks' transaction basis reporting forms submitted to the CBRT. Trade Credits: Net Acquisition of Financial Assets consists of credits extended by resident exporters to nonresident importers and the related repayments for these exports at the maturity. For the pre-2006 period, data on credits extended are based on the Turkstat's type of payments figures for exports, while the associated repayment figures are estimated by the moving average method, where the maturities are based on the results of a survey conducted with the exporting companies. Net Incurrence of Liabilities consists of credits extended by nonresident exporters to resident importers and the related payments for these imports at the maturity. For the pre-2006 period, data on disbursements are based on the Turkstat figures for imports in the type of cash against goods and banks' reports for the remaining types of payments, while the associated repayment figures are estimations by the moving average method, where the maturities are based on the results of a survey conducted with the importing companies and banks' reports, respectively. Starting from January 2006, with the direct reporting study, of which the framework was created by the CBRT and field application of the study carried out by Turkstat in 2021, information on the amount of receivables and payables, foreign currency composition, average repayments and information on advances have began to be compiled from companies at micro level. As a result, stock data on short-term trade credit receivables and payables are obtained using the data provided through direct reporting method from trade-related companies. Flow data adjusted for exchange rate changes for the short-term assets and liabilities of trade credit and advances have been estimated by using the aforementioned stock data and reflected on the balance of payments statistics starting from January 2006 publication. Reserves: Data on changes in reserves are obtained from the records of the CBRT. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Goods: Exports (f.o.b.) and Imports (c.i.f.) data are compiled on a general trade basis by TurkStat from customs declaration forms since 2013. TurkStat provides data on exports and imports of non-monetary gold and an adjustment is made to reclassify these items. Adjustments are used to make for free trade zones and warehouses with the data before 2013 provided by TurkStat to convert special trade basis foreign trade statistics to general trade basis.Adjustments are also made for freight and insurance to reduce the import totals to f.o.b. basis. The amounts deducted for freight and insurance services provided by nonresidents are recorded as "transport (freight)" and "insurance" expenditure, respectively. Data of shuttle trade are compiled through surveys by estimating average "per capita" expenditures and by multiplying this figure by the related number of foreign visitors engaged in this activity. Data on exports and imports published by TurkStat include goods for processing in Türkiye and goods for processing abroad. However, as these transactions do not involve change-in-ownership in accordance with BPM6, only the processing fee is recorded as "Manufacturing services on physical inputs owned by others" under Services. Hence, the related exports and imports figures are deducted from general exports and imports figures through the "Adjustment: Coverage" item. "Goods procured in ports by carriers" covering exports and imports of fuels and provisions procured at ports by carriers is not presented as a separate item, exports of which are already included in TurkStat's exports f.o.b. figures, whereas imports of which are included under general imports through the "Adjustment: Coverage" item. Services: Balance of Payments statistics including International Trade in Services Statistics (ITSS) have been revised using the administrative records of Turkish Revenue Administration for the period of 2013-2015 and year-2020, and the results of the ITSS Survey for the period of 2016-2021. For the current year's monthly Balance of Payments Statistics, the statistics compiled from administrative data by the TurkStat are used until ITSS results are revealed. The periodicity of the ITSS survey is annual. Until the ITSS survey results are available, the monthly data obtained from administrative records (GIB) and processed/provided to CBRT by TurkStat are used as a proxy and temporary figures. These temporary figures will be replaced with ITSS results when available. Import data on freight and insurance are estimated by the TurkStat according to a model using data on country of origin in customs declarations, value of goods, type of goods transported and country of the transport vehicle (flag of vehicle). Total import value of freight and insurance are calculated by combining estimated and declared values of freight and insurance. ITSS survey results are crosschecked with the data provided from Revenue Administration, so that the Turkish resident carriers' freight services can be decomposed according to the direction of the freight service such as from Türkiye to abroad, from abroad to Türkiye, and from abroad to abroad. Hence, the new system extracts the share of resident carriers with foreign flag in total freight debit figures and brings an improved method to solve overestimation issue stemming from "flag of convenience". For the travel revenues, estimates are computed by multiplying the number of foreign visitors plus citizen visitors residing abroad and of residents travelling abroad with the related average expenditure, obtained from surveys. In addition, the credit card data of the Interbank Card Center (BKM), the results of the health tourism research, and the fees paid by the international students compiled from the Council of Higher Education (YÖK), are also used as other data sources. Business and personal travel are identified separately in the surveys. Surveys are conducted at the border gates, on a nationality basis, four times a year so as to cover quarterly periods. The survey results estimate the average expenditures of foreign visitors on a nationality basis with the breakdown of overnight-stays and the excursions. On package tours, only the expenditures in Türkiye are taken into consideration. These figures on average expenditures are expanded with the number of foreign visitors provided by the Directorate General for Security for the related periods. Likewise, the average expenditures of citizen visitors residing abroad are expanded by the number of citizen visitors based on another survey. The travel expenditures are compiled from "Arriving Citizens Survey" conducted by the Turkstat. In order to estimate per capita expenditure figures for Turkish travelers abroad, these sample surveys are also conducted on a quarterly basis for resident visitors arriving in Türkiye. Primary Income: Compensation of employees: For the credit item, the number of workers sent abroad from the records of the General Directorate of Turkish Employment Agency (İŞKUR) and the number of Turkish personnel working in foreign embassies, consulates and international organizations from the Ministry of Foreign Affairs are used. For the debit item, the number of non-resident employees registered in the Social Security Institution (SGK) system and wage data are used. Data on direct investment, portfolio investment, and other investment income are obtained from the CBRT and banks' reports. Annual figures on reinvested earnings' debit item calculated from "Inward Direct Investment Survey" are allocated equally to each month. Financial Account: Direct investment: Data are compiled on an asset/liabilitybasis, with a breakdown for equity capital, reinvested earnings and other capital. Portfolio investment: Data provide a breakdown into assets and liabilities and a further breakdown by sectors. Other investment: Data are available by types, sectors and maturities (short and long term). 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Seasonal Adjustment: No seasonal adjustment takes place. Geographic Detail: There is a geographical breakdown by country for foreign direct investment. None
