 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Concept : The Monthly Economic Activity Indicator of Ecuador (IMAEc) is a high-frequency indicator that allows timely and forward-looking monitoring of the evolution of the country's economic activity within the framework of the National Accounts system. Definition : The IMAEc is a monthly chained-volume estimate of economic activity constructed from supply indicators, direct or indirect in the absence of available information, which are extrapolated over the reference levels of the National Accounts System. Its methodology ensures conceptual coherence and statistical consistency with the National Accounts framework. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Classification used : The IMAEc is constructed from statistical series under the assumption that the behavior of each industry, in accordance with the International Standard Industrial Classification Revision 4 (ISIC Rev. 4), is adequately represented by the selected indicator. Scope of the data · Scope of the data Industrial coverage : The selected statistical series cover the full set of economic activities of the country, with national coverage, in accordance with the structure of the Annual National Accounts with reference year 2018. The selection considers the relative weight of each activity in total Gross Domestic Product (GDP), as well as the availability of statistical information with monthly periodicity. Economic activities and weights in the IMAEc relative to 2018 GDP: Agriculture, livestock, forestry, and fishing (7.6%) Oil and mining (7.9%) Manufacture (13.4%) Construction (5.1%) Commerce (14.2%) Services (46.2%) The remaining 5.6% corresponds to Net Taxes on Products. Structure sources : The structures are based on the Annual National Accounts with a chain link and reference year 2018 = 100. Item selection : Items were selected according to their relevance within the structure of the Annual National Accounts, as well as the availability of basic indicators with monthly periodicity. Unit selection : The IMAEc does not strictly correspond to an Industrial Production Index (IPI), as it incorporates economic activities that are not limited to the manufacturing sector. Sample size : Not applicable. Data collection methods : Data are received through electronic means and in digital format. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Nature of the indicators used: The compilation of the IMAEc considers two types of indicators: value indicators, expressed in monetary terms, and price indicators, expressed as indices with base year 2018 = 100. Item/product specification : The data received correspond to real figures expressed in monetary values—such as, for example, sales values reported to the Internal Revenue Service—which are deflated using price indices, such as the National Producer Price Index, the Consumer Price Index, the Construction Price Index, and External Trade Price Indices. In general, the basic data come s from administrative records, reports, and statistical forms that meet the information requirements of the IMAEc. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness The basic data of the IMAEc have a monthly periodicity. 3.2.1 Source data assessment 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Procedures for nonresponse : The IMAEc does not collect information directly from firms or production units. In cases where timely data is not available, indirect estimates of a temporary nature are used, which are replaced once the information becomes available. Treatment of missing prices : Not applicable. Selection of replacement items : If it becomes necessary to replace an indicator, an alternative series that meets the statistical requirements established for the calculation of the IMAEc is selected. Adjustments for quality differences : Not applicable. Introduction of new units and products : In the case of the IMAEc, it is possible to further disaggregate the indicator into new industries; for this purpose, a new time series must be constructed to estimate the activity of the incorporated industry. The inclusion of new series, following statistical evaluation and validation, implies a revision of the individual structures that make up the IMAEc. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Nature of the structures : The structures were calculated based on the Annual National Accounts, using a chain link with reference year 2018 = 100. Period of the structures : Annual. Frequency of updating the structures: Annual. Calculation of lower-level indices : The calculation is based on estimating gross value-added levels by industry, expressed in volume terms and valued at average prices of the previous year. Aggregation : The IMAEc corresponds to the sum of gross value added by industry plus net taxes on products. In this sense, it is equivalent to the GDP estimate calculated using the production approach. Alignment of weights and base period : Not applicable. Linking of reweighted series: It was not necessary to establish a link or splicing between the old and new series. Reference period : Reference year, 2018 = 100. Seasonally adjusted indices : The seasonally adjusted IMAEc corrects for calendar-related seasonal effects, including holidays and leap years. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results Data verification : The validation of the new series incorporates a statistical analysis supported by conventional time series tests, such as stationarity analysis, outlier detection, intersectoral coherence, and consistency with quarterly and annual aggregates.
