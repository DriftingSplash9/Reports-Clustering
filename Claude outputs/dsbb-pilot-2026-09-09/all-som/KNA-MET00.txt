I. Analytical Framework, Concepts, Definitions, and Classifications
 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Merchandise trade statistics are compiled in accordance with recommendations on International Trade Statistics Concepts and Definitions of the United Nations, Statistical Papers series M No.52 of 1970. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Data are disseminated by commodity classification, volume, value, supplementary quantity where applicable and by country or origin/destination. Commodities are classified using both the Harmonized System (HS) and Standard International Trade Classification (SITC) Rev. 3. 

II. Scope of the data
 Scope of the data 
 Merchandise trade covers the movement of goods to and from St. Kitts and Nevis, and the rest of the world via sea, air and parcel post (goods passing through the post office). Items traded include the value of improvements and repairs to goods, non-monetary gold, and goods leased for period exceeding one year. Exceptions to coverage Trade statistics is compiled using the special system of trade. Hence, goods are normally only considered imports after they have been cleared by the Customs and Excise Department. Imports therefore exclude all goods brought into the country but are held in customs bonded warehouses, likewise goods re-exported form customs warehouses are excluded from exports. But, over the past few years due the impact of hurricanes, some estimation is made for goods that were either lost/damage or entered the country unrecorded by custom's officials.

III. Accounting Conventions
 2.4.1 Valuation The valuation of imports is C.I.F, and total exports (the sum of domestic exports and re-exports) are recorded at F.O.B.

IV. Nature of the Basic Data Sources
 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The primary source of trade data is the customs declaration forms, which are processed by the Statistical Office using the EUROTRACE computerized trade-processing system. In addition, information on sugar exports is obtained from the St. Kitts Sugar Manufacturing Corporation (SSMC), and export of manufactured articles (under SITC sections 7 & 8) to the United States is obtained from the website of the US Census Bureau.
