 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Concept and definition: In line with the sixth edition of the IMF's Balance of Payments manual (BPM6) methodological requirements and the ECB recommendation on Eurosystem reserve assets (see the page dedicated to International reserves on the ECB's website - https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/balance_of_payments_and_external/ international_reserves/html/index.en.html). Both the official external reserve assets and the complete template are consistent with the IMF's Operational Guidelines for the compilation of the data template on international reserves and foreign currency liquidity. The reserve assets must: (i) be under the effective control of the Central Bank of Ireland and (ii) refer to highly liquid, marketable and creditworthy non-euro area currency-denominated claims on non-residents of the euro area as well as gold, special drawing rights and the reserve positions in the IMF of Ireland. Deposits with banks located in Ireland and/or in other euro area Member States are therefore not considered as part of the official reserves. Reserve assets are compiled on a gross basis without any netting of reserve-related liabilities (with the exception of those reserve assets included in the sub-item of financial derivatives, which are recorded on a net basis). All reversible gold transactions are treated as collateralised loans and recorded accordingly in the national reserves statistics. Other issues: No deviations from the ECB recommendations contained in the document "The statistical treatment of positions and transactions involving the Eurosystem's international reserves". 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Classification: Both the official external reserve assets and the complete template are consistent with the IMF's Operational Guidelines . 2.2.1 Scope Classification: Both the official external reserve assets and the complete template are consistent with the IMF's Operational Guidelines . Scope of the data Institutional coverage: Central Bank of Ireland for official reserves and Department of Finance for the rest of the template. Instrument coverage: Monetary gold, SDRs, Reserve position in IMF and Foreign Exchange claims on non-euro area resident (including financial derivatives). Financial derivatives coverage: Financial Derivatives can be identified separately. 2.4.1 Valuation Valuation: The valuation is based on market prices by using: (i) the prevailing market prices at the time when the transaction takes place for transactions; and (ii) the closing mid-market prices at the end of the appropriate period for holdings. Conversion rates: The conversion of foreign currency-denominated assets into euro is based on: (i) the mid-point foreign-exchange rate of the day when the transaction takes place for flows; and (ii) as regards stocks, the mid-point foreign exchange rate on the last day of the reference period. 2.4.2 Recording basis Frequency of publication and revaluation: Reserve assets are calculated on a monthly basis. The figures are revaluated on a monthly basis as regards the foreign exchange rates and quarterly based on market prices, to meet the b.o.p/i.i.p. standards and to take into account the accruals principle. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Data sources: Financial Control Department of the Central Bank of Ireland, and data from the Department of Finance. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Statistical adjustments: No country specific particularities None
