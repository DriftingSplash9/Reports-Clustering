 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Analytical framework: A monthly sectoral balance sheet and a survey are prepared for the CBT along the lines in the IMF's 2016 Monetary and Financial Statistics Manual And Compilation Guide ( MFSMCG ) by reclassifying or regrouping the items in its end-of-month accounting statement. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The classification of financial assets and the sectorization of counterparts institutional units in the source data on the CBT is consistent with the methodology in the IMF's 2016 Monetary And Financial Statistics Manual And Compilation Guide ( MFSMCG ). The concept of residence used in the classification of certain positions is consistent with the definition given in the sixth edition of the IMF's Balance of Payments Manual . 2.2.1 Scope The classification of financial assets and the sectorization of counterparts institutional units in the source data on the CBT is consistent with the methodology in the IMF's 2016 Monetary And Financial Statistics Manual And Compilation Guide ( MFSMCG ). The concept of residence used in the classification of certain positions is consistent with the definition given in the sixth edition of the IMF's Balance of Payments Manual . Scope of the data Institutional coverage: The data on the end-of-month accounting statement of the CBT relate to the positions of its headquarters and branches. 2.4.1 Valuation Accounting practices: The data are end-of-period stocks and are expressed in thousands or millions of Tunisian dinars. Valuation of foreign-currency-denominated instruments: Positions denominated in foreign currency are converted to national currency values at the market exchange rate prevailing at the point in time to which the statement applies 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The monthly accounting statement of the CBT is prepared based on the end-of-month positions of all accounts in the trial balance 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Statistical adjustment: Balance sheet items are not subject to any adjustment or statistical manipulation (seasonal adjustment) 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results Data verification: Claims and liabilities on/to other depository corporations in the central bank's sectoral balance sheet are assessed against liabilities and claims to/on central bank in the other depository corporations (ODCs) sectoral balance sheet.
