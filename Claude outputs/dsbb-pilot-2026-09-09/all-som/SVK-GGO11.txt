 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Analytical Framework: General government operations - annual data are compiled in accordance with the Government Finance Statistics Manual, IMF, 2014 (GFSM 2014). General government operations data can be also used for the analyses of the general government influence on the other sectors of the economy as well as analyses of the macroeconomic indicators influence on the general government revenue and expenditure. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The general government sector and its subsectors are defined in accordance with the ESA 2010 guidelines and the ESA 2010 Manual on Government Deficit and Debt, 2022 edition, which are also consistent with the 2008 SNA and GFSM 2014. The general government in Slovak Republic consists of central government, local government, and social security funds. Scope of the data Institutional coverage: The general government covers of the central government, local government and social security funds, which are specified in the Register of subjects of the general government on the Statistical Office of the Slovak Republic website only in Slovak. ( http://slovak.statistics.sk/wps/portal/ext/Databases/administration/ ). Exceptions to coverage Unrecorded activity 2.4.1 Valuation Valuation of foreign current transaction: The transactions denominated in the foreign currency are valued at the relevant European Central Bank rate which is accepted on the transaction day. 2.4.2 Recording basis Accounting basis: The released data are recorded on an accrual basis. Complementary period: No complementary period. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures Adjustments for grossing and netting of transactions: The revenues and expenditure balance does not include transactions recorded on the net basis. The net purchases of non-financial assets are recorded of separately. Consolidation: Data are consolidated. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Data sources: Data on General Government Operations are compiled by the Public Sector Financial Statistics Department of Ministry of Finance of Slovak Republic. Data source for the data on General Government operations compilation are the financial statements reported by subjects of general government in line with the Directive No. MF/017353/2017-352 as amended, on laying down the layout, content, method, date and place of submission of information from the accounts and data necessary for the purposes of assessing the implementation of the budget of the public administration. The Directive is available on MoF´s website solely in Slovak version https://www.mfsr.sk/sk/financie/statne-vykaznictvo/legislativa/ . 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording None 5.1.5 Dissemination on request 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points
