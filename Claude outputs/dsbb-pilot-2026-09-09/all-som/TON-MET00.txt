 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Commodities are classified using the Harmonized System (HS) and covers all exports and imports from Tonga. A media release is prepared in English and Tongan for each quarter and year, and disseminated to the media. Data on exports and imports are published by HS (to the four digit level), country of destination/origin, institutional sector of exporter or importer, duty collected, quantity and value, total exports, total imports and balance of trade. Beginning year 2000, total exports and total imports and balance of trade for the previous 10 years are also included. Imports are also classified by broad economic categories (BEC) and end use. Scope of the data Provisional monthly and quarterly data are sometimes released to Ministry of Finance for internal use before the quarterly report is released. Bonded imports are included in statistics at the time they leave the bond store. This generally applies to tobacco and alcohol imports. Joint venture fishing with foreign and local ownership is carried out in the Tonga economic zone. This fishing activity is regarded as part of the domestic economy and, for this fleet, no imports of fish or sales of (bunker) fuel are recorded in the merchandise trade statistics. Fish from joint venture vessels that is landed in other countries or transhipped at sea is not recorded in Customs and is excluded from the merchandise trade record. Squash (Pumpkins) are a significant export. Difficulties arise in the recording of exports and in assigning values as most are sold on consignment. This can give rise to substantial revisions in export values. A similar situation does occur with fish exports but such revisions are smaller. Exceptions to coverage Unrecorded activity Bunkering of foreign aircraft is not recorded in merchandise trade but is included in the BoP measure of exports. 2.4.1 Valuation Monthly data on total exports (fob) and imports (cif) are compiled by the Statistics Department, and quarterly data disseminated in a report titled “Foreign Trade Statistics”. An annual Foreign Trade Report is also prepared and disseminated two months after the end of the year. 2.4.2 Recording basis Import payments and export receipts are recorded in the monthly foreign exchange statistics produced by the banking system. These have a lower coverage than Custom based data but are produced within one month after the end of each reference month. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The data source for trade statistics are import and export entries from the Customs Department. There is a legal requirement that all exporters and importers complete an entry for each consignment of imports or exports. The Statistics Department collects copies of these entries on a daily basis from the Customs Department. These entries are checked, coded, then processed using PC-Trade(2000 version). 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures There are substantial revision to export values because some significant exports are sold on consignment.
