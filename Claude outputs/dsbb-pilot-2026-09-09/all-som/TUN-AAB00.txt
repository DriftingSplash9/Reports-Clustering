 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Analytical Framework: An analytical survey is prepared for the depository corporations by consolidating the analytical survey on CBT and the analytical survey on depository corporations other than the CBT. These surveys are compiled along the lines in the IMF's 2016 Monetary and Financial Statistics Manual and Compilation Guide ( MFSMCG ) by reclassifying or regrouping the items in their end-of-month accounting statements. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The classification of financial assets in the source data on the CBT and on (commercial) banks is consistent with the methodology in the IMF's 2016 Monetary and Financial Statistics Manual and Compilation Guide ( MFSMCG ). The sectorization of counterparts institutional units of (commercial) banks is available for all types of transactions in the statistical reporting in accordance with the appendices of the circular n° 2017-06 to banks and financial institutions. The concept of residence used in the classification of certain positions is consistent with the definition given in the sixth edition of the IMF's Balance of Payments Manual . 2.2.1 Scope The classification of financial assets in the source data on the CBT and on (commercial) banks is consistent with the methodology in the IMF's 2016 Monetary and Financial Statistics Manual and Compilation Guide ( MFSMCG ). The sectorization of counterparts institutional units of (commercial) banks is available for all types of transactions in the statistical reporting in accordance with the appendices of the circular n° 2017-06 to banks and financial institutions. The concept of residence used in the classification of certain positions is consistent with the definition given in the sixth edition of the IMF's Balance of Payments Manual . Scope of the data Institutional coverage: Data in the depository corporations survey include data on the CBT and data on (commercial) banks. 2.4.1 Valuation Accounting practices: The data are end-of-period stocks and are expressed in thousands or millions of Tunisian dinars. Valuation of foreign-currency-denominated instruments: Positions denominated in foreign currency are converted to national currency values at the market exchange rate prevailing at the point in time to which the statement applies. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures Reciprocal positions between the CBT and the aggregate other depository corporations are netted out in the depository corporations analytical survey. This applies in particular to all deposits/credits positions and to the currency positions. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Data sources on the CBT: the monthly accounting statement of the CBT is prepared based on the end-of-month positions of all accounts in the trial balance. Data sources on other depository corporations: monthly balance sheet and appendices submitted individually by (commercial) banks to the CBT are aggregated 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Statistical adjustment: Balance sheet items are not subject to any adjustment or statistical manipulation (seasonal adjustment). 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Non applicable. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results Data verification: Claims and liabilities on/to other depository corporations in the central bank's sectoral balance sheet are assessed against liabilities and claims to/on central bank in the other depository corporations (ODCs) sectoral balance sheet.
