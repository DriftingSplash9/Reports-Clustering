 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The sixth edition of the manual, BPM6, is the current version and is implemented in the Norwegian Balance of Payments (BOP) and the Norwegian international investment position (IIP) from December 2014. Back data based on the guidelines from BPM6 is implemented in the financial account (BOP and IIP). The IIP financial account has time series going back to 1st quarter 2012. On the Statistics Norway website the IIP is published as "Foreign assets and liabilities". Annual end-of-period stocks of external financial assets and liabilities are presented under the following data categories: direct investment, portfolio investment, other investment and reserve assets. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The Norwegian International investment position is presented in accordance with the latest internationally approved guidelines. These are specified in "The Balance of Payments Manual, 6th edition" (BPM6), published by the International Monetary Fund (IMF). The underlying principles and definitions presented there are in full accordance with corresponding international rules for the National Accounts, as laid down in the manual "System of National Accounts 2008" (2008 SNA ), which is published by a number of international organizations jointly, including the United Nations and also the IMF. The IIP classification by function is followed, which implies that assets and liabilities are broken down into direct investment, portfolio investment, other investment, and reserve assets. All sectors are included. Scope of the data The Norwegian International investment position is presented in accordance with the latest internationally approved guidelines. These are specified in "The Balance of Payments Manual, 6th edition" (BPM6), published by the International Monetary Fund (IMF). The underlying principles and definitions presented there are in full accordance with corresponding international rules for the National Accounts, as laid down in the manual "System of National Accounts 2008" (2008 SNA ), which is published by a number of international organizations jointly, including the United Nations and also the IMF. The Norwegian IIP covers the stock of external financial assets and liabilities. Data are valued in million of Norwegian kroner (NOK). IIP covers all institutional sectors: Norges Bank (the central bank), deposit-taking corporations except the central bank, money market fund, general government, financial corporations other than MFIs and non-financial corporations, households, and non-profit institutions serving households. IIP is sub-classified by function: Direct investment, portfolio investment, other investment and reserve assets. Financial derivatives are published under the function "other investment". A direct investment is defined as an investor who owns 20 percent or more of the ordinary shares or voting power in a foreign enterprise. This includes entities that are subsidiaries, associates and branches. The definition is followed due to Norwegian accounting standards. Portfolio investment includes debt securities and equity securities not included in direct investment or reserve assets. Other investment is a residual category that includes all financial transactions not covered in direct investment, portfolio investment or reserve assets. The category includes mainly bank deposits, loans, financial derivates, trade credit. Reserve assets comprise monetary gold, special drawing rights (SDR), the reserve position with the IMF and foreign exchange reserves. 2.4.1 Valuation Direct investment is mainly valued at book value, but for some enterprises we have market prices. Portfolio investment is valued at current market prices. On the liability side we can split between listed and unlisted companies. All listed companies are valued at stock exchange market prices, unlisted companies are valued at own funds at book value. 2.4.2 Recording basis Since the basic sources are accounting data, accrual accounting is applied. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures Data are on a gross basis. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The main sources for the financial account; Quarterly accounting statistics for financial corporations under supervision. Positions are reported and some transactions (equity, other debt securities and loans) and some revaluations (exchange rate changes and other price changes) quarterly and annually. Quarterly accounting statistics for non-financial corporations and financial institutions not under supervision. Positions and revaluations are reported quarterly and annually. Annual accounting statistics for the general government. Positions and revaluations (exchange rate changes and other price changes) are reported. Quarterly data from the Norwegian Central Securities Depository (VPS) and data from a separate survey on mutual funds. Positions and transactions are reported. Data collected for non-financial corporations and mutual funds are based on sample surveys. For areas with incomplete statistical coverage, it is necessary to do estimations or use supplementary sources such as tax returns. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording The scope of the International investment position is defined in international guidelines in the Balance of Payments and International Investment Position Manual (BPM6), System of National Accounts (2008 SNA, published by the UN, OECD, IMF, World Bank and the European Commission) and the European System of National and Regional Accounts (ESA 2010). Like Balance of Payments, International investment positions are constructed around three basic concepts: statistical units, economic values and transactions. Briefly, the accounting systems describe transactions between statistical units in which economic values are provided or received in exchange for other economic values. The IIP shall in principle include all positions between unit’s resident of the Norwegian economic territory and non-resident units. The delineation of the economy towards the rest of the world is based on the concept of resident units. A unit is a resident unit when it has a centre of economic interest in the economic territory in question, i.e. when it is engaged in economic activity in a territory for a long period of time (at least one year). The Norwegian economic territory includes mainland Norway together with the Norwegian part of the Continental Shelf, Svalbard and Jan Mayen with Bjørnøya. All position shall be valued at market prices. Market prices are defined as amounts of money that willing buyers pay to acquire something from willing sellers; the exchanges are made between independent parties and on the basis of commercial considerations only. The exchange rate on the position date or the average rate for the shortest period applicable shall be used for converting positions in foreign currencies into the national currency. Stocks of assets and liabilities are to be valued at prices or rates in effect at the time to which the balance sheet relates. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Norway's IIP data is available three months after the reference period, and thus meets the SDDS requirements. 3.2.1 Source data assessment Revisions: The IIP data are subject to control and revisions before each publication. The data is integrated with the Balance of Payments and National Accounts thereby allowing for consistency checks against total transactions and positions for the various items. Also checks on the breakdown of positions into price and volume components accommodates for evaluation of the results on transactions. Estimations: For most of the IIP items the figures used are as shown in the primary sources. Others are derived through estimations of which the most important are: Financial transactions are to a large extent estimated starting with observed investment positions. The definitional identity employed is: opening position + transactions + revaluations = closing position. Most of the revaluations due to exchange rate movements are estimated combining exchange rates and information on foreign currencies in use for different variables. The household’s holiday houses abroad are estimated based on tax information. Price changes and exchange rates are estimated from information from various countries. The non-financial enterprises and financial institutions not under supervision are based on a quarterly sample survey. This covers the major enterprises in the population and has a sample size of 500-600 enterprises. Quarterly numbers are then grossed up with figures from the annual BoP reporting that has a sample size of 3,000 enterprises. This is done by adding the enterprises only included in yearly survey to the fourth quarter and are then copied the following three quarters. These enterprises represent approximately ten percent of non-financial enterprises' total assets and liabilities. Direct investment: Direct investment data is mainly collected by a sample survey. This survey has recently been established to serve mainly BoP/IIP purposes, and is continuously assessed/evaluated. The Response error is continually corrected as questionnaires are returned to Statistics Norway. Portfolio investment: Portfolio investments are based on information from a great variety of statistical sources. For the sector general government and most of the financial institutions data are based on censuses and data from the Norwegian Securities Depository. This implies 100 percent coverage. Data received from respondents are quality checked. Non-financial enterprises portfolio investments are collected by a sample survey and by using data from the Norwegian securities depository/register. The sample survey covers about 90 to 95 percent of the enterprises. Data received from respondents are quality checked. In case of low response rate imputations are used. Other investment: Other investments are collected from both censuses and sample surveys depending on sector. Data received from respondents are quality checked. Reserve assets: Data are reported by the Central Bank. Data are quality checked. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Parts of the IIP data are based on sample surveys.
