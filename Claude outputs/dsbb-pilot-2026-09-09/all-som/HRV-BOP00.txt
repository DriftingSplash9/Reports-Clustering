 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Analytical framework: Balance of payments data are compiled in accordance with the recommendations of the sixth edition of the IMF's Balance of Payments Manual ( BPM6 ) and the ESA2010. Definition: The balance of payments is a statistical statement that systematically summarizes, for a specific time period, the economic transactions of residents of Croatia with the rest of the world. Transactions, for the most part between residents and nonresidents, consist of those involving goods, services, primary and secondary income, those involving financial claims on and liabilities to the rest of the world; and those (such as gifts) classified as transfers, which involve offsetting entries to balance—in an accounting sense—one-sided transactions. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The classification of transactions is based on the sixth edition of Balance of Payments Manual ( BPM6 ) and the ESA2010. Scope of the data Data are disseminated in millions of euro, showing exports and imports of goods, exports and imports of services and the balance of goods and services, net primary income, net secondary income, and the capital and financial account, i.e. change in official reserves assets and financial transactions (direct investments, portfolio investments, financial derivatives and other investments). The data cover the entire economy. Transaction coverage: All transactions, as recommended in BPM6 are included in the balance of payments. Sectorial coverage: The sector classification of the portfolio and other investment involves the sector classification of residents according to ESA 2010 and is fully harmonised with the sector classification of the gross external debt by domestic sectors and the IIP. Geographical coverage: A geographical attribution of the data is available for all partner countries, but due to the confidentiality reason not all countries will be published. Unrecorded activity: No estimates for smuggling are prepared. Due to certain reporting tresholds the reporting system, in practice, may not cover all transactions and estimates are used. 2.4.1 Valuation Valuation: Transactions are mostly valued at market prices. Otherwise, book values are used. Conversion to unit of account: Data for transactions reported in foreign currencies are converted to euro at the daily exchange rate. 2.4.2 Recording basis Recording of income: Income is recorded on an accrual basis. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Goods: Up to 1 July 2013., the main data source for export and import of goods was the Report of the Central Bureau of Statistics on merchandise trade of the Republic of Croatia, compiled from customs declarations. After the accession of the Republic of Croatia to the European Union, on 1 July 2013, data on foreign trade in goods statistics of the Republic of Croatia are acquired from two different sources: the Intrastat form for the statistics on trading in goods between EU Member States (Intrastat) and the Single Administrative Document for statistics on the trading in goods with the non-EU countries (Extrastat). Estimates of purchases of goods by nonresidents in Croatia and by Croatian residents abroad are obtained from the Survey on Consumption of Foreign Travelers, that is carried out jointly by the CNB and outer agency. As from the second quarter of 2019, the goods imports data have been modified by the data on the imports of used cars by natural persons from EU countries that are not covered by the Intrastat reporting system. The imports of goods has been revised starting from 2013. Services: The sources of data on transportation services are the CNB survey on international transportation services, with an exception; revenues from road transport are compiled on the basis of the first releases on transportation of the Croatian Bureau of Statistics and estimates of the Road Fright Transporters Association. Expenditures on the basis of the road freight transportation, equal transportation and insurance costs related to imports of goods which belong to non-residents, are estimated by adjusting the value of imports at c.i.f. parity to the value of imports f.o.b. Revenues from services rendered to foreign travellers and tourists, as well as expenditures incurred by domestic travellers and tourists abroad are shown under the positions Travel. In the 1993–1998 period, these positions were assessed by using various data sources. However, since the available sources for this period do not provide for a full coverage in accordance with the recommended methodology, for the 1999–2002 period, the Survey on Consumption of Foreign Travellers in Croatia and Domestic Travellers Abroad is used for these assessments, which the CNB has carried out since the second half of 1998. The data on the average consumption of travellers from this source, which is the result of the survey of travellers (stratified sample) at border crossings, have been combined with the Ministry of the Interior and Central Bureau of Statistics data on the number of foreign and domestic travellers, in order to assess the total consumption of foreign travellers in the RC and of domestic travellers abroad in the mentioned period. For the following period, 2003–2012, the balance of payments data on revenues on the position Travel are not computed using the described standard methodological combination of volume indicators and estimated average consumption from the Survey on Consumption of Foreign Travellers, but for the sake of greater reliability of the estimate for this period on the revenues side an econometric estimation method is applied, developed by the CNB. The indicators of the Ministry of the Interior and the CBS, as well as other relevant variables, are used as input data for this econometric estimate. Finally, for the period from 2013 on, the data on revenues and expenditures on the positions Travel are recalculated as the multiple of the indicators of the number of travellers and the corresponding survey indicators of their average consumption. The Ministry of the Interior data and the results of the CNB's Survey on Consumption of Travellers serve as the main source of data for this calculation. Starting from 2020, as a result of aggravated conditions for the survey of travellers at border crossings due to the coronavirus pandemic, which have reduced the statistical reliability of computed average consumption, travel revenues are calculated by the method that uses data on card consumption by non-residents (foreigners and Croatian citizens living outside the RC) from the CNB's payment statistics and data on the use of cash and cards for payment transactions from the fiscalised consumption statistics of the Ministry of Finance - Tax Administration. The scope of the use of Tax Administration data is limited to fiscalised consumption statistics' data on only three divisions of NACE Rev. 2: retail trade, accommodation and food and beverage service activities. Other services position is compiled by using different data sources. As of 2011, the uniform statistical survey is used for estimating the position of Other services, which encompasses 30 different types of services, the classification of which is prescribed by the IMF's Balance of Payments Manual, 6th edition. Primary income: The source of data on compensation of employees on the revenue side (including border, seasonal and other workers) is estimated by a model based on the aggregate data of banks on inflows of resident natural persons from non-residents. On the expenditures side, the data source is Tax authority as an administrative source. The source of data on direct investment income (including reinvested earnings) is the CNB Survey on Foreign Direct Investment. The sources of data on portfolio investment income are the CNB Survey on Foreign Direct Investment (income on equity), securities statistics and external debt statistics. The sources of data on other investment income are internal CNB records (for income on reserve assets) and internal CNB statistics on Croatian External Outstanding Debt. Secondary income: The sources of data on official and private transfers are the Ministry of Finance, the Croatian Pension Insurance Administration, model based on aggregate data of banks, survey on trade in international services containing a special part for possible current transfers from and to foreign countries and the international trade statistics. As from the second quarter of 2019, workers' remittances include the estimated share of workers' remittances that bypass the formal transfer channels, such as banking payment transactions and money transfer operators. For this informal part of workers' remittances (cash money transfers), the data time series starting from 2000 has been revised. Capital account: The source of data are data of the Ministry of Finance and data from the survey on trade in international services, containing a special part for possible capital transfers from and to foreign countries. Direct investment: The source of data on direct investment is the CNB Survey on Foreign Direct Investment. Portfolio investment: The sources of data on portfolio investment are the CNB Survey on Foreign Direct Investment (for private sector transactions), CNB external debt statistics, securities statistics and other official data sources (for official sector transactions). Financial derivatives: From the first quarter of 2010 in the balance of payments are included transactions arising from contracts concluded with the characteristics of financial derivatives. Reporting institutions are commercial banks and other financial institutions. From the fourth quarter of 2012 reporting population was expanded to non-financial institutions which are usually included in these jobs to protect against changes in market conditions. Other investment: The source of data on currency and deposits for deposit money banks is monetary statistics. The source of data on other sectors currency and deposits assets is BIS. The source of data on loans received from and extended to nonresidents are the reports on CNB statistics on Croatian External Outstanding Debt. Reserve assets: The source of data on Reserve Assets are internal CNB records. From the second quarter of 1999 onwards, data on reserve assets are directly obtained from the CNB Accounting Department in original currency. From the first quarter of 2013 for this position the data are actual transactions of the Directorate for International Reserves and Foreign Currency Liquidity of the Croatian National Bank. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Seasonal adjustments: No seasonal adjustment takes place.
