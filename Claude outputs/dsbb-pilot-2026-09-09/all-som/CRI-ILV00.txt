 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Concept: Reserve assets include foreign assets under the control of the Central Bank of Costa Rica (BCCR), which may make immediate use of them for purposes of directly financing BOP imbalances or indirectly managing the size of these imbalances through intervention in foreign exchange markets by modifying exchange rates, as well as for other purposes. Definition: Official reserve assets held by the Central Bank of Costa Rica. Relationship to international guidelines: The definition, contents, and calculation of official reserve assets held by the BCCR are in complete agreement with IMF methodology, as established in the sixth edition of the Balance of Payments and International Investment Position Manual 6th edition. In regard to the reserve assets and the liquidity template they are made by the use of the International Reserves and Foreign Currency Liquidity, Guidelines for a Data Template. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Classification: Reserve assets are classified as: Reserves in foreign currency (including coins and bank notes, as well as demand and term deposits of the BCCR with foreign banks, debt securities and financial derivatives ) ;Reserve position in the Fund; SDRs; Monetary gold; Financial derivatives Other reserve assets (some interests receivable). Scope of the data Institutional coverage: All the instruments included in official reserve assets are held and controlled by the Central Bank of Costa Rica. Instrument coverage: These assets are available for limited use for balance of payments purposes. Financial derivatives coverage: Reserve assets include financial derivatives. Exceptions to coverage None Unrecorded activity None 2.4.1 Valuation Valuation: Monetary gold and debt securities are revalued daily in accordance with the market price. Gold is valued daily at the price per troy ounce listed on the New York Stock Exchange on the last business day in question, debt securities are valued according to their quotes on the market obtained by Bloomberg. Other assets are valued on the basis of their historical cost. Exchange rates: Reserve assets are converted into U.S. dollars on a daily basis, based on the exchange rates announced by Bloomberg. SDR´s are converted to U.S. dollars with the exchange rate posted by the IMF on a daily basis. Frequency of calculation and revaluation: Reserve assets are calculated and revalued daily 2.4.2 Recording basis Unit of account: Costa Rica's International reserves and the foreign currency liquidity are recorded in U.S. dollars. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures Following the recommendations of BPM6, stocks are recorded on a gross basis. And liabilities included in the liquidity template are recorded con a net basis. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Source of data: The accounting balances of the Central Bank of Costa Rica are the source of information for calculating official reserve assets. External Debt data provided by MOF and available directly to the BCCR through DMFAS. Data for domestic debt in foreign currency are obtained from the records of domestic debt throughout the access to National System of Electronic Payments (SINPE for its acronym in Spanish) of the BCCR. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Reserve assets: The BCCR’s assets include monetary gold (when such holdings exist), the reserve position in the IMF, holdings of SDR’s, bank deposits abroad, debt securities and financial derivatives. Data are obtained from BCCR statements. External public debt is calculated by the DMFAS system on a due-for-payment basis. Data for domestic debt in foreign currency are obtained from the records of domestic debt throughout the access to National System of Electronic Payments (SINPE for its acronym in Spanish) of the BCCR 3.1.3 Source data timeliness The timeliness is mainly determined by the requirements of the SDDS. Data collection and processing timetables adequately meet the standards of timeliness and periodicity recommended for disseminating the international reserves and the foreign currency liquidity 3.2.1 Source data assessment The BCCR has developed procedures to reconcile external debt statistics prepared by the ESE vis-à-vis external sector statistics prepared by the MOF. This reconciliation is made electronically in a special format. The ESE has direct access to DMFAS, which is the software shared by MOF and BCCR to manage debt statistics. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques No statistical adjustments are made to the data. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures There are no other statistical procedures. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results Information reported in the financial press is used to verify transactions, such as debt issues abroad by residents. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data The BCCR is responsible for the quality of the data regarding all external sector statistics. Through a series of consultative structures, the quality of the intermediate data is assessed by the ESE in coordination with EMF. In principle, RES and FXT are consistent because they are compiled from the same source data. In addition, data on investment income are assessed in relation to corresponding stock data. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs The results are checked against the Monetary and Financial Statistics to validate or detect any inadequacies in regular data sources. External debt data used in the template is checked in its consistency with the data forecast used in the macroeconomic programming of the Central Bank 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses There are no formal studies to assess revisions to the reserves statistics and international liquidity. However, the BCCR is constantly seeking to improve its statistical processes, and it takes into account the recommendations of external experts who have provided technical assistance. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses Users are informed about the preliminary nature of the data, by means of a footnote. As mentioned above, there is no explicit schedule for revisions, in part because these are done as the definitive information becomes available, and generally within the following days . There are no marks in the statistics indicating revisions because preliminary data are clearly indicated. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request Nonpublished and nonconfidential data on the RES and FXT, are disseminated on request to the Central Bank authorities. 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points The BCCR provides assistance to users that require information on external sector indicators and statistics. Each set of statistical tables identifies names and e-mail addresses of officials in charge of particular areas of information to channel the questions to experts in each area. 5.3.2 Availability of documents and services catalogs A detailed index of statistical publications is available on the BCCR website and updated each year. Hard copies of the same information are available, free of charge, in the Information Center of the BCCR
