 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Analytical Framework: The methodology for compiling government finance statistics is broadly consistent with the analytical framework set out in the IMF's Manual on Government Finance Statistics 2014 (GFSM 2014). The government finance statistics reported for publication in the IMF's Government Finance Statistics Yearbook is presented following the format of the GFSM 2014. Relationship with international guidelines: Major fiscal aggregates of the compiled general government operations are based on the GFSM 2014. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Classification system: The budget classification of the Republic of Belarus is a systematized grouping of revenue and expenditure, sources to finance budget deficit (ways to allocate budget surplus) that provide for republican and international compatibility of indicators. Classification of budget revenue: The classification of budget revenue is a grouping of budget revenue in accordance with underlying legislative acts. The national presentation of budget revenue is in line with the GFSM 2014 except for including some of the transactions in financial and non financial assets in revenue data (e.g. revenues from property disposal, intellectual property rights, seized property, land sales). Functional classification of budget expenditure: The functional classification of budget expenditure reflects the allocation of budget funds for the performance of the main functions of government. The national functional classification is based on the Classification of the Functions of Government published by the United Nations and adopted in the GFSM 2014. Scope of the data Institutional coverage of general government in reported data: Data are disseminated on general government operations covering budgetary accounts of central government units (Republic government units), Social Protection Fund of the Ministry of Labor and Social Protection (SPF), and local government units (local budgets). Resources of other (except for the SPF) extrabudgetary funds, as well as proceeds from extrabudgetary activities of organizations supported by the budget, are not included in general government operations. The SPF encompasses the functions of the social insurance (including pension insurance). According to the Laws on the Republican Budget for 2025 (e.g. Law 48-Z of December13, 2024), the Republican Budget for 2026 (e.g. Law 125-Z of December29, 2025), the republican budget includes following state earmarked budgetary funds: Republic Centralized Innovation Fund; The National Development Fund; The Republican Road Fund; Republic Fund for Universal Service of Digital Development and Communication; The Republican Civil Aviation Fund. Local budgets include budgets of the regions and the city of Minsk, cities, districts, town, semi-urban centers, and rural Soviets. According to the Lawson the Republican Budget for 2025 (e.g. Law 48-Z of December13, 2024), the Republican Budget for 2026 (e.g. Law 125-Z of December29, 2025)local budgets include the resources of: Local Innovation Funds. Exceptions to coverage Resources of other extrabudgetary funds, as well as proceeds from extrabudgetary activities of organizations supported by the budget, are not included in general government operations. 2.4.1 Valuation All cash transactions reflect actual prices. Amounts denominated in foreign currency are converted to Belarusian rubles at the official exchange rates, as provided by the National Bank. 2.4.2 Recording basis Accounting basis: Revenue and expenditures are recorded on a cash basis for compilation of fiscal data. All financing data, except arrears, are recorded on a cash basis, in line with the GFSM 2014. Complementary period: None. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures Adjustments for grossing and netting of transactions: most transactions are shown on a gross basis, in line with the GFSM 2014. Tax refunds are deducted from tax revenue, and financing is presented on a net basis. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Data sources: Budget execution data are maintained in the Ministry of Finance, including: budget execution and deficit financing (ways to allocate budget surplus) data, recorder by the State Treasury Department of the Ministry of Finance; foreign deficit financing data, recorded by Public Debt Department of the Ministry of Finance. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording The budget classifications of revenue and functional classification of expenditure are brought closer to the GFSM 2014. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Source data timeliness is in line with the SDDS requirements. 3.2.1 Source data assessment Verification procedures are in place to assess the accuracy of the budget execution data and some additional resources are planned. There are reviews and revisions of the data reported, and comparison of data reported with indicators prepared for this purpose in the Treasury. The results of these reviews are monitored and made available to guide planning. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Consolidation: the data are presented on a consolidated basis, with transactions between the units of the general government sector: republican budget, SPF and local budgets, eliminated from the data. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data All discrepancies between GFS and intermediate budgetary data, and those between the intermediate budgetary data and accounting series, are investigated. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs Detected statistical discrepancies are investigated and resolved. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses No revision studies and analyses are undertaken. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses No revision studies and analyses are undertaken. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request Unpublished data are distributed according to the concluded treaties. 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points A contact person is identified and the name, email, and telephone number are included on web site of the Ministry of Finance: https://minfin.gov.by/ru/sdds/a6fefe8e49c39b64.html
