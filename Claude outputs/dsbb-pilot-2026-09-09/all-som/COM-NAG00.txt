 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The methodology used for the calculation of GDP is currently in a transitional phase. Since May 2011 the DGSP has been engaged in the updating of its national accounts through the implementation of SNA 1993, with the help of the ERETES software package, taking 2007 as the base year. This is meant to replace the previous methodology (SNA 1968), for which 1999 served as the base year. Currently national accounts statistics are limited to the calculation of GDP and its principal components. GDP is calculated in accordance with the production and consumption approach. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization PRIMARY SECTOR Scope of the data GDP and its components cover the entire economic territory of the Union of the Comoros, namely the islands of Grand Comore, Anjouan, and Mohéli. All economic activities are covered, including those in the informal sector. Exceptions to coverage GDP statistics cover the entire economic territory of the Union of the Comoros. Unrecorded activity All economic activities are captured. 2.4.1 Valuation GDP statistics are prepared on the basis of market prices. 2.4.2 Recording basis The time of recording of GDP data is not specifically stated, as the data used are compiled on a gross basis, that is, the recording basis is not a consideration in their use. No adjustment is made with regard to the recording basis. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures GDP statistics are prepared on a gross basis. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The calculation of GDP is derived from the following sources: - government statistics (the Table of Government Financial Operations, public investment projects, and the Budget Law) ; - external and internal trade statistics; - the balance of payments; - production statistics for the primary agriculture sector (production in terms of volume); - statistics on the actual number of persons employed; - surveys at the enterprise level (nonfinancial corporations, financial corporations, and nonprofit institutions). None 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses There are no revision studies. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses There are no revision studies for GDP statistics. With regard to the transition from the former basis and methodology referred to above, an Explanatory Note for the National Accounts Results for the Base Year 2007 According to SNA 93 provides explanations concerning the differences in results between the two sets of statistics. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request The methods for dissemination to the general public have not yet been determined. According to Article 6 of the new law on statistics, however, statistical products must be provided “to any user who requests them.”
