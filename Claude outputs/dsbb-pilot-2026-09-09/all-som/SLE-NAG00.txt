Unrecorded activity Illegal activities are not estimated but many transactions of the informal sector are estimated. 2.4.1 Valuation All transaction categories are included. All transactions in goods and services are recorded at market prices. Output is recorded at producer’s prices. The recording period is calendar year. SSL estimates the transactions of General Government on a calendar year basis. Imports are measured using c.i.f. prices, while exports are measured using f.o.b. prices. 2.4.2 Recording basis Transactions are recorded on an accrual basis, data on government, which are on cash basis are adjusted to an accrual basis. Transactions in goods and services are recorded at market prices. Outputs are recorded at producers’ prices. The recording period is based on a calendar year. National Government operations are estimated by calendar year, which also coincide with the government calendar. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Production Approach. The Statistics Sierra Leone sends out questionnaires to various institutions on its mailing list to collect production as well as financial data. These institutions include local governments owned, central government owned and private owned institutions. Other data sources include surveys and administrative records. Agriculture, Hunting and Forestry Estimates for Crop production are based on data obtained from the Planning, Evaluation, Monitoring and Statistics Division (PEMSD) in the Ministry of Agriculture and Food Security. The results from the round of crop survey conducted by MAFS have been used in the current estimates. However, a recent survey on Crops and Livestock (Integrated Survey on Farm Production, Food Security and Vulnerability and Nutrition) was conducted in March/April 2005 by MAFS and FAO but analysis of this survey is yet to be done . Livestock The output of this sector include: goat, sheep, mutton, pork products, ducks, eggs and poultry, beef, cattle. Estimates for slaughter figures are no longer provided by the City Council nor are they provided by the Agriculture ministry. As a result, head counts of Livestock figures provided by ministry are used as indicators for estimating the constant while the CPI prices for these live stocks are used to inflate for current price estimation. Forestry Estimates of Timber, Poles, Charcoal and Fire wood are obtained from Agriculture ministry. However, these estimates have proved unreliable and inconsistent; especially for Firewood and Charcoal. Estimates are now partially done by using the Household data and partly by using the Timber figures. Prices for Timber are easily obtained from the market while prices for other items are obtained from the CPI basket. Fishing Estimates of fish production include the total catch recorded by the ministry of Marine Resources and Fisheries plus an estimate of the value of subsistence. The average producer price of fish is derived by discounting the retail price of fish collected for the computation of the CPI. Different discounting rates are used for industrial and traditional and subsistence fishing. Mining and Quarrying At the moment, only diamond mining and quarrying are estimated for in this sector. Estimate of carats exported and their values are obtained from the Government Gold and Diamond Office (GGDO). In estimating, it is assumed that diamond export is equal to total production since all of the diamond productions are meant for export. Additional figures are obtained from the Balance of Payment section in the Bank of Sierra Leone to account for the value of smuggled diamond. The volume index of carats of diamond exported is used as indicator for the constant estimates and these are inflated by the average price index to estimate the current figures. A mark-up of about 15% is used to account for quarrying. Manufacturing Manufacturing establishments are now estimated in three different categories: Data received from the Bank of Sierra Leone production Figures Returns from the survey questionnaires and The informal sector data from the Household data. Comparison is done in greater detail to avoid over lapping of institutions and hence double counting. Ratios used for estimation are computed from the survey data. Electricity and Water Supply In Sierra Leone, the National Power Authority and Bo-Kenema Power Services manage electricity in the entire country. The financial and production data relating to the generation of electricity and input requirements are obtained from these institutions for compilation. The Guma Valley Water Company is the only agency engaged in water supply in the western area. The company provides detailed annual statistics to the Statistics Sierra Leone and these have been used to calculate its contribution to GDP. The water supply services undertaken by Sierra Leone Water Company (SALWACO) in the provincial towns are also included in these estimates. Construction Estimates are made from the survey questionnaires sent out and the index of the sum of cement manufactured and imported is used to deflate the current estimates in order to estimate the constant figures. Since all construction companies have their head offices in the City, estimates are said to reflect national figure. These estimates will become more solid when the master sample frame is prepared after the Establishment Census that is presently going on. Wholesale and Retail Trade Trading activity is a keystone in the Sierra Leone National Accounts. This is because quite a large proportion of private final expenditure, all capital expenditure on plant equipment and a high proportion of intermediate product pass through this sector. Hence, it provides a vital connection between the production and expenditure sides of the national accounts. At the moment commodity flow approach is used to estimate this sector. Estimates are done under the following groupings and using various ratios from surveys: Crops Fishery Forestry Livestock Imports Manufacturing Hotels and Restaurants Estimates for this sector is done by two approaches: By collecting Room Revenue figures and other data from the National Tourist Board And the Household data for the estimation of food-out-of-home. Estimated ratios from the survey are then applied on the above figures for the current estimates while the employment index is used to deflate for the constant estimates. Transport and Communication Data are obtained from Transport Sector Project in the Ministry of Transport and Communications and from the Sierra Leone Road Transport Authority. Additional data are also obtained from the annual survey using Specialised questionnaires especially for the Air Transport and communication. This sector is compiled under the following grouping: Road Air: Local air lines Foreign air lines (Agents) Sea (handling agencies etc) Finance, Insurance, Real Estate and Business Services The required information on the income and expenditure of the Commercial Banks, the Central Bank, Insurance Companies and Credit Societies are furnished directly by the establishments themselves through the conduct of the National Accounts survey. Details of the cost structure of the sector are extracted from the data supplied. In calculating the contribution of the sector to GDP, a service charge equal to the excess of interest received over interest paid is calculated. The procedure for estimating the gross national output is not akin to the one used in estimating the output of most other sectors. If the transaction of financial institutions were conceptually treated like the transactions of other activity sectors, the gross output would be underestimated. The annual rental value of dwellings includes owner occupied dwellings. It has been estimated separately for: Government owned houses. Private houses in the country using the Household Survey data. Government Services Data on government final consumption expenditure and contribution of government to GDP are extracted from the budget documents. The local government is newly established institution in Sierra Leone; their figures are lumped in the Central Government but strategies are being put in place to estimate the Local government separately. As per SNA, the contribution of government services to GDP is equal to the value of government Compensation of employees plus capital consumption. Other Services For education the gross valued added is taken as the total wages and salaries of teachers and other staff employed in educational institutions and the information for Education comes from the budget document of the Ministry of Finance. Medical and Health expenditure are treated in a similar fashion. The number of private modern medical practitioners has been obtained by using a certain ratio on government estimates provided by previous surveys. Expenditure Method Final consumption expenditure Data on government final consumption is obtained from government budget and accounting records. Data on government final consumption have been estimated as Government expenditure on goods and services less revenue or receipt for sale, fees and other surpluses. Private final Consumption has been estimated using the Sierra Leone Integrated Household Survey; population figures and prices are now used to project for years after 2003 in which the survey was conducted. Capital formation Capital formation is estimated following the commodity flow method. Fixed capital formation is measured in terms of domestic production plus import of capital goods. In Sierra Leone the production of capital goods is confined to building and construction work. The building and construction component equals the value of output of the construction industry less the value of maintenance work. The value of imported machinery and equipment is extracted from the import statistics and appropriate markups have been used to allow for import duties and commercial charges. Details on stock variation have been collected in respect to mining companies, public corporations, and some large manufacturing establishments. For other establishments, stock variations are assumed to be zero. Consumption of fixed capital Data on consumption of fixed capital is extracted from questionnaires completed by industrial establishments and other public corporations. There is little information available for the transport industry or other sectors and where an estimate of depreciation is done, it is calculated as a percentage of the value of gross output. Imports and exports The data on overall exports, imports, factor income flows and other international transactions have been extracted from balance of payments statistics published by the Bank of Sierra Leone, although for current periods export and import of goods are taken directly from merchandise trade data. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Production Approach The constant price estimates are now based on 2001. The household survey on consumer expenditure (SLIHS) was conducted in 2003/2004, which has provided relevant information to set another base year for the CPI. For consistency checks, the vertical check was used to ensure that the changes in output reflect changes in prices as measured by the percentage change in the GDP deflator. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data Production Approach For consistency checks, the vertical check was used to ensure that the changes in output reflect changes in prices as measured by the percentage change in the GDP deflator.
