 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Data are compiled following the International Recommendations for the Index of Industrial Production 2009.The Index is calculated on the fixed base method using a formula that is derived from the traditional Laspeyres formula. The use of the modified formula allows for calculation of the Index on a monthly basis. Since the weights are fixed for the base period, they are calculated only once. The base period quantity is known and the only variable is the quantity collected in the reporting period. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The following industrial groups are identified in the index following the International Standard Industrial Classification (ISIC Rev. 3): mining and quarrying, electricity, water and gas, food, beverages and tobacco, wearing apparel, wooden furniture, chemicals, petroleum products, non-metallic mineral products, electronic components, other manufacturing. In each group, the establishments selected in the sample represent in general about 70 % of the total value added. The weights attached to each group are those of the base year 1994. Scope of the data The index of industrial production covers the following activities: mining and quarrying, electricity, water and gas, and the manufacturing industry. It measures the change in quantities produced by a representative sample of establishments. 2.4.1 Valuation Not Applicable. 2.4.2 Recording basis Not Applicable. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures Not Applicable. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The source of data for the Index is the monthly Survey of Production and Sales .
