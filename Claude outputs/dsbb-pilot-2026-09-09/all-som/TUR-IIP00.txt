 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The data are compiled largely in accordance with the Sixth Edition of the IMF's Balance of Payments and International Investment Position Manual (BPM6). Analytical framework: The conceptual framework and classification standards for Türkiye's International Investment Position (IIP) are broadly consistent with BPM6. Stock position of the external assets and liabilities are presented under the following data categories: direct investment, portfolio investment, other investment and reserve assets. Definition: Türkiye's international investment position is the statistical statement of the stock of external financial assets and liabilities. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Classification: The classification of Türkiye's IIP is broadly consistent with the classification specified in BPM6. Assets are divided into direct investment, portfolio investment (equity and debt securities), other investment and reserve assets, liabilities are divided the same way except for reserve assets. These are further classified by instrument of investment, sector and original maturity. 2.2.1 Scope Classification: The classification of Türkiye's IIP is broadly consistent with the classification specified in BPM6. Assets are divided into direct investment, portfolio investment (equity and debt securities), other investment and reserve assets, liabilities are divided the same way except for reserve assets. These are further classified by instrument of investment, sector and original maturity. Scope of the data Coverage of external financial assets and liabilities: Türkiye's IIP statistics summarizes the external assets and liabilities of all resident sectors at a point in time. Geographical coverage: The geographic coverage corresponds to the national economic territory. Exceptions to coverage Financial derivative transactions are classified under Portfolio Investment. 2.4.1 Valuation Conversion to unit of account: Stock positions originally denominated in foreign currencies are converted to U.S. dollars using the end-of-period exchange rates. Valuation: The principle is to use the market values. Direct investment liabilities stocks are adjusted to market prices by using stock exchange market value/book value ratio for different sectors to convert the book values to market values if market values are not provided. Book values and if available market values are obtained from survey. Market values are consistent with the market values that are announced by the Borsa Istanbul (BIST) if the reporting direct investment enterprises' equity shares are traded in the BIST. If a reporting direct investment enterprise is not registered to the Borsa Istanbul, the above mentioned calculation method is used. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs General remarks: The primary data sources employed by the CBRT to compile the IIP are: i) stock data derived from the foreign exchange records of the CBRT and the banks; ii) external debt stock data announced by the Ministry of Treasury and Finance; iii) annual/quarterly surveys;iv) reports of the custodians, Central Registry Agency Inc. of Türkiye (CRA),and BIST on portfolio investment; v) Securities Statistics of CBRT; and vi) Bank for International Settlements' (BIS) international banking statistics. Direct investment: Direct investment in the IIP is classified on an asset/liability basis with an instrument breakdown (equity capital and other capital). Data on direct investment in Türkiye are collected through an annual survey of foreign direct investment enterprises conducted by BOP division of CBRT. The data regarding foreign direct investment abroad by residents of Türkiye for the 2008-2014 period are based on the survey conducted by the Ministry of Treasury and Finance. Starting from 2015, the data started to be obtained via a survey conducted by the Ministry of Trade and also by direct reporting. Moreover, the geographical and sectoral breakdown of the investments are also published. The "Other Capital" item which consists of the credits extended to the branches, subsidiaries and affiliates abroad by the parent company in Türkiye, is also acquired through the aforesaid survey. Since the survey is conducted annually, the results of the survey are disseminated in the following year. Until that time, data on direct investment abroad are obtained by adding the balance of payments flow figures to the FDI stock on the basis of previous year's survey figures. Portfolio investment: Data on portfolio investment assets are obtained from a quarterly survey based on the data obtained from end-investors covering banks, intermediary institutions, and resident enterprises. The quarterly surveys on portfolio investment assets include country and sectoral breakdowns. Regarding data on portfolio investment liabilities, the stock of the bond issues abroad by the public banks and the Ministry of Treasury and Finance, are provided by the Ministry of Treasury and Finance while private sectors' bond issues abroad are provided by foreign exchange records of the banks until 2011Q4. Securities Statistics are used for the nonresident holdings of the stock of Treasury's bond issues starting from 2012Q1, and resident banks' and other sector's bond issues starting from 2020Q3 (resident banks' and resident nonfinancial sector's holdings of bonds issued abroad by the Ministry of Treasury and Finance are deducted from the figures starting from 2001 and 2003, respectively). Related data are compiled with respect to the "economic ownership" principle within the scope of compliance to the Securities Statistics Handbook of Bank of International Settlements (BIS), European Central Bank (ECB) and International Monetary Fund (IMF).. Starting from 2005, data on non-residents' 'equity security investments in Türkiye are obtained from the CRA. Other investment: Data on stocks of other investment assets are based on stock data derived from the foreign exchange records of the CBRT and banks, Turkish Statistical Institute's (Turkstat) quarterly survey for trade credits, as well as BIS statistics. Since BIS statistics only cover deposit accounts of non-bank sectorabroad, foreign currencies held by households as a store value are not included in other investment assets. Data on other investment liabilities are based on stock data on public and private medium- and long-term external debt compiled by the Ministry of Treasury and Finance and the CBRT, and stock data derived from the banks' foreign exchange records. Additionally, external funds obtained by repurchase agreements are recorded under this item as loan liability of banks. Reserve assets: Reserve assets are derived from the foreign exchange records of the CBRT. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures No seasonal adjustment to data is made. None
