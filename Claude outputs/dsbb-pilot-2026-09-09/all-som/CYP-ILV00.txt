 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions As mentioned above, the Reserve Template tables are prepared based on the methodology, concepts and definitions of the corresponding International Reserve and Foreign Currency Liquidity Guide of the IMF. In addition, the balance of payments and the international investment position statistics of the CBC are generally consistent with the methodology of the sixth edition of the IMF Balance of Payments and International Investment Position Manual (BPM6). Regulations, agreements, guidelines and recommendations applicable to members of the European Union and Eurozone also impact the overall structure of the Cyprus’s external statistics. Finally, the same concepts that are included in the External Debt Guide (e.g. maturity, residency, accrual principle, etc) are also followed by the CBC for the external debt compilation. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The Reserve Template prepared by the CBC follows the classifications indicated in the International Reserve and Foreign Currency Liquitidy Guide. 2.2.1 Scope The Reserve Template prepared by the CBC follows the classifications indicated in the International Reserve and Foreign Currency Liquitidy Guide. Scope of the data In principle, our external statistics produced by the CBC cover all transactions between residents and nonresidents. Concerning residency definition, the general principles on residency, as laid down in the BPM6 manual, are applied. Special purpose entities (SPEs), with the exception of shipping companies for which a special regime is applied (economic ownership), are considered residents if registered/incorporated in Cyprus. SPEs data do not affect data on Reserve assets 2.4.1 Valuation Stocks: At the beginning the foreign amount is obtained and thereafter the market price is collected. Flows (Interest and capital): The same principle is applied. 2.4.2 Recording basis In the case of data relating to reserves, the accruals principle is fully applied. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The following agents report data to the B.O.P. Section of the CBC: The Payments and Settlement Section of the CBC provides the monthly balance sheet of the CBC, from which data on reserve assets and other required items are obtained. The Ministry of Finance provides data on government debt. Finally, the Market Operation Unit of the CBC provides us with all the necessary material relating to reserves (valuation reports, deposit liabilities, credit lines, position by currency, etc). From all the above data sources, the data obtained includes both capital amounts as well as interest payments. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness The source data for compiling the monthly reserve template is received on time. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results Data received from the Payments and Settlement Section and the Market Operations Unit are cross-checked to spot any differences. Similar checks are performed on the data received from the Ministry of Finance that provides data on government debt. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data Any discrepancies are properly followed up and resolved prior to the finalisation of the reserve template dataset. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs Whenever any of the figures show a significant change or an unusual movement, they are investigated further to find the reasons. This investigation, often involves contacting the reporting section / department concerned for more details. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses Revisions are performed on an ad hoc basis if deemed necessary. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request See our comments on 5.1.1. above. In general, however, non-confidential statistics are available to users upon request, free of charge.
