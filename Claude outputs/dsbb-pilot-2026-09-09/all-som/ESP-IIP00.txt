 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions International Investment Position data are elaborated according to the sixth edition of the Balance of Payments and International Investment Position Manual (MBP6) guidelines. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The classification used to compile Spain's International Investment Position is essentially the one set forth in the BPM6 and fully consistent with the SNA2008 (System of National Accounts). Exceptions are noted below. The sector breakdown, functional categories and instrument classification are fully consistent with the BPM6. Scope of the data The data cover the entire Spanish territory. Resident institutional units are defined in conformity with the sixth edition of the Balance of Payments and International Investment Position Manual's concepts of economic territory and center of economic interest. Exceptions to coverage As regards direct reporting to the Banco de España of the Other Residents Sector, if the amounts of transactions during the immediately preceding year, or balances of assets and liabilities on December 31 of the previous year, are less than €1 million, the form is only to be sent at the express request of the Banco de España within a maximum period of two months from the date of request. Unrecorded activity There is no unrecorded activity. 2.4.1 Valuation Following BPM6 recommendations, the stocks are valued at market prices at the end of the reference period as far as they are available. Loans, deposits and other accounts payable/receivable are valued at nominal value (with the exception of Monetary and Financial Institutions where face value is used). Non-listed equity within Foreign Direct Investment in general is valued at Own Funds at Book Value (OFBV) of the DIE Conversion to unit of account: in general terms, stocks are converted into euros at the average market Exchange rate (the average of the buying and selling rates) of the closing quarterly date. 2.4.2 Recording basis 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The following blocks can be distinguished: 1. Data collection by the Statistics Department of the Banco de España. Reporting of foreign economic transactions and financial asset and liability balances by Spanish residents. Covers individuals and legal persons (public or private) who are resident in Spain, other than providers of payment services entered in the official register of the Bank of Spain, when they carry out transactions with non-residents (acts, business and operations that involve or derive in charges, payments and/or exterior transfers, as well as variations in accounts or financial debtor or creditor positions) or maintain assets or liabilities overseas. Data reported: a) Operations with non-residents, whatever their nature and regardless of how these are settled, i.e. by means of foreign transfers or through payment or debts in bank accounts or inter-company settlements, be it by offsetting or by delivery of cash. b) Balances and changes in assets or liabilities abroad, in whatever form they materialize (accounts in banking or financial entities, accounts between companies, deposits of cash or securities, equity, instruments of debt, financial instruments, derivatives, real estate, etc.). Reporting of transactions and stocks of marketable securities. Information on tradable securities is also collected from resident custodians. For tradable securities held by residents through non-resident custodians, the declaration is made directly by the holder under the system described in the first paragraph or under the integrated declaration of financial statements by credit institutions if the holder pertains to this sector. The ECB Centralized Securities DataBase (CSDB) and Securities Issues Statistics - CSEC are used for compilation purposes. 2. Data provided by other Departments of the Banco de España Positions of the Banco de España The demand of information from credit institutions to cover external statistics has been included in the integrated financial reporting system of declaration to the Banco de España under the Circular that specifies the financial statements reported for statistics and supervision purposes. 3. Data provided by other statistical agencies Tax Authority: data on real estate located in Spain owned by non-residents and form 720 regarding information return on assets and rights held abroad. National Statistics Office: data from the Tourist Expenditure Survey (EGATUR) and related to Housing price indexes. General Council of Notaries: transactions of real estate located in Spain. Ministry of Economy, Trade and Business: Spanish Treasury, Instituto de Crédito Oficial (ICO), Spanish Executive Resolution Authority and Directorate General of Insurance and Pension Funds and Foreign Direct Investment Register (RIE). 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Definitions, scope and classifications used in source data are broadly in line with the BPM6. In some cases, adjustment procedures exist to ensure that source data conform to the coverage, classification, valuation and timing conventions of the BPM6. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness In general source data are timely as regards the compilation of te International Investment Position statistics. According to the established threshold, some direct reporting declarations of Other Resident Sectors are made on annual basis, 2 months after the end of the reference year. Previous stocks are keep constant through quarters until yearly data is available and then the variation is distributed linearly. Data on Foreign Direct Investment equity stocks of the Other Resident Sectors from the Investment Register of the Ministry is available with a delay of 1+1/2 years. Transactions data are used to extrapolate new quarters from the last stock available until data for a new year are received. The benchmark used for assets in deposits of Other Resident Sectors, mirror data from the Bank for International Settlement's statistics “Locational banking statistics by residence”, are available not for the first publication of each quarter, but for the first revision. 3.2.1 Source data assessment For sectors that are supervised and for the General Government and Monetary Authority, exhaustiveness is guaranteed. For other sectors, the declaration of external transactions on behalf of their clients made by the Payment Services Providers is used to check possible non-response in other sources. For Foreign Direct Investment equity, the investment register of the Ministry is almost exhaustive with a very low threshold. In International Investment Position data non-response is checked in every update of information by crossing different sources of information and by assessment of temporal consistency for which checking of consistency between flows and stocks plays an important role. Direct interaction with the respondents assures the subsequent collection of information. Examples of sources used to cross-check the data are: data on Foreign Direct Investment equity transactions from the Investment Register of the Ministry of Economy, Trade and Business; and information from CNMV- National Commission of the Securities Market (agency in charge of supervising and inspecting the Spanish Stock Markets and the activities of all the participants in those markets). To estimate insurance and pension fund technical reserves hold by households, mirror data from insurance corporations and pension funds under statistical ECB reporting regulation are used. For deposits abroad of the Other Residents Sector, mirror data from the Bank for International Settlements' statistics "Locational banking statistics by residence" and Balance Sheet Items (BSI) statistics (ECB statistics based on aggregated or consolidated balance sheet of the monetary financial institutions sector) and Form 720 of the Tax Agency (a declaration of assets held abroad) for the remaining countries are used. Real state stock liabilities are estimated combining different data sources: a) the Tourist Expenditure Survey (EGATUR) (that informs about non-residents coming to Spain and staying in their own dwellings), b) data provided by the tax authority (Land register + non-resident declarations), c) transactions recorded by notaries and d) prices provided by the NSO. Real state stock assets are also estimated combining two sources. On one hand, direct reporting to Banco de España, and, on the other, the acquisitions and sells from the Tax Authority. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Portfolio Investment: Stocks in negotiable securities are assigned to the related institutional sector on the basis of the resident issuer in the case of liabilities, and to the resident sector underwriting or buying the securities in the case of assets. The liabilities of debt instruments in portfolio investment begin to be estimated using the residual approach for all sectors, on a security by security basis since 2020 data. This estimate is obtained as the difference between the total issuance and the portfolios of resident investors. Data from Securities Issues Statistics - CSEC (issuance statistics with information from the ECB's centralized securities database, security by security, and including principal indexation in inflation-indexed issuances) and data on holdings, also security by security, of resident investors are used. Other Investment: For deposits abroad of the Other Residents Sector, the data of the direct reporting system has potential coverage problems and therefore the Bank for International Settlements' statistics "Locational banking statistics by residence", Balance Sheet Items (BSI) statistics (ECB statistics based on aggregated or consolidated balance sheet of the monetary financial institutions sector) and Form 720 of the Tax Agency is used. To estimate insurance and pension fund technical reserves hold by households, mirror data compiled by ECB from insurance corporations and pension funds under statistical reporting regulation is used. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs a) Consistency with: Rest of the World sector in Financial Accounts, Balance Sheet Items statistics (ECB statistics based on aggregated or consolidated balance sheet of the monetary financial institutions sector) and Securities Holdings statistics (ECB Statistics collected on a security-by-security) b) Several bilateral comparison exercises with mirror data of other countries (FDI European Network, Asymmetry Resolution Meeting (ARM)) ESCB and ESS quality reports. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses Revisions are stored and assessed on an ongoing basis. Data are considered definitive in the fourth year after the reference year, according to the revisions calendar specified in 4.3.1. Monitoring of revisions is considered to be a normal part of the compilation process. Studies of the causes, magnitude and direction of revisions over a given period of time are part of the set of quality indicators that the compilers go through in every dissemination cycle. This kind of quality indicators is also included in the ECB's biannual quality report and Eurostat's annual quality report. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses A detailed analysis of differences between revised and preliminary data is only performed for internal use as part of every compilation/pre-dissemination cycle. Studies and analyses of the revision with explanation of the reasons underlying changes and of the impact on the data are disseminated only when major methodological changes occur, or when there is a break due to a new collection system. This is done in the monthly press-release of Balance of Payments or the quarterly Balance of Payments/International Investment Position press-release and/or the news section of External Statistics, the methodological note by a specific statistical note posted at the website under the External Sector section of 'Statistics'. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request The Banco de España data laboratory (BELab) has been created with the aim of providing the research community with greater access to high-quality microdata (anonymized), either onsite or remotely (depending on the sensitivity level of the data), in a controlled environment that ensures data confidentiality. 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points Assistance on statistics compiled and/or disseminated by the Banco de España can be requested by users by means of the telephone (34 91 338 5651) or by a contact form ( https://app.bde.es/gnt_seg/en/contacto?a=NwbElZKVDdF0cMf6ebybP9BztpFPLW91 ).
