 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Concepts and definitions used to compile the IPI meet the Internationally accepted standards. Specifically, the Producer Price Index Manual: Theory and Practice (2004) is followed. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Activities are classified according to the establishment census in 2018. Groups are classified by this census; industrial activities are classified according to the ISIC Rev4 classification 2.2.1 Scope Activities are classified according to the establishment census in 2018. Groups are classified by this census; industrial activities are classified according to the ISIC Rev4 classification Scope of the data The IPI for Jordan is compiled at national level, for the industrial sector only. The IPI estimates are compiled at 4-digit ISIC classification. The current Base year of the IPI is 2018. The weights were derived from combined data 2018 Annual Industrial Survey and the 2018 Establishment Census. · Unrecorded activity Based on 2018 Establishment Census, 87 industrial activities were found practiced in Jordan. Among these activities 61 activities were selected to represent Industrial sector, where activities with relative importance 0.3% or more were selected. The weights of uncovered activities were allocated on the selected activities proportionally. The weights of uncovered activities were allocated on the selected activities proportionally. 2.4.1 Valuation Not applicable because the IPI is a quantity index. 2.4.2 Recording basis Not applicable. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures Not applicable. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The quantities of IPI based items are collected monthly for 785 items from 250 industrial establishments 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording The sample of manufacturing establishments is established and the main product is selected for analysis. 3.2.1 Source data assessment No seasonal adjustment is done on the monthly Industrial Production Index (IPI). 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Not applicable. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures There is no lack in coverage of the monthly IPI, therefore no adjustment need to be performed on the activities. Some statistical treatments are conducted for those establishments, which cannot provide DOS with price data but only with monthly revenue data. Quality change adjustments are conducted for some items. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results The quantities are edited in the field monthly by the IPI compilers. In addition it is compared with other administrative source. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data Intermediate data on major activities, mainly in industry, are assessed against related indicators, such as volume and quantity trends. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs Assessment of potential discrepancies of major intermediate data are done, and adjustments are made to remove the discrepancies. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses The direction and magnitude of revisions are observed, but studies and analyses of revisions are not conducted. Periodic changes in IPI weights are analyzed. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses Periodic changes in IPI weights are analyzed, but these revised and analyzed studies are used for internal purposes only. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request Unpublished but non confidential series or tabulations can be provided upon request. 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points A contact person for IPI is published in a foot notes in the IPI press release. 5.3.2 Availability of documents and services catalogs A list of publications is given in the DOS catalog of publications and on Dos website: ( www.dos.gov.jo ). The cost of the publications is stated, as well as information on how they may be obtained.
