 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions National Accounts data in Vanuatu are based on the 1968 System of National Accounts (SNA) and cover: GDP by Industry at current and constant (1983) prices. GDP by Income and Expenditure Approach at current and constant (1983) prices. GNI and GDI at current prices. Capital Finance Account. External Transactions Account. Household Income and Outlay Account. General Government Income and Outlay Account. Data are compiled by the National Statistics Office (NSO), and all are disseminated in the publication Consolidated Accounts of the Nation . 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Country adoption of the UN International Standard Industrial Classification (ISIC) with some disaggregation of Agriculture, Fishing and Forestry to reflect the relative importance of these activities in the Vanuatu economy. At the most aggregate level, activities are: Agriculture, Forestry & Fishing; Mining & Quarrying; Manufacturing; Electricity & Water; Building & Construction; Trade, Hotels, Cafes, etc.; Transport & Communication; Finance, Insurance, Real Estate & Business Service; Community, Social & Personal Services; Others. Merchandise Trade data are classified by HS and concorded to SITC. Scope of the data Transaction Coverage: All market transactions and subsistence activities are included in principle. Geographic Coverage: The entire territory of Vanuatu. Unit Coverage: All resident entities excluding off-shore enterprises. Unrecorded activity The domestic operations of off-shore enterprises are included by way of normal data capture. No estimates are made for the value of illegal activities. 2.4.1 Valuation Transactions are valued at current market prices. Subsistence production in rural areas is valued at 70% of urban market prices. 2.4.2 Recording basis Transactions are recorded on a cash basis. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Agriculture: Agriculture census, Annual Business Surveys, Household Surveys. Industry: Administrative data, Annual Business Surveys. Services: Administrative data, Annual Business Surveys. (Surveys cover ALL registered businesses). Coordinated approach between appropriate government and private institutions e.g., follow up to reconcile differences between data reported by survey and values reported for taxation purposes. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Business survey results are checked against the value added tax (VAT) aggregates; major discrepancies are resolved by investigation. Constant price estimates are obtained by a combination of quantity extrapolation and price deflation of current price estimates. None
