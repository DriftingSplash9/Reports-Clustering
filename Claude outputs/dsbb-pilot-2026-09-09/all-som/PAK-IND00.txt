 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Quantum Index of Manufacturing (QIM) measures the changes in physical output (Quantity) of fixed set of products for Large Scale Manufacturing Industries. ( Industries having 10 or more employees are regarded as Large Scale Industries).It is calculated according to internationally accepted standards and guidelines using weighted aggregative Laspeyers Formula with base year 2005-06. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Pakistan Standard Industrial Classification PSIC-4 developed on the basis of UN International Standard Industrial Classification ISIC-4 has been used to classify manufacturing activities. Scope of the data The scope of QIM is exclusively to Large-Scale enterprises and covers 112 items. These items contribute 70.3 percent of value added as a weight to the large-scale Industries on the basis of 2005-06 Census of Manufacturing Industries (CMI) results. Exceptions to coverage Unrecognized Large Scale Industries as well as Industries which do not fall under the definition of Large Scale Industries are not included in the computation of QIM. Unrecorded activity Items comprising of 29.7% of value added to the Large Scale Industries remains unrecorded because their data cannot be collected periodically by the sources which provide data for QIM. 2.4.1 Valuation Industrial production collected on monthly basis along with weights computed on the basis of fixed base year price are used to calculate the QIM. 2.4.2 Recording basis QIM is calculated on monthly and annual basis. However, QIM on quarterly basis is kept for record. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures QIM is calculated for YOY (cumulative as well as month wise) and MOM basis. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The sources of data are: the Ministry of Industries and Production, Provincial Bureau of Statistics, Oil Companies Advisory Committee (OCAC) and Pakistan Electronics Manufacturers Associations (PEMA). 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording The sources described in 3.1.1 conduct monthly survey of industrial production and employment to collect production data as per definition of Large Scale Industries on monthly basis following the reference period. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Sources supply data on monthly basis and QIM is disseminated within 6 weeks following the reference month as per GDDS and SDDS standards. 3.2.1 Source data assessment The data is assessed every month by consulting the sources for any kind of irregularity. Overall the assessment of source data is carried out by conducting census of Manufacturing Industries after every 5 years. On the basis of this census weights are calculated (on the basis of contribution to the GDP) which are used in the computation of QIM. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques The sources collect the data on monthly basis according to their prescribed sample design. Pakistan Bureau of Statistics(PBS) (Former FBS) conduct the CMI on regular basis (after every 5 years) using standard statistical methodologies. Any discrepancies and non-responses are estimated according to the frame developed by the Business Register Section of PBS. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures The weights have been derived on the basis of 2005-06 CMI value added at factor cost (gross value of production less industrial cost less direct tax and plus subsidies) including the results of a non-response CMI survey. These weights are used in the compilation of QIM. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results Results are validated for any kind of inconsistent growth by comparing with previous monthly and annual indices. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data The data is assessed every month by consulting the sources for any kind of irregularity. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs The data provided by the sources is verified and any discrepancy found is rectified before issuance of QIM. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request The data is disseminated to private users and researchers on request in the form of hard copies as well as soft copies through e-mail and CDs.
