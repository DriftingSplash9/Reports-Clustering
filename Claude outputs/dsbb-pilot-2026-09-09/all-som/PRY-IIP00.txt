 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The Central Bank of Paraguay (BCP) compiles and disseminates quarterly international investment position statistics expressed in millions of U.S. dollars. These statistics are produced by the External Sector Statistics Department and are compiled largely in accordance with the methodological guidelines set out in the IMF's Balance of Payments and International Investment Position Manual, Sixth Edition (BPM6). In 2020, the BCP completed the migration of international investment position statistics for the period since 2008 to the BPM6 presentation framework. In accordance with Article 82 of the BCP Charter (Law No. 489 of July 3, 1995), the Central Bank is mandated to compile and publish macroeconomic statistics, including international investment position data. To fulfill this responsibility, the BCP obtains information on the main international investment position components directly and, where appropriate, in coordination with various public sector institutions on a regular basis. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization International investment position data are classified in accordance with the standard components set out in the Balance of Payments and International Investment Position Manual, Sixth Edition (BPM6). Recording positions in financial assets and liabilities. Financial assets are classified into direct investment, portfolio investment, financial derivatives, and other investment, while liabilities are classified into direct investment in the reporting economy, portfolio investment, financial derivatives, and other investment. In addition, international investment position statistics include reserve assets. Scope of the data Geographic coverage: The reference economic territory corresponds to the territory of Paraguay. Unit coverage: In principle, international investment position data encompass all positions in assets and liabilities between residents of Paraguay and nonresidents. Residents are defined as the general government and its embassies abroad, individuals and legal entities whose center of predominant economic interest is located in Paraguay, as well as entities engaged in economic activities in the country for a period of one year or more. In 2012, the BCP revised the statistical treatment of binational hydroelectric enterprises, which are now classified as 50 percent residents of Paraguay, whereas they had previously been classified as nonresidents. This methodological change had a significant impact on the national accounts, balance of payments, and international investment position (IIP) statistics. The revision was publicly announced during a press conference held in early 2012 in conjunction with the dissemination of quarterly national accounts data. The transition period concluded in December 2012 with the publication of the preliminary annual economic report. Exceptions to coverage There are limitations in the availability of source data for derivatives, other financial assets and liabilities with nonresidents. Consequently, the coverage of these components in the international investment position statistics is partial. Unrecorded activity 2.4.1 Valuation International investment position data are valued at market prices or, if that is not possible, at their nominal value. 2.4.2 Recording basis All balances are recorded in U.S. dollars. Balances in other currencies are converted into U.S. dollars using the average buying and selling exchange rate of the business day in question. Time of recording Balances are recorded on an accrual basis in accordance with the guidelines of the BPM6, except public debt and reserve asset balances. Public debt does not include interest accrued and not yet due. Reserve assets exclude interest accrued and not yet received. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures Positions in the financial account are recorded on a net basis, separately for each functional category of assets and liabilities. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The reference geographic territory corresponds to the territory of the Republic of Paraguay. In principle, international investment position statistics cover all positions with nonresidents undertaken by the general government and its diplomatic missions abroad, as well as by individuals and legal entities resident in Paraguay. The main data sources include official reports, databases, and publications from the National Directorate of Tax Revenues (DNIT), the Directorate of Public Credit and Public Debt, the Superintendency of Banks and Finance Companies (SBF), the Superintendency of Insurance (SS), nonfinancial public enterprises, binational enterprises (Itaipú and Yacyretá), international organizations, and various departments of the BCP. In addition, the BCP conducts surveys of private sector enterprises. Some difficulties persist in capturing positions related to financial derivatives. International Investment Position statements are compiled and disseminated on a calendar-year basis. While some resident financial and nonfinancial enterprises operate on fiscal years that differ from the calendar year, no adjustments are made to align this information with calendar-year reporting. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Direct investment: Balances are obtained for financial enterprises using information from the Superintendency of Banks and Finance Companies, for insurance companies using information from the Superintendency of Insurance, and for nonfinancial private enterprises using surveys conducted by the BCP and balance sheets. Portfolio investment: The data are based on the balance sheets of enterprises and on information from surveys carried out by the BCP. Other investment: Medium- and long-term external public debt comes from the External Debt Management and Financial Analysis System (DMFAS), which records the debt of the BCP, general government, and other financial and nonfinancial public sector institutions. The public enterprise ANDE provides information on its external debt with private banks, not recorded in DMFAS. The binational hydroelectric companies provide data assets and liabilities according to the official balance sheet. Financial private sector assets and liabilities (loans, currency and deposit, etc.) are obtained from the SBF, and those of the nonfinancial private sector from surveys of the largest enterprises and official BIS information.. Reserve assets: Reserve assets are obtained from the BCP's accounting records. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Calendars for the collection and processing of data are adequate to meet the standards of punctuality and regularity governing the disclosure of statistics on IIP. Compilers have monitoring procedures to ensure timely receipt of the data (for example, calling the person responsible for providing the data). 3.2.1 Source data assessment 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques The BCP makes various adjustments to calculate assets and liabilities with nonresidents, including, in particular: (a) the elimination of atypical values and estimation of information omitted in the surveys of some enterprises; (b) classification adjustments to the information from surveys or databases of other institutions, to make the information consistent; (c) use of the SDR quotation obtained from the IMF instead of the one recorded in the BCP's accounting records; and (d) reconciliation of the information in the balance sheets of enterprises with the data obtained in surveys. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Specific procedures are developed to fit the data obtained from various sources in order to improve coverage, classification and valuation in accordance with the guidelines set out in the appropriate manual (BPM6). 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results The quarterly surveys of foreign investment enterprises are compared with the annual return filed for tax purposes with the Ministry of Economics and Finance. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data Yields of assets and liabilities of the International Investment Position are consistent because they are from the table of results in the balance sheets of Foreign Direct Investment companies, banks, insurance companies and Ministry of Economics and Finance and other institutions. Some returns are estimated by applying rates asset or liability when no accounting data. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses The analysis of the revisions is released when it corresponds to significant changes in the statistical series. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses Major revisions are clearly explained and communicated to the public. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request Users have access only to the balance of payments data that are officially disseminated. No additional disaggregations or unit-level data are provided beyond those published on the Central Bank of Paraguay (BCP) website; however, general methodological clarifications may be provided to assist users in interpreting the published statistics. 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points A designated contact person for external sector statistics is identified for Paraguay on the IMF Dissemination Standards Bulletin Board (DSBB). Methodological inquiries related to balance of payments and other external sector statistics are received and addressed by compilers at the Central Bank of Paraguay (BCP) and should be submitted using the contact information provided on the DSBB Paraguay contacts page: https://dsbb.imf.org/sdds/country/PRY/contacts-list 5.3.2 Availability of documents and services catalogs Information on statistical publications, databases, and methodological documentation produced by the Central Bank of Paraguay (BCP), including those related to balance of payments and other external sector statistics, is available on the BCP website. These materials are organized by statistical area and are accessible free of charge in electronic format.
