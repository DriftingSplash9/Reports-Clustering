 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Definition: Foreign trade statistics generally records the movement of goods between the Slovak Republic and the rest of the world. The total import includes goods entering the Slovak Republic to be added to its material resources and goods imported for processing to be subsequently re-exported in the form of compensating products. The total export includes goods taken from the material resources of the Slovak Republic permanently leaving its territory or goods leaving this territory for the purposes of processing, which are to be re-imported after processing in the form of compensating products. Information on trade in goods with non-EU countries is based mainly on customs declarations. Information on trade in goods with EU Member States as partner countries is obtained particularly from INTRASTAT statistical surveys. The results of these two statistics are however presented to users as a whole in the description of Slovak foreign trade. The following statistical variables are disseminated: value of goods being compiled as FOB-type value: The value includes the transaction value of goods and the values of services (e.g. transport, insurance, reloading, storing of the goods etc.) performed to deliver goods to the border of exporting country. partner country being country of last known destination for export and country of origin for import: Country of last known destination is the last country to which it is known, at the time of the export, that the goods are to be delivered; Country of origin is a country where the goods were obtained, produced or where the goods underwent their last, substantial, economically-justified processing. net mass: the mass of goods without any packaging. quantity in supplementary units: supplementary units measure quantity other than net mass, which is expressed in the measurement units laid down in CN. International or regional guidelines: Foreign trade statistics of the Slovak Republic is compiled in accordance with the EU legislation applicable for this area and with international recommendations of the UN as presented in ‛International Merchandise Trade Statistics: Concepts and definitions 2010‛. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Classification: Goods are classified according to the 8-digit Combined nomenclature (CN), which is based on the Harmonized System (HS). Data are also published according to the Standard International Trade Classification (SITC Rev. 4), by Broad Economic Categories (BEC Rev. 4) and by Classes of classification of products by activity (CPA). Countries are coded according to 2-digit ISO 3166 alphabetic codes and in accordance with EU Geonomenclature of countries and territories for the European statistics on international trade in goods. Scope of the data Transaction coverage: Included are all movable property, including electricity, natural gas, water and processing trade (since January 1998). Geographic coverage:Statistical territory of the Slovak Republic corresponds to its customs territory, i.e. the full geographical territory of the Slovak Republic is covered. Goods placed in the Slovak customs warehouses are included in the imports statistics at the moment of their release into the free circulation area. No transactions with specific countries or territories are excluded from the foreign trade statistics of the Slovak Republic. Since July 1, 2001 foreign trade statistics is compiled according to nomenclature countries and territories (GEONOM) used in EU which describes the statistical territory of the partner country. Exceptions to coverage A comprehensive list of goods excluded for methodological reasons is given in the Appendix of Annex V of the Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2022/1197. The main exclusions are transit operations, goods that are in temporary use, operational leases within 24 months, goods intended for repair and after their repair including substituted parts. Quasi-imports and quasi-exports, i.e. imports and exports when only customs clearance was carried out in the Slovak Republic, are excluded from statistics published in accordance with the national concept (according to this methodology data are recalculated since the year 2006). Within the INTRASTAT statistical surveys, there are not reported transactions of the subjects whose total annual value of intra-EU imports of goods is below 1 million EUR or total annual value of intra-EU exports of goods is below 1 million EUR (so called reporting thresholds). Except economic operators registered in the Slovak statistical business register with the main economic activity according to SK NACE: 01 Crop and animal production, hunting and related service activities, 02 Forestry and logging, 03 Fishing and aquaculture, 10 Manufacture of food products, 11 Manufacture of beverages, and 12 Manufacture of tobacco products, whose total annual value of the intra-EU imports of goods exceeds 200 thousand EUR or total annual value of the intra-EU exports of goods exceeds 400 thousand EUR. In addition, the imputation of the trade of those traders, which do not fulfil their reporting obligation, is made. For subjects, whose total annual value of intra-EU imports or intra-EU exports of goods is below reporting thresholds, estimates are made. The VAT data and mathematical and statistical methods are used to make those estimates and imputation. In addition, the estimates for intra-EU imports for subjects not registered for VAT in the Slovak Republic carried out distance sales are made since 2022. There is new data source for this trade from Financial directorate. Unrecorded activity No estimates for smuggling are made. 2.4.1 Valuation Conversion to unit of account: Since accession of the Slovak Republic into Eurozone on January 1, 2009, the data are published in EUR. In INTRASTAT surveys, the exchange rate that was used for tax purposes (VAT) or the rate according to customs regulations valid in the day of physical movement of goods is used for conversion of foreign currency to EUR. Within the trade with non-EU countries customs administration uses the exchange rate for the second-last Wednesday of a month published by European Central Bank on that or following day for conversion to EUR. The data up to 2008 published in EUR are based on the conversion rate of EUR 1 = SKK 30,126. Valuation: Total exports and total imports are published as a FOB-type value. Total imports are compiled also as CIF-type value. Value of non-invoiced merchandise is value which would have been invoiced in the event of any sale or purchase. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Sources: Source of information for statistics on trade between EU Member States is implemented as statistical surveys at consignors and consignees of goods being taxable persons and non-taxable legal persons registered for VAT in the Slovak Republic. Only the taxable persons and non-taxable legal persons with annual value of intra-EU imports of good exceeding 1 million EUR or total annual value of intra-EU exports of goods exceeding 1 million EUR are obliged to provide statistical data. Except economic operators registered in the Slovak statistical business register with the main economic activity according to SK NACE: 01 Crop and animal production, hunting and related service activities, 02 Forestry and logging, 03 Fishing and aquaculture, 10 Manufacture of food products, 11 Manufacture of beverages, and 12 Manufacture of tobacco products, whose total annual value of the intra-EU imports of goods exceeds 200 thousand EUR or total annual value of the intra-EU exports of goods exceeds 400 thousand EUR. Intra-EU trade data may be recorded in specific cases also from the customs declarations for instance for goods moving between the Slovak Republic and territories, that are parts of EU customs territory but do not apply Directive 2006/112/ES on the common system of VAT. Customs administration collects, checks and pre-processes statistical declarations and provides subsequently data in electronic format to the Statistical Office of the SR. Customs administration creates a database of information on trade of the Slovak Republic with non-EU countries (EXTRASTAT) from documents obtained within the custom proceeding and provides complete data for given month and updated data for previous months to the Statistical Office of the SR. Data where customs declarations are lodged in another EU Member States under Single Authorization for Simplified Procedures (SASP) are obtained directly from the holder of the authorisation or their Slovak representative or partner. This data may also be obtained from the competent national statistical authority in the EU Member States where the custom declaration was lodged. In such a case, the exchanged data will be used to compile statistics instead of the data directly reported by exporter/importer. In addition, data on the quantity of electricity flowing across the Slovak border, measured at interconnection points, are used to compile foreign trade in electricity. These data are provided by energy statistics. Data on quantity of natural gas crossing the Slovak border from transmission system operator and from storage operator are used to compile foreign trade in natural gas. Unit values calculated from INTRASTAT surveys and customs declarations with recorded electricity and natural gas are used to derive statistical value of these goods. Estimates for intra-EU imports for subjects not registered for VAT in the Slovak Republic carried out distance sales are made since 2022. There is new data source for this trade from Financial directorate. Type of data collected: For INTRASTAT surveys: identification of reporting unit, country of origin, region of origin/destination (not mandatory since January 1, 2012), Member State of consignment, Member State of destination, 8-digit CN code of commodity, invoice value, net mass, quantity in supplementary measurement unit, nature of transaction, mode of transport, reference month, VAT ID number of partner in Member State of destination. For extra-EU trade: country of origin/destination, region of origin/destination, identification of importer/exporter, currency, 8-digit CN code of commodity, statistical value, nature of transaction, mode of transport, net mass, quantity in supplementary measurement unit, date of acceptance of customs declaration, date of release of the goods into proposed custom procedure, Member State of destination, Member State of actual export etc. Basis of country data: Country of origin for total imports and country of last known destination for total exports. Since May 1, 2004 total imports statistics can be compiled also according to country of consignment. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Adjustments to improve coverage/timing/valuation: Primary validation and processing is conducted by customs administration for both type of data from INTRASTAT statistical surveys and from customs declarations. Data for given reference month are recorded up till the 25th day of the following month. Further data processing and checking takes place at the Statistical Office of the SR. Within the INTRASTAT surveys, the Statistical Office of the SR makes correction on the basis of information from reporting units. Processing system: Customs data are collected and processed by locally developed system. As regards INTRASTAT, locally developed systems are used for data collection, as well as for data processing within the customs administration and the Statistical Office of the SR. Site of processing: The data are centrally processed by customs administration. Selected data elements from the customs declarations and complete information from INTRASTAT declarations are submitted to the Statistical Office of the SR for further processing and aggregation. Timing of recording transaction: For customs declaration, it is a calendar month when the customs declaration was accepted. For INTRASTAT surveys it is a calendar month in which the chargeable event occurred according to the Act on VAT. In other cases the reference period is the calendar month of physical import or export of goods. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Seasonal adjustments: Seasonally adjusted data are calculated by using application JDEMETRA+. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results Data validation: Completeness and validity checks (mandatory fields checks, code lists checks, data type checks, etc.) are performed during the import of data into the production system of the Statistical Office of the SR for Foreign Trade Statistics. All data must pass through validity checks. Credibility checks (e.g. unit value checks) are then performed by a separate procedure that identifies suspicious data. The next procedure is chosen according to the severity of the errors. For the most serious ones, the importer/exporter is directly contacted with a request to submit a corrective INTRASTAT declaration or a correction of the customs declaration. Less serious shall be corrected by imputation on the basis of information on similar transactions. In addition, the time series of the significant importers/exporters are monitored and suspicious values are verified. Vessels and aircraft recorded on INTRASTAT declarations or customs declarations are verified at the economic operators who has reported them. Reconciliation: Comparisons with the Czech Republic take place regularly. Corrections of non-definitive data incorrectly reported by importers/exporters are made on the basis of these comparisons. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses Revision policy: Routine monthly revisions of the foreign trade statistics data are realised for each previous month of the reference year already published when the monthly data are released. If there are changes in concepts, methodologies, classifications or code lists, the revision of already published data from the beginning of the year is ensured. The revision does not have to be undertaken after an immediate change in some of the above reasons, but only after an analysis of the effects of these changes on already published data. The revision policy of the Statistical Office of the Slovak Republic is published on the Office's website: slovak.statistics.sk > Produkty > Politika a kalendár revízií (Content not available in English). 5.1.5 Dissemination on request More detailed information is available on request, but it does not concern individual information.
