 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Definition: The gross external debt position is the outstanding liabilities side of Greece's overall financial relations with countries abroad. External debt data measure both domestic and foreign currency debt owed to nonresidents. The Bank of Greece compiles the external debt in accordance with the concepts and methodology set out in the External Debt Statistics:Guide for Compilers and Users and the IMF's BPM 6 . 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Greece's external debt liabilities are part of the international investment position, the compilation of which is based on the sixth edition of the BPM6 . The classification is presented according to the External Debt Statistics: Guide for Compilers and Users and follows the ECB's Guideline ECB/2018/19 (amending Guideline ECB/2011/23). External debt liabilities are classified under the sector category of the debtor and not of any guarantor. External debt liabilities are attributed to the sector of the immediate borrower. Short-term/long-term classification is on an original maturity basis. Scope of the data Transaction coverage: Short term debt includes: money market instruments, loans, trade credits, and other debt liabilities. External debt includes securities issued in the domestic markets by residents and purchased by non residents. Financial leases with non residents are treated as loans and included in the gross external debt position. In principle, foreign currency external debt includes foreign currency-linked external debt; that is debt payable in domestic currency with the amounts to be paid linked to a foreign-currency. Geographical coverage: The economic territory of Greece. Sector coverage: external debt data can be classified into four sectors (general government, monetary authorities, Deposit Taking Corporations, and other sectors). External debt liabilities of the Central Bank are separately identified from the liabilities of the general government, and external debt liabilities of households and small private enterprises are included under "other sectors." Unit coverage: Data on arrears and those on private sector debt are included in the gross external debt position under Deposit Taking Corporations and/or other sectors to the extent they are reported by the reporting units concerned. Offshore enterprises, residents in Greece, usually associated with shipping, are treated as far as possible as residents units in application to the relevant BPM6 definition. Exceptions to coverage Transactions coverage: The quarterly external debt does not include the financial liabilities arising from participation rights, i.e. shares and investment funds units related to portfolio investment, and equity capital, reinvested earnings and real estate investment, regarding direct investment. Position coverage: Positions in financial derivatives, equity securities, and equity capital excluded from the gross external debt position. Specific items: Direct investment:As regards the direct investment component of the IIP, only the liabilities part of external loans is included in external debt. Portfolio investment: Equity securities are not included in the gross external debt position. External debt liabilities payable in goods and/or services are notincluded in the external debt position. 2.4.1 Valuation Valuation: All tradable instruments are valued at market value. The stocks are also valued at market prices, thus outstanding liabilities arising from periodic interest costs that have accrued and are not yet payable are included in the gross external debt position. Conversion to unit of account: Positions in foreign currencies are converted into euros by using the ECB's euro/foreign exchange reference rate of the last working day of the reporting period. Loans, currency and deposits, trade credits and other liabilities. The results are valued at the end of new period exchange rates. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Greece's external debt data are mainly based on quarterly stocks statistics of monetary financial institutions and domestic companies and on annual foreign direct investment statistics of non-financial enterprises. The quarterly external debt data are compiled after the compilation of thei nternational investment position, which is estimated using balance ofpayments data, monthly MFI's balance sheet data, and monthly portfolio investment data (security-by-security) reported by custodians. Comments on special items: Direct investment: Estimates based on the accumulation of flows since the last positions surveyed are produced to obtain quarterly statistics and preliminary annual data. Final data are compiled from annual surveys. Portfolio investment: A monthly security-by-security reporting system, based on the ISIN code, provides the information for portfolio investment for general government. Survey data on debt securities for non-financial sectors are basedup on an annual survey run also by the Bank of Greece. Other investment: Data on the monetary authorities are derived from the Bank of Greece balance sheet. Data on general government are collected directly from Treasury records. Data on Deposit Taking Corporations' external liabilities are based on the external positions of the MFI's' balance sheets, as reported to the Bank of Greece. Information on loans and trade credits from non- resident Deposit Taking Corporations is collected from an annual enterprise survey. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Debt securities: Stocks data include accruals (calculated on a security-by-security basis). This mechanism was especially designed for zero-coupon bonds, but it also works for all other kinds of short and long-term securities. Stocks and flows are closely reconciled. Exchange rate and price adjustments are calculated security-by-security, using information from the securities database and CSDB (mainly quotations and exchange rates). Other adjustments (due to reclassifications) between sectors and instruments are also identified automatically in this context. The remaining differences are calculated as residual adjustments. Debt securities stocks are valued at market prices. Loans, deposits and trade credits: Other investment liabilities are based on nominal values. Other investment reports by banks and non-banks are almost exclusively based on stock reports. Separate reporting items for short-term and long-term original maturities exist. Discrepancies between accounting principles andBOP/IIP concepts are seen as being insignificant. 3.2.1 Source data assessment Compilation practices follow relevant instructions of the ECB. An additional check is made using MFI balance sheet statistics. · Debt securities: Survey data on debt securities for non-financial sectors are only available on an annual basis and quarterly data are estimated by accumulation of balance of payments transactions data. · Intercompany lending: Liabilities to affiliated enterprises recorded on an ‘asset/liabilities' basis in the IIP statistics are recorded in the external debt statistics. Data are based on annual sample surveys. Major reclassifications are clearly identified through table footnotes. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Direct investment: The sub-item other capital of the Direct Investment, when owed to non-resident direct investors or affiliates, is included in the gross external debt position. Resident companies, independently of the nature of the FDI relationship – either inward or outward – classify direct investment loans as borrower liabilities raised. Portfolio investment: As regards the Portfolio Investment instruments, which represent liabilities to non-residents, debt securities –bonds and notes, and money market instruments – are included. Other investment: All the components of other investment – trade credits, loans,currency and deposits, and other debt liabilities – which represent liabilities to non residents are included in the gross external debt position. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Loans, currency and deposits, trade credits and other liabilities. For the monetary authorities and monetary financial institutions sectors no statistical adjustments are made. For general government and other sectors end-of-quarter stocks are estimated by accumulation of balance of payments transactions data. Data is not seasonally adjusted. Traded debt instruments issued by residents abroad, but in hands of residents excluded from the gross external debt position to the extent that when resident Deposit Taking Corporations report debt instruments by residents abroad, this is deducted from the aggregates held by residents. The Bank of Greece is not able to identify separately payments on non current and/or contingent debt liabilities excluded from debt service payments. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data Statistical cross checks are made between pure stock statistics used for the compilation of the IIP and BOP flows data from respondent Deposit Taking Corporations and direct declarants such as custodians, resident stock brokers, mutual funds, and investment companies. An additional check is made using MFI balance sheet statistics. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs Whenever any of the figures of external debt show a significant change or an unusual movement, they are investigated further to find the reasons. This investigation, often involves contacting the reporting entity concerned for more details. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses After the first release of the external debt figures, data are adjusted when revised and supplementary statistical data become available.
