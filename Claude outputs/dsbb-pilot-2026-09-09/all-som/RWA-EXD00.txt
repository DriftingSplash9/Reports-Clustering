 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Central government debt takes into account all central Government financial obligations vis-à-vis the other sectors of the economy such as real sector, External Sector, Fiscal Sector and Monetary Sector) which involves the outstanding stock and flows ( principal and interest repayments). The residency criterion as defined by the Balance of Payments Manual (BPM6)); is used to determine whether a debt is an external or Domestic debt. The principal and interest repayments for the loans contracted are incorporated each year into the annual Budget Law. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The External debt is broken down into: External public debt, which includes debts contracted to multilateral, bilateral and commercial creditors as well as government-guaranteed debt. External Private debt, includes debts borrowed abroad by private institutions. According to the external debt classification, all External public debt are currently only for Medium and long-term period. For the private sector debt, trade-related credit is reported separately from long-term loans. 2.2.1 Scope The External debt is broken down into: External public debt, which includes debts contracted to multilateral, bilateral and commercial creditors as well as government-guaranteed debt. External Private debt, includes debts borrowed abroad by private institutions. According to the external debt classification, all External public debt are currently only for Medium and long-term period. For the private sector debt, trade-related credit is reported separately from long-term loans. Scope of the data Transaction Coverage All government operations vis-à-vis its external creditors and the other sectors of the national economy. The external debt is managed using the UNCTAD’s DMFAS software , while Debt Sustainability Analysis (DSA) and Medium Term Debt Management Strategy (MTDS) tools are used for analytical work. The external debt statistics are published in the official MINECOFIN websites. Coverage: Central Government Debt. Unit Coverage: The public debt statistics are produced by the Debt Management Unit in the Ministry of Finance and Economic Planning ( MINECOFIN ). They cover the sovereign debt (i.e., debt contracted directly by the government) and the guaranteed debt (private sector debt guaranteed by the government). 2.4.1 Valuation Stock and flows are reported on face value 2.4.2 Recording basis Public debt data are recorded on a cash basis. The external debt data are recorded in its loan currency, but published statistics are produced in dollars and Rwanda francs. The amount of the Public External debt is converted at the end-of-period exchange rate for stocks and at the average exchange rate for flows (disbursements, debt service). 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The Debt Management Unit is responsible for debt service payment.
