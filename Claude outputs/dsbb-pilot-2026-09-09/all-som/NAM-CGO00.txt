All transactions of the budgetary central government should be included in its reporting, but some foreign-financed development project expenditures (generally for smaller projects) are not included as part of Namibia's development spending. Sources of funding of development expenditure, as shown in the annual development budget - the Three-Year Rolling Development Budget for the Financial Year - are broken down between internal and external funding. According to the Three-Year Rolling Development Budget for the Financial Year, internal funding includes only resources financed by the government. External funding corresponds to financial resources provided by external funding agencies. This source of funding can be either in grant or loan form. External grants are channelled inside or outside the State Revenue Fund, while the external loans are supposed to be channelled through the State Revenue Fund. However, this is not yet fully realized. Therefore, substantial portions of external grants and external loans may be channelled outside the State Revenue Fund and thus would not be included in the accounts of budgetary central government. Coverage: Namibian government accounts, as presented in the MFPE's annual "Budget Statement for the Financial Year" and "Estimate of Revenue and Expenditure for the Financial Year" for the Fiscal Year encompass the central government ministries which, together with other government agencies and offices are presented fewer than 35 votes. These data exclude operations of (a) the extra budgetary accounts and funds and (b) the Social Security Commission. Namibia is a unitary state with devolution of powers and functions implemented under the decentralization policy. State government (Regional Councils) and local governments (municipalities) have their own separate budgets, but they largely depend on budgetary transfers from budgetary central government. Exceptions to coverage The budgetary central government operations data reported only cover budgetary administrative records not including Extra-budgetary units. The monthly and quarterly budgetary central government operation data compilation does not include extra-budgetary units and social security funds, these are only reported as part of the general government operations on an annual basis. Plan to cover extra-budgetary units in future. Unrecorded activity Data are all recorded and if unrecorded activity is detected it will immediately be investigated. 2.4.1 Valuation State Account through treasury authorities provide accounting and reporting in national currency Namibian dollar (N$). Operations denominated in foreign currency are reflected in reports in national currency by recalculating foreign currency amount using Bank of Namibia exchange rate. Foreign currency transactions are valued at the prevailing exchange rate on the day of the transaction at the valued at market. 2.4.2 Recording basis The base of recording Budget execution transactions are recorded on cash basis, the fiscal year is the same as the financial year (April to March). 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures All transactions in assets and liabilities are recorded as net basis, acquisition less disposals. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Monthly data for the central government are compiled from administrative records specifically Treasury (General Ledger), no data estimated. All tax and non-tax revenue is collected, recorded, and compiled by the Department of Revenue Inland now NAMRA, Revenue and Custom and Excise. Any grants for current purposes are recorded and compiled by the State Accounts Department of MFPE. Capital grants for development purposes are recorded and compiled by the National Planning Commission (NPC), based on reporting from each individual ministry. Transactions by all ministries and other agencies of government are recorded in the General Ledger. In recent years, the NPC has established a donor database, which enables validation of ministries reporting on donor-financed development expenditures. As required for SDDS subscriber, the advance release calendar indicates the SDDS data category, coverage, periodicity and timelines of posting data on the website. 3.2.1 Source data assessment The statistical data collection system is not fully automated and does not provide control over the data at each stage of its preparation. Data are collected from administrative records such as general ledger, debt department, from financial statements for extra-budgetary units and compilation follows the guidelines of GFSM 2014. All the central government operations data are subject to an annual audit by the Office of the Auditor-General of the Republic of Namibia. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Transactions by all ministries and other agencies of government, as recorded in the General Ledger, are adjusted monthly against other information from BON accounts and other MFPE sources. No consolidation among government units is carried out in the reporting of any transactions at the level of central government Recorded cash is the basis of Central government operations data and the data compilation (monthly, quarter and annual) are compliant with GFSM 2014 methodology. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Created a bridge table(s) linking between National Chart of Account and GFSM 2014 classifications for monthly budgetary central government sources data. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results Validation is applied by checking in the compilation files, comparison with previous month's data and data for the same month the previous year. Data are assessed on monthly/quarterly and annual, if detect discrepancy adjustment and correction is done in the following month or quarter. Reported annual data are final, if encounter discrepancy is investigated and corrected immediately before the data released. Monthly, quarterly data are balanced against the annual audited reports. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data All statistics data (revenue, expenditure and financing) are assessed to ensure internal consistency between data sources and compiler, if an error or discrepancies detected will be corrected in the following month or quarter. Reported annual data are final and discrepancy is investigated and corrected immediately before the data released. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs In the reporting and compilation of annual fiscal data, information should be provided in a full and complete manner from all reporting units. If missing data arise, the needed information may be acquired through inquiries to the relevant sources of the information, or perhaps even through the estimation of missing values from historical data. If detected statistical discrepancy efforts are being applied and discrepancy investigated from above and below the line to ensure that data are accurate and consistency. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses Revisions are done monthly and quarterly. Audited financial statements data are used to revise preliminary data on an annual basis. Unallocated data are reclassified to the correct categories, if need be, monthly or quarterly. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses Disseminated times series data and metadata on the website of the Fiscal statistics data for Ministry of Finance and Public Enterprises Namibia; Revisions are prepared monthly, quarterly and will be revised annually and adjusted where necessary. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request Data can be obtained on official request through the MFPE contact person.
