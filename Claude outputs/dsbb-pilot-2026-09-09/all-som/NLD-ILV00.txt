 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions DNB publishes the international reserves statistics and the Reserves Data Template according to the sixth edition of the Balance of Payments and International Investment Position Manual ( BPM6 ) and the ECB recommendation on Eurosystem reserve assets (see methodological note http://www.ecb.int ). According to these guidelines the reserve assets must: (i) be under the effective control of DNB and (ii) refer to highly liquid, marketable and creditworthy non-euro area currency-denominated claims on non-residents of the euro area as well as gold, special drawing rights and the reserve positions in the IMF of The Netherlands. Deposits with banks located in The Netherlands and/or in other euro area Member States are therefore not considered as part of the official reserves. Reserve assets are compiled on a gross basis without any netting of reserve-related liabilities (with the exception of those reserve assets included in the sub-item of financial derivatives, which are recorded on a net basis). All reversible gold transactions are treated as collateralized loans and recorded accordingly in the national reserves statistics. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Instrument classification: monetary gold, special drawing rights (SDRs), reserve position in the IMF, and other reserve assets. In line with the BPM6 methodological requirements and the ECB recommendation on Eurosystem reserve assets (see methodological note http://www.ecb.int/ ). All reversible gold transactions are treated as collateralized loans and recorded accordingly in the national reserves statistics. Scope of the data The coverage of the official reserves and the Reserves Data Template is consistent with the IMF Data template on International Reserves and Foreign Currency Liquidity , as published by the IMF in 2013. Institutional coverage: DNB. Deposits with banks located in The Netherlands and/or in other euro area Member States are not considered as part of the official reserves. Instrument coverage: monetary gold, special drawing rights (SDRs), reserve position in the IMF, and other reserve assets. In the Reserves Data Template, “monetary gold” corresponds to “gold (including gold deposits and, if appropriate, gold swapped),” and “other reserve assets” corresponds to the two items, “foreign currency reserves (in convertible foreign currencies)” and “other reserve assets.” “Other reserve assets” in the Reserves Data Template has subcategories for financial derivatives, loans to nonbank nonresidents, and other. Data are disseminated in millions of Euros. 2.4.1 Valuation Valuation and conversion rates: The valuation is based on market prices by using: (i) the prevailing market prices at the time when the transaction takes place for transactions; and (ii) the closing mid-market prices at the end of the appropriate period for holdings. The conversion of foreign currency-denominated assets into euro is based on: (i) the monthly average foreign exchange rate for flows; and (ii) as regards stocks, the mid-point foreign exchange rate on the last day of the reference period. Gold is revalued at market rate at the end of each month. 2.4.2 Recording basis Frequency of calculation and revaluation: Reserve assets are calculated on a monthly basis. The figures are revalued on a monthly basis to meet the b.o.p and i.i.p. standards and to take into account the accruals principle. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures Reserve assets are compiled on a gross basis without any netting of reserve-related liabilities (with the exception of those reserve assets included in the sub-item of financial derivatives, which are recorded on a net basis). 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Finance department: FAV: Finance (Controller's Department). There are no statistical adjustments. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results Data are subject to an internal audit as well as an external audit. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request Detailed data can be disseminated on request. Before dissemination it is checked whether the confidentiality of the data is secured. These requests can be made known by sending an e-mail to the e-mail address that is stated on the statistics part of the DNB website. Questions about the statistics can be asked in the same way. 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points The DNB website ( Statistics | De Nederlandsche Bank ) provides contact information. Questions are usually answered within a few days. Assistance to users is monitored and reviewed regularly. When questions appear to be asked more often, they will be added to the FAQ section on the website. 5.3.2 Availability of documents and services catalogs All statistical news releases and articles ( DNBulletin ) are published on the DNB website ( Statistics | De Nederlandsche Bank ). All statistical products and services are provided free of charge.
