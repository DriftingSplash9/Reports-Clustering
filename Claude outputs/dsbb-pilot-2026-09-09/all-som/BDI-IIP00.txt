 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Burundi's International Investment Position (IIP) is generally compiled in accordance with the concepts, definitions and methodology recommended and published in the IMF’s sixth edition of Balance of Payments and International Investment Position Manual (BPM6). The Burundi’s IIP is a statistical statement that shows at a point in time the value and composition of Burundi’s financial assets and liabilities vis-à-vis the rest of the world. Burundi’s IIP reflects stock of financial assets and liabilities vis-à-vis the rest of the world. For any given period, there is the opening position at the beginning of period and the closing position at the end of the period. Burundi’s IIP is compiled by the Balance of Payments Division of the BRB. The difference between assets and liabilities position at a given period determines the net financial position of Burundi vis-à-vis the rest of the world. The IIP has the same structure as the financial account of BOP, including the same functional categories and the same instruments. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The current IIP statistical framework is disaggregated into five main components namely direct investment, portfolio investment, financial derivatives, other investment as well as reserves. 2.2.1 Scope The current IIP statistical framework is disaggregated into five main components namely direct investment, portfolio investment, financial derivatives, other investment as well as reserves. Scope of the data In principal Burundi’s IIP include all assets and liabilities of sectors for which informations are available. The stock data covered in the IIP statistics are those of Burundi residents with the rest of the world and vice versa. IIP Cover all the components of financial account of the balance of payments which include direct investment, portfolio investment and other investment. Some individual transactions for investment purposes are also covered. Exceptions to coverage Only the major units of the reporting economy with a high level of turnover are subject of surveyed (FDI survey) and that once in two years. 2.4.2 Recording basis The stock data on international investment position are mainly collected in Burundi francs. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures Financial account transactions are recorded net 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Data on the IIP account components are obtained from different sources depending to the components of the financial account. Direct investment Data on inflows and outflows are mainly obtained from FDI survey. However, because of lack in financial resources, a survey is done every two years to update the data on the direct investment. OPortfolio investment Data on inflows and outflows are obtained with FDI surveys. Other investment Transactions in government assets and liabilities are obtained from data provided by the Ministry of Finance, supplemented by information obtained from the Treasury. Data on foreign asset and liabilities of the BRB and commercial banks are obtained from the Monetary and Financial Statistics division of BRB (1SR & 2SR). It includes also the trade credit on import and exports estimated base on the total import value and the amount paid for imports or the total value of export and the value of money paid for exports. However, the data could be adjusted by the information on trade credit from FDI survey. Data on other components of other investments could be collected through FDI survey. Reserve assets Information on the reserve assets is provided by the MFS department, but data on the SDR holding and reserve tranche position to IMF could be also collected from the IMF website. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Data collection instruments such as questionnaires and templates used in data collection are in line with standard BPM6 definitions, conventions and standards, scope and economic sectorization. Currently, the collection of FDI survey data use MEFMI questionnaires. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness The timeliness of source data is not adequate and depends on the data source. Data from MFS and ITRS are monthly available while data on FDI survey is conducted once in two years. 3.2.1 Source data assessment The assessment is frequently done by the technical team (Central Bank and NSO staff) with regard to censuses, sample surveys and templates used to collect FDI and discussions on methodology and financial resources. Reconciliation on public sector debt is always undertaken between the BRB records and records of Ministry of Finance. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques The compilation of data is done in line with BPM6. IIP data cover all foreign assets and liabilities with non-residents. When data are available, the processing and compilation of IIP take account the BMP6 requirements. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results A reconciliation exercise between transactions recorded in the BOP and stocks recorded in the IIP is done by compilers for all components to ensure the consistence. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data Discrepancies assessment is always done to check the consistency between other macroeconomic data. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses There is no policy of systematic revision but when any change is made, we proceed by updating data. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses Not done 5.1.5 Dissemination on request Aggregate data can be disseminated on the individualized request
