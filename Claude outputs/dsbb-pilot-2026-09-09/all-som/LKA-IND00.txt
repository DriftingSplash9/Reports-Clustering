 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Factory Industry-Production Index (hitherto called the Industrial Production Index): Factory Industry-Production Index (FIPI), is a Laspeyres index (2010 = 100), which measures the output of the factory industry sector, a subsector within the manufacturing sector. Classification of sectors within the FIPI is based on the United Nations’ International Standard Industrial Classification of All Economic Activities (ISIC), Revision 4. Data on the FIPI and the sub-indices computed for 17 major industry categories are disseminated monthly. Further details pertaining to the sub-sectors within the FIPI are published annually. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Classification of sectors within the FIPI is based on the ISIC, revision 4. Currently, the FIPI comprises 17 divisions, as given below (the relevant weights are given within parentheses). With the base year of 1997 based on ISIC revision 2, it measured the output of factory industries in terms of nine major industry categories. Presently, FIPI, revised based on the ISIC, revision 4, with 17 major industry categories is compiled based on a survey of 175 factories in the corporate sector. Production quantities, units of measurement, and unit prices are collected in the survey.
