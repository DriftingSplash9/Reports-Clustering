 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The overall structure of concepts and definitions follows internationally accepted standards. The framework for external debt statistics follows the External debt statistics: Guide for compilers and users, IMF, 2013 and the Balance of Payments and International Investment Position Manual (BPM6), IMF, 2009. The results are produced according to the standard presentation (in US Dollars, Euros and Macedonian Denars). The concepts and definitions used are in broad conformity with those used in IMF'. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The sectorization follows the External debt statistics: Guide for compilers and users . Positions are classified according to the standard components of gross external debt. An important improvement introduced in the time series, for the period since 2004, was that claims from and liabilities to nonresidents for loans and trade credit between affiliated enterprises have been separately identified and properly classified under direct investment; intercompany lending (except if both parties are financial intermediaries), instead of under the adequate debt creating instrument, as previously. This also was a part of the recommendations of the ROSC mission that was successfully implemented. 2.2.1 Scope The sectorization follows the External debt statistics: Guide for compilers and users . Positions are classified according to the standard components of gross external debt. An important improvement introduced in the time series, for the period since 2004, was that claims from and liabilities to nonresidents for loans and trade credit between affiliated enterprises have been separately identified and properly classified under direct investment; intercompany lending (except if both parties are financial intermediaries), instead of under the adequate debt creating instrument, as previously. This also was a part of the recommendations of the ROSC mission that was successfully implemented. Scope of the data The scope is broadly consistent with internationally accepted standards, covering all debt creating instruments between residents and nonresidents. An important improvement of the scope of data has been achieved by including short-term trade credits, securities, currency and deposits since 2004. 2.4.1 Valuation For non-traded debt instruments the stocks reflect nominal value at the end period, while for traded debt instruments (such as debt securities) market prices are used to value stocks. Valuation principles follow External debt statistics: Guide for compilers and users methodology. The conversions at the individual debt instruments have been made using the average end-period exchange rate. A major part of the debt instruments are denominated in Euros, so the euro is a more relevant currency for assessing international performance. 2.4.2 Recording basis Under External debt statistics: Guide for compilers and users methodology the basic rule for time of recording is the principle of ownership i.e. claims and liabilities arise at the data of change of economic ownership. External debt statistics in North Macedonia is in full compliance with this principle. For interest on debt, since 2010, data for the loan component are on an accrual basis. Data are based on the NBRNM survey of external debt. Interest on debt securities is based on accrual basis since 2008. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures The grossing and netting procedures are broadly consistent with international guidelines. Data are published in gross terms (gross external debt and claims) and net terms (net external debt). 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Source data are obtained from comprehensive data collection programs that are based upon North Macedonia's specific conditions: reporting system on external debt (ED); reporting system on external claims (EC); The data base of the Central Securities Depositary (for debt securities issued domestically, held by nonresidents) while for calculating the market value of securities, market prices from the Macedonian Stock Exchange are used; reports of the NBRNM on the official foreign reserves of the Republic of North Macedonia; reports of the banks on assets and liabilities - monthly balance sheet of the commercial banks; the monthly reports on stocks and flows (changes and stocks) on the accounts held abroad of the residents which are not authorized banks and the stock and flow on settlement accounts (monthly reports-MR); quarterly reports on the claims and liabilities on the basis of international commercial operations of residents (KIPO Form); quarterly reports on the overtaking liabilities and buying claims on non-residents (POZ Form) annual direct investment survey - inward (VS 22) and outward (VS 11), which resident legal entities submit to the NBRNM on annual basis, and are related to the investments to and from other countries. The annual survey for direct investments abroad (VS-11) is on a census basis, while the survey for direct investments in the reporting country, starting from reporting period 2017, was transformed from census to sample survey. reports for stocks and flows data on investments in debt securities (VHV-2). The reporting system on external debt (ED) -the credit records statistics is conveyed by the NBRNM and is based on a loan-by-loan data base containing various elements. Various data can be obtained from this data base on the stock of external debt, disbursement, repayments, arrears etc.; as well as for the reporting system on external claims (EC). For intercompany lending data are used from the reporting system on external debt (ED) and the reporting system on external claims (EC), that contain information on relations between creditors and debtors with regard to ownership; for intercompany lending in the form of trade credits data are used from the quarterly reports on the claims and liabilities on the basis of international commercial operations of domestic companies. In order to increase the scope and data quality of the portfolio investments on the assets side, a new survey was introduced in January 2015 capturing the investments made by residents in debt securities issued abroad. Reports are provided by the residents authorized for participation in foreign capital markets (Reports for stocks and flows data on investments in debt securities VHV-2). 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording The source data used for the external debt statistics generally adopt definitions; scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording that are close to External debt statistics: Guide for compilers and users standards. The surveys are set up to provide data that follow External debt statistics: Guide for compilers and users concepts on definitions, scope, and classifications. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Source data are timely. Data on debt transactions/positions from the reporting systems on external debt (ED) and claims (EC) are provided in sufficient time for external debt statistics purposes. All other data sources are adequate, with regards to timeliness, except for the annual FDI surveys for which estimations are derived in order to produce quarterly data. However, the experience with some quarterly and monthly surveys indicates that slow response of enterprises could be a potential problem to be monitored. 3.2.1 Source data assessment Source data—including censuses and sample surveys are routinely assessed, e.g., for coverage, sample error, response error, and non-sampling error; the results of the assessments are monitored and made available to guide statistical processes. All surveys use enterprise identification numbers that allow related data to be cross-checked. There is extensive cross-checking of data among various data sources. Debt transactions from the reporting systems are also identified and checked with matching ITRS transactions. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Data compilation employs sound statistical techniques to deal with data sources. The check procedures include checks to avoid miscoding and misreporting. Data sources are checked with any available related indicators, for example, debt transactions in the ITRS are checked against the debt survey. Data are checked for temporal consistency. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures A potential problem with the use of surveys for external debt purposes could come from incomplete response in some of the surveys, in initial data (due to reporting delay) or in all cases (due to nonresponse). 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results Intermediate data are validated using other sources whenever possible. Newspapers and information agencies are monitored for information about large international transactions, and it is confirmed that the corresponding flows are covered in the balance of payments and External debt respectively. Survey data are checked against ITRS records and fully reconciled on a monthly basis. Output data are examined for plausibility. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data Statistical discrepancies in intermediate data are assessed and investigated. Flow data are reconciled with the relevant stock data (e.g., banks' transactions with opening and closing positions, debt transactions and levels, direct investment transactions and levels). 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs Statistical discrepancies and other potential indicators of problems in statistical outputs are investigated. For the loans item, the statistical reporting systems are based on a perpetual inventory model, thus following changes in stock due to flows, exchange rate or other changes, reclassifications etc. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses The NBRNM studies and analyzes the revisions as part of its data quality control process. Studies and analyses of revisions are used internally. Revisions of individual records are carefully monitored. They are documented as to the cause, identified by the staff member making the amendment. It is checked that the revisions flow through to aggregate results. The aggregates are also compared with data for the same month as reported in the previous month's publication. Changes are checked, to ensure that they can be explained. Significant revisions are noted in the e-mail to users who request the monthly tables. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request Statistics not regularly published are made available to interested users upon their request and approval by the Governor's Office.
