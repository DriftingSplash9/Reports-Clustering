 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The data are compiled in accordance with the methodology set out in the sixth edition of the IMF's Balance of Payments Manual. https://www.bcr.gob.sv/bcrsite/uploaded/content/cuadro/1781270677.pdf 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization All transactions and institutional sectors in general, correspond to those prescribed by the Sixth Balance of Payments and IIP Manual (BPM6) Scope of the data Data are disseminated in U.S. dollars on the balance of payments which cover the entire economy of El Salvador. 2.4.1 Valuation The transactions of goods is valued at FOB prices, most of transactions registered at market prices. 2.4.2 Recording basis Partial accrual basis accounting 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures According to the provisions under the Sixth Balance of Payments and IIP Manual, transactions in current and capital accounts are registered in gross values; transactions of the financial account are registered in net values. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Constant reviews are carried out to expand the coverage of information whenever necessary. A significant part of the data is based on results from the surveys from SIBEX (most of the services, current and capital transfers, assets from the other sectors and at a lesser degree revenues and liabilities of the other sectors) and on the international transactions system (family remittances, construction and part of government services). Quaterly surveys are also carried out to determine the average expenditure of travelers. It has made for Tourism Ministry. International trade of goods statistics come out of the records in the customs officers, supplemented with information from other sources on goods purchased at the port. A biannual survey is conducted to determine the percentage of family remittances entering the country in cash. Financial account flows are recorded excluding prices and exchange rates revaluation in most cases. The Department of National Account of Central Bank, based on data provided by the Ministry of Finances, provides information on the government's external transactions and on the external debt from other sectors. It also provides monetary and financial statistics (transactions with non-residents) to record bank transactions from Development Bank of El Salvador (BANDESAL) and other banks. Statistics of Bank for International Settlements is used for deposits abroad The rest of the assets are collected from surveys, in addition data from the Central Securities Depository are used. Assets and liabilities of central bank and banks are based on financial statements Private sector loans are based and administrative records of Central Bank , it receives that information in order to provide authorizations (related to tax exemptions) to international banks that give loans to residents. Portfolio investment assest como from administrative records and reports from banks and securities custodians. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording In general, there is a good degree of compliance with the definitions, scope, classification and time of recording prescribed by the Sixth edition of the Balance of Payments and IIP Manual (BPM6) . 3.1.3 Source data timeliness The SIBEX survey system, the International Transactions System (SITI), and data delivery agreements coincide with the dates for submitting statistics. 3.2.1 Source data assessment The data source is reliable. The surveys and other records include the most important companies or institutions 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Transactions for high amounts reported in the surveys or in the administrative records are confirmed by informants Forms for income/expenses for external operations are requested for those operations equal to or exceeding $5000, in the case of the international transactions system, used as the source for some balance of payments variables. Coherence of the data source with the balance of payments is controlled, mainly in that of reference periods and balance of payments ratings. When there is not report from the respondents data imputation is made based on the recent behavior of the transactions of the company or institution. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Data are adjusted during two years following publication date of said statistics. In the current year, the previous quarters of that year are adjusted, Adjustments are made considering the information that is not reported timely, corrections to the information registered or improvements in data coverage. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results Information on balance international transactions (ITRS) is used in some cases to validate data. Reports from the Ministry of Economy and INVEST on direct foreign-owned investment are also used. Foreign trade data per country are compared with those from the main business partner (United States) or other partners. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data The series of data are compared with other related variables in some cases. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs Errors and omissions are examined quarterly; in the event the results are too high, those facts occurred during the quarter, which therefore may have affected the results of errors and omissions, are evaluated. Every now and then, comparisons of bilateral trade statistics with the United States and other business partners, and with the BIS external debt statistics are made. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses In recent years, studies have been considered in service accounts, FDI, personal remittances, portfolio investment and financial derivatives. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses Bank’s publications disclose users the reasons giving way to review the balance of payments statistics, although the studies explain in detail such changes dare not available to the public. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request All information requested and unpublished, is provided as long as the information is not confidential. 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points Requests for information are received through the Information Officer's mailbox and/or directly from those responsible for generating the statistics. 5.3.2 Availability of documents and services catalogs There is no catalog of publications, documents and other services. All BCR publications are free.
