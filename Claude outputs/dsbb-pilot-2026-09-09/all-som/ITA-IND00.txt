 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Relationship with international guidelines: The main concepts are in accordance with the recommendations of the UN draft on International Recommendations on the Index of Industrial Production (updating of the UN Index numbers of industrial production -1950) and Eurostat Methodology of industrial short-term indicators, rules and recommendations, in particular Regulation of the EU Council 1165/1998, 1158/2005 and 656/2007 (amending Regulation (EC) No 586/2001). The legal basis for the STS indicators are the Regulation (EU) 2019/2152 of the European Parliament and of the Council on European business statistics, repealing 10 legal acts in the field of business statistics (EBS-Regulation) and the Commission Implementing Regulation 2020/1197 laying down technical specifications and arrangements pursuant to Regulation (EU) 2019/2152 (General Implementing Act). The Regulation relating to short-term statistics establishes as regards the definitions of variables, the list of variables and the frequency of data compilation), as regards definitions of variables, list of variables and frequency of data compilation) Concept: Measure of the evolution of the physical volume of industrial production as a proxy of the monthly value added. Definition: The index of industrial production measures variations in time of the physical volumes of goods produced by the industrial sector. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The data are classified according to ATECO 2007, Italian version of the European NACE Rev. 2, and ISIC Rev. 4. 2.2.1 Scope The data are classified according to ATECO 2007, Italian version of the European NACE Rev. 2, and ISIC Rev. 4. Scope of the data The production index covers the mining, manufacturing, and energy sectors, excluding construction. The area covered by the production index is the whole national territory. Industrial coverage: Enterprises with more than 20 persons employed (in some branches lower thresholds apply) classified in sections B, C and D of the NACE Rev. 2 (Isic rev.4). For the manufacturing (section C), the coverage is about 100 percent at the two-digit and three-digit level and more than 97 percent at the four-digit level of industrial value added. Product coverage: Industrial goods classified in sections B, C and D of NACE Rev. 2. The 584 elementary products have a coverage of at least 70% in terms of value of production in each class (four-digit level). Unrecorded activity: No coverage of unrecorded activities Exceptions to coverage The information on production is not required for Division 36 and Groups 35.3 and 38.3 of NACE Rev. 2 (see REGULATION (EC) No 1983/2006 OF THE EUROPEAN PARLIAMENT AND OF THE COUNCIL of 20 December 2006. Moreover, divisions 05, 07 and 09 are excluded. Unrecorded activity No coverage of unrecorded activities 2.4.2 Recording basis Products which needs more than a month for the production are recorded in value or in worked hours only for the part accrued to the reference period 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures Production in value are deflated by total output prices. Hours worked are adjusted with productivity coefficients based on short term statistics data. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Basic information is collected through a sample survey on industrial enterprises employing, generally, at least 20 persons. The enterprises surveyed are taken from ASIA Business Register (Archivio Statistico delle Imprese Attive). Sources of weights: The weighting structure of the industrial production indices is desgined using various sources. Weights of all aggregates, from economic activity classes (4-digit Ateco 2007) up to the total industry are calculated on the basis of the gross value added of factor costs measured by the SBS Frame statistical register and by the structural economic surveys that contributes to the "Enterprises economic indicators". For the definition of the weighting structure of the 2024 base, the most recent data available relate to the year 2022. The source for calculating weighting coefficients for aggregating quantities of products to elementary indices is annual ProdCom survey on industrial production, currently referring to year 2023. Products selected for the link period 2024 were recoded [1] according to the ProdCom list corresponding to the Nace Rev. 2 classification and relative weights are derived from the total production value (net of any reuse). [1] A correspondence table is defined annually, which links each single product detected by the monthly survey to one or more codes of the ProdCom list. Attribution of weights for each level of aggregation is based on the assumption that, at each level, the products and the economic activities surveyed are representative of those not involved in the survey, so that the total weight of the higher level is distributed among products. The use of annual chain-linking implies the annual update of weights, therefore the latest releases of ProdCom and SBS Frame will be used for this purpose. The timeliness of the release of SBS causes a misalignment when compared to the industrial production index, consequently to guarantee the best representativity of the weighting structure, weights are also updated for the previous year. Based on the current schedule of data release, the weights will be updated as follows: • for the provisional/revised indices of year t , ProdCom referring to year t-2 and SBS Frame referring to year t-3 will be used; • for the annual revised indices of year t , ProdCom t-1 and SBS Frame at t-2 will be used. The wheights update requires that for each "year inde x " two weighting structures will be calculated on two different ProdCom/SBS Frame datasets, one for the provisional and revised indices and one for the annually revised indices. Together with the dissemination of the indices based on the 2024 calculation, the new weights are made available. Item selection: The selected items are those ones with the largest coverage in terms of output value until at least 70% of output in the class is reached. There are almost 940 products (including 131 car models). Unit selection: Non-random draw. For each product, enterprises were ranked according to the value of output they represent for this product. Subsequently, the highest ranked units were drawn until 70% of the value of total output of the item was reached. Sample sizes: Information is collected on more of 9,200 basic observations directly from more than 5,500 KAU each month. For specific sectors (mining, slaughtered livestock, production and distribution of electric energy and gas), data are collected from other sources. Almost 940 elementary products are surveyed, which are aggregated into 584 groups of products. Data collection methods: Self-administered data collection via electronic transmission, data are collected through a web portal called Portale Imprese https://imprese.istat.it/ 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Timing of production observations: Basic data are collected on a monthly basis. Nature of indicators used: With the update of the weighting structure, the incidence of the proxies used to measure the trend of industry output have changed compared to the previous base of 2022. As can be imagined, the share –expressed in terms of the relative weight on the general index – of products recorded in value to the detriment of the other two measures. As expected, the share - expressed in terms of the relative weight on the general index - of products recorded in value is growing to the detriment of the other two measures. The incidence of products recorded in quantity remains predominant, although it is decreasing compared to the previous base (from 73.6% to 70.5%), followed by products recorded in production value (18.9%), whose share, however, is increasing by almost 5 percentage points compared to the 2021 base. Finally, the incidence of products in hours worked is also decreasing (10.6%). 3.2.1 Source data assessment The procedures identify outliers and other atypical differences in periodic responses by individual survey units. Extremes values are confirmed with respondents and records maintained on the confirmation. Source data are analyzed to correct for underreporting/misreporting, in particular to check for temporal consistency. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Introducing new units and products: the updating of the panel of New units (enterprises) carried out with the chaining of the indices made it possible to follow the company demographics more effectively, also introducing the possibility of a more frequent rotation of the companies involved in the survey. With the adoption of the 2024 calculation base, the product basket was also revised. Specifically, 83 products were eliminated, 65 of which were due to the integration of the survey data with those from the industrial turnover survey. At the same time, in order to increase coverage in the Ateco classes followed by the survey, 18 new products that had become significant over time were introduced. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Period of current index weights: Calendar year 2021 as the reference year. Frequency of weight updates: annually for the year set as the weighting reference period Reference period: 2021=100. Every five years as the reference year. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results Verification of processing: Different processing procedures are followed which should generate identical results. Consistency with other indicators of industrial production: Comparisons are made with indices of turnover and new orders. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data Verification of data: Check on outliers, additive outliers, temporary changes, level shift are manually fixed at the beginning of the year when seasonal adjustment models are revised. Telephone follow-up on detected outliers. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs Appropriate adjustments are made to remove discrepancies. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses Studies of revisions are carried out in any revision occasion (e.g. availability of new information, change in classification, change of the base year). We investigate the sources of major revision and maintain internal documentation on it, used to refine data collection. Also studies of long-term trends in the revision pattern are conducted periodically to identify potential bias in the revision process. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses Analysis of revision for IPI are monthly disseminated with press release ( http://www.istat.it/it/archivio/produzione+industriale ).
