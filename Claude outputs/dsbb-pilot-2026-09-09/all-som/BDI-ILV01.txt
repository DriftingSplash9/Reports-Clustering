 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Monthly statistics on the gross international official reserves of Burundi are published in millions of Burundi francs in the Bulletin mensuel (Monthly Survey) of the Bank of the Republic of Burundi (BRB) in a table entitled "International Reserves." The international official reserves consist of the foreign assets and stocks of monetary gold to which the BRB has free access. This definition of the international official reserves is consistent with international practice, including that recommended in the 6th edition of the Balance of Payments Manual ( BPM 6 ). In addition to the official reserves, the statistics on the international reserves include the foreign liabilities of the BRB. This facilitates calculation of the net official reserves and the foreign assets and liabilities of the deposit banks, which in turn facilitates calculation of the gross and net international reserves. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The definition of international official reserves comprises the above items held by the monetary authority, represented solely by the BRB. 2.2.1 Scope The definition of international official reserves comprises the above items held by the monetary authority, represented solely by the BRB. Scope of the data Burundi's official international reserves consist of monetary gold, SDRs, the reserve tranche position in the IMF, and foreign currency-denominated assets held by the BRB. Exceptions to coverage Assets not immediately usable are not included in the international official reserves. Unrecorded activity & 2.4.1 Valuation Foreign currencies are valued at the exchange rate prevailing on the day the statement of international official reserves is compiled. Gold is valued at a price based on the Reuters world gold price (in U.S. dollars per ounce, and the BRB converts these dollars into Burundi francs, using the day's prevailing average exchange rate). Information on the value of the SDR and the reserve tranche in the IMF is provided by the IMF Treasurer's Department. 2.4.2 Recording basis The statement of international official reserves is generated from the accounting statements of the BRB and is therefore normally compiled on an accrual basis. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures The various components of the official reserves are established on a gross basis. In addition to the gross official reserves, the foreign liabilities of the BRB are also presented, as well as the net official reserves. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The statement of international official reserves is generated from the accounting statements of the BRB. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording The accounting classification of the various components of the reserves is detailed enough to allow for statistical classification. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness The accounting statements of the BRB are compiled daily and are available two or three days later. 3.2.1 Source data assessment & 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Apart from the possible valuation adjustments required by changes in foreign currency exchange rates, the statement of official reserves being derived from the accounting statements of the BRB, no statistical technique is necessary for its compilation. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures & 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results & 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data & 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs & 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses Revision studies are not performed. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses Not applicable, as the statement of international official reserves is not the subject of revision studies. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request Non-confidential additional data can be provided on request.
