 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Switzerland follows the guidelines of the System of National Accounts (SNA), the European System of National Accounts (ESA 2010) and those of the Government Finance Statistics Manual (GFSM 2014) in the compilation of data on the fiscal sector. Both nonfinancial and financial accounts of the general government are covered. Used concepts and definitions are closely in line with these guidelines 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Sectorization: The general government sector and its sub-sectors are defined in accordance with the regulations of ESA 2010, which are also consistent with the SNA 2008 and GFSM 2014.The sector general government in Switzerland consists of the central government, state governments, local governments and social security funds. To ensure consistency, the sectorization concept is being defined and implemented in close cooperation with representatives from National Accounts of the Federal Statistical Office, Financial Accounts of the Swiss National Bank and Federal Finance Statistics of the Federal Finance Administration. Classification: The classification of economic flows of the general Government sector follows the regulations of ESA 2010, as well as the regulations of the GFSM 2014. 2.2.1 Scope Sectorization: The general government sector and its sub-sectors are defined in accordance with the regulations of ESA 2010, which are also consistent with the SNA 2008 and GFSM 2014.The sector general government in Switzerland consists of the central government, state governments, local governments and social security funds. To ensure consistency, the sectorization concept is being defined and implemented in close cooperation with representatives from National Accounts of the Federal Statistical Office, Financial Accounts of the Swiss National Bank and Federal Finance Statistics of the Federal Finance Administration. Classification: The classification of economic flows of the general Government sector follows the regulations of ESA 2010, as well as the regulations of the GFSM 2014. Scope of the data The data on General Government Operations (GGO) under the SDDS Plus covers Revenue and Expenses by type (2nd digit), and Transactions in non-financial assets by type (3rd digit) of the Confederation, the cantons and communes as well as the social security funds. Exceptions to coverage None. Unrecorded activity There is no unrecorded activity in the source data. In the target data some positions are unrecorded (provisions, write offs) as prescribed by ESA/GFS. 2.4.1 Valuation Transactions and stocks are valued according to GFSM 2014 and ESA 2010 regulations. Whenever available, market prices are being used for the valuation of financial stocks. 2.4.2 Recording basis Accounting basis: Transactions data are mostly accrual based. The source data are collected on an accrual basis except for the Central Government whose data were collected on a cash basis before 2008. The national accounting models are aligned to the International Public Sector Accounting Standards (IPSAS ), which requires transactions to be recorded on an accrual basis. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures Adjustments for grossing and netting of transactions: All transactions are reported on a gross basis. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The main source of the data is the annual accounts of the individual units of each sub-sector as well as higher frequency data from the central government. Most quarterly data are the result of a temporal disaggregation of the annual series using econometric techniques. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording In some cases, source data only partially meet the definitions, scope, sectorization, classifications, valuation, and time of recording required. The major discrepancies are the following: Sectorization not in line with SNA/ESA/GFS concept Only a sub-sample of all institutional units (municipalities) is available. Stock positions not recorded at market value Stock positions not classified by instruments Stock positions or economic flows are not available at the needed degree of details Stock positions or economic flows not delivered with the expected classification source code In these cases, adjustments are made using recoding, statistical techniques such as extrapolation and/or additional data sources are used. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Due to the much decentralized structure of the Swiss State and the high degree of autonomy of the sub-central entities (e.g. cantons and municipalities), data from these entities may come with a substantial delay. In these cases, provisional data are being used and/or estimates and predictions are made. The Federal Finance Statistics of the Federal Finance Administration is currently working in close cooperation with the Federal Statistical Office and the cantons in order to improve timeliness further. 3.2.1 Source data assessment Source data assessment for the annual data is an integral part of the internal quality control process and undergo a multiple stage quality control system before compilation according to ESA2010/GFSM2014 starts. Furthermore, the consistency of quarterly data is being checked. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques See adjustments mentioned in 3.1.2. Consistency between quarterly and yearly data is taken care with temporal disaggregation methods. If source data are not available on time (especially for the current year), estimations are produced. The estimated data are replaced by the actual data when available. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures See adjustments mentioned in 3.1.2. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results The discrepancies between stock variation and flows as well as between financial and non-financial transactions are regularly assessed and analyzed for possible improvements. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data See 3.4.1 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs See 3.4.1 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses For quality purposes, the source of revisions is being documented and if necessary inquired further.
