 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The OPS compiles the merchandise trade statistics broadly according to the United Nations, Department of Economic and Social Affairs, Statistics Division, guidelines recommended in International Merchandise Trade Statistics (IMTS2): Concepts and Definitions 2010 , published by the United Nations. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Merchandise trade data are published according to Harmonized System (HS) Classification and Broad Economic Classification (BEC). Scope of the data International merchandise trade statistics cover all internationally transacted goods in the Republic of Palau. Exceptions to coverage No merchandise export data are published. 2.4.1 Valuation Customs data on imports and exports are provided on the basis of c.i.f. and f.o.b. and presented in current US dollars. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures Import data includes imports for re-exports. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Source data for merchandise imports and exports statistics are provided by the Bureau of Customs and Protection Board. These are compiled every month with HS Classification. The OPS compiles monthly data into annual totals. 3.2.1 Source data assessment The OPS compiler examines the Customs data. When discrepancies are found, they discuss with the customs office for data clarification and correction. None 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses No revision studies were conducted.
