 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The Central Bank of Trinidad and Tobago (CBTT) disseminates foreign reserve balances in millions of US dollars. The definition is consistent with the recommendations of the sixth edition of the IMF’s Balance of Payments and International Investments Position Manual – Sixth Edition - 2009 (BPM6). 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Classification of the reserve assets are consistent with those outlined the IMF’s Balance of Payments and International Investments Position Manual – Sixth Edition - 2009 (BPM6). . 2.2.1 Scope Classification of the reserve assets are consistent with those outlined the IMF’s Balance of Payments and International Investments Position Manual – Sixth Edition - 2009 (BPM6). . Scope of the data The official foreign reserves of Trinidad and Tobago comprise the foreign balances of the Central Bank, Central Government. The Central Bank foreign balances comprise its reserve assets held in the form of balances and securities, IMF Reserve Tranche Position and Special Drawing Rights (SDR) Holdings. The central government's foreign balances consist of its cash balances held at the CBTT. Data are also published on the commercial banks net foreign position. Exceptions to coverage There are currently no exceptions to the recommended coverage. Unrecorded activity While there is no unrecorded activity, interest accrued on gold holdings is reflected on an annual basis. 2.4.1 Valuation Valuations of reserve items are at market prices. 2.4.2 Recording basis The data are compiled on an accrual basis. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures All reserve items are recoded on a gross basis. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Data are sourced from the Reserve Management arm of the Reserves and Domestic Market Management Department and Finance and Accounting Department on a weekly basis. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Source data are received in a standard format, with classification outlined by the Statistic s Department. The data are then processed and compiled by the Central Bank and Money Unit. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Monthly data are available and published two weeks after the end of the month via the CBTT’s Data Centre on the website. 3.2.1 Source data assessment Source data are assessed during compilation for possible errors and omissions and are queried with the compilers of the source data. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Data are submitted in the standard format as outlined by the Statistics Department. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results Data validation checks are conducted routinely to ensure data consistency. Internally flows are also analysed to account for changes in the period-to-period balances. Anomalies are queried with the source data suppliers. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs All discrepancies are investigated with the source data suppliers. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses Revisions are published in the reporting period after adjustment has been made. Any methodological updates are advised by the Balance of Payments Unit of the Statistic s Department, the changes are then reflected and the entire dataset is revised to incorporate any changes. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request Unpublished data are available to the Ministry of Finance with approval from the Governor.
