 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Concept: For the compilation of the IIP data, the NBM uses the concepts, definitions and conceptual framework as set forth in IMF's Balance of Payments and International Investment Position Manual , sixth edition (BPM6), International Monetary Fund, 2009. Standard and integrated IIP statements are compiled. Currently the IIP time series in BPM6 format for the both IIP presentations are available starting with data for the beginning of 2009. Coverage, definitions and classification are identical for all periods. Definition : The IIP of Moldova is a statistical statement reflecting, at a particular point of time, the value of the positions of external financial assets and liabilities of the country's residents vis-a vis nonresidents. The ESSD compiles the IIP of the Republic of Moldova on a quarterly and annual basis, expressed in millions of USD. Quarterly and annually IIP data are also recalculated from USD into EUR at quarter-end / year-end exchange rates. The data in EUR are presented in annexes to the quarterly Analytical Notes and in International Accounts (IA) Yearbook. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Classification : The IIP classification system applied by the NBM follows the BPM6 recommendations. Primarily, the data are split into assets and liabilities. Data are further detailed by functional categories (direct, portfolio and other investment, financial derivatives (other than reserves) and employee stock options, and official reserve assets). Financial instruments are used within functional categories: equity and investment fund shares, debt instruments (special drawings rights, currency and deposits, debt securities, loans, trade credits and advances, other accounts receivable / payable), and other financial assets / liabilities (monetary gold, financial derivatives and employee stock options). Financial instruments recorded under financial derivatives (other than reserves) and employee stock options, portfolio and other investment are further subdivided by domestic institutional sector. Portfolio and other investment are also registered by maturity: long-term, which refers to financial instruments with original maturity more than 12 months, and short-term – to those with a maturity of 12 months or less. Scope of the data Unit Coverage: The IIP covers all positions in external financial assets and liabilities of Moldovan residents to non-residents. Foreign embassies and consulates, representatives of international organizations accredited in Moldova and representative offices of foreign enterprises located in Moldova are treated as non-residents. Data Coverage: The IIP of Moldova includes the positions in external assets and liabilities for all sectors: central bank, general government, deposit-taking corporations except the central bank and other sectors further detailed into two sub-sectors (1) other financial corporations (except banks) and (2) nonfinancial corporations, households and non-profit institutions serving households ( NPISHs ). Transaction Coverage: The IIP of Moldova covers almost all external financial assets and liabilities with non-residents held by resident central bank, general government, deposit-taking corporations except the central bank, and other sectors with separate presentation of other financial corporations (except banks) and nonfinancial corporations, households, and NPISHs . Geographic Coverage: The economic territory of Moldova consists of the geographic territory administrated by the Government of Moldova, including Moldovan embassies, consulates and representatives abroad. Embassies, consulates and entities of foreign governments placed on the territory of Moldova are considered extraterritorial for the Moldovan economy. Representative offices of foreign institutions on the territory of the Republic of Moldova are treated as non-residents. Exceptions to coverage Transaction Coverage: Households' external assets and liabilities with non-residents may not be fully covered by the existing sources and methods. Geographic Coverage: The IIP of Moldova does not include data on external assets and liabilities of the residents from the left side of the Nistru River and municipality of Bender. Unrecorded activity The NBM makes estimates for households' savings in foreign exchange based on the personal remittances estimation model and reports on demand and supply of foreign currency from / to individuals. 2.4.1 Valuation Conversion to Unit of Account : The IIP data are compiled and published in millions of USD. The positions in external assets and liabilities denominated in other currencies are converted into USD at the official exchange rate set by the NBM at the end of the reference period. The impact of foreign exchange rate changes of original currencies against the USD, price changes and other changes in volume is calculated evaluating the positions. These adjustments are calculated according to the IMF methodology. Estimations are made in USD, taking into account the evolution of other currencies. Standard presentations of IIP are also available in EUR converted at the cross exchange rate at period-end. Annual EUR-denominated data are available starting with reference year 2013 (presented in the annexes to the Yearbook for 2017) and for quarterly data – starting with data for 2024 (presented in the annexes to the Analytical notes). Valuation: The positions in financial assets and liabilities are valued mostly at nominal value. The book value is used when the market value is not appropriate or not available, i.e. foreign direct investment equity and trade credits and advances positions are valued at book value as reported in financial statements and 1-Invest report. For some instruments positions are historically cumulated (position is formed as accumulation of BOP flows). 2.4.2 Recording basis Recording Convention: The recording basis for the IIP is the accrual accounting, except for loans, for which due-for-payment basis is used. Investment is considered as direct when a non-resident owns 10% or more of the ordinary shares, or voting power (for an incorporated enterprise) or the equivalent (for an unincorporated enterprise). 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures Grossing / netting procedures: Changes in positions reflecting transactions are recorded on a net basis separately for assets and liabilities. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The main data sources for the IIP compilation are the NBS' databases of legal entities reports on direct investment in the Republic of Moldova, financial statements at the level of individual legal entity, reports of government institutions, licensed banks and foreign exchange offices, as well as data from various departments of the NBM, and other legal entities. A. Direct investment The basic data source for statistics on direct investment liabilities is the database of individual joint ventures' (except for banks), based on quarterly reports on direct investment in equity and retained earnings collected by the NBS and transmitted to the NBM for processing. Individual data received from the NBS are supplemented with data from the ITRS and reports on transactions with non-residents on primary and secondary security markets provided by the National Commission for Financial Markets. Data on direct investment in equity liabilities are cross-checked with legal entities' financial statements on an annual basis. For other sectors, the value of the direct investment in equity position is estimated using the "own funds at book value" method based on the books of resident direct investment enterprises. The Banking Supervision Department of the NBM provides data on investment in equity in the banking sector based on licensed banks' balance sheets and quarterly reports on changes in banks' equity by components and information on banks' shareholding structure. Data on direct investment – debt instruments - liabilities include debt positions between affiliated enterprises on loans, trade credits and advances, and other accounts payable. Data on intercompany lending are derived by the NBM from the DMFAS and are supplemented with data from the ITRS and data from the quarterly statistical report 1-Invest. Debt positions between affiliated enterprises on trade credits and advances are derived from legal entities' financial statements. Data on direct investment in equity - assets are estimated based on the ITRS data, financial statements and information on authorized transactions with non-residents in foreign exchange. The values of positions are calculated as accumulation of flows of direct investment in equity. B. Portfolio investment Positions are evaluated based on the BOP flows for assets and liabilities on portfolio investment – equity and investment funds share and Debt securities - other sectors. The NBM compiles positions on liabilities - portfolio investment – general government based on the report on government debt securities, held by non-residents, submitted by the Central Securities Depository. Positions on assets – portfolio investment – banks – debt securities are obtained from the reports on open foreign exchange position of licensed banks submitted by the Risk Monitoring and Reporting Division of the NBM. C. Financial derivatives (other than reserves) and employee stock options Positions are evaluated based on the BOP flows for which the ITRS is the main data source. D. Other investment 1. Other equity : Participation in some international organizations is classified as other equity. The main data sources are the MOF reports and ITRS data. 2. Currency and deposits : Position data on foreign assets and liabilities of the NBM and licensed banks are compiled based on the balance sheet data reported by the licensed banks and the NBM. Other sectors' assets in the form of currency and deposits are calculated based on the legal entities' reports on their direct accounts abroad. The NBM makes estimates for households' savings in foreign exchange based on personal remittances estimation model and the reports of deposit-taking corporations on purchases / sales of foreign currency from / to individuals. 3. Loans : Data on liabilities in the form of loans for all sectors are produced by the NBM based on the DMFAS, administered by the MOF (for public debt except NBM loans from IMF) and by the NBM (for private debt and NBM loans from IMF). The DMFAS data are supplemented and cross-checked loan-by-loan with data from the ITRS. 4. Trade credits and advances : The data sources for trade credits components are: · Prior to 2008 positions on advances received or paid by economic agents from / to non-residents (quarterly and annual data) and on credits extended directly by the suppliers of goods and services to their non-resident customers were calculated based on the consolidated data from the legal entities' balance sheets, processed by the NBS. · Since 2008 quarterly estimations are made based on comparison of payments and receipts recorded in the ITRS for the imports / exports of goods with the customs declarations database data. In addition, estimations comprise several types of services exported / imported in the reporting economy, paid in advance. · Quarterly positions on trade credits and advances are estimated by adding flows to positions in a particular period. · Annual positions on trade credits and advances are reconciled with individual data from the legal entities' financial statements and annual 1-Invest statistical report, collected by the NBS and processed by the NBM. 5. Other accounts receivable / payable : This category covers information on positions in other arrears comprising deferred payments of dividends to non-residents, as well as liabilities to non-residents on transactions with investment or income, which are accumulated as the sum of BOP flows. The main data sources are: reports on transactions in securities in the secondary market involving non-residents, the NBS data on foreign direct investment, and financial statements. 6. Special Drawings Rights : The main data sources are the reports received from the MOF and the DMFAS data. E. Reserve assets Data are produced based on the foreign exchange position of the NBM and reports submitted by the Financial Markets Department of the NBM. The data provided are detailed so that the ESSD is able to calculate daily positions, flows, exchange rate changes, price changes at the official cross-rates of original currencies in reserve assets' composition against US dollar. Securities in reserve assets are reevaluated monthly at their market price on the last day of the month. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Source data generally comply with methodological standards and guidelines for compiling IIP statistics. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness The source data are timely for compiling and disseminating quarterly IIP statistics according to the SDDS requirements. For surveys, the enterprises are expected to report promptly and follow-up procedures to ensure the timely receipt of data. Non-respondents are followed up accordingly. Data from the ITRS are available on a timely basis. Formal arrangements are in place to ensure the timely basis of administrative data. 3.2.1 Source data assessment All data go through validations with related sources. Individual records with high values are checked with other sources. Necessary follow-up is done either to correct misreported data or to obtain missing data. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques The NBM monitors the compatibility of data and ensures the internal integrity of the IIP. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Seasonal adjustment : the IIP data are not seasonally adjusted. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results Intermediate results are checked against various alternative data sources and estimates. Collection of data from multiple sources enables the ESSD to validate statistical outputs. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data The behaviour of time series is cross-checked with related series or indicators, where available. Financial flows are reconciled with changes in positions. Whenever surveys or administrative data are used, they are cross-checked with the ITRS results. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs Data on changes in position are reconciled with statistics on monetary aggregates, ED and international reserves. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses Analysis and studies of revisions are carried out on a routine basis and used to improve sources and statistical techniques. The information regarding revisions, by items and periods affected, together with the reasons for revisions is published in the International Accounts of the Republic of Moldova Statistical Yearbook. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses Information on revisions in terms of the reasons, items and periods affected is published in a table in the International Accounts of the Republic of Moldova Statistical Yearbook. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request Ad hoc IIP statistics are routinely provided upon request to ministries and to international organizations, embassies, international rating agencies etc. The ESSD also provides data in response to requests from students and researchers. Customized tabulations are provided free of charge to meet specific requests. The annual publication International Accounts of the Republic of Moldova Statistical Yearbook provides contact details for additional information. 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points The "International Accounts of the Republic of Moldova" Statistical Yearbook provides a clear reference to the division in charge of compiling and disseminating IIP statistics, along with contact details of the person who could assist users. Contact information for public relations and media is also available on the NBM's website, together with an online feedback form for comments and questions. 5.3.2 Availability of documents and services catalogs The NBM publishes all NBM's statistical publications on its website, which also includes a comprehensive list of publications.
