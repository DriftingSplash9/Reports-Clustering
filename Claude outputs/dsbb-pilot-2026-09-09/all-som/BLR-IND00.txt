 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Concept: The industrial production index is calculated in order to assess the short-term trends in industry at the national and regional levels, to estimate GDP and GRP. Definition: The industrial production index is calculated monthly on a set of representative goods (services) (more than 1400 most important items in physical or value terms) and is based on the calculation of individual indices of production of specific industrial products with further gradual aggregation of individual indices in the production indices of the corresponding economic activities: subclasses, classes, groups, divisions and sections related to Mining and quarrying; Manufacturing; Electricity, gas, steam, hot water and air conditioning supply; Water supply; waste management and remediation activities, and for the industry as a whole. At the first stage, the volume of production of representative goods/services for the base year is used as weights for aggregation. At further stages the structure of gross value added by economic activity for the base year is used as weights for aggregation. The base year is t-2, where t is the reference year. The base year is changed annually. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Industries are classified according to the National Classification of Economic Activities (hereafter, OKED), which is harmonized with NACE, Rev.2 at 4-digit level and with ISIC, Rev. 4 at 2-4-digit level. Industrial products are classified according to the national classification ОКРБ 007-2012 "Classification of Products by Economic Activity", which is harmonized with CPA 2008 at 6-digit level and with OKED at 4-digit level. 2.2.1 Scope Industries are classified according to the National Classification of Economic Activities (hereafter, OKED), which is harmonized with NACE, Rev.2 at 4-digit level and with ISIC, Rev. 4 at 2-4-digit level. Industrial products are classified according to the national classification ОКРБ 007-2012 "Classification of Products by Economic Activity", which is harmonized with CPA 2008 at 6-digit level and with OKED at 4-digit level. Scope of the data Industrial coverage : Industrial production covers economic activities classified in sections B, C, D, E of the National Classification of the Republic of Belarus ОКРБ 005-2011 "Kinds of Economic Activity". Product coverage : The index of industrial production is calculated based on the production dynamics of major representative goods and services (in physical or value terms). The determined sets of representative goods/services include the most significant products and make up 85-90 percent of the total industrial production for each economic activity. Exceptions to coverage New for the country products having minor share in the industrial output are not included in the set of representative goods and services and, consequently, in the industrial production index. Unrecorded activity Within the framework of industrial statistics, imputations are made for the volume of industrial production of natural persons who carry out individual entrepreneurial activities . 2.4.1 Valuation In the state statistical reports the enterprises reflect the volume of industrial production in actual sales prices (producer prices) net of VAT and other taxes and charges payable from revenue and net of product delivery expenses. 2.4.2 Recording basis Timing of production observations: Enterprises provide information on the output in physical and value terms to state statistics bodies on a monthly basis (no later than 4th day of every month). The information contains data for a reference month, previous month and a corresponding month of previous year. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures The value of industrial production is defined as a gross measure. Industrial production indices are computed using the gross value added of the respective economic activities as weights. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Source of industrial production data: monthly state statistical survey industrial production statistics. Unit coverage: About 2 800 enterprises engaged in activities classified under OKED sections B, C, D and E (hereafter, referred to industry) are surveyed monthly. Exhaustive coverage applies to enterprises (50 and more employees) of all ownership types and state-owned enterprises (0-49 employees) with the principal economic activity in industry. Sample coverage (criterion: importance of the contribution of the surveyed unit in the volume of industrial production for specific economic activity, i.e. a cut-off sample) applies to enterprises of all ownership types with the secondary economic activity in industry and private enterprises (0-49 employees) with the principal economic activity in industry. Source of weights: annual state statistical surveys on business statistics. Within the framework of the annual survey, all large, medium and small enterprises (50-100 employees) engaged in activities referred to industry are surveyed exhaustively, and small enterprises (0-49 employees) engaged in activities referred to industry are surveyed using a sampling method (exhaustively once every five years). Data collection methods: Enterprises fill out state statistical reporting forms electronically (on-line). 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Definition: The source variable for the calculation of industrial production indices is the quantity (value) by types of industrial goods and services produced by enterprises without subcontracting and intended for sale or recording as fixed assets, as well as products produced from raw materials supplied by customers. The volume of produced goods and services is recorded in reporting either: in physical terms or in value terms (in current prices of the reference period, net of VAT and other taxes payable from proceeds and transport expenses). Product coverage: Enterprises provide information by types of produced industrial goods and services in compliance with the statistical classification CK 25.006-2015 "Industrial Products" which is an abridged version of the national classification ОКРБ 007-2012 "Classification of industrial products by economic activity" and includes all products produced in the Republic of Belarus. Selection of products for the calculation of industrial production index: The set of representative goods and services for the calculation of industrial production index includes more than 1400 goods items covering 85-90 percent of the volume of industrial production in each economic activity at the level of subclasses (OKED 5 digits). 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Source data collection: Respondents submit reports on the 4th day after the reference month. Preliminary information on industrial production indices is available on the 16th day after the reference month, which is in compliance with the data standard applied. 3.2.1 Source data assessment Information received from enterprises is subject to arithmetic and logical checks and in case of errors corrections are made before publishing. The data are also compared with the relevant data from other sources. Previously published data are benchmarked against the final annual data. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Procedures for non-response: Pursuant to the Law of the Republic of Belarus On State Statistics, enterprises are obliged to submit state statistical reporting to statistics bodies. In the event that enterprises are at the liquidation stage, they are obliged to submit state statistical reporting to state statistics bodies before an entry regarding their liquidation is made in the Single Register of Legal Entities and Individual Entrepreneurs of the Republic of Belarus. Introduction of new observation units and new goods: Once a year, before the start of the following reporting year, a sample of respondents for the monthly survey is updated in order to maintain its representativeness. The list of representative goods/services is updated annually in order to include in the index calculation new commodity items and take into account quality changes. New goods/services are included only if data on the volume of their production in the base year are available. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Source variable: Output of representative goods and services in physical (or value) terms in the periods under comparison. Calculation formula: The Laspeyres formula is used to calculate the industrial production index. The volume of production for the period t is compared with the volume of production for the period t-1 by comparing the deviations of the t and t-1 periods from the average monthly value of the base year. Base year: t-2, where t is the reference year. Weights used for aggregation: at the level of commodity group, the value of output of representative goods/services in the base year; at the level of economic activities, the structure of gross value added in economic activity in the base year. Frequency of weights update: annually both at the level of commodity group and at the level of economic activity. Calculation of the lower-level indices: At the first stage individual indices of production of specific types of industrial products are calculated on the basis of data on the output of representative goods (services) in physical or value terms for the periods under comparison, valued in average annual prices of the base year (t-2). Aggregation: At later stages the individual indices are aggregated in the production indices of the corresponding classes, groups, divisions and sections related to mining and quarrying; manufacturing; electricity, gas, hot water and air conditioning supply; water supply, waste management and remediation activities; and for the industry as a whole. Aggregation of indices using gross value added for the base year is carried out from the level of 3-digit groupings by economic activity. Change-over to new weights, chain-linking method: To ensure the continuity and coherence of the multiannual time series of industrial production indices with changing weights, the chain-linking method is applied. The linking is carried out using the chaining coefficients. The coefficients are calculated based on the overlap period of one month (December). Base period of indices: 2015. Seasonal adjustments: Monthly data are adjusted for seasonal and calendar factors by the TRAMO/SEATS method using the software product JDEMETRA+ 2.2.0. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results Validation of calculated industrial production indices includes the analysis of reliability of produced index values by included commodity groups and industries. Consistency with other indicators of industrial production: Benchmarking against more detailed annual data reported by enterprises. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data Statistical discrepancies in the intermediate (current) data are assessed based on the more detailed and revised annual data reported by enterprises. The variation coefficient is calculated for current and annual estimates and makes up not more than 0.2% at the level of industry as a whole and OKED sections, and not more than 2% at the level of OKED subsections, which fully complies with the international data validation practice. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs In case of discrepancies in previously published data changes are made to this data in subsequent publications. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses Due to changeover to NACE, Rev. 2 starting from January 2016, annual indices of industrial production for 2011-2015 and monthly indices for 2015 were revised backward. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses When revised, previously published data on industrial production index are subject to update in further publications. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request State statistical bodies provide aggregated statistical data upon user request unless they contain any confidential information. 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points Statistical publications contain information on contact persons responsible for data preparation. 5.3.2 Availability of documents and services catalogs The catalogue of statistical publications is compiled annually, is available on Belstat's website and contains brief annotations to statistical books to be published in the current year ( https://www.belstat.gov.by/en/ofitsialnaya-statistika/publications/catalogues-of-statistic al-publications/ ).
