 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Definition: The international investment position (IIP) is defined as the balance sheet of the country's stock of external financial assets and liabilities. Consequently, this indicator shows the value of the stock of financial assets and liabilities included in the standard components of the balance of payments' financial account. The data are compiled in accordance with the methodology established in the sixth edition of the Balance of Payments and International Investment Position IMF. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The primary distinction is between assets and liabilities; the difference between them is the net position. Asset and liability are broken down into the functional categories of the balance of payments, i.e., direct investment, portfolio investment (equity and investment fund shares and debt instruments), other investment and official reserve assets. The classification is accordance with methodology established in the sixth edition of the Balance of Payments and IPP- IMF(BMP6): Assets: External assets include financial rights or holding of Salvadoran residents before non residents. Assets are classified as direct investment, portfolio investment, other investment and reserve assets. Foreign direct investment reflects the objective of a resident entity to acquire a long-term share in a non resident company. The investment portfolio includes equity securities, debt securities in other economies as money market instruments, bonds and notes. Financial derivatives. Financial instruments used to trade specific financial risk by its own. Other investment is a residual category that includes several elements: currency and deposits, loans, trade credits and advances and other assets not included in the direct or portfolio investment or reserve assets Currency and deposits refer to bank notes and coins in circulation and residents' transferable deposits used to make payments abroad. Loans refer to the credit extended through direct lending of funds by a resident lender (creditor) to a nonresident borrower (debtor) pursuant to an arrangement. In this category is also included financial leasing. Trade credits consist of claims arising from international trade, namely the direct extension of credit by resident suppliers to non residents buyers of goods and services. The credit is equal to the amount of goods and services traded. This is the case when disbursements are made not in cash but in kind and transactions's services and fees are paid in cash. Other assets refer to operations not included, by definition, in the above three categories of other investment Reserve assets consist of those external and highly liquidity assets under control by the monetary authority. Those assets include monetary gold, SDRs, foreign exchange assets, reserve position in the IMF and other assets. Liabilities: Foreign liabilities consist of liabilities issued by residents and holded by non residents. These are classified as direct investment, portfolio investment and other investment Direct investment reflects the intention of a nonresident company to acquire in a permanent basis, a long-term share in a resident company in El Salvador. The investment portfolio includes equity securities, debt securities in other economies as money market instruments, bonds and notes. Financial derivatives. Financial instruments used to trade specific financial risk by its own Other investment is a residual category that includes several elements: currency and deposits, loans, trade credits and advances and other assets not included in the direct or portfolio investment or reserve assets Scope of the data The data cover the assets and liabilities of the four institutional sectors defined in the sixth edition of the IMF's Balance of Payments and International Investment Position (BPM6), which are the Central Bank, Deposit-taking corporations, except the central bank, the General government and other sectors of El Salvador Data cover the entire territory. Exceptions to coverage In the case of foreign assets, there is not information on purchases made in international markets. 2.4.1 Valuation According to "The Monetary Integration Act" in force since January 1st, 2001, the US Dollar is legal tender and the exchange rate between the Colon and the US Dollar is fixed at 8.75 Colones per 1US Dollar and all external assets and liabilities included in El Salvador's IIP are valued at this exchange rate. Direct investment is valued at book value for equity.Debt Securities and other investment uses nominal value. Portfolio investment and reserve assets are valued at market prices. Financial derivatives are valued a market value on a gross basis. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures Figures are registered in their gross value. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Government and other sectors' foreing debt data are obtained from the Central Reserve Bank and Commercial Bank's balance sheets and administrative data from the Central Reserve Bank and from quarterly business surveys for other sectors. Direct investment has as a source of information quarterly business surveys conducted by the Central Reserve Bank's, Deparment of the External Sector using SIBEX. The portfolio investment is estimated using domestic data from Central Bank related to foreign debt securities issued by The Central Government such as treasury bills and eurobonds. Data from the banks are reported from their balance sheets. For the other sectors the source is quarterly business surveys, administrative reports and custodian of securities. Financial Derivatives has as a source of informations quarterly businedd surveys conducted by the Central Reserve Bank's, Department of the External Sector using SIBEX. For other investment, the balance of commercial credits, loans, currency and deposits and other assets are calculated using the Central Reserve Bank's balance sheet figures, report government debt, quarterly business surveys, estimates in dollars in circulation, statistics of international bank payments and commercial Bank's balance sheets. Reserve assets are calculated using the Central Reserve Bank's balance sheet figures. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Generally, there is a good degree of application of definitions, scope, classification and time of the registry prescribed by the Sixth edition of the Balance of Payments and International Investment Position Manual (BPM6) . 3.1.3 Source data timeliness The survey system in SIBEX and the agreements for data delivery match the dates to submit statistics. 3.2.1 Source data assessment Direct investment: Stock data are provided on an quaterly basis by FDI firms in response to a survey carried out by the Central Bank. Portfolio investment: Stock data are provided for Finance Ministry for public sector liabilities information of Central Securities Depository (CEDEVAL) is used for the non financial sector assets. And administrative record for information on pension funds. Other investment: For public sector liabilities and for financial sector assets and liabilities, data are obtained from instances directly related with the operations. Statistics on deposits provided for Bank for International Settlements are used for private non-financial sector. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Transactions for high amounts reported in the surveys or in the administrative records are consulted to the informants. When there is no report from the respondents, data imputation is made based on the recent behavior of the transactions of said company or institution. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Data are adjusted during two years following publication date of said statistics. In the current year the quarters are adjusted. In exceptional cases there may be adjustements over a wider range of years. Adjustments are made considering the information that is not reported timely, corrections to the information registered or improvements in data coverage. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results Evolution of the data reported through surveys is evaluated on a regular basis, the coverage is also reviewed. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses No such studies have been carried out. Except for analysis or review by the IMF experts under the Regional Project on Harmonization of external sector statistics (CAPTAC-DR) 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses Bank publications disclose users the reasons giving way to review statistics, although the studies explain in detail such changes dare not available to the public. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request At the public's request, non-confidential information that is not disclosed in the bank’s publications is provided. 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points No contacts are listed at this time. 5.3.2 Availability of documents and services catalogs There is no catalog of publications, documents and other services. All BCR publications are free.
