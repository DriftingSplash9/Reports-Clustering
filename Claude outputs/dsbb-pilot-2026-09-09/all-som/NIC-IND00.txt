 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Each month, the Central Bank of Nicaragua (CBN) calculates and disseminates the Industrial Production Index (IPI). The index is a Laspeyres-type index (base 1998=100), which estimates the trend of the production of the most representative products in each sector of activity within the manufacturing industry. The IPI is published with a breakdown of 11 sub-indices corresponding to groups of products classified by economic activity. The new index was linked to the previous one, which covered the period January 1992-December 1997, with base 1994. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Products are classified by economic activity, using the International Standard Industrial Classification (ISIC rev. 3). Scope of the data A total of 95 products is researched whose value represents approximately 80 percent of the value of total manufacturing sales in the base period. The data on quantities produced each month are obtained from 140 reporting establishments located in various cities, except those on the Atlantic coast which are excluded for budgetary reasons. Base-year sales are used as weights, and sales of product i are calculated relative to the total sales of activity sector j, as well as total sales of activity sector j relative to total industry sales.
