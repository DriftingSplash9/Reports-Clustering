 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions International investment position Data on the International investment position are based on the methodology set out in the sixth edition of the IMF's Balance of Payments Manual, so it is the statistical statement that presents the value and composition of the stock of financial claims and liabilities on/to non-residents as of the end of the respective period. The data are divided into the following prescribed components: Direct investment Equity and investment fund shares Debt instruments Portfolio investment Equity and investment fund shares Debt securities Financial derivatives and employee stock options Other investment Other equity Currency and deposits Loans Insurance, pension and standardized guarantee schemes Trade credits and advances Other accounts receivable or payable Special drawing rights (net incurrence of liabilities) Reserve assets 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The structure of IIP is equivalent to the classification and ordering of items in the financial account, the primary income account and the other changes in financial assets and liabilities account, which allows easier reconciliation. Scope of the data The structure of the IIP follows functional categories as defined in the Manual, i.e. direct investment, portfolio investment, financial derivatives, other investment, and reserve assets. The category other investment is further broken down into other equity, currency and deposits, loans, insurance, pension and standardized guarantee schemes, trade credits and advances, other accounts receivable or payable and special drawing rights. The following breakdown of all the items of the IIP is according to their character and is followed by a sector breakdown (central bank, deposit-taking corporations except the central bank, general government, and other sectors) and by a maturity breakdown. Resident institutional units are defined in accordance to the BPM6 concepts of predominant center of economic interest. The sector classification has been amended to make it consistent with the 2008SNA. Geographical breakdown is also available, but is not published. Breakdown of investment position by main currencies like CZK, EUR, USD and other currencies is available. Exceptions to coverage Monetary and financial institution and central government are fully covered. Nonfinancial corporations and Local government are covered on base of predominant (large) institutions or corporations. As far as the sector coverage concerns, working on a more complete data coverage for households and NPISH is a continous effort. No standardized guarantee scheme has been identified yet, the situation is permanently monitored in liaison with the CZSO. 2.4.1 Valuation Stocks are valued at market prices if available: Direct investment: the equity stock data are recorded at book value for unlisted companies and at market value for listed companies (actually both prices – market and book – are available for compilers). Portfolio investments and financial derivatives are valued at market prices. Other investments are recorded at nominal value. Reserves assets are valued at market price. Conversion to unit of account: the official end of period central bank exchange rate is used to convert assets and liabilities denominated in foreign currencies into CZK as unit of account and conversely to EUR and USD (for publication only). 2.4.2 Recording basis The assets and liabilities of one particular item are recorded separately, on a “gross” basis. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs a) Statistical statements and surveys: Monthly statement of the foreign exchange and CZK positions of the CNB and commercial banks Monthly specification of government loans Monthly and quarterly statement of foreign assets and liabilities of non-bank entities Quarterly statement of the purchase, sale and ownership of domestic securities by non-residents Statistical survey in the area of direct investment (annual) b) Administrative data sources: Supplementary information from commercial banks and various units of the CNB Information (both regular and on request) from the Czech Statistical Office, the Ministry of Finance of the Czech Republic, the Prague Stock Exchange, the Prague Central Securities Depository, the Cadastral Office and other entities c) Information from the media d) The CNB’s own calculations The authorisation to demand information needed to compile the balance of payments stems from the provisions of Act No. 6/1993 Coll., on the Czech National Bank, as amended, and Act No. 15/1998 Coll., on Supervision in the Capital Market Area, as amended. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Adjustments to direct investment, portfolio investment and other investment: Generally there are no statistical adjustments in coverage, classification, timing, and valuation of data in government and banking sectors. Some statistical adjustments are made in enterprise surveys data. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Debt instruments issued by residents abroad, but in hands of residents are partially excluded. Reconciliation of stocks in IIP statements and flows related to transactions shown in the financial account and other changes in volume, exchange rate changes, and other price changes at the level functional category/ instrument/sector is provided. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request Geographic details of data: Geographic breakdown of stock data on direct investment abroad and in the Czech Republic and portfolio investment abroad is available.
