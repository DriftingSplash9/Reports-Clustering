 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The International Investment Position (IIP) reflects the external position of the economy in financial assets and liabilities at the end of a given period. Being the data consistent with the corresponding items of the financial account of the Balance of Payments. The IIP of Angola is compiled in US Dollars by the Statistics Department of the National Bank of Angola (BNA), according to the 6th Edition of the Balance of Payments and International Investment Position Manual (BPM6) of the International Monetary Fund (IMF). 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The IIP classification/sectorization of Angola basically conforms to the BPM6. The Assets are subdivided into direct investment, portfolio investment, other investments (trade credits, loans, currency and deposits, and other assets) and reserve assets. Liabilities include the same composition, except for reserve assets. There was an improvement in liabilities in particular other investment with the inclusion of the debt of commercial banks. Scope of the data In principle, the IIP data include all asset and liability positions between residents and non-residents of Angola. The geographic coverage spans the territory of the Republic of Angola, according to the BPM6 definition of economic territory. Exceptions to coverage There isn’t any exception, in term of coverage. Unrecorded activity No informal or illegal activity is recorded in the International Investment Position of Angola. 2.4.1 Valuation The IIP of Angola is compiled in US Dollars. The data reported in domestic currency are converted into US Dollars at the average exchange rate for the period in which the transaction took place. The balances are valued at market prices or, if not possible, at their nominal value. 2.4.2 Recording basis The balances are recorded on a commitment (accrual basis), according to the guidelines of the BPM6. The IIP reflects the opening balance of the preceeding period plus the transactions and adjustment of price/ exchange rate during the reference period. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures The procedures that we use are broadly consistent with internationally accepted standards, following the recommendations of the BPM6. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Basically the data for compilation of the IIP are obtained mainly through surveys, Debt Management Financing Analyzing System (DMFAS), the Balance Sheets of Monetary Accounts (2SR “Standard Report” and Balance Sheet of Central Bank), Bank for International Settlements (BIS) and others. Direct Investment This information is obtained from surveys covering investment firms, oil, diamond, constructors, and service providers and from the Exchange Control Department of the National Bank of Angola Portfolio Investment This information is obtained mainly from the surveys of the oil sector and the Balance Sheets of Monetary Accounts (2SR). Other Investment This information is obtained from surveys, the Balance Sheets of Monetary Accounts (2SR), BIS and DMFAS. Reserve Assets This Information is obtained from the Balance Sheets of Monetary Accounts (Reserve Assets) 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording For compilation of the IIP components, we use the same data sources used for the compilation of the financial account of the BOP. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness In general the timeliness of the source data needs to be improved, although it allows meeting the GDDS recommendations. 3.2.1 Source data assessment To the extent possible, data collected for the IIP are cross checked with information reported from the line ministries and other alternative sources of information. Discrepancies are analyzed and the results are used to improve data collection. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques The Central Bank (BNA) makes several adjustments to calculate the assets and liabilities to non-residents, including in particular: (i) Adjustments of the classification of information obtained from the enterprise surveys and the databases from other institutions to make information consistent; (ii) Adjustments resulting from the reconciliation of balance sheet data of the companies with the data obtained from the surveys. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Seasonal adjustments are not made​​ in the IIP of Angola. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results It is observed. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data It is observed. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs It is observed. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses It is not observed. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses Not applicable 5.1.5 Dissemination on request It is observed. 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points The contacts are in IIP bulletin and more details in NSDP and e-GDDS. 5.3.2 Availability of documents and services catalogs On www.bna.ao
