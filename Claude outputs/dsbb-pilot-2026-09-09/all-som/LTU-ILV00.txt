 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Concept: The concept of foreign currency liquidity is broader than that of official reserve assets and refers to the amount of foreign exchange resources that is readily available considering both predetermined and potential net drains of such resources. This is in line with the IMF Operational Guidelines for a Data Template on International Reserves and Foreign Currency Liquidity . The conceptual framework of official reserve assets data disseminated by the LB is based on the sixth edition of the IMF Balance of Payments and International Investment Position Manual (BPM6) . Thus, the data comprise external assets that are readily available to and controlled by the LB and that may be use for direct financing of the payments' imbalances and other similar purposes. Starting with the January 2015, the claims on non-euro area residents denominated in convertible foreign currencies (other than the euro), monetary gold, Special Drawing Right and reserve position in the IMF are classified as official reserve assets. Definition: The gross official reserve assets aggregate that is reported within the SDDS is defined as the official reserve assets of Lithuania and covers the components of reserve assets as defined in BPM6 . Foreign currency liquidity position in addition comprises other foreign currency assets at the disposal of the authorities to meet a sudden increase in the demand for foreign exchange and the potential (net) drains on foreign currency resources resulting from the short-term foreign currency liabilities and off-balance-sheet activities of the authorities. Relationship to international guideline: The concepts, definitions and classification underlying the data on official reserve assets disseminated by the LB are based on the BPM6 and IMF Operational guidelines for a Data Template on International Reserves and Foreign Currency Liquidity published by the IMF in 2013. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Classification: The classification system follows that of the BPM6 . The disseminated data specify the following components of official reserve assets: monetary gold, holdings of SDR, reserve position in the IMF, and other official reserve assets. Other official reserve assets position is presented as the sum of assets in form of currency and deposits, securities, financial derivatives, and other claims. Scope of the data Institutional coverage: Official reserve assets data comprise reserve assets held by the LB and claims on the IMF. Template data also cover activity of the central government, excluding social security funds. 2.4.1 Valuation Calculation: International reserves are measured from stocks of claims on nonresidents. Valuation: International reserves are valued at prevailing market prices. Conversion rates: Data are converted into euro based on euro foreign exchange reference rates announced by the ECB prevailing at the end of the reference period. Frequency of calculation and revaluation: gold holdings are revalued once a month on the last business day based on the gold market price in US dollars per Troy ounce. Holdings of foreign exchange (cash) are revalued on a currency-by-currency basis on each business day at the official exchange rate of that day. Securities are revalued monthly at market prices prevailing at the time of the revaluation date. Exceptions and special cases: None. 2.4.2 Recording basis Value of securities and deposits at the reference date includes accrued interest. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Data sources: the LB and Ministry of Finance of Lithuania. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Statistical adjustments: No statistical adjustments are applied to accounting records. 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points Information on contact points for statistical information compiled by the LB is published on the LB website https://www.lb.lt/en/statistics-contacts .
