 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The analytical framework used by the NBRNM in compiling Depository Corporations Survey reflects concepts and principles from the IMF' Monetary and Financial Statistics Manual and Compilation Guide from 2016 (MFSMCG - IMF, 2016). Starting from June 2008, NBRNM applied a new methodology (IMF standard report forms) for compilation of monetary statistics. The time series of data for the period from January 2009 onward has been revised for the purposes of applying a new methodology in accordance with the new IMF Monetary and Financial Statistics Manual and Compilation Guide, 2016. A Depository Corporation Survey is consisted of NBRNM, banks and saving houses data and is compiled on a monthly basis, in compliance with the IMF Monetary and Financial Statistics Manual and Compilation Guide. The data are presented as stock data in millions of Denars, with foreign positions valued at current exchange rate. The major components are the following: Net foreign assets represent the difference between foreign assets and foreign liabilities of NBRNM and other depository corporations (banks and savings houses). This indicates that all foreign assets and liabilities of NBRNM and other depository corporations are aggregated and presented on net and gross basis. Domestic credits are consisted of depository corporations (NBRNM, banks and savings houses) claims on government(represented on net basis), claims on local government, public nonfinancial corporations, private sector and other financial corporations (represented on gross basis). Monetary aggregates are defined with narrow and broader money definition : M0 (reserve money ) = currency in circulation, banks and savings houses' accounts in NBRNM, banks' cash in vaults, banks' and savings houses' reserve requirements in Denars, banks' reserve requirements in foreign currency, compulsory deposit with the NBRNM as well as other financial corporations' accounts in NBRNM (mainly the account of the Deposit Insurance Fund). Money supply M1 = currency in circulation and demand deposits (current accounts at banks and saving houses; other financial corporations' current accounts at NBRNM). Money supply M2 = Monetary aggregate M1 and short-term deposits (sight deposits, time and saving deposits up to one year, in domestic and foreign currency). Money supply M3 (new standardized definition of broad money) = Monetary aggregate M2 and long-term deposits with maturity from one up to two years. Capital accounts in depository corporation survey incorporate capital accounts from NBRNM survey and other depository corporations (banks and savings houses) survey. Category Other items, net presents all categories not comprised in NBRNM survey and banks and savings houses survey, as well as all unclassified assets and liabilities from these surveys. Monthly balance sheets for NBRNM, banks and saving houses are produced as a basis for the monetary survey compilation. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The sectorization adopted for Central Bank Survey is in line with the MFSM (consistent with the Classification of the financial instruments in the SNA 2008 and ESA 95). Reporting units distinguish resident from nonresident counterparts, using the definitions on residency prescribed in the Law on Foreign Exchange Operations ("Official Gazette of the Republic of Macedonia" no. 34/01, 49/01, 103/01 and 51/03). Residency of institutional units is defined under the Law on Foreign Exchange Operations, where natural persons staying temporarily in the Republic of North Macedonia based on a valid residence permit or work permit for a period of at least six months are treated as residents of the country. (Such definition of residency of natural persons and its application in the process of creating statistical data is an exception to the recommendation given in the international statistical standards, according to which a criterion for determining residency is a stay or intention to stay in a country for a period of one year or more.) The resident sector is divided into five sub sectors:(i) Nonfinancial corporations, covering public and other (private) nonfinancial corporations; (ii) Government, which is disaggregated to central government ,social security's funds, local government, (iii) Depository corporations (NBRNM, banks and savings houses); (iv) Other financial corporations (Insurance companies, Pension funds and Other financial corporations) (v) Other resident sector including households (individuals and self-employed individuals) and Nonprofit institutions serving households. 2.2.1 Scope The sectorization adopted for Central Bank Survey is in line with the MFSM (consistent with the Classification of the financial instruments in the SNA 2008 and ESA 95). Scope of the data The depository sector in North Macedonia consists of the NBRNM, banks, and savings houses. The current Depository Corporation Survey compiled by the NBRNM covers the NBRNM, banks and saving houses. 2.4.1 Valuation The general recommendation of the MFSMCG is to evaluate financial assets and liabilities based on market prices or market price equivalents. The exceptions to this rule is the valuation of deposits and loans (and most other accounts payable/receivable categories) and for the liabilities in form of shares and other equity. These principles are broadly followed in North Macedonia's Depository corporation Survey. Monetary gold is valued monthly using the market price quoted at the London Stock Exchange; traded financial instruments are valued monthly using market prices. Conversion of foreign-currency- denominated assets and liabilities into local currency is carried out using official exchange rates in effect at the balance sheet date. For this purpose, the NBRNM and all banks use the end-period midpoint of buying and selling rates quoted by the NBRNM. The official exchange rate is a weighted average rate determined by the interbank foreign exchange market conditions. Likewise, SDR- denominated accounts are also converted into local currency using the end-of-period market exchange rate. Valuation adjustments are presented in the valuation accounts in the balance sheets of the NBRNM and banks, but not separately identified in their analytical accounts. 2.4.2 Recording basis In North Macedonia's Depository Corporation Survey, financial transactions are recorded on an accrual basis. Interest on financial assets or liabilities is accrued at the end of each month, and accrued interest is recorded under the underlying financial instruments consistent with MFSMCG recommendations. The MFSMCG also recommends that interest arrears be included with the underlying instruments. The current practice of the NBRNM, is consistent with the MFSMCG. Financial transactions are recorded simultaneously between transactors in conformity with MFSMCG guidelines. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures A general principle in macroeconomic statistics is that data should be collected and compiled on a gross basis. For data presented on a net basis, the underlying gross data should also be shown. In North Macedonia's Depository Corporation Survey, assets and liabilities are presented on a gross or net basis in conformity with the MFSMCG guidelines. In particular, claims on a particular transactor or group of transactors are not netted against liabilities to that transactor or group. Also, provisions against loans are shown on a gross basis on the liability side, and loans are included in domestic credit on a gross basis on the asset side. The Depository Corporation Survey shows underlying gross data for net foreign assets and net claims on central government. In the Depository Corporation Survey, appropriate claims and liabilities between depository corporations are appropriately canceled out, while preserving the presentation of data on claims on or liabilities to other domestic sectors and nonresidents. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The source data for compiling Depository Corporation Survey are: The central bank survey is prepared from the balance sheet data compiled from the NBRNM' chart of accounts which are provided by the NBRNM's Financial, Accounting and Control Department, on a monthly basis. This chart of accounts is generated by an electronic general ledger system based on complete accounting records. The other depository corporation's survey is prepared from the banks and saving houses' balance sheets. The source data for banks and saving houses' balance sheets are the individual chart of accounts reported to the NBRNM on a monthly basis. The source data, generated from banks and saving houses' accounting records, are transmitted electronically to NBRNM's database. With the implementation of the New charts of accounts for the NBRNM (January 2009), consistent with the International Accounting Standards, the banks and saving houses' reporting format, provides sufficient detail to compile monetary statistics in conformity with the MFSMCG guidelines. The source data are kept under continuous review to ensure that the data collection system remains comprehensive, reflects recent developments, and meets new data demands. These reviews take into account (i) ongoing revisions to the charts of accounts, (ii) concerns arising from the NBRNM's weekly liquidity planning meeting, and (iii) issues raised by other government agencies and other data users. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording The source data, reported in accordance with NBRNM requirements, reasonably approximate the definitions, scope, classification, valuation, and timing of reporting needed for monetary statistics. One deviation in accounting practice is that securities held to maturity are recorded at book value. The NBRNM provides banks and saving houses with guidance in the form of reporting instructions and ad-hoc consultation in completing the report forms. The NBRNM's Depository Corporation Survey compilers maintain regular contact with reporting banks to resolve data issues that have important implications for the accuracy of the accounting data for use in Depository Corporation Survey. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness The NBRNM, banks and saving houses' chart of accounts are required to become available eight days after the end of the reference month. These source data are provided sufficiently timely to allow for timely compilation of Depository Corporation Survey. 3.2.1 Source data assessment The NBRNM Depository Corporation Survey compilers, in collaboration with other NBRNM units involved in source data collection, review the reporting system for its adequacy for providing reliable source data. Automated data validation procedures are employed to check the internal consistency of reported data. In addition, source data are also reviewed against those of previous periods to identify out-of-trend items. The identified data inconsistencies are investigated with the reporting units. However, questions about the adequacy of the processes in place for assessing the source data arose from data inconsistency and misclassification identified by users on several occasions. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques The forms for reporting source data are designed to encourage use of computer programs, which enable prompt data processing. The potential for processing errors is minimized by the use of electronic reporting and processing procedures. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures The procedure for delayed reporting of data by banks and saving houses is to carry forward the chart of accounts of the last month of the late reporting bank into the current month's statistics. So far, no reporting delays have occurred. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results The accuracy and reliability of the reported source data for the NBRNM, banks and saving houses are checked against other sources or information; for instance, data on NBRNM's government bond holdings are checked against records of the Financial Market Operations Department. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data The NBRNM Depository Corporation Survey compilers identify statistical discrepancies (e.g. discrepancies in interbank positions) and, in collaboration with Financial, Accounting and Control Department and Division of Supervision, Banking Regulations and Financial Stability, investigate their causes. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs Procedures are in place to investigate large and unexplained movements in monetary and credit aggregates to detect possible classification and sectorization errors. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses The NBRNM studies and analyzes revisions as part of its data quality control process. The NBRNM data compilers cross-check preliminary data with final data and investigate any significant discrepancies. The results of investigations and adjustments made in the data compiling process are taken into account in subsequent periods. In general, revisions tend to be small in magnitude. Regular revision policy of final data is applied on the end of each year. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses Studies and analysis of revisions are undertaken ad hoc to measure, assess, and explain the source of revisions made to the data. These studies are internally documented and not made available to the public. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request Statistics not regularly published are made available to interested users upon their request and approval by the Governor's Office, without a fee. 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points Information on specific contact persons is posted on the NBRNM`s internet website for users to send comments, queries, and requests concerning any category of Depository Corporation Survey published by the NBRNM. Users frequently request assistance in the form of clarification and explanation of data through email, telephone, facsimile, or mail, and the assistance is in general promptly provided. The NBRNM's reviews closely comments, queries, and requests from general data users as well as from the NBRNM Council to obtain users' feedback. In particular, all user queries sent to the Governor's Office are distributed to concerned NBRNM departments. Actions that are taken to address these issues are reported to the Governor's Office to ensure that users' requests are addressed. 5.3.2 Availability of documents and services catalogs The NBRNM publishes a list of publications on its internet website.
