 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The national accounts are compiled following the System of National Accounts 2008 , compatible with methodology of the European System of Accounts, version-2010 (SNA-UN- 2008/ESA-2010) . National accounts are compiled on a quarterly and annual basis. NBS publishes, on a regular basis, flash (preliminary) data on quarterly national accounts in current and comparable prices, that are further reconciled with annual data. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The classification and sectorization used in the compilation of the national accounts are in broad conformity with international recommendations. The Classifier of Activities in Moldova Economy (CAEM) is used to develop the national accounts variables by economic activities; the CAEM is harmonized with NACE (Classification of Economic Activities in the European Community), rev.2, widely used in statistics and in other areas. The final consumption calculation of households is made according to COICOP (4 digits) For the purpose of surveying the physical volume of industrial production, the National Nomenclature of industrial products and services (PRODMOLD) is used, harmonized with the European List of Production-PRODCOM. Exported and imported goods are classified according to Combined Nomenclature of Goods, based on the Harmonized System of Codification and Description of Goods (HS-2012) and Combined Nomenclature of European Union (CN). The variables of Gross Domestic Product are grouped by Ownership forms classifier. Scope of the data Scope of the data: The national accounts statistics cover all tables and accounts that the Inter-Secretariat Working Group on National Accounts (ISWGNA) has defined as a minimum requirement for implementation of the 2008 SNA/ESA 2010 , as well as those whose compilation the ISWGNA has recommended. The NBS annualy compiles and disseminates the following accounts: Goods and services account Production account Generation of income account Allocation of primary income account Secondary distribution of income account Use of gross disposable income account Capital account The rest of the world account Unit Coverage: The national accounts data cover all resident units. Workers who work up to one year in another country are considered residents. Transaction Coverage: All non-financial transactions are covered by national accounts data. Exceptions to coverage The data do not cover the territory of Transnistria region (the left side of the Nistru river and municipality Bender). Unrecorded activity Activities «missing» from the basic information and used for compiling national accounts, either because they are hidden activity, illegal, informal or that is production of households for own use or as a result of shortcomings in the system of data collection, are defined as non-observed economy and their inclusion in the national accounts is called measurement of non-observed economy. Estimates are produced by economic activities and sectors (except for the illegal sector)- formal, informal, household production for own consumption, on quarterly and annual basis . 2.4.1 Valuation The valuation rules for recording flows and stocks are in accordance with the SNA-UN- 2008/ESA-2010. Market prices are used for the valuation of flows and stocks. Output is valued at basic prices and intermediate consumption is valued at purchase prices excluding the deductible part of the value added taxes. Total exports and imports are valued on a f.o.b. basis. Transactions in foreign currency are converted to the national currency using the daily average exchange rate. All calculations are made at current prices, prices of the base year, and at prices of the previous year. The reference period is quarter and calendar year. 2.4.2 Recording basis Transactions are registered at the moment the operations are produced. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures Information regarding income, turnover presented by units in statistical and financial reports are based on calculation method. Income and Expenditure of National Public Budget(Ministry of Finance) is recorded on cash basis. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Data from different sources are used to develop the accounts: 1. Statistical surveys carried out by NBS: AnnualStructural Survey of Enterprises; HouseholdsBudgets Survey Earnings LaborForce Survey Balance ofagricultural production Annual and infraannual surveys on industry, constructions, services, trade Population Census data 2. Administrative sources Information developed and supplied by the Ministry of Finance: the National Public Budget structured in the State Budget, the Territorial-Administrative Units' Budget, State Social Insurance Budget, Compulsory Health Insurance Funds; Data provided by National Bank of Moldova and other banking institutions (balance of payments etc.); Information developed and supplied by the State Tax Service and Customs Service; Information regarding the financial market activity developed and supplied by the National Commission for Financial Markets; Financial(accounting) reports of entities, collected, processed, and centralized by NBS; Other sources of administrative data supplied by local and central public authorities. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Definitions, data sources, scope, classifications, time of recording, reference periods, and valuation of the national accounts follow international standards. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness The timeliness of National Accounts data meets requirements. 3.2.1 Source data assessment Source data are subjected to a series control, which is made at the level of economic unit. Databases are crosschecked from different data sources, thus being checked the accuracy of data presentation, and the methodology of indicators. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques National Accounts combine data from different statistical sources. Data collection techniques vary a lot, depending on compilation method, on available data sources, on timeliness of dissemination etc. Thus: Statistical surveys are based on samples Financial reports of economic units are collected exhaustively Administrative sources are presented to NBS on the basis of agreements of data exchange At the compilation of GDP when using 3 approaches, in current prices, are used all data sources – statistical surveys, financial results, banking systems, administrative sources. Compilation of GDP through production and use approach, in constant prices, is made by deflating or extrapolating. At deflation, are used consumer prices indices, price indices for industrial production, agriculture, construction etc.. At extrapolation, quantitative indicators are used (number of employees). 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Specific quarterly compilation techniques Quarterly calculations of GDP are seasonally adjusted using soft DEMETRA and method TRAMO-SEATS. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results The data are compared and analyzed on internal, temporal consistency and with other data sources. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses The main methodological revisions are documented, and the reasons for the revision are explained to the users. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request NBS makes available for users' additional statistical information beyond the data presented in the statistical publications, news releases, analytical reports placed on the official website, in the limits of available information, in line with the Law on Official Statistics.
