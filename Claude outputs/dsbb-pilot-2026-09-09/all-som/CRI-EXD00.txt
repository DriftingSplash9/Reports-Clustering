 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The Costa Rica's statement is compiled according to international standards and conventions described in the sixth edition of the Balance of Payments Manual and International Investment Position ( BPM6 ) 2009 of the International Monetary Fund (IMF) and the External Debt Statistics: Guide for Compilers and Users 2013. Analytical framework: The basic structure of Costa Rica's external debt consists of the statistical statement that presents the value and composition of financial liabilities of the public and private sector with non-residents at period-end. Liability stocks are group into institutional units and the instruments as to enhance the analytical usefulness of the data. Institutional units are grouped into institutional sectors, and financial instruments are classified by their nature into instrument categories. The institutional units are as fallow: General Government, Central Bank, Deposit-Taking Corporations, except the Central Bank and Other sectors. The instruments categories used are: special drawing rights (allocations), currency and deposits, debt securities, loans, trade credit and advances and other debt liabilities. Scope of the data The external debt statistics record stocks of liabilities between residents and non-residents other than capital shares. Residents are defined as the institutional units that hold its economic interest in the Costa Rican territory. Coverage of the stocks: The stocks hold by the financial system, the General Government and non-financial public enterprises are fully covered, since the AESE has access to financial enterprises statements as well as to the Debt Management and Financial Analysis System (DMFAS) of the Ministry of Finance. Stocks recorded in colones are converted into dollars using the closing date exchange rate for the reporting period. Valuation: Stocks of liabilities are recorded at market prices. When this price is not known, the historical price is used. 2.4.2 Recording basis Unit of account: Costa Rica's external debt is recorded in U.S. dollars. All stocks based on surveys are recorded according to the time the respondents made the payment or when the liability accounts were transacted in. All data from banks correspond to the records of financial statements, on an accrual basis. All records on the general government and state institutions correspond to the time of budget execution. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures Following the recommendations of BPM6, the recorded of financial account is done on a net basis, separately for liabilities. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The data sources used to compile external debt included in the financial account are considered adequate. The main sources to compile these accounts are: • External debt data provided by Ministry of Finance; are available directly at the BCCR through the Debt Management and Financial Analysis System (DMFAS) of the Ministry of Finance. • AEMF information about external liabilities of the Costa Rican financial system. • Surveys for FDI firms and domestic firms that hold liabilities with nonresidents. These surveys include items for the components of the financial account, including the external debt of the nonfinancial private sector. • The set of accounts, reported by each financial institution to SUGEF does not contain the classification used by the AESE. However, the information is requested throughout an annex form the financial institutions report directly to BCCR, this includes loans to nonresidents. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording The main sources used to compile the components of the external debt of Costa Rica are: • External debt data provided by Ministry of Finance, available directly in the BCCR through DMFAS. However, an adjustment to for residence principle must be added, which is necessary to adjust the stock of foreign bonds of the public sector hold by residents. • Information of the Area of Monetary and Financial Statistics (AEMF) about of the external debt stocks of the financial system in Costa Rica. • Financial supervised institutions to adjust the holdings of Costa Rican debt held by residents. • Data from the Clearing House is used to adjust domestically issue debt when the holder is identified as a non-resident as external debt • Surveys for FDI and domestic firms, with holdings to nonresidents. Data collection and processing timetables adequately meet the standards of timeliness and periodicity recommended for disseminating external debt. In some cases, however, owing to the periodicity of the source data or established deadlines, preliminary figures are published based on estimates that are revised as soon as the next round of data processing is in place. The process of conciliation with the rest of the world account takes longer (up to three years of the reference year) but the NA team is working on the reduction of the lag. 3.2.1 Source data assessment The AIE has prepared procedural manual that document the management, processing, and quality control of the annual and quarterly balance of payments and international investment position surveys (annual and quarterly); these surveys include information about external debt. The manuals are for internal use and are also used to train new staff in the AIE and the ESE. The surveys are designed to be easy to complete and process electronically. A series of measures have been established to control quality and to conduct appropriate and timely verification of the information provided in the surveys before and after their electronic processing. The ESE validates the responses to ensure the inter-sectorial, historic and statistical consistency of the data. Information about non-sampling errors is monitored, including under-coverage, misclassification, and nonresponse. The AIE and the ESE identify outliers and other atypical differences in periodic responses by reporters. Extreme values are confirmed with respondents. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques In general, solid procedures to compile data are used. Complementary information is employed in cases of nonresponse and for the elaboration of estimates. The annual survey is a sample that includes the most representative companies subject to the general regime and those operating under special regimes. Because it is a sample based on pure identification criteria, the sampling value cannot be determined. However, the representativeness of the results of the survey is evaluated by comparison with the principal components of the international external debt. The quarterly surveys on the other hand, are samples that have increased the coverage from 100 enterprises in 2001 to 384 in 2023. Also, the annual survey works on a sample of 150 additional respondents. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Currently, no partner-country data are used to adjust trade data. The BCCR makes no seasonal adjustment of the external debt. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results Information reported in the financial press is used to verify direct investment and other stocks, such as debt issues abroad by residents. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data The BCCR is responsible for the quality of the data regarding all external sector statistics. Through a series of consultative structures, the quality of the intermediate data is assessed by the AESE in coordination with AEE. In principle, EXD is consistent with the IIP because they are compiled from the same source data. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs The EXD results are checked against the IIP to validate or detect any inadequacies in regular data sources. The financial data from the Central Bank and the national banking system are checked in their consistency with the monetary and financial statistics. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses There are no formal studies to assess revisions to the external debt statistics. However, the BCCR is constantly seeking to improve its statistical processes, and it takes into account the recommendations of external experts who have provided technical assistance. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses The factors that generate differences between preliminary estimates and the final data are evaluated and explained to the authorities of BCCR and users who request them. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request Non-published and non-confidential data on the EXD, are disseminated on request to the Central Bank authorities. The BCCR provides the external debt statistics on a regular basis in a customized version to the World Bank/QEDS. 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points The BCCR provides assistance to users that require information on external sector indicators and statistics.
