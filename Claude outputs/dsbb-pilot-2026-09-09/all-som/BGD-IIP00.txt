 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Definition: International Investment position (IIP) data compiled and disseminated on quarterly basis by Bangladesh Bank (BB) in accordance with the principals and methodology recommended in the sixth edition of the IMF " Balance of Payments & International Investment Position Manual". IIP data are compiled and disseminated on quarterly basis. Data are presented in millions of taka. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Classification system: As per the BPM6 The components of the IIP are grouped according to the assets and liabilities of the country which includes Direct investment broken down by equity including reinvested earnings and debt instruments, Portfolio investment broken down by Equity securities and Debt securities and Other investment trade credits, loans, currency and deposits, other accounts receivable and as well as data on foreign reserve assets of the country. 2.2.1 Scope Classification system: As per the BPM6 The components of the IIP are grouped according to the assets and liabilities of the country which includes Direct investment broken down by equity including reinvested earnings and debt instruments, Portfolio investment broken down by Equity securities and Debt securities and Other investment trade credits, loans, currency and deposits, other accounts receivable and as well as data on foreign reserve assets of the country. Scope of the data Coverage of the IIP of Bangladesh : external sector financial assets and liabilities covers all sectors recommended in the BPM6 and their external financial assets and liabilities. The economic territory consists of the geographic territory, administered by the Government of Bangladesh, and non-resident counter-part countries/regions : United Kingdom; USA; Canada; Japan; European Union; other European countries; member countries of the Organization of Islamic Conference; member countries of ASEAN; other Asian countries; and the rest of the world. Exceptions to coverage Nil Unrecorded activity No estimates are made for smuggling or other illegal flows. 2.4.1 Valuation Valuation: Transactions are recorded as per market price. Revaluations of foreign exchange positions and gold holdings are calculated daily. Data are presented in crores of taka (1 crore = 10 million). Gross international reserves cover only the foreign assets of the Bangladesh Bank. Daily data on reserves, covering monetary gold and foreign exchange, are prepared for internal government use. The reserves data in terms of US dollars are converted into taka at the weighted average interbank rates of the Taka vis-à-vis the US dollar. Reserves in other currencies are converted into taka using relevant cross rates to the US dollar. External Debt data are compiled and disseminated in USD on the reference quarter ended date. A s a part of External Debt, debt securities are valued at book value. 2.4.2 Recording basis IIP Data are compiled on quarterly basis and the reference period is end of March, June, September and December. IIP statement, the components are compiled from quarterly stock data. If stock data are not available, closing position is estimated based on opening position, transactions during the period and other changes. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures As per BPM6. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Data sources: Data collected by the Bangladesh Bank (BB) from all the available sources on foreign assets and liabilities, viz. External Debt, International Reserves and Foreign Currency Liquidity and balance of Payments etc., and are integrated together to compile the International Investment Data. Direct investment: Based on FDI survey data (compiled by the Statistics Department, BB as per CDIS guidelines). Portfolio investment: Based on portfolio investment survey data (compiled by the SD, BB as per CPIS guidelines). Other investment: Assets & Liabilities Statement submitted by banks, Head office of Bangladesh Scheduled banks, External debt statistics etc., records maintained by SD, BB etc., through quarterly surveys . Reserve assets: Data on reserve assets are obtained from the BB, supplemented by information from the IMF on Reserve Position in the Fund. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Sectorization and classifications are done as per BPM6. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness In case of monthly data, all data are available within 2-3 weeks after the reference period. In case of quarterly data, all data are available within 6 weeks after the reference period 3.2.1 Source data assessment Data collection procedure is the process of census not on the basis of sample survey. The IIP Team regularly assesses source data and make sure that the data is correct. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Data are collected through Enterprise Data Warehouse (EDW). It is run by Oracle software. There are many Rationalized Input Templates (RIT) which are filled and uploaded by authorized dealers using central bank's website. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures For compilation of direct investment, flow data are reported by enterprises through commercial banks (i.e., hard copy as well as soft copy) on quarterly basis used to arrive stock position at the quarter end (data are adjusted for exchange rate changes); Other items are compiled based on various returns submitted to the SD, BB by based on various surveys; 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results All data submitted by different sources is subject to editing, verifying the validity of the data where banking transaction records collected through the foreign exchange transactions reporting system (exchange control records) are analyzed by Statistics Department of the Bangladesh Bank (BB) prior to use in compiling the accounts. After getting outputs, all components are checked and data are validated in consultation with data providers. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data All discrepancies are removed in consultation with data providers. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs Prior to dissemination we make sure that all outputs are correct. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses If any revised data are available we prepare new outputs and make necessary corrections in International Investment Position data.
