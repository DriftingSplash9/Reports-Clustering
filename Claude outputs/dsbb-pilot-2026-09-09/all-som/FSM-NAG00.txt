 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Gross Domestic Product (GDP) estimates for the FSM are compiled, in both constant and current prices, and by both institutional sectors and income components, according to the United Nations, System of National Accounts 2008 . 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization In the FSM’s national accounts statistics, the following institutional sectors are recognized: private enterprise, public enterprise, finance (banks), government, NGOs, and households. In addition, the national accounts statistics are available state-wise—namely, for each of the four states mentioned above. 2.2.1 Scope In the FSM’s national accounts statistics, the following institutional sectors are recognized: private enterprise, public enterprise, finance (banks), government, NGOs, and households. In addition, the national accounts statistics are available state-wise—namely, for each of the four states mentioned above. Scope of the data The national accounts statistics of the FSM cover all the four states of the FSM—namely, Kosrae, Pohnpei, Chuuk, and Yap. 2.4.1 Valuation GDP estimates are compiled at producer and purchaser prices. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs GDP estimates are compiled (primarily based on income approach) from the following source data: quarterly social security data; annual Customs and Tax Administration (CTA)’s Business Gross Revenue Tax (BGRT) data; audited reports of the Federal and State governments; reports for public enterprises; HIES; Census reports; Payroll data; and CPI data. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques One technique used is application of industry-wise fixed ratios on gross revenues of private sector companies to estimate operating surpluses. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses No revision studies are prepared. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses As the national accounts statistics are not revised, no revision studies are prepared.
