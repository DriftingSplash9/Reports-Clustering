 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The Coordinated Portfolio Investment Survey (CPIS) is a voluntary data collection compiled by the Central Bank of Chile, conducted under the auspices of the IMF that collects an economy's data on its holdings of portfolio investment securities (data are separately requested for equity and investment fund shares, long-term debt instruments, and short-term debt instruments). The survey also shows breakdown by country and institutional sector. The CPIS has been compiled annually for the period 2001 to 2012 and semi-annually from 2013. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Classification system: It is based mainly on the Sixth edition of the BOP manual and CPIS guidelines. 2.2.1 Scope Classification system: It is based mainly on the Sixth edition of the BOP manual and CPIS guidelines. Scope of the data Coverage: The CPIS considers external portfolio investment assets. 2.4.1 Valuation Most positions are recorded directly in US dollars, or converted from other foreign currencies to US dollars using end of period market exchange rates. Regarding valuation, in general market values are used. 2.4.2 Recording basis 100% 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures Grossing/netting procedures are broadly consistent with BPM6 standards. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs CPIS: Primary sources for assets are forms sent to the Central Bank by domestic banks, the government and private sector domestic investors, and information disseminated by regulatory authorities (pension funds and others). 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording In general, source data for CPIS approximate statistical requirements for the IIP. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Timeliness of data meets SDDS requirements. 3.2.1 Source data assessment For Pension funds Superintendecy provide us of all data In most cases where database management procedures exist; computerized edit checks are used to identify coding and other errors in source data, and to minimize processing errors of editing and coding data of companies. Also, surveys are assessed and revised periodically to meet new requirements. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Most of the data derived from primary sources are validated and adjusted to improve the coverage. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request Requests for unpublished but non-confidential statistics are evaluated on a case by case basis, according to guidelines established by resolution number 1504-01-091008. Requests should be addressed to the Central Bank’s General Manager. 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points A contact person is identified on the DSBB.
