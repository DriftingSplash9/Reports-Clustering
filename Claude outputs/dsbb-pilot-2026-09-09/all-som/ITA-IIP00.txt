 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Concepts: in line with the sixth edition of the IMF Balance of Payments and International Investment Position Manual (BPM6) methodological requirements and the ECB Guideline and EUROSTAT Regulation. Definitions: A balance sheet of the stock of external financial assets and liabilities (claims on non-residents, liabilities to non-residents, monetary gold and SDRs). 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Positions are classified consistently with the BPM6. The sector breakdown is fully consistent with the ECB Guideline; the following sectors are identified: central bank, deposit-taking corporations except the central bank, money market funds, general government, other sectors, non financial corporations, households and NPISH, financial corporations other than MFIs. Direct investment: This item includes equity capital and assets/liabilities between affiliated enterprises. Cross-border positions involving real estates and constructions are also included. Portfolio investment: This item comprises positions in equity securities, debt securities and money market instruments that are not included in direct investment and reserve assets. Financial derivatives: The item includes assets/liabilities positions in financial derivatives and employee stock options. Other investment: This item covers all the financial assets/liabilities not recorded in the other items. Allocation of SDRs are included in coherence with BPM6 methodology. Reserve assets: They include highly liquid, marketable and creditworthy non-euro area currency denominated claims on non-residents of the euro area, as well as monetary gold, special drawing rights and reserve position in the Fund. Scope of the data In principle, Italy's international investment position statistics cover all resident-non resident positions. Resident institutional units are defined in conformity with the BPM6 's concepts of economic territory and centre of economic interest. Exceptions to coverage 2.4.1 Valuation Type of prices: Sector "Monetary authorities": listed securities are valued at end-of-month market prices; all other securities are valued at historical cost or fair value. Sectors "General government"/ "Other sectors": listed securities are valued at the market prices prevailing at the end of the reference period. Unlisted securites market values are estimated by means of information derived from Monetary financial institutions balance sheets. Sector "Other Monetary and Financial Institutions" (MFIs): positions in listed and unpledged securities are valued at end-of-month market prices; all other securities are valued at the historical cost or at the fair value). Conversion to unit of account: Assets and liabilities denominated in foreign currency are converted into the unit of account at end-of-period (midpoint) exchange rates. Reserve assets: Monetary gold is valued at end-of-month market prices (fixing 3 p.m.; London Market). 2.4.2 Recording basis 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Sectors "Central bank" and "Deposit taking corporations except the central bank": Pure stock figures for assets and liabilities (with the exception of PI liabilities) are available on a monthly basis from balance-sheet data. Information is broken down by type of asset/liability, currency, maturity and debtor/creditor country. Assets in securities are reported on a security-by security basis. Sectors "General government": With the exception of PI assets/laibilities stock data are calculated by cumulating flows to a benchmark, taking into account exchange rates and market prices. The benchmark for other items is based on 1988 stock data that were gathered by Banca d'Italia. Sector "Other sectors": as from the second half of 2010 the main source of data is represented by the results of the "Direct reporting", a set of surveys in non-financial and financial transactions/position of a sample of insurance and non-financial corporations. As from 2016 for resident households data on financial assets held abroad are mainly derived from Italian Revenue Agency reports. For PI assets/liabilities the main sources of data are monthly s-b-s indirect reports by custodians, including the Italian Central Securities Depositary, complemented with direct reporting by Monetary financial institutions on their own assets and with estimates of securities directly held in custody abroad as derived from a sample of insurance and non-financial enterprises. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording 3.1.3 Source data timeliness In general source data are timely as regards the compilation of international investment position statistics. The Bank of Italy (BI) and other banks report their positions within 12 working days after the end of the reference month. Results of monthly "Direct reporting" surveys are available within 1 month after the end of the reference period. Results of the annual "Direct reporting" survey on stocks data are available 6 months after the end of the reference year. 3.2.1 Source data assessment The population of reporting banks, an essential element in the Italian data collection system, is precisely defined in the framework of ECB regulations addressing the reporting population for monetary policy purposes. A long-standing relationship with the banking community derives also from the supervisory functions which the BI has the institutional task to undertake on monetary and financial institutions. Reports from banks and nonbanks are automatically checked for internal consistency. BI provides respondents with free software supporting electronic reporting with build-in accounting checks. A full set of quality checks are re-run. In case of wrong or implausible returns, the respondent is contacted for solving the problem. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures For General Government, assets and liabilities other than Portfolio investment and Financial derivatives are calculated by cumulating flows to a benchmark, taking into account changes due to movements in exchange rates and market prices. Other investment: Bond lending and repos are considered as collateralized loans and included in other investment. Financial derivatives: Data are recorded on a gross basis. No valuation at market price is performed. Estimates of reinvested earnings are included under direct investment assets/liabilities. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results A number of validations on intermediate data and statistical outputs are in place. Time series checks are performed for each bank and for main items. A number of checks is performed on the results of "Direct reporting" surveys. These include time series and cross-sections checks and some external checks with balance-sheet information. Cross-border holdings of the banks are checked against the statistics based on the consolidated balance sheets. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses Revisions are stored and assessed on an ongoing basis. In case of revisions following a major break in the approach followed to collect the data, surveys are addressed to anticipate the likely direction of the revision. In some cases the studies are made available with advance communications to public. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses Studies and analyses of revision are made available only when major methodological changes occur, or when there is a break due to a new collection system. This is done by a press-release followed by an explanation of the reason underlying the change and the expected impact on the data. 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points In the BI's Statistical database a dedicated e-mail address can be used for any questions on the disseminated data.
