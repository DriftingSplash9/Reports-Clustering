 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions In general, the structure of the balance of payments statistics (BOP) conforms with the “ Balance of Payments Manual” , 6th edition (BPM 6) guidelines. The standard structure of the BOP includes the following: Full conformity Current account : exports and imports of goods and services, income, and current incomes Full conformity Capital account: capital transfers; Full conformity Financial account: direct investments in Tajikistan and abroad, assets and liabilities for portfolio investment, other investments, as well as changes in international reserve assets and net errors and omissions. Balance of Payments is a statistical statement reflecting the economic transactions between residents and non-residents during a specific period of time (quarter, year). 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The classification/sectorization system in general follows the standard components presentation of the BPM6 in terms of the institutional units, financial instruments, and maturities of financial instruments. 2.2.1 Scope The classification/sectorization system in general follows the standard components presentation of the BPM6 in terms of the institutional units, financial instruments, and maturities of financial instruments. Scope of the data The BOP statistics covers all types of transactions between the residents and nonresidents. Estimates are applied for non-recorded foreign transactions of individuals. In 2001 new reporting forms for survey of enterprises with foreign investments were developed and introduced jointly with the Statistical Agency. New reporting forms for importers also were developed and introduced to estimate the cost of freight and insurance. The entire country is covered All foreign transactions of all economic sectors are covered in the BOP statistics. For BOP purposes, the NBT uses a comprehensive register of all operating banks and enterprises, regardless of their form of ownership and whether or not they conduct international transactions . Exceptions to coverage Main articles that do not cover some BOP transactions are the following: Government services. The Ministry of Finance provides a report on expenses of Tajik embassies and representations abroad. Expenses of foreign embassies and representations are recorded through banking reports, and the transactions paid through accounts held with nonresident banks and cash transactions are thus not covered Travel. There is no information on the registration of travel related transactions and currently no surveys are conducted. Methods for estimating travel costs are being developed. Compensation of employees. Since expatriate workers are not surveyed, the BOP data do not account for the amounts received by seasonal workers. Only money transfers through bank accounts are covered. 2.4.1 Valuation BOP statistics are compiled in thousands of US dollars. Transactions in national and other currencies in bank reports are converted into US dollars using the exchange rate at the time of the transaction. Transactions of enterprises and individuals, as well as transactions derived from quarterly stock data (direct and portfolio investment), are valued at end-quarter exchange rate. Transactions are, whenever possible, valued at market prices at the time the legal ownership is transferred. However, certain goods (gas, electricity, etc.) and services (railway and pipeline transport) are valued at contract prices since they are sold in over-the-counter market or in barter trade. 2.4.2 Recording basis Goods are recorded at the time of customs clearance and most services are recorded when they are rendered. For government and government-guaranteed loans, loan drawings are recorded at the time of disbursement, while interest payments are recorded on an accrual basis. Dividends on investment and interest on portfolio assets are recorded on a cash basis at the time the settlement is reported by the bank. For other loans not guaranteed by the government, principal and interest payments are recorded at the time the NBT receives respective reports from enterprises. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures Current account data are compiled and reflected on a gross basis, while capital and financial account data are recorded net. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs NBT applies integrated data collection system, including the International Transaction Reporting System (ITRS), through which reports on transactions of banks, credit organizations, enterprises, and individuals are generated, as well as administrative data within and outside NBT. The ITRS is based on detailed monthly reports on foreign transactions reflected in national currency (somoni) and US dollars. The System was introduced in 1999 and covers reports of all operating commercial banks and credit organizations. Under the ITRS, banks and credit organizations report their foreign transactions and those of other residents regardless of their participation in the transaction and whether those transactions affect their foreign positions or not. The balance of incoming and outgoing flows equals the net volume of foreign transactions. The ITRS data are mainly used to compile estimates of services, income, and private transfers of current account, as well portfolio and other investments of financial account. Reporting forms submitted by the Statistical Agency provide data on certain types of services (transport, travel, insurance, operational lease, and part of government services). Also, banks and credit organizations are required to submit reports on their foreign positions as of the beginning and end of the reporting period with a breakdown by currencies. Statistical Agency collects several reporting forms to support the BOP compilation. Principal report is the foreign investment report submitted by registered enterprises with foreign direct investment. This reporting form has been developed by NBT and was last improved in 2004, including filing instructions, for enabling data collection on equities and value indicators for international investment position. Statistical Agency also conducts the following surveys: transportation services, humanitarian aid, communication services, and construction services. In addition, Statistical Agency obtains data from the Customs Department; these data are supplemented with information from other sources and then submitted to NBT. The NBT also conducts a survey to estimate the correction factor for c.i.f/f.o.b adjustment. The Ministry of Finance provides data on external debt, budget revenue and expenditure related to nonresidents. Other NBT departments, in turn, provide data on IMF credit and other international financial institutions, international reserves, foreign exchange positions of NBT, depositary and other financial organizations in foreign currencies. Exports and imports of goods : Official statistics on foreign trade is based on customs declarations that are collected by the Customs Department. Later these data are finalized by the Statistical Agency. Foreign trade data are supplemented with the value of repairs and goods acquired by transportation companies abroad. These data are obtained from the reports of transportation companies. Services: Transportation : There is no data on private freight and passenger transportation. Travel : Travel data is partly provided by the Statistics Agency Income : Statistics on compensation of employees is derived from the ITRS and statistical reporting form № 1- ИП ( Individual Entrepreneur ). The NBT estimates data on received and paid income on direct, portfolio and other investments based on the computerised reporting form No.1-АМ ( Financial Operation ) “External financial operations by economic agents” on a quarterly basis. The Accounting and Reporting Department provides data on income from the management of international reserves of NBT. The MOF provides quarterly data on interest payments on government and guaranteed by the government debt. The Private External Debt Division provides data on accrued interest on non-guaranteed private external debt. Current transfers : Data on humanitarian aid are provided by the Customs Department, while information on Tajikistan’s membership fees to various international organizations is provided by the MOF. Other current transfers are estimated based on the ITRS and statistical reporting form № 1- ИП. Capital transfers : Data on capital transfers are compiled based on quarterly reports of MOF and data formed on the basis of the computerised reporting form No.1-АМ “External financial operations by economic agents” that cover data on grants and external debt forgiveness Direct investment : Data on direct investment are derived from the computerised reporting form No.1-AM “External finance operations by economic agents” on quarterly basis. The NBT collects these data from reports of direct investment enterprises that include data on financial flows, direct investment income, as well as stock data. Portfolio investment : Data on portfolio investment are derived from the computerised reporting form No.1-AM “External finance operations by economic agents” on quarterly basis. The NBT collects these data from reports of portfolio investment enterprises that include data on financial flows, direct investment income, as well as stock data. Other investment: Data on cash currency and deposits, distribution of special drawing rights (SDRs) and other receivables/payables are formed based on the monetary statistics on the NBT positions, depository and other financial organizations, and also based on the data of the Report “On Cash Flow of Enterprise on Foreign Banks Accounts” of the Statistical Agency. Data on loans and credits are formed on the basis of the computerised reporting form No. 1-AM “External financial operations by economic agents”, as well as the reports developed by the Ministry of Finance on attracted loans from the Government. Data on trade credits and advances are formed based on the Statistical Agency's Report No.1-Finances “On External Receivables and Payables of Enterprises”. Data on insurance technical reserves are based on data from insurance company reports. International reserves : Data are provided by the International Reserves Management Department and the NBT exchange rate policy. The statistics of gross international reserves do not include international reserves placed held by Ministry of Finance. In this regards, the draft law “On the National Bank of the Republic of Tajikistan” was amended by the National Bank. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Full conformity. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Ninety days after the reporting period 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques In general, source data reasonably approximate required definitions, scope, classification, valuation, and time of recording. The NBT has developed the following specific procedures for obtaining final estimates based on various sources. Exports and imports of goods : Data on exports, imports and humanitarian aid are provided by the Customs Department through a monthly report. Statistical Agency provides data on merchandise trade taking into account the adjustment for exports/imports of electricity and gas. Main adjustment applied to customs data is related to non-recorded imports. NBT applies adjustments on imports of goods to reflect imports from CIF prices to FOB based on additional information received from the Customs Service according to customs regimes and features. Services : The c.i.ff.o.b. adjustment of imports related to transportation services of nonresidents is added to the imports of transportation services. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results Possible mistakes and data inconsistencies in the reports of banks are highlighted through an automated system that compares new data with past trends as the reports are received. These inconsistencies then are checked and reconciled with banks. The data provided by Statistical Agency are cross-checked with the reports for previous periods and similar enterprise reports of corresponding industry and then are discussed with the Statistical Agency. Similar checks and reconciliations are also applied for administrative data. In case of two data sources, NBT cross-checks one with the other. For instance, data on direct foreign investments from bank reports are cross-checked with the survey data. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs Net errors and omissions are analyzed to monitor developments in their size when their volume reaches 10 percent of the total of credits and debits of the current account.
