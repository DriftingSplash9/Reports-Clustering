 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Official Reports of Budget Statement to the Cabinet and Parliament are based on Myanmar Accounting Method and Reporting Format. The Budget Department of MOPF compiles Myanmar Government Finance Statistics according to the Government Finance Statistics Manual 2014 (GFSM 2014 cash basis) methodology for dissemination. The classifications of the government accounts are not the same with the classifications of GFS M 2014. The bridge tables have to construct between National Classifications and IMF’s GFS classification. By constructing the bridge tables, it meets most the requirements for reporting data (cash basis) according to the GFSM 2014 classifications. But the grouping of outlays lacks consistency in the classification of payments for acquisition of non-financial assets. The classification of projects is by status, instead of by the type of asset to be acquired. The classification lacks consistent coding to allow computerized processing of the transactions. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The scope of the Central Government operations data produced by the MOPF, BD is defined by the consolidation of Central Government subsector which include Budgetary Government subsector, Extra Budgetary subsector and Social Security Fund subsector. When Central Government transactions are consolidated, they are the equivalent of the Central Government sector concept used in GFSM 2014 Cash Basic. Budgetary Government subsector refer to the State Administrative Organizations and Union Ministries and Departments comprising 25 ministries with 133 departments and Nay Pyi Taw Council) Extra Budgetary subsector refers to as 9 Cantonment Municipalities, Nay Pyi Taw Development Committee. Social Security Fund Subsector refers to as Social Security Board. Annual Preliminary data are derived from administrative data which substitute to replace on Temporary Actual data(TA), Revise Estimate (RE) and Budget Estimate(BE). Annual Preliminary data are replaced in the next issue if revised information becomes available. 2.4.1 Valuation Transactions are generally valued at market prices. All data are reported to the Budget Department in local currency with foreign currency transactions converted to local currency using exchange rates prevalent at the time of the transaction for Temporary Actual and Provisional Actual. 2.4.2 Recording basis The accounting procedures broadly follow the cash basis, but Foreign Grant is recorded not only in cash but also in kind and in services. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures The Accounting System practice for recording the “gross” and “net” recording are similar with the “gross” and “net” recording guidelines of the GFSM 2014 . Corrective transactions and repayments are netted in the original transactions as far as practically possible. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Key data sources for annual fiscal statistics are from based on the data of the Budget Proposals. In the Budget Proposals, line agencies submit the budget proposals for the following year, revised estimate for the current Year, and the provisional actual of the previous year according to prescribed forms and budget classifications. The ministries provide the MOPF with three datasets: (i) Temporary actuals of monthly, quarterly and annual are reported to respective section of the Budget Department; (ii) Provisional actuals are reported to respective section of the Budget Department; and (iii) Actuals are reported to the Accounting and Reporting Division of the Treasury Department. The difference between these data sets is that the actual data are reconciled data. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording As above 3.1.3 Source data timeliness The reporting arrangements for the annual data from respective agencies to the Budget Department depend on timeline of requesting the budget proposals for the following year. Budget Department compiles annual fiscal data based on the respective agencies submission. The Provisional Actual Data is about 12-month lag. The key reason for the late production of fiscal data is delay encountered by the ministries, departments, and agencies in providing their financial data to the MOPF. The Temporary Actual is usually compiled within two to three weeks after the end of each month. The Actual data have two or more months of delay. There are more than 350 township primary accounting units, and some of them produce and mail their monthly financial reports to their respective line ministry with delay. Upon urgent request, the primary accounting units report their data by fax. In addition, there are delays in bank reconciliation processes because of lack of sufficient computerization in banks. The delays are related to insufficient number of computers and staff training and capacity building constraints. Internet is still slow in some areas. Line ministries have embarked on computerization of accounting and reporting processes in the center, state, and region levels, and some part of township offices. 3.2.1 Source data assessment All agencies are required to report within the deadline and need to focus on accuracy of data. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques As above 5.1.5 Dissemination on request The simultaneous dissemination of the data on Ministry of Planning and Finance’s website.
