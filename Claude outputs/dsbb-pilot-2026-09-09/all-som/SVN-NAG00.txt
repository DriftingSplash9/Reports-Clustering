 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Analytical Framework: The methodology follows the ESA 2010 . Defined and classified according to the European System of National and Regional Accounts 2010 .(ESA 2010) . /Regulation of the European Parliament and of the Council No. 549/2013 of 21 May 2013/ Definition: Gross domestic product measures the market value of the goods and services produced by all resident units engaged in productive activity before deduction of provisions for the consumption of fixed capital. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Classification systems: According to ESA 2010. Scope of the data Transaction coverage: The production boundary is in accordance with ESA 2010. Geographic coverage: Nationwide. Unrecorded activity Unrecorded activity: In general the level of underreporting, as established in the annual accounts, is extrapolated on the basis of developments in the corresponding recorded activity. Illegal activities are included in GDP estimate. 2.4.1 Valuation Valuation: Follows the rules in ESA 2010 . 2.4.2 Recording basis Time of recording: Accrual basis. Recording period: C alendar quarters. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs 3.1.1 Source data collection programs When compiling quarterly accounts much effort is made to use the same sources and calculation methods as in the annual accounts. However, the basic data available in quarterly accounts are less comprehensive and often provisionally. When preparing the estimates of national accounts aggregates at a quarterly level all the data sources that are available on monthly or quarterly basis are used. For the majority of the estimates the results of basic statistical surveys conducted by SURS are used. Beside the statistical surveys also the data from other sources are used: balance of payments from the Bank of Slovenia, data provided by the Financial administration of the RS, Ministry of Finance, Ministry of the Interior, and other sources. The most significant data sources in the quarterly accounts are: Monthly survey in industry : It covers enterprises and their units performing one or more activities from the fields of B Mining and quarrying, C Manufacturing and Electricity supply (part of D) based on the sample. Units in the sample are selected based on the number of persons in paid employment (at least 18) and yearly turnover. Monthly survey in construction : It covers construction enterprises and enterprises that engage in the organisation of the execution of building projects, and their units which engage in construction or execution of building projects and other enterprises performing construction works based on the sample. Units in the sample are selected based on turnover for VAT purposes. Monthly survey on number of overnight stays : It includes enterprises, companies as well as other organisations offering accommodation services to tourists. Monthly survey in services : It covers enterprises whose principal activity is classified into accommodation and food service activities, transportation and storage, information and communication, professional and technical activities and other administrative and support service activities. Data refer to the whole enterprise, including eventual secondary activity. Transport statistics data are obtained by several monthly surveys, where reporting units are enterprises or their parts that are engaged in transport activity. Data on road transport are collected from the units which are included into the survey according to the turnover in a certain period in the past and according to the number of employees. Data on railway transport are reported by Slovenske zeleznice (Slovenian Railways), the data on postal services by Posta Slovenije (Post of Slovenia), and the telephone communications by Telekom Slovenije (Slovenian Telekom). Units registered for carrying out individual transport activities report on the urban passenger transport and air transport and re-loading. Quarterly performance of enterprises by activity : It covers non-financial enterprises (companies, branches of foreign companies and cooperatives) in Slovenia classified in all activities based on the sample. Covered are all units with more than 49 employees and a sample of units with 49 or fewer employees. Statistical Register of Employment : The Register is updated from M-forms, i.e. Registration data for introduction and monitoring of records on retirement and disability insurance and health insurance (records on stipulated employment). Monthly survey on earnings : The survey covers all companies, enterprises and organisations on the territory of Slovenia except private enterprises with only one or two persons in paid employment. Deflators: producer price indices (domestic, non-domestic market) consumer price indices, imports and exports unit value indices, import price indices, services producer price indices, construction cost indices, producer price indices of agricultural products, agricultural input price indices. External trade statistics : The data source for external trade statistics are data from Intrastat system (trade in goods within EU) and customs declarations, which are provided monthly by the Financial administration of the RS. In external trade statistics, exports of goods are valued f.o.b. and imports of goods are valued c.i.f. Balance of Payments : Balance of payments is compiled by the Bank of Slovenia on the basis of the International Monetary Fund's recommendations (Balance of Payments Manual, 6th Edition – BPM6). The BoP is compiled on the basis of the following sources: ITRS (International Transactions' Reporting System), Intrastat system, customs declarations, reports to the Bank of Slovenia, accounting data of the Bank of Slovenia, commercial banks' balance sheet data and estimates. The balance of general government (by subsectors: S.1311 Central government, S.1313 Local governments, S.1314 Social security funds) revenues and expenditures : It is published monthly by the Ministry of Finance. VAT data and excise duties : Data on value added tax are provided quarterly by the Financial administration of the RS. Quarterly balance sheets of insurance companies and banks , provided by the Supervisory Insurance Agency and the Bank of Slovenia. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Output approach : Value added is estimated for more than 85 branches of activity, which are than aggregated for publication. For estimation of value added by activities several methods are used, namely single extrapolation of value added, interpolation/trend extrapolation of annual data and double indicator method. Agriculture, hunting and forestry : The index of the physical volume of agricultural production is used for the estimation of the quarterly movements of value added. Current price estimates are obtained by reflating constant price estimates. Fishing: Value added by quarters is mainly estimated using employment as an indicator. Mining and quarrying : Value added is estimated with value/volume of industrial production. Manufacturing: Value added is estimated with value/volume of industrial production. Electricity, gas and water supply : Estimates of value added are prepared on the basis of data provided by the quarterly survey and relevant price indices. Construction : The movement of value added by quarters is estimated on the basis of the data on the value of construction works done from the monthly survey in construction. For deflation the construction price index is used. Wholesale and retail trade; certain repair : The main source of data for the estimation of quarterly value added movement is the monthly survey on services. Index of consumer prices is used to deflate sales. Hotels and restaurants : Two indicators are used for the estimation of value added in this activity: the number of overnight stays and value/volume of turnover. Transport, storage and communications : Several indicators are used for extrapolation of value added: the number of ton kilometres, number of passenger kilometres, number of passengers transported and turnover data. Financial intermediation : Value added is estimated on the basis of several data sources, balance sheets of banks and insurance companies, employment and price indices. FISIM (fin. intermediation services indirectly measured) is separately estimated and than inputed in the total value added of financial intermediation. Other market services : Value added of housing by quarters is estimated by interpolation/trend extrapolation of annual data. Value added in casinos is estimated on the basis of turnover in casinos, where the value of turnover is estimated on the basis of turnover tax. Value added of R&D is estimated on the basis of compensation of employees and employment in this activity. Value added for other activities is estimated either on the basis of turnover or employment. Non-market services (government and NPISH): Value added in non-market services is estimated as the sum of compensation of employees, other taxes on production and consumption of fixed capital. Main data sources are GFS, employment and wages statistics. Expenditure approach: Household final consumption expenditure : Final consumption expenditure of households is estimated on the basis of various data sources, namely quarterly retail trade survey, VAT data, statistical surveys on transport, telecommunication, hotel and restaurants, data provided by the Ministry of Interior, balance of payments, and other sources. Estimates are prepared for 36 groups of products. For deflation/inflation of the estimates the consumer price index is used. Government final consumption expenditure : The government final consumption expenditure is estimated as the value of output, measured as the sum of costs (value added and intermediate consumption), less the value of incidental sales, plus purchases of market goods and services. The basic data source for government final consumption expenditure are GFS data from the Ministry of Finance. For government individual and collective services, output at constant prices is estimated as the sum of value added at constant prices and intermediate consumption, deflated by basic types of inputs. Except for transport services, output at constant prices is estimated with direct output method (extrapolation by quantity indicator: passenger-kilometres). The implicit deflators for output are used to deflate government final consumption expenditure and incidental sales. Expenditures on the goods and services that were produced by market producers and that are intended for households as social transfers in kind are deflated by individual types of goods and services by the relevant price indices. Final consumption expenditure of NPISH : The final consumption expenditure of NPISH is estimated as the value of output, measured as the sum of costs, less the value of sales of goods and services. Expenditure at constant prices is estimated by the deflation with implicit output deflator. Gross fixed capital formation is estimated on the basis of various data sources (construction statistics, external trade statistics, data provided by the Ministry of Interior and other sources). Estimates are prepared for 20 main components of assets. For conversion of current/constant price estimates to constant/current price values deflation by diferent price indices (producer price indices, construction price indices, import price indices…) is used and by components R&D and computer software, produced on own account extrapolation using employment as an quantity indicator is used. Changes in inventories are estimated on the basis of data from the survey: quarterly performance of enterprises by activity, which provides the data on the opening and closing level of inventories by types of inventories (raw materials, work-in-progress, finished goods and goods for resale). The value of inventories at the beginning and at the end of the quarter is recalculated to the average prices of the quarter, and the changes in inventories are obtained as the difference between closing and opening levels at the average prices of the quarter. Calculations are done separately for all four types of inventories and for 15 activities. The relevant price indices (PPI, construction price indices, CPI) are used as deflators for the calculation of the average prices of the period. Changes in inventories at constant prices are estimated for the same types and for the same activities as the estimations at current prices, and the same types of indices are used for deflation. Exports and imports of goods are estimated on the basis of external trade statistics data. Imports of goods, as shown in external trade statistics, is adjusted from c.i.f. to f.o.b. values at the aggregate level by the coefficient estimated by the Bank of Slovenia on the sample of customs declarations. Balance of payments is also the source for adjustment for transactions that are not statistically covered. For the deflation of exports and imports of goods, mainly genuine price indices for exports and imports are used. The data source for the estimation of the exports and imports of services is the balance of payments. For deflation of export and imports of services consumer price indices, catering price indices and exchange rates changes are used. Income approach: Compensation of employees is estimated mainly on the basis of employment and wages statistics. Taxes on production and imports are estimated on the basis of tax statistics provided by the Financial administration of the RS and the GFS data provided by the Ministry of Finance. Subsidies on production are estimated with GFS data. Gross operating surplus/mixed income is estimated as a residual item. Base year and update cycle GDP volume measures use previous year as a base year. Data on earlier bases are re-referenced by chain-linking periods using the annual overlap method. Seasonal adjustment All published series are seasonally including working days adjusted using the Tramo/Seats method. The seasonal adjustment is applied to the quarterly national accounts series. Gross domestic product and other aggregates are seasonally adjusted directly. The exception is external trade balance which is obtained as a difference between exports of goods and services, and imports of goods and services. Geographic detail The quarterly national accounts contain no geographical breakdown. None
