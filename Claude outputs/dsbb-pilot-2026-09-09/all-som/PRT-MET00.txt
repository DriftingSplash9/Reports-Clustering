 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Definition: concepts used are determined by European Regulations, in accordance with UN International Trade Statistics Concepts and Definitions. Relationship of national practices with international or regional guidelines: there are no differences in concepts, definitions, and classifications between the merchandise trade data reported in SDDS and the national standards. National standards follow European guidelines. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Classifications used: Harmonized System (HS) expanded with 6 digits to the Combined Nomenclature (8 digit) for data collection and dissemination; Standard International Trade Classification system (SITC), Revision 3 and the Classification by Broad Economic Categories (BEC), Revision 3, for dissemination; Standard International Trade Classification system (SITC), Revision 4 and the Classification by Broad Economic Categories (BEC), Revision 4, for dissemination; International Standard Industrial Classification for All Economic Activities (ISIC), Revision 3.1 Broad Economic Categories Classification (BEC); Revision 3 Standard International Trade Classification, Rev.3 Portuguese Classification of Economic Activities (CAE), Revision 2.1 which is compatible with the European NACE, Revision 1 and 1.1 and CPA 2002; Portuguese Classification of Economic Activities (CAE), Revision 3 and 4 which is compatible with the corresponding European NACE revisions. 2. Geonomenclature, nomenclature of countries published in Official Journal of the European Union. Relationship of national practices with international or regional guidelines: There are no differences in concepts, definitions, and classifications between the merchandise trade data reported in the SDDS and the national standards. The national standards follow the European guidelines. Scope of the data Transactions coverage: in general the following categories are included : barter trade, goods on consignment, goods on financial lease, goods traded between enterprises under common ownership, and others. Transactions in military goods may be hidden in the aggregates. Geographic coverage: the entire country of Portugal (including Autonomous Region of Madeira and Azores). Data can be broken down until the fourth level of the Nomenclature of Territorial Units for Statistics (Municipality). Exceptions to coverage The transactions excluded are determined by European Regulations: Implementing Regulation (EU) No. 2020/1197 of 30/07 Annex V. The following transactions are excluded in trade in goods statistics: goods moving between a Member State and its territorial enclaves (include embassies and national armed forces stationed outside the territory of the mother country) in other Member States; the host Member State and territorial enclaves of other Member States or international organizations; monetary gold and means of payment which are legal tender and securities; goods declared orally to Customs authorities which are either of a commercial nature provided that their value does not exceed the statistical threshold of 1 000 EUR or 1 000 kilograms or of a non-commercial nature; goods released for free circulation after being subject to the customs procedures of inward processing or processing under customs control. Unrecorded activity Only for Intra-EU statistics: Monthly and annually - Adjustment for below threshold transactions; Adjustments for non or late-responses. 2.4.1 Valuation Imports are valued cost, insurance, and freight (c.i.f.) and Exports valued free on board (f.o.b.). 2.4.2 Recording basis Conversion to unit of account: conversion is done using (i) the market exchange rates as of the day customs formalities are concluded for Extra-EU trade, and (ii) in general, Central Bank (or any other commercial bank) average monthly rate for Intra-EU trade outside Euro Zone. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures Not applicable. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Data concerning trade with Third Countries (Extra-EU trade) is derived from customs declarations. Consequently, statistical data on Extra-EU trade is sourced from administrative records provided by the Portuguese Tax and Customs Authority. In contrast, statistics on Intra-EU trade are compiled using data collected through the Intrastat System. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Type of data collected includes: Intra-EU trade - flow, fiscal identification operator, member state of consignment or destination, ID number of partner (VIN), country of origin, region of origin/destination (optional from 2019), mode of transport, port/airport of origin/destination, delivery terms, nature of the transaction (sale, gift, hire, loan, operational leasing or other temporary use, etc.), CN products, invoice value, statistical value (only for approximately 9% of the enterprises), quantities in net mass and supplementary units. Extra-EU trade - flow, country of destination/consignment, fiscal identification operator, country of origin on imports, region of destination, nationality of the means of transport, mode of transport at the frontier, internal mode of transport, delivery terms, nature of the transaction, customs procedure, statistical procedure, custom-house, preference on imports, container, CN products or TARIC, statistical value, quantities expressed in net and gross mass and supplementary units. Basis of country data: Intra-EU trade - country of consignment for imports, country of destination for exports and country of origin for imports and exports. Extra-EU trade - country of origin and/or consignment for imports and country of destination for exports. (“country of consignment” means the country from which the goods were initially dispatched to the importing Member State, without any halt or legal operation not inherent in their transport having occurred in an intermediate country; if such halts or legal operations have occurred, the final intermediate country shall be regarded as the country of consignment); Valuation: Imports are valued cost, insurance, and freight (c.i.f.) and exports valued free on board (f.o.b.). Time of recording: the reference month. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Timing of recording transactions: Intra-EU trade – Statistics Portugal receives the enterprises declarations according to the Intrastat system within 15 calendar days after the reference month. Extra-EU trade - Customs send the data to Statistics Portugal on a weekly basis for imports and on a daily basis for exports. Both Intra-EU and Extra-EU merchandise trade data are released within 40 days after the end of the reference month. 3.2.1 Source data assessment Intra-EU trade - intentional sample survey on the basis of the Intrastat declarations provided by the trade operators not exempted from the statistical obligation (thresholds). Intra-EU Exports microdata Exchange – being progressively incorporating this new data source into the compilation of merchandise trade statistics Extra-EU trade - collect exhaustive data on the basis of the statistical part of the "Single Administrative Document" (SAD) provided by the Customs authorities. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Monthly adjustments are made for Intrastat. Processing system: Statistics Portugal collects all Intra-EU trade data from a specific survey to enterprises, through Intrastat System. All Extra-EU trade data is collected from SADs. Customs send the data about exports by paper (1.0%) and by an automated system (99%) (STADA Automatic Processing System of Customs Order) and 0.5% of the imports by paper, and 99.5% of imports by an automated system (STADA - Automatic Processing System of Customs Order). Further processing according to an IT system developed by Statistics Portugal. Site of processing: data are processed at Statistics Portugal. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Seasonal adjustment: no seasonal adjustment is applied; Adjustment for below threshold transactions; Adjustment for non or late-responses. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results Validations are made in intermediate data for quantities, values and year-on-year growth rate. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data Reconciliation: statistical discrepancies are analyzed - contacts with the responsible for delivering information to Statistics Portugal follows the analysis of discrepancies. Asymmetries analyses, based on COMEXT data and, more recently, Intra-EU exports microdata being exchanged since 2022. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs Analysis of aggregated data by BEC, CN 2 digits level and partner country; Identification of possible “outliers” and correction of the values with error. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses Revisions are done every month, mostly due to the inclusion of new information and corrections from the respondents/enterprises. According to the revisions policy defined for international trade in goods statistics, each month is revised the same number of times (4 times). Monthly analysis are performed, including the comparison with other sources (VAT, Turnover Index, E-Invoice,...). Other complementary studies are also carried out, like mirror statistics (Intra-EU exports microdata), business statistics, etc.. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses Major revisions in methodology are noted in the relevant publications following the introduction of the change in methodology. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request Unpublished information, but not-confidential, can be requested to Statistics Portugal. 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points The User Support Service can be contacted directly by submitting a request on the website: https://www.ine.pt/xportal/xmain?xpid=INE&xpgid=ine_ped_informacao&xlang=en or via email at info@ine.pt .
