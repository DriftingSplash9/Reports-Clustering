 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Burkina Faso's old series of national accounts follows the basis recommendations of the United Nations 1993 "System of National Accounts" ("SNA"). The quasi-definitive accounts of the new base year 2015 according to 2008 SNA are available and will be finalized before the end of 2018, the time to take in account the recommendations made by experts from IMF, World Bank, AFRISTAT, WAEMU during an international workshop held in November 2018. The accounts of the first current year 2016 will also be available at the same period. All the three approaches preconized in the SNA are used to produce GDP estimates, namely the production, the expenditure and the income approaches. National accounts are estimated at current prices (nominal) and constant prices with chained indexes: reference year for price each time. 115 sectors of activity and 203 products are used to compile the current series starting form 2015 with ERETES Software. . The accounts published include the Supply and Use Table (SUT) and the Integrated Economics Accounts Table. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The classification of economic activities used is an adaptation of the "International Standard Industrial Classification of all Economic Activities" (ISIC, Rev. 4). The classification of products is derived from CPC Rev.2. The institutional sector classification is that of the 2008 SNA. 2.2.1 Scope The classification of economic activities used is an adaptation of the "International Standard Industrial Classification of all Economic Activities" (ISIC, Rev. 4). The classification of products is derived from CPC Rev.2. The institutional sector classification is that of the 2008 SNA. Scope of the data The transactions covered are those pertaining to goods and services, distribution and financial transactions. All economic units resident in the national territory of Burkina Faso are covered. Informal sector is estimated through a national survey on employment and informal sector. The minimum recommended tables determined by ISWGNA that are covered by the actual series are: annual value added and GDP at current and constant prices by activity; annual expenditures of GDP at current and constant prices; annual value added components at current prices by activity; sequence of accounts for the total economy (until net lending) with an annual frequency; annual rest of the world accounts (until net lending); Annual Non-financial corporation sector accounts (until net lending); Annual Financial corporations accounts (until net lending) Annual General government sector accounts (until net lending) Annual Household sector accounts (until net lending) quarterly value added and GDP at current and constant prices by activity; Annual Non-profit institutions serving households sector accounts (until net lending). Tables don’t cover : Quarterly sequence of accounts for the total economy (until net lending) Quarterly Rest of the world accounts (until net lending) quarterly expenditures of GDP at current and constant prices Unrecorded activity Drug and prostitution are not recorded 2.4.1 Valuation Domestic transactions are valued at market prices. Imports are valued c.i.f. and exports f.o.b. 2.4.2 Recording basis Transactions are recorded on an accrual basis. However, central government receipts are recorded on a cash basis. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Production approach Agriculture: the data are obtained mainly through an ongoing agricultural sample survey carried out each year by the Agricultural Statistics Unit (SSA) of the Directorate for Sectorial Statistics in the Ministry in charge of Agriculture. Cotton production statistics are gathered from the Textile Fiber Companies (SOFITEX, SOCOMA, FASO COTON). Cereal prices (millet, sorghum, corn, etc.) are drawn from a weekly survey conducted under the Market Information System (SIM) and the prices of other products are furnished by the INSD based on price sampling carried out in the framework of the consumer price index. Livestock: livestock statistics come from the Directorate for Sectorial Statistics in the Ministry in charge of Animal Resources. Herds’ numbers are estimated annually by the animal statistics unit. These estimates are based on the 2003 national herd survey (ENEC). That Ministry also supplies producer prices of breeds through surveys. Forestry-fishing-hunting: INSD estimates based on administrative records from the Ministry of Environment and Water. For the mining industry, foods, beverages and tobacco, textile industries, Manufacture of paper, Chemical industries, Manufacture of non-metallic mineral products, Wood and metal products, Construction and public works, the information used is derived from statistical and fiscal declarations (DSF), and accounting records of modern enterprises. Electricity, Gas and Water: accounting records of enterprises in the industry. Trade, Hotels-Bars-Restaurants, Transportation-Storage-Communication: data drawn from the accounting records of enterprises that prepare DSFs. Financial institutions, Insurance companies: data from the accounting records of all companies in these sectors Market services: data drawn from accounting sources, from non-DSF modern sector estimates and from the informal sector. Non-market services: data from general government and public sectors including decentralized collectivities accounts, NGOs and households (domestic wages): data and estimates come from a national survey of NGOs Informal Sector: data and estimates from the national survey on employment and informal sector realized for the needs of the national accounts year rebasing. In the formal sector, data found in DSF are coherent to OHADA recommendations and are sufficiently detailed so it is possible to derive national accounts sequence. Business census is realized each 5 years (the latest one is 2016). Business register is available and up-to-date specific surveys are conducted such as intermediate consumption, structure of taxes on production and imports, etc. Household income and expenditure surveys are conducted each 5 years (latest one is 2014). Expenditure approach Final Household consumption are estimated as projection of the latest household survey. Government final consumption is derived from government financial statistics (central government) and from data collected with regional and specific public agencies. Gross fixed capital formation: accounting data pertaining to modern enterprises, government (based on the public investment), activity reports and accounting records of local Administrations and NGOs. Changes in inventories: data from the corporate and quasi-corporate sector, domestically financed government, households and individual entrepreneurs. Imports and exports of goods and services: customs statistics and balance of payments. Quarterly compilation Chow & Lin Method implemented in R is usually used as benchmarking method, with appropriate high frequency indicators. Both Unadjusted and adjusted quarterly estimates of GDP is derived. TramoSeats method implemented in the JDEMETRA software is used for series adjustment. 3.2.1 Source data assessment The source data assessment is assured by the main steps of national accounts compiling. After the data processing, the following step is called “prearbitrage” (first arbitration) and the next is the arbitration where indicators are used to judge the likelihood of data. There is also an iterative process which allows the correction of discrepancies. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Data generally are reported by the three categories of modern enterprises in the DSF (small, average and big). Data at current values are compiled from accounting records and from estimates. Data at constant values are derived generally by deflation. Database exists for modern sector DSF and comparison within years and within similar enterprises (same sector of activity for example) are done and guide for correction of errors. ERETES is also a database, so all economic information are kept there and in the iterative processes, comparison of data sources is made and the best source which is the most coherent statistically and economically is accepted. Value-added at constant prices is determined by the double deflation method. There are 203 products at a detailed level for the evaluation of production, intermediate and final consumption, etc. Owner - occupied dwellings and FISIM are valuated as recommended by 2008 SNA 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Other statistical procedures are based on the comparison of data between two years, checking of data exhaustiveness by activity, etc. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results Many steps exists for intermediate data validation and in each step, discrepancies are evaluated and decisions are taken and implemented. We can list : 1st , 2nd and 3rd decentralization and centralization Indicators per head checking; Synthesis of Supply and Use table Synthesis of Economic integrated accounts table. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data The previous methods described in 3.4.1 include the assessment of intermediate data. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs The previous methods described in 3.4.1 include the assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical problems. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses For quarterly accounts, the previous accounts are revised in the next quarter estimates. In the publication, a section is reserved to revisions and indicates clearly the quarters revised and the levels of aggregates or growth rates before and after revisions. The reasons of the revisions are also given in that section. Revisions of previous Annual national accounts are also included in this section and explained: especially when provisional and definitive annual accounts estimates are available. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses See section 4.3.1. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request According to INSD data dissemination policy, national accounts data that are not routinely published are available for free upon request if the tabulation needs no particular effort for workers. If the tabulation of these statistics needs an important effort, for example if the user request a specific social accounting matrix, the cost of this request is evaluated and must be paid by the user. 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points Contact points are published at the last page of each publication. In 2015, INSD Dissemination directory have developed an email dissemination application. When publishing national accounts in this way, some users directly post their questions by email and responses are given by national accounts unit.
