 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The information presented on the web page of the MINFIN correspond to the data of incomes, expenses, assets and liabilities, classification of expenditure by functions of government according to divisions and groups and the statement of operation of the Central Government according to the definitions set in the Government Finance Statistics Manual 2014 -GFSM 2014- 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The Central Government includes all the entities who are covered by the General Budget of Revenue and Egress of the State which is approved by the Congress of the Republic of Guatemala and the Extrabudgetary entities. 2.2.1 Scope The Central Government includes all the entities who are covered by the General Budget of Revenue and Egress of the State which is approved by the Congress of the Republic of Guatemala and the Extrabudgetary entities. Scope of the data The information only includes data from the Central Government, which includes the Budgetary and Extrabudgetary entities. The Data respond only to economic flows and the stock position is not included. There are important differences between the national methodology and the data presented using the standards set in the GFSM 2014, for example, in the national methodology you use more a flow of cash approach while with the international concept, it includes the classification of the transactions that affect only the assets and liabilities. For the moment, there are some transaction correspond to other economics flows that are not included in the data, for example, the transactions that reflect a variation in the assets due to fluctuation in the exchange rate. This is because the country still is working in adopting completely the standards set in the GFSM 2014. Exceptions to coverage The information only includes data from the Central Government so doesn’t show data related to Local Governments, Social Security Funds nor Public Corporations. You can check the methodology and the metadata that is included directly in the MINFIN web page to know more about which entities are covered. Unrecorded activity Do not apply. 2.4.1 Valuation The information responds only to economic flows and is not included the Stocks data. 2.4.2 Recording basis The Statistics does not completely meet the accrual accounting, starting with fact that there is no information about accrued interest and only is presented for cashflow uses. Another problem is with the revenue data, by law you must pay your tax declaration at the time you presented to the tax administration service. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures For the moment, the information in the Statement of Operations doesn’t include the Consumption of fixed capital so the operational result doesn’t’ does not include net values. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The primary source of information is the Integrated Accounting System -SICOIN from the acronym in Spanish- which is the official national system. This system is used to collect the statistical data then it is processed using a Business Intelligence software where it gets converted from the national nomenclature to the International Monetary Fund -IMF-classification standard. After the information gets converted, it validated and then disseminated by the MINFIN on his Web Page. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness SICOIN gets the information on real time, and once a month this information is converted to the IMF nomenclature by using a Business Intelligence Software. Then it takes between one or two days to be validated before it gets published. 3.2.1 Source data assessment SICOIN provides the official data of revenues and expenses. This is the only source of information to validate the data that it is presented using the IMF nomenclature. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques The information used to present the data from the Central Government using de International Standard, is obtained automatically from SICOIN each month and then is processed using a Business Intelligence software where it gets converted from the national nomenclature to the IMF classification standard. Before the release each month of the information from the Central Government using de International Standard, the Direction of Analysis and Fiscal Policy -DAPF for the acronym in Spanish- makes sure that the data is consistent with the national methodology 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures The information of the Value Added-Tax gets an adjustment by removing the devolutions that are made by the Central Government during the same period. In the national methodology, these devolutions are part of the expenses of the Government. It´s important to explain that you can find more information about the Non-Financial Public Sector in the MINFIN web page but this data uses a national methodology so it is not compatible with the GFSM 2014. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results For the moment, there is no assessment of the statistical discrepancies however, as we continue adopting the GFSM 2014, this is part of the thing to do within the action plan. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data For the moment, there is no assessment of the statistical discrepancies however, as we continue adopting the GFSM 2014, this is part of the thing to do within the action plan. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs For the moment, there is no assessment of the statistical discrepancies however, as we continue adopting the GFSM 2014, this is part of the thing to do within the action plan. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses In the MINFIN web page, you can find two documents that are constantly being updated, the first one is a Methodology of how the data is converted from national nomenclature to the IMF nomenclature, the second one is a Metadata which explains the main thing to take in consideration from the Central Government using the GFSM 2014. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses In the MINFIN web page, you can find two documents that are constantly being updated, the first one is a Methodology of how the data is converted from national nomenclature to the IMF nomenclature, the second one is a Metadata which explains the main thing to take in consideration from the Central Government using the GFSM 2014. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request The Country has Public Information Access Law and you can make an official request by accessing the MINFIN web page.
