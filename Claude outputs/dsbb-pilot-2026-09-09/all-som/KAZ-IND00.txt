 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Concept : Measure of change in the volume of production given a reference level of output and technology for all producer units (establishments), or a subgroup thereof. Definition : Measure of change in the volume of production of a fixed set of products of constant price and quality for all producer units, or a subgroup thereof. The methodology is based on the standards set by Eurostat. The Industrial Production Index (IPI) is a weighted average of the change in the production output of industrial enterprises operating under any legal form, except individuals. Production output values for the previous year are used as weights in the aggregation of primary data. Previously the IPI was calculated on the basis of comparable prices, but the data have been based on physical indicators since 1994 (European Standard Classification of Products by Activity). 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Classification : Classification occurs in accordance with the National Classification of Economic Activities (NCEA) harmonized with the International Standard Industrial Classification (ISIC) and the European Classification of Economic Activities (NACE Rev2). Scope of the data Industrial coverage: The industrial production physical volume index covers mining and manufacturing; production and distribution of electricity, gas, and water. Product coverage : The industrial production physical volume index is calculated based on the dynamics of critical types of products (in prices of the preceding year) with an adjustment for indices for services of an industrial nature and of the household sector. Each month 350 types of representative commodities from 1,750 enterprises and production lines are used in calculation of the IPI. Scope of production covered: Within an industry based product classification such as CPA, coverage of primary products of establishments, coverage of both primary and secondary products, within a market-based classification such as CPC, coverage of goods, goods and selected services, etc. None 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The data sources are: the monthly report on the value and physical volume of the production of enterprises with more than 50 workers, and the quarterly report on the value and physical volume of the production of enterprises with 50 workers or less (a figure equal to one third of these quarterly data is imputed to each month of the relevant quarter based on coefficients). Sources of weights : Amounts for the cost of products for the preceding year are used as weights for aggregating primary data. Item selection : 350 types of products are used for calculating the IFO [physical volume index]. The aforementioned set of representative products covers more than 80 percent of the volume of industrial products, which ensures that the calculations are as representative as needed. Unit selection : 350 types of products are used for calculating the IFO. The aforementioned set of representative products covers more than 80 percent of the volume of industrial products, which ensures that the calculations are as representative as needed. Sample sizes : Each month 350 representative items from 1,750 enterprise and production lines are used. Item/product specification : Level of detail of specifications for products and number included. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Nature of indicators used : Quantity measures of output. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Timing of production observations: Frequency of data collection in the sampled establishments (monthly, quarterly), including actual reporting dates. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Procedures for non-response : Not applied. Treatment of missing prices : Process for imputing missing production volumes. Selection of replacement items : Not applied. Adjustments for quality differences : Not applied. Introducing new units and products : Not applied. Verification of data : Procedures to validate the accuracy and reliability of collected production information. Verification of processing : Data are analyzed, and in the event of discrepancies they are clarified with the producers of the items. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Nature of weights : Output. Period of current index weights : Calendar year. Frequency of weight updates : One year. Computation of lowest level indices : The industrial production index consists of an average-weighted value of the change in quantity of products manufactured by industries of any legal form except natural persons. Aggregation : Formula used to aggregate the basic item indices to obtain category, group, and the total indices—the Laspeyres indices. Alignment of value of weights and base period : Not applied. Linking of reweighted index to historical index : Not applied. Reference period : December 2001=100 Seasonally-adjusted indices : Data are not adjusted for seasonal fluctuations. None
