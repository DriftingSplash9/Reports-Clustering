 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Analytical framework: The annual and quarterly statistics published on the national accounts were consistent with the recommendations of SNA1968 from 1980 to 2005 . In accordance with SNA93 , the annual and quarterly accounts with the base year 1998 have been completed and published for the series 1998-2013. Another annual accounts' series, produced in accordance with 2008 SNA and the base year2007, is completely achieved and available for the series 2007-2020. Quarterly accounts Compiled in line with the 2008 SNA and the base year 07 are available from the first quarter of 2007 to the fourth quarter of 2021. A new annual accounts' series, produced in accordance with 2008 SNA and referring to a new benchmark year (2014) is available for 2014 to 2019 in final version, 2020 in semi final version and 2021 in provisional version. Compilation of the quarterly accounts in accordance with 2008 SNA and 2014 as benchmark year have already been completed for the series 2014-2021 and 2022 (first quarter) Definition of GDP: GDP is calculated according to the production approach, expenditure approach and income approach (just for the base year). 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Classification systems: The classification systems used are in accordance with international classifications. Currently, published accounts are based on the Moroccan classification of activities (NMA2010), which is in line with the ‘'International Standard Industrial Classification of All Economic Activities'' ISIC Revision 4. The Moroccan classification of products (NMP 2010) is compatible with the ‘'Central Product Classification'' (CPC-Ver.2.1) In the institutional sectors, the distinction between non-financial institutions, financial institutions and those belonging to households is only made for manufacturing activities and services. All agricultural activities are included in the household sectors. This distinction was made possible thanks to the informal sector survey conducted by HCP; its results were incorporated into the new national accounts base 2014 and also in the former accounts of 2007 and 1998 bases. Scope of the data Transaction coverage: The production and assets boundary are consistent with SNA2008. Geographic coverage: Economic activity includes territorial enclaves located elsewhere in the world and employees who work part of the year in another country. Offshore areas are taken into account. The institutional sector accounts are compiled regularly for all sectors: non financial institutions, financial institutions, government, households and nonprofit institutions and the rest of the world as fictitious sector. The sequence of accounts includes all current accounts, the capital and financial account as part of the accumulation accounts. They are available for the series 2014-2020. The series of components of the commodity's supply and use balance are available annually and quarterly, at constant and current prices. Exceptions to coverage Offshore areas are not taken into account. 2.4.1 Valuation The valuation methods used to record stocks and flows are in line with SNA2008. Output and value added are valued at basic prices. In the national accounts of the old base of 1998 and 2007, imports and exports by product are valued respectively at CIF and FOB prices, then they are both expressed at the global level on an FOB basis after introducing a CIF adjustment. /FOB. whereas in the 2014 base, imports and exports by product are both valued at FOB prices and no adjustment is introduced. The others transactions on goods and services are valued at purchasers' prices. As the accounts produced in 1998 are in line with 1993 SNA; 2007 and 2014 bases are consistent with 2008 SNA, the volume estimates of transactions and macroeconomic aggregates are referring to the previous year's prices. 2.4.2 Recording basis However, transactions involving general government are partly recorded on a cash basis particular as regards taxes. For transactions derived from the balance of payments (BP), are normally established in accordance with the principles of the fifth edition of the Manual of the IMF Balance of Payments. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures Transactions between establishments of a single enterprise are recorded on a gross basis when the statistical data allow. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Production approach: There is not yet a national business register. The existing data sources cover the different departments of the country for administrative reasons and do not necessarily meet the more general needs of macroeconomic statistics (because their content and structure may differ significantly). Typically, these files only cover large units and are not updated regularly. The most frequently used records are related to taxes (accounting data for taxes themselves are not available). There is a register of establishments that pay the business license fee. Another file contains all legal persons, that is to say companies subject to corporation tax and required to file their accounting entries. Certain natural persons (subject to general tax on professional income) are also required to provide complete accounting documents. Others may only have to report a limited number of income and expenditure figures, and yet another category are people who pay a flat income tax. Efforts are made to cover units not included in the files through employment surveys. For manufacturing activities, the administrative file kept by the MCI is extremely important. However, the HCP carried out an economic census (in 2001 and 2002) with a view to establishing a directory of companies and their establishments. The work in progress consists of linking the file resulting from this census with the other administrative files (taxes, Moroccan Office of Industrial and Commercial Property, National Social Security Fund). The file generated was used as a sampling frame for the annual surveys of construction, trade and service companies. The realization of these surveys was launched from 2005 for the financial year 2004 until today. These surveys made it possible in part to update this file, particularly in terms of activities carried out. This file, supplemented by that of the ministry responsible for industry, is currently used to carry out structural surveys of non-agricultural businesses during the 2006 financial year and in 2014. These surveys, combined with those aimed at the informal sector and the standard of living of households and others (notably the survey on Government investments (1998, 2007 and 2014) and the survey on non-profit institutions launched from 2008 for the 2007 financial year), are used to modify the base year of the national accounts. The texts establishing the directory and the single tax number of companies and their establishments, including an update, was adopted by the Government in July 2016. The completed files serve, among other things, as a sampling base for future surveys. A master sample has been available for the population and households since 2004; it has been updated on the basis of the results of the general population and housing census of 2014. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording According to annual national accounts, output is estimated by industry (100 positions) and product (248 positions) in accordance with the classifications of industries and products mentioned in the previous paragraphs. Several methods are envisaged for adjusting the data to improve the coverage of data sources, definitions, classifications, and assessments, to align them more closely with international standards. 3.2.1 Source data assessment The source data for the national accounts are analyzed in order to correct errors, including a check based on an analysis of consistency over time and consistency with other related data sources. In particular, the sources data are checked with regard to the national accounts definitions, the assessment principles involved, reference periods, and proper classifications. The accuracy of administrative data, such as fiscal data and foreign trade statistics, is also checked regularly and explanations are requested if there is any doubt. For surveys, the DCN has data on sampling errors. The extrapolation coefficients are adjusted to take account of non responses. The data are checked at the central level and during the collection process. Outliers phenomena are identified and corrected and doubtful operations are confirmed with the reporting entities. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Production approach: In the annual Moroccan national accounts, the output is estimated by industry: Agriculture, forestry, and fisheries: Output and intermediate consumption are calculated by product or group of products; the overall value added of this industry is determined as the difference between the total value of its production and the total value of its intermediate consumption. Agricultural production is recorded on harvest basis. Intermediate consumption is estimated according to the major cost categories. In this context, several data sources are used such as: - annual surveys conducted by the Department of Agriculture on crops - production and prices - and livestock farming; - Various statistics produced by the administrations concerned; - Structural surveys conducted periodically by the High Commission for Planning (such as the survey on consumption and household spending and that on the living standards of households); - Structural survey conducted periodically by the High Commission for Planning for fishing companies (formal and informal); - Statistical indices (retail and wholesale prices). Mining and energy: The data for this sector (non-metallic minerals including phosphates, metal ores, career and energy products (oil refining, electricity and water)) are collected from: - annual activity reports; -statistics about the mining and energy sector established by the Ministry of Energy and Mines; -statistical indices. Manufacturing: One of the main statistical sources used in the development of production by branches of industrial activity is the annual survey of processing industries, conducted by the Ministry of Industry. For the base year, this source was used in addition to the structural investigation of the sector. Also, the indices of industrial production price indices for industrial production are used in the provisional accounts. However, this survey does not cover the process industry field in its entirety. Non seizures enterprises by this survey are individual companies. The estimate of their production is determined directly from the Results of the surveys of the informal sector for the base year and from statistics on employment (permanent employment survey conducted among households by the office of Statistics) for current years, delivering the annual evolution of this production. Construction and Civil engineering works: Regarding the base year, the statistics used to calculate the relative production in construction sector come directly from structural surveys of formal and informal enterprises operating in this field. In current years, we use the results of annual surveys of the sector and the data related to household demand for housing and other sectors, including enterprises and government, in different construction products. This data is used to identify rates of annual variation in production by value and volume. Similarly, estimates of residential and non-residential buildings requested by households and businesses are made from building permit statistics. The value of the constructions of the year is estimated through a grid of the coefficients of realization of the authorized constructions. This table allows the passage from the expected value of the construction to the realized value at the price of the year of authorization, an update is done using appropriate price indices to pass from the year of authorization to the year of final realization. Concerning the rest of the demand for construction and construction products (Civil engineering works), the statistical sources used are companies' accounting data, major users of public works products, and detailed statistics from government budget. Transport: For each transport activity (road, rail, and air), production and intermediate consumption are estimated from structural surveys of formal and informal enterprises operating in this field in the base year. For current year, production and intermediate consumption of rail and aerial activities (passenger and freight), are approached directly from the accounting records of the companies operating in these areas, it is particularly the Moroccan National Railways Office and the Royal Air Maroc. Statistics on maritime transport by Moroccan armament are used to approximate the development of the activity of maritime transport, while those from the National Transportation Agency (public institution responsible for regulating road transport of goods) are used for road freight transport. Regard to road transport of passengers, indirect methods, based on the components of demand, are used. The output of transport auxiliaries is approached through the accounting data of the companies responsible for the management of infrastructure related to the different types of transport (ports, airports, road freight transport, bus stations, highway and pipeline) and data relating to passengers on air and sea transport. · Post and telecommunications: production of the post and telecommunications sector is calculated through the accounting records of the various operators engaged in this field. It is essentially of Morocco Telecom, Orange-Maroc, Inwi and poste-Maroc. Trade: The output of trade activity consists of trade margins by product. For the base year, it was evaluated from the structural survey and for the current years, the estimate of the value of production is based on the updating commercial margins. Finance and insurance: Production and value added are calculated directly using the data available in the institutions' operating accounts. Real estate services, and rental and leasing services: The determination of the rent housing was conducted on the basis of data from the National Survey of Household Living Standards (1998-2007), the national survey on consumption and household expenditure (2001, 2014) and the National Survey on employment (permanent survey). Starting with database, rents were estimated for the current year by calculating the index of evolution of renter households accommodated free or benefiting from official accommodation and the index of evolution of owner households or accessing property (data provided by the annual survey of employment). These indices have produced changes in volume of rent. For the price evolution, we use the price index for consumption CPI regarding the rent. Regard to rent of the other constructions; it was determined from the accounting records of the relevant entities. In terms of the production of real estate development, it was calculated based on the results of structural investigations. With regard to the production of other real estate (property intermediation), it is assumed to perform as the actual rent. Other market services: The production of the base year is prepared, like other sectors of market activity other than agriculture, directly from the results of structural surveys of formal and informal businesses. For current years, the production approach is performed using evolution of indicators, calculated on the basis of data that are specific to each type of activity. For example, the change indicators of production activity of "accommodation and food" are derived from the statistics on tourist nights and the balance of payments. Services provided to companies are determined based on the technical coefficients related to previous year, on productions of the year concerned of the different branches consumers and on balance of payments data for estimating imports and export these services. The evolution of the production of cultural and recreational activities is estimated using data from the revenue generated by the domestic film business, the tax for the promotion of audio-visual landscape, the summary statements of Maghreb Agency of Press (MAP) and the receipts from the National Fund for Cultural Activities. Finally, the production of personal and domestic services was calculated on the basis of final consumption. Nonmarket services provided by general government: The production evaluation of this sector is measured by production costs (intermediate consumption, compensation of employees, other taxes on production and consumption of fixed capital). They are based on information taken from administrative documents. For expenditure and revenue, General Treasury issues and revenue collection are used, as well as Treasury statistics (Directorate of the Treasury and External Financing, DTFE), public institutions, and non-profit, local government and social security administrations. Services provided by nonprofit institutions serving households: The National account directorate (DCN) is currently estimating services provided by NPISHs using the survey on nonprofit organizations carried out for the base year. For the current years, the estimates are done using the evolution of appropriate indicators. Taxes and duties on imports: The relevant data are provided by the Ministry of Finance. Intermediate consumption (annual accounts) · Agricultural sector for the base year, intermediate consumption of the agricultural sector was calculated by product in each branch forming the sector. It was determined from the results of the household living standards survey combined with various other data relating in particular to the production and distribution of products which might be used as an input by the various activities falling the sector. By exploiting the questionnaire component relating to self-employment on the agriculture section. This section provides information on production, inputs used and the investment and that, separately for crops and livestock. For the fishing industry, intermediate consumption by product was obtained directly from the structural survey (formal part) and the survey of the informal sector. In current years, with the exception of the structural survey on the living standards of households that is not conducted annually, other data are generally available every year. Data from these sources are used to calculate indicators of evolution in value and volume for the different positions of intermediate consumption of each branch. · Non-agricultural market sector The determination of intermediate consumption of the various branches, in that sector, required for the base year, the realization of structural surveys of non-financial companies with accounting and a survey of the informal sector, which has affected every non-agricultural production units that do not have accounts. Intermediate consumption related to current year's results from the projection of technical coefficients of the previous year, adjusted of course on the basis of accounting data relating to operating expenses and the use of chains and fixed cells (data available some important positions) and through the alignment of data to the levels of SUB and Supply and Use Table (SUT) synthesis. For financial activities, intermediate consumption was identified directly from their accounting records (retail account income and expenses) for both the base year for the current years. · Government sector as the production of this sector is mainly non-market's one, intermediate consumption and also its added value is directly determined with calculation of output as mentioned in the paragraph relating to the production of this sector. Value added It represents the income generated by all production units belonging concerned branch. It is obtained by the difference between actual output and intermediate consumption of all these units. Expenditure approach (annual accounts) National accounts database allows the calculation of GDP from the expenditure approach, that is to say the difference between the total final demand (final consumption, gross fixed capital formation, changes in inventories, Acquisitions less disposals of valuables and exports) and imports. Final consumption expenditure The final consumption expenditure of resident households is calculated by product on the basis of survey data on the living standards of households (1998, 2001, 2007 and 2014). Those of non-residents are obtained from statistics of the balance of payments and the results of the international tourism survey conducted by the Department of Tourism. The data emerging from these surveys are then adjusted as part of the ensuring the consistency of data and the establishment of supply and use balance (SUB) by product. For current year, estimates are made by post the list of products (248 items) by direct allocation of resources into goods and services or as part of the establishment of SUB Regard to final consumption expenditure of general government, they are calculated by the difference between the total production of this sector on the one hand and non-market production and the partial payments made by the other households. These various operations are determined from budget statistics, local government administrative accounts and records of non-profit public institutions. The final consumption of nonprofit institutions serving households is assimilated to the total non-market production of this sector. Household final consumption is calculated as a residual at the aggregated level, owing to the lack of direct data sources. For the goods and services accounts, the DCN publishes household domestic consumption, a concept that includes the consumption of nonresidents in Morocco and excludes the consumption of residents abroad. For the income accounts, the residency concept is used, thus properly reflecting the final consumption expenditure of resident households. Gross fixed capital formation (GFCF) For the base year, estimates of GFCF is made through data from the structural surveys (surveys of formal and informal enterprises, entities of government and households), accounting documents (fiscal and businesses) and external trade statistics and the agricultural component data such as those on plantations and livestock. This GFCF is determined by customer and by product of the nomenclature of products chosen and depending on the origin of these products local or external (imported). In current years, the foreign trade statistics and the annual survey data on industrial production allow to release evolution of GFCF by industrial product according to the origin indicators. Accounting data (government budget and enterprises accounts) and statistics on construction permits to determine GFCF in construction and Civil engineering works Should be noted that government's GFCF is determined for the base year (1998, 2007 and2014) from the results of the survey of the government investment by sub-sector and by type of assets. In current years, the situation of the financial operations of local authorities as well as the accounting records of the various public administrative establishments used for the calculation of GFCF of this sector. Changes in inventories The estimation of changes in inventories is determined by product making a distinction between changes in producer and user stocks. For the base year, the calculation of these two components was done on the basis of data from structural surveys. Regarding the current years, changes in producer stocks of industrial products is determined from the annual survey of manufacturing industries while for that of other products, accounting or administrative nature statistics are used as in the case mining and energy products. Acquisitions less disposals of valuables: Valuables are assets which are not used for production or consumption and which are acquired and held to serve as a store of value. Their value is estimated from household expenditure survey and from some acquisitions of financial corporation (non-monetary Gold) Imports and exports of goods and services Imports and exports of goods and services are taken directly from foreign trade statistics and those of balance of payments. Specific techniques for compiling quarterly data Once the most appropriate quarterly indicators for tracking developments in the various industries have been selected, the DCN takes a three-stage approach. The first stage consists of seasonally adjusting the indicators according to an internationally accepted method (X-12-ARIMA). The second stage consists of "calibrating" the seasonally adjusted indicator using the annual data, i.e., finding a mathematical ratio (generally estimated by the least squares method) between the trend revealed by the indicator, on the one hand, and the values considered correct from the annual estimates, on the other. By applying the resulting ratio to the most recent year, it is possible to estimate the trend of the activity of the industry in question for the quarters of the current year. The third stage in compiling the quarterly accounts consists of smoothing the discrepancies between the annual and quarterly accounts when the new annual accounts data become available. This procedure, known as "calibration," is performed automatically by the "EVIEWS" software. Quarterly GDP: For Quarterly national accounts resulting from the accounting framework of the annual accounts based on the same conceptual and methodological principles are, however, adapted to the nature of annual infra information available. So the preparation of these accounts is done at the level of aggregation of nomenclatures of provisional annual accounts (28 posts for the nomenclature of branches and 28 posts for the product) and is currently limited to the calculation of GDP by production approach It is being used on the basis of infra annual indicators and calibration models and smoothing that maintain the accounting consistency with the annual accounts. The production approach is used to calculate quarterly GDP, but the components of the supply and use table (final consumption, GFCF, changes in inventories, acquisitions less disposals of valuables, imports and exports) are calculated subsequently, as well as disposable gross national income, gross national savings, and the capital account at the national level. Also, their compilation is made according to base year (which is for the annual national accounts 2014). This requires chaining indexes volumes generated from annual accounts causing loss of additivity and consequently the sum of added values is different from the total value added calculated after chaining. The quarterly accounts relating to GDP, economic growth and the evolution of value added by industry in volume and value are published every quarter. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request Estimates are made for 55 industries at the summary level, but the publications show a breakdown of only 28 groups of activities. However, detailed data are available upon request. For quarterly GDP, the level of detail is 16 activities and is disseminated at the same level.
