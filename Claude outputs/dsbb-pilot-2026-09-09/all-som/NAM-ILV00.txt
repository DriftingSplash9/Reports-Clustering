 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The concepts and definitions for compiling International reserves follows the requirements in BPM6. The international reserves are measured in millions of U.S. dollars and millions of Namibia dollars. They consist of holdings of Special Drawing Rights (SDRs), reserve position in the Fund, and foreign exchange assets of the Bank of Namibia. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Data to determine the external position is broadly consistent with the classification and sectorization systems used with the internationally accepted guidelines, while the data for determining the import coverage needs to be revisited and consistently aligned with the former. 2.2.1 Scope Data to determine the external position is broadly consistent with the classification and sectorization systems used with the internationally accepted guidelines, while the data for determining the import coverage needs to be revisited and consistently aligned with the former. Scope of the data The data is extracted from the central bank trial and balance sheet. A reconciliation is done between financial markets, Monetary and Financial Statistics (MFS) and BoP to ensure that the data is in full harmony. Data covers broadly all variables needed to compile the Reserve assets, such as: SDRs, Reserve position in the Fund and Foreign exchange. Exceptions to coverage None Unrecorded activity None 2.4.1 Valuation Average exchange rate is used to value flows, while end of period exchange rate is used to value stock data. The valuation of the stocks of securities are based on market price and the end of the reference period. 2.4.2 Recording basis This meets the requirements for accrual accounting, which consider for instance account receivable. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures Data is recorded on a gross basis. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The data on reserve assets are available from the records of the Bank of Namibia. The data is provided internally from Finance and Administration department. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording The source data broadly approximate the definitions, scope, classifications, valuation and time of recording as internationally required. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness The compilation of foreign reserves data are done on a monthly basis. The data is sourced internally from the financial markets department which makes it easier to produce it in a timely manner. Namibia currently meets the GDDS requirements and is in the process of migrating to SDDS. 3.2.1 Source data assessment Routine assessment of source data is constantly done, which led to the detection of different data used for reserves. This deviation from using consistent and correct data will be rectified shortly. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Source data are relevant and aligned with targeted concepts. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures None 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results The validation and investigation of statistical discrepancies are in place and inconsistencies that have been detected will be corrected accordingly. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data Discrepancies within the data are regularly assessed and discussions are held with data sources to determine ways, which are in line with accepted international practices. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs Investigations of statistical discrepancies are done regularly, and concerted efforts are being made to produce consistent data before reaching the output level. However, some problems need to be resolved within a longer timeframe than others. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses There is rarely a need for revisions on reserves data, However, in such rare cases, revisions to the data are done and published on the quarterly basis in the Quarterly Bulletin. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request Unpublished reserves data are not shared on request as they are confidential.
