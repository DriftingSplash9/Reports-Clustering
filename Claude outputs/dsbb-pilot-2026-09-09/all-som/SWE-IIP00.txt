 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Analytical framework: The International Investment Position (IIP) covers external financial assets and liabilities following the conceptual framework of the sixth edition of the IMF's Balance of Payments and International Investment Position Manual (BPM6). The data published are end-of-period stocks, organized under five main headings: Direct investment, portfolio investment, financial derivatives, other investment, and reserve assets. Definition: The International Investment Position (IIP) is a statistical statement of the value and composition of the stock of Sweden's external financial assets and liabilities. Relationship to international guidelines: The stock data are compiled and presented in accordance with the BPM6 . The published data do, however, not specify changes in positions during the period associated with transactions, exchange rate changes, price changes, and other adjustments. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Classification: Data are classified in accordance with the sixth edition of the IMF's Balance of Payments and International Investment Position Manual (BPM6) . Scope of the data Coverage of external financial assets and liabilities: Coverage according to the BPM6. Data for the whole of Sweden cover gross figures for all assets and liabilities vis-à-vis non-residents for all domestic sectors. Geographical coverage: Data refer to the territory of Sweden. 2.4.1 Valuation Conversion to unit of account: Data are converted at spot rate at the end of the period. Valuation: Most data are valued at current market values with the following exceptions: Direct investment: The data are at book values. A market valuation of direct investment stocks is published separately. 2.4.2 Recording basis Recording basis: The recording of data follows the accrual principle.2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Direct investment: The primary data sources consist of the monthly cut‑off survey for FDI flows and the annual FDI survey for positions, the latter based on a group business register. These sources are supplemented with estimates of private, non‑business real estate and with information on intra‑group trade credits derived from a quarterly sample survey. Portfolio investment: Debt securities: Data from reference period 2019 and onwards is based on national databases Securities holding statistics and Securities issues. Foreign holdings in Swedish debt securities are estimated by subtracting Swedish investors' holdings from total Swedish issues of debt securities. Financial derivatives: Monthly surveys. Other investment: The sources are, listed by subcomponents: Trade credits: Quarterly survey Loans, deposits, etc.: Monthly surveys of the financial sector, quarterly surveys of non-financial companies. Insurance saving: Saving in the form of life and pension insurance is estimated from accumulated transactions adjusted for movements in exchange rates, interest rates and stock market prices. Reserve assets: Data are based on Sveriges Riksbank's balance sheet data but för interest-bearing securities, data from reference period 2019 and onwards is based on national databases Securities holding statistics and Securities issues. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Direct investment: Stock data are reconciled with balance of payments data. Portfolio investment: Transaction data are used to derive quarterly stocks on shares and funds, based on the semi-annual survey.Repurchase agreements are treated as loans, not as debt securities. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses Statistics Sweden recently conducted an analysis of the revisions in BOP and IIP statistics. This work was also aiming at developing tools for a more systematic follow up on future revisions. This includes identifying BOP and IIP items that are systematically revised according to the measures Mean Absolute Revision (MAR) and Mean Revision (MR). This more systematic approach will potentially help identifying statistical production processes that could be improved. 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points Information about contact points is published in the form of e-mail and telephone numbers and is found on Statistics Sweden website. 5.3.2 Availability of documents and services catalogs Contact information and documents regarding our statistics are available on Statistics Sweden website.
