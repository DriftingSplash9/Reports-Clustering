 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions General Government Operations data are compiled in line with ESA 2010 methodology, Government Finance Statistics Manual 2014and Guide for Compilers and Users 2011. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The general government sector and its subsectors are defined in accordance with the ESA 2010 guidelines and the ESA 2010 Manual on Government Deficit and Debt, 2022 edition, which are also consistent with the 2008 SNA and GFSM 2014. The general government in Slovak Republic consists of central government, local government, and social security funds. The Ministry of Finance of the Slovak Republic compiles government finance statistics for each subsector as well as for the general government sector as a whole. 2.2.1 Scope The general government sector and its subsectors are defined in accordance with the ESA 2010 guidelines and the ESA 2010 Manual on Government Deficit and Debt, 2022 edition, which are also consistent with the 2008 SNA and GFSM 2014. The general government in Slovak Republic consists of central government, local government, and social security funds. The Ministry of Finance of the Slovak Republic compiles government finance statistics for each subsector as well as for the general government sector as a whole. Scope of the data The institutional coverage corresponds to general government sector according to the methodology ESA 2010. In the Slovak Republic the Statistical Office of the Slovak Republic is responsible for the delimitation of general government sector and its updating. SO SR is responsible for the Register of subjects of the general government. Register is available on the Statistical website only in Slovak version. https://slovak.statistics.sk/wps/portal/ext/Databases/administration/!ut/p/z1/ Exceptions to coverage No exceptions. 2.4.1 Valuation All transactions (financial and non-financial) are valued according to ESA 2010 Regulations. The transactions denominated in the foreign currency are valued at the relevant European Central Bank rate which is accepted on the transaction day. 2.4.2 Recording basis The released data are recorded on accrual basis. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures Adjustments for grossing and netting of transactions: The revenues and expenditure balance does not include transactions recorded on the net basis. The net purchases of non-financial assets are recorded of separately. Consolidation: Data are consolidated. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Data sources: Data on Central Government Operations are compiled by the Public Sector Financial Statistics Department of Ministry of Finance of Slovak Republic in co-operation with the State Treasury. Data source for the data on General Government operations compilation are the financial statements reported by subjects of general government in line with the Directive No. MF/017353/2017-352 as amended, on laying down the layout, content, method, date and place of submission of information from the accounts and data necessary for the purposes of assessing the implementation of the budget of the public administration. The directive is available on MoF´s website solely in slovak version https://www.mfsr.sk/sk/financie/statne-vykaznictvo/legislativa/ . 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Source data timeliness meets the required standards for SDDS plus. 3.2.1 Source data assessment Source data are based especially on financial statements that cover most of government units, i.e. response is 90 - 100 %. Assessment is carried out during the processing of the statements; results are monitored, and extreme mistakes are discussed with the concrete unit. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Since the General Government Operations compilation is based on financial statements and response is about 90 -100 %, and the statements fulfill the requirements to compile the statistical data, no estimation is needed. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures If needed consolidation is established within the sector general government. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results Validation of intermediate result is carried out automatically during the processing of data sources. The discrepancy between financial and non-financial transactions is regularly assessed and analyzed for possible improvement. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data See 3.4.1. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs See 3.4.1. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses In each March and September, annual and quarterly data are aligned, and revisions are analyzed for future processings. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses None. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request The corresponding statistical department is responsible for fulfillment of such requirement depending on availability of data sources. 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points Contact points for each subject field are available on the website https://www.mfsr.sk/en/finance/national-reporting/sdds-plus .
