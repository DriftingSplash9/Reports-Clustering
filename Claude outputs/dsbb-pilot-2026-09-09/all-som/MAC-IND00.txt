I. Analytical Framework, Concepts, Definitions, and Classifications
 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Index of Industrial Production (IIP) reflects the change of the volume of goods and services produced over time of the manufacturing and the electricity, gas & water supply sectors of Macao. The current IIP, with 2008 as the reference year, is compiled using the annually re-weighted approach, i.e. updating weights every year using gross value added of industries in the preceding year. 
 
 
 The IIP being a volume index means the index is not influenced by price fluctuations . 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The IPI includes 10 manufacturing industries, namely manufacture of food and beverages, manufacture of tobacco, manufacture of textiles, manufacture of wearing apparel, manufacture of other non-metallic mineral products, manufacture of leather and footwear, manufacture of chemicals and chemical products, manufacture of office, accounting and computing machinery, manufacture of electrical machinery and apparatus not elsewhere classified, as well as manufacture of furniture and manufacturing not elsewhere classified. The classification list is based on ISIC rev.3 with minor modifications to cater for the situation of Macao. 
 
 
 Only the first 5 manufacturing industries are individually tabulated, whereas the other 5 industries are grouped as “others”. 

II. Scope of the data
 2.2.1 Scope The IPI includes 10 manufacturing industries, namely manufacture of food and beverages, manufacture of tobacco, manufacture of textiles, manufacture of wearing apparel, manufacture of other non-metallic mineral products, manufacture of leather and footwear, manufacture of chemicals and chemical products, manufacture of office, accounting and computing machinery, manufacture of electrical machinery and apparatus not elsewhere classified, as well as manufacture of furniture and manufacturing not elsewhere classified. The classification list is based on ISIC rev.3 with minor modifications to cater for the situation of Macao. 
 
 
 Only the first 5 manufacturing industries are individually tabulated, whereas the other 5 industries are grouped as “others”. Scope of the data Index of Industrial Production covers the manufacturing and the electricity, gas & water supply sectors. 
 Exceptions to coverage Nil. 

III. Accounting Conventions
 2.4.1 Valuation Only the IIP for the manufacture of other food products and beverages is derived from the value of production collected from establishments through quarterly sample surveys, deflated by the food & non-alcoholic beverages of Consumer Price Index. Market price for production is usually recorded by the respondents as most of them are engaged in direct sale of their products. 2.4.2 Recording basis Not applicable. 

IV. Nature of the Basic Data Sources
 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Industrial production covers a wide range of manufacturing activities, and the primary data used vary according to the characteristics of each industry. For the export-oriented manufacturing sector (including: manufacture of textiles, manufacture of wearing apparel, manufacture of tobacco, manufacture of leather and footwear, manufacture of chemical products, manufacture of office equipment, manufacture of machinery and furniture, as well as other manufacturing not elsewhere classified), the IIP is calculated using the value of domestic exports of each quarter from External Merchandise Trade Statistics as output, deflated by the price index of domestic exports. For industries with products merely for local sale (including: production of concrete & cement in manufacture of other non-metallic mineral products and electricity & water supply industries), the IIP is compiled using the volume of production collected from establishments through quarterly surveys. 
 
 
 In manufacture of food products and beverages, retail sales volume index is employed in compiling the IIP of manufacture of Chinese bakery products; for manufacture of other food products and beverages, the IIP is derived from the value of production collected from establishments through quarterly sample surveys, deflated by the food & non-alcoholic beverages of Consumer Price Index. 
 
 
 The reference year for the current IIP is 2008, and the weight is updated every year using gross value added of industries of the preceding year. No seasonal adjustment is made to the IIP. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Compilation of the source data is completed five weeks after the end of the reference quarter. 3.2.1 Source data assessment Cross analysis with merchandise trade data. 

V. Compilation Practices
 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Imputation for non-response cases. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures IIP adopts the chained-link approach with annually updated weights as recommended by UNSD.
