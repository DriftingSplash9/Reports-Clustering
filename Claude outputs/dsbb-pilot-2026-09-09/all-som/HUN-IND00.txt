 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Concept: the purpose of the indicator is to measure the change in volume of production, monitoring the performance of industry. Definition: chained Paasche-type index (2021 monthly average = 100). Production is defined as gross output value of industrial activities performed by enterprises classified to the industry, including performance of subcontractors. The effect of price movement is eliminated by deflating these data using the producer price Laspeyres-type index at the 4-digit level of the national branch classification. Internationally accepted standard: UN Statistical Division’s “International Recommendations for the Index of Industrial Production”. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Classification: the Hungarian Standard Industrial Classification of All Economic Activities (TEÁOR'08) was put in force in 2008. The earlier version (TEÁOR'03) had been valid in 2003-2007. TEÁOR'08 conforms with ISIC Rev.4 and NACE Rev.2. TEÁOR'08 is harmonised with ISIC Rev.4 only at 2-digit level. (At 3- and 4-digit levels there were some discrepancies between the nomenclatures). 2.2.1 Scope Classification: the Hungarian Standard Industrial Classification of All Economic Activities (TEÁOR'08) was put in force in 2008. The earlier version (TEÁOR'03) had been valid in 2003-2007. TEÁOR'08 conforms with ISIC Rev.4 and NACE Rev.2. TEÁOR'08 is harmonised with ISIC Rev.4 only at 2-digit level. (At 3- and 4-digit levels there were some discrepancies between the nomenclatures). Scope of the data The index covers sales of finished and intermediate industrial goods produced and services performed, excluding transactions within establishments of the same enterprise, and subsidiary production of the enterprise. Adjustment is made for work in progress and for changes in stocks of finished products. Industrial coverage: the total industry (mining and quarrying, manufacturing, electricity, gas, steam and air-conditioning supply) is covered. Enterprises having more than 49 employees, classified to the industry are observed by full-scope survey. In 1997 the lowest cut-off of the survey was 51 persons. Since 1998 the enterprises with 5-49 employees have been observed by sample survey. In 1997 the lower and upper threshold of the observation was 11 and 50 persons, respectively. The data of enterprises having employees below 5 persons are estimated for the total industry from administrative records of value added tax of the last two years. The percentages of sampled enterprises according to the number of employees (September 2025) are the following: 5-9: 10.5% 10-19: 13.7% 20-49: 21.6% 50 and more: 100% Exceptions to coverage Non-industrial activities are not included. Excludes transactions within establishments of the same enterprise. Unrecorded activity 2.4.1 Valuation Excise duties, registration, public health product tax and value added taxes are not included, price supplements are accounted. 2.4.2 Recording basis Timing of production observations: frequency of data collection is monthly. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The data on gross output value are derived from a full-scope survey of enterprises with more than 49 employees, on a sample survey of enterprises with 5-49 employees, and on estimations from administrative records for enterprises with fewer than 5 employees. Sources of weights: industry surveys. Unit selection: selection of the sample from the sampling frame occurs through random sampling. The target population covers all working enterprises. The sampling is stratified. The method of grossing up is the multiplication of the sample mean by the sample size for the various strata. The standard error is calculated in the traditional way. Sample sizes: the percentages of sampled enterprises according to the number of employees (September 2025) are the following: 5-9: 10.5% 10-19: 13.7% 20-49: 21.6% 50 and more: 100% Data collection methods: via electronic data collection system. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Nature of indicators used: deflated gross output value. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness The deadline of responses is the 20th of the month after the reference month. 3.2.1 Source data assessment 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Procedure of nonresponse: nonresponse rate for full-scope survey was 3.13 percent in September 2025. Missing data are estimated. The imputation is carried out individually. Nonresponse rate for sample survey was 9.17 percent in September 2025. Missing data of enterprises obtained by sample survey are not supplemented individually, but taken into consideration at the number of respondents for the multiplication factor. Introducing new units: every new enterprise having more than 49 employees, stored in the register of the Hungarian Central Statistical Office, is surveyed. The sample for the enterprises with 5-49 employees is drawn and updated annually (generally in December). The sampling frame is the register. The new enterprises in the course of the year are not involved in the sample but they are taken into consideration at the multiplication factor of grossing up. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Nature of weights: for aggregation output weights of 4-digit branches (classes) are used. Period of current index weights: reference month. Frequency of weights updates: weights are changed every year. Computation of lowest level indices: 4-digit class indices are compiled from deflated gross output value of the enterprises having more than 4 employees. Aggregation: Paasche index is used. Index of the total industry covers every enterprise classified to the industry. Reference period: 2021=100. Seasonally-adjusted indices: seasonal and working-day adjustment is implemented by the computer program package Tramo-Seats with J. Demetra+ 2.2.0 interface. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results In the case of the crude (non-adjusted) indices confrontation is carried out with the Annual Integrated Survey of Economic Statistics as well as with infra-annual value added tax files. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data Missing data are urged and requested via electronic data collection system (furthermore by telephone contacts and via e-mail). 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses 5.1.5 Dissemination on request Unpublished and non-confidential data are disseminated on request, before the dissemination the confidentiality of data is checked.
