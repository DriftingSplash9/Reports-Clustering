 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The Dominican Republic’s national accounts are compiled and disseminated by the National Accounts and Economic Statistics Department of the Central Bank of the Dominican Republic. The national accounts are currently estimated with a methodology based on the concepts and definitions of the System of National Accounts, 2008 (2008 SNA) that apply to the Dominican context and for which statistical information is available, and the Quarterly National Accounts Manual of the International Monetary Fund (IMF) for the quarterly and monthly estimates. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The following are the main classifications for designing and compiling the national accounting framework in the Dominican Republic: • Based on the five major institutional sectors identified in the 2008 SNA, institutional units are grouped in non-financial corporations, financial corporations, general government, and households and non-profit institutions serving households merged as a single sector. • Transactions are grouped considering the classification proposed by the SNA. • Products, industries and economic activities classification, based on the Central Product Classification, Version 2 (CPC Version 2) and the International Standard Industrial Classification of All Economic Activities, Revision 4 (ISIC Rev. 4), respectively. A National Product and Economic Activities Classification with 175 groups of products and 35 economic activities was developed according to its significance level and the availability of the information. Imports and exports of goods are initially classified with the Harmonized commodity description and coding System and converted into a national classification through the CPC Version 2. 2.2.1 Scope The following are the main classifications for designing and compiling the national accounting framework in the Dominican Republic: • Based on the five major institutional sectors identified in the 2008 SNA, institutional units are grouped in non-financial corporations, financial corporations, general government, and households and non-profit institutions serving households merged as a single sector. • Transactions are grouped considering the classification proposed by the SNA. • Products, industries and economic activities classification, based on the Central Product Classification, Version 2 (CPC Version 2) and the International Standard Industrial Classification of All Economic Activities, Revision 4 (ISIC Rev. 4), respectively. A National Product and Economic Activities Classification with 175 groups of products and 35 economic activities was developed according to its significance level and the availability of the information. Imports and exports of goods are initially classified with the Harmonized commodity description and coding System and converted into a national classification through the CPC Version 2. Scope of the data The national accounts cover the entire geographical area of the Dominican Republic. There are no regional estimates. All goods and services transactions are covered, both in the domestic economy and with the rest of the world. The set of data currently presented includes: Gross Domestic Product, compiled using the production and expenditure approaches, both at current prices and chain-linked volume indices with 2007 as the reference year; supply and use tables; integrated economic accounts; and the Monthly Economic Activity Index (IMAE in Spanish), which is a chain-linked volume index with 2007 as the reference year, which series are also presented seasonally adjusted data and in trend-cycle terms. Unrecorded activity Illicit activities are not estimated. 2.4.1 Valuation The output is valued at basic and producer prices, intermediate consumption at market prices and GDP at market prices. Imports are CIF (Cost, Insurance and Freight) recorded. Exports are FOB (Free On Board) recorded. Uses of goods and services are valued at purchasers' prices (including margins and taxes). Foreign-currency transactions are converted using the monthly average buying and selling exchange rate including transfers and excluding the Central Bank average spot exchange rates. 2.4.2 Recording basis All transactions are recorded on an accrual basis. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures A distinction is made between uses and resources and among assets and liabilities. Taxes on products are presented isolated from subsidies in the annual Supply and Use Table. However, on a quarterly basis, these are presented in net values. The main macroeconomic aggregates are expressed in gross terms. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Annual and monthly information on Agriculture is provided by the Ministry of Agriculture and its dependencies. It contains volume and prices data, as well as operational costs and work-in-progress by product. Non-financial institutions information is gathered in financial statements provided by representative units according to its economic activity. Direct communication is performed between these units and the technicians of the National Accounts Department, therefore timely information is received for annual, quarterly and monthly estimates of volume and prices. Government data is opportunely received comprising all income and expenditure operations performed by this sector. Financial data is available through online banking system, with detailed information on income, operational costs, assets and liabilities. Households information is collected through the National Continuous Labor Force Survey with quarterly result and carried out on by the Central Bank. For detailed and structured data a Households Income and Expenditure Survey was completed for 2007. Volume and prices indicators are grouped for final consumption quarterly estimates. Goods and services exports and imports statistics are received on a monthly basis, allowing the classification of the latter by economic uses. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording The valuation and time of recording of the source data are consistent with the recommendations of the 2008 SNA that apply to the Dominican context. Dominican Republic’s national accounts have product classifications of economic activities, institutional sectors and economic transactions following the 2008 SNA and other international classifications. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Financial sector, Government and Balance of Payments statistics are timely completed. Non-financial sector units report volume and prices information directly to the Central Bank on a monthly basis, and financial statements in an annual basis. 3.2.1 Source data assessment The information is processed following the proper evaluation criteria. There is an ongoing contact with the information providers, in which atypical values are confirmed. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Statistical techniques are implemented in data compilation in order to guarantee thorough results. These techniques involve procedures which significantly reduce processing errors. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures In current terms, annual preliminary estimates are the four quarters sum. For volume estimates, quarterly chain-linked volume indices are developed to compose the quarterly growth rates of real GDP; Proportional Denton procedure is implemented as the "benchmarking” methodology to combine these indices with annual estimates. The X-13 ARIMA-SEATS process is used to obtain the seasonally adjusted data and trend-cycle estimates series for both, the quarterly GDP and the IMAE (monthly series). 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results Results are cross-checked against data from administrative sources along with the performance of related indicators as references. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data Irregular information is confirmed or reconciled with the data supplier and the explanations are kept documented. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs On a regular basis, for annual estimates, any supply-use discrepancy is considered to be part of the least reliable source of information. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses Annual estimates revisions are performed once the compilation of the supply and use tables is integrated in annual series. Monthly and quarterly revisions are introduced due to replacements of forecasts for real data. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request Responses to requests of unpublished but non-confidential available information are provided to users.
