 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The Balance of Payments statistics are compiled by the Department of Statistics (DOS) of the Department of Economic Planning and Development (DEPD), Ministry of Finance and Economy. The compilation incorporates elements of the IMF’s Balance of Payments and International Investment Position Manual, Sixth Edition (BPM6). Brunei Darussalam’s BOP is a statistical summary of international transactions between Brunei Darussalam’s residents and the rest of the world. The analytical framework consists of goods and services account, primary income account, secondary income account, capital account, financial account, reserve assets, and net errors and omissions. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The classification system follows closely the recommendations of the BPM6. 2.2.1 Scope The classification system follows closely the recommendations of the BPM6. Scope of the data Transactions coverage: The balance of payments covers all transactions of Brunei residents and the rest of the world. All transactions by resident individuals, enterprises, corporations, institutions and entities, and other external transactions of the economy are covered, in line with the standard and guidelines recommended in BPM6. The data are presented in Brunei Dollars. Geographical coverage: The geographical territory of reference is the territory of Brunei Darussalam. Exceptions to coverage No transactions are intentionally excluded or included. Unrecorded activity No estimates are made for unrecorded activities. 2.4.1 Valuation Valuation: Imports of goods are valued at Cost Insurance and Freight (CIF) basis and are adjusted to Free on Board (FOB) valuation using benchmark ratios. Exports are valued at FOB basis. Other transactions whenever possible are valued at market prices. 2.4.2 Recording basis Conversion to unit of account: Data reported in foreign currency are converted to Brunei dollars using the exchange rates provided by the Autoriti Monetari Brunei Darussalam (AMBD) at the average rates in the reference period. Time of recording : All transactions are recorded when the change of ownership takes place. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The principal data sources for balance of payments are from reports by major enterprises collected through quarterly and annual surveys conducted by the Department, as well as from administrative records provided by government agencies and AMBD. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Current Accounts: Goods : Trade in goods items in BOP covers goods whose economic ownership is changed between residents and non-residents. Adjustments are made to trade statistics for the “goods” component of the BOP, to convert CIF imports to FOB basis. The adjustments are calculated on the basis of freight and insurance rates obtained from Royal Customs and Excise Department. Services : The primary data sources are the Survey of International Services and administrative data. The data on transport services are sourced from administrative data sources and several surveys i.e. airline and shipping surveys. The data sources are organized by mode of transport, consistent with the BPM6 standard components. On the travel services, the data sources are control posts data from Immigration Department, tourism data from Ministry of Primary Resources and Tourism, expenditure data from Household Expenditure Survey by the Department, health-related travel data from the Ministry of Health, and education-related travel expenditures from the Ministry of Education On other services, data on cross-border services transactions are captured through the Survey of International Services for enterprises and administrative data for general government. Primary income: Data on investment income are derived from various data sources including from AMBD, Survey of International Investment and other administrative records. Secondary Income: The data sources are from administrative records of government agencies including AMBD. Capital Account: Currently there are no records of capital transfers from government and other agencies. Financial Account Direct Investment: There are no data on foreign direct investment abroad as no data can be obtained for the external transactions. On the foreign direct investment in Brunei Darussalam, data sources are primarily from the Survey of International Investment. Portfolio Investment: Information is obtained from AMBD, general government, corporations and households. Other Investment: The data source for various components of other investment is Survey of International Investment as well as from AMBD. Reserve Assets: Data for reserve assets are provided by AMBD. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Data sources are compiled on quarterly and annual basis. 3.2.1 Source data assessment Updating of survey coverage is performed on a quarterly basis. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Goods: Adjustments are made to exclude the cost of freight and insurance from imports from CIF basis. The adjustments are based on customs data from Royal Customs and Excise Department. Services: Transports: For imports of freight services, the data are extracted from import CIF of goods. Travel services: Travel services credits: Data model incorporating average expenditure, number of foreign tourist and length of stay from Immigration Department and Ministry of Primary Resources and Tourism. Travel services debits: Data model using average expenditure from Household Expenditure Survey, incorporating number of travelers from the Immigration Department, health-related data from Ministry of Health and education-related data from the Ministry of Education. Insurance services: For imports of insurance on freight, the data are extracted from import CIF of goods. Other services: No adjustments are made to source data. Primary Income: No adjustments are made to source data. Secondary Income: No adjustments are made to source data. Direct investment: The Survey of International Investment is designed for collecting data on foreign direct investment in Brunei Darussalam. The movements in financial assets and liabilities due to other changes are excluded from the value of transactions. Portfolio investment: No adjustments are made to source data. Other investment: No adjustments are made to source data. Reserve assets: No adjustments are made to source data. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Seasonal adjustment: No seasonal adjustment are compiled in the balance of payments data 5.1.5 Dissemination on request Data can be requested via email at info.statistics@jpke.gov.bn
