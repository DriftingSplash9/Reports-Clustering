 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The Budgetary Central Government Statement of Operations integrates, from a statistical perspective, the flows of revenue, expenses, the net investment in nonfinancial assets, the net acquisition of financial assets, and the net incurrence of liabilities. The Budgetary Central Government Statement of Operations is compiled based on the concepts and definitions of the Government Finance Statistics Manual 2014 of the International Monetary Fund (IMF). The Fiscal Analysis and Policy Directorate (DGAPF) of the Ministry of Finance and the Fiscal Affairs Division (DAF) of the Central Bank are responsible for compiling and publishing the Budgetary Central Government Statement of Operations. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Revenue and expenditure classifications are based on the Public Sectors’ Budgetary Classification Manual: Revenue is classified by economic type and by collection agency and economic type. Expenditure is classified by economic type, function, purpose, and institution. Scope of the data The Dominican Republic’s “Central government’s” (budgetary government) Statement of Operations includes revenues on a cash basis, expenditures on accrual basis, and external financing on a due for payment basis. Since the implementation of SIGEF, however, information is available on commitments, accruals, and payments. Resident units are covered. In addition, the net credit reports of the Depository Companies to the Non-Financial Public Sector of the Central Bank are included. Budgetary central government statistics cover state ministries, the Office of the Attorney General of the Republic, special bodies (Central Electoral Board and Chamber of Accounts), Senate and Chamber of Deputies, and the Supreme Court of Justice and the judicial system. Transactions of the Rest of the Non-Financial Public Sector corresponding to financial assets and liabilities are also included. The rest of Non-Financial Public Sector includes the rest of the central government, such as decentralized and autonomous institutions and social security, as well as city councils and non-public enterprises financial statements classified according to the Government Finance Statistics Manual of the International Monetary Fund. Unrecorded activity Some expenditure for fixed capital formation financed from abroad are not completely recorded or are recorded with delays because of the lags in reporting by executing agencies. 2.4.1 Valuation Data is available in the local currency, Dominican pesos. Any transactions in foreign currency are expressed in Dominican pesos using the prevailing exchange rate at the moment the transaction or activity was registered. 2.4.2 Recording basis Revenue is recorded at the time physical information is received from collecting offices. Revenue is recorded on a cash basis. Interest payments and amortization on external debt, is adjusted to a due for payment basis. Expenditure is recorded at the time of commitment, accrual, and payment. Transactions in financial assets and liabilities are recorded at the time of commitment, accrual, and payment. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs SIGEF is the official recording system for the budgetary central government. Sources for daily reports containing preliminary “revenue” data are the “revenue” reports submitted by the General Directorate of Domestic Taxes, the General Directorate of Customs, and the National Treasury Office. This information is provided daily by SIGEF since August 2004. For public debt data, SIGADE is the official recording system. The Public Credit Department (DCP) of the Ministry of Finance keeps records of and administers payments on the external debt. From August 2004 on, it maintains the external debt database, generates the corresponding reports, and take care of payments management, which until then had been done by the International Department of the Central Bank. The Monetary and Financial Statistics division of the Central Bank records the variation in the net credit of the Central Bank and commercial banks to the Non-Financial Public Sector. . 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques No adjustments are made to the source data, which in the budgetary central government is provided by SIGEF. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Consolidation of the information obtained from SIGEF and SIGADE on disbursements for investment projects.
