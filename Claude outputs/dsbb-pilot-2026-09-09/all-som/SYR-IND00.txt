 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The Central Bureau of Statistics (CBStat) compiles an Index Number for Industrial Production (IPI) confined to the industrial production of the public sector (public corporations). The IPI is based on the Laspeyres method of calculation. The current base year for the IPI is 2000. The weights used in the index are also based on 2000 data. The IPI shows the change in real output of the industrial production of the public sector. The CBStat also compiles and disseminates data on the private sector industrial production. The following types of data are published: industrial production by value, production in quantities. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The IPI is classified based on ISIC (rev.3). Scope of the data The IPI covers all public sector units engaged in industrial production. It covers all industrial products produced by these enterprises. Annual survey data on industrial production of private enterprises covers all the provinces in Syria and all establishments with 8 or more employees starting in 2007 for 2006 data. In the past (including data for 2005), all establishments with 10 or more employees were covered. These data cover a number of specific key products (in quantities) and all products produced by these units (by value). Units with less than 8 employees are covered by a sample of about 5000 establishments. Quarterly industrial survey data of private enterprises cover a sample of 400 enterprises representing key industrial activities. The product coverage includes all industrial products of the surveyed establishments. 2.4.1 Valuation The valuation of IPI is based on current prices. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The source for compiling the IPI is the annual reports of the public sector enterprises engaged in industrial production. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Annual reports of public sector enterprises are due in 3-6 months after the reference year. The annual survey of private enterprises is conducted based on the CBStat annual plan; the survey responses are received in about 5 days after the field work. The quarterly survey of private enterprises is conducted in the first 10 days after the end of the quarter and completed responses are received by the end of the month. None None
