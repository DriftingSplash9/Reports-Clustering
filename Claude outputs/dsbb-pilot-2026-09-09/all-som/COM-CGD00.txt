 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions As defined by the DND, debt includes the financial liabilities of the government with respect to third parties. This refers mainly to external liabilities since domestic debt is limited to payment arrears. The Excel tables produced by the DND are more of an administrative report than statistics in the macroeconomic sense, that is, in the sense used in the 2001 Government Finance Statistics Manual or in the Public Sector Debt Statistics Guide for Compilers and users (IMF, 2011, currently available only in English). 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Neither a presentation of Comorian public debt nor an activity report from the National Statistics Directorate (DNS) is disseminated to the public. The outstanding debt managed by the DND is presented in an Excel file listing individual loans grouped as either bilateral or multilateral debt. Scope of the data Since the Union is the only entity authorized to engage in domestic and external borrowing, the debt managed by the Debt Directorate covers all contractual obligations of the Comorian government , that is, the central administration (the Union). The public debt of the Comoros is comprised primarily of external debt. The Comoros do not have any conventional domestic debt since there is no domestic market that allows for the issuing of government securities. Exceptions to coverage The financial obligations of the government that are comprised of statutory advances from the BCC and substantial arrears on payments of wages and goods and services, as well as cross debt with the pension fund and government enterprises, are not included in the public debt managed by the DND. This domestic debt is recorded by the Treasury and is comprised essentially of advances. 2.4.1 Valuation Public debt is valued at nominal value, corresponding to the actual contractual liability and interest arrears, if any. The exchange rate in effect at the end of the accounting period is used for the conversion of outstanding debt in Comorian francs. 2.4.2 Recording basis Outstanding public debt is recorded by the DND at the time the debt is incurred, which corresponds to the accrual basis. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures Public debt is recorded on a gross basis. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The source data for external debt statistics are drawn from contracts and agreements signed with creditors. The source data for domestic liabilities referred to above (see heading 2.2.1.2) are drawn from government financial statements. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Generally speaking, the characteristics and degree of detail of information contained in agreements with creditors, as well as information from government financial statements, should be enough to prepare various types of breakdowns in accordance with international standards on debt, including, as needed, an economic classification by type of instrument and creditor. This type of breakdown is not currently being produced. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Source data are derived from the documentation needed by the DND to manage the debt that falls within the competence. These data are generated on a continuous basis and are continuously available to officers in the Debt Directorate. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Where applicable, for purposes of calculating outstanding debt at the end of a period, adjustments are made to take account of exchange rate movements. Such revaluation is carried out on the basis of the exchange rate between the Comorian franc and the currency in which the debt is denominated, as at the date the debt statement is prepared. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results An annual reconciliation is performed with donors and creditors. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses The data on external public debt are not generally subject to revision, except possibly in the process of reconciliation with donors and creditors. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses Public debt statistics are not disseminated to the public. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request Public debt statistics are not disseminated to the public.
