 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Concepts, definitions and methodologies comply basically with recommendations of the UN Statistical Agency, IMTS Rev.3 enacted in 2010. Before 2009, statistics of international trade in goods in Vietnam are synthesized by expending special trade system (Relaxed Special Trade System). Since 2009, follow the general trade system (General Trade System) 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization International merchandise trade statistics are collected based on the list of import and export goods of Vietnam using Harmonized System (HS 6 digit) of the World Customs Organization, regularly updated by the new version. The current version is HS 2017. Annual figures are converted to SITC and ISIC using compatible encoding of statistical agency of the United Nations Breakdown by partner country based on classification of country / territory of the United Nations Statistics. 2.2.1 Scope International merchandise trade statistics are collected based on the list of import and export goods of Vietnam using Harmonized System (HS 6 digit) of the World Customs Organization, regularly updated by the new version. Scope of the data 1.Exports: include domestic goods and re-export goods which are exported to the rest of the world, reducing domestic physical wealth. Exports under FOB price (or similar others) is the cost of product being delivered to export port (excluding insurance and freight) for a defined period of time and by USD, of which: Domestic goods are goods originally produced, processed and exploited in Viet Nam Re-export: goods imported into Vietnam and then exported again in the same state as previously imported or just initially processing, preserving or repacking without changing the basic nature of the goods, 2. Imports: include foreign goods and re-import goods entered Vietnam from the rest of the world, increasing domestic physical wealth. Imports under CIF price (or similar others) is the cost of product being delivered to the first import port for a defined period of time and by USD, of which: Foreign goods are goods originally produced, processed and exploited a broad under origin conditions of Viet Nam. Re-import: goods of Viet Nam exported abroad and then imported again in the same state as previously imported or just initially processing, preserving or repacking without changing the basic nature of the goods, 3. Specific Coverage: Goods brought into or taken out of the country on a contract; Goods traded in accordance with barter agreements. These are goods that are exchanged between countries without use of any means of payment. Goods for processing with or without change of ownership. Goods for processing are goods that are sent abroad or brought into a country under a specific arrangement between the involved parties (which may or may not include the change of ownership) as materials for processing; goods sample for processing… Goods of business transactions between parent companies, subsidiary direct investment abroad; Re-export/re-import: foreign goods/goods originated from Viet Nam imported/exported and compiled into imports/exports of Viet Nam, and then exported/imported again in the same state as previously imported/exported or just initially processing, preserving or repacking without changing the basic nature of the goods, except goods temporarily imported subject to inspection and supervision by the customs authorities and be re-exported under the provisions of law - Goods on the type of loan, government aid, non-governmental aid; Goods leaving or entering under the government foreign-aid programmes or sponsored by non-governmental organisations; Goods under financial lease contracts, under which the tenant with rights and responsibilities, risk relating to goods. If the contract does not specify the contents above, it is based on lease term with 12 months or more; Goods returned in import and export business; Temporary import and re-export. These include goods temporarily imported within a limited time and then converted into other customs code (out of code of temporarily import and re-export). These goods includes: Exhibition goods; Materials, equipment for workshop science research, education, sport competition, cultural performances, research and development of products, transportation vehicles, containers and equipment relating to transportation….. Shuttle trade without trade contract; The typical goods: Non-monetary gold: gold in the form of bars, ingots, powder, chips, flakes imported and exported by enterprises, commercial banks (excluding banks receive transactions authorized by the State Bank) for purposes of commerce, processing, manipulated in accordance with law; Bank notes, securities to be issued, coins not into circulation; coin or paper money collections; Means of information storage, image: magnetic tapes, magnetic disks, CD-ROM, smart card ... whether or not recording, video recording, data or computer software, designed to be shared or to buy / sell (except for goods produced by specific requirements of foreign customers); Goods sent abroad or sent from abroad by mail or delivery, valued in excess of duty-free export regulations, import as prescribed by law; Electricity, gas and water supply; Goods exported or imported using the method of e-commerce: the exchange of information, orders, contracts and payment of trade with foreign countries is done through the Internet but the goods are taken out (or into) the territory of Vietnam and implementation of common customs procedures; Goods, fuel purchasing and selling vehicles for use in international cruise traffic; Minerals explored in continental shelf areas, international border, overlapping areas ... and sold to foreign countries; Drilling Rig equipment to buy, sell off; Aircraft, boats and other vehicles of the transaction does not make customs declarations; Goods received or sent abroad by international organizations are included in imports of the countries receiving goods from such organizations and in exports of countries sending goods to those organizations (excluding goods sending between international organizations); Migrants’ effects. Waste and scrap having no commercial value 2.4.1 Valuation Value of Imported goods is based on CIF and value of Exported goods is based on FOB. Time for statistics is the time for the Customs agencies accept the customs declaration. 2.4.2 Recording basis Based on customs declaration. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Import and export goods data are mainly aggregated from customs declaration. Additional data sources for the transaction without making customs declaration: from report of businesses on exporting crude oil in offshore areas. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Consistent with the standards of UN statistics on the definitions, scope, classifications, Valuation and time of recording for monthly dissemination. For yearly official data: time of dissemination is 10 months late. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Trade data of HS 6 digit was supplied by customs administration, which is late under regulation 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Index of goods volume: not fully meet the provisions of United Nations Statistics agency. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures The change in value is adjusted regularly if there are changes or adjustments for goods declaration or declaration previously recorded with temporary price (for example the case of crude oil). After Customs Office processes and distributes by HS-based system, data are transferred to the General Statistics Office (GSO)..General Statistics Office (GSO) complemented into total imports the data of fuel bought a broad for aircraft from report of Viet Nam aviation corporation and integrated for partner countries and major goods under SITC, VSIC (under ISIC 4) …… 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs Data compared to some main partners (e.g. China, Singapore) was difference between countries, trading partners groups (country of consignment and country of origin), smuggling good across border, etc. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request Providing data under request of international organizations, embassies, other organizations, businesses and individuals and ensuring the principle of confidentiality in accordance with the Statistics Law.
