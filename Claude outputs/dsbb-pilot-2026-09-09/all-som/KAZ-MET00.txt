 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Merchandise exports are goods exported from Kazakhstan (including exports of temporarily imported goods for inward processing, exports of previously imported goods in free circulation, and exports of goods from customs warehouses) to be traded with the rest of the world. Merchandise imports are goods from the rest of the world entering free circulation within Kazakhstan; imports cover goods declared for home use, temporary imports of goods for processing and re-export, and imports into customs warehouses. The coverage of the data relates to the general trade principle, following the United Nations International Trade Statistics: Concepts and Definitions. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Merchandise trade is classified using the Commodity Nomenclature for Foreign Economic Activity of CIS Countries classification system (TN VED CIS). Relationship of national practices with international and regional guidelines: The Commodity Nomenclature for Foreign Economic Activity of CIS Countries classification system is developed on the basis of the six-digit Harmonized Commodity Description and Coding System (HS) and the Combined Tariff and Statistical Nomenclature of the European Union (CN EU). Compilation of the merchandise statistics is in line with the recommendations of UN ITS and IMF's Balance of Payments Statistics Manual (BPM5). Scope of the data The coverage of the data relates to the general trade principle, following the United Nations International Trade Statistics: Concepts and Definitions . Exports cover all goods exported from Kazakhstan (including exports of temporarily imported goods for inward processing, exports of previously imported goods in free circulation, and exports of goods from customs warehouses), excluding monetary gold, goods in transit, goods to and from territorial enclaves, leased goods, merchandise for embassies and staff, humanitarian aid and merchandise exported from and imported into customs warehouses and free zones.] Imports cover goods declared for home use, temporary imports of goods for processing and re-export, and imports into customs warehouses. The geographic distribution of external trade flows is the entire economic territory of the country coinciding with the customs territory: Exports - by country of destination (consumption) of the goods; Imports - by country of shipment of the goods (1994-1997), and beginning in 1998-by country of origin of the goods. Exceptions to coverage The General Trade System is used to compile trade data that covers all international transactions in merchandise goods are covered excluding monetary gold, goods in transit, goods to and from territorial enclaves, leased goods, merchandise for embassies and staff, humanitarian aid and merchandise exported from and imported into customs warehouses and free zones. Unrecorded activity Estimates of unrecorded transactions, including smuggling and 'shuttle' trade, are presently not included in merchandise trade statistics. 2.4.1 Valuation Data are disseminated in millions of US dollars on total exports, total imports , and the trade balance. Exports are valued free on board (f.o.b) and imports on cost, insurance and freight (c.i.f) basis. Barter transactions are valued at market price.Data are monthly. The conversion of foreign currencies into dollars is effected at the official exchange rate established by the Republic's National Bank as of the date of acceptance of the customs declaration for processing. 2.4.2 Recording basis Transactions are recorded daily at the customs points in the currencies of transaction and converted into US dollars at the official exchange rate established by the Republic's National Bank as of the date of acceptance of the customs declaration for processing. Timing of recording transactions: Imports/exports are recorded at the time when they enter/leave the economic territory as noted on the corresponding customs declarations. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The data are based on customs cargo declarations provided by the customs office. Exports and imports are recorded using the "Commodity Nomenclature for Foreign Economic Activity of CIS Countries" classification system (TN VED CIS), which was developed on the basis of the six-figure Harmonized Commodity Description and Coding System (HS) and the Combined Tariff and Statistical Nomenclature of the European Union (CN EU). Type of data collected: Value of imports and exports in US dollars and quantities in mass units and supplementary units and by country of origin/destination. Information is also collected on the types of transactions and mode of transport. Basis of country data: Imports are distinguished by country of origin and exports by country of consignment. Processing system: Customs data are processed automatically in a nationally developed computerized system. Site of processing: Statistical Office (SARK). 3.2.1 Source data assessment Adjustments to improve coverage/classification/timing/valuation: Presently adjustments are not made for unrecorded transactions. Neither are adjustments made to classification, timing and valuation. However, different quality checks are undertaken (validity checks, price controls, and logical controls) before the final release of the data. None None
