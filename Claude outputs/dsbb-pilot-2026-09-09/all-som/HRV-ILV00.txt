 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Concept: Gross international reserves comprise external assets that are readily available to and controlled by monetary authorities for direct financing of payment imbalances. As of January 2023, after the Republic of Croatia joined the euro area, the national currency kuna was exchanged for the euro. Therefore, the first part of the Template shows the total foreign currency asset of the CNB, i.e. the assets in a currency other than the euro. The foreign currency asset shown in the first part of the Template are divided into two sections. Section IA shows the total foreign currency assets of the CNB by non-euro area counterparties, accounting for international reserves. Section IB shows other foreign currency asset of the CNB, which includes foreign currency claims towards euro area counterparties. The decline of value presented in the data is caused by Croatia joining to the euro area. Definition: Gross external reserves are composed of liquid foreign currency claims on highly ranked foreign institutions or governments. Relationship to international guidelines: Data are compiled and published on the basis of definitions contained in the sixth edition of the IMF's Balance of Payments Manual and International Reserves and Foreign Currency Liquidity Guidelines for a data template . 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Classification: Instruments included within the definition of gross international reserves are classified into Special Drawing Rights, the Reserve Position in the IMF, and holdings of foreign currency, deposits, and securities. Scope of the data Institutional coverage: Only claims of CNB on nonresidents are included in gross international reserves. Instrument coverage: Reserve assets consists of highly liquid assets in the form of instruments such as deposits and securities of highly ranked foreign banks and foreign government securities and also total amount of SDRs held by the CNB with the International Monetary Fund. No assets, which are commited, pledged or subject of restriction are included. 2.4.1 Valuation Valuation: Data on international reserves are valuated at prevailing market prices. Conversion rates: International reserves data are converted into euro using end of period official exchange rate. The official exchange rate is published daily by Croatian National Bank.The values for SDRs and Reserve Position in the IMF are determined at the IMF basket valuation for the SDR. 2.4.2 Recording basis Frequency of calculation and revaluation: International reserves are calculated monthly. Data are publicly disseminated on a monthly basis. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Data sources: Data are obtained from the accounting data of Croatian National Bank and Reserve asset management department.
