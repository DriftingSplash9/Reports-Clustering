 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions In line with the sixth edition of the IMF's Balance of Payments Manual ( BPM6) methodological requirements, the IMF's Operational Guidelines for the compilation of the data template on international reserves and foreign currency liquidity, and the ECB recommendation on Eurosystem reserve assets (see methodological note (http://www.ecb.int) ). The reserve assets must: (i) be under the effective control of the Bank of Italy; and (ii) refer to highly liquid, marketable and creditworthy non-euro area currency-denominated claims on non-residents of the euro area as well as gold, special drawing rights and the reserve positions in the IMF of Italy. Deposits with banks located in Italy and/or in other euro area Member States are, therefore, not considered as part of the official reserves. All reversible gold transactions are treated as collateralized loans and recorded accordingly in the national reserves statistics. Scope of the data Institutional coverage: Bank of Italy. Instrument coverage: Data grouped in three items: gold and gold receivables, receivables from the IMF and other claims on non euro area residents in foreign (non euro) currencies. Financial derivatives coverage: Financial derivatives are identified separately in the Italy's international reserves and foreign currency liquidity and, at an annual frequency , in the Statistical Appendix of the BI's Annual Report. Exceptions to coverage 2.4.1 Valuation The valuation is based on market prices by using: (i) the prevailing market prices at the time when the transaction takes place for transactions; and (ii) the closing mid-market prices at the end of the appropriate period for holdings. The conversion of foreign currency-denominated assets into euro is based on: (i) monthly average foreign exchange rate for flows; and (ii) as regards stocks, the mid-point foreign exchange rate on the last day of the reference period. Specific issues: Gold is valued monthly at end of month market price; foreign exchange holdings are revalued/devalued at the end of month foreign exchange rates. 2.4.2 Recording basis Frequency of calculation and revaluation: Reserve assets are calculated on a monthly basis. The figures are revalued on a monthly basis to meet the b.o.p/i.i.p. standards and to take into account the accrual principle. The monthly revaluation criterion has been introduced from the September 2000 press release: Bank of Italy Balance Sheet Aggregates and Official Reserves (before, quarterly revaluation). As from 1999, the value of the gold holdings is based on market value at the end of the period. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures Reserve assets are compiled on a gross basis without any netting of reserve-related liabilities (with the exception of those reserve assets included in the sub-item of financial derivatives, which are recorded on a net basis). 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The data are based on the information provided by the Accounting Department of the Bank of Italy and, for a part of the Reserve assets template, by the Ministry of Economy and Finance. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Definitions follow the IMF International reserves and foreign currency liquidity - Guidelines for a data template, the ECB Guideline on the statistical reporting requirements of the ECB in the field of balance of payments and international investment position statistics, and the international reserves template (ECB/2004/15, amended by the Guideline ECB/2007/3), and the ECB Statistical treatment of the Eurosystem's international reserves . 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Source data are timely as regards the compilation of International reserves statistics. The Bank of Italy (BI) reports first provisional data within the seventh calendar day after the end of the reference month. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Flows are derived as change in end-of-month stock data, corrected for exchange rate changes and price changes (on a monthly basis). Price and exchange rate revaluations/devaluations are carried out monthly at end of month prices. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs No deviations from the ECB recommendations contained in the document The statistical treatment of positions and transactions involving the Eurosystem's international reserves. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points
