 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The dissemination of the international reserves statistics complies with the recommendations of the sixth edition of the IMF's Balance of Payments Manual (BPM6) , the IMF manual on international reserves and foreign currency liquidity and the ECB's recommendation for the compilation of the Eurosystem's reserve assets. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Data are disseminated in millions of Euro and US dollars. Data disseminated comprises: Official international reserves broken down by major components: monetary gold, foreign exchange assets, SDRs, the reserve position in the Fund and other foreign currency assets Predetermined short-term net drains on foreign currency assets Contingent short-term net drains of foreign currency assets Memo items Scope of the data Institutional coverage: Central Bank of Malta. Instrumental coverage: For the reserve assets, coverage consists of claims on non-euro area residents denominated in foreign currencies, monetary gold, SDRs and reserve position in the IMF. For other currency assets coverage comprises claims of the central bank on euro-area residents in foreign currency including financial derivatives.' Financial derivatives coverage: financial derivatives can be identified separately. 2.4.1 Valuation The valuation is based on market prices by using (i) the prevailing market prices at the time the transaction takes places and (ii) the market prices prevailing on the respective stock exchange at the close of business on the last working day of the reference period for holdings. The conversion of foreign currency denominated assets into euro is based on the actual exchange rate used on the transaction date. As regards stocks the exchange rate is that prevailing at the close of business on the last working day of the reference period. 2.4.2 Recording basis Full accruals accounting is used. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures Reserve assets are compiled on a gross basis without any netting of reserve–related liabilities (with the exception of those reserve assets included in the sub-item of financial derivatives which are recorded on a net basis). 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Data sources for the compilation of the reserves template: Data relevant for the monetary authorities are obtained from the Financial Control Department of the Central Bank of Malta. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Data obtained from the Financial Control Department of the Central Bank of Malta are consistent with statistical requirements. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Data collection and processing timetables are designed to meet the ECB data transmission deadlines. 3.2.1 Source data assessment The data are obtained from the returns filled in by the CBM Financial Control Department. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques There is no need for any grossing up procedures to make up for any missing data. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Data disseminated are non-seasonally adjusted. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results The checking and vetting of the data focus mainly on identifying any irregularities, inconsistencies, reclassifications and any outliers throughout the reported return. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data The reserve asset figure is also reconciled with the balance sheet of the CBM as reported in the BR06 returns. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs The Financial Control Department may be asked for ad hoc information on non-plausible changes and alerted for any discrepancies in the stock data and the necessary corrections are carried out accordingly. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses In the case of reclassifications, data may be revised over a longer time period. If these reclassifications are substantial, they are explained in the notes. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses When major revisions to the compilation methodology are carried out, the CBM provides explanations, in the form of articles or box items, in its official publications or on its website. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request It is the policy of the CBM to provide consolidated or aggregated statistics to the public or the media when it deems necessary . Statistics to be provided should not be confidential or sensitive in nature. Statistics to be provided are principally those for which the compilation of which the CBM is either fully responsible or has shared responsibility. Requests for statistics, or queries on statistics, are received in writing or by e-mail, through the Statistics Department’s general email address contact@centralbankmalta.org 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points Adequate assistance is given to the general public or the media on request. Any requests for data are either received in the CBM’s Statistics Department’s general e-mail address contact@centralbankmalta.org , or in BOP Section's mailbox bop@centralbankmalta.org . 5.3.2 Availability of documents and services catalogs Information can be found on the CBM’s website.
