 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Concepts and Definition The IIP statistics for Mauritius is consistent with the methodology prescribed in the sixth edition of the IMF's Balance of Payments and International Investment Position Manual (BPM6). 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Classification/Sectorization The IIP, by convention, is classified into assets and liabilities with the difference between the two representing the net asset position. Consistent with the standard components of the financial account of the balance of payments, the components are broken down by functional category, where assets are divided into direct investment, portfolio investment, other investment, financial derivatives and reserve assets while liabilities are divided into direct investment, portfolio investment, financial derivatives and other investment (i.e., standard components recommended by the BPM6). With regard to sectorization, within ‘other sectors’, Global Business License Holders (GBLH) companies are separately identified. 2.2.1 Scope Classification/Sectorization The IIP, by convention, is classified into assets and liabilities with the difference between the two representing the net asset position. Consistent with the standard components of the financial account of the balance of payments, the components are broken down by functional category, where assets are divided into direct investment, portfolio investment, other investment, financial derivatives and reserve assets while liabilities are divided into direct investment, portfolio investment, financial derivatives and other investment (i.e., standard components recommended by the BPM6). With regard to sectorization, within ‘other sectors’, Global Business License Holders (GBLH) companies are separately identified. Scope of the data In principle, IIP statistics produced by the Bank is a statistical statement that presents the market value and composition of the stocks of the financial assets and liabilities of residents vis-à-vis non-residents. Concerning the definition of residency, the general principles set in the BPM6 Manual are applied for both household and institutional units. Exceptions to coverage The only exception to the present coverage is the cross-border positions of Authorised Companies. Unrecorded activity The IIP does not contain any estimate made for illegal activity and for other transactions for which data are not available. 2.4.1 Valuation Market values are applied to most securities falling under portfolio investment and reserve assets. Wherever market value is not available, the fair value is used instead. 2.4.2 Recording basis To the extent possible, the recording of transactions is carried out on an accrual basis. However, transactions data culled from the ITRS (that are sometimes used to derive the position data) are on a cash basis. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures Grossing and netting procedures are consistent with BPM6 recommendations. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The following agents report data to the Economic Analysis & Research and Statistics Department of the Bank: (i) Monetary authorities sector: The Accounting and Budgeting Division of the Bank provides a monthly balance sheet to the Economic Analysis & Research and Statistics Department containing disaggregated data on reserve assets as well as data on non-residents' holdings of Bank securities and other liabilities of the Bank. (ii) General government sector: The Ministry of Finance provides data on outstanding external debt of general government as well as public financial and non-financial institutions. The Accounting and Budgeting Division provides data on the stock of non-residents' holdings of government and Bank of Mauritius money market instruments. As from September 2023, the National Pensions Fund (NPF) is requested to provide a quarterly statement showing the position data for its portfolio assets. (iii) Deposit-taking corporations, except central bank: The Monetary and Financial Statistics Section within the Statistics Division of the Economic Analysis & Research and Statistics Department of the Bank provides the aggregated balance sheet of banks collected through the Other Depository Corporations (ODC) survey on a monthly basis. (iv) Other sectors: Data on the investment position (liabilities) of listed companies (i.e. equity held by non-resident shareholders) on the Stock Exchange is culled from the Central Depository System (CDS) in its capacity as a central securities depository and registry of shareholders for listed companies. The Bank currently conducts three surveys, the FALS, ESSNAC and Other Financial Corporations (OFC) Survey that separately collect cross-border transactions as well as position data of financial and non-financial entities. Specific items: Direct investment Position data are obtained from the Foreign Assets and Liabilities Survey (FALS) and ESSNAC Survey as well as OFC Survey. A simple accumulation of flows is used to estimate FDI position for those sectors not covered in the FALS. Data is also culled from the Supervision Department of the Bank for FDI data on banks. Portfolio investment The stock of portfolio investment assets is obtained from two different surveys: FALS and CPIS. The end-investor (CPIS) survey covers all resident financial entities – namely banks, insurance companies, financial services companies, investment companies and other collective investment schemes – and a sample of non-financial domestic and international companies with a physical presence in Mauritius. The National Pensions Fund's portfolio assets as well as those of the National Savings Fund are included with effect from end-December 2012. Concerning portfolio investment liabilities vis-à-vis non-residents, the data for listed companies are culled from the Central Depository System (CDS) of the Stock Exchange as well as ODC and OFC surveys. Financial derivatives Position data for financial derivatives vis-à-vis non-residents are obtained primarily from the balance sheets of banks as well as the annual ESSNAC survey and quarterly OFC survey.. Other investment Complete data on other investment assets and liabilities of the banking sector are compiled from the balance sheet of banks as well as the central bank. Outstanding private sector external debt is also obtained from the FALS. Trade credit assets and liabilities are obtained from the FALS and are supplemented by deriving estimates using goods exports and imports, respectively, as a basis. Reserve Assets The data source for reserve asset position is the Accounting and Budgeting Division of the Bank. The BOM also participates in the IMF's semi-annual Survey of the Geographical Distribution of Securities Held as Foreign Exchange Reserves (SEFER). 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording The source data definitions, scope, classification, valuation and time of recording are generally in line with BPM6. (For deviations please refer to 2.4.1.) 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Broadly, the source data for compiling the quarterlyl IIP are timely. In cases where data are not available at the time of dissemination, preliminary estimates are included. 3.2.1 Source data assessment The survey questionnaires designed by the Bank are revised to reflect additional data requirements as well as developments in international standards and accounting standards. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Corrections and adjustments on the reported data are made only when required or when confirmed with the reporting entities. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Data are not seasonally adjusted. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results Validation rules are implemented to check consistencies of data both within the survey form and with other sources such as stock prices, administrative data on registered equity, companies' published accounts and international transactions reporting system (ITRS). When detected, discrepancies are investigated and addressed. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data Several procedures are in place to ensure high-quality intermediate data. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs A time-consistency check is carried out for the IIP and BOP and statistical discrepancies or outliers are generally investigated prior to dissemination. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses Revisions to preliminary estimates of the IIP statistics for the previous year are carried out according to the revision policy for the External Sector Statistics posted on the Bank's website. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses Revised data are generally accompanied with an explanatory note for revision. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request Non-confidential statistics that have not been published are provided to users upon request and free of charge. 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points All publications contain a specific e-mail address relating to the subject field being disseminated. All ESS publications' contact e-mail address for users to send their queries is ess@bom.mu. 5.3.2 Availability of documents and services catalogs No formal catalogue is disseminated.
