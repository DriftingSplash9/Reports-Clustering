 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Measure of values and quantities of exports and imports between Italy and the rest of the world. Relationship of national practices with international or regional guidelines: the statistical surveys on intra EU and extra EU trade in goods are carried out according to EU Community concepts and definitions. The criteria are established by Community Legal Acts (CEE) 1736/75 of 24 June 1975 and further modifications, as the introduction in 1988 of the "Combined Nomenclature" (CN) and the "Single Administrative Document" (SAD). Since 1993, the merchandise trade among EU countries has been ruled by the INTRASTAT system on the basis of CEE Council Regulation no. 3330/91, CEE Commission Regulations no. 3046/92 and CEE Commission Regulations no. 1669/2001. The Regulations in force starting from 1 January 2022 are: Regulation (EU) 2019/2152 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 27 November 2019 on European business statistics, repealing 10 legal acts in the field of business statistics. Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2020/1197 of 30 July 2020 laying down technical specifications and arrangements pursuant to Regulation (EU) 2019/2152 of the European Parliament and of the Council on European business statistics repealing 10 legal acts in the field of business statistics. Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2021/1704 of 14 July 2021 supplementing Regulation (EU) 2019/2152 of the European Parliament and of the Council by further specifying the details for the statistical information to be provided by tax and customs authorities and amending its Annexes V and VI. Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2021/1225 specifying the arrangements for the data exchanges pursuant to Regulation (EU) 2019/2152 and amending Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2020/1197, as regards the Member State of extra-Union export and the obligations of reporting units. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Commodities: Combined Nomenclature (CN), Harmonised System (HS), Classification of products according to economic activity (CPATECO), Main Industrial Groupings (MIGS), Standard International Trade Classification (SITC) and Statistical Nomenclature by mode of transport (NST). Countries: the Eurostat Geonomenclature. Other information: Origin and destination province, mode of transport, statistical regime and foreign currency for the Extra-EU trade. Scope of the data Geographic coverage: Statistics of foreign trade are compiled with regard to customs territory. Vatican City is third country. The disseminated data for Republic of San Marino are just referred to natural gas and electricity. Free points and warehouses are additionally excluded from the customs territory. Exceptions to coverage Transactions coverage: The foreign trade surveys are realised on the basis of the special trade system, which covers goods that cross the customs frontier plus goods that are imported into and exported from custom bonded areas. The special trade system does not include goods in transit on the national territory, foreign goods placed in customs warehouses and not released for consumption and/or temporary import, goods returned to a foreign country. From 2010, data shall not be required for the following goods: means of payment which are legal tender, and securities; monetary gold; because of the diplomatic or similar nature of their intended use; provided that the trade is temporary; provided that they are not the subject of a commercial transaction; products used as part of exceptional common measures for the protection of persons or of the environment; goods used as carriers of information; goods dispatched to national armed forces stationed outside the statistical territory as well as goods received which had been conveyed outside the statistical territory by the national armed forces, as well as goods acquired or disposed of on the statistical territory of a Member State by the armed forces of a foreign State which are stationed there; satellite launchers on dispatch and on arrival; goods that are imported or exported for the repair, as well as the parts replaced during the repairs. Unrecorded activity Following the SLIM initiative of Eurostat and on the basis of Ministerial Decree dated 4 February 1998 and subsequent updates, since January 1998 a large part of economic operators that trade merchandise within the EU market have been relieved from the burden to report the statistical value of the merchandise even though they are still required to report their total turnover. Moreover, in order to reduce the Intrastat statistical burden, only Intra-EU traders with trade higher than specific thresholds are required to report their Intra-EU trade through the Intrastat data collection system. Since 2013 (Regulation EU 1093/2013), minimum data coverage required by EU regulations is 97% for intra-EU exports and 93% for intra-EU import. Since 2017, the Italian Intrastat system covers 97% for intra-EU exports and 94% for intra-EU imports. Starting from 1 January 2022, minimum data coverage required by EU regulations is 95% for intra-EU exports. No minimum coverage is required for intra-EU imports. Since 2022, the Italian Intrastat system covers 97% for intra-EU export and 91% for intra-EU imports. Appropriate estimation techniques are adopted in order to estimate the total intra-EU flows. None 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Data are collected by the Customs Agency. In the case of Extra-EU trade, data are collected on the basis of customs declarations (EUCDM B and H messages) that are filled by economic operator at every trade transaction. In the case of Intra-EU trade, data are derived from trade transactions declarations (Intrastat form) made by economic operators to Customs Agency for fiscal and statistical purposes. As the Intrastat survey is sample-based, other fiscal and administrative sources are used. Since January 2022, micro-data on intra-EU exports provided by the other EU countries (MDE - Micro-data Exchange) have been available on a monthly basis and are used to compile statistics on intra-EU imports. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Foreign trade surveys measure values and quantities of exports and imports between Italy and the rest of the world. Statistical value of merchandise is defined according to international agreements and refer to declared transaction value (imports c.i.f., exports f.o.b.). Volumes are expressed in Kilograms or in a supplementary unit of measure, according to European Regulation. Other collected information is: the merchandise code (CN8), country of origin and country of consignment for imports and country of consumption (last known destination) for exports, the province of origin or destination of merchandise flow, the mode of transport, the terms of consignment, the nature of transaction For Extrastat data also the following variables are collected: internal mode of transport, invoice currency, statistical procedure. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Extra-EU survey: collection of non-EU merchandise trade statistics as derived from administrative data on the basis of customs declarations (EUCDM B and H messages). Timing of recording transactions is the time of customs clearance. Intra-EU survey: the system concerning statistics on traded goods between EU Member States, in which time deadlines are fixed by Ministerial decree. Since 2010, economic operators have to submit monthly forms by the 25th of the month following the reference month. Since January 2018, monthly Intrastat statistical declarations have only been mandatory for economic operators who have exceeded100,000 euros for intra-EU exports, or 200,000 euros for intra-EU imports, in at least one of the previous four quarters. Regarding intra-EU imports, since January 2022, monthly Intrastat statistical declarations have only been mandatory for economic operators who have exceeded 350,000 euros for imports, in at least one of the previous four quarters. From January 2026, monthly Intrastat import declarations became mandatory only for economic operators who have exceeded 2,000,000 euros for intra-EU imports, in at least one of the previous four quarters. Micro-data on intra-EU exports of the other EU countries (MDE) are available by the 30th of the month following the reference month. VAT fiscal data are available around the 25th of the month following the reference month. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Seasonal adjustments are carried out using TRAMO-SEATS. Index numbers: merchandise trade indices are calculated on a monthly base as Fisher chained indices, the reference year is 2021. Unit Value Links are calculated at the Combined Nomenclature level. The breakdown available refers to main countries and geographic areas, sections, divisions and groups of the CPATECO 2007 classification and Main Industrial Groupings (MIGs). 5.1.5 Dissemination on request Customized statistics may be supplied if a request for statistical information cannot be met through the data bank "External Trade Statistics", in compliance with statistical confidentiality rules. Costs and time schedule will be notified to users by the competent office (https://contact.istat.it/s/?language=en_US).
