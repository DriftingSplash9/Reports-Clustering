 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The concepts and definitions used are broadly consistent with SNA93. The current publication includes current prices GDP and constant prices GDP (Base year 2000/01). GDP are compiled using both the production and expenditure approach but only the production base figures are disseminated. GNI is calculated using data from the Balance of Payments. Income and Outlay Account produced. Data are produced and disseminated by economic activity (i.e. 7 major activity). An estimate of value added is done for each industry using a mover / indicator for that industry. For government, non-market activity, is calculated each year using the government financial account. For market production groups, there was an initial estimate of value added prepared in the 1995/96 base year. Estimates for subsequent years are made using surrogates as movers from the base year. Most movers are in constant price terms. Current price values are calculated using elements of the CPI. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Industry classification by 7 production groups is consistent with ISIC Classification (Rev 1), and SNA 1993 for the Institutional Sector and Expenditure grouping. Scope of the data National Accounts covers the whole territory of Tonga. Value added and expenditure are covered. Not included are illegal activities. All resident entities engaged in an economic activity or transaction, including subsistence fishing and agriculture, are included Offshore fishing , mainly carried out as a joint venture with non-residents under license, is included. Unrecorded activity Illegal activities are not covered. An adjustment is made for the under reporting of fish and squash. 2.4.1 Valuation Subsistence value added is based on the market value of gross output less an allowance for transport and intermediate input.. Valuation: This is generally market value and follows SNA 93 conventions for non market output. 2.4.2 Recording basis Government data are on a cash basis with no allowance for depreciation. The private sector is generally on an accrual basis. The recording period is the fiscal year ended June. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs There is no formal document describing sources and methods. Production Approach: Agriculture: Talamahu market report, Exports of Agricultural and fisheries products, subsistance activity is estimated using movement in population projection. Mining and Quarrying: Sales of sand and qravel from Ministry of Land and Survey. Manufacturing: Imputed, using results of quarterly manufacturing survey and information from a survey carried out by a local bank. Electricity and Water: Production of electricity and water from administration data. Construction: Imports of building materials, housing loans from banking system, local production of building materials. Commerce & Restaurants: Tourist arrivals and level of imports. Finance, insurance and business services: Number of bank accounts, volume of bank deposits and loans, volume of insurance premiums. Government administration: Government wage bill. Personal services: Based on population change. Ownership of buildings: Base on movement in population projection. Imputed Bank Service Charge: From annual accounts of the banks. Indirect Tax: From government account. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques An adjustment is made for the under-coverage of agriculture produce in domestic market report, for the understatement or fish and agricultural exports and the changes in value where sold on consignment, and an adjustment is made for effects of natural disasters.
