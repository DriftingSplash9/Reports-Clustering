 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The concepts and definitions adopted in the compilation of Hong Kong's merchandise trade statistics are in conformity with the standards recommended by the United Nations in the International Merchandise Trade Statistics : Concepts and Definitions 2010 . The "general" system is adopted for compiling trade statistics. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Two commodity classification systems are used: the Harmonized System developed by the World Customs Organisation and the Standard International Trade Classification (SITC) developed by the United Nations. The former is mainly used for import/export declaration purpose, while the latter is used mainly for statistical reporting. Import statistics are available by country/territory of origin and/or country/territory of consignment while domestic export figures are analysed by country/territory of consignment. For re-exports, merchandise trade statistics are available by country/territory of origin and/or country/territory of consignment. 2.2.1 Scope Two commodity classification systems are used: the Harmonized System developed by the World Customs Organisation and the Standard International Trade Classification (SITC) developed by the United Nations. The former is mainly used for import/export declaration purpose, while the latter is used mainly for statistical reporting. Import statistics are available by country/territory of origin and/or country/territory of consignment while domestic export figures are analysed by country/territory of consignment. For re-exports, merchandise trade statistics are available by country/territory of origin and/or country/territory of consignment. Scope of the data Transaction coverage: Hong Kong's merchandise trade statistics refer to the movements of merchandise between Hong Kong and her trading partners, by land, air, water and by post. Geographic coverage: Hong Kong's trade with all countries/territories is included. Exceptions to coverage Movements of exempted articles, gold and specie are not included. Imports and exports of gold and specie are separately recorded and published. Unrecorded activity No estimates are made for smuggling activity. 2.4.1 Valuation Conversion to unit of account: Any transaction values in foreign currency are converted to their Hong Kong dollar equivalent based on the exchange rates prevailing at the time of importation/exportation. Valuation: Valuation of goods is based on the transaction values provided by importers/exporters in import/export declarations. Imports are valued on c.i.f. basis while domestic exports and re-exports are valued on f.o.b. basis. 2.4.2 Recording basis The time when the goods move into or out of Hong Kong is used as the time of recording. Calendar month is used as the standard reference month for monthly statistics. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures The concepts and definitions adopted in the compilation of Hong Kong's merchandise trade statistics are in conformity with the standards recommended by the United Nations in the International Merchandise Trade Statistics : Concepts and Definitions 2010 . 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Hong Kong's merchandise trade statistics are compiled based on information in import/export declarations. All declarations are processed in compiling trade statistics. There is a matching exercise against cargo manifests to ensure completeness in the submission of declarations. Major types of data collected include trade values, quantities, units of quantity, descriptions of goods (with Hong Kong Harmonized System commodity codes), mode of transport and countries/territories of origin/consignment for imports and re-exports, and countries/territories of consignment for domestic exports. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Data are supplied according to "GETS - Implementation Instructions". The information on data requirement is available on the website of the C&SD ( https://www.censtatd.gov.hk/en/page_94.html ). 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Importers/exporters are required to lodge import/export declarations within 14 days. Upon processing and verification as necessary, merchandise statistics are released within four weeks from end of the statistical month. Allowing some time for collating price data and compiling unit values, trade indices are released in another three weeks' time. 3.2.1 Source data assessment The declarations submitted by importers/exporters are matched against cargo manifests to ensure that there is no omission of declaration. It is a legal requirement for shipping companies, airline companies, transport companies or their agents to submit to the Government a manifest in respect of the cargoes carried on every ship, airplane or other transport vehicle that enters or leaves Hong Kong. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Hong Kong's merchandise trade statistics are compiled based on information given in trade declarations which importers and exporters are required by the "Import and Export (Registration) Regulations" (Chapter 60E, Laws of Hong Kong) to lodge with the Customs and Excise Department. Trade declarations received by the Customs and Excise Department are then forwarded to the Census and Statistics Department for compilation of aggregate trade statistics. Quality assurance procedures are in place to verify whether particulars in the trade declarations are sufficiently clear and complete for compiling trade statistics. Where appropriate, minor statistical adjustments are incorporated to enhance the data quality of trade statistics. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures The seasonally adjusted series is compiled by the X-12 ARIMA method. The X-12 ARIMA method and its related versions are the standard method commonly employed in compiling seasonally adjusted data series. The method can be used to detect whether outlier(s) is present in the original series for estimation of seasonal factors. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results Immediate results are reviewed against the historical data at detailed level. Where applicable, they are also checked against other related statistical indicators. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data For significant discrepancies, either from past trend or related indicators, the causes will be investigated and findings documented. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs The discrepancies in statistical outputs are generally not significant. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses Adjustments, where necessary, are incorporated in year-to-date figures.
