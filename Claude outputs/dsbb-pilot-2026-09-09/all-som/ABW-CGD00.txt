 D. Other Scope of the data Central government debt comprises all debt, short-term and long-term, incurred by the central government of Aruba. 
 
 The economic territory of Aruba is defined in accordance with the GFSM 1986 . 
 
 The central government of Aruba comprises all government Ministries and Departments, as well as the Representative Offices in the Hague, Netherlands “Arubahuis”, and Washington, DC. In addition, it comprises the Fondo Desaroyo Aruba (FDA) and the European Development Fund (EDF). Exceptions to coverage Certain institutions that need to be covered in the central government sector according to GFSM, are currently not included, as the CBA does not yet have sufficient data to fully reconcile them. These include, but are not limited to the Aruba Tourism Authority (ATA), the General Health Insurance Fund (AZV), the Social Insurance Bank (SVB), etc. 

III. Accounting Conventions
 2.4.1 Valuation Government debt is recorded based on the face value or redemption value stated in the different contracts. 
 The central government has outstanding debt in local currency, US dollars, and Euro. US dollar denominated debt is converted to local currency using a fixed exchange rate of USD 1 = Afl. 1.80. Euro denominated debt is converted to local currency using the exchange rate prevailing in the market at the end of the quarter in which the transaction takes place. Repayments are recorded based on the exchange rate prevailing in the market at the time the transaction takes place. 2.4.2 Recording basis Transactions are recorded when economic value is transferred, i.e. once a creditor has a claim on the government. The debt recording date for the most part coincides with the date on which the different contracts are signed, however, this is not always the case. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures The recording of government debt is on a gross basis. 

IV. Nature of the Basic Data Sources
 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The Statistics Department of the CBA compiles government debt data from information received in hardcopy or PDF from its Operations Department with regard to short-term debt securities. Information on long term debt securities and loans are received in hardcopy or PDF from the Department of Finance. Administrative records are checked against debt securities and loans contracts. International Monetary Fund (IMF) guidelines ( GFSM2014 ) are monitored for changes that need to be taken into account in the recording of government debt. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording The sources used for the compilation of GFS conform to the definitions and concepts of GFSM 1986 . However, they do not provide sufficient information to compile GFS on an accrual basis. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Source data updates are currently not always provided with sufficient timeliness to meet timely compilation and dissemination of statistics for central government. Reporting units are given a deadline for reporting to the CBA at the time of the data request. The CBA maintains regular contact with the reporting units to pursue the timely delivery of source data. 3.2.1 Source data assessment The source data are checked for consistency with other available related data sources. Through direct contact with the reporting units, any concerns regarding the accuracy of source data are addressed. 

V. Compilation Practices
 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Incomplete/ preliminary data are subsequently replaced by the most updated data, for each period. All data are corrected when more accurate data become available. The compilation procedures of the Statistics Department of the CBA are documented in an Internal Manual. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures There are internal tables available to facilitate adjustments to the source data, when necessary. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results Intermediate results are checked against other independent data sources, where applicable and available. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data Government debt is checked with related series/ publications and investigated when discrepancies arise. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs Values that are not within expected ranges are identified and investigated. In addition, data discrepancies are resolved and reconciled. 

VI. Other Aspects
 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses Statistics are regularly archived, and can be retrieved for revision studies. Revision in time-series are recorded in a memorandum, and appear in the explanatory notes. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses Revisions are made known in the explanatory notes. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request The CBA disseminates non-confidential government debt statistics on request.
