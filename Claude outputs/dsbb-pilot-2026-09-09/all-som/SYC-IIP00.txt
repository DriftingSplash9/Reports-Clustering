 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The balance of payments statistics are compiled broadly following the guidelines recommended in the IMF’s Balance of Payments and Investment Position Manual , sixth edition ( BPM6 ). 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization For the compilation of IIP, the classification system of the BPM6 is followed. The classification of accounts used in the national presentation contains both aggregated and detailed items None 2.4.1 Valuation Transactions are valued at market prices where they are available. Imports and exports are valued f.o.b. Transactions in currencies other than the US dollar are converted to US dollar using the official exchange rate applicable at the time the transaction is recorded. 2.4.2 Recording basis In principle, data are compiled on an accrual basis 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures To the extent possible, all reporting are on a gross basis 3.1.3 Source data timeliness External Sector data are compiled and disseminated on a quarterly basis consistent with the SDDS requirement 3.2.1 Source data assessment Survey results are assessed on an annual basis with the objective to improve response rate and/or find alternative means of collecting the required statistics when appropriate 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results Where applicable, consistency of source data is validated against data from other sources. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request Non-confidential unpublished data are made available on request
