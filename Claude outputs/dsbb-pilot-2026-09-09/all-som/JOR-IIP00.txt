 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The conceptual framework and classification structure for Jordan's international investment position (IIP) statistics follows the IMF's Balance of Payments and International Investment Position Manual, Sixth Edition (BPM6), The IIP is fully integrated with Jordan's balance of payments statistics. Jordan's IIP is a statistical statement summarizing the level of Jordan's foreign financial assets and liabilities at a point in time (end of quarter).Summary analytic presentations show Jordan's net international investment position as the difference between the levels of gross external financial assets and gross external financial liabilities. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Jordan's international investment position statistics are classified according to BPM6 Assets and liabilities are classified into direct investment, portfolio investment (showing a breakdown of equity and debt), other investment and reserve assets (assets only). Direct Investment is classified according to the asset and liabilities principle, while portfolio and other investments are disaggregated by instrument, institutional sector, and maturity. There are no known transactions (and positions) in financial derivatives in Jordan. 2.2.1 Scope Jordan's international investment position statistics are classified according to BPM6 Assets and liabilities are classified into direct investment, portfolio investment (showing a breakdown of equity and debt), other investment and reserve assets (assets only). Direct Investment is classified according to the asset and liabilities principle, while portfolio and other investments are disaggregated by instrument, institutional sector, and maturity. There are no known transactions (and positions) in financial derivatives in Jordan. Scope of the data Jordan's IIP attempts to cover all of Jordan's external financial assets and liabilities, together with all related financial transactions. All institutional sectors are covered. However, the international investment activity of households may not be fully captured by existing sources and compilation methods. 2.4.1 Valuation Stock positions originally denominated in foreign currencies are converted to Jordanian dinars using prevailing market rates of exchange. Transactions are sought on the basis of conversion using the mid-point of the buying and selling exchange rates on the day of the transaction. Transactions and positions data are sought at market prices. In practice, the values recorded in business accounts may be used. These are considered a close approximation to market prices in Jordan's case. In cases where significant distortion may occur to the valuation of direct equity, adjustments are made on the basis of total market capitalization. 2.4.2 Recording basis Recording is done on accrual basis wherever possible. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The main source of Jordan's IIP data is the CBJ Balance sheet, Consolidated Balance sheet of Jordanian licensed banks and administrative records of the government, FDI survey, CCD, department of land and survey, ASE, SDC, direct reporting. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Source data are collected consistent with the definitions, scope, classifications, valuation and time of recording required. · Direct Investment : For Years 2004, 2005, 2008 ,2009, 2019, 2020, 2021 and 2022, both assets and liabilities are from a foreign investment surveys conducted by the Department of Statistics. other years the liability side the main sources of data are administrative information from Ministry of Industry and Trade (MIT), Amman Stock Exchange (ASE) enterprises reports, telephone reminders and personal canvassing and internal FDI project depending on the Balance sheets of enterprises of ASE, CCD, and investments in lands and real states according to the Balance of Payments and Investment Position Manual (BPM6). · Portfolio investment : For Years 2004, 2005, 2008 ,2009, 2019, 2020, 2021 and 2022, both assets and liabilities are from a foreign investment surveys conducted by the Department of Statistics. other years the liability side the main sources of data are administrative information from Ministry of Industry and Trade (MIT), Amman Stock Exchange (ASE) enterprises reports, CCD. · Other investment : The sources are the CBJ, banks' balance sheets and monthly reports, MOF quarterly reports, government agencies, enterprises reports, and telephone reminders. Data on transactions of Central Government, mainly the transactions on debt instruments, are obtained from MOF and CBJ. · Reserve assets: The CBJ balance sheet. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness The CBJ and licensed Banks balance sheets also provides quality data in sufficient time. 3.2.1 Source data assessment The BOP Division continuously verifies the reported statistics. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Adjustments in line with the IIP guidelines are applied in the compilation of the IIP data when necessary as following: · Financial Account: Estimates on some investment data are based on information obtained from the companies operating in those domains or from institutions and ministries responsible for organizing investment activities, and from the foreign investments survey. In addition to the internal project of the CBJ. · Reserve assets: stocks data derived from the CBJ and licensed banks balance sheet. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures The BOP division within the CBJ has an internal development database for estimating the FDI and portfolio investment liabilities which is also used to validate the Foreign Investment survey conducting by DOS 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results All data submitted by businesses in the foreign Investment survey are subject to rigorous editing verification and validation procedures, established by DOS and CBJ. Where possible, data are also validated against other data sources. Aggregates are assessed for their plausibility and may result in further investigation of individual business data. These assessments include assessment for consistency with BPM6 framework and reconciliation with accounting identities. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data The behavior of a series is only periodically crosschecked with related series or indicators. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs The BOP Division validate its IIP data against other statistical output, including the balance of payments, central bank balance sheet, the consolidated balance sheet of licenced banks, and external debt data compiled by the Ministry of Finance (MOF) and Central Bank of Jordan (CBJ). 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses Not applicable because IIP first disseminated in 2007. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses There are currently no revision studies as the IIP is a new statistical product. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request More detailed annual statistics since 2004 are available on request. Non published but non confidential sub aggregated data made are available upon request. 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points A contact telephone, fax, e-mail and address are identified in the CBJ's MSB and website, as well as on the IMF's Dissemination Standard Bulletin Board, where contact names are provided specially for the IIP. Assistance to users of statistics is available upon request. User surveys are also available in CBJ website continuously. 5.3.2 Availability of documents and services catalogs No catalogue of publications is available, but users have access to information provided on the CBJ website. Also, the Monthly Statistical Bulletin , and Annual report of the CBJ are free of charge.
