 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions General Government Gross Debt is compiled in line with ESA 2010 methodology, Government Finance Statistics Manual 2014, Manual on General Government Deficit and Debt 2022 edition and Guide for Compilers and Users 2011. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The general government sector and its subsectors are defined in accordance with the ESA 2010 guidelines and the ESA 2010 Manual on Government Deficit and Debt, 2022 edition, which are also consistent with the 2008 SNA and GFSM 2014. The general government in Slovak Republic consists of central government, local government, and social security funds. The Ministry of Finance of the Slovak Republic compiles government finance statistics for each subsector as well as for the general government sector as a whole. 2.2.1 Scope The general government sector and its subsectors are defined in accordance with the ESA 2010 guidelines and the ESA 2010 Manual on Government Deficit and Debt, 2022 edition, which are also consistent with the 2008 SNA and GFSM 2014. The general government in Slovak Republic consists of central government, local government, and social security funds. The Ministry of Finance of the Slovak Republic compiles government finance statistics for each subsector as well as for the general government sector as a whole. Scope of the data The institutional coverage corresponds to general government sector according to the methodology ESA 2010. In the Slovak Republic the Statistical Office of the Slovak Republic is responsible for the delimitation of general government sector and its updating. SO SR is responsible for the Register of subjects of the general government. Register is available on the Statistical website only in Slovak version. https://slovak.statistics.sk/wps/portal/ext/Databases/administration/!ut/p/z1/ Exceptions to coverage No exceptions. Unrecorded activity The data on debt securities at market value are not available. 2.4.1 Valuation General Government Debt is valued in nominal prices. The valuing of foreign-currency debt is based on swap rates or ECB exchange rates at the end of the period. The valuations are made quarterly. Data are presented in millions of Euros. 2.4.2 Recording basis Data are expressed in millions of Euros. The accounting year (fiscal year) is the calendar year. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures Gross versus net: The General Government Debt is recorded on a gross basis. Slovak Republic applies grossing/netting procedures consistently with ESA2010 guidelines. For the debt no financial assets are offset against liabilities. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Data sources: Data on Central Government Debt are compiled by the Public Sector Financial Statistics Department of Ministry of Finance of the Slovak Republic in co-operation with the Agency for the State Debt Management and the State Treasury. The sources of data are loan agreements, or any other agreements related to issuing state bonds. The Agency for the State Debt Management (ARDAL) is a principal agent of the state regarding to payments made by the government. Data source for the data on General Government Gross Debt compilation are accounting statements reported in line with the Act No. 431/2002 Coll. on Accounting as amended and financial statements reported by subjects of general government in line with the Directive No. MF/017353/2017-352 as amended, on laying down the layout, content, method, date and place of submission of information from the accounts and data necessary for the purposes of assessing the implementation of the budget of the public administration. The directive is available on MoF´s website solely in Slovak version https://www.mfsr.sk/sk/financie/statne-vykaznictvo/legislativa/ . 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Data for General Government Gross debt compilation are available in time t+50 (or t+70) days after the end of previous quarter. 3.2.1 Source data assessment Source data are based especially on financial and accounting statements that cover most of government units, i.e. response is 90 - 100 %. Assessment is carried out during the processing of the statements; results are monitored, and extreme mistakes are discussed with the concrete unit. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Since the General Government Gross Debt compilation is based on financial and accounting statements and response is about 90 -100 %, and the statements fulfill the requirements to compile the statistical data, no estimation is needed. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures If needed consolidation is established within the sector general government. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results Validation of intermediate result is carried out automatically during the processing of data sources. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data Items of exceptional value are checked, individual questioning (with the unit) is used, if necessary. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs Validation of intermediate result is carried out automatically during the processing of data sources. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses In each March and September, annual and quarterly data are aligned, and revisions are analyzed for future processing. Most common are revisions due to new source data or changes in counterpart information. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses None. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request General Government Gross D ebt The corresponding statistical department is responsible for fulfillment of such requirement depending on availability of data sources. 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points Contact points for each subject field are available on the website https://www.mfsr.sk/en/finance/national-reporting/sdds-plus .
