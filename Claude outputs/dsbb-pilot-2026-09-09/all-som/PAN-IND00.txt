 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The integrated volume, price, and value indices for manufacturing industry are statistical measurements designed to show periodic changes in the volume, value and price of industrial activity in the short term. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The composite index is composed of indices calculated by class of activity (4 digits), grouping (3 digits), and industrial division (2 digits) of the "National Standard Industrial Classification of all Economic Activities" (CINU), Revision 3. CINU, Revision 3 is the Panamanian version of the UN International Standard Industrial Classification of all Economic Activities (ISIC), Revision 3. Scope of the data The basic data are collected from a sample of 458 establishments, in which 187 products are studied. None 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The sample used was designed using the results of the five National Economic Censuses conducted in 2002 and covering 2001. Production for each product is measured in appropriate physical units; weights were determined as the percentage share in 1992 in the total value of output within the class of activity. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures The weights for each group and industrial division are determined as the percentage of gross value added within the total for the manufacturing industry, according to the 2001 national accounts. The indices are not adjusted to reflect seasonal variations. None
