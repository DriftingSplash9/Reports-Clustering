 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The Industrial Production Index (IIP) is the main indicator of industrial production in China. It reflects the volume change of industrial production activities. The IIP is a volume measure. The Producer Price Index (PPI) is used as the price index to deflate value-added at current prices into value added in real terms. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Data are collected from enterprises, which are classified according to the 4-digit level of China’s "National Economic Industrial Classification (GB/T 4754-2011) ". The national classification is consistent with the class level of the ISIC Rev.4. In this classification, the IPI covers 581 industries at the 4-digit level, 201 industries at the 3-digit level, and 41 industries at the 2-digit level. 2.2.1 Scope Data are collected from enterprises, which are classified according to the 4-digit level of China’s "National Economic Industrial Classification (GB/T 4754-2011) ". The national classification is consistent with the class level of the ISIC Rev.4. In this classification, the IPI covers 581 industries at the 4-digit level, 201 industries at the 3-digit level, and 41 industries at the 2-digit level. Scope of the data The IIP of China covers mining and quarrying, manufacturing and electricity, gas steam and water supply, which are consistent with sections B, C, D and E of the International Standard Industrial Classification of All Economic Activities (ISIC Rev.4). Exceptions to coverage None. 2.4.1 Valuation The output of industrial enterprises is valued at producer prices. Value-added tax is excluded from the output valuation. 2.4.2 Recording basis The IPI is recorded on an accrual basis. Enterprises report both monthly and cumulative monthly output data. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Source data for the IPI are collected through comprehensive monthly statistical survey on enterprises above a designated size. Currently, enterprises with annual revenue above 20 million RMB are surveyed. For industrial enterprises above designated size, the coverage is full and the monthly data are reported online through an official data collecting system. Each enterprise reports the output of products at the 4-digit level of the industrial classification. The data are collected by the Department of Industry Statistics of the NBS for compiling the IIP. The enterprises above the designated size are selected on the basis of a business register. The business register is maintained by the Department of Census Center of the NBS. To ensure the reliability of the business register, information on active enterprises operating in the country is collected annually. New built enterprises are incorporated in the business register when they attain the designated size and operate stably for three consecutive months. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording The calculation of the IPI is based on the source data from enterprises above designated size. The survey form is called “Report of Industrial Production, Selling and Volumes of Main Products”. Enterprises above the 20 million RMB cover more than 90 percent of the gross output of the overall industry. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Enterprises above designated size report their monthly data through the online data collecting system within 6 days the end of the reference month. 3.2.1 Source data assessment To guarantee the integrity and quality of the source data, the online data collecting system automatically examines and verifies data entry. The system automatically checks the value ranges, logical relationships and unusual fluctuations of the data and gives hints about the possible mistakes. For example, as the output data should be a nonnegative number, the system warns if a negative number is filled in. Users can fix the error promptly and submit the correct values. Quality checks on the data are undertaken, enterprises are contacted when deemed appropriate. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Output data are collected at the 4-digit level from source data, as each enterprise reports its output at that detail level. As value-added data at 3-digit sectors are more stable, the index is calculated at 3-digit level. Value-added for each economic activity at the 3-digit level is calculated by multiplying the sum of output of each 3-digit sector by the ratio of value-added to output of the same 3-digit activity： Value-added at 3-digit sector in current price=Output at 3-digit sector in current price×Ratio of Value-added to Output at 3-digit sector where Ratio of Value-added to Output is obtained from accounting data that sources from annual financial survey and adopts the method of the income approach. ( The calculation formula is: Value-added = Depreciation of fixed capital +Compensation of employees + Net taxes on production + Operating surplus.) Then, value-added of each 3-digit industry is deflated by the Producer Price Index (also specified at the 3-digit level), that is, Value-added at 3-digit level in real terms= Value-added at 3-digit level in current price / Producer Price Index at 3-digit level. As the coverage of enterprise above designated size is full above designated size, the total value-added is aggregated by summing the value-added of all 3-digit industries in real terms. The IPI is derived by the ratio between the monthly value-added in real terms and the annual average of the value-added in real-terms in 2010, that is Industry Production Index (IPI)=Sum of monthly value-added of all 3-digit industries in real terms of the current period / Annual average of the sum of monthly value-added of all 3-digit industries in real terms of the base period 2010×100. Currently, the IPI uses the year 2010 as weight period and reference period. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Year-on-year growth rates of the IPI are calculated by comparing the value-added in real terms of each month with the same month of the previous year. Month-to-month growth rates of the IPI are calculated by comparing the seasonally adjusted data of value-added in real terms between two consecutive months. The seasonal adjustment is done on the level of the total index using the seasonal adjustment software NBS-SA, which has been developed by the NBS and Nankai University. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results During the data collection process, quality checks are implemented to detect large changes or unusual fluctuations in the source data. NBS statisticians will request the enterprises to check the data and explain the reasons. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data The IPI is validated against the volume changes of industrial products. Cross-checks are done to check the consistency between volume changes of an industrial product and the index of the relevant industry, for example, the volume changes of the cars and the index of the automobile manufacturing industry. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses There is no revision for unadjusted data. For the seasonally adjusted data, differences are checked between the released data and the subsequent data when new information is incorporated. 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points Statistics Information Services provide contact points on the official website http:// www.stats.gov.cn and by telephone 86-10-68520066/6320 (Fax).
