 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Definition: Exports are goods exiting the borders of Japan that are traded with the rest of the world and imports are goods entering Japanese borders from the rest of the world during the month. The data are compiled according to the "General System". "Foreign trade statistics" are almost compiled according to IMTS ver.2 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Goods are classified according to the Harmonized Commodity Description and Coding System (HS). Exceptions to coverage The export and import statistics do not include the following goods: goods of small amount of value of less than 200,000 yen; samples; goods smuggled; gifts; ship's and aircraft's stores; personal effects of travellers; goods of performance of travelling entertainers; goods belonging to US and UN Forces; exhibits to the fair; and containers of repeated used. 2.4.1 Valuation The value of exports is based on the F.O.B. value, where as the value of imports is based on the C.I.F. value. 2.4.2 Recording basis Conversion to unit of account: Weekly customs posted rate based on the average inter-bank exchange rate of two weeks earlier than the declaration day. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Merchandise Trade data is are based on customs declarations. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Sources: Merchandise Trade is compiled on the basis of information contained in customs documents such as export declarations, import declarations, re-shipment declarations, etc., which were submitted to the customs authority for clearance. Basis of country data: (Export) Final destination, (Import) Country of origin. Type of data collected: i) Unit of account: 1,000 yen; and ii) Quantities: The unit of quantity is indicated on 'Export Statistical Schedule' and 'Customs Tariff Schedule of Japan' (a local classification). 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Timing of compilation: (Exports) Date of departure of carriers loading exported cargoes; (Import) Date of Customs permission. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Processing system: Nippon Automated Cargo and Port Consolidated System (NACCS). Processing site: Ministry of Finance (Customs and Tariff Bureau) and Customs offices. Geographic detail: Data are available by country of destination (exports) and country of origin (imports). Commodity detail: Data are available at the 9-digit HS level. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Seasonal adjustment: The X-12ARIMA method is used to adjust the monthly import and export totals. None
