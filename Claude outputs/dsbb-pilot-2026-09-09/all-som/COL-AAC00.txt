 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The concepts and definitions used by the BanRep for compiling monetary statistics follow the MFSM in terms of identifying institutional units and valuating financial assets. The departures in terms of concepts and definitions, coverage, classification/sectorization, concept of residency, accrual accounting, and presentation of gross assets and liabilities are explained below. In 2023 the IMF's Statistics Department (STA) conducted a TA mission for Colombia to improve the compilation of its monetary and financial statistics (MFS). The mission worked with officials of the BanRep to review the compilation framework of monetary statistics for the Central Bank (CB), other depository corporations (ODCs), and other financial corporations (OFCs) in accordance with the methodology set out in the Monetary and Financial Statistics Manual and Compilation Guide 2016 (MFSMCG 2016) BanRep is implementing TA recommendations during 2024 and revising historical data (back to 2018). For that reason, there will be some changes compared to previously released data and some discrepancies might exist temporarily when comparing different publications (i.e. SR submissions to IMF vs data on BanRep's website or monthly vs weekly data). 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The principles underlying the classification of financial instruments in the monetary statistics are broadly consistent with the MFSM recommendations. The financial instruments in the balance sheets are classified according to degree of liquidity and the legal characteristics describing the underlying relationship between creditors and debtors. Assets and liabilities are classified according to the institutional sector of the counterparts. According to the recommendations of IMF technical assistance in 2023, some minor reclassifications of sectors and financial instruments were implemented. As of July 2024 (information with data as of June 2024), the variables and definitions of the sectorization report of the Banco de la República were adjusted, allowing the application of the guidelines of the IMF Monetary and Financial Statistics Manual of 2016. The series are reprocessed since January 2018. Temporarily, the data presented in this report may present discrepancies with those of other BanRep's publications while the methodological changes are implemented. Gross assets vis-à-vis ODCs: mostly repurchase agreements. Net assets vis-à-vis ODCs: gross assets vis-à-vis ODCs minus deposits holding by ODCs at the central bank (for reserve requirements and other purposes). Net assets vis-à-vis OFCs: on the asset side it corresponds to repurchase agreements with OFCs, and on the liability side to deposits of OFCs at the central bank (including those of OFCs that provide intermediation services to ODCs). Net assets vis-à-vis the central government: on the asset side it includes securities issued by Central Government and other accounts receivable (trade credit and advances), and on the liability side it includes deposits of the Central Government at the central bank and other accounts payable (trade credit and advances). Net assets vis-à-vis private sector: on the asset side includes mostly credit to employees, and on the liability side other accounts payable (trade credit). Assets vis-à-vis non-residents sector: it corresponds to gross assets against non-residents (mostly International Reserves). Exceptions to coverage The central bank has not provided new credit to the nonfinancial public sector since 1991. By law, the Central Bank cannot lend to the nonfinancial public sector enterprises. Unrecorded activity Further to central bank activity, other activities like cultural activity are allowed to the BanRep. All the activities of the BanRep are recorded on its countable balance sheet. 2.4.1 Valuation The BanRep values monetary gold holdings monthly using the price of gold in the international market at the BanRep balance sheet closing date. The BanRep and the rest of the financial corporations value foreign-currency-denominated instruments at the midpoint exchange rate prevailing on the balance sheet closing date. 2.4.2 Recording basis International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS) constitute the base accounting model or reference applicable to the Bank in those aspects that are not contrary to its special regime, as described in Law 31/1992 and in its bylaws. Consequently, as per Resolution 037/2017 and its amendments, the Bank must apply the regulatory technical framework outlined in the attachment to National Decree 2420/2015 and its amendments, except in the following instances: If it is contrary to the Bank's special regime, as per Law 31/1992, and its bylaws issued through Decree 2520/1993 and amended by Decree 2386 of December 11, 2015, particularly with respect to: i) accounting treatment of the exchange adjustment for foreign reserves caused by fluctuations in the peso exchange rate for the currencies in which those reserves are represented (Article 62, Numeral 4 of the bylaws, as amended by Article 2 of Decree 2386/2015) and ii) the accounting treatment of issued metallic currency (Article 62, Literal b, Numeral 1 of the bylaws). Treatment of the loan portfolio and its detriment, for which the Bank applies the special technical standards, interpretations and guidelines on accounting and financial information issued by the Office of the Financial Superintendent of Colombia. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The source for compiling the Central Bank Survey (CBS) is the general balance sheet of the BanRep prepared by the accounting department. The sectoral balance sheet of the BR and the CBS are produced on a monthly basis by FSS. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording The BanRep makes ongoing efforts to use source data that reasonably approximate the definitions, scope, classification, valuation, and time of recording to compile sound monetary statistics. The source data for the CBS provide sufficient information to derive the accounts according to the recommendations of the MFSM . Even though the chart of accounts does not contain the residency criterion or sectorization, the access to the accounting records of the BanRep allows the production of the monetary balance sheet with all the necessary information. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness The FSS compiles the sectoral balance sheet of the BanRep on a monthly basis based on daily data. 3.2.1 Source data assessment The Accounting Department and the Financial Sector Section of the BanRep analyze the source data for the CBS for accuracy. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques As BanRep's statistics are based on balance sheet data. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures The BanRep does not use any other statistical procedure/technique in its monetary statistics. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results The BanRep balance sheet is consolidated through technological way. Besides, the FSS validates hierarchical and intertemporal consistency. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data Regularly the FSS check selected balance information whit others department information like Open Market Department or Trust Department. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs It is not common discrepancies into BanRep balance sheet. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses Data for the previous month are always revised when the current month is processed. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses The FSS analyzes revisions, and it documents them in internal reports. 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points The FSS offers, on request by users inside and outside the BanRep, timely and knowledgeable assistance on the information published. Contact points for monetary statistics by e-mail are specifically identified on the BanRep's website and the DSBB. Assistance to users is monitored regularly to ensure that prompt responses are sent to queries. 5.3.2 Availability of documents and services catalogs The BanRep has a catalog of publications listing all bulletins, reports, essays, and drafts. All BanRep publications available electronically are free of charge.
