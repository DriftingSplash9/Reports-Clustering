 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Concept: The data template on international reserves and foreign currency liquidity is compiled in accordance with the IMF's International Reserves and Foreign Currency Liquidity: Guidelines for a Data Template (2016), the concept of foreign currency liquidity being broader than that of official reserve assets. It refers to the amount of foreign currency resources that is readily available, taking into account both predetermined and potential net drains of such resources. Definition: In accordance with the Guidelines, o fficial reserve assets which coincide with gross official reserves, are external assets readily available to and controlled by the NBM for direct financing of balance of payments imbalances, for indirectly regulating the magnitude of such imbalances through intervention in the foreign exchange market to support the currency exchange rate, or for other purposes. Other foreign currency assets are defined as foreign currency assets of the NBM that are not included in official reserve assets as well as such assets held by the central government. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Classification: The official reserve assets and other foreign currency assets are classified as follows: · A. Official reserve assets (1) Foreign currency reserves (in convertible currencies) (a) Securities (b) Total deposits with: (i) other central banks, BIS and IMF (ii) banks headquartered outside the Republic of Moldova (2) IMF reserve position (3) SDRs (4) Gold (5) Other reserve assets · B. Other foreign currency assets The international reserves and foreign currency liquidity are classified as follows: I. Official reserve assets and other foreign currency assets broken down by major components vis-à-vis convertible foreign currency; II. Predetermined short-term drains on foreign currency assets; III. Contingent short-term drains on foreign currency assets; IV. Related (memo) items. Relationship to international guideline: The concepts, definitions and classification underlying the data template on international reserves and foreign currency liquidity disseminated by the NBM are based on the IMF's International Reserves and Foreign Currency Liquidity: Guidelines for a Data Template, 2016, and are in line with the requirements of the sixth edition of the Balance of Payments and International Investment Position Manual (BPM6). Scope of the data Institutional coverage: Reserve assets data comprise official reserve assets held by the NBM. Template data also cover the activity of the government, excluding social security funds. Instrument coverage: Foreign assets in nonconvertible currencies, as well as all other assets that do not meet reserve assets concepts, are excluded. Financial derivatives: Financial derivatives (foreign exchange swaps) are included in the other reserve assets and financial derivatives on a marked-to-market basis are included in the memo items. Exceptions to coverage Geographic Coverage : IRFCLT of Moldova does not include data on the left side of the Nistru River and municipality Bender. Unrecorded activity None 2.4.1 Valuation Valuation: Foreign securities are revalued monthly at the end of the reporting period at the market price for each type of instruments. Special Drawing Rights are revalued daily according to the quotation informed by the IMF. As of January 2, 2025, the NBM uses the Euro as the reference currency for setting the official exchange rate of the Moldovan leu, replacing the US dollar. The decision reflects the Republic of Moldova's economic shift towards the European Union market, which plays a dominant role in the country's trade and financial structure. Conversion rates : Data are converted into US dollars equivalents based on the official exchange rates announced by the NBM daily. Assets denominated in other currencies are converted into US dollars according to cross-rates based on MDL exchange rates published daily by the NBM. 2.4.2 Recording basis Frequency of calculation and revaluation: International reserve assets are calculated daily. The reserves are revalued daily except for positions with the IMF, which are revalued monthly and/or when transactions are made. Accrued interest on deposits is recorded daily and accrued interest on securities is recorded monthly. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Data sources: NBM, MOF of the Republic of Moldova. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Source data generally comply with methodological standards and guidelines for compiling monthly IRFCLT. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness The source data are timely for compiling and disseminating monthly IRFCLT. Formal arrangements are in place to ensure the timely basis of administrative data. 3.2.1 Source data assessment All data go through validations with related sources. Necessary follow-up is done to either correct misreported data or to obtain missing data. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques The NBM supervises the compatibility of data and ensures the internal integrity of the IRFCL statistics. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results Intermediate results are checked against alternative data sources. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data The behaviour of time series is cross-checked with related series or indicators if available. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs Data on IRFCL are reconciled with quarterly data from international investment position statistics. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses Revision studies: Analysis and studies of revisions are carried out. The information regarding revisions, by items, periods affected, together with the reasons is published.
