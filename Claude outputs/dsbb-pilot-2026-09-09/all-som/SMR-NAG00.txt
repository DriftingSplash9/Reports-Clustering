 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Analytical framework: The National Accounts is based on the European System of National Accounts ESA 95 , which derive from the System of National Accounts ( SNA1993 ). Definition: Concepts and definitions used to compile the statistical series are in broad conformity with guidelines outlined in the ESA95. GDP measures the market value of the good and services produced in San Marino after the deduction of intermediate consumption and before the deduction of provisions for consumption of fixed capital. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The classification and sectorization used in the compilation of national accounts are in broad conformity with internationally recommended system: ESA95 is followed to classify: Statistics units Flow and stocks National account system interdependences among economic sectors ATECO 2002 is used to classify the principal economic activities, which is in line with the NACE Rev. 1.1 from the 4 digit split to upper level. The CPA is adopted to classify products. Final household consumption expenditures are classified in reference to COICOP; non- profit institutions expenditures are classified in reference to COPNI; Government expenditures are classified in reference to COFOG. Capital formation follows the Pi6 level of ESA95 (6 items). Foreign trade data are classified according to the Harmonized System. Scope of the data The scope of the national accounts statistics covers ESA95 accounts. Transactions coverage: gross domestic product and gross national income; taxes and subsidies on production; gross value added at current prices and at previous year's prices (chain-linked); private consumption; purchases of private households by purpose; expenditure of general government, gross fixed capital formation at current prices and at previous year's prices (chain-linked); imports and exports of goods and services at current prices and at previous year’s prices (chain-linked), economic transactions with the rest of the world; distribution of wage and property income; compensation of employees; gross wages and salaries at current prices. Definition of residence is in accordance with SNA93 and BPM5. The economic territory definition follows the Decision of the European Commission of July 1991. It includes: 1) territorial enclaves, 2) bonded warehouses and free zone activities, 3) workers who work only part of the year in another country. The production boundarie s is in line with SNA93. Among other things it includes: research and development on own-account, output of goods for own-account fixed capital formation, mineral exploration, production of entertainment, literary or artistic originals, production of computer software. Own-account production: The national accounts follow the convention of ESA95 paragraph 3.08 b, which says that the estimates for own-account production of households should be limited to the production, storage, and processing of agricultural products and to the construction of dwellings. The asset boundary is defined in accordance with the 1993 SNA, implying that the following items are in scope for determining the assets boundary: Among tangible assets : 1) defense related assets that could be used for civilian purposes; 2) valuables; 3) vistorical monuments when they would be sold (balance sheets are not included). Among intangible assets : 1) mineral exploration (whether successful or not); 2) systems and standard applications computer software and data bases (purchased or built in-house); 3) entertainment, literary or artistic originals; 4) patented entities; 5) leases and other transferable contracts. 2.4.1 Valuation The valuation rules used for the recording of flows and stocks are in accordance with the ESA95. GDP is recorded at market prices; GDP components are recorded at market prices, while value added is recorded both at producer and basic prices. GDP components are published at current prices, previous year’s prices and chained-linked volumes. Income aggregates are estimated only at current prices. Both total imports and exports are valued on an f.o.b. basis using the information on insurance and freight for merchandise imports. 2.4.2 Recording basis The timing of recording rules used for recording flows are in accordance with the ESA95. GDP components are in general recorded at the time its ownership changes. For final consumption expenditures by general government they are recorded at the time they are produced (accrual basis), with some exceptions where the cash figures are known to closely correspond to the accruals (government expenditure). Recording basis: Transactions and flows are recorded on an accrual basis. Recording period: Calendar years and quarters. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures Grossing/netting producers are in accordance with the ESA95 . 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The data source is kept under continuous review to ensure that the data collection program is comprehensive. The data sources of the data collection program are sufficient to compile the national account statistics. The following agencies produce data for the national accounts: General Directorate of Public Finances and General Accounting Office (the data produced by these offices is obtained from the State Final Statement). Tax Office Social Security Institute Other Autonomous Agencies (the data produced by these agencies are obtained from their final statements) Central Bank Chamber of Commerce Office of the Registrar of Buildings and Land Enterprise Statistics Many improvements have been made with regards to the data on the statements of the companies. At the present these data originate from files of the Chamber of Commerce, while previously they were manually inputted into the files by the Office of Economic Planning, Data Processing, and Statistics. This new procedure has notably improved the quality of the data and it has reduced the times for their publication. The enterprise statistics are elaborated by a Database program. This program contains all the statements of companies and the statistics are elaborated by specific query. These data are related to all the enterprises of the territory and they are not samples. The data includes, in particular: Profits Fixed Assets Consumption of Fixed Capital Final Inventory Time of recording: annually (September). The total number of enterprises is published quarterly in the Bulletin of Statistic. Household data The Household Survey is conducted annually on a regular basis. The survey questionnaire is constructed according to sound design principles. It is periodically reviewed to take into account the change in circumstances. This is a sample survey. The sampling size is equal to 10% of the total population. The household survey is conducted during the first month of the year. The data includes, in particular: the expenses for consumptions divided by category of consumption. Household’s income Time of recording: annually (May). Comprehensive government finance statistic The data coming from the State Final Statement (and the data coming from the Autonomous Agencies Final statements) are considered reliable in as much as they are official data. Time of recording: annually (November). Tax data The data coming from the Tax Office is constantly verified both by the Tax Office and by the Office of Economic Planning, Data Processing, and Statistics. Time of recording of import / export: quarterly Office of the Registry of Buildings and Land The data includes, in particular: Number of civil buildings Number of commercial buildings Time of recording: annually (January). Income data Social Security National Institute (ISS) Time of recording: annually (March) 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Definition, scope, classification and valuation of the data source are described in 3.1.1. The agencies that provide the source data for the national accounts, continuously record the data and they produce the aggregates in the suitable time periods indicated in 3.1.1. 3.2.1 Source data assessment Source data are quarterly assessed. In particular: Import/export: the data are quarterly adjourned by the tax office. Number of enterprises and number of employed workers: the data are quarterly published in the Bulletin of Statistics. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques The statistical techniques uses for national accounts deal with data source. The program allows to directly input the data from the sources. Data sources are definite according to the techniques of elaboration of the ESA95. Annually compilation techniques The majority of the data from sources are certain and final data. In particular: Income data Income Tax (source: ISS) Import – Export (source: tax office) Social Contributions (source: ISS and Tax Office) Production and import tax (source: tax office and State Final Statement) Production and import contributions (source: State Final Statement) Gross result of Companies (legal persons) (source: tax office and State and other State enterprise final statement) National incomes from capital (source: Central Bank) Other current transfers (source: State Final Statement and ISS) Other little part of the data source are estimates. In particular: Final consumptions Enterprise final statement Final Consumption Final consumption in San Marino is estimated through the Survey on Consumption and Survey on the San Marino Families Life Style. The household survey is conducted annually on a regular basis. The survey questionnaire is constructed according to sound design principles. It is periodically reviewed to take into account the change in circumstances. This is a self forming sample enquiry. The response rate is equal to 10% of the total population. The household survey is conducted in the first month of the year. The data included is, in particular: the expenses for consumptions divided by category of consumption. Household’s income Time of recording: annually (May). Enterprise Final Statement Enterprise final statement data are for the greater part real (98%) and in small part estimated (2%). Real data is obtained from the Chamber of Commerce. The estimated part covers the errors of data input. In this case, we provide for an adjustment of the data when we notice that data is not in accord with the series and with the other correlated series. Our interest mainly concerns these data: Profits The net immobilizations and the amortization funds. The final inventory. Time of recording: annually (September) GDP is calculated by income approach techniques in according with ESA95 System. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Quarterly Compilation techniques An appropriate benchmarking technique is followed to combine annual estimates with the quarterly indicators. Office of Economic Planning, Data Processing, and Statistics has been built a system of correlations, that has allowed to estimate the dependence of the historical series that compose the PIL with the data quarterly published. With the calculation of the correlations of the number of employed persons, it is possible to calculate some estimates of the lacking data using as a starting point the available data of the quarterly period. The data are not seasonally adjusted. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results The data, obtained from the main sources and which is used to compile the national accounts statistics, are checked against other independent data sources. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data The discrepancies in intermediate data are routinely investigated and appropriate adjustments are made to remove them. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses Annually, studies and analyses of revisions are carried out. Studies assess the initial estimates against revised or final estimates. This is done for verifying the forecast model, which is still in an experimental phase due to the fact that the historical series are still too short. The quarterly estimates are continually revised according to the data source adjustments. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses Revision are measured, assessed and explained in the statistical publication and in the database accessible by users.
