 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The data for the central government are cash based. Although the government finance statistics presentation in the monthly news release and monthly statement follows the budgetary framework, the concepts and definitions are similar to the ESA 2010. Hence financing transactions such as sale of shares are not recorded as revenue and acquisition of shares are not recorded as expenditure. The CBM compiles the monthly Statement of Sources and Uses of Cash in line with the GFSM 2014 methodology. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The monthly central government finance data as presented by the NSO and Treasury captures the activities of the administrative ministries/departments conducted through the Consolidated Fund and exclude the extra-budgetary units. The Consolidated Fund is the main government’s account. Scope of the data The objective is to compile the data according to internationally accepted standards and guidelines. Subsequently, the data are used for policy-making purposes and analysis. The monthly data are broadly in line with the European System of Account 2010 (ESA 2010) and Government Finance Statistics Manual 2014 (GFSM 2014). 2.4.1 Valuation The flows are valued at market prices. 2.4.2 Recording basis The reporting of the monthly central government finance data is on a cash basis. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures The reporting of the monthly central government finance data is on a gross basis. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Data for the budgetary central government are directly obtained from the government accounting system the Corporate Financial Management System (CFMS) managed by the Office of the Accountant General at the Treasury. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording The source data are consistent with the definitions, scope, classification and valuation of the international standard guidelines. The monthly data are broadly in line with the European System of Account 2010 (ESA 2010) and Government Finance Statistics Manual 2014 (GFSM 2014). 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Source data on the operations of the central government are received by the last week of the month succeeding the reference period, except data for the last month of the fiscal year, which are available not later than three months after the end of the fiscal year. In this case, Malta will make use of the SDDS's flexibility option and will publish the monthly data for December in March of the successive year. 3.2.1 Source data assessment The final source data are based on audited annual accounts, which reflect the monthly data. In terms of Article 65 of the Financial Administration and Audit Act, the Accountant General has to publish the Government of Malta, Financial Report. In this report, the audited annual accounts of the central government (excluding extra-budgetary units) are presented. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Data for central government are collected from the CFMS and do not require the use of statistical techniques. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request Detailed analytical presentations not routinely disseminated in the News Release are made available to users on request. 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points The hardcopy publications and the NSO's website: www.nso.gov.mt , provide comprehensive information on how users can contact the NSO. The CBM has a general e-mail address: info@centralbankmalta.org to which the general public can request additional information. 5.3.2 Availability of documents and services catalogs NSO’s monthly news release on central government operations can be downloaded for free from NSO’s website: www.nso.gov.mt .
