 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Definition: Average measure of change in prices of goods and services bought for the purpose of consumption by the vast majority of households in the Slovak Republic. The concepts and definitions of the consumer price index in the Slovak Republic are based on Council and Commission Regulations concerning of HICP methodology. In general, methodological basis for the CPI follows the European System of Accounts 2010 (ESA 2010), Council and Commission Regulations (which are obligatory for us) and also Manual of CPI: Concept and Methods 2020 (CPI Manual 2020). 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Classification: The CPI is divided into the divisions, groups, classes and subclasses of the COICOP 2018 classification. Scope of the data Population coverage: From the population aspect the index covers about 90 percent households. It includes the following 5 main groups of population: employees (wage and salary workers): manual workers outside agriculture; non-manual workers. self-employed; farmers (mainly in co-operatives); pensioners; institutional households Separate indices are compiled for: all households (total), employees, pensioners, low-income households. Geographical coverage: The entire territory of the Slovak Republic, i. e. urban and rural areas, but most of the prices are collected in urban areas. Item coverage: All goods and services bought by the reference population for the purposes of consumption (see also exceptions to coverage). The CPI calculation is based on a consumer basket with representative items. The list of major divisions and their 2026 weights are presented below. The discounts are not separately surveyed. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Source of weights: The weights are derived from 2024 national accounts data. For derivation of the weights at the lower level the household budget survey 2021, scanner data, additional external and administrative data sources. Time period of current weights: Calendar year 2025. Weights are price updated. Frequency of weight updates: Every year. Item selection: Purposive; most important criterion for representatives items selection was the share of expenditure for them on the overall household expenditure. The information from price collectors and other information (internet) are used by annual updating of consumer basket. Selection of products/varieties is also purposive; price collectors select products/varieties according to the item specification, with significant market share, in cooperation with outlets representatives. Outlet selection: Purposive selection of outlets where consumers usually do their shopping. There are 37 monitored regions selected. The selection is based on the popularity of the outlet (volume of sales), consistency and completeness of the range of products, geographical location and type of outlet. As a rule, three to five outlets are selected for each item in the different regions, more outlets are selected in Bratislava, the capital city of the SR. Fewer number of outlets are selected in those regions where a limited outlet network or set of items is. The centrally administered prices (e.g. Gas, Electricity, Passenger transport by railway, Post and telecom fees) are collected centrally by staff of the Statistical Office of the SR. Sample sizes: Prices of goods and services are collected in about 9 252 outlets and business premises. Number of price quotations is about 41 928. Price collection methods: Through personal visits by the staff of the regional statistical authorities to the outlets (retail sale shops, service business premises, housing co-operatives). The prices collected for each of the 37 regions are entered into computer files located at the Regional administrations of the Statistical Office of the SR. The collected price data are directly sent to national computing centre. These prices are valid in Slovakia (eg postal services, gas, railways, newspapers, etc.). It is centrally populated prices of some items representing purchase via the Internet. Item/product specification: The level of detail of specification for varieties of product (e.g. trade mark, producer, packing) depends on the kind of representative items. For some of items it is very detailed, for others specification is rather broad and price collectors themselves write the detailed description of the product observed by them. The price collectors write varieties of products by the software, which is regularly updated. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Timing of price observation: Prices are surveyed on a monthly basis, during the first 20 days of a reference month. Since the year 2009 prices of fuels (petrol and oil-fuel) have been collected weekly. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Computation of lowest level indices: Ratio of unweighted arithmetic average at the current and base period. Aggregation: Elementary indices are aggregated to several levels using the Laspeyres formula. Alignment of expenditure and base period: The weight reference period: the calendar year 2024. The weights are price updated. Reference period: Average of year 2025 (Index=100). The base period for weights: Calendar year 2024. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Treatment of missing prices: The software enables to impute missing prices. The imputation of missing price is done according to the average price increase of observed prices for certain item. Selection of replacement items: The quality, utility properties and also the price level are considered. Adjustments for quality differences: The bridged overlap quality adjustment method is used in forced replacement. During the year 2010 QA methods were completed by % evaluation of the quality change of replaced and replacement product. Starting with the year 2011 the treatment of seasonal products was changed according to the Commission Regulation (EC) No. 330/2009. Introducing new products: The list of varieties reviewed continuously and rules are established to introduce new products. The information from price collectors as well as other information (internet) are used for identification of the new products. Seasonal items: Prices are collected only during the season. Out of season (if not available) the last observed price is carried forward until the new season. The weights for seasonal items are held constant throughout year. Owner-occupied housing: Since 1997, owner-occupied housing have been included in the CPI. Since the beginning of the year 2010 the imputed rent is derived from the monthly rent index, fund for repair works index and the price index of the building materials used for the repairs and maintenance of the flats and family houses. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results Verification of prices: The control of prices is done at a very detailed level. Price collectors write to electronic files not only observed prices of certain good/service but also its characteristics. The files are then sent to the national computing center where the control is done. The files contain the address and telephone number of the outlet where the price was observed, so there is also possibility to verify the price directly in the outlet. Compilers and statisticians at the national computing center analyze every item index. Verification of processing: In weight calculation, there were compared data obtained from the household budget survey (family accounts) statistics, the national accounts statistics and administrative sources.
