 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The BOP is a statistical statement that systematically summarizes, for a specific time period, the economic transactions between Lao PDR residents and nonresidents. Transactions between residents and nonresidents, consist of those involving goods, services, and primary income; those involving financial claims on, and liabilities to the rest of the world, and those (such as gifts and grants) classified as secondary income, which involve offsetting entries to balance--in an accounting sense--one-sided transactions. For Lao PDR, BOP is compiled and disseminated on a monthly, quarterly, and annual basis by the Bank of Lao PDR (BOL) in accordance with the principles and methodology recommended in the sixth edition of the Balance of Payments and International Investment Position Manual (BPM6) . Data are presented in millions of US dollar (USD). Monthly data are aggregated to derive quarterly and annual data, and presented in calendar year. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization BOP data disseminated by the BOL follows classification of the current account (goods, services, primary income, and secondary income), capital account, and functional categories and sectorization of financial account transactions as recommended in the BPM6. 2.2.1 Scope BOP data disseminated by the BOL follows classification of the current account (goods, services, primary income, and secondary income), capital account, and functional categories and sectorization of financial account transactions as recommended in the BPM6. Scope of the data • Transactions coverage: BOP data disseminated by the BOL covers main components of the current account, capital account, and financial account as specified in the BPM6. No external transaction is intentionally excluded. The residency concept as guided by the BPM6 is adopted. • Geographic coverage: All territories under the administration of the government of the Lao PDR, including special economic zones, are treated as being part of the economic territory of the Lao PDR. Exceptions to coverage ◦ Goods: Merchanting is currently not covered, but is assessed to be insignificant. ◦ Primary income: Reinvested earnings of non-banks are yet to be included (pending completion of the IIP survey). ◦ Secondary income: Insurance net premium and claims are not yet included. ◦ Financial account: • Reinvestment of earnings of non-banks are yet to be included (pending completion of the IIP survey). • Financial derivatives are not yet included, but are assessed to be insignificant. • Trade credits are not yet included. • Foreign currency cash held by households Unrecorded activity No estimates are made for smuggling or other illegal transactions. 2.4.1 Valuation Exports and imports are recorded at f.o.b. price. Financial account transactions are generally recorded at market value (for items sourced from international transaction reporting system - ITRS), nominal value (for loan and debt securities sourced from administrative data sources), and book value for financial transactions of banks derived from positions data reported for the monetary and financial statistics (MFS). 2.4.2 Recording basis Accrual basis is not yet adopted for financial account debt items. Transactions are converted to USD equivalent using monthly average exchange rate. Goods are recorded at the time the goods passed the Customs formalities and recorded by the Customs. Items sourced from ITRS are cash-based, and thus generally reflects recording at the settlement date. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures Broadly consistent with BPM6 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Goods: Customs and Ministry of Energy and Mines Services: ITRS, administrative data (for travel), direct report (for telecommunication service, financial services, part of government services n.i.e.), and estimation (import freight, insurance on imports, and travel) Primary Income: ITRS, direct report (reinvested earnings of banking sector), and estimation (compensation of employees) Secondary Income: ITRS, administrative data (grants and donation), and estimation (workers' remittances) Capital account: Administrative data (capital transfers) Financial account: ITRS, administrative data (government foreign borrowing, assets and liabilities of BOL, private sector external borrowing and investment), and direct report (transactions of banking sector) Reserve assets: Administrative data from Operation Department 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording are broadly consistent with the requirements stated in the BPM6. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Most source data are available within 1-2 months after the reference period. Data from the MOF are available approximately 1 quarters after the reference period. 3.2.1 Source data assessment Source data are monitored regularly to ensure comprehensive coverage of the BOP. Mirror data for main counterparts for selected BOP components are used to cross-check with exports and imports reported by the Customs. The BOL also maintains close collaboration and discussion with relevant authorities (government agencies, private sector, and main counterpart countries) to explore new data sources periodically. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Data compilation is based on reasonably sound statistical techniques (e.g., estimation models for travel, transport, compensation of employees and workers' remittances are based on variables obtained from administrative data sources, and cross-checked with partner data). 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results All data are validated in consultation with data providers: - High-valued transactions reported through ITRS are verified with banks - Administrative data are verified with source agencies, in case of doubtful transactions - Mirror data disseminated by main partner countries and database maintained by international organizations are also used to validate key data components (e.g. trade in goods, travel, FDI, etc.) 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs Net errors and omissions are regularly monitored. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses Revision studies are conducted periodically and the results are used for internal analysis and quality control (e.g., revision analysis was conducted as part of the preparations for dissemination of BPM6 time series). 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses See 3.5.1 5.1.5 Dissemination on request Not applicable
