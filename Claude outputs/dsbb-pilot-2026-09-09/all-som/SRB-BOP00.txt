 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The concepts, definitions and procedures used in the compilation of the balance of payments are to large extent in line with the guidelines contained in the sixth edition of the IMF Balance of Payments Manual, 2009 (BPM6). Definition: Balance of payments (BOP) is a statistical statement which is summarizing the economic transactions of the Republic of Serbia with the rest of the world (transactions between residents and nonresidents) during a certain period. The BOP is compiled on a monthly and annual level in EUR and USD. Quarterly data can be derived from the monthly presentation. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization BOP transactions are classified according to the standard components of BPM6. Transactions of portfolio and other investments are additionally divided by sectors: central bank, deposit-taking corporations except central bank, general government and other sectors including other financial institutions, nonfinancial corporations, households and NPISH (non-profit institutions serving households). 2.2.1 Scope BOP transactions are classified according to the standard components of BPM6. Transactions of portfolio and other investments are additionally divided by sectors: central bank, deposit-taking corporations except central bank, general government and other sectors including other financial institutions, nonfinancial corporations, households and NPISH (non-profit institutions serving households). Scope of the data The scope of BOP is broadly consistent with internationally accepted standards, covering all transactions between residents and nonresidents. Residence is established following the BPM6 principle. The data cover the entire economy. - Transaction coverage: All transactions, as recommended in BPM6, are included in the balance of payments. - Sectorial coverage: All sectors, as prescribed in BPM6, are covered. In published BOP presentation sectorial breakdown is shown for portfolio and other investments. - Geographical coverage: A geographical attribution is available for all partner countries, but due to confidentiality not all data are publically available. - Unrecorded activity: Estimates for illegal activities are included since 2015 onwards. Exceptions to coverage There are no BOP transactions that are systematically excluded. Unrecorded activity Data on illegal activity (smuggling of cigarettes, drug trafficking and prostitutions) are estimated by the Statistical Office of the Republic of Serbia and are included in BOP data. 2.4.1 Valuation In BOP statistics market valuations are generally used. Exports and imports of goods are valued on f.o.b. basis. BoP data are expressed in US dollars and EUR, and the calculation of the transaction value from original currencies into the reporting currency is performed by applying the NBS official foreign exchange rate on the transaction day for each individual transaction. 2.4.2 Recording basis Recording of flows is basically on an accrual basis, i.e. at the time at which economic value is created, transformed, exchanged, transferred, or extinguished (except interest on loans and securities, dividends where income is recorded on a settlement basis). 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures Grossing and netting procedures correspond to the IMF BPM6 recommendations . Current and capital account transactions are shown on a gross basis (except for merchanting), while financial account transactions are recorded on a net basis. In case of the current and capital account a net (balance) is credit minus debit and in case of financial account a net corresponds to assets minus liabilities. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The main data sources for the compilation of BoP statistics are: 1. External trade on goods, illegal trade, travel, etc. calculated and provided to the NBS by the Statistical Office of the Republic of Serbia, 2. ITRS – International Transactions Reporting System (closed system), 3. direct reports on resident accounts abroad (form RN), 4. direct reports on construction works (form GRU), 5. direct reports on direct investments (forms DI-1 and DI-2), 6. reports from MTOs, 7. direct reports on portfolio investments (forms DA-1, DA-2 and DAP), 8. direct reports on loans (forms KZ and KO), 9. direct reports received from banks, insurance and pension funds, leasing and factoring on their external assets and liabilities transactions collected by the Monetary and Financial Statistics Division, 10. financial statements of insurance and pension funds collected by supervisory divisions within the NBS, 11. NBS foreign exchange reserves data (collected by the division responsible for managing NBS FX reserves within the NBS), 12. administrative data (Central Security and Depository House, Business Registry Agency, etc.) Goods: The main data source for export and import of goods is the Statistical Office of the Republic of Serbia on foreign trade (based on data from customs declarations). Data from ITRS are used for estimation of merchanting. F.o.b/c.i.f. adjustment is done using information received from the Statistical Office of the Republic of Serbia. Services: Data on services are mostly obtained from the ITRS and combined with data received from direct reports (residents’ accounts abroad and construction work). FISIM is estimated using data from direct reports on loans (calculating the average interbank reference rate and applying it to calculate FISIM). Estimates of travel services and f.o.b/c.i.f. adjustment are based on data received from the Statistical Office of the Republic of Serbia. Primary Income: ITRS is the data source on compensation of employees and income on investment, except for reinvested earnings. Data source on reinvested earnings is direct reporting of enterprises (forms DI-1 and DI-2). Secondary Income: The main data sources for official and private transfers are ITRS, Statistical Office of Republic of Serbia and data collected by other parts of the NBS (e.g. official grants, exchange bureaus, etc.). A part of remittances, the so-called unrecorded remittances are estimated using data on new savings and exchange bureaus. For additional information data received by MTOs are used. Capital Account: The data source on capital transfers and acquisition/disposal of non-produced non-financial assets is ITRS. Direct Investments: The data sources for FDI are direct reports (special forms for FDI: DI-1 and DI-2, and for loans: KZ and KO), Statistical Office of the Republic of Serbia and ITRS. For cross-checking and additional data, Business Registry Agency data are used. Portfolio Investment: The data sources for portfolio investment are data received from the Central Security and Depository House and direct reports (forms DA-1, DA-2 and DAP). Other Investment: Other equity is estimated as residual using data from ITRS, Central Depository and Clearing House and Business Registry Agency. Currency and Deposits are calculated using data from Monetary and Financial Statistics Division and direct reports on accounts abroad (form RN). Loans are recorded using data received directly from reporting agents on cross-border financial loans transactions (forms KZ and KO). Insurance, pension, and standardized guarantee schemes are estimated using data from financial statements of insurance and pension funds and payed premiums/claims form ITRS. Trade credit and advances are calculated using data from forms KZ and KO (for long term trade loans), additional data from main energy importers and model based estimation for short term trade loans. Reserve assets: Data on reserve assets are obtained from the division within the NBS responsible for managing NBS FX reserves. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording The source data generally cover definitions, scope, classifications, valuation and time recording, almost fully in line with the BPM6 methodology. The ITRS and surveys are set up to provide data that follow BPM6 concepts on definition, scope and classification. The timing of goods is according to customs clearance, which is the approximation for the time of change of economic ownership. Transactions which are calculated using the ITRS are based on the time of payment. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Data sources are timely and received electronically. Data from main data sources are available within the adequate timeline in order to compile BOP. Statistical Office of the Republic of Serbia’s data are available within 5 weeks after the reference period. ITRS data are available 3 days after the end of the 10-day reporting period. Direct reports from enterprises are available 10 days after the end of reporting period (quarterly and monthly based). Data from the Central Security and Depository House and Business Registry Agency are daily updated. 3.2.1 Source data assessment Source data are routinely assessed for coverage, sample error, response error and non-sampling error and the results of the assessments are monitored. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques The source data statistical techniques for the balance of payments components are as follows: I. Current account Data on exports and imports of goods are obtained from the Statistical Office of the Republic of Serbia (based on the customs declarations and estimation model for illegal trade). In line with the BPM6 methodology, export and import of goods are adjusted to f.o.b. parity using additional information provided by the Statistical Office of the Republic of Serbia. Data on merchanting are estimated using ITRS data. Data on export and import of services are calculated using data from the ITRS and direct reports, and several additional data sources for estimation of travel, transportation and insurance services. Data on services cover: manufacturing services on physical inputs owned by others, maintenance and repair services, transportation, travel, construction, insurance and pension services, financial services (FISIM separately shown), charges for the use of intellectual property, telecommunications, computer, and information services, other business services (including research and development services, professional and management consulting services, technical, trade-related, etc.), personal, cultural, and recreational services and government goods and services n.i.e. Data on primary income account are obtained from the ITRS and direct reports and include receipt and expenditure on compensation of employees, investment income – direct, portfolio other investment and reserve assets. Data on secondary income account are obtained from ITRS, Statistical Office of the Republic of Serbia and data collected by other parts of the NBS (e.g. official grants, exchange bureaus, etc.). Data are classified according to the BPM6 methodology to general government and other sectors. They include receipt and expenditure on taxes, subsidies and official grants (government) and personal transfers and other current transfers. II. Capital account The capital account is compiled from ITRS records. The two main entries are the gross acquisitions / disposals of nonproduced nonfinancial assets and other capital transfers. III. Financial account Direct investment – net is classified on assets/liabilities principal. Definition of FDI follows the ten percent rule and FDIR. Direct investments cover investments in the form of equity capital (including reinvested earnings) and debt instruments. Portfolio investment – net covers cross-border investments in securities (equity less than 10% of ownership and debt short-term and long-term) . Portfolio investment is classified on assets and liabilities principal and includes claims on equity securities and debt securities issued by non-residents and liabilities on equity securities and debt securities issued by residents. Other investment includes data on other equity capital, currency and deposits, loans, insurance, pension, and standardized guarantee schemes, trade credit and advances, other accounts receivable/payable and special drawing rights (only liability side). Reserve assets show changes of the stock of NBS foreign reserves during the period excluding the effect of inter-currency changes and price changes. They include NBS claims on monetary gold, special drawing rights, reserve position in the IMF, currency and deposits on the accounts abroad, foreign securities and derivatives and other claims. IV. Errors and omissions - net are determined residually, as defined by BPM6 and constitute an accounting offsetting entry. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Balance of payments data are not seasonally adjusted. For services and direct investment geographical breakdown is provided and published, and for direct investments breakdown by economic activity is published as well. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results Balance of Payments data are assessed and investigated using other source when available. Information reports in the financial press are used to verify high-value direct investment and other transactions. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data Statistical discrepancies in intermediate data are assessed and investigated. Merchandise trade and associated financial flows are monitored and reviewed. The calculations are made as a part of the estimation of trade credit, but fluctuations are investigated for possible data problems. Data on travel-related transactions are assessed in relation to information compiled by the RS Statistical Office on the average number of days of stay of non-residents in Serbia and the number of tourists visiting Serbia. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs Statistical discrepancies and other potential indicators of problems in statistical outputs are monitored and investigated. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses Studies and analyses of revisions are carried out and used internally to inform statistical processes. The revision is carefully monitored for each individual record. The aggregates are compared with data for the same month as reported in the previous month’s publication. Changes are checked, to ensure that they can be explained. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses Revision studies are not disseminated. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request It is possible to provide unpublished but non-confidential statistics on official request.
