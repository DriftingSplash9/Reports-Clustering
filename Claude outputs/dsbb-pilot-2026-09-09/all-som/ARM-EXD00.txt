 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Analytical framework: The conceptual framework for external debt statistics is the sixth edition of the Balance of Payments Manual ( BPM6 ) and External Debt Statistics: Guide for Compilers and Users ( External Debt Guide ). The data on end-of-quarter gross stocks of external debt liabilities are presented under the following data categories: for each of four sectors— general government sector, monetary authorities (central bank), banks, other sectors—data are broken down by maturity (short- and long-term) on an original maturity basis and by debt instrument as specified in the BPM6 — debt securities, trade credits, loans, currency and deposits, other debt liabilities, and intercompany loans; data on intercompany lending (liabilities) under direct investment relationships are separately identified without sector attribution; debt data are consistent with the corresponding series in the international investment position (IIP) statistics. Definition: Gross external debt is defined as the outstanding amount (at any given point in time) of all actual current, and not contingent, liabilities that require payment(s) of principal and/or interest by the debtor at some point(s) in the future and that are owed to nonresidents by residents of an economy. Relationship to international guidelines: The Gross external debt of RA is compiled by the Central bank of Armenia (CBA) in accordance with the concepts, definitions and classifications outlined in the BPM6 and External Debt Guide, as far as the availability and characteristics of the source data allow. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Classification: System of standard components is used to classify external debt liabilities according to the BPM6. 2.2.1 Scope Classification: System of standard components is used to classify external debt liabilities according to the BPM6. Scope of the data Coverage of external debt: All the operations outlined in the BPM6 methodology are covered. Geographical coverage: The geographical territory of Republic of Armenia (RA) matches with its economic territory. Unrecorded activity Liabilities of households are not identified. 2.4.1 Valuation Conversion to unit of account: Data in several currencies are converted to common currency (U.S. dollars) on the exchange rate adopted by the Central Bank at the last day of the reference period. Valuation: External financial assets and liabilities are valued at the book values, except government securities and asset side of banking securities. 2.4.2 Recording basis Recording basis: Accrual principle is used for recording debt position data. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Debt securities: The source of data for debt securities acquired by non-residents is the administrative register. The CBA computes data on short-term trade credits by using data of one-time surveys (organized by the Armstat) of organizations engaged in external economic activity. Trade credits: The data for long term trade credits are derived through enterprise surveys conducted by the Armstat. The Armstat provides data on short-term trade credits using data of one-time surveys of organizations engaged in external economic activity. Loans: The CBA collects data for loans from enterprise surveys conducted by the Armstat, from the Ministry of Finance and also from financial institutions. Currency and deposits: For compiling currency and deposits items the CBA takes financial institutions’ and its own data and also data of sample surveys on remittances conducted by the CBA. Other debt liabilities: The CBA obtains data for other debt liabilities from the Armstat derived through enterprise surveys, and from financial institutions. Intercompany loans: The sources of data for intercompany loans are derived through enterprise surveys conducted by the Armstat and data from financial sector collected by the CBA. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording The framework, concepts, and definitions are in conformity with the methodology set out in BPM6 and External debt statistics guide. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Data collection and processing are adequate to meet timeliness and periodicity for disseminating the external debt statistics. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Generally there are no statistical adjustments in coverage, classification, timing, and valuation of data in government and banking sectors. Some statistical adjustments are made in enterprise surveys data. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Data are not seasonally adjusted. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results Cross-checks of mirror data is conducted periodically with partner countries and international organizations on remittances, foreign direct investments, trade and other investments data. 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points Central bank of Armenia Contact information (e-mail addresses and telephone numbers) are available on the CBA website. Assistance and support is provided to users, who can pose queries by contacting the contact point or the Press Office of the CBA, which will forward the inquiries to the relevant department. Statistical Committee of the Republic of Armenia (Armstat) The Statistical Information Dissemination Division of the Armstat is responsible for statistical issues. Information on contact points is specified in all the publications of the Armstat, respectively. In addition, the Statistical Yearbook and the ad-hoc publications include contact information on respective responsible persons (name, surname and telephone). 5.3.2 Availability of documents and services catalogs Central bank of Armenia The CBA website provides users with considerable amount of information about electronic and paper-based publications and documents. The CBA publications can be obtained free of charge from the contact point upon request. Statistical Committee of the Republic of Armenia (Armstat) According to the Annual Statistical Program, every year the Statistical Committee disseminates the Calendar of Statistical Publications. The Calendar presents each publication by title, through a short summary of its contents, periodicity and publication deadline. The Calendar aims to guide users about the statistical products published by the Armstat. The electronic version of the Calendar for 2023 is available on the Armstat's website at https://www.armstat.am/file/calendar/2023-i_Oracuyc_en.pdf .
