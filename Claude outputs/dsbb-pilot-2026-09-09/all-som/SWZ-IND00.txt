 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The classification system used is ISIC rev2 as used for the national accounts. Scope of the data The index of industrial production measures short-term developments in the volume of output in selected manufacturing establishments that are considered to be large in terms of employment or output. In total, more than 20 establishments are surveyed, contributing a large share of total manufacturing gross output. None 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The data are collected by means of the Quarterly Survey of Industrial Production. The questionnaires are usually sent out by mail at the end of the reference period and collected by staff of the customs and excise department together with their data on exports during the following month. The survey collects quarterly data on both quantities produced and sales for all major commodities produced by the establishment. The index has a fixed base year of 1993. The lowest level indices are calculated as the output volume of the current quarter, expressed in units like kilograms, square meters, liters, etc., divided by output volume in the base year quarter. These are aggregated according to the Laspeyres formula to obtain group and overall indices. If no data at all are reported, the volume changes for the corresponding period in the previous year are applied on the last quoted output volume. The weights are determined by the aggregate quantities for each branch and are based on production output. These branch indices are then aggregated into the total index using value added weights from the national accounts. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures The weights for the major categories are: Sugar refining 0.047 Other agro-processing 0.037 Drink processing 0.693 Wood pulp & Timber 0.029 Other manufacturing 0.194 None
