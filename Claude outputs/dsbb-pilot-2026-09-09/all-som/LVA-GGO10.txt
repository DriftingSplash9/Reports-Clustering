 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The presentation form corresponds to the IMF´s Government Finance Statistics Manual 2014 framework (Statement of operations). Data on General Government Operations are in accordance with European System of Accounts 2010 (ESA 2010) and the Manual on Government Deficit and Debt (MGDD). Data are consolidated between the subsectors of the government and within the sector General Government (consolidation is made in full compliance with the methodology set in the ESA 2010). 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The general government sector and its subsectors (central government, local government, and social security funds) are defined in accordance with the ESA 2010 guidelines and the ESA 2010 Manual on Government Deficit and Debt. 2.2.1 Scope The general government sector and its subsectors (central government, local government, and social security funds) are defined in accordance with the ESA 2010 guidelines and the ESA 2010 Manual on Government Deficit and Debt. Scope of the data Data on General Government Operations cover the central government, the local government and the social security funds in accordance with the ESA 2010 guidelines and the ESA 2010 Manual on Government Deficit and Debt. These subsectors consist of main units and reclassified units to the government sector. Exceptions to coverage There are no exceptions to coverage. Unrecorded activity There are no unrecorded activities. 2.4.1 Valuation All transactions (financial and non-financial) are valued according to the ESA 2010 guidelines. Valuation of foreign currency transactions: Exchange rates used to convert foreign currency into denominated currency are approved by Central Bank, applicable at the time of the transaction. 2.4.2 Recording basis Data are recorded on an accrual basis in line with the ESA2010. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures Grossing/netting procedures are made in line with the ESA 2010. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Source data for general government operations statistics: - Monthly report on the central and local government budget execution (the Treasury). - Quarterly report on financial assets and liabilities of state and municipal budget and budget institutions (the Treasury); - Quarterly statistical survey of financial assets and liabilities of the units reclassified to the government sector (the Central Statistical Bureau of Latvia). 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Source data meet the requirements of the ESA 2010 and Eurostat Manual on Government Deficit and Debt regarding definitions, scope, classifications, valuation and time of recording. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Source data timeliness meets the required standards for SDDS Plus. All source data are available within the time schedule set for the processing of the ESA 2010 results. 3.2.1 Source data assessment A routine assessment is regularly performed. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Preliminary data are subsequently replaced by final data for each period. All data are corrected when more accurate data become available. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Not applicable. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results Validation of intermediate result is carried out during the processing of data sources, comparison with the previous report is made. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data Intermediate data are assessed during the production process on different levels. The source data is compared with counterpart information. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs The results are compared with annual reports of individual units. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses Quarterly data are revised within current year. Previous year´s quarterly data are revised after publication of annual national accounts or after revision of annual financial accounts. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses Detailed documentation on major revisions is provided in different media: Internet and press releases. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request Key statistics is available on the website of the Official Statistics Portal . All other non-confidential data are available on request. Depending on the requested statistics and the amount of work done, they are provided free of charge or for fee. Information on paid services of the Central Statistical Bureau can be found here . 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points The information on contact points is presented in each publication, news releases and in statistical database. All database tables include information on contact points (section "About table/Footnotes/Metadata/Contact").
