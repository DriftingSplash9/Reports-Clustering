 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Consumer price index is compiled in accordance with the Consumer Price Index Manual, 2020 – Concepts and Methods , https://www.elibrary.imf.org/display/book/9781484354841/9781484354841.xml. Definition: Consumer price index measures the evolution of price for goods bought and for tariffs of services used by the population in a certain period (current period), as against previous period (base period). Consumer price index is calculated only for elements entering the population direct consumption, excluding: consumption from own resources, expenditures for investments and accumulation, interests paid for credits, insurance rates, fines, taxes etc. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The Consumer Price Index is calculated on the basis of the following classifications: - COICOP classification harmonised with ECOICOP 2013 classification; - the national classification of goods and services, which is harmonised with COICOP and is used to respond to the needs of national users, but also to continue the dynamic time series of the indices. Scope of the data Population coverage: The reference population for the index is all private households from urban and rural areas (population is presented for the whole of the country, except the left side of river Nistru and municipality Bender). Geographic coverage: Prices are collected from 900 outlets (retail trade and rendering services units to population) from 8 cities of the Republic of Moldova. Item coverage: Sample of goods and services – includes more than 1300 items in total having an important weight in the population consumption. The used nomenclature is divided into 3 aggregation levels: groups, positions and items as follows: major group of food goods contains 109 groups with 400 items; major group of non-food goods contains 158 groups with 700 items; major group of services contains 79 groups with 200 items. Exceptions to coverage The data do not cover the territory of Transnistria region (the left side of the Nistru river and municipality Bender). 2.4.1 Valuation Types of prices: The actual price of a product available for sale to the population (not including goods sold under confessional terms) and paid in cash is subject to recording. The consumer price includes the VAT, excise tax, sales tax, and other indirect taxes. 2.4.2 Recording basis Time of recording: Prices for each foodstuff product are recorded twice a month (in the first and in the second half of the month). Prices for non-foodstuff products and services are recorded only once in a month. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Sources of weights: Statistics on expenditures by the population for the product groups included in the CPI calculation are applied from Household Budget Survey. The survey sample is a probabilistic sample representative at the country level, by area and statistical region: North, Center, South and mun. Chisinau. A sample of about 1200 households, both urban and rural, is used annually for the Household Budget Survey. Persons who live in such institutional units as prisons, sanatoriums, old people's homes, hospices etc. are not covered in the survey. Frequency of revisions to weights: Weights used for CPI calculations are updated each year. Period of current index weights: As weights for CPI calculation in current year the structure of household budget expenditures is used for the year t-2 adjusted to the structure of expenditure from National Accounts. Considering the impact of the Covid 19 pandemic on the social and economic situation in the country in 2020, the expenditure weights used for the CPI calculation in 2021 (based on the population's monetary expenditure in 2019) have been maintained for the CPI calculation in 2022. Item selection: The sample of goods and services covers the products that have a significant weight in population's consumption. Outlet selection : The sample of surveyed units covers stores and units providing services to the population. The selection was based on the need for ensuring the representativeness from the perspective of production distribution volume and service rendering. The units where prices/tariffs are recorded are kept in the sample for as long as possible in order to ensure continuity and comparability in time of series. The sample contains 900 outlets. Sample size and design: The sample of localities is established respecting the representativeness restrictions depending on the geographical area (8 zone centers), number of inhabitants and the volume of production distribution of goods and services to the population. Price collection methods: Price collectors record prices/tariffs for representative goods/services using Tablets. The collectors have the possibility to select the outlets and the goods/services for which they record the price. Prices are registered for goods/services defining a clear and precise description (constructive qualitative and functional characteristics). Item/product specification: One of the priorities when building the CPI is the development of the Nomenclature of representative goods and services . When establishing commodity groups, the homogeneous products were chosen from the point of view of dimension, weight, content, raw material and price changes. The product/service is defined clearly, precisely thought its qualitative, constructive and functional features that would make the corresponding product/service individual within the position it is placed. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Generally, the data source is consistent with the definitions, scope, classifications, time of recording required for the elaboration of qualitative CPI statistics. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness The timeliness of CPI data meets requirements. 3.2.1 Source data assessment Source data are assessed for their accuracy on a routine basis. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Computation of lowest level indices: The prices collected are combined as an unweighted geometric mean to give the average monthly price for each item. Aggregation formula : Laspeyres formula is used to obtain consumer price indices. Aggregated price indices are compiled, using the appropriate weights. Alignment of expenditure and base period : Adjustments are made to the weights to assure that the base periods of the weights and prices are comparable. Linking of reweighted index to historical index : To ensure the comparability of information in two successive years, indices of missing prices for the previous year are imputed. The index is chain – linked. Index reference period (index = 100): December of the previous year is used as period when the price index = 100. Indices are calculated and published as against previous month, December, corresponding month and period of previous year. In addition, for analytical purposes, data with the following reference periods is published (calculated with the chain linking method with the use of indices with previous period=100): • December 1993=100 (monthly indices) • 2015=100 (monthly indices). 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Treatment of missing prices : Prices for missing items are imputed using the price for a similar item or by extrapolating the price from the previous month or by using the index of the group as a whole. Selection of replacement items : If an item ceases to be available, a replacement is selected, based on the advice of the regional collectors. If the old and new items were both available in some period the ratio of these overlap prices are used to link the series. On the other hand, if the prices for the two items were not available in the same period, the group index is used to link them together. Adjustments for quality differences : An adjustment for quality differences when replacement items are selected must be performed following the current methodology for the computation of consumer price indices through a price adjustment, and if it is not possible to do so, through a change in the base price. Introduction of new products : Introducing the new goods and services items is made in the beginning of the year. Before the next weight update introducing new items means that the old weight of item is split in different ways depending on additional source of data. Seasonal items: For seasonal items prices are collected when the item is sold in sufficient quantities. Seasonally-adjusted indices: Prices are being imputed for all out-of-season products using imputation procedures that are self-correcting. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results Verification of prices: The verification of prices is done visually and using the software, when the information is send and received from 8 cities of the Republic of Moldova. Verification of processing: The control of CPI calculation and primary data processing of prices and CPI is carried out visuall y and using the software. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request NBS makes available for users' additional statistical information beyond the data presented in the statistical publications, news releases, analytical reports placed on the official website, in the limits of available information, in line with the Law on Official Statistics.
