 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Central Bank of Bahrain (CBB) compiles data on international reserves as defined in the Balance of Payments Manual (BPM 6 ) . Detailed data are collected, complied, and published in the Monthly Statistical Bulletin. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Foreign positions are distinguished based on residency criterion. The residency concept follows the definition of the Balance of Payments Manual, 6th edition (BPM 6 ) . Resident entities are defined as those entities, except for embassies and international organizations that are located in Bahrain, whether or not associated with an institution that is located outside Bahrain. Conversely, non-residents are entities located outside Bahrain, whether or not owned – wholly or in part – by entities inside Bahrain. None 2.4.1 Valuation Valuation principles: All CBB foreign assets and liabilities are reported at current market values, to the extent possible. Valuation of foreign-currency-denominated Instruments: All foreign currency denominated assets and liabilities are converted into the national currency equivalent using the prevailing market exchange rate on the reference date. 2.4.2 Recording basis Time of recording: Interest accrued on foreign assets and liabilities, to the extent possible, are directly attributed to the underlying accounts. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs CBB Accounts Directorate provides the balance sheet of the CBB,. It gathers the data from Reserve Management Directorate (Monetary Gold and Foreign Exchange). 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording In general, the CBB’s summary balance sheet used by Financial Stability Directorate (FSD) contains sufficient detail for compilation of foreign assets and liabilities. However, greater detail on positions by instrument, currency and counterpart is needed for compiling gross international reserves and its respective components. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness The CBB’s monthly balance sheet is available on the fifth business day following the reference month. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques The statistics are derived from the balance sheet records and satisfy internal constraints imposed by the structure of the double-entry system of bookkeeping. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results The CBB’s accounts are subject to annual external audits.
