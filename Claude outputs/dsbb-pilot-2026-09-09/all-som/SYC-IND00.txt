Data is collected monthly and also disseminated on a monthly basis. 3.2.1 Source data assessment The compilation of the Industrial Production Index (IPI) occurs monthly and the IPI compiler works in close collaboration with the production statistics compiler to maintain consistency. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques The SIPI is calculated using the Laspeyres formula which is considered acceptable by international standards. The Laspeyres method measures the percentage change in the production value of a set of products whose quantities are fixed in an earlier period. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures To use the Laspeyres index accurately, the value shares (weights), unit quantity, quantity relative are derived. The value share (weights) reflects the relative importance of a product, product group or industry within the overall scope of the industrial production. The unit quantity is gathered on a monthly basis from the participating manufacturers. The quantity relative is calculated by taking the quantity in the current month divided by the reference quantity. The reference quantity is the average of the monthly quantity for the previous year. A seasonally adjusted series is also published. The SIPI team uses the same software as National Accounts to do this in order to maintain consistency. None
