 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Concept: The data are compiled in accordance with the sixth edition of the IMF's Balance of Payments Manual ( BPM6 ) and the ECB recommendation on Eurosystem reserve assets (see methodological note http://www.ecb.int ). The reserve assets must be under the effective control of the National Bank of Slovakia; and refer to highly liquid, marketable and creditworthy non-euro area currency-denominated claims on non-residents of the euro area as well as gold, special drawing rights and the reserve positions in the IMF of Slovakia. Reserve assets are compiled on a gross basis without any netting of reserve-related liabilities (with the exception of those reserve assets included in the sub-item of financial derivatives, which are recorded on a net basis). Definition: Official reserve assets are the reserves holdings of National Bank of Slovakia. Relationship to international guidelines: Data are compiled in accordance to the IMF's International Reserves and Foreign Currency Liquidity - Guidelines for a Data Template , which provides the complete metadata for the reserves template and in accordance to ESCB methodology layed down in the document ECB Eurosystem's international reserves, July 2000 . Scope of the data Institutional coverage: National Bank of Slovakia controls all of the Slovak Republic official reserve assets. Instrument coverage: Monetary Gold, SDR Holdings, Reserve position in the IMF, Foreign exchange, derivatives, and other items. Financial derivative coverage: Financial Derivatives can be identified separately. 2.4.1 Valuation Valuation: International reserves are valued in market values. As of 2016, data for international reserves, and data for data template on international reserves and foreign currency liquidity are presented in EUR. Conversion rates: Till end of 2015 International reserves are presented in US dollars and are converted into USD according official exchange rate of ECB on the day of the reference period. Figures in data template are presented in EUR. Conversion of foreign currency denominated assets is done by using official ECB exchange rate of the day of reference period. As of 2016, both data for International Reserves, and data for Data Template are presented in EUR. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Data sources: Settlement Department of the National Bank of Slovakia. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Statistical adjustments: Statistical adjustments of data are not used. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Seasonal adjustment of data is not applicable.
