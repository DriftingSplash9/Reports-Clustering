 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Compilation of Government Finance Statistics for Bosnia and Herzegovina is done in accordance with concepts of GFSM 2014, and three of four of GFS analytical framework reports are compiled and disseminated, namely Statement of Government Operations, Balance Sheet Statement and Other Economic Flows Statement, while Statement On Sources and Uses of Cash is still not being compiled for Bosnia and Herzegovina. The structure of Statement of Government Operations is as given: Revenues Expenses Gross Operational Balance Transactions in Nonfinancial Assets Net Lending/Borrowing Transaction in Financial Assets Transactions in Financial Liabilities The data breakdown differs on quarterly and annual level. While annual level of reporting goes as far as Table 1-3, quarterly reporting for most data is on synthetic level presented in Statement. Also Acquisition of Financial Assets and Liabilities are still not reported in quarterly reports for Central government. In Expenses side as well as Nonfinancial Transactions data for Amortization and CFC-Consumption of fixed Capital are missing due to lack of source data in annual as well as quarterly reports. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Classification of components of revenues and expenditures are similiar to GFSM 2014 classification with exemption of identified items. The quarterly reports on operations of the central government are consisted of the following: Revenues: taxes, social contributions, grants and other receipts (on cash basis), Expenditures: compensation of employees, purchases of goods and services, interest, subsidies, grants, social benefits and other payments (on accrual basis), net acquisition of nonfinancial assets (purchases of nonfinancial assets, sales of nonfinancial assets), deficit/surplus. Net acquisition of financial assets other than cash (domestic, foreign), net incurrence of liabilities (domestic, foreign) and net change in the stock of cash is under consideration how to implemented on quarterly level. 2.2.1 Scope Classification of components of revenues and expenditures are similiar to GFSM 2014 classification with exemption of identified items. The quarterly reports on operations of the central government are consisted of the following: Revenues: taxes, social contributions, grants and other receipts (on cash basis), Expenditures: compensation of employees, purchases of goods and services, interest, subsidies, grants, social benefits and other payments (on accrual basis), net acquisition of nonfinancial assets (purchases of nonfinancial assets, sales of nonfinancial assets), deficit/surplus. Net acquisition of financial assets other than cash (domestic, foreign), net incurrence of liabilities (domestic, foreign) and net change in the stock of cash is under consideration how to implemented on quarterly level. Scope of the data Data on central government level on quarterly level comprise of budgetary units (14 MoFs) but does not include the following data: Public Enterprises for Highways of both entities Republic of Srpska and Federation of Bosnia and Herzegovina and Public Enterprise for Highways of Brcko District (3). Public Enterprises for Road Maintenance of two entities Republic of Srpska and Federation of Bosnia and Herzegovina as well as three Public Enterprises for Road Maintenance on Cantonal level (2+3). Exceptions to coverage Quarterly data does not include extra-budgetary funds, municipalities and transactions on acquisition of financial assets and liabilities due to lack of source data on quarterly level. Unrecorded activity Grants in kind on revenues side are also not fully covered, because in national accounting practice there are treated only as changes in stocks but not transactions. Amortization- CFC in Expenses is missing in Statement of Government Operations due to lack of reliable data sources. 2.4.1 Valuation Current market prices are used to value revenue, expenses and transactions in non-financial assets i.e. transactions are recorded on the basis of their value at the time of the underlying economic event, which, for exchange transactions, generally corresponds to market prices. Face and nominal value is used to value transactions in financial assets and liabilities. 2.4.2 Recording basis Data are recorded on Accrual (Expense and Transaction in non- financial assets) and Cash (Revenue) basis for reporting units. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures The querterly data are presented on a fully consolidated basis, that is, transactions between the various items of central government budget have been eliminated from the data during the process of consolidation. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Source data for compiling of GFS for budgetary units and social security funds are: budget execution reports which cover all monetary transaction of unit reported as well as for number of units Gross balance sheet report. As additional data sources for some types of transactions and cross-checking purposes, CBBH GFS unit uses: IndirectTax authority data on collected indiricted taxes, Report of foreign debt from BH Ministry of finance and treasury covering Government foreign debt, disbursements, payments and debt outstanding by creditors 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Source data consists of Gross Balance sheets, periodical reports of reporting units and additional information that are provided on demand /clarification basis. Source data for revenues, expenses and transactions in non-financial assets cover all monetary transactions of all units currently included in Central Government Sector. Non-monetary transactions often require additional investigation of different data sources, counterpart information, and sometimes data adjustments. Classification of transaction requires substantial reclassifications, especially in sales of goods and services in revenues and treatments of use of goods and services and social transfers in expenses. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Deadlines for delivering quarterly source data are 45 days after end of referent period/quarter. 3.2.1 Source data assessment Source data are based on budget accounting and budget execution reports as legally binding forms of reporting so there is no need for statistical assessments of source data. Only for sectorization issues, assessment of all units included in Central Government Sector is done on irregular basis. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Except for reclassifying transactions within and between categories, in accordance with GFSM 2014, no other statistical technique is used. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures If significant discrepancy between different data sources occurs, further research is carried out to perform data adjustment, time of recording and sometimes to adjust type of valuations (especially for securities). 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results Validation of intermediate results are applied as follows : Mathematical data validation of totals to be equal to subtotals on level of each unit and on level of each sector and subsector. Logical data validation by types of revenues and expenses and its attribution to sectors and subsectors. Cross-checking of different source data, and if there are substantial discrepancies investigation of possible transactions unrecorded, coverage and scope issues, valuation and time of recording issues. Statistical discrepancies between transactions in revenues, expenses and nonfinancial transactions on one side, and financial assets and liabilities on other side, which can result in data adjustments mostly in expenses side (practices of deviation of accrual to cash treatment of expenses), adjustments of transactions in financial assets and liabilities, mostly unrecorded, in order to narrow discrepancy. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data The intermediate data series compiled by the Bank in accordance with GFSM 2014 methodology are broadly consistent with related data series produced by the MoFs according to the national methodology. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs There are number of differences between GFSM 2014 data and data received from Monetary and financial statistics. The difference/discrepancies are mainly caused sectorization issues, and wrong classification of units within existing sectorization. Discrepancies are analyzed jointly with MFS unit, and where possible corrections are made. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses No revision studies are carried out, but data is regularly revised and updated. Please see 0.4.2 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses There are no formal studies and analyses of revisions nor dissemination. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request More detailed data are available on request in the written form to statstical department using the following email address: statistics@cbbh.ba. 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points The email (please see 5.1.5) includes email of CGO compiler (Head of the unit).
