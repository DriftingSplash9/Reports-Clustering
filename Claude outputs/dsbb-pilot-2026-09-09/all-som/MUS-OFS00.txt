 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The analytical framework used for compiling monetary and financial statistics is based on the IMF MFSMCG 2016. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The classification of financial instruments and the principles of sectorization applied in the MFS compiled by the BOM are fully consistent with the guidelines of the MFSMCG 2016 . 2.2.1 Scope The classification of financial instruments and the principles of sectorization applied in the MFS compiled by the BOM are fully consistent with the guidelines of the MFSMCG 2016 . Scope of the data The other financial corporations survey is derived from financial accounts data. The financial accounts serve to record all transactions in financial assets and liabilities, broken down by institutional sectors. Geographic coverage: The financial accounts cover the Mauritian economic territory. The other financial corporations in Mauritius comprises of the following subsectors: Pension funds Global business companies (GBCs) Investment companies Insurance companies and other financial auxiliaries Exceptions to coverage The coverage follows the IMF TA recommendations and includes the most relevant OFCs. Unrecorded activity There is no unrecorded activity in the ‘Other Financial Corporations Survey’ data in terms of financial instruments and sectorization. 2.4.1 Valuation The valuation of financial assets and liabilities is based on market prices or, for financial assets and liabilities that are not traded or traded infrequently, on market-price equivalents (fair values). Loans and deposits, however, are included in the statistics based on book value, which includes the nominal value of the principal augmented by the accrued interest. This practice is consistent with the MFSMCG guidelines. Loans are reported on a gross basis, without netting provisions for expected losses. These provisions are reported in liabilities as part of other accounts payable and are identified in the reporting template. Financial instruments denominated in foreign currency are converted into national currency values at the mid-point market exchange rate prevailing at the balance sheet date or, for transactions, at the transaction date. This practice is consistent with the recommendations of the MFSMCG . 2.4.2 Recording basis Quarterly data is reported on an accrual basis. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures Data is collected and compiled on a gross basis. Assets and liabilities of the OFCs are reported on a gross basis in their quarterly returns. Consistent with the standard recommended in the MFSMCG , netting and consolidation adjustments are made only in the analytical surveys (OFCS and FCS). 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Data on Other Financial Corporations are compiled from a call report form being shared with BOM by the FSC. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Source data used for compiling financial statistics provides a reasonably good approximation of the concepts, definitions, scope, classifications, and recording principles for compiling sound statistics in line with MFSMCG 2016 . The BOM and FSC provide other financial corporations with guidance in the form of reporting instructions and ad-hoc consultation in completing the report forms. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness The data collection system allows sufficient time for the compilation of financial statistics within the agreed timeframe of 4 months after the end of the reference quarter. 3.2.1 Source data assessment Automated procedures are used to facilitate the monitoring of the accuracy of data reported by individual financial corporations. The data received from OFCs are verified using built-in consistency checks. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Compilation procedures rely on automated consistency checks to minimize processing errors such as coding, editing, and tabulation errors. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Given that data are fully based on accounting records, no estimation techniques are employed to adjust data for missing balance sheet items. However, in line with MFS TA October 2022 recommendations, the OFC survey and FC survey are being compiled using GBCs’ adjusted data (i.e., with nonresident equity liability positions treated as foreign liabilities) for BOM publications and to make MFS data more consistent with the IIP. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results The quality of financial statistics is monitored throughout the compilation process, which is automated. The source data from OFCs are subject to several built-in internal consistency checks and validation routines. Any inconsistencies and unexplained large data variations are shared with the FSC for further investigations. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data The MFS BOM compilers identify statistical discrepancies (e.g. discrepancies in inter sectoral positions) in collaboration with the FSC for further investigations and corrected where necessary. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs The procedures for ensuring internal and intertemporal consistency that are built in the data processing and compilation system provide adequate safeguards against unexplained fluctuations and large distortions in financial statistics. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses No revision studies are undertaken. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses No formal revision studies and analyses are prepared and made available to the public. However, if there is a major revision to the compilation methodology, the BOM will release an explanatory note on its website. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request Unpublished and non-confidential data are made available to users upon request. A footnote is inserted on the published analytical tables indicating to users that more granular data can be made available on request. 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points Users are requested to send their queries on all MFS publications of the Bank to the email address mfs@bom.mu . 5.3.2 Availability of documents and services catalogs None
