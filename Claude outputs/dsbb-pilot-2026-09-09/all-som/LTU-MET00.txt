 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Definition: The objective of ITGS is to measure the cross-border movements of goods between Lithuania and other countries. In principle, all physically incoming and outcoming goods are recorded (for exceptions see chapter 2.2.1) Since May 1, 2004, the ITGS consist of Extra-EU trade (movements of goods between Lithuania and non-EU countries) and Intra-EU trade (movements of goods between Lithuania and EU countries). The main data source for Extra-EU trade statistics is customs declarations. Intrastat is a system for the collection of data on Lithuania's trade with the EU Member States; data sources are Intrastat statistical reports. Relationship of national practices with international and regional guidelines: Compilation of the merchandise trade statistics is in line with EU legislation in the field of International trade in goods statistics. In general, the definitions used are in accordance with IMTS Concepts and Definitions (UN, 2010). 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Classification: Merchandise trade is classified according to the Combined Nomenclature (8-digit level) which is based on Harmonized Commodity Description and Coding System (HS). For analytical purposes, the trade data are classified according to the Standard International Trade Classification (SITC), Classification by Broad Economic Categories (BEC), and Statistical Classification of Products by Activity in the European Economic Community (CPA). Countries are classified according to the Nomenclature of countries and territories for the European statistics on international trade in goods. Scope of the data In Lithuania ITGS follow the EU concept. For Extra-EU trade in principle the relaxed definition of the special trade system is applied, whereas intra-EU trade is close to the general trade system. Geographical coverage: Statistical territory of Lithuania corresponds to the Lithuanian customs territory. Exceptions to coverage Goods and movements excluded from ITGS: Monetary gold; Means of payment which are legal tender and securities; Goods moving between: a) Lithuania and its territorial enclaves in other country, and b) Lithuania (the host country) and territorial enclaves of other countries or international organisations. Territorial enclaves include embassies and national armed forces stationed outside the territory of the mother country; Goods for and following temporary use, provided all the following conditions are met: a) no processing is planned or made; b) the expected duration of the temporary use is no longer than 24 months; c) no change of ownership took place; Goods for and after repair or maintenance and replacement parts that are incorporated in the framework of the repair or maintenance and replaced defective parts; Goods used as carriers of customised information including software; Data and software downloaded from the internet; Means of transport travelling in the course of their work; Periodicals under subscription. A comprehensive list of goods excluded for methodological reasons is given in the Appendix of Annex V of the Commission Implementing Regulation No 2020/1197. Unrecorded activity Unrecorded activity: Estimates of unrecorded transactions, including smuggling, are presently not included in merchandise trade statistics. 2.4.1 Valuation Conversion to unit of account: Extra trade part: the value of transactions in foreign currencies is converted to the national currency at the exchange rate on the date of customs clearance. Customs fixed monthly exchange rate is used for conversion. Intra trade part: values of transactions are submitted in national currency. The official exchange rate of the Bank of Lithuania is used on the date when taxable amount is determined for VAT purposes. Valuation: F.o.b.-type value is used for exports of goods and c.i.f.-type value – for imports of goods. In case of processing transactions, the value of the goods s is the total amount which would have been invoiced in case of sale or purchase, i.e. current sales value must be established both for the primary material delivered free of charge and for the finished product respectively. If certain values are unknown, accurate estimates, for instance with the help of current market prices of the same or similar commodities, are made. Barter transactions are also valued at market price. 2.4.2 Recording basis The reference period is the calendar month in which the import or export takes place. According to the EU legislation it can be adapted to the month during which VAT becomes chargeable (Intra-EU trade) or the month during which the customs declaration is accepted by Lithuanian Customs (Extra-EU trade). 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Sources: Extra-EU trade: importers' and exporters' customs declarations. Intra-EU trade: Intrastat statistical reports collected directly from traders by the customs of the Republic of Lithuania. Intrastat exemption threshold defines the value (the value of the trade with the EU during the previous calendar year or the running total value of the trade with the EU since the beginning of the year) below which VAT-registered units are exempted from providing any Intrastat Information. Thresholds are defined separately for dispatches and arrivals. VAT data are used to estimate the value of the trade below Intrastat thresholds and non-response. Based on the requirements of EU legislation, from the reference period of January 2024, if the Member State of actual export can be determined, goods in quasi-export must be included in the statistics of the Member State of actual export. Quasi-export occurs when goods are brought from one Member State to the Member State where customs clearance takes place, for the sole purpose of declaring the goods to customs. Therefore, customs data received from other Member States related to goods in quasi-export, where the Member State of actual export is Lithuania, are included in Lithuania's international trade in goods statistics. Data on goods in quasi-export declared to Lithuanian Customs, when the Member State of actual export can be determined, are submitted to that Member State, and are not included in Lithuania's international trade in goods statistics. In cases where it is impossible to accurately determine the Member State of actual export, goods in quasi-export continue to be included in the statistics of Lithuania's international trade in goods. Type of data collected: Extra-EU trade: CN 8-digit commodity code or TARIC, country of destination/consignment, country of origin (on imports only), county (on exports only for the goods of Lithuanian origin), mode of transport at the frontier, container, nationality of the means of transport, internal mode of transport, nature of transaction, statistical value, quantity expressed in kilograms and supplementary units, preference on imports, customs procedure. Intra-EU trade: CN 8-digit product code, country of destination/consignment, country of origin, county (on exports only for goods of Lithuanian origin), mode of transport, nature of transaction, delivery terms, statistical value, invoiced value, quantity expressed in kilograms and supplementary units, ID number of partner operator. Basis of country data: Partner country refers to the country of destination for exports, for imports the country of origin for Extra-EU trade, and the country of consignment for Intra-EU trade. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording Source data fully comply with requirements of EU legislation on ITGS. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Concerning Extra-EU trade, data are recorded on the time of clearance by Customs and transmitted to the SL on monthly basis. Concerning Intra-EU trade, the 10th working day after the end of the reference month is the deadline of submission for monthly Intrastat statistical reports. 3.2.1 Source data assessment Intrastat data are regularly assessed using VAT data received from the State Tax Inspectorate. The estimations for trade below Intrastat thresholds and non-response are evaluated and if needed improved. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Processing system: After the implementation of Intrastat system in Lithuania in 2004, the new national Foreign Trade Data Processing and Dissemination system (UPS IS) was developed and implemented. UPS IS is designed to store, analyse and disseminate statistical information on Lithuania's international trade in goods using data collected by Extrastat and Intrastat systems. Site of processing: the SL. Adjustments to improve coverage/classification/timing/valuation: Extrastat data reflects complete coverage of data. For the trade with EU countries, monthly adjustments for non-response and the trade below threshold are made based on VAT data. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures Seasonal effects have been adjusted for since 2002. Seasonal adjustment is applied to the aggregated time series of three indicators: Exports (total international trade in goods data), thousand euros; Exports of Lithuanian origin (total international trade in goods data), thousand euros; and Imports (total international trade in goods data), thousand euros. The periodicity of the time series is monthly or quarterly. The comparisons between Intrastat and VAT/VIES data are performed. Other available data sources are used. Periodically data are compared with the foreign trade statistics published by partner countries. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results Different quality checks (validity checks, credibility checks, price controls, and logical controls) are undertaken before the final release of the data. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data The assessment of data is carried out regularly within the data processing framework. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs Investigations of statistical data discrepancies are carried out regularly. If necessary, data are corrected. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses All the revisions of the data are studied systematically. The new findings are used to supplement routines of data processing procedures of international trade in goods statistics. The data control system is improved when the revisions concern errors. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses The information on revisions including the average size (the relative mean absolute revision) is published in Metadata on International Trade in Goods Statistics . 5.1.5 Dissemination on request Unpublished non-confidential international trade in goods statistics is available upon request. 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points The information on contact points is presented in the news releases, Database of Indicators and Predefined tables. 5.3.2 Availability of documents and services catalogs Terms and conditions for subscription, as well as a catalogue of statistical publications, are published on the SL's Internet website Statistical information dissemination and data reuse services - Valstybės duomenų agentūra .
