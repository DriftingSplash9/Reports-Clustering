 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Production index are compiled according to the International Recommendations for the Index of Industrial Production 2009 (New York, January 2009: United Nations Statistics Division). The scope of the industrial sector cover section B - mining and quarrying and section C - manufacturing of ISIC rev4; 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization At the national, regional and state levels, indices are published for general industry, industry classes, and industrial activities, based on the National Classification of Economic Activities (CNAE) in accordance with ISIC Rev. 4. Scope of the data Cut-off sampling is used in the selection of products and respondents, based on the variable Value of Production of products obtained in the Annual Survey of Mining and Manufacturing Industry - Product Statistics. None 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The Industrial Production Index is based on the Monthly Industrial Survey of Physical Production. Primary data are obtained directly from companies, through eletronic questionnaire on IBGE's website. In 2023, the survey was been updated, based on information from the 2019 Annual Survey of Mining and Manufacturing Industry and it covers 88 percent of the value added of mining and the manufacturing industry (Section B and C ISIC Rev.4). The new sample comprises 1042 products produced in approximately 8,500 enterprises and 12,500 local units. Independent samples were selected for Brazil as a whole, for the northeast region and for the states of Amazonas, Pará, Maranhão, Ceará, Rio Grande do Norte, Pernambuco, Bahia, Minas Gerais, Espírito Santo, Rio de Janeiro, São Paulo, Paraná, Santa Catarina, Rio Grande do Sul, Mato Grosso do Sul, Mato Grosso and Goiás 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Respondents are made aware of the deadlines set for reporting and mostly data are collected in the first ten days of the month. 3.2.1 Source data assessment Information about physical production is analysed for the selected products, based on firms’ accounting records, as well as reasons for changes in quantities produced (above 10% and below -10%), selected from a report of 16 possible reasons. The information and the assessment and consistency reports generated by the system are used in the process of confirming the physical production data. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures The weighting system is fixed and it folows the structure of the Industrial Value Added of year 2019 (reference period for weighting). The system of weights is based on the relative importance of each product in the Value Added of General Industry. The sum of the product weights equals the weight of the industrial activity, the sum of which, in turn, yields the weights of the sections (mining and the processing industry). The sum of the mining and manufacturing sections, in turn, is the total for general industry. As the index does not cover the universe of industry activities and products, the weights of segments and products not represented in the calculation were distributed in proportion to the relative importance of those that were calculated. The formula used is a short-run modified Laspeyres index. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request Only public and regularly disseminated data are available; no further breakdowns are released. 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points IBGE’s publications contain the names of all personnel responsible for the compilation of data.
