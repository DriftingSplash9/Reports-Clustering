 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions Estimates of GDP at market prices are prepared by the Statistics Division on a regular basis; Most recent estimates were made using the production approach and are available by industry in both real (base year 2006) and nominal terms; no estimates are available for income. The expenditure approach is under development (as at December 2012); Underlying concepts are based on the SNA 1993; GNI estimates are now compiled on a regular basis and available on demand in Excel spreadsheets. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Industries classified using ISIC Rev 3. International trade in goods statistics is classified by Harmonized System 2002. The harmonized System 2012 should be implemented in 2013. The data are also available according to the SITC Rev 3. Scope of the data All market transactions and non-market production are covered in principle. All resident entities engaging in economic activities or transactions are included. Under the present system this does not include nonresident foreign fishing vessels operating non permanently in Kiribati waters. Covers the entire country. It is worth noting that the Kiribati economy and overall land mass are very small by world standards, but that the geographic boundaries of Kiribati are extremely large, spanning around 2,000 km from east to west - this poses major logistical problems for a small statistical system e.g., getting import documentation physically from Kiritimati Island in the east to Tarawa in the west; travelers flying between Tarawa and Kiritimati must transit through Fiji. Unrecorded activity No estimates are made for the value of illegal activities to be included in the estimation of GDP Informal or subsistence activities are recorded to the extent possible with the available source data. 2.4.1 Valuation Transactions are valued at current market prices (including subsistence production); calendar year. 2.4.2 Recording basis Cash basis for households and the government sector and accrual basis for other sectors 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Combination of administrative records (covering the whole country e.g., tax data, merchandise trade), and surveys (which only cover businesses operating in South Tarawa, the commercial part of the main island). The Household Income and Expenditure Survey was run in 1996, 2006 and is planned for 2013. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques Constant price estimates are obtained by a mixture of quantity extrapolation (for major commodities e.g., electricity) and price deflation of current price values for the balance Historically the estimates have not been subject to major revision.
