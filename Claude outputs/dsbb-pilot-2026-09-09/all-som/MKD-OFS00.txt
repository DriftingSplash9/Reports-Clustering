 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions The analytical framework used by the NBRNM in compiling Other Financial Corporations Survey reflects concepts and principles from the IMF' Monetary and Financial Statistics Manual and Compilation Guide from 2016 (MFSMCG - IMF, 2016).From January 2017 onward data has been revised for the purposes of applying a new methodology in accordance with the new IMF Monetary and Financial Statistics Manual and Compilation Guide, 2016. An Other Financial Corporation Survey is consisted of other financial corporations (investment funds, pension funds, insurance companies, financial leasing companies, financial companies, investment funds management companies and pension funds management companies) data and is compiled on a quarterly basis, in compliance with the MFSMCG. The data are presented as stock data in millions of Denars, with foreign positions valued at current exchange rate. The major components are the following: Net foreign assets represent the difference between foreign assets (Claims on nonresidents) and foreign liabilities (Liabilities to nonresidents) of other financial corporations (investment funds, pension funds, insurance companies, financial leasing companies, financial companies, investment funds management companies and pension funds management companies). This indicates that all foreign assets and liabilities of other financial corporations are aggregated and presented on net and gross basis. Domestic claims are consisted of other financial corporations' claims on government (represented on net basis), claims on depository corporations and claims on other sectors (represented on gross basis). Liabilities to depository corporations refer to loans from the other depository corporations that banks extend to the other financial corporations. Other domestic liabilities (except those included in Other items (net)) are consisted of the liabilities of the other financial corporations to the other domestic sectors based on loans, shares, and other liabilities. Shares and other equity in other financial corporations' survey incorporate the funds contributed by owners, (retained earnings, current year result, general and specific impairment and the special reserves, as well as the valuation adjustments. Category Other items, net presents all categories not comprised in Other Financial Corporations Balance sheet, as well as all unclassified assets and liabilities from this survey. Quarterly balance sheet of Other Financial Corporations (investment funds, pension funds, insurance companies, financial leasing companies, financial companies, investment funds management companies and pension funds management companies) are produced from quarterly reports on the stock and the flow of assets and liabilities of the other financial corporations (OFI-1 Form) and these balance sheets are basis for the compilation of Other Financial Corporations Survey. 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization The sectorization adopted for Other Financial Corporation Survey is in line with the MFSM (consistent with the Classification of the financial instruments and institutional sectors in the SNA 1993 and ESA 1995). Reporting units distinguish resident from nonresident counterparts, using the definitions on residency prescribed in the Law on Foreign Exchange Operations (Official Gazette No. 34/01, 49/01, 103/01 and 51/03). Residency of institutional units is defined under the Law on Foreign Exchange Operations, where natural persons staying temporarily in the Republic of North Macedonia based on a valid residence permit or work permit for a period of at least six months are treated as residents of the country. (Such definition of residency of natural persons and its application in the process of creating statistical data is an exception to the recommendation given in the international statistical standards, according to which a criterion for determining residency is a stay or intention to stay in a country for a period of one year or more.) The resident sector is divided into five sub sectors:(i) Non-financial corporations, covering public and other (private) non-financial corporations; (ii) Government, which is disaggregated to central government ,social security's funds, local government, (iii) Depository corporations (NBRNM, banks and savings houses); (iv) Other financial corporations (Insurance companies, Pension funds and Other financial corporations) (v) Other resident sector including households (individuals and self-employed individuals) and Nonprofit institutions serving households. 2.2.1 Scope The sectorization adopted for Other Financial Corporation Survey is in line with the MFSM (consistent with the Classification of the financial instruments and institutional sectors in the SNA 1993 and ESA 1995). Scope of the data The other financial sector in North Macedonia consists of: a) investment funds, b) pension funds, c) insurance companies, d) financial leasing companies, e) financial companies, f) investment funds management companies and g) pension funds management companies. The scope is broadly consistent with internationally accepted standards, covering the OFI's balance sheet data. 2.4.1 Valuation The general recommendation of the MFSMCG is to evaluate financial assets and liabilities based on market prices or market price equivalents. The exceptions to this rule are the valuation of deposits and loans (and most other accounts payable/receivable categories) and for the liabilities in form of shares and other equity. These principles are broadly followed in North Macedonia's Other Financial corporation Survey. Traded financial instruments are valued monthly using market prices. Conversion of foreign-currency- denominated assets and liabilities into local currency is carried out using official exchange rates in effect at the balance sheet date. For this purpose, other financial corporations use the end-period midpoint of buying and selling rates quoted by the NBRNM. The official exchange rate is a weighted average rate determined by the interbank foreign exchange market conditions. Valuation adjustments are presented in the valuation accounts in the balance sheets of the other financial corporations, but not separately identified in their analytical accounts. 2.4.2 Recording basis In North Macedonia's Other Financial Corporation Survey, financial transactions are recorded on an accrual basis. Interest on financial assets or liabilities is accrued at the end of each month, and accrued interest is recorded under the underlying financial instruments consistent with MFSMCG recommendations. The MFSMCG also recommends that interest arrears be included with the underlying instruments. The current practice of the NBRNM is consistent with the MFSMCG. Financial transactions are recorded simultaneously between transactors in conformity with MFSMCG guidelines. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures A general principle in macroeconomic statistics is that data should be collected and compiled on a gross basis. For data presented on a net basis, the underlying gross data should also be shown. In North Macedonia's Other Financial Corporations Survey, assets and liabilities are presented on a gross or net basis in conformity with the MFSMCG guidelines. In particular, claims on a particular transactor or group of transactors are not netted against liabilities to that transactor or group. Also, provisions against loans are shown on a gross basis on the liability side, and loans are included in domestic credit on a gross basis on the asset side. The Other Financial Corporations Survey shows underlying gross data for net foreign assets and net claims on central government. In the Other Financial Corporation Survey, appropriate claims and liabilities between other financial corporations are appropriately canceled out, while preserving the presentation of data on claims on or liabilities to other domestic sectors and nonresidents. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs The source data for compiling Other Financial Corporation Survey are: (i) The Other Financial Corporations' Balance sheet is prepared from separate balance sheets of investment funds, pension funds, insurance companies, financial leasing companies, financial companies, investment funds management companies and pension funds management companies which are compiled from the individual "Quarterly reports on the stock and the flow of assets and liabilities of the other financial corporations (OFI-1 Form)" provided by the reporters, on a quarterly basis and are transmitted electronically to NBRNM's database. Requirements and rules for compiling the "Quarterly reports on the stock and the flow of assets and liabilities of the other financial corporations (OFI-1 Form)", are consistent with the International Accounting Standards, and so, other financial corporations' reporting format, provides sufficient detail to compile data in conformity with the MFSMCG guidelines. The source data are kept under continuous review to ensure that the data collection system remains comprehensive, reflects recent developments, and meets new data demands. 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording The source data, reported in accordance with NBRNM requirements, reasonably approximate the definitions, scope, classification, valuation, and timing of reporting needed for monetary statistics. One deviation in accounting practice is that securities held to maturity are recorded at book value. The NBRNM provides other financial corporations with guidance in the form of reporting instructions and ad-hoc consultation in completing the report forms. The NBRNM's Other Financial Corporations Survey compilers maintain regular contact with reporting institutions to resolve data issues that have important implications for the accuracy of the accounting data for use in Other Financial Corporation Survey. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness The "Quarterly reports on the stock and the flow of assets and liabilities of the other financial corporations (OFI-1 Form)" are required to become available 20 days after the end of the reference quarter. These source data are provided sufficiently timely to allow for timely compilation of Other Financial Corporation Survey. 3.2.1 Source data assessment The NBRNM' Other Financial Corporations Survey compilers, in collaboration with other NBRNM units involved in source data collection, review the reporting system for its adequacy for providing reliable source data. Automated data validation procedures are employed to check the internal consistency of reported data. In addition, source data are also reviewed against those of previous periods to identify out-of-trend items. The identified data inconsistencies are investigated with the reporting units. However, questions about the adequacy of the processes in place for assessing the source data arose from data inconsistency and misclassification identified by users on several occasions. 3.3.1 Source data statistical techniques The forms for reporting source data are designed to encourage use of computer programs, which enable prompt data processing. The potential for processing errors is minimized by the use of electronic reporting and processing procedures. 3.3.2 Other statistical procedures The procedure for delayed reporting of data by other financial corporations is to carry forward the data of the last quarter of the late reporting institution into the current quarter's statistics. So far, no reporting delays have occurred. 3.4.1 Validation of intermediate results The accuracy and reliability of the reported source data for the other financial corporations are checked against other sources or information. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data The NBRNM' Other Financial Corporations Survey compilers identify statistical discrepancies (e.g. discrepancies in inter sectoral positions) and, in collaboration with Supervisory agency and data reporters, investigate their causes. 3.4.3 Assessment of discrepancies and other problems in statistical outputs Procedures are in place to investigate large and unexplained movements in financial indicators to detect possible classification and sectorization errors. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses The NBRNM studies and analyzes revisions as part of its data quality control process. The NBRNM data compilers cross-check preliminary data with final data and investigate any significant discrepancies. The results of investigations and adjustments made in the data compiling process are taken into account in subsequent periods. In general, revisions tend to be small in magnitude. Regular revision policy of final data is applied on the end of each year. 4.3.3 Dissemination of revision studies and analyses Studies and analysis of revisions are undertaken ad hoc to measure, assess, and explain the source of revisions made to the data. These studies are internally documented and not made available to the public. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request Statistics not regularly published are made available to interested users upon their request and approval by the Governor's Office, without a fee. 5.3.1 Dissemination of information on contact points Information on specific contact persons is posted on the NBRNM internet website for users to send comments, queries, and requests concerning any category of Other Financial Corporations Survey published by the NBRNM. Users frequently request assistance in the form of clarification and explanation of data through email, telephone, or mail, and the assistance is in general promptly provided. The NBRNM reviews closely comments, queries, and requests from general data users as well as from the NBRNM Council to obtain users' feedback. In particular, all user queries sent to the Governor's Office are distributed to concerned NBRNM departments. Actions that are taken to address these issues are reported to the Governor's Office to ensure that users' requests are addressed. 5.3.2 Availability of documents and services catalogs The NBRNM publishes a list of publications on its website. NBRNM publications are also provided to users upon request free of charge.
