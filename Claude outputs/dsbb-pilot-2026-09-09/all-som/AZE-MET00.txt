 2.1.1 Concepts and definitions International Merchandise Trade Statistics: Concepts and Definitions (IMTS, Rev.2) 2.3.1 Classification/sectorization Harmonized Commodity Description and Coding System (Harmonized System or HS). 2.2.1 Scope Harmonized Commodity Description and Coding System (Harmonized System or HS). Scope of the data Record all goods which add to or subtract from the stock of material resources of a country by entering (imports) or leaving (exports) its economic territory: Non-monetary gold; Unissued& banknotes and securities, and coins not in circulation; Goods traded in accordance with barter agreements Goods traded government account; Food and other humanitarian aid; Goods on consignment; Goods used as carriers of information and software; Goods for processing; Returned goods; Electricity and gas Goods under financial lease; Exceptions to coverage The goods crossing frontiers without changing ownership are not covered under goods. The types of goods that excluded are: direct transit trade; returned exports and imports; goods shipped under operational leasing arrangements; temporary exports and imports of goods that are not for sale; goods that cross frontiers and are lost or destroyed. Unrecorded activity The main adjustments are to exclude the cost of freight and insurance from imports valued on a c.i.f. basis to obtain an estimated f.o.b. basis. The exports/imports data are reflected on an f.o.b. basis. The c.i.ff.o.b. factor for valuing imports is estimated on the basis of the customs and statistical declarations on the terms of delivery of the goods. 2.4.1 Valuation The statistical value of imported goods be a CIF-type value; The statistical value of exported goods be a FOB-type value; 2.4.2 Recording basis Transactions involving foreign trade should be recorded at the time of the change of ownership of the goods. Goods are considered to change ownership when the parties enter the goods in their books as a real asset and make a corresponding change to their financial assets and liabilities. In practice, the timing of the change of ownership is usually assumed to be approximately the same as the time of customs recording. 2.4.3 Grossing/netting procedures Foreign trade transactions are recorded on a gross basis. 3.1.1 Source data collection programs Data on imports/exports of goods are compiled from customs declarations, which are processed by the Customs Committee (CC) and the aggregated results are forwarded to the State Statistical Committee (SSC). 3.1.2 Source data definitions, scope, classifications, valuation, and time of recording The main source of information is customs statistics of foreign trade of the Republic of Azerbaijan. 3.1.3 Source data timeliness Data on trade are compiled monthly. 3.2.1 Source data assessment Foreign trade data are based on data of customs statistics monthly basis. 3.4.2 Assessment of intermediate data Intermediate data are analyzed for internal consistency. 3.5.1 Revision studies and analyses Revisions in the foreign trade statistics are constantly monitored and discussed in bilateral meetings between the CBAR and the AzStat. 5.1.5 Dissemination on request Request from individual and legal entities are responded and provided with the non-confidential data.
